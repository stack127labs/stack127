## Does a good fit mean I'll be accepted?

No. The requirements checker gives an estimate, based on each network's published rules and the sites we track. Every network still reviews your content, where your traffic comes from, and your account history before it says yes.

Open Details on any network for its published numbers, the date we checked them, and a link to its own page.
