## Is WHOIS still used?

Less and less. ICANN stopped requiring WHOIS for most generic endings, such as `.org`, on 28 January 2025, and RDAP replaced it. Some country endings, such as `.it`, `.eu`, and `.jp`, still offer only WHOIS. The tools here use RDAP when the registry offers it and the registry's WHOIS server when it doesn't. The [domain lookup](/domain-lookup) page explains how the two differ.

## Can every domain ending be looked up?

Most can, but not all. A few endings, mostly small country endings, have no lookup service the tools can use. For those, the tools say they can't check the name, which doesn't mean it's free. The [TLD directory](/tld-directory) shows how each ending is checked and links each registry.

## Can I see who owned a domain before?

No. The registry returns only the current record, with no past owners or old nameservers. Sites that show history use copies they saved over time.

## What happens to the names I look up?

The name goes from stack127 to the registry, and to the registrar that holds it. stack127 keeps saved answers by domain name, not linked to you. A result can be a saved answer up to 24 hours old, and it shows when it was fetched.
