## How does Kindle Unlimited pay for page reads?

KDP pays a set amount for each page read, called the KENP rate. It sets the rate each month from the KDP Select Global Fund and the pages read in each marketplace.

<Formula title="Kindle Unlimited royalty">

`KENP Read × KENP rate`

The rate itself: `the marketplace's fund ÷ total KENP read there that month`

</Formula>

So the rate changes every month and differs between marketplaces. KDP pays only for pages a customer reads the first time. Rereading the same pages doesn't earn again. See KDP's [Kindle Unlimited royalty guide](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201541130).

## What's the difference between KENPC and KENP Read?

KENPC is your book's length. KENP Read is how many pages people read.

| Term | What it is | Where to find it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KENPC | Your book's page count, measured by KDP with standard font and spacing | Your Bookshelf, on the book's Promote and Advertise page |
| KENP Read | Pages customers read that month | KDP Reports |

One full read of a book is worth about its KENPC × the rate. A book with a KENPC of 300, at 0.00458 USD per page, earns 1.37 USD per full read.

<Note title="The 3,000-page limit">

KDP pays for up to 3,000 pages per book per customer. Your monthly total can be much higher, because it adds up many customers. Enter the total from your report as it is. The calculator can't see single customers, so it doesn't apply the limit.

</Note>

## Why doesn't my estimate match what KDP paid?

A few things can make them differ:

- **The numbers weren't final.** KENP totals can change until near the 15th of the next month.
- **The rate wasn't that month's.** A rate from another month or store gives a different answer.
- **Currency.** If KDP pays you in another currency, the exchange rate at payment time changes the amount.

For a past month, KDP's [Prior Months' Royalties report](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVTTXHKHVPAPBEDQ) shows what your book actually earned.

## What doesn't this calculator include?

- Ordinary eBook and print sales. Use the [KDP royalty calculator](/kdp-royalty-calculator).
- [KDP Select bonuses](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201623400), taxes, or currency conversion.
- Future reads. It doesn't predict how many pages people will read.

To see when KDP will pay a month's royalties, use the [KDP payment date calculator](/kdp-payment-date-calculator).
