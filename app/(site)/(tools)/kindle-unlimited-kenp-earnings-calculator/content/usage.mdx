## What to enter

- Earnings month and marketplace. The month and store you're estimating, like `2026-08` and `Amazon.com`. Reads and rate must both be for this month and store.
- KENP Read. This book's page-read total for the month in [KDP Reports](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVTTXHKHVPAPBEDQ). Type digits only, like `60000`, not `60,000`. Don't use the book's KENPC or print page count.
- Currency code. Three letters that match the rate, like `USD` or `JPY`. The calculator doesn't convert currencies.
- Rate per KENP. What KDP paid per page read that month. KDP announces each month's fund near the 15th of the next month, and its [Royalties Estimator](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G6BZ4M9PVJ8YSCW8) uses the latest rate. Up to eight decimals.
- Rate basis. Pick **Rate I entered from KDP for this month** only if the rate is KDP's for that month. If you borrowed another month's rate or are testing one, pick **My planning assumption**. It labels the result. The calculator can't check the rate.

## How to read the result

The result is KENP Read × your rate, rounded once to the currency's smallest unit: cents for USD, whole yen for JPY. A positive estimate that rounds to zero shows 'Less than' the currency's smallest unit, such as `Less than 0.01 USD`. Zero reads with a valid rate show `0.00 USD`. A blank rate gives no result, because the amount is unknown, not zero.

<Example title="60,000 pages read">

At a rate of 0.00458 USD per page: 60,000 × 0.00458 = 274.80 USD. This rate is only an example, not a current KDP rate.

</Example>
