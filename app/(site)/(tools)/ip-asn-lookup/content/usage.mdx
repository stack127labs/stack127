## What to enter

Enter one public IPv4 or IPv6 address, or an AS number such as `AS13335` or `13335`. To look up a domain, find its IP address first with the [DNS lookup](/dns-lookup).

Private addresses such as `192.168.1.10` have no public record, so the tool doesn't look them up. They only exist inside a local network.

## How to read the result

The top line names the registered holder and the registry that holds the record. Below it are the address range, the abuse contact, and the current route.

<Example title="8.8.8.8">

The lead reads `8.8.8.8: Google LLC (ARIN).` The allocation is `8.8.8.0 to 8.8.8.255`, and the abuse contact is `network-abuse@google.com`.

The current BGP route shows origin `AS15169` and announced prefix `8.8.8.0/24`, from Team Cymru.

</Example>

A field marked `Unknown` or `Not published` means the registry left it out. It doesn't mean the value is zero or the organization doesn't exist.
