## How to read the result

The large number is the time since the current registration began, rounded to years and months. Use the date under it when the exact day matters.

If you paste a full address, the checker looks up the registered domain. `https://shop.example.com/products` checks `example.com`, because a subdomain has no registration of its own.

Some registries publish a re-registration date. When they do, the age counts from that later date, and the timeline still shows the first registration. The record doesn't say why the name was registered again or whether the owner changed.
