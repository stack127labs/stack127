## What to enter

Recognized revenue is revenue for the service you delivered in the period. It isn't cash collected or MRR. A customer who pays for a year in January is usually recognized across the year as you deliver the service, not all in January.

Use revenue and costs from the same scope. For one product, enter only that product's revenue and the costs of delivering it.

Put each delivery cost in one field, once. For someone who splits their time, such as a founder who also answers support, include only the delivery share. Leave out sales, marketing, building new features, and general admin.

## How to read the result

<Example title="$200,000 of revenue in a quarter">

Hosting $22,000. Third-party APIs $8,000. Payment processing $6,000. Support people $12,000. Other $2,000.

Cost of revenue is $50,000. Gross profit is $150,000.00, and gross margin is 75.00%.

</Example>

The table lists each cost so you can check it against your books. A cost higher than revenue gives a negative margin, which is a real result, not an error.
