## What do I need to enter?

Your monthly traffic from your analytics or ad dashboard, plus one or two numbers from your ad network.

| Tab | You enter | You get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue | Traffic and RPM | Monthly and yearly revenue |
| CPM to RPM | CPM and ad impressions per pageview or session | Your RPM, plus revenue if you add traffic |
| Find RPM | One month's ad revenue and traffic | Your RPM |

Pick pageviews or sessions to match your network. AdSense reports page RPM. Mediavine reports session RPM.

## How do I read the result?

The yearly figure assumes every month looks like this one. Ad rates move through the year, so treat it as a rough total, not a forecast.
