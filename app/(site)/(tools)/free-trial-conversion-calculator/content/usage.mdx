## What to enter

1. Pick one cohort: everyone who started a trial in one period, such as August.
2. Count how many of those same accounts paid, whenever they paid. Leave out payments from people who started in another month.
3. Count accounts in both fields. If your billing tool counts subscriptions or seats, one customer can show up twice.

Paid means the first payment went through. A card on file, a pending invoice, or a failed charge isn't a payment yet.

The `Window, activation, revenue, and cohorts` section is optional. Leave it closed and the answer uses your two counts alone.

| Add | When you want to know |
| --- | --- |
| `Follow-up window` and `Cohort age` | Whether the cohort has had long enough to pay. A young cohort shows `Provisional` and the days left. |
| `Activated` | Where people drop off. Use your own activation event, such as creating a first project, and count everyone who paid in it. |
| `Target conversion` | How many more paying accounts you'd need to reach a higher rate. |
| `Monthly price` and `Annual price` | The new MRR these customers added. With both prices, also enter how many paid accounts are on annual plans. |
| `Other cohorts` | Whether the rate holds across months, channels, or plans. Each cohort needs its own accounts. |

## How to read the result

The big number is the share of your trial starts that paid, with the counts under it so you can check the division. The grid shows the same share as people out of every 100.

The bar under it shows your rate as `You`. The line under the track is your likely range: where the true rate probably sits, given how many trials you counted. The colored bands are published medians and top quarters for the trial type you picked. They count payments within six months, so they show only when your follow-up window is six months or not set. With a 30-day window, the bar shows your rate and likely range alone.

With other cohorts, `All cohorts` divides total paid by total starts. It leaves out cohorts still marked `Provisional`, so a young month doesn't pull the rate down. If every cohort is still provisional, it combines them all, and its `?` says so.

<Example title="160 trial starts, 3 paid">

The rate is 1.88%, and 157 didn't pay. The likely range runs from 0.63% to 5.37%.

That range overlaps the published no-card median of 4 to 6%. With 160 trials, you can't yet tell whether your rate is lower than typical. A few more months of trials will narrow the range.
