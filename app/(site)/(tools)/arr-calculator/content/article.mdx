## What does ARR mean?

ARR means either annualized run rate or annual recurring revenue. Both use the same three letters, but they count different things. This calculator gives annualized run rate.

| | Annualized run rate | Annual recurring revenue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How it's worked out | MRR × 12 | Each contract's value ÷ its years, for contracts of 12 months or longer |
| Counts monthly plans | Yes | No |
| Fits | Businesses billing mostly monthly | Businesses billing mostly yearly or on multi-year contracts |

[ChartMogul's ARR guide](https://chartmogul.com/saas-metrics/arr/) sets out both meanings and says most SaaS companies now mean run rate. If you bill both monthly and yearly, run rate is the one that counts everything.

<Example title="Same business, two ARR figures">

MRR is $10,000: $8,000 from monthly plans and $2,000 from yearly plans that pay $24,000 a year.

- Annualized run rate: $10,000 × 12 = $120,000
- Annual recurring revenue: $24,000, from the yearly plans only

</Example>

## Is ARR the revenue you'll make this year?

No. ARR is today's monthly rate stretched over a year. It isn't cash collected, revenue already earned, or a forecast. New customers, upgrades, and cancellations change it next month.

## Why doesn't a company's reported ARR match MRR × 12?

Companies set their own ARR rules, and many explain them in their filings. [Vertex](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1806837/000110465926090420/verx-20260630x10q.htm) uses MRR from the last month of the period × 12. [Bentley Systems](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1031308/000103130824000008/a24q2exhibit992.htm) adds the yearly value of its recurring contracts to three months of usage-based subscription revenue, annualized.

Before you compare two ARR figures, check which meaning each uses, what it includes, and the date it was taken.

## How do you turn ARR into MRR?

Divide annualized run rate by 12. $120,000 of ARR is $10,000 of MRR. This only works for run-rate ARR. Annual recurring revenue leaves out monthly plans, so dividing it by 12 understates MRR.
