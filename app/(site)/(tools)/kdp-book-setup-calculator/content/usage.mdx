## What to enter

- Page count. Use your final interior file's page count, blank pages included. Odd counts round up to the next even number, and that number sets the margins, spine, and page range check.
- Interior has bleed. Tick it only if something in your interior, like a photo or background color, runs off the page edge. The cover always has bleed either way.
- DPI. Changes only the pixel column. Use 300 for KDP.
- Reading direction. Right to left swaps the back and front panels. It doesn't check whether KDP prints your language that way.

## How to read the result

- **Cover file** is the full size of your cover PDF. It's exact for a paperback and an estimate for a hardcover.
- **Margins** are the smallest KDP allows for this setup.
- **Page range** says whether the even page count fits KDP's range for this trim, ink, and paper. Outside it, you still get dimensions, but KDP won't print that setup.
- **Copy setup receipt** copies the key numbers as text, ready to send to a cover designer.

<Example title="A 300-page 6 × 9 in paperback">

Black and white on white paper, no interior bleed. The spine is 300 × 0.002252 = 0.6756 in. The cover is 0.125 + 6 + 0.6756 + 6 + 0.125 = 12.9256 in wide and 9 + 0.25 = 9.25 in tall, or 3878 × 2775 pixels at 300 DPI. The inside margin is 0.5 in, and the other margins are 0.25 in.

</Example>
