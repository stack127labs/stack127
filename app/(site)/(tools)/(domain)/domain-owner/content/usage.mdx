## How to read the result

| Result | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Published` | The record names a registrant, usually an organization. The name is as the owner gave it, not checked by anyone. |
| `Not published` | No name was returned. A redaction table shows what was withheld, when the provider says. |
| `Cannot determine` | The registry or registrar didn't answer, or the ending has no public lookup service. Try again before treating the name as hidden. |

Under the result, `How to make contact` lists every contact route the record gives. The abuse address is only for reports of phishing, malware, or spam.

`Published`: the registrant organization is GitHub, Inc. The name, street, email, and phone are redacted, and the record gives a MarkMonitor contact form instead.
