## How to read the result

The large name is the registrar the registry has on record. Under it, `Who does what` shows the chain from registry to registrar to any reseller. A reseller only appears when the record names one, so `Not published` doesn't mean there isn't one.

The abuse email and phone are for reporting phishing, malware, or spam. They won't help with account access or billing.

The registrar is MarkMonitor Inc., IANA ID 292. The abuse contact is abusecomplaints@markmonitor.com.

</Example>
