## How is the KDP payment month worked out?

KDP pays royalties every month, about 60 days after the end of the month the sale was reported. For Expanded Distribution it's about 90 days. That's KDP's [payment timing rule](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GK2MKZUL6U3SFBPZ).

<Formula title="Payment month">

Standard: `reported month + 2 months`

Expanded Distribution: `reported month + 3 months`

</Formula>

KDP's own example: January royalties are paid by the end of March. KDP doesn't publish an exact day, so the result is a month, not a date.

## What if my balance is below the payment threshold?

Then KDP holds it and pays later than the estimate. Direct deposit has no threshold. Checks and wire transfers do, and [each marketplace has a separate balance](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201207800). Its threshold depends on the payment method and currency, after applicable tax withholding.

### Selected marketplace thresholds

| Marketplace | Check or wire threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Amazon.com | $100 USD |
| Amazon.co.uk | £100 |
| Amazon.de | €100 |

KDP carries the balance forward. For standard royalties, payment is due about 60 days after the end of the month the accumulated balance meets the threshold. The calculator doesn't know your balance, so it can't tell you when that will be. With direct deposit, a bank's own minimum can still apply after currency conversion or tax withholding.

## How long does payment delivery take?

From the payment date, direct deposit takes 1 to 5 business days, or 7 to 10 for banks in India. A wire takes 5 to 10 days, and a check can take up to 30 days to reach your mailing address. The result lists these with your estimate.

## My KDP payment hasn't arrived. What should I check?

Start with your [Payment Report](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201436840). Find the sales period and look at two fields:

- Payment status. Paid or Failed.
- Payment date. The day Amazon sent the money. It can be days before it shows in your account.

If a wire payment says Paid and the delivery time has passed, KDP suggests [asking your bank](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G4EQZQ73RYEX8RQQ). If there's no payment for that period, check your balance against the threshold above.
