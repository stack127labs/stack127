## What to enter

- Reported earnings month. The month from your KDP royalty report, written as `YYYY-MM`, like `2026-01` for January 2026. Use the month the sale was reported, not the day a reader bought the book.
- Distribution. Pick Standard unless the royalties came through Expanded Distribution. A paperback can earn through both, so the format alone doesn't tell you.

## How to read the result

The result is the month KDP should send the payment, usually toward the end of it. Below it you'll see the payment threshold rules and how long each payment method takes to reach you, counted from the payment date.

The tool uses KDP's current schedule for any year from 2007 to 2100. For a past month, it doesn't confirm that you were paid or that a payment is late.

<Example title="One month, two schedules">

Your January 2026 report has royalties from both channels, so estimate each one separately. Standard royalties should be paid in March 2026, and Expanded Distribution royalties in April 2026.

Late-year months roll into the next year. November 2026 royalties should be paid in January 2027, or February 2027 for Expanded Distribution.
