## What is the difference between a registry, a registrar, and a reseller?

The registry runs a domain ending, the registrar sells and manages names in it, and a reseller sells through a registrar. You deal with the last one in the chain.

| Role | What it does | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registry | Runs one ending and keeps its records | Verisign runs `.com`, Nominet runs `.uk` |
| Registrar | Registers names with the registry and manages them | MarkMonitor, Hostinger |
| Reseller | Sells names through a registrar, often with hosting | A web host or agency |

## Why is the registrar a company I've never heard of?

Usually because you bought the domain through a reseller. Many hosting companies sell domains through wholesale registrars such as Tucows or PDR, so the registrar's name is on the record while your invoice shows the host. A registrar that was bought or renamed can also leave an unfamiliar name.

To find who you actually pay, search your email for a renewal receipt. Billing and support go to that company. Transfers and account recovery often need the registrar too.

## How do you report abuse to a registrar?

Write to the abuse email from the result. Name the exact domain and say what you saw, when, and where, with evidence such as the full URL or the email headers. The registrar may not host the website, and a report doesn't guarantee it will suspend the domain.

If an ICANN-accredited registrar ignores its duties, you can file an [ICANN compliance complaint](https://www.icann.org/compliance/complaint).

## How do you get back into a domain someone else registered for you?

Start with the registrar's or reseller's account recovery, and expect to prove the domain is yours. This often comes up when an agency, contractor, or former employee set it up. Have documents ready that link the domain to you or your business, such as invoices or the registrant details on file.

## Can you tell when a domain changed registrar?

Sometimes. A few registries publish a transfer date, and the result shows it when they do. `Record last changed` isn't a transfer date, because renewals, nameserver edits, and contact changes all move it.

## What is an IANA registrar ID?

It's the fixed number IANA gives each accredited registrar, listed in the [IANA registrar ID registry](https://www.iana.org/assignments/registrar-ids/registrar-ids.xhtml). Registrar names are written in different ways across systems, but the number stays the same, so use it when matching records or filing a complaint.
