## How is KDP royalty calculated?

KDP pays you a share of the list price, minus what it costs to print or deliver the book.

<Formula title="KDP royalty per sale">

Print book: `rate × list price - printing cost`

eBook at 70%: `70% × (list price - delivery cost)`

eBook at 35%: `35% × list price`

</Formula>

| Format | List price | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paperback or hardcover | Up to $9.98 | 50% |
| Paperback or hardcover | $9.99 to $250 | 60% |
| eBook | $2.99 to $12.99 | 70%, if you choose it |
| eBook | Up to $200 | 35% |

Printing cost is a fixed fee plus a cost per page, set by trim size, ink, and paper. Some short books pay one flat fee instead. See KDP's [paperback](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834340) and [hardcover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GHT976ZKSKUXBB6H) printing costs, and its [paperback](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834330) and [hardcover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G77F3WPD3KQLJTFS) royalty rules.

eBook delivery costs $0.15 per MB, at least $0.01, and applies only at 70%. KDP rounds the file size up to the next KB. See KDP's [eBook royalty rules](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634500). Larger files also need a higher lowest price. The [KDP minimum list price calculator](/kdp-minimum-list-price-calculator) lists the price bands.

## Why does one cent change my paperback royalty by a dollar?

The print rate jumps from 50% to 60% at $9.99. For the 300-page paperback above, $9.98 earns $0.39 and $9.99 earns $1.39. If your price is just under $9.99, check that extra cent.

## When does 35% pay more than 70%?

It happens above $12.99 and with large files at low prices.

- A 5 MB eBook earns $8.57 at $12.99. At $13.00 it drops to 35% and earns $4.55. You'd need $24.48 to earn $8.57 again.
- A 20 MB eBook costs $3.00 to deliver. At $2.99 it earns -$0.01 at 70%, but $1.05 at 35%.

## Is royalty the same as profit?

No. Royalty comes before your own costs, like editing, cover design, ads, and your time. To compare paperback and hardcover editions and add your costs, use the [KDP price and profit calculator](/kdp-price-profit-calculator).

## What doesn't this calculator cover?

Results are estimates for Amazon.com in US dollars, using KDP rules checked on September 16, 2026. It doesn't cover:

- Other Amazon stores, currencies, or Expanded Distribution.
- Taxes, refunds, promotions, or price matching.
- Kindle Unlimited page reads. Use the [Kindle Unlimited KENP earnings calculator](/kindle-unlimited-kenp-earnings-calculator).
- Other 70% rules. KDP pays 35% for books that are mostly public domain content, and for eBooks priced less than 20% below your print edition on Amazon.
- Whether KDP will accept your book.

It runs in your browser and doesn't touch your Amazon account. Confirm your price in KDP before you publish.
