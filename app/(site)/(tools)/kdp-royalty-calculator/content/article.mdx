## How is KDP royalty calculated?

KDP pays you a share of the list price, then takes out the cost to print or deliver the book.

<Formula title="KDP royalty per copy">

Print book: `rate × list price - printing cost`

eBook on 70%: `70% × (list price - delivery cost)`

eBook on 35%: `35% × list price`

</Formula>

| Format | List price | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paperback or hardcover | Up to $9.98 | 50% |
| Paperback or hardcover | $9.99 to $250 | 60% |
| Kindle eBook | $2.99 to $12.99 | 70%, if you choose it |
| Kindle eBook | Up to $200 | 35% |

Printing cost is a fixed fee plus a cost per page, set by trim size, ink, and paper. Some short books pay one flat fee instead. See KDP's [paperback](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834340) and [hardcover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GHT976ZKSKUXBB6H) printing costs, and its [paperback](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834330) and [hardcover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G77F3WPD3KQLJTFS) royalty rules.

eBook delivery costs $0.15 per MB of the converted file, rounded up to the next KB, and at least $0.01. You pay it only on the 70% plan. See KDP's [digital book pricing](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634500).

## Why does one cent change a paperback royalty by $1?

The print rate jumps from 50% to 60% at $9.99. A 200-page paperback earns $1.59 at $9.98 and $2.59 at $9.99, because you get 60% of the price instead of 50%, while printing costs the same. If your price sits just under $9.99, check that extra cent.

## Should you choose 35% or 70% for your eBook?

From $2.99 to $12.99, 70% pays more as long as delivery costs less than half your price. A 2 MB eBook at $4.99 earns $3.28 on 70% and $1.75 on 35%.

Two things can make 35% the better or the only choice:

- Pricing above $12.99. KDP raised the top of the 70% band from $9.99 to $12.99 on July 7, 2026. Above it, every sale pays 35%. A 2 MB eBook earns $8.88 at $12.99, but $4.90 at $13.99. At 35% you'd need $25.36 to earn $8.88 again.
- A large file at a low price. A 30 MB eBook costs $4.50 to deliver. At $2.99 the 70% plan pays nothing, while 35% pays $1.05.

KDP also pays 35% in some stores, for books that are mostly public domain content, and in some cases when the eBook isn't priced at least 20% below its print edition. See KDP's [eBook list price rules](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634560).

## What is the lowest list price KDP allows?

For a print book, it's the price where your share just covers printing. A 200-page paperback costs $3.40 to print, so its lowest price is $6.80, where 50% pays exactly $3.40 and you earn nothing.

A long book can skip the 50% rate entirely, because its lowest price lands above $9.98. A 600-page paperback costs $8.20 to print, so its lowest price is $13.67 at 60%. At $14.99 it earns $0.79.

eBooks have a lowest price set by file size: $0.99 under 3 MB, $1.99 from 3 MB to under 10 MB, and $2.99 at 10 MB or more. The 70% plan starts at $2.99 for every size.

The lowest price is a floor KDP sets, not a price it recommends. What readers will pay is a separate question.

## What does Expanded Distribution pay?

Expanded Distribution sells your paperback to bookstores and libraries. It pays 40% of the list price, minus printing, applicable taxes, and withholding. A 200-page paperback at $12.99 earns $1.80 there, against $4.39 on Amazon.com.

KDP says this channel has its own lowest price, but doesn't publish how it's worked out. The calculator shows the break-even price instead, where 40% just covers printing: $8.50 for that paperback. KDP's real minimum may be higher. Your book's Rights & Pricing page in KDP shows the actual figure. See KDP's [Expanded Distribution](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GQTT4W3T5AYK7L45) page.

## Is a KDP royalty the same as profit?

No. A royalty is what KDP pays you before your own costs: editing, cover design, ads, and tax. To add your costs and compare editions, use the [KDP price and profit calculator](/kdp-price-profit-calculator).

## What doesn't this calculator cover?

Results are estimates for Amazon.com in US dollars, using KDP's US rules, last checked October 11, 2026. The calculator doesn't cover:

- Other Amazon stores and currencies. Each has its own printing costs, VAT, and price rules.
- Taxes, refunds, promotions, or price matching, which can change what you're paid.
- Whether your book qualifies for 70% or Expanded Distribution, or whether KDP will accept it.
- Kindle Unlimited page reads. Use the [Kindle Unlimited KENP earnings calculator](/kindle-unlimited-kenp-earnings-calculator).

To find the price for a royalty you want, use the [KDP minimum list price calculator](/kdp-minimum-list-price-calculator). To set an ad bid from your break-even ACoS, use the [KDP book ads bid check](/kdp-break-even-acos-calculator).

The calculator runs in your browser and never touches your Amazon account. Confirm your price in KDP before you publish.
