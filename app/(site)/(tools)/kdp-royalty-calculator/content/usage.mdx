## What to enter

- Page count: every page in your final interior file, blank pages included. KDP prints an even number of pages, so an odd count rounds up. No final file yet? Use the [KDP page count estimator](/kdp-page-count-estimator).
- Trim size and paper: the calculator assumes 6 × 9 in on white paper. If your book differs, change them under Trim, paper, channel, and goal. Larger trims cost more per page.
- eBook file size: KDP charges delivery on the file it converts, which can be larger or smaller than your upload. It shows that size on your book's pricing page. Until you have it, the calculator uses 2 MB.
- Monthly royalty goal: optional. It shows how many copies a month earn that amount.

## How to read the result

| Result | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Royalty per copy | What KDP pays you for one sale at this price. |
| Bar | Where the list price goes: your royalty, printing or delivery, and Amazon's share. |
| Lowest list price | The lowest price KDP lets you set for this book. If you enter less, the answer switches to this price. |
| Share of list price | Your royalty divided by the price. It's also your break-even ACoS: if ads cost more than this share of the sales they bring, each of those sales loses money. |
| At $9.99, On the 35% plan, and similar | The next thing worth checking: a price where the rate changes, or what the other plan or channel pays. |
| Chart | Your royalty at every nearby price, with your price marked. Hover to read any point. |

<Example title="A 200-page paperback at $12.99">

A 6 × 9 in black and white paperback on white paper costs $1.00 + 200 × $0.012 = $3.40 to print. At $12.99 KDP pays 60%, which is $7.79. Take away printing and you earn $4.39 a copy. Amazon keeps the other $5.20.

To earn $1,000 a month from this book, you'd need 228 sales.

</Example>
