## What to enter

- Page count. Use your final interior file's page count, blank pages included. Odd counts round up to the next even number. No final file yet? Use the [KDP page count estimator](/kdp-page-count-estimator).
- eBook file size. KDP measures the file itself, so its size can differ from your upload. Your upload's size is a fair first guess.
- Royalty option. 70% works only from $2.99 to $12.99. Outside that range, the calculator uses 35% and tells you.
- Monthly income target. Optional. It shows how many sales a month you'd need.

## How to read the result

| Result | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Royalty per sale | What Amazon pays you for one copy. |
| Minimum list price | The lowest price KDP lets you set. For print, you earn little or nothing at this price. For an eBook, this shows the 35% file-size minimum, even when you select 70%. Prices below $2.99 use 35%. |
| Royalty margin | Your royalty as a share of the price. |
| Break-even ad spend | The most you can spend on ads per sale before the sale earns nothing. To turn it into a click bid, use the [KDP book ads bid calculator](/kdp-break-even-acos-calculator). |
| Break-even ACoS | That limit as a share of the price. ACoS is ad spend divided by ad sales. |
| Price table | Nearby prices, including where the rate changes. These aren't price suggestions. |

<Example title="A 300-page paperback at $14.99">

A 6 × 9 in black and white paperback on white paper costs $1.00 + 300 × $0.012 = $4.60 to print. At $14.99 the rate is 60%, so you earn $8.99 - $4.60 = $4.39 per sale. A $500 monthly target needs 114 sales.

</Example>
