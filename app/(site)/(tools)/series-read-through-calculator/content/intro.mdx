When someone buys book 1 of your series, some of them go on to buy book 2, then book 3, and so on. Put in each book's price and how many copies it sold, and you'll see how many readers carry on at each step. You'll also see what one new book 1 reader earns you across the whole series, and how much you can spend on ads to get one.

You need each book's Kindle price and copies sold over the same dates, from your KDP reports.
