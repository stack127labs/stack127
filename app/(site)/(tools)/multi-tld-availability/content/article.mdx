## Which endings should I check?

Start with the endings your audience knows and that you're allowed to register. The preselected endings are a starting set that can all be checked, not a recommendation.

For a local business, add your country's ending and read its rules, since some only sell to residents or local companies. For protecting a brand, check the endings people might confuse with yours. This check isn't trademark clearance.

## Why is a free name sometimes not for sale?

The registration record never shows price or who's allowed to buy. A name with no registration can still be reserved, premium-priced, or restricted. The [domain availability checker](/domain-availability) explains each case. Confirm the first-year and renewal price at a registrar before you choose.

## Why is the check limited to twelve endings?

Each ending is a separate request to a different registry, and registries limit how often one address can ask. Every visitor shares stack127's address, so a cap per check and per visitor keeps the tools working for everyone. If you hit a limit, the page says so.

Each name goes from stack127 to its registry. Saved answers are stored by name, not linked to you. A `Registered` row can be up to 24 hours old and a `No registration found` row up to 1 hour old, and each row shows when it was fetched.
