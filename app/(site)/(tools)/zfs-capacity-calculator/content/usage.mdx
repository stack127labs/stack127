## What to enter

Add each vdev on its own. Four drives as two mirrors are two vdevs, not one four-way mirror.

Pick the block size most of your data will use: `recordsize` for datasets, 128 KiB by default, or `volblocksize` for zvols. Small blocks on RAIDZ can lose a lot of space to padding. Leave `ashift` at 12, for 4 KiB sectors, unless you know your drives need another value.

Planning headroom is extra free space you don't plan to fill. It lowers the planning target, not the writable capacity. Spares, SLOG, and L2ARC devices don't add space. A special vdev holds metadata, and the result counts it apart from the data vdevs.

Results show in TiB, the binary unit `zfs list` uses. Switch to TB to compare them with drive labels.

`Share configuration` puts your inputs in a link. The `zpool create -n` command uses placeholder device names, so replace them and check it before you run anything.

<Example title="Six 8 TB drives in one RAIDZ2 vdev">

With a 128 KiB `recordsize` and `ashift=12`, estimated writable capacity is 28.98 TiB, which is 31.86 TB. Parity takes 14.55 TiB and ZFS keeps back 0.13 TiB. With 20% headroom, the planning target is 23.18 TiB.
