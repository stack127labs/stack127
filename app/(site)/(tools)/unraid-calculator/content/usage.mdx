## What to enter

Add your parity drives and data drives separately. The calculator doesn't pick parity for you, because that choice decides which drives you can add later.

Use `Plan a future data drive` to check one you might buy. It isn't added to today's capacity.

Results show in TiB. Switch to TB to compare them with drive labels.

`Share configuration` makes a link with your drives and their roles, so anyone you send it to can see them.
