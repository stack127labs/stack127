## How do I work out my maximum CPC for book ads?

Subtract the profit you want from your royalty, then multiply by your purchase rate.

<Formula title="Maximum CPC">

`(royalty per sale - amount you want to keep) × purchase rate`

</Formula>

The purchase rate goes in as a decimal here, so 10% is 0.10. The calculator rounds down to the cent, so the maximum never goes past your target. If your CPC is above it, you need a lower CPC, a higher purchase rate, or a lower target.

## Is ACoS the same as profit?

No. Amazon Ads [defines ACoS](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/acos-advertising-cost-of-sales) as ad spend ÷ ad sales × 100. Ad sales are what customers paid. Your profit comes from your royalty, which is only part of that.

A 20% ACoS can be fine for one book and a loss for another. It depends on how much of the price you keep. That's why this calculator starts from your royalty, and shows ACoS only when you enter a sales value.

With a target of $0.00, the ACoS ceiling is your break-even ACoS: royalty ÷ sales value. The [KDP royalty calculator](/kdp-royalty-calculator) shows the same figure, using your list price as the sales value.

## Why does the result say my target can't be met?

There are three cases, and each has a different fix.

| What you see | What it means | What to try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No positive CPC at 0% purchases | With no sales from clicks, any click cost is a loss. | Enter the purchase rate you expect, even a low one. |
| Target exceeds royalty | You want to keep more than KDP pays you, even with free clicks. If the target equals your royalty, the maximum CPC is $0.00. | Lower the target, or raise your price. |
| Minimum purchase rate: Above 100%, unattainable | The CPC you entered is too high for any purchase rate. The maximum CPC above it still shows what would work. | Lower the CPC or the target. |

A blank required box or a number like `1,00` or `10%` gives a field error. The optional sales-value box can stay blank. Fix it and check again.

## Which numbers should I use from my ads report?

For a past campaign, use its average CPC, purchases, and sales from your Amazon Ads report. Amazon counts a purchase as coming from an ad within a set time window, so your purchase rate follows its rules. See Amazon Ads' [metric guide](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-us/library/guides/basics-of-success-understanding-amazon-advertising) and [purchase rate definition](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GLPHMX59DC5WSSQW).

The calculator doesn't connect to your ads account. It also leaves out money an ad can bring in later, like sales of the next book in a series, Kindle Unlimited page reads, or extra sales that don't come through ads. It also leaves out taxes and refunds. If your book is in Kindle Unlimited, estimate those reads with the [Kindle Unlimited KENP earnings calculator](/kindle-unlimited-kenp-earnings-calculator). For more on running ads for a book, see Amazon's [Sponsored Products guide for authors](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/authors-guide-to-sponsored-products).
