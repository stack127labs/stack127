## What to enter

Current data is what you store now, not the size of your drives. To estimate annual growth, compare it with how much you stored a year ago. Count snapshots and backups only if they live on this storage.

Operating reserve is free space you never plan to fill. Average draw is the whole system's power use, measured at the wall if you can, not the drives' label ratings. Replacement reserve is a yearly share of the drive cost set aside for failed drives.

`Share plan` makes a link with your inputs, so anyone you send it to can see them.

<Example title="10 TB growing 20% a year on six 12 TB drives with dual parity">

Usable space is 48 TB, or 38.4 TB after a 20% reserve. Data reaches 24.88 TB by year 5, so the plan covers all 5 years. At 40% growth it falls short in year 4. Hardware costs $1,600 and power at 60 W and $0.15 per kWh adds $394.20, for a total of $1,994.20.

</Example>
