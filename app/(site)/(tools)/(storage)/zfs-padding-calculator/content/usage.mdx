## What to enter

Vdev width is the number of drives in this one RAIDZ vdev, not in the whole pool. `zpool get ashift` shows `ashift`. 12 means 4 KiB sectors.

Pick Filesystem for a dataset, where the setting is `recordsize`, or Zvol for a block device, where it's `volblocksize`. `recordsize` is the largest block a file uses, so files smaller than it use one smaller block.

`Share configuration` puts your inputs in a link you can send or bookmark.

<Example title="An 8 KiB block on a six-drive RAIDZ2 with ashift=12">

| Part | Sectors | Space |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data | 2 | 8 KiB |
| Parity | 2 | 8 KiB |
| Padding | 2 | 8 KiB |
| Total | 6 | 24 KiB |

The 8 KiB block takes 24 KiB on disk, so a third of the space holds data.

</Example>
