## What to enter

Protected data is the size of the files you back up, not the size of the disks they sit on.

Retained points is how many restore points you keep, such as 30 for a month of daily backups. For changed data per point, divide a recent incremental backup's size by your data size.

For the recovery check, use restore speeds you've seen on real transfers, not your plan's advertised speed.

`Share plan` makes a link with your inputs, so anyone you send it to can see them.

<Example title="2 TB of data, growing 20% a year">

Over 3 years the data grows to 3.46 TB. Thirty full-plus-incremental points with 5% change each need 8.47 TB. With 20% headroom, each target needs 10.16 TB, so both need 20.32 TB.

With the recovery check on, a full local restore at 1,000 Mbps and 80% efficiency takes 9.6 hours. Off-site at 100 Mbps it takes 96 hours, which misses a 12-hour target.

</Example>
