## What to enter

- ISBN: copy it from your ISBN agency's record or from KDP. Ten or thirteen digits both work, with or without dashes. If you type the dashes, the "ISBN" line above the bars keeps them. Without dashes, that line prints the 13 digits in a row.
- List price: the US price you set for this edition. It becomes the five-digit price code to the right of the bars. Leave it empty if you'd rather not print a price.
- Add-on and size: you can usually skip these. Leave off the price add-on if you sell outside the US and Canada, where book barcodes usually don't carry one. Set a size only if your designer or printer asked for one. Empty picks the largest size, up to 100%, that fits KDP's barcode box.

## How to read the result

| Result | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Barcode | What will print on your cover. Check that the ISBN and price digits are right. |
| Valid ISBN | Your ISBN passed every check. It shows the ISBN-13 that's in the barcode, even if you typed an ISBN-10. |
| Price code | The five digits in the small barcode, and the price they stand for. |
| Size and Printed size | How large the barcode is compared with the standard, and how much room it takes on the cover, clear space included. |
| KDP box and IngramSpark area | Whether the barcode fits each printer's barcode area. If it doesn't, you'll see the largest size that does. |

Then download the file you need. If you change anything, press Make barcode again. The downloads stay off until you do, so you can't save an old barcode by mistake.

<Example title="A $14.99 paperback">

Type 978-0-306-40615-7 and 14.99. The price becomes the code 51499: a 5 for US dollars, then 1499 cents. The barcode comes out at 90%, or 1.99 × 1.10 in, which is the largest size that fits KDP's 2 × 1.2 in box. It doesn't fit IngramSpark's area at any size. The article below explains why.
