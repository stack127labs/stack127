## What to enter

- Printed pages. Use your final interior file's page count. Odd counts round up to the next even number. No final file yet? Use the [KDP page count estimator](/kdp-page-count-estimator).
- eBook file size. In MB, up to three decimals. At 35%, it sets the lowest price. At 70%, it doesn't.
- Desired royalty per copy. Leave it blank to get KDP's minimum. Enter an amount to get the lowest price that earns at least that much. Enter `0` for the lowest price that doesn't lose money, which matters for large eBooks at 70%.

## How to read the result

| Result | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| KDP minimum list price | The lowest price KDP accepts. For print, you earn little or nothing here. |
| Lowest price for your royalty goal | Shown instead when you enter a goal, with how far it sits above the KDP minimum. |
| Estimated royalty per sale | What you earn at that price, before your own costs. |
| Printing cost or delivery deduction | What KDP takes off first. The 35% eBook option has none. |

If you see **No price meets this royalty goal**, no price in the allowed range earns that much. The result shows the most you can earn in that range. Lower the goal, change the print setup, or switch the eBook option. At 70%, a 5 MB eBook earns at most $8.57, at $12.99.

<Example title="A $1.39 goal for a 300-page paperback">

A 6 × 9 in black and white paperback on white paper costs $4.60 to print. KDP's minimum is $9.20, where you earn $0.00. A $1.39 goal returns $9.99, $0.79 above the minimum, because the rate rises from 50% to 60% at that price.

</Example>
