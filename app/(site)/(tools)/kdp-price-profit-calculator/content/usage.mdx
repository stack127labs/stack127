## What to enter

- Shared list price. One price for every option, so the differences come only from the print setup.
- Printed pages. Give each option its own count. A bigger trim fits more words on a page, so the same book has fewer pages. Odd counts round up to the next even number. To estimate a count for a new trim, use the [KDP page count estimator](/kdp-page-count-estimator).
- Include my costs. Optional. Turn it on to see profit.
  - Planned copies sold per option. The same number for every option.
  - Other cost per sale. Costs per copy outside KDP. Don't include printing. KDP already takes it out of your royalty.
  - One-time cost. Costs you pay once for that edition, like a cover or formatting.
  - Enter `0` for a cost that doesn't apply. A blank box stops the comparison.

## How to read the result

The first line says how many options meet KDP's minimum at your price. An option below its minimum still shows its printing cost and minimum price, but no royalty or profit. Raise the shared price or change that option's setup.

<Example title="Paperback or hardcover at $19.99">

Both options are 300 pages, 6 × 9 in, black ink on white paper. The profit row assumes 100 copies, $1.00 other cost per sale, and $100.00 one-time cost for each.

| | Paperback | Hardcover |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Printing cost | $4.60 | $9.25 |
| KDP minimum price | $9.20 | $15.42 |
| Royalty per sale | $7.39 | $2.74 |
| Estimated profit | $539.00 | $74.00 |

</Example>
