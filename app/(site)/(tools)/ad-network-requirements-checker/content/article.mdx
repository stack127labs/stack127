## What do ad networks require?

This is what each network publishes, checked in October 2026. Every network also reviews your content and where your traffic comes from.

| Network | What it asks for |
| --- | --- |
| [Google AdSense](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724?hl=en) | No traffic minimum. Original content that follows its policies. |
| [Journey by Mediavine](https://www.mediavine.com/application-requirements/) | 1,000 Tier 1 sessions in 30 days, with its Grow plugin running for 30 days. |
| [Monumetric](https://www.monumetric.com/monumetric-program-overview/) | 10,000 pageviews a month for 3 months, half from the US, UK, Canada, and Australia. |
| [Raptive](https://help.raptive.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032840891-Who-is-eligible-for-Raptive) | 25,000 pageviews a month, a domain at least 6 months old, and 50% Tier 1 traffic (40% from 100,000 pageviews). |
| [SHE Media](https://www.shemedia.com/partner-network/faqs) | 50,000 pageviews a month, 30% from the US, and 90 days live. |
| [Mediavine](https://help.mediavine.com/programs-and-the-publisher-path-to-growth) | $5,000 in ad revenue in the previous calendar year. |
| [Newor Media](https://newormedia.com/programs) | Elevate has no minimum. Catapult starts at 60,000 US pageviews a month. |
| [Ezoic](https://support.ezoic.com/kb/article/getting-started-ezoics-requirements?id=getting-started-ezoics-requirements&lang=es-ES) | 250,000 monthly users. Its Incubator takes a few smaller sites each month. |
| [PubFuture](https://pubfuture.com/home) | 100,000 pageviews a month. |
| [Clickio](https://docs.clickio.com/books/clickio-entry-standards/page/clickio-entry-standards) | About 100,000 pageviews a month from the US, UK, Canada, Australia, or France, or over $500 a month in ad revenue. |
| [Playwire](https://www.playwire.com/faq) | 500,000 pageviews a month, though sites [usually apply from 100,000](https://www.playwire.com/qualification-criteria). |
| [Freestar](https://freestar.com/publisher-quality-requirements/) | Half your traffic from Tier 1 countries and half from organic sources. |
| [Publift](https://www.publift.com/blog/publift-requirements) | Over $2,000 a month in ad revenue. |
| [MGID](https://help.mgid.com/publishers-general-information) | 90,000 visitors a month and at least 10 current articles. |
| [Taboola](https://pubhelp.taboola.com/hc/en-us/articles/39280014062487-How-To-Get-New-Account) | No traffic minimum. |

The rest, with pay terms, are in the [ad networks directory](/ad-networks). To see which networks a site you know uses, try the [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).

## Why is a network I know missing?

- You already use it, or something bigger.
- It's built for another niche, such as gaming or developer sites.
- It wants much bigger sites. The closest ones show under `Grow toward`.
- It no longer takes new sites.

## How are networks ranked?

By how close your numbers are to each network's published bar. Your niche, Tier 1 share, and the sites we track in your niche adjust the order. It's a list to try, not a ranking by pay.
