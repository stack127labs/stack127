## What do I need to enter?

Only your niche and pageviews are required. Leave anything else you don't know blank.

- **Pageviews and visits.** From your analytics, for a typical month. Some networks count visits, so add them too if you have them.
- **Tier 1 share.** Your traffic from the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand together.
- **Current ad network.** The list skips it and anything smaller.
- **Ad revenue a month.** Ads only, in US dollars.

## How do I read the result?

**Good fit** means your numbers meet the network's published bar. **Worth a try** means it reviews sites like yours case by case. Open `Details` for the rule and its source, and `More options` for networks to add alongside or grow toward.
