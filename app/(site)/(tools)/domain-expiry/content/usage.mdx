## How to read the result

The countdown uses the registry's expiry date. When the registrar publishes a different date, both appear side by side. Check your registrar account before trusting either one.

Once a domain has expired, the large result names its stage, such as `Redemption period`, from the status codes the registry returns. It doesn't guess the stage by counting days from the expiry date.
