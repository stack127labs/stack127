## How to read the result

The bar splits the raw space of all drives into four parts: the estimated pool, Synology's system reserve, protection, and unused space. The band table shows how the calculator cuts the drives into layers, with the drives that reach each layer.

`Share configuration` makes a link with your drive sizes, so anyone you send it to can see them.

<Example title="Two 4 TB and two 8 TB drives">

SHR-1 gives an estimated pool of 15.96 TB, with 8 TB for protection and nothing unused. SHR-2 gives 7.96 TB, with 8 TB for protection and 8 TB unused.

</Example>
