## What to enter

Only the title is required. Add any of these to compare your title against:

- Book topic. A sentence on what the book covers.
- Intended reader. Who it's for, like `apartment gardeners`.
- Target phrases. Up to three, one per line, with at most 80 characters each.

Without those details, you still get counts, repetition prompts, and the supported title-rule checks.

## How to read the result

| Result | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Found in title or subtitle | Topic or reader words your title uses. Small words like "the" are skipped. |
| Only in supplied context | Topic or reader words it doesn't use. That's a cue to reread, not a word you must add. |
| Target phrase matches | Title, Subtitle, Both fields, or Not found. |
| Local counts | Words and characters per field, and the combined count out of 200. |
| Prompts | A word repeated twice in a row, a two-word phrase in both fields, HTML, a title with no letters or numbers, or 200 or more characters. |

<Example title="Balcony Herbs">

Title `Balcony Herbs`, subtitle `Growing Food in Small Spaces`, topic `Grow herbs on apartment balconies and small patios`, reader `Apartment gardeners`, target phrases `balcony herbs` and `urban gardening`.

- Topic words found: `herbs`, `small`. Only in the topic: `grow`, `apartment`, `balconies`, `patios`.
- Reader words found: none.
- `balcony herbs` is in the title. `urban gardening` isn't found.
- 42 of 200 characters, no prompts.
