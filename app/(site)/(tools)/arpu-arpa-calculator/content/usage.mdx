## Which average should you pick?

Pick by what you count: people or paying accounts.

| | ARPU | ARPA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue | Net revenue over the whole period | MRR at the month end |
| Count | Every active person, paying or not | Paying customer accounts |
| Fits | Apps with free users, ads, or one-off purchases | Subscriptions billed per account or team |

## What to enter

For ARPU, decide what "active" means, such as signed in or made a purchase, and use the same rule every time. There's no standard definition. Count each person once across the whole period.

For ARPA, use MRR, so a yearly plan counts as one twelfth of its price. The [MRR calculator](/mrr-calculator) works that out from your plans.

<Example title="One quarter, one month end">

ARPU: $75,000 of net revenue from April to June, and 5,000 active people in that time. ARPU is $15.00 for the quarter.

ARPA: $12,000 of MRR at the end of April, and 120 paying accounts. ARPA is $100.00 a month.

</Example>
