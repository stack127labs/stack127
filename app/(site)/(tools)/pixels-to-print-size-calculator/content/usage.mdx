## What to enter

- Pixel width and height. Whole numbers from 1 to 1,000,000, without commas. The gray numbers in empty boxes are examples, not your image's size.
- Print resolution (PPI). Starts at 300, the minimum KDP asks for in print books. Change it if your printer asks for something else. You can use up to three decimals.

The result is the whole image's width × height in all three units, rounded to four decimals. Compare it with the space you have in your layout.

<Example title="A 3,600 × 2,400 pixel photo">

At 300 PPI it prints at 12 × 8 in, or 30.48 × 20.32 cm. At 150 PPI the same file prints at 24 × 16 in.
