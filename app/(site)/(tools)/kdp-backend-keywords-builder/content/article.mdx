## How does the builder fill the seven fields?

It takes your phrases in order and puts each in the first field with room, counting one space between phrases. A short phrase lower on your list can still land in field 1. It doesn't score, reorder, or reword anything. To change a field, edit your list and build again.

## What does KDP say about keywords?

KDP allows up to seven keywords or short phrases. Its [keyword guidance](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201298500) and [metadata guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201097560) suggest phrases of about two to three words, in the order people search: "military science fiction", not "fiction science military". KDP also suggests looking at what Amazon's search box suggests as you type.

KDP asks you to leave out:

- Words already in your title, other book details, or categories.
- Quality claims like "best novel ever" and time-sensitive words like "new" or "on sale".
- Words that fit most books, like "book".
- Misspellings, and variants that differ only in spacing, punctuation, capitals, or plurals.
- Other authors' names, or brands you aren't allowed to use.
- Amazon program names like Kindle Unlimited, quotation marks, and HTML.

The builder catches duplicates with the same word sequence after lowercasing, replacing punctuation with spaces, and collapsing repeated spaces. Joined and separated forms such as `80GB` and `80 GB` aren't treated as duplicates. It also checks overlap with the details you enter, quotation marks, HTML, and a few program and promotional words. It doesn't catch plurals, misspellings, other authors' names, or phrases that don't fit your book.

## Is each KDP keyword field 50 characters?

KDP's help doesn't give a number. It says to watch the limit shown in the text field. Many guides say 50, so the builder starts there. Set the cap to what your form shows.

## Should a word appear in only one field?

KDP's cited help pages don't state a separate one-use-per-word rule. The builder points out repeats so you can review how you use the space.

## What the builder doesn't do

It doesn't suggest phrases, search Amazon, measure demand, or predict ranking or sales. It can't tell if you're allowed to use a name or brand.
