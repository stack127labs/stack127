## How to read the result

| Result | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Registered` | Someone holds the name. The registrar, registration date, and expiry date appear when published. |
| `No registration found` | The registry has no record of the name. It's promising, but you may still not be able to buy it. |
| `Cannot determine` | The ending has no public lookup service, or the registry didn't answer. This says nothing about the name. |

<Example title="quietlanternstudio, checked on 9 October 2026">

`quietlanternstudio.com` is `Registered`, since 6 October 2025. `quietlanternstudio.io` shows `No registration found`.

</Example>

To try one name across several endings at once, use the [multi-TLD availability checker](/multi-tld-availability).
