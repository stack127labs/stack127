## What to enter

Count accounts, not subscriptions. An account with three subscriptions counts once. Leave out free trials and free plans.

An opening account is lost when nothing it pays for is still active at the end. One that cancelled and came back before the end isn't lost. One that joined and left within the period stays out of both counts.

## How to read the result

<Example title="200 accounts at the start of the month">

9 of them had stopped paying by month end. Customer churn is 4.50%, and 191 opening accounts are still paying.

</Example>

The 191 isn't your customer count at month end. New accounts that joined during the month aren't in it.
