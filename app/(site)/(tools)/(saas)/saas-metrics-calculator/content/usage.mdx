## What to enter

Use monthly amounts for every MRR figure, so a yearly plan counts as one twelfth of its price. The [MRR calculator](/mrr-calculator) lists what counts.

The extra metrics come in groups. Fill in a whole group or leave it blank. A blank group shows `Unavailable` with the reason, which is better than a result built on a guessed zero.

- Account counts: count the same unit throughout. If a company is one paying account, count companies, not seats.
- Recognized recurring revenue is revenue for service delivered that month. It isn't MRR or cash collected.
- Net cash burn comes from your cash records: cash paid out minus cash received. The calculator doesn't work it out from the costs you entered.

## How to read the result

<Example title="April snapshot">

Starting MRR $10,000. New $2,000, expansion $1,000, contraction $500, churned $500. 100 starting accounts, 25 new, 5 churned. Recognized revenue $11,000, service costs $2,200, acquisition spend $4,000, net cash burn $12,000.

| Result | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Ending MRR | $12,000.00 |
| MRR growth | 20.00% |
| Net revenue retention | 100.00% |
| Gross revenue retention | 90.00% |
| Customer churn | 5.00% |
| Monthly ARPA | $100.00 |
| Gross margin | 80.00% |
| CAC | $160.00 |
| Simple LTV | $1,600.00 |
| CAC payback | 2.00 months |
| Burn multiple | 0.50× |

</Example>

Net revenue retention is 100% here even though 5 accounts left, because expansion made up for every lost dollar. Gross revenue retention leaves expansion out, so it shows the 10% that was lost.
