## What to enter

Net cash burn is the cash you spent running the business minus the cash customers paid you. Leave out money from investors and loans. If customers paid you more than you spent, enter a negative amount.

Net new ARR is ARR at the end of the period minus ARR at the start. ARR is already a yearly rate, so don't annualize the change, and don't multiply a quarter's burn by four.

If you track MRR, net new ARR is the change in MRR × 12. MRR going from $40,000 to $42,000 is $24,000 of net new ARR.

## How to read the result

<Example title="One quarter">

You burned $36,000. ARR grew from $480,000 to $504,000, so net new ARR is $24,000.

Burn multiple is 1.50×. You spent $1.50 of cash for each $1 of new ARR.

</Example>

If you didn't burn cash, or ARR didn't grow, there's no multiple to show. The result says which one happened.
