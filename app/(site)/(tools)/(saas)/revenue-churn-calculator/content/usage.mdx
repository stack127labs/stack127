## What to enter

Opening MRR comes from customers paying when the month began. Leave out anyone who first paid during the month.

Compare each of those customers at the start and end of the month. If they pay nothing at month end, their whole opening MRR is canceled. If they still pay, but less, only the drop counts as reduced. If they cancelled and came back within the month, use where they ended.

Count each starting dollar once. A customer who drops from $100 to $80, goes back to $100, and ends at $80 lost $20, not $40. A billing report that adds up every downgrade can show more, sometimes more than your whole opening MRR.

## How to read the result

<Example title="$8,000 opening MRR">

Canceled opening MRR is $360. Reduced opening MRR is $120.

Gross revenue churn is 6.00%. Opening MRR still present is $7,520.00.

</Example>

Divide `Opening MRR still present` by opening MRR and you get gross revenue retention, 94% here.
