## What to enter

Opening cohort MRR is the MRR from customers paying just before the month's first day. Every other field covers only those customers. Anyone who first paid during the month stays out.

Count each loss once. A customer who pays less but stays is contraction. A customer who cancels everything is churn.

Reactivation here is narrow. It's for someone in the opening group who cancelled and came back in the same month. Enter their loss as churned MRR and their return as reactivation. A former customer who had already left before the month began isn't in this group.

## How to read the result

<Example title="$20,000 opening MRR">

Expansion $1,500. Contraction $400. Churned $1,100, of which $200 came back as reactivation.

Ending cohort MRR is $20,200. NRR is 101.00%.

</Example>

The breakdown table walks from opening to ending cohort MRR. In the example, NRR is above 100% even though $1,100 churned, because expansion more than made up for it.
