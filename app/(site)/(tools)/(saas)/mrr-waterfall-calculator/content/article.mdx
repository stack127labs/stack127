## What do the MRR movements mean?

Each movement is a change in MRR from one kind of customer event. These are the common meanings. If your billing tool defines them differently, use its numbers.

| Movement | What it records |
| --- | --- |
| New MRR | A customer's first paid subscription, such as a trial user who starts paying |
| Expansion MRR | An existing customer pays more, through an upgrade or more seats |
| Reactivation MRR | A former customer starts paying again |
| Contraction MRR | A customer pays less but stays, through a downgrade or fewer seats |
| Churned MRR | A customer stops paying entirely, through a cancellation or failed renewal |

[ChartMogul's movement guide](https://help.chartmogul.com/article/163-understanding-mrr-movements) uses these five, plus a neutral movement for changes that leave MRR the same. It also counts a new discount as contraction.

## How do you calculate ending MRR?

Add the gains to starting MRR and subtract the losses.

<Formula title="Ending MRR">

Ending MRR = starting MRR + new + expansion + reactivation - contraction - churned

Net new MRR = ending MRR - starting MRR

</Formula>

The order of the bars is fixed: gains first, then losses. It isn't the order things happened during the month, and the order doesn't change the ending balance.

Ending MRR is a run rate on the last day of the month, not the revenue you earned during it. The [MRR calculator](/mrr-calculator) explains what counts toward MRR.

## Why doesn't my report's ending MRR match?

Usually because the figures follow different rules. Check that every amount covers the same month, currency, and definition of MRR.

Timing matters too. [ChartMogul's churn recognition setting](https://help.chartmogul.com/article/242-churn-recognition) can record a cancellation when it's made, when it's scheduled, or when the paid period ends. Each choice can move churn into a different month.

## What can you work out from these movements?

The same numbers feed most monthly SaaS metrics.

- The [net revenue retention calculator](/net-revenue-retention-calculator) shows how much starting MRR your existing customers kept, with expansion counted.
- The [revenue churn calculator](/revenue-churn-calculator) shows the share of starting MRR lost to contraction and churn.
- The [SaaS metrics calculator](/saas-metrics-calculator) puts growth and retention for the month on one page.
