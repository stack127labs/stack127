## What to enter

Starting MRR is the balance just before the month began. It is usually last month's ending MRR.

Type each change as a positive amount. The calculator adds new, expansion, and reactivation MRR, and subtracts contraction and churned MRR.

Use monthly amounts for every change. A new customer on a $1,200 yearly plan adds $100 to new MRR, not $1,200.

## How to read the result

<Example title="A month with all five changes">

Start at $10,000. Add $1,200 new, $400 expansion, and $150 reactivation. Subtract $250 contraction and $500 churned.

Ending MRR is $11,000.00. Net new MRR is +$1,000.00.

</Example>

The table under the chart gives the balance after each step. If your billing report shows its own ending MRR, compare it with this one. A gap usually means a change is missing or counted twice.
