## Why doesn't a shortcut work for me?

Usually something else has claimed the keys first. Check these in order:

- You or an extension changed it. Apps such as VS Code and 1Password let you remap shortcuts, and your keys replace the defaults listed here.
- Your keyboard isn't a US layout. Many references, including Microsoft's for Excel and VS Code, assume one, so keys such as `/` and `[` can sit elsewhere.
- The operating system took it. Your system can reserve a combination before the app sees it. On a Mac, the top row may control brightness and volume until you hold Fn.
- Your app version differs. The lists follow current desktop releases.
