## Which launch directories give a dofollow link?

Choose Dofollow under Link type to see them. Each result comes from one published listing we opened, or from the plan the site's terms describe, so free and paid listings on the same site can differ.

[Google says](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links) links marked nofollow, ugc, or sponsored will generally not be followed. A dofollow link still doesn't promise indexing, rankings, or traffic.

<Terms>
<Term name="Dofollow">The sampled link had no nofollow, ugc, or sponsored value, or the site's terms promise that.</Term>
<Term name="Nofollow">The link asks search engines not to follow it.</Term>
<Term name="UGC">The link is marked as user-generated content.</Term>
<Term name="Sponsored">The link is marked as paid.</Term>
<Term name="Mixed">The link carried more than one value, such as ugc and nofollow.</Term>
<Term name="Varies">Free, paid, or top-ranked listings get different links. The details say which.</Term>
<Term name="No link">The sampled listing doesn't link to the maker's own site.</Term>
</Terms>
