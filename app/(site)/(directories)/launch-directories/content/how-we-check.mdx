## How do we check each place?

We read each site's own submission page, pricing, and rules, then open one of its published listings to see what link it gives the maker. Each place's page links every source we used, with the date we checked it. Some sources are older than the record date, which is our latest check of anything on that place.

Card prices show the lowest price we found, and "From" means higher plans exist. Domain ratings are Domain Rating by [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/), and popularity comes from the Tranco list of the most visited sites. Prices, review times, and link rules change, so check the site's own page before you pay.
