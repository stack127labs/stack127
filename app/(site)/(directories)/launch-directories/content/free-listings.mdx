## What does a free listing ask for?

Some free plans ask for something in return, such as a backlink to the directory, proof that you own the domain, or votes and comments on other launches. Where we checked the free flow, the card lists these under Free plan.

The Free without required backlink filter shows only free places where we checked the free flow and it doesn't ask for a backlink or badge. Places we haven't checked yet stay out of it, even if their price looks free. A place can still change its rules, so read the card before you submit.
