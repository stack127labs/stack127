## Which launch sites come first?

Six well-known launch sites come first: Product Hunt, BetaList, DevHunt, Peerlist Launchpad, AlternativeTo, and SaaSHub. That's our pick of where most makers start, not a ranking by traffic. After them:

1. Places with a checked submission page come before places without one.
2. Launch boards and beta sites come next, then startup and software listings, then AI, developer, design, and regional directories.
3. Within each group, free options come first, then dofollow links.
