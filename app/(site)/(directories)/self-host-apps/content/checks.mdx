## How are apps checked?

Each app is checked against its official website, repository, docs, and image pages, and we write its summary ourselves. Facts we couldn't confirm show as unknown or "not verified" instead of a guess. The list includes open source, source-available, and closed-source apps, so check the license before you build on one. [Software licenses for self-hosting](/software-licenses-for-self-hosting) explains what each license lets you do.

The full dataset is on [GitHub](https://github.com/stack127labs/stack127-datasets/tree/main/self-host-apps).
