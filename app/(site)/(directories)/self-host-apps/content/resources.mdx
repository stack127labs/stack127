## How much RAM and storage does a self-hosted app need?

Each app shows our estimate for a typical small setup. We base it on the publisher's guidance when there is some. Real use depends on your users and data, so treat it as a starting point.

The RAM and storage filters use the low end of each estimate, and each choice includes the smaller ones. "Up to 2GB" shows every app that can start in 2 GB or less.

| Storage | Low end of the estimate |
| --- | --- |
| Tiny | Under 1 GB |
| Low | 1 to 10 GB |
| Medium | 10 to 100 GB |
| High | 100 GB or more |

To check whether several apps fit on one server, use the [self-hosted app resource planner](/self-hosted-app-resource-planner).
