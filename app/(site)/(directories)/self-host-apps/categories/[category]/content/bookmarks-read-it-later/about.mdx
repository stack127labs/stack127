## How do you choose a bookmark or reading app?

Decide first what you want to keep: links, articles to read later, feeds, or full copies of pages. Each is a different job, and only some apps do two.

- **Saving from anywhere.** You'll save most links from a browser or a phone. Check the app's links for a browser extension and a phone app or share option.
- **Import.** If you're moving from another service, look for import of its export file. Feed readers use OPML files for this.
- **Storage.** Archivers that keep full pages or screenshots grow fast. Bookmark-only apps stay small. The Storage filter shows the difference.
- **RAM.** Some bookmark apps add AI tagging or run a headless browser to grab pages, which takes more memory. Use the RAM filter on a small server.

## What should you back up?

The database holds your links, tags, and read status. Back it up with any saved page archive. A feed reader checks feeds on a timer, so it needs to stay running to catch every post.
