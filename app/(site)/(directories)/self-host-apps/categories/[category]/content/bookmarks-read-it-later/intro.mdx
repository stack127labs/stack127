Want your saved links and feeds on your own server, not in Pocket or Feedly? This list has bookmark managers such as Karakeep, Linkwarden, and linkding, read-it-later apps such as Wallabag, RSS readers such as FreshRSS and Miniflux, and web archivers such as ArchiveBox. Each row shows the license, whether the app is still maintained, Docker support, and the minimum RAM when we know it.
