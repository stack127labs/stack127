## How do you choose a music server?

Start with your library and the apps you want to listen in.

- **Client apps.** Many music servers support the Subsonic API, which lets you pick from lots of phone and desktop players. Check for it if you want a choice.
- **Formats.** Check support for your files, such as FLAC or Opus, and whether it can convert to a smaller format on slow connections.
- **Tags.** A library with clean tags looks better in every server. A tagger is worth running before you import.
- **Resources.** Use the RAM filter if you want the server on a small machine like a Raspberry Pi.

## What should you back up?

Your music files are the big part, so plan storage first. Back up the server's database too, since it holds playlists, ratings, and play counts. To listen away from home, use HTTPS or a VPN.
