## How do you choose a budgeting or finance app?

Start with how your transactions get in. Typing each one by hand works for some people and is why others give up.

- **Bank import.** Check whether it imports CSV, OFX, or QIF files from your bank, or connects to a bank sync service. Bank sync is often paid or limited to some countries.
- **Budget style.** Some apps use envelope budgeting, where you plan every bit of money before you spend it. Others track spending after it happens.
- **Invoicing.** If you bill clients, check tax, currency, and payment support for where you work.
- **Crypto nodes** are a different kind of app. A full node needs a lot of disk and stays online all the time, so check the Storage filter.

## What should you back up?

Your financial history is hard to rebuild. Back up the database often, and keep the app behind a login or a VPN rather than open to the internet.
