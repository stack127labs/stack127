## How do you choose a search app?

Decide what you're searching: the web, your site's content, or your own files.

- **Metasearch engines** send your query to other search engines and strip the tracking. If many people share one, those engines may start blocking it.
- **Search servers** index your data for an app or site. Check for client libraries in your language and for typo tolerance.
- **RAM.** Many search engines keep their index in memory, so RAM grows with your data. Treat the RAM estimate as a minimum.
- **AI search** over your files needs a model, which adds GPU or API costs.

## What should you plan for when you run one?

An index can be rebuilt from your data, so back up the data first. Keep search servers off the public internet, and require API keys for every app that queries them.
