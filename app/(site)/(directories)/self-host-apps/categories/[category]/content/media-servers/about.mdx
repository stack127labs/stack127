## How do you choose a media server?

Start with where you'll watch. Then check:

- **Client apps.** Check for apps on your TV, phone, and streaming stick. They decide more of your daily experience than the server does.
- **Transcoding.** When a device can't play a file, the server converts it on the fly. A GPU or a CPU with built-in video encoding makes that much lighter. Some servers keep hardware transcoding behind a paid plan.
- **License.** Some well-known media servers are closed source with paid tiers. The Source model filter shows which.
- **Watching away from home.** Check whether it streams through the publisher's relay or needs your own remote setup.

## What should you plan for?

Media takes the most space, so plan your disks first. Back up the server's database, which holds watch history and metadata, apart from the media files. Collection managers work with download clients and indexers, so check what each expects before you build the stack.
