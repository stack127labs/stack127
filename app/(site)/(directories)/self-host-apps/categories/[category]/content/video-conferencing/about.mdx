## How do you choose a video app?

Decide whether you need meetings, live streams to an audience, or a video platform to build your own app on.

- **Bandwidth.** Video uses your server's upload speed, and group calls multiply it. A home connection may not be enough for larger calls.
- **Network setup.** Video often needs UDP ports and a TURN server, a relay that lets people behind strict firewalls join.
- **Joining.** Check whether guests can join from a browser or need to install an app.
- **Recording.** Recording and streaming often need an extra service with more CPU and RAM.

## What should you plan for when you run one?

These apps have to be reachable from the internet, so a VPS with good bandwidth is often easier than a home server. Recordings take space, so check the Storage filter.
