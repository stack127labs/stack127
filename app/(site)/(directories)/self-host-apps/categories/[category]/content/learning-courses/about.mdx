## How do you choose a course or learning app?

Decide who's learning: a class, paying customers, or just you.

- **Course platforms** handle lessons, quizzes, and grades. The big ones take real setup and memory, so check the RAM filter first.
- **Standards.** If you reuse course content from other systems, check for SCORM or LTI support.
- **Selling courses.** Check that it takes payments, or plan to add them yourself.
- **Personal tools,** such as flashcard sync or language readers, are a different job. They have one user and need far less setup.

## What should you plan for when you run one?

Student work, grades, and videos need regular backups. Video takes the most space, so check the Storage filter or host videos somewhere else. A course site faces the internet, so keep it updated.
