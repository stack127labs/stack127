## How do you choose a password manager to self-host?

Once you self-host it, keeping your passwords safe is your job. Check:

- **Clients.** Check for browser extensions and phone apps. Some servers work with another project's official apps.
- **Encryption.** Look for end-to-end encryption, where the server never sees your vault unlocked.
- **Sharing.** For a family or team, check how shared vaults and permissions work.
- **Health.** Security fixes matter more here than anywhere. Prefer apps the Health filter marks Active.

Secrets managers are for apps and servers, not people. For those, check API access and audit logs instead.

## What should you plan for when you run one?

If the server dies, you'll need your passwords to fix it. Keep an offline export or a copy of the vault on your devices. Back up the database often, test a restore, and update quickly when a security fix comes out.
