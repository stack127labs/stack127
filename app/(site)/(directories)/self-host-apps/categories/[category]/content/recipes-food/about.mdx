## How do you choose a recipe or pantry app?

Start with what you'll actually do in the kitchen.

- **Saving recipes.** Check whether it can pull a recipe from a website link. That saves the most typing.
- **Meal plans and shopping lists.** Check that a meal plan turns into a shopping list you can use on your phone.
- **Pantry tracking** only works if adding items is quick, such as by scanning barcodes. Otherwise you'll stop updating it.
- **Household use.** Check for shared lists and separate accounts.

## What should you plan for when you run one?

Back up the database and recipe photos. Use the RAM filter to find one that fits a small home server. If you want the shopping list in the store, you'll need a way in from outside, such as a VPN.
