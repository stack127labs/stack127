## How do you choose a remote access app?

Decide what you need to reach: a whole network, a few web apps, a terminal, or a full desktop.

- **Mesh VPNs** connect your devices as if they were on one network. Check whether the coordination server runs on your machine or the publisher's.
- **Access gateways** put logins and logs in front of servers. For a team, check for single sign-on and session recording.
- **Remote desktop** needs low lag. Check for client apps on your devices and support for GPU encoding.
- **License.** Some tools here keep features in a paid edition. Check the Source model filter.

## What should you plan for when you run one?

These apps are the way into your network, so a hole in them is a hole in everything. Update quickly, use two-factor logins, and limit what each user can reach. Keep a second way in for when the remote tool breaks.
