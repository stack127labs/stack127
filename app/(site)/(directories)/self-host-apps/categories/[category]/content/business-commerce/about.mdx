## How do you choose a business app?

Pick by the job you need done first, such as selling, invoicing, support, or customer records. Suites that try to do everything take the longest to set up.

- **License and editions.** Some ERP and CRM projects sell a paid edition next to a free one. Check the License and Source model filters, then read what the free edition leaves out.
- **Health.** You'll run this for years, so the Health filter matters more here than for most apps.
- **Resources.** ERP suites usually want more RAM than a small VPS has. Use the RAM filter before you commit.
- **Payments and tax.** For a shop, check that the app supports your payment provider and your country's tax rules. That's on the app's own site, not the card.

## What should you plan for before you run one?

Orders, invoices, and customer details are data you can't lose. Plan backups of the database and uploaded files from day one, and keep the app updated. Customer data can also bring privacy duties in the places you sell.
