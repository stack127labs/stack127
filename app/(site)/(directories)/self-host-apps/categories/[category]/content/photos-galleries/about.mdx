## How do you choose a photo app?

Decide whether you want automatic backup from your phone, a gallery to show your work, or both.

- **Phone backup.** Check the app's links for a phone app that uploads in the background.
- **Your existing folders.** Some apps read your photo folders as they are. Others import copies into their own structure.
- **Search and faces.** Smart search and face grouping use machine learning, which needs more RAM and CPU. Compare with the RAM filter.
- **Formats.** Check support for HEIC, RAW, and video.

## What should you plan for when you run one?

Photo libraries grow every year, so plan storage first. The app isn't a backup on its own. Keep the original files in a separate backup with older versions, along with the app's database.
