Want a Google Photos or iCloud alternative for your own pictures? This list has photo backup and libraries such as Immich, PhotoPrism, and Ente, galleries such as Lychee and Piwigo, photo sharing such as PixelFed, and tools such as icloudpd to pull photos out of iCloud. Each row shows the license, whether the app is still maintained, Docker support, and the minimum RAM when we know it.
