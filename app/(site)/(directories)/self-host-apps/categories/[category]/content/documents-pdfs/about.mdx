## How do you choose a document or PDF app?

Decide whether you need to store documents, edit them, or change them, such as merging or compressing PDFs.

- **Document archives** scan, read, and tag your paper. Check OCR (text recognition) support for your languages and whether it can watch a folder for new scans.
- **PDF tools** do the work either in your browser or on the server. If privacy is why you self-host, check which one it is.
- **Office suites** usually plug into a file server rather than run on their own. Check that they work with the one you use.
- **E-signature.** Check what audit trail it keeps, and whether its signatures meet the rules where you sign.

## What should you back up?

An archive often becomes the only copy of your paperwork. Back up the original files, the database, and the search index together. Use the Storage filter to plan for years of scans, and keep the app off the public internet unless you need to share from it.
