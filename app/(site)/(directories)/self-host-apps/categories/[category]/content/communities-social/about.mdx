## How do you choose a forum or social server?

Decide whether you want a standalone community or one that joins the Fediverse, the network of servers whose users can follow each other.

- **Federation.** A Fediverse server connects your members to people elsewhere. It also pulls in outside posts that you'll need to moderate.
- **Moderation tools.** Check for reporting, blocklists, and spam controls before you open signups.
- **Email.** Forums send signup and notification emails, so you'll need a working mail sender.
- **Storage.** Fediverse servers keep copies of remote media and need regular cleanup. The Storage filter shows which expect a lot of space.

## What should you plan for when you run one?

A public community has to be reachable from the internet, so plan for a domain, HTTPS, and regular updates. Back up the database and uploads. Rules on user content and personal data vary by country, so check yours before you open to the public.
