## How do you choose a forms or events app?

Decide what you're collecting: survey answers, signups, meeting times, or ticket payments.

- **Questions and logic.** Check for the field types and branching you need, and whether you can embed forms on your own site.
- **Payments.** For paid events, check which payment providers it supports and how it handles refunds and tax.
- **Spam.** Public forms attract bots. Look for a captcha or rate limiting.
- **Getting answers out.** Check that you can export responses to CSV or send them to other apps with webhooks.

## What should you plan for when you run one?

People fill in forms from anywhere, so the app has to face the internet. Keep it updated. Responses often include names and emails, so back up the database and check the privacy rules for the people you collect from.
