## How do you choose a health or fitness app?

Health data is personal, and keeping it private is often the reason to self-host it. Check:

- **Phone use.** You'll log workouts and meals on your phone. Check the official links for a phone app or a web app that works well on a small screen.
- **Imports.** Look for import from the app or device you use now, such as a CSV export or a watch sync.
- **Family use.** Check for separate accounts for each person.
- **Medical use.** Glucose and health record tools usually come with warnings about medical use. Read them before you rely on one for care decisions.

## What should you back up?

Back up the database, and keep the app behind a login or a VPN. If you open it to the internet for a phone app, use HTTPS and strong passwords.
