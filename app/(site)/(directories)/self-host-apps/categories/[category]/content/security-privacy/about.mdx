## How do you choose a security or privacy app?

This category mixes tools that protect you, test your systems, and watch for problems. Name the job first, then check:

- **Health.** A security tool that isn't maintained can become your weak spot. Prefer apps the Health filter marks Active.
- **Where your data goes.** Some blockers and private front ends still send your queries to an outside service. Read how each one handles them.
- **Permission.** Point scanners only at systems you own or have permission to test.
- **Resources.** Camera recorders and scanners can be heavy. Treat the RAM estimate as a minimum.

## What should you plan for when you run one?

Many of these apps sit in front of everything else, so a failure can cut off your network. Keep a fallback, back up the config, and update quickly. Don't expose admin pages to the internet.
