## How do we price analytics?

Each tool is priced for one site and one user at 10,000, 100,000, and 1 million pageviews a month. Tools that bill by something else, such as revenue or tracked users, are marked not comparable, and their panel says how they bill.

"Cookieless" means the tool says it doesn't set cookies by default. Whether you need a consent banner also depends on your country and what else your site runs.
