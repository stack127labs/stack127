## How do we price email apps?

Newsletter and marketing apps are priced for 1,000, 5,000, 10,000, and 50,000 subscribers, with four campaigns a month to everyone. At 10,000 subscribers that's 40,000 emails.

App email services are priced for 3,000, 10,000, 50,000, and 100,000 emails a month sent from your code. Some of them don't allow newsletters, so the card says whether you can send one.
