## How do we price monitoring?

Error trackers are priced for 5,000, 50,000, and 500,000 error reports a month, for one builder with three apps and 30 days of detail. Uptime tools are priced for 5, 25, and 100 endpoints checked every 60 seconds from one location, with email alerts.

For tools you can run yourself, the card shows the date of the last release, so you can see whether it's still maintained.
