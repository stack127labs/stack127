## How do we price databases?

Each product is priced for the same usage, in its own job:

- **Small SQL app:** 1 GB stored, 100,000 reads, and 20,000 writes a month. The idle project keeps the same data with no traffic.
- **File storage:** 100 GB stored, 100,000 writes, 1 million reads, and 100 GB out a month.
- **Jobs:** 10,000 jobs a month, 1 second each.

Some free tiers pause or delete idle projects. Open a product to see its rules, its regions, and how you get your data out.
