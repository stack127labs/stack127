## How do we price hosting?

Every host is priced for the same three workloads:

- **Static site:** 1 million requests and 100 GB of data out a month.
- **Small app API:** 1 million requests and 100 GB out a month.
- **App + Postgres:** the small app plus a database with 1 GiB of memory and 10 GB of storage.

Self-hosted panels are free software, so you also pay for the server they run on. Open a host to see what's included, what costs extra, and whether a free plan allows commercial use.
