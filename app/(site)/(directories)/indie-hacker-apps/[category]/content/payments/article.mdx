## How do we compare payment fees?

Each card shows the standard fee for one domestic online card payment, for a seller based in the country you picked. It's the provider's published rate, in its own currency, before any volume discount.

A merchant of record sells to your customer for you and handles sales tax, so its fee is higher. A gateway charges less, but you're the seller and you handle tax and disputes.

When a provider publishes one global rate, we use it for every country and mark it unclear. When sellers in a country can't sign up, the card says so.
