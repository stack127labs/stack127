## How are these apps checked?

We read each app's own pricing, docs, terms, and status pages, and record every fact with its source link and the date we checked it. When a vendor doesn't publish something, the app says "vendor doesn't say" instead of a guess.

Prices are monthly list prices before tax, with no launch discounts, in the vendor's own currency. We work them out at the same usage for every app in a category, and the math is one click away in each app's panel.

## What counts as a change?

The list above shows sales, closures, security incidents, regulator actions, and licence changes, with the date each one happened. Each one also shows as a badge on the app's card.
