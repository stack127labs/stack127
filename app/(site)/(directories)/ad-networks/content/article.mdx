Most networks publish a list of the publishers they work with. We start with those lists, then add sites from Chrome's public traffic data and other manual sources. A site counts for a network only if the network appears in the site's ads.txt file (a public list of who's allowed to sell its ads) or in the ad code running on its pages. Networks are ranked by how many sites we found them on. "Top" just means the most sites. It isn't our opinion of which network is best.

Open any ad network above to see the sites it works with, its joining requirements, and stats like top niches, average domain rating, average site age, and the technologies those sites use.

You can also check out our [ad network stats page](/ad-networks/stats) and [niche pages](/ad-networks/niches) to see how the networks compare and which might suit you best.
