## Who is The Moneytizer a fit for?

Sites on their own domain with strong organic traffic from the US, UK, and Canada, that want a managed service without exclusivity. Sites that mostly live on paid or social referral traffic may not count enough organic visitors.

## Why does the visitor type matter?

The rule counts organic unique visitors only. A site with plenty of pageviews can still fall short if much of its traffic is paid, or comes from outside the three countries.

## Is the low threshold a reason to pick it?

It helps cash flow, since a small balance still gets paid. It isn't a joining rule.
