## Who is The Moneytizer a fit for?

Publishers who want a managed header bidding service with a low payment minimum of €50 or $50. That suits a small site that would wait months to reach a $100 minimum.

## When does The Moneytizer pay you?

A couple of months after the month you earn it. The payment simulator on its site shows how the timing works.

## How do you join The Moneytizer?

Sign up on its site. The Moneytizer doesn't publish entry rules, so its team decides.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share, how you're paid, and whether The Moneytizer must be your only ad partner. Ask how much notice you need to leave. None of these are in its payment information.

## How can you check if a site uses The Moneytizer?

Look for The Moneytizer in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
