## Who is The Moneytizer a fit for?

Publishers who want a managed header bidding service with a low payment minimum of €50 or $50. That suits a small site that would wait months to reach a $100 minimum.

## When does The Moneytizer pay you?

About two months after your invoice. The Moneytizer creates the invoice once your earnings reach the minimum. The payment simulator on its site shows how the timing works.

## How do you join The Moneytizer?

Sign up on its site and share your traffic stats. The Moneytizer doesn't publish a traffic minimum. Its team reviews each site for advertiser fit and content.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Whether The Moneytizer must be your only ad partner, and how much notice you need to leave. Neither is in its terms or payment information.

## How can you check if a site uses The Moneytizer?

Look for The Moneytizer in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
