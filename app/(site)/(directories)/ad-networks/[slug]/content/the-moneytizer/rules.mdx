## What rules does The Moneytizer set?

The Moneytizer doesn't need to be your only ad partner. Its terms bar content that is racist, xenophobic, pedophilic, against human dignity, or illegal. You can remove its tags at any time if they clash with your editorial policy, and you choose which ad formats appear.

The terms set no fixed contract length. If you joined after March 11, 2024, you must give 30 days' notice to leave, or your last period's earnings may not be paid as agreed. The Moneytizer can close your account at any time if it earns $5 a month or less, or remove it if you break the terms. Its terms give no notice period for either.
