## What rules does The Moneytizer set?

The [terms](https://www.themoneytizer.com/cgu) are non-exclusive, so other ad partners can stay. You choose which formats appear, and you can remove its tags if they clash with your editorial policy, explaining why in writing.

There's no fixed contract length. If you registered after March 11, 2024, give 30 days' notice to leave, or recent earnings may not be paid. The Moneytizer can close an account earning $5 a month or less at any time. Racist, sexual, xenophobic, or illegal content isn't allowed.
