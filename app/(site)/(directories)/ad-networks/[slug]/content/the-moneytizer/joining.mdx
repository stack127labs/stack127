The [US validation criteria](https://us.themoneytizer.com/faq/validation-criteria) count unique visitors from organic traffic in the 30 days before you register, and only those from the US, UK, and Canada. That's not pageviews, sessions, or all visitors, so check your analytics by channel and country first. The page doesn't give an effective date, and other regional sites may differ.

You must own the domain and have server access. Subdomains, subpages, and sites on Wix or Jimdo aren't accepted, and neither are streaming, illegal downloads, sexual or violent content, dating, crypto, social, or gambling sites. Register on the [US site](https://us.themoneytizer.com/), add your website, and wait for the quality review. Then add ads.txt and the ad tags, plus a consent banner for EU visitors.
