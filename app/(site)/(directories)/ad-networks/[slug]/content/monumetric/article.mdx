## Is Monumetric a fit for a new blog?

Yes, once your traffic has held steady for three months, most readers are in the four countries, and you're on WordPress or Blogger. Budget for the implementation fee, and make sure your layout has room for six ads.

## What does one blogger's start look like?

The personal finance blog Perfection Hangover [reported](https://perfectionhangover.com/5-financial-adjustments-to-make-if-youve-been-laid-off-from-work/) reaching 10,000 monthly pageviews in its fourth month but applying around month 12. Its first Monumetric month, February 2019, earned $436. That's one older report, not a forecast.

## Who won't Monumetric take?

Sites with most readers outside the US, UK, Canada, and Australia, sites not in English, and sites with porn, drugs, hate speech, or copied content.

## How does Monumetric compare with Raptive and Journey?

[Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) has a lower traffic bar, but asks you to run its Grow plugin. [Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) asks for more pageviews but publishes its share and pays sooner. Monumetric doesn't publish its share.
