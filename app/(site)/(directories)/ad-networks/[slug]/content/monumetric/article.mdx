## Is Monumetric a fit for a new blog?

It can be, if you write in English and your readers are mainly in the US, UK, Canada, or Australia. Propel is its program for smaller sites, and its pageview bar is lower than most managed networks that publish one.

## Who won't Monumetric take?

Sites with most readers outside those four countries, sites not in English, and sites with porn, drugs, hate speech, or copied content.

## When does Monumetric pay you?

A couple of months after you earn it, early in the month. Small balances carry over until they pass the minimum.

## How does Monumetric compare with Raptive and Journey?

[Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) has a lower bar, just over 1,000 monthly sessions. [Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) asks for more pageviews but publishes a 75% share and pays on Net 45. Monumetric doesn't publish its share.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share, whether Monumetric must be your only ad partner, and how much notice you need to leave. None of these are on its public pages.

## How can you check if a site uses Monumetric?

Look for Monumetric in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
