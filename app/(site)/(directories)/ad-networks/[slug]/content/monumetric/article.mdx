## Is Monumetric a fit for a new blog?

It can be, if you write in English and your readers are mainly in the US, UK, Canada, or Australia. Propel is its program for smaller sites.

## When does Monumetric pay you?

A couple of months after you earn it, early in the month. Small balances carry over until they pass the minimum.

## How can you check if a site uses Monumetric?

Look for Monumetric in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Monumetric.
