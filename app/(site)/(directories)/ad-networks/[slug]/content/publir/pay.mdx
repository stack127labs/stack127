## How does Publir pay?

What you keep depends on the service. Pro charges a fee. Under the service agreement, you keep most of your programmatic and subscription revenue, custom ad revenue is split evenly, and Publir takes a fee on crowdfunding. There's no single share for every publisher.

Programmatic and custom ad earnings are paid at least monthly, on Net 60. Subscription revenue is paid at least monthly.

The payment minimum, methods, and currency are not public.
