## What does Publir do for you?

Publir offers ad optimization, ad block recovery, subscriptions, crowdfunding, and QuickShop. Pro support is by email during business hours. Enterprise adds real-time analytics and phone support.

The service agreement says onboarding can take ten business days. Support runs weekdays from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern, with a 12-hour reply target outside those hours.
