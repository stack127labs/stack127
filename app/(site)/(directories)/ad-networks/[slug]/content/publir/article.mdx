## Which Publir model fits you?

Pro suits a site that wants Publir to run its ads for a fee. Custom ad deals under the service agreement work on a revenue split instead. Ask which model you'd be on before you sign.

## Can Publir change its fees?

Yes. The service agreement allows Publir to change its percentages for costs and fees.

## What else does Publir offer besides ads?

Ad block recovery, subscriptions, crowdfunding, and QuickShop. These can add income beyond ad slots.

## How can you check if a site uses Publir?

Look for Publir in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Publir.
