## Who is Publir a fit for?

Sites of any size, since it has no traffic or revenue minimum. It suits publishers who want more than ad slots, such as ad block recovery, subscriptions, or crowdfunding.

## Which Publir model fits you?

Pro suits a site that wants Publir to run its ads for a fee. Custom ad deals under the service agreement work on a revenue split instead. Ask which model you'd be on before you sign.

## Can Publir change its fees?

Yes. The service agreement allows Publir to change its percentages for costs and fees. Ask how you'd be told about a change.

## How long does it take to start?

The service agreement says onboarding may take ten business days. Plan your switch around that.

## What should you ask before you sign?

The payment minimum and how you're paid. Neither is public.

## How can you check if a site uses Publir?

Look for Publir in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
