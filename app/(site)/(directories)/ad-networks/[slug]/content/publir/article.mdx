## Who is Publir a fit for?

Publications that want readers to pay as well as see ads, such as a news site adding subscriptions or a blog adding crowdfunding. If you only want a bigger ad check, compare managed ad networks first.

## Can you use Publir with another ad network?

For subscriptions, ad block recovery, and crowdfunding, yes. Those can sit on top of your current ads. Its programmatic service serves 100% of the ads it covers, so that part replaces your other ad network.

## What should you ask before you sign?

1. Which fees come out before your share?
2. What's the payment minimum, and how are you paid?
3. Is the 2024 agreement still the current one?
