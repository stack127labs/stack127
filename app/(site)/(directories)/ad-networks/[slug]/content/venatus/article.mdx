## Who is Venatus for?

Publishers in gaming, sports, and entertainment. If your site covers those topics, Venatus is built around that kind of audience.

## How do you apply to Venatus?

Fill in the publisher interest form on its site. A person on its team audits your site against its content rules. Venatus doesn't publish a traffic or region bar, so its team decides the rest.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share, when you're paid, and the payment minimum. Ask whether Venatus must be your only ad partner, who decides where ads go, and how much notice you need to leave. None of this is public.

## How can you check if a site uses Venatus?

Look for Venatus in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
