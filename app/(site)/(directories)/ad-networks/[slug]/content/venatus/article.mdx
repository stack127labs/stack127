## Who is Venatus a fit for?

Established gaming, game platform, sports, and entertainment publishers. There's too little public evidence to call it a beginner option.

## What do publishers say?

Very little, with no traffic figures. One gaming publisher [wrote on Trustpilot in May 2025](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.venatus.com) that after four months their RPM was about three times what AdSense paid. In a [2026 Reddit thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/1sza527/venatus_vs_publisher_collective/), a commenter said they moved from Publisher Collective to Venatus in January 2026, without sharing results.

## What should gaming sites compare?

Video fill, server-side setup, and exclusivity, against [Playwire](/ad-networks/playwire), [Publisher Collective](/ad-networks/publisher-collective), and [NitroPay](/ad-networks/nitropay).
