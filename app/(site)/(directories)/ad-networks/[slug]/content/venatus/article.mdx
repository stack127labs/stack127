## Who is Venatus for?

Publishers in gaming, sports, and entertainment. If your site covers those topics, Venatus is built around that kind of audience.

## How do you apply to Venatus?

Fill in the publisher interest form on its site. Venatus doesn't publish entry rules, so its team decides.

## How can you check if a site uses Venatus?

Look for Venatus in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Venatus.
