Start on the [Snack Media publishers page](https://www.snack-media.com/publishers/). The [2025 publisher agreement](https://cdn.snack-media.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Snack-Ad-Network-Agreement-TCs.pdf) sets no general traffic minimum. You share live traffic stats, and tags must be live within 14 days of signing.

The impressions figure comes from the [referral program](https://www.snack-media.com/referral/), which also asks for Google policy compliance and leaves out sensitive and gambling sites. Those rules decide referral rewards, not who can join.
