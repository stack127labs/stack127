## Who is Snack Media a fit for?

Sports publishers, clubs, and fan communities, plus entertainment sites, that want direct sales or video. A general blog has little public evidence of fit.

## Managed or bespoke?

Pick managed if you want Snack Media to run everything. Pick bespoke if you have your own setup and just want more demand. The exclusivity scope differs, so read the cover sheet for yours.

## What should you check on the cover sheet?

Your commission, the length of the first term, and exactly which ad sales are exclusive to Snack Media.
