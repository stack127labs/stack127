## What should you have ready before you join Snack Media?

Live traffic stats to share, and time to add its ad tags. The tags must be live within 14 days of signing.

## Can you leave Snack Media?

Yes. Either side can end the contract with 30 days' notice, and after the first term it runs month to month.

## Does Snack Media need to be your only ad partner?

Only if your order says so. Check the cover sheet before you sign.

## When does Snack Media pay?

Within 30 days after the month it receives the advertiser's money. So your pay depends on when advertisers pay Snack Media, not only on when you earned it.

## What should you check on the cover sheet?

Your share, the length of the first term, and any exclusivity. The standard terms leave all three to the cover sheet.

## How can you check if a site uses Snack Media?

Look for Snack Media in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
