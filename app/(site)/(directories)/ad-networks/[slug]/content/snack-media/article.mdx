## Can you leave Snack Media?

Yes. Either side can end the contract with notice, and after the first term it runs month to month.

## Does Snack Media need to be your only ad partner?

Only if your order says so. Check the cover sheet before you sign.

## How can you check if a site uses Snack Media?

Look for Snack Media in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Snack Media.
