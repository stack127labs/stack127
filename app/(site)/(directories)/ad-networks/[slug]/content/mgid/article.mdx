## Who is MGID a fit for?

Article sites, including news, entertainment, and sites outside English, that want native ads as a second income source. Thin sites with few articles won't pass.

## Daily or monthly: which traffic rule should you use?

Whichever you meet. A site with steady daily traffic may clear the daily count, while one with spiky traffic may only clear the monthly total. Check unique visitors in your analytics, not pageviews.

## Can you use MGID with AdSense?

Yes. MGID's own materials say so, as long as your pages still meet placement and ad density rules.

## What else runs native ads?

[Taboola](/ad-networks/taboola) and [Revcontent](/ad-networks/revcontent) both run recommendation and native ads. Taboola must be your only recommendation service while your deal runs.
