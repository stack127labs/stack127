## Who is MGID a fit for?

Sites that want native ads, the kind that look like content links, alongside or instead of banners. MGID doesn't publish one traffic minimum for its main service. Its separate Google Discover offer says it has none. Either way, your site has to pass its traffic quality and content rules. Adult, illegal, hateful, and infringing content isn't allowed.

## Why does MGID's share depend on the offer?

MGID sets pay in each publisher's order, not in one public rate card. The share in one regional offer may not be what you're offered, so compare the order you get.

## Which payment method should you pick?

One with a minimum your earnings clear. MGID's minimums range from $50 to $1,000 depending on the method, so a small site can wait a long time on a high one.

## What could get your MGID account in trouble?

Invalid traffic. MGID's terms cover it, and they say your revenue can be adjusted. Don't buy traffic or encourage clicks.

## What else runs native ads?

[Taboola](/ad-networks/taboola) and [Revcontent](/ad-networks/revcontent) both run recommendation and native ads. Taboola must be your only recommendation service while your deal runs.

## How can you check if a site uses MGID?

Look for MGID in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
