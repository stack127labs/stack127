## Why does MGID's share depend on the offer?

MGID sets pay in each publisher's order, not in one public rate card. The share in one regional offer may not be what you're offered, so compare the order you get.

## Does MGID take small sites?

It doesn't publish a single traffic minimum, and its Discover program says it has none. Your site still has to pass its quality rules.

## How can you check if a site uses MGID?

Look for MGID in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to MGID.
