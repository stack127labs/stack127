## Who is Teads a fit for?

Established news, education, and local publishers with room for in-article video or premium formats. Small sites wanting a self-serve display tag have little evidence of fit.

## Is Teads the same as Outbrain?

Teads now includes what used to be Outbrain's business, so you may see either name in older material.

## Does Teads replace your current ads?

Usually not. It tends to add video and premium demand next to your setup. Confirm the scope for each format in your contract.
