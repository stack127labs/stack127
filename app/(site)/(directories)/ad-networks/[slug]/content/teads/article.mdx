## Who is Teads a fit for?

Publishers with web, connected TV, or app inventory who want premium ad placements. Teads also offers content extension. It doesn't publish entry rules.

## Is Teads the same as Outbrain?

Teads now includes what used to be Outbrain's business.

## How do you join Teads?

Teads doesn't publish an application process. Its media owner page is where to start a partnership conversation.

## When does Teads pay?

Your contract sets it. A Teads company filing says publisher payment terms usually run 30 to 60 days.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share, the payment minimum, and whether Teads must be your only ad partner. Ask how long the contract runs and how much notice you need to leave.

## How can you check if a site uses Teads?

Look for Teads in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
