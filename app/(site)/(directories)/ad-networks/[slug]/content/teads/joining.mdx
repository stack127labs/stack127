Start on the [Teads media owners page](https://www.teads.com/media-owners) or its [contact form](https://www.teads.com/teads-contact/). There's no public application or traffic rule. Fit depends on your formats and market.

Approval and going live can be separate steps. One Italian education site [said on Reddit in March 2026](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/1rr1frr/) it had been approved and installed tags, but ads hadn't started serving yet.
