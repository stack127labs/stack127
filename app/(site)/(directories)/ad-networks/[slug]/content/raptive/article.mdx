## Who is Raptive a fit for?

Sites with fully original content and most readers in the US, Canada, UK, Australia, or New Zealand. If most of your readers are elsewhere, Raptive won't take you, however much traffic you have.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

Google Analytics set up on your site, since Raptive asks for it. Check there that you're above the pageview bar and that most visitors come from its five countries. Raptive doesn't publish how long review takes.

## How does Raptive's pay compare with Mediavine?

Both publish a 75% share. Raptive pays on Net 45, sooner than [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine)'s Net 65, and its minimum is $25. Mediavine sets a yearly ad revenue bar instead of a pageview bar.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Whether Raptive must be your only ad partner, how long the contract runs, and how much notice you need to leave. Ask what placement controls you keep. None of these are public.

## What if your site doesn't qualify yet?

[Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) starts at just over 1,000 monthly sessions. [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric)'s Propel program has a lower pageview bar, with a similar reader location rule. If most readers are outside Raptive's countries, [Clickio](/ad-networks/clickio) sets its bar by region, and [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum.

## How can you check if a site uses Raptive?

Look for Raptive in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
