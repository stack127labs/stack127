## Who is Raptive a fit for?

Sites with original, human-led content and a large share of readers in the US, Canada, UK, Australia, or New Zealand. Below 100,000 monthly pageviews, at least half your traffic must come from those countries. Above that, the bar is 40%.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

Google Analytics set up on your site, since Raptive asks for it. Check there that you're above the pageview bar and that enough visitors come from its five countries for your traffic tier. Your domain must also be at least six months old. Raptive doesn't publish how long review takes.

## How does Raptive's pay compare with Mediavine?

Raptive publishes a 75% share. [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine)'s ranges from 75% to 90%, depending on its program tier. Raptive pays on Net 45, sooner than Mediavine's Net 65, and its minimum is $25 for most methods. Mediavine sets a yearly ad revenue bar instead of a pageview bar.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Whether Raptive must be your only ad partner, how long the contract runs, and how much notice you need to leave. Ask what placement controls you keep. None of these are public.

## What if your site doesn't qualify yet?

[Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) starts at 1,000 premium sessions a month. [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric)'s Propel program has a lower pageview bar, with a similar reader location rule. If too few readers are in Raptive's countries, [Clickio](/ad-networks/clickio) sets its bar by region, and [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum.

## How can you check if a site uses Raptive?

Look for Raptive in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
