## Is Raptive a fit for your site?

It is if you write original content and most of your readers are in the US, Canada, UK, Australia, or New Zealand. Sites with most traffic elsewhere won't qualify.

## Does your Raptive service change as you grow?

Yes. A dedicated manager, custom layouts, and fuller reporting depend on your tier.

## How can you check if a site uses Raptive?

Look for Raptive in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Raptive.
