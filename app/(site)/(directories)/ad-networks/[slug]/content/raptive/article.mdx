## Who is Raptive a fit for?

Food, lifestyle, and editorial blogs, plus gaming sites, with original, human-led content and enough readers in the five countries. Thin, copied, or fully AI-written sites won't pass.

## What should you check before you apply?

1. Monthly pageviews in Google Analytics.
2. The share of visitors from the five countries, against the rule for your size.
3. Your domain is at least six months old.
4. Long-form content on most pages, if you're under 100,000 pageviews.

## How does Raptive compare with Mediavine?

Both start new sites on the same share. [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine) raises it in higher tiers and judges you on last year's ad revenue. Raptive judges pageviews and countries, and pays sooner.

## What if your site doesn't qualify yet?

[Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) starts at 1,000 Tier 1 sessions in 30 days. [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric)'s Propel program has a lower pageview bar with a similar country rule. If too few readers are in Raptive's countries, [Clickio](/ad-networks/clickio) sets its guide by region.
