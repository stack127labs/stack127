Raptive's [eligibility guide](https://help.raptive.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032840891-Who-is-eligible-for-Raptive), updated in September 2026, counts five countries only: the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand. Other high-income countries don't count toward the share. The share you need drops once you pass 100,000 monthly pageviews.

Below that size, most pages also need long-form content. You apply from the [Raptive dashboard](https://dashboard.raptive.com/) and [connect Google Analytics](https://help.raptive.com/hc/en-us/articles/6681661647515-Applying-to-Raptive-with-Google-Analytics) with read-only access.
