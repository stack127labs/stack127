## What is a contextual ad?

It's an ad picked for the words on the page, not for the person reading it. A page about hiking shows hiking ads.

## Who is Media.net a fit for?

Sites you own and run, with real, unpaid traffic. Media.net checks for bought or invalid traffic, and too much of it can get your account suspended.

## How can you check if a site uses Media.net?

Look for Media.net in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Media.net.
