## Who is Media.net a fit for?

Sites you own and run, with real, unpaid traffic and original content. Media.net checks for bought or invalid traffic, and too much of it can get your account suspended. If another ad provider has suspended or dropped you before, Media.net won't take you.

## What is a contextual ad?

It's an ad picked for the words on the page, not for the person reading it. A page about hiking shows hiking ads.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

An ads.txt file with Media.net's lines in it, and accurate details about your site. Check its inventory policy for the topics it doesn't allow.

## How fast does Media.net pay?

It pays monthly on Net 30, so a month's earnings arrive about a month after it ends. That's faster than many managed networks here, such as [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine) on Net 65.

Your pay is based on valid clicks, so earnings depend on readers clicking ads, not only seeing them.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share and the payment minimum. Neither is public.

## How can you check if a site uses Media.net?

Look for Media.net in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
