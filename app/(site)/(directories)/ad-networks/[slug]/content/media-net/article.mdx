## Who is Media.net a fit for?

News, finance, how-to, and other editorial sites with mostly unpaid traffic. A site that buys most of its visitors, or relies on low-quality traffic, is a poor fit.

## What is a contextual ad?

An ad picked for the words on the page, not the person reading it. A page about hiking shows hiking ads.

## Can you run Media.net next to another network?

Be careful. The agreement bars services that show substantially similar ads, so a contextual or display network on the same pages may need Media.net's written approval.

## What do publishers say about Media.net?

Reports are old and vague. One publisher [said in 2023](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/15ql4jb) they'd gone about six months without support replies. That's one account, with no site size.

## How fast does Media.net pay?

Faster than many managed networks here, such as [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine) on Net 65. Because pay is based on clicks, earnings depend on readers clicking ads, not only seeing them.
