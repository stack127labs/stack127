Media.net doesn't publish a traffic minimum or accepted regions. Its agreement covers sites you own, run, or control, and you apply through its publisher program.

Most of your traffic must be organic, and its inventory policy bans invalid or bought traffic above acceptable levels. Your ad requests and site details must be accurate.

You can't have been suspended or dropped by another ad provider before. The agreement also asks you to tell Media.net about certain past traffic or policy suspensions.
