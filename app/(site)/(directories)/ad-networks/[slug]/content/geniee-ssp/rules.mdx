## What rules does Geniee SSP set?

These provisions come from Geniee's publisher terms, and we haven't confirmed each one in the 2025 version. Check them in the agreement you sign.

You tell Geniee where you'll place ads. If it asks for changes and you can't agree, Geniee decides. You also have to tell Geniee before you use a similar service alongside it, or replace it with one.

Geniee can end the agreement with 30 days' notice, or at once if you break the terms. The terms don't say how much notice you give to leave.
