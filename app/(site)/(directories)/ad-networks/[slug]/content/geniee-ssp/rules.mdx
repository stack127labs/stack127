## What rules does Geniee SSP set?

Geniee's terms of use set the content rules, and you must keep its tags in place. You tell Geniee where you'll place ads. If it asks for changes and you can't agree, Geniee decides the placement. You also must tell Geniee before you use a similar service alongside it, or replace it with one.

The agreement runs from the day Geniee approves you until it ends. Geniee can end it with 30 days' notice, or at once if you break the terms. The terms don't say how much notice you must give to leave.
