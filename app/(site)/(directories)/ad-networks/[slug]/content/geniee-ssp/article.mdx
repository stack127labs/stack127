## What is a supply-side platform?

A supply-side platform (SSP) sells a publisher's ad space to many buyers at once, in real time.

## How do you join Geniee SSP?

Sign up through Geniee's online publisher form. Its terms of use set the content rules and how you're paid.

## What should you ask Geniee before you sign?

Your share, since Geniee doesn't publish a fixed one. Also ask how much notice you need to give to leave, since the terms only say how Geniee can end the agreement.

## What else can you join with public rules?

[Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum. [Sovrn](/ad-networks/sovrn) runs an ad exchange for sites with more than 100,000 visits a month, with a $25 payment minimum.

## How can you check if a site uses Geniee SSP?

Look for Geniee SSP in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
