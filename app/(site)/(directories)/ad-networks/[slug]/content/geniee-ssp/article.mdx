## What is a supply-side platform?

A supply-side platform (SSP) sells a publisher's ad space to many buyers at once, in real time.

## How do you join Geniee SSP?

Geniee doesn't publish an application route for publishers. Contact Geniee to ask.

## What should you ask Geniee before you sign?

Your share, when you're paid, the payment minimum, and how you're paid. Ask whether there's a contract term and how much notice you need to leave. None of this is public.

## What if you want a network you can join yourself?

[Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum. [Sovrn](/ad-networks/sovrn) runs an ad exchange with no traffic minimum and a $25 payment minimum.

## How can you check if a site uses Geniee SSP?

Look for Geniee SSP in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
