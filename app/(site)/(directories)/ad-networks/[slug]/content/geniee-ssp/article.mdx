## Who is Geniee SSP a fit for?

Established web or app publishers with Japanese or Asian audiences, and someone who manages the ad setup. An SSP adds demand to a bigger setup. It isn't a plug-and-play network for a new blog.

For a first ad network you run yourself, [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) is simpler.

## What should you ask Geniee before you sign?

1. What share do you keep?
2. What are the current payment minimums?
3. How much notice do you give to leave?
