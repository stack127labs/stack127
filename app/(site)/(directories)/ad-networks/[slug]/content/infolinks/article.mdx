## Is Infolinks a fit for a small site?

As an extra, yes. With no traffic minimum, it's one of the few ad networks a small site can add today. Don't expect it to replace a main display network. It earns from extra formats around your text.

## What are in-text ads?

Ads inside your words. Infolinks links some words in your text, and an ad appears when a reader hovers over one.

## Are Infolinks' public terms current?

Partly. The service agreement dates from 2015, while the payment and sign-up help pages are current. Where they differ, ask Infolinks which applies to you.
