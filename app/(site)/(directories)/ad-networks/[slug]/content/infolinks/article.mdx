## Is Infolinks a fit for a small site?

It can be. Infolinks is open to sites of any size, so it can work before you qualify for a managed network.

## How current are Infolinks' public terms?

The public agreement is years old. Ask Infolinks for the current version before you rely on its numbers.

## How can you check if a site uses Infolinks?

Look for Infolinks in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Infolinks.
