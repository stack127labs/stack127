## Is Infolinks a fit for a small site?

It can be. Infolinks is open to sites of any size, so it can work before you qualify for a managed network. It won't take illegal, offensive, adult, or porn content.

## How long does Infolinks take to approve you?

Usually about 48 hours after you send its short sign-up form. Once approved, you add the Infolinks script to your site.

## What are in-text ads?

Ads that sit inside your words. Infolinks links some words in your text, and an ad appears when a reader hovers over one. You can change how they look and how many show.

## Can you try Infolinks before you commit?

The old public agreement starts with a 30-day trial, then renews every 12 months. It can be ended on one business day's notice. That agreement was last updated in 2015, so ask whether the current one still works this way.

## What else can a small site try?

[Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum. [Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) starts at 1,000 premium sessions a month.

## How can you check if a site uses Infolinks?

Look for Infolinks in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
