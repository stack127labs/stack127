## What rules does Infolinks set?

These are provisions of the 2015 agreement, not confirmed current terms. It starts with a 30-day trial, then renews every 12 months, and can be ended on one business day's notice. It also bars publishers terminated by another ad platform, such as AdSense, in the past 24 months.

The current help pages don't restate them or say whether you can run other ad networks alongside. Check the agreement you're offered.
