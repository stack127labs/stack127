There's no PubGalaxy application any more. The old publisher page now opens [Improve Digital](https://improvedigital.com/), which has separate access requests for two products:

- **Improve Digital Yield**, a managed service that runs your ads for you.
- **Improve Digital SSP**, for publishers who run their own ad setup and want more demand.

Improve Digital doesn't publish entry rules for either. Applying there is a new application, not a PubGalaxy one.
