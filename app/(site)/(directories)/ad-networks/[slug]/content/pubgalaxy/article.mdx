## Is PubGalaxy still running?

Yes, as part of Improve Digital. Azerion bought PubGalaxy in 2021 and moved its publisher services under the Improve Digital name in September 2026. The pubgalaxy.com site now opens Improve Digital's site, so new publishers start there. Sites that already run PubGalaxy still name it in their ads.txt files, so we keep listing them here.

## Does PubGalaxy take sites below its suggested traffic?

It can. The traffic level is a suggestion, not a hard rule, and each site is reviewed on its own.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

Six months of traffic history, and original, brand-safe content. PubGalaxy checks Google's Transparency Report for copyright claims against your site, so look yourself first.

## Why does PubGalaxy list two shares?

One covers ads sold through its ad partners, and a lower one covers direct deals. Both are minimums in the published terms. Since the move to Improve Digital, ask whether these terms still apply to new publishers.

## What do you give up with PubGalaxy?

You let it manage your ad inventory, and it can make changes to improve the reader experience. If you want full control of your layout, ask what you can still decide.

## How can you check if a site uses PubGalaxy?

Look for PubGalaxy in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
