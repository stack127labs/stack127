## Is PubGalaxy still running?

PubGalaxy's website now redirects to [Improve Digital](https://improvedigital.com/), which offers publisher services under its own name. Some sites still name PubGalaxy in their ads.txt files, so we keep listing them.

## Which Improve Digital product could fit?

It depends on who runs your ads. Pick Yield if you want a managed service that takes over your ad setup. Pick the SSP if you run your own setup and want extra buyers.

## Can you still compare PubGalaxy's terms?

The PubGalaxy domain now redirects to Improve Digital. Its current site lists publisher services but no terms for a PubGalaxy plan. Ask Improve Digital which terms apply to your account.
