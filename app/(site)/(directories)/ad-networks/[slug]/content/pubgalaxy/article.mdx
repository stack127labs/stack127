## Is PubGalaxy still running?

Not under its own name. Its owner Azerion [announced on September 23, 2026](https://www.azerion.com/azerion-unifies-its-publisher-supply-business-under-improve-digital-launching-a-full-circle-solution-for-publishers/) that its publisher business now sits under Improve Digital. Some sites still name PubGalaxy in their ads.txt files, so we keep listing them.

## Which Improve Digital product replaces it?

It depends on who runs your ads. Pick Yield if you want a managed service that takes over your ad setup. Pick the SSP if you run your own setup and want extra buyers.

## Can you still compare PubGalaxy's terms?

Not usefully. Its old pay terms and traffic rules belong to the retired service. Ask Improve Digital for the terms you'd actually get.
