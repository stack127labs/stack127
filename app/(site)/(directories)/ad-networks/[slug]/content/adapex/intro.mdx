Adapex is a managed ad partner for mid-sized publishers. You keep the ad setup you already have, like AdSense, Ad Exchange or Prebid, and Adapex's team works on top of it through Google Ad Manager to earn more from the same traffic.

There's no long-term contract to get started. It works on a revenue share that's agreed with each publisher, so expect a sales conversation rather than a self-serve sign-up.
