## Who is Adapex a fit for?

A publisher who already sells ads through Google and wants a team to run the setup. If you use AdSense, Ad Exchange, or Prebid today, Adapex can take that over, and you keep your own Google Ad Manager account.

If you're just starting out, [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum and you can set it up yourself.

## What does onboarding through MCM mean?

MCM is Google's way of letting a partner manage ads for another publisher. Your Ad Manager account stays yours, and Adapex works with it under that link.

## Can you try Adapex without a long contract?

Yes. Adapex says no long-term contract is needed. It offers better terms on longer contracts, so ask what you'd give up by staying short.

## How long will you wait for your money?

About two months. On Net 60, each month's earnings arrive 60 days after the month ends. [Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) pays on Net 45 and [Media.net](/ad-networks/media-net) on Net 30, so plan for a longer gap if you switch from a faster payer.

## What should you ask Adapex before you sign?

Ask for your share, the payment minimum, and whether Adapex must be your only ad partner. None of these are public. If you're offered a longer contract, get the better share or features it adds in writing.

## How can you check if a site uses Adapex?

Look for Adapex in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
