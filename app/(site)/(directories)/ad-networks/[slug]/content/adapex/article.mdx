## Who is Adapex a fit for?

An established site that already earns from AdSense, Ad Exchange, or Prebid and wants more from it without hiring an ad operations person. Adapex adds itself to that setup.

A new blog without Google Ad Manager is a weak fit. Start with [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense), then come back once ad revenue is worth managing.

## What does joining through MCM mean?

MCM is Google's way of letting a partner sell ads for your site. Your Ad Manager account stays yours, and Google reviews the link before ads run.

## How long will you wait for your money?

About two months from the end of each month. If you're moving from a Net 30 network such as [Media.net](/ad-networks/media-net), plan for a gap in cash flow while the first payments come through.

## What should you ask Adapex before you sign?

1. What share of ad revenue do you keep?
2. What's the payment minimum?
3. What does a longer contract add, and what notice ends it?
