## Who is Adapex a fit for?

Adapex suits a publisher who already sells ads through Google and wants a team to manage the setup. You keep your own Google Ad Manager account, so you aren't starting from zero.

## Can you try Adapex without a long contract?

Yes. Adapex says no long-term contract is needed. It offers better terms on longer contracts, so ask what you'd give up by staying short.

## What does onboarding through MCM mean?

MCM is Google's way of letting a partner manage ads for another publisher. Your Ad Manager account stays yours, and Adapex works with it under that link.

## How can you check if a site uses Adapex?

Look for Adapex in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Adapex.
