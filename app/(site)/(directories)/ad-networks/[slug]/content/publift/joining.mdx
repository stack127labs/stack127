Publift's [requirements guide](https://www.publift.com/blog/publift-requirements), updated in October 2026, makes monthly ad revenue the main test. Pageviews are a secondary signal, not a minimum. Sites below the revenue line that are growing fast are invited to get in touch.

Publift says it [works best](https://www.publift.com/how-it-works) when most readers are in the Americas, Europe, or Australia and New Zealand, without calling that a cutoff. You start on the [Let's talk form](https://www.publift.com/lets-talk), which asks for your monthly revenue, pageviews, and where most traffic comes from.
