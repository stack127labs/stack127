Publift looks at your monthly ad revenue first. Pageviews are a secondary signal, not a strict rule. Your traffic should be steady and genuine, and your content useful and safe.

Publift works best when most of your readers are in the Americas, Europe, or Australia and New Zealand. It doesn't call that a strict cutoff.

You apply for a site review. Publift gives a time for sending your ad tags, but it doesn't say how much of that is the review itself.
