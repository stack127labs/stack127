## Who is Publift a fit for?

Sites already earning real money from ads, in any niche, with mostly Western or Australian readers. A site with fewer pageviews but strong earnings can fit better than a big one that earns little.

## Should you trust a Publift revenue forecast?

Treat it as a sales estimate. In an [October 2026 Reddit thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/1wp5uz8/), a gaming site with about 1.4 million monthly pageviews said Publift quoted $4,000 to $10,000 a month, and the owner questioned it. Compare your current actual revenue instead.

## Where else can a global site go?

[Clickio](/ad-networks/clickio) sets its traffic guide by visitor region. [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic number to hit.
