## Who is Publift a fit for?

Sites that already earn steadily from ads. Publift works best when most readers are in the Americas, Europe, or Australia and New Zealand, though it doesn't call that a hard cutoff. Your traffic should be steady and genuine, and your content useful and safe.

## Does Publift care more about revenue or traffic?

Revenue. Publift uses monthly ad revenue as its main test and treats pageviews as a hint. A site with fewer pageviews but strong earnings can still fit.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

Your recent monthly ad earnings and traffic numbers. Since revenue comes first, have your earnings reports ready to show.

## What should you confirm in your order?

Your share, whether the service is month to month, and any exclusivity or layout rules. Publift's 2026 comparison describes an 80/20 split and month-to-month service, but the requirements page doesn't, so get both in writing.

## Where else can a global site go?

[Clickio](/ad-networks/clickio) sets its traffic bar by visitor region. [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum.

## How can you check if a site uses Publift?

Look for Publift in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
