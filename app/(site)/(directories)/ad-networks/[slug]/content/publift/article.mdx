## Does Publift care more about revenue or traffic?

Revenue. Publift uses monthly ad revenue as its main test and treats pageviews as a hint. A site with fewer pageviews but strong earnings can still fit.

## When does Publift pay?

It depends on where the money comes from. Google revenue is paid sooner than revenue from other networks. Check the dates in your order.

## How can you check if a site uses Publift?

Look for Publift in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Publift.
