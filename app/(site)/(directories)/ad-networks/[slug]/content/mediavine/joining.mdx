Mediavine's [Publisher Path](https://help.mediavine.com/programs-and-the-publisher-path-to-growth) counts ad revenue from the previous calendar year. Last month's earnings, or the last 12 months, aren't the same thing, even if they add up to more. Check the [application requirements](https://www.mediavine.com/application-requirements/) before you apply.

You don't need to have used AdSense before. Still growing? [Journey](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) is Mediavine's separate program for smaller sites.
