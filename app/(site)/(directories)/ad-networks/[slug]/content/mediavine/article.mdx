## Who is Mediavine a fit for?

Content sites with enough ad revenue earned during the previous calendar year, often in food, travel, home, parenting, or personal finance. Earnings from part of that year can count. If you only reached the revenue target this year, you may have to wait until the new year to qualify.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

1. Proof of last calendar year's ad revenue for the site.
2. Read-only Google Analytics access to share during review.
3. A Google account with no active AdSense or Ad Exchange issues.

## Does meeting the revenue rule mean you're in?

No. Mediavine still reviews content and audience. In 2026, one publisher [said on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/1wz7cw6) they expected to qualify on last year's earnings but were placed in Journey instead. Another, with about 1.6 million monthly users, [described repeated declines](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/1xb1r9c) and a 60-day wait to reapply. Neither shared their full revenue or site details.

## How does Mediavine compare with Raptive?

[Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) sets a pageview bar and a reader location rule. Mediavine sets a revenue bar. Both start new sites on the same share, but Mediavine's rises in its higher tiers, while Raptive pays sooner.
