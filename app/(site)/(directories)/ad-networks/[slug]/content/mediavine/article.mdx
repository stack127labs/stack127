## How do you join Mediavine?

You apply with your site, and Mediavine reviews your ad revenue, traffic, and content against the rules here. If you're approved, you add their script and their ads.txt lines, and they set up and run the ads for you.

## What if your site is too small for Mediavine?

Mediavine runs a second program, [Journey](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine), for smaller sites. It has a lower bar to join and the same company behind it. Journey's page lists its rules and the sites that use it.

## What does the pay day mean?

Mediavine pays each month's earnings a set number of days after the month ends. Earnings from January arrive a couple of months later, not at the start of February. Plan for that gap if you switch from a network that pays faster.

## Can you run other ads with Mediavine?

Mediavine's help center lists its rules on ad placement and other ad partners. Affiliate links and sponsored posts are usually separate, but check the current rules with Mediavine before you sign.

## How can you check if a site uses Mediavine?

Look for Mediavine in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
