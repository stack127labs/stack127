## Who is Mediavine a fit for?

Content sites that already earn steady ad revenue, often in food, travel, home, and similar topics. Mediavine looks at what your site earns from ads in a year, not a traffic number. Your content needs to be original, high quality, and evergreen.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

Proof of what your ads earn now, since the bar is yearly ad revenue. Your account must not have an active AdSense ban, but you don't need to have used AdSense before.

## What if your site is too small for Mediavine?

Mediavine runs a second program, [Journey](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine), for smaller sites. It has a lower bar to join and the same company behind it. Journey's page lists its rules and the sites that use it.

## What does the pay day mean?

Mediavine pays each month's earnings a set number of days after the month ends. Earnings from January arrive a couple of months later, not at the start of February. Plan for that gap if you switch from a network that pays faster.

## How does Mediavine compare with Raptive?

Both publish a 75% share. [Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) sets a pageview bar and a reader location rule, while Mediavine sets a revenue bar. Raptive pays on Net 45, sooner than Mediavine's Net 65. Mediavine says there's no long-term contract.

## How can you check if a site uses Mediavine?

Look for Mediavine in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
