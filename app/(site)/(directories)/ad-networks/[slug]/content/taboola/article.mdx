## Who is Taboola a fit for?

News and editorial sites with enough articles to fill recommendation widgets. It isn't a display network replacement.

## Can you run Taboola with another recommendation widget?

No. [Revcontent](/ad-networks/revcontent) and [MGID](/ad-networks/mgid) run similar widgets, so you'd pick one of them or Taboola. Your display ad network can stay.

## What do Taboola's case studies show?

Large publishers. Its [case study on The Independent](https://www.taboola.com/resources/case-studies/the-independent-uk), with about 63 million global visitors, reports 14% more Taboola revenue after adding in-article recommendations. It's Taboola's own report about a huge site, not what a small site should expect.
