## Can you run Taboola with another recommendation widget?

No. Taboola must be your only recommendation service during the agreement. The rule covers recommendation services, so check your order for anything else.

## What content can't run Taboola?

Fake news, porn, copyright violations, defamation, threats, and criminal material are banned under its Publisher Policy.

## How can you check if a site uses Taboola?

Look for Taboola in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Taboola.
