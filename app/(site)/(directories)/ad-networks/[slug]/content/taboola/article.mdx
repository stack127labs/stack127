## Who is Taboola a fit for?

Sites that want recommended stories and ads under or beside their content. Taboola doesn't publish a traffic minimum, but your site must follow its Publisher Policy.

## Can you run Taboola with another recommendation widget?

No. Taboola must be your only recommendation service during the agreement. The rule covers recommendation services, so check your order for anything else.

## How does Taboola pay?

Through Payoneer, in US dollars, within 45 days after the month ends. Set up a Payoneer account before you start.

## What should you check in your order?

Your share, the contract length, and how to end it. Taboola's order sets all three, and they aren't public.

## What else runs recommendation ads?

[Revcontent](/ad-networks/revcontent) and [MGID](/ad-networks/mgid) both run native and recommendation ads. Because of Taboola's exclusivity rule, you'd pick Taboola or one of them, not both.

## How can you check if a site uses Taboola?

Look for Taboola in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
