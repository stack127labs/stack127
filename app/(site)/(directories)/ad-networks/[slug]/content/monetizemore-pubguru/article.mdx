## What's the difference between MonetizeMore and PubGuru?

MonetizeMore is the company. PubGuru is its ad platform, and PubGuru Premium is the higher tier with extra tools and a support team.

## Is PubGuru Premium a fit for a smaller site?

Not yet. Premium is for sites with high ad revenue or traffic. Ask MonetizeMore what it offers below that level.

## How can you check if a site uses MonetizeMore?

Look for MonetizeMore in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to MonetizeMore.
