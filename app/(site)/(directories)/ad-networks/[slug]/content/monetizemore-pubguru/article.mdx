## Who is PubGuru Premium a fit for?

Big content sites and portfolios, including recipe sites and international groups, that need someone to run yield optimization full time. MonetizeMore's case studies show the scale: a European group with 20+ sites and over 100 million monthly visitors, and the recipe site Panlasang Pinoy. Both are MonetizeMore's own reports, far above the entry rule.

## What's the difference between MonetizeMore and PubGuru?

MonetizeMore is the company. PubGuru is its ad platform, and PubGuru Premium is the higher tier with extra tools and a support team.

## What if you're below both Premium rules?

Ask MonetizeMore what it offers below Premium. [Publift](/ad-networks/publift) also qualifies sites on ad revenue, with a lower bar. [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine) uses last calendar year's ad revenue.

## What should you ask before you sign?

1. What's your exact share, and when are you paid?
2. What's the payment minimum?
3. Can you keep your other ad partners?
4. How much notice ends the contract?
