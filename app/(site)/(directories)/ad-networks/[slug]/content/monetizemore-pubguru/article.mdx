## Who is PubGuru Premium a fit for?

Sites that already earn well from ads or get heavy traffic. You qualify on either monthly ad revenue or monthly pageviews, counted across your sites.

## What's the difference between MonetizeMore and PubGuru?

MonetizeMore is the company. PubGuru is its ad platform, and PubGuru Premium is the higher tier with extra tools and a support team.

## Is PubGuru Premium a fit for a smaller site?

Not yet. Ask MonetizeMore what it offers below the Premium level. [Publift](/ad-networks/publift) also qualifies sites on ad revenue, with a lower bar. [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine) uses a yearly ad revenue bar.

## What should you have ready before you contact MonetizeMore?

Your monthly ad revenue and pageviews for every site you'd bring. If you run several sites, add them up, since Premium counts them together.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your exact share, pay day, payment minimum, and how much notice you need to leave. None of these are on the Premium page. Ask whether you can keep other ad partners.

## How can you check if a site uses MonetizeMore?

Look for MonetizeMore in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
