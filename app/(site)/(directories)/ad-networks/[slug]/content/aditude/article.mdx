## Who is Aditude for?

Aditude works with web and app publishers on their ad technology. It doesn't say who qualifies, so the only way to know is to ask its team.

## What should you ask Aditude before you sign?

Aditude publishes no publisher terms, so get these answers in writing:

1. What share of ad revenue do you keep?
2. When are you paid, and what's the minimum?
3. Must Aditude be your only ad partner?
4. Who decides where ads go on your pages?
5. How much notice do you need to give to leave?

## What if you want public terms before you talk to anyone?

Pick a network that publishes its rules. [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum. [Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) and [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine) publish their share and pay day. [Ezoic](/ad-networks/ezoic) publishes its share in its terms.

## How can you check if a site uses Aditude?

Look for Aditude in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
