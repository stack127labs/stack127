## Who is Aditude a fit for?

Larger publishers with real ad operations needs, such as a gaming site, a media group, or an app with many daily users. You'll get the most from it if someone on your side can judge a technical setup.

A small blog looking for a quick ad tag isn't the target. [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) is the simpler start.

## What do publishers say about Aditude?

Independent reports are thin. One operator of a free design app with about 1.1 million monthly users [wrote on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/1ozpww8) that they started with Aditude and later tested other partners after problems with ad placement. That's one account, not a pattern.

## What should you ask in the demo?

1. What share of ad revenue do you keep, and when are you paid?
2. Must Aditude be your only ad partner?
3. Who decides where ads go on your pages?
4. How much notice ends the contract?

## Which networks publish their terms instead?

[Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) and [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine) publish their share and pay day. [Ezoic](/ad-networks/ezoic) puts its share in its legal terms.
