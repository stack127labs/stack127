## Who is Aditude for?

Aditude works with web and app publishers on their ad technology. It doesn't say who qualifies, so the only way to know is to ask.

## What should you ask Aditude before you sign?

Ask for the share you'd get, when you'd be paid, and whether Aditude must be your only ad partner. Ask how much notice you'd need to leave. None of this is on its public pages, so get it in writing.

## How can you check if a site uses Aditude?

Look for Aditude in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Aditude.
