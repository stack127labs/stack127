## Who is AdPushup a fit for?

An established content site, such as a tech, media, or consumer blog, that already earns from display ads and wants managed testing. If your ad revenue is still small, the work AdPushup does has little to improve. [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) is the simpler start.

## Are AdPushup's public terms current?

They were last revised in April 2021, and AdPushup still links them. Your share and any newer terms come from the Publisher Agreement you're offered. Read that one before you sign.

## What could get your account in trouble?

Invalid traffic, such as bought visits or click schemes. The terms ban it, and so do most other networks.
