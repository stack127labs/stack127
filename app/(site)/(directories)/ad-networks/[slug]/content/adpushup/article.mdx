## Who is AdPushup for?

Publishers who want help managing ad placements and price floors, with real-time reports. AdPushup says it has no pageview minimum, but it looks for about $3,000 a month in ad revenue. Submit your site and see what its review says.

## Are AdPushup's public terms current?

The terms AdPushup links to were last revised in April 2021. They don't set your share. That comes from a separate Publisher Agreement, so read the one you're offered and go by that.

## What share do you keep?

It's set in your Publisher Agreement, not in the public terms. Ask AdPushup to show the rate in writing, along with the $50 minimum and Net 45 timing, before you sign.

## What could get your AdPushup account in trouble?

Invalid traffic. The terms ban it along with illegal, adult, hateful, fraudulent, and infringing content. If you buy traffic or run click schemes, expect problems with AdPushup and with most other networks.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Ask whether AdPushup must be your only ad partner and how much notice you need to leave. Neither is in its current public pages.

## How can you check if a site uses AdPushup?

Look for AdPushup in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
