## Who is AdPushup for?

Publishers who want help managing ad placements and price floors. AdPushup doesn't publish who qualifies, so submit your site and see what its review says.

## Are AdPushup's public terms current?

Maybe not. The public terms are several years old and may not match today's agreements. Read the agreement you're offered, and go by that.

## How can you check if a site uses AdPushup?

Look for AdPushup in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to AdPushup.
