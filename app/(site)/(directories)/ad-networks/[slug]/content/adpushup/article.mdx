## Who is AdPushup for?

Publishers who want help managing ad placements and price floors, with real-time reports. AdPushup doesn't publish who qualifies, so submit your site and see what its review says.

## Are AdPushup's public terms current?

Maybe not. The public terms were revised in 2018 and may not match today's agreements. Read the agreement you're offered, and go by that.

## What did the older terms say about your share?

They set a 20% service fee, so you kept 80%. Treat that as a starting point for the conversation, not a promise. Ask AdPushup to confirm the fee, the $50 minimum, and the Net 45 timing in your agreement.

## What could get your AdPushup account in trouble?

Invalid traffic. The older terms ban it along with other policy breaks. If you buy traffic or run click schemes, expect problems with AdPushup and with most other networks.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Ask whether AdPushup must be your only ad partner, how much notice you need to leave, and which payment methods it uses. None of these are in its current public pages.

## How can you check if a site uses AdPushup?

Look for AdPushup in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
