Submit your site on the [AdPushup get started page](https://www.adpushup.com/publisher/get-started/), and its team approves or rejects it.

AdPushup cares more about your ad revenue than your pageviews. The revenue figure comes from its own [blog post on small publishers](https://www.adpushup.com/blog/best-ad-networks-for-small-publishers/), and the sign-up page doesn't repeat it, so treat it as a guide, not a rule.
