Freestar runs ads and ad operations for big, brand-safe sites in sports, news, finance, gaming and entertainment. Its typical publisher gets around a million pageviews a month, though smaller sites can still apply.

It cares a lot about audience quality. Freestar looks for mostly organic readers from the US, UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand, and original, long-form content. Going from kickoff to live usually takes three to six weeks.
