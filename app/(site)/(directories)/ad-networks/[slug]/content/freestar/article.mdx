## Is Freestar a fit for a growing site?

Only once it's large. Freestar usually works with high-traffic sites, and it checks where your readers are. A site with most readers outside the US, UK, Canada, New Zealand, and Australia won't meet the rule, even if it's big.

## How long does it take to start with Freestar?

Plan for several weeks. An account manager and a technical team guide the setup.

## What does Freestar check before it accepts you?

Your content, your standing with ad providers, and where your traffic comes from. It may ask for GA4 access and your traffic and revenue history.

## How can you check if a site uses Freestar?

Look for Freestar in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Freestar.
