## Is Freestar a fit for a growing site?

Only once it's large. Freestar usually works with high-traffic sites, and it checks where your readers are. A site with most readers outside the US, UK, Canada, New Zealand, and Australia won't meet the rule, even if it's big.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

1. GA4 access to share during review.
2. Your past traffic and revenue numbers.
3. Original content made for people, and brand safe.
4. Good standing with major ad providers.

## How long does it take to start with Freestar?

Plan for several weeks. Freestar says onboarding commonly takes three to six weeks, with an account manager and a technical team guiding the setup.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share, pay day, payment minimum, and whether Freestar must be your only ad partner. None of these are public. Ask how much notice you need to leave, too.

## What fits if you're not at Freestar's size yet?

[Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) has a much lower pageview bar with a similar location rule. [Playwire](/ad-networks/playwire) asks for less traffic and looks for a good footprint in English-speaking countries, not a set share. [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine) looks at yearly ad revenue instead of pageviews.

## How can you check if a site uses Freestar?

Look for Freestar in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
