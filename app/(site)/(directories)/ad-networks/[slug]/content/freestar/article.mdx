## Is Freestar a fit for a growing site?

If your readers fit. Size is a guide, but the two audience checks aren't. A big site with most readers outside the five countries, or mostly paid traffic, won't pass.

## Does social traffic count as organic?

Yes. Freestar counts social, news feeds, YouTube, Reddit, and syndication, as well as search. A site that grew on Pinterest or Discover can still pass the organic check.

## What do publishers say about getting in?

Reports are old and thin. A [2019 Reddit thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/bxgb6c) has publishers saying smaller sites were accepted, but they counted ad impressions, not pageviews, so they don't tell you the bar today.

## What fits if you're not at Freestar's size yet?

[Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) has a lower bar with a similar location rule. [Playwire](/ad-networks/playwire) asks for less traffic. [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine) looks at yearly ad revenue instead of pageviews.
