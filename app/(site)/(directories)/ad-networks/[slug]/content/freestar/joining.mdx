## Who can join Freestar?

Freestar's traffic rule is what it usually asks for, not a strict cutoff, so ask its team if you're close. At least 50% of your traffic must come from the US, UK, Canada, New Zealand, or Australia.

Your content must be original and made for people, and you need good standing with major ad providers. Explicit sexual content, piracy, violence, hate speech, and illegal drugs aren't allowed.

During review, Freestar may ask for GA4 access and your past traffic and revenue.
