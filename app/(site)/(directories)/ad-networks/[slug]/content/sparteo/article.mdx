## Who is Sparteo a fit for?

Content publishers with enough scale to need a managed setup, especially education and reference sites like W3Schools in Sparteo's [case study](https://corporate.sparteo.com/post/w3schools-rpm-uplift-unified-monetisation-stack). That case is Sparteo's own and gives no traffic figures.

A typical solo blog has little public evidence of fit. [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) is the simpler start.

## What should you ask in the sales call?

1. Do you run my whole ad setup or just some tools?
2. How do you work with my Google Ad Manager and current bidders?
3. What share do I keep, and when am I paid?
