## What does Sparteo offer besides display ads?

Video through Viously, consent management through FastCMP, and Actirise, plus analytics on revenue and page speed.

## Who is Sparteo a fit for?

Publishers who want ads, video, and consent tools from one company, with reports that include Core Web Vitals. Sparteo doesn't publish who qualifies.

## How do you work with Sparteo?

Sparteo doesn't publish how to apply. Contact the company to ask.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share, when you're paid, the payment minimum, and how you're paid. Ask whether Sparteo must be your only ad partner and how much notice you need to leave. None of this is public.

## How can you check if a site uses Sparteo?

Look for Sparteo in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
