## Who is Setupad a fit for?

Publishers who want a platform to run their display ads. Its older articles mention a traffic level and readers from the US, UK, Canada, and Australia, but we didn't find a current criteria page. Ask its team.

## Why are Setupad's rules unclear?

Setupad's published rules are in blog articles from different years, and they don't fully agree. Ask its team for current eligibility before you count on them.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

An ads.txt file you can update, and a site that follows ad policies. Both are in its published guidance.

## How can Setupad pay you?

By bank transfer, PayPal, Payoneer, Wise, Revolut, or Paysera, according to its guidance.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share, the currency you're paid in, and whether Setupad must be your only ad partner. Ask how long the contract runs and how much notice you need to leave.

## How can you check if a site uses Setupad?

Look for Setupad in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
