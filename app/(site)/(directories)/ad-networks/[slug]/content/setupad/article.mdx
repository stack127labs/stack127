## Which Setupad product fits you?

Most independent sites should look at the managed service. Self-Serve only makes sense if you already have tens of millions of monthly visits, your own Ad Manager, and direct deals with ad exchanges.

## Why are Setupad's managed rules unclear?

Its guidance uses visitors in one place and sessions in another, and the figure isn't on a current rules page. Ask Setupad for current managed eligibility before you count on it.

## What do publishers report?

One Japanese publisher with about 3 million monthly pageviews, mostly from Asia, [wrote in October 2024](https://medium.com/@vibrant_mango_impala_136/setupad-review-my-1-year-with-setupad-and-why-i-ultimately-switched-back-to-google-adsense-150fd77506d5) that they used Setupad for a year and went back to AdSense over setup and performance concerns. It's one account without revenue figures.
