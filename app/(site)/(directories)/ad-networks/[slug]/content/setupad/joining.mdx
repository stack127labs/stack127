**Managed service.** Setupad's [publisher guide](https://setupad.com/blog/best-ad-networks-for-publishers/) says most traffic should come from the US, UK, Canada, or Australia, and that low-quality sites aren't accepted. Its older traffic figure counts visitors in one place and sessions in another, and isn't confirmed on a current rules page. You set up ads.txt and use Setupad's demand, including Google. Contact Setupad through [its site](https://setupad.com/) to ask.

**Self-Serve.** The [plans](https://setupad.com/setupad-self-serve/) start at 10 million monthly visits and need your own Google Ad Manager, direct ad exchange accounts, a high-quality site, and Setupad's approval. The live [sign-up form](https://signup.setupad.com/?lang=en&type=7) is for Self-Serve only.
