## Who is SHE Media a fit for?

US-focused blogs in lifestyle, food, parenting, DIY, and women's interest topics, with original, human-led content and a site at least 90 days old. Label any AI-generated images clearly.

## Can a smaller site get in?

Sometimes, by individual review. Old reports don't settle it. One lifestyle blogger [reported in 2020](https://imperfectidealist.com/she-media-review/) earning $124 in a month at about 25,500 sessions, but sessions aren't pageviews, and that isn't proof of an exception.

## What should you check before you sign?

1. Whether the 2023 share and minimum still apply.
2. Your renewal date, since stopping it takes 60 days' notice.
3. Which other ads must come off, since banners are exclusive.
