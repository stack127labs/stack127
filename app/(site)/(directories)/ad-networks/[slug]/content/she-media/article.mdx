## How do you join SHE Media?

SHE Media doesn't publish a general application checklist. Its referral program lists criteria for referred sites, which is the clearest public guide.

## What does the referral program look for?

Quality content, Google Analytics, and a traffic level for referred sites. Treat that as a hint, not a rule for every applicant.

## Can publishers outside the US work with SHE Media?

Yes. SHE Media has payment options for publishers outside the US, including PayPal, Wise, ACH, and wire. Payoneer isn't one of them, so set up another method before you join.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share, pay day, and payment minimum. Ask whether SHE Media must be your only ad partner and how much notice you need to leave. None of these are public.

## How can you check if a site uses SHE Media?

Look for SHE Media in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
