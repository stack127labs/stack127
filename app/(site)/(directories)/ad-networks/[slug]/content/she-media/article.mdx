## How do you join SHE Media?

SHE Media doesn't publish a general application checklist. Its referral program lists criteria for referred sites, which is the clearest public guide.

## Can publishers outside the US work with SHE Media?

Yes. SHE Media has payment options for publishers outside the US, including PayPal, Wise, ACH, and wire. Payoneer isn't one of them.

## How can you check if a site uses SHE Media?

Look for SHE Media in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to SHE Media.
