## How do you join SHE Media?

Apply online and give SHE Media access to your Google Analytics. It typically looks for 50,000+ monthly pageviews and at least 30% US traffic, though it may consider smaller sites case by case. Expect a reply in three to four weeks.

## What does SHE Media look for?

Original, brand-safe, human-led content on a site that's at least 90 days old. You also need a good track record with Google's ad products. If you use AI-generated images, label them clearly.

## Can publishers outside the US work with SHE Media?

Yes, but check how you'll be paid. Its FAQ says international publishers must use PayPal, and it can't work with countries where PayPal isn't available. A 2025 support guide adds Wise, ACH, and wire, if approved. Payoneer isn't one of them, so ask which method you'll get before you join.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Whether the 75% share and $20 minimum in its 2023 Member Guide still apply to you, and when your 12-month contract renews, since you must give 60 days' notice to stop it. SHE Media must be your only banner ad partner, so check which other ads you can keep.

## How can you check if a site uses SHE Media?

Look for SHE Media in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
