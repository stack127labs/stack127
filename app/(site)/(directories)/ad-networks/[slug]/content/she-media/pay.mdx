## How does SHE Media pay?

The share and minimum come from SHE Media's Member Guide, last updated in March 2023, so check both against your contract.

Outside the US, its pages disagree. The FAQ says all international partners are paid by PayPal, and sites in countries that can't receive PayPal can't join. An [April 2025 support guide](https://support.shemedia.com/hc/en-us/articles/35987097977627-Payment-Options-for-Publishers-Outside-the-US) adds Wise, ACH, or wire if approved. Ask which applies before you join.
