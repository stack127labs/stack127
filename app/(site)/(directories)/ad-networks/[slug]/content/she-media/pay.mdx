## How does SHE Media pay?

SHE Media pays after each month closes. Its share and payment minimum come from its Member Guide, last updated in March 2023, so check both against your contract.

US publishers are paid by direct deposit, or by check if ACH isn't available. Outside the US, SHE Media's pages disagree. Its FAQ says you must use PayPal, and it can't work with countries where PayPal isn't available. An April 2025 support guide says PayPal if you earn under $1,000 a month, with wire preferred above that, and Wise or ACH if approved. Payoneer isn't accepted. Currency is not public.
