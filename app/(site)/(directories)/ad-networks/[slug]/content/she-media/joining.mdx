Apply on the [SHE Media application](https://apply.shemedia.com/), which asks for your Google Analytics pageviews, current ad networks, and publishing platform, and requests read-only Analytics access. Sites built on Wix aren't eligible.

The [partner FAQ](https://www.shemedia.com/partner-network/faqs) treats the pageview figure as the standard. Smaller sites can get an individual look, based on content, consistency, and growth, but no lower number is published. Onboarding includes Google MCM and ads.txt.
