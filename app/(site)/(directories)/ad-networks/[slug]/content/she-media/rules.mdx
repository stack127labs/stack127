## What rules does SHE Media set?

SHE Media must be your only partner for mobile and desktop banner ads. Affiliate links and affiliate banners are allowed alongside it. Its support team works with you on an ad layout you're happy with.

Contracts run for 12 months and renew on their own. To stop the renewal, tell SHE Media at least 60 days before it. Leave the ad code in place until the end date SHE Media gives you. Its FAQ doesn't say how much notice SHE Media must give you.
