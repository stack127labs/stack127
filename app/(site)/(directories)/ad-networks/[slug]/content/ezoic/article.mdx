## Who is Ezoic a fit for now?

Large blogs, news sites, and web tools with a lot of monthly users and Google Analytics already set up. You usually don't need AdSense approval first.

## Can a small site still join Ezoic?

Rarely, as a new site. The Incubator is the only route below the rule, and with about 20 places a month, it isn't reliable access. One owner of a 3.5-month-old site with under 2,000 monthly visitors [said in 2026](https://www.reddit.com/r/Blogging/comments/1uv87ne) the Incubator turned them down.

## Do older Ezoic earnings reports still apply?

Not for joining. Most posts about small sites on Ezoic predate the February 2026 rule, and they count visits or pageviews, not monthly active users.

## Where can you go if Ezoic says no?

[Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) and [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric) take smaller sites, mostly with readers in the US and a few other English-speaking countries. [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum.
