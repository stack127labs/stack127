## Can a small site still join Ezoic?

Not as a new site on the main program. New sites below the traffic rule can apply to the Ezoic Incubator instead. Sites already earning with Ezoic before the rule changed can stay.

## Why do Ezoic's payment dates look different in different places?

Ezoic's legal terms and its payment help page describe the timing differently. The terms are the contract, so we show those. Ask Ezoic to confirm your schedule before you count on a date.

## What content does Ezoic accept?

Original content that follows AdSense policies, with visitors who are real people rather than bots.

## How can you check if a site uses Ezoic?

Look for Ezoic in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Ezoic.
