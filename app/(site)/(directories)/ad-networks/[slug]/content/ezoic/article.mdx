## Who is Ezoic a fit for now?

Large sites. New sites need a lot of monthly users to join the main program, and the content must be original, in a supported language, and follow AdSense policies. You don't need AdSense approval first for most sites.

## Can a small site still join Ezoic?

Not as a new site on the main program. New sites below the traffic rule can apply to the Ezoic Incubator instead. Sites already earning with Ezoic before the rule changed can stay.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

Google Analytics on your site, since Ezoic uses it to check your traffic. Your visitors need to be real people, not bots.

## How does Ezoic's share compare?

The terms give you at least 82% of net ad revenue, and the minimum payout is $20. That share is of net revenue, so don't compare it head to head with a share of gross. Ask Ezoic what's taken out before the split.

## Where can you go if Ezoic says no?

[Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) starts at just over 1,000 monthly sessions. [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric) and [Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) take smaller sites, if most readers are in the US or a few other English-speaking countries. [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum.

## How can you check if a site uses Ezoic?

Look for Ezoic in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
