## Who is NitroPay a fit for?

Publishers who want their money fast. NitroPay doesn't publish who qualifies, so contact its team to apply.

## How fast does NitroPay pay?

Faster than most networks on this list. Its docs describe payment about a week after the month, with no minimum beyond the transaction fee. Many managed networks here pay on Net 60, such as [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric) and [Playwire](/ad-networks/playwire).

## How long should you test NitroPay?

NitroPay recommends a testing period of a few months before you judge results. Compare its results with what you earned before, over a similar stretch of time.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your base share, how you'll be paid, and in which currency. Ask about contract length and how much control you keep over ad placement. None of these are in its public docs.

## Does NitroPay pay bonuses?

Its bonus page lists share increases for some agreements. That's on top of the base share, not the starting rate, so ask whether you'd qualify.

## How can you check if a site uses NitroPay?

Look for NitroPay in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
