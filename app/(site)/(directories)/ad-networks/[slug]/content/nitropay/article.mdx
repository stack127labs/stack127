## How fast does NitroPay pay?

Faster than most networks on this list. Its docs describe payment about a week after the month, with no minimum beyond the transaction fee.

## How long should you test NitroPay?

NitroPay recommends a testing period of a few months before you judge results.

## How can you check if a site uses NitroPay?

Look for NitroPay in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to NitroPay.
