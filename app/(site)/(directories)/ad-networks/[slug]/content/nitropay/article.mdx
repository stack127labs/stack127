## Who is NitroPay a fit for?

Gaming, enthusiast, and interactive sites with real traffic and clean content, run by someone comfortable adding ad tags and a Google Ad Manager link. App-only publishers should look elsewhere for now.

## How fast does NitroPay pay?

Faster than almost any network here. [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric) and [Playwire](/ad-networks/playwire) wait about two months. Fast pay helps a small publisher's cash flow, but it says nothing about how much you'll earn.

## What do publishers report earning?

Results vary a lot. One gaming download site [reported on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/1vep1yo/gaming-site-getting-133-ad-revenue-despite-600k/) in mid-2026 about 600,000 pageviews in 30 days, mostly from Europe, and $133 from NitroPay. That's one site with few US readers, not a typical rate.
