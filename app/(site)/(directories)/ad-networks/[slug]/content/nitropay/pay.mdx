## How does NitroPay pay?

NitroPay pays early each month, with no minimum as long as your earnings cover the transaction fee. It pays by local bank transfer in many countries, such as ACH in the US or SEPA in Europe, and by wire elsewhere. Its docs list which currency goes with some of these methods. The base share is not public.

A separate bonus page lists share increases for some agreements. That's a bonus, not the starting share.
