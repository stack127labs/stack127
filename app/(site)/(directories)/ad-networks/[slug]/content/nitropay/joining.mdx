NitroPay reviews how much traffic you have, where your visitors are, and what your site covers. It doesn't publish a traffic minimum or a list of accepted regions. Its guidelines bar adult, illegal, violent, and infringing content.

Once your site is accepted, you add Nitro's ad tags and link your Google Ad Manager account through Google MCM, Google's program for letting a partner sell your ads. Giving NitroPay access to your GA4 data can speed up review. You go live once enough ad buyers are set up for your site.
