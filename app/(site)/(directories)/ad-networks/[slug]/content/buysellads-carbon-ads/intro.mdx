Carbon Ads, run by BuySellAds, shows one small, quiet ad per page to developers and designers. If you've seen a tiny ad on a coding docs site or a dev tool's homepage, it was probably Carbon.

The catch is that Carbon has to be the only ad on your site. It's invite only, and it works best for English-language tech blogs, docs and open source projects that would rather show one clean ad than a page full of banners.
