## Who is Carbon Ads for?

Sites that want one ad built to match their design, instead of standard banner slots. Carbon Ads builds each unit to fit the publisher's site. BuySellAds reviews your traffic, how well its ads fit your readers, and whether the site is kept up. Demand is strongest for developer and design sites.

## What's the difference between BuySellAds and Carbon Ads?

BuySellAds is the company. Carbon Ads is one of its ad programs for publishers.

## How do you add Carbon Ads to your site?

Through the Carbon tag or its API. The API route suits you if you want to control exactly how the ad is drawn on your page.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share, since it isn't public. Ask too how much notice you need to leave. Carbon must be the only ad network on your site, so drop any others first.

## What's similar if Carbon Ads doesn't reply?

[EthicalAds](/ad-networks/ethicalads) also shows one ad that fits the page, and it publishes its share and payment minimum. It suits developer and open source sites.

## How can you check if a site uses Carbon Ads?

Look for Carbon Ads in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
