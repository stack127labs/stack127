## Who is Carbon Ads a fit for?

Sites about software development, web design, DevOps, or developer tools, read mostly in English. If you want one tidy ad and your readers would leave over a cluttered page, Carbon fits.

A general lifestyle blog, or a site that earns well from several ad networks today, is a poor match. Carbon replaces all of them.

## Can you run Carbon with AdSense or another network?

No. Carbon's exclusivity covers the whole site, not one ad slot. Compare what Carbon might pay against everything your current ads earn before you switch.

## What's the difference between BuySellAds and Carbon Ads?

BuySellAds is the company. Carbon Ads is its ad program for developer and design sites.

## When should you use the API instead of the tag?

When you want full control over how the ad looks on your page, such as in a docs theme or a single-page app.

## What's similar if Carbon doesn't have room?

[EthicalAds](/ad-networks/ethicalads) also shows one ad per page to developers, and it publishes its share and payment minimum.
