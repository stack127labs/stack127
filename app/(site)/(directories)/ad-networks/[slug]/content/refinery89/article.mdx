## Who is Refinery89 for?

Publishers who want one named contact for their ads. Each publisher works with a Publisher Success Manager. Refinery89 doesn't publish who qualifies, so contact its publisher team.

## When does Refinery89 pay?

It depends on your terms. Refinery89 has more than one payment schedule, so ask your Publisher Success Manager which one is yours. The minimum is €250 or $250 a month, so a small site may carry a balance for a while.

## What can you see in the Refinery89 dashboard?

Daily revenue, CPM, RPM, pageviews, fill rate, and viewability, plus invoices and support tickets. You can export your data and manage ads.txt there too.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share, how you're paid, and whether Refinery89 must be your only ad partner. Ask how long the contract runs and how much notice you need to leave.

## How can you check if a site uses Refinery89?

Look for Refinery89 in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
