## Who is Refinery89 a fit for?

Websites and app publishers that want a managed setup with a named contact, including local news and lifestyle publishers and groups with several sites.

## Is the payment minimum an entry rule?

No. It's how much you need to earn before a payout. Refinery89 has no published traffic or revenue rule for joining.

## What should you ask before you sign?

1. What's your share, and when are you paid?
2. Does the setup replace your current ads or add formats?
3. How long does the contract run, and what notice ends it?
