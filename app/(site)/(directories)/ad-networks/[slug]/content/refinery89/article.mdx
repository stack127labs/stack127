## When does Refinery89 pay?

It depends on your terms. Refinery89 has more than one payment schedule, so ask your Publisher Success Manager which one is yours.

## What can you see in the Refinery89 dashboard?

Daily revenue, CPM, RPM, pageviews, fill rate, and viewability, plus invoices and support tickets. You can export your data and manage ads.txt there too.

## How can you check if a site uses Refinery89?

Look for Refinery89 in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Refinery89.
