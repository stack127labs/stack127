## How does Google AdSense pay?

For ads on your content, you get 80% after the advertiser platform fee. When the ad comes from Google Ads, that works out to about 68% of what the advertiser pays. Other AdSense products have different shares.

Google pays once a month, usually between the 21st and 26th, when your balance passes the minimum and there's no hold on your account. The minimum is set for each currency, and payment methods depend on your country.
