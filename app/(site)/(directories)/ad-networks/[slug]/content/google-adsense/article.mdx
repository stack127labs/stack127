## Is AdSense a good first ad network?

For many new sites it's the easiest start. There's no traffic minimum, and you set it up yourself in your AdSense account. Help comes through the AdSense Help Center, so you place and tune ads on your own.

## What should you check before you apply?

Make sure your content is original and follows the AdSense Program Policies, and that you can edit your site's HTML to add the code. Check that AdSense, and a payment method you can use, are available in your country. Review usually takes a few days, but can take two to four weeks.

## When will you see your first payment?

Only once your balance passes the minimum. If you earn $20 a month, that's five months before your first payment. After that, Google pays once a month.

## Can you use AdSense with another network?

AdSense doesn't ask to be your only ad partner. Some managed networks do, such as [Newor Media](/ad-networks/newor-media)'s Elevate program and [Playwire](/ad-networks/playwire), so check their rules before you add one.

## Where can you go once you outgrow AdSense?

[Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) starts at 1,000 premium sessions a month. [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric)'s Propel program starts at 10,000 monthly pageviews and [Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) at 25,000. Both want a large share of readers in the US and a few other English-speaking countries. [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine) looks at yearly ad revenue instead of traffic.

## How can you check if a site uses Google AdSense?

Look for Google AdSense in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
