## Is AdSense a good first ad network?

For most new sites, yes. There's no traffic number to hit and no contract, and you control every ad yourself. The trade-off is that nobody tunes your ads for you.

## Why does AdSense reject new sites?

Google judges your content, not a traffic number. It looks for original, useful pages that follow its policies. One owner of a three-week-old calculator site [said in 2026](https://www.reddit.com/r/passive_income/comments/1tyq291) they were rejected twice, without a clear reason.

## When will you see your first payment?

Only once your balance passes the minimum. A site earning $20 a month waits about five months for its first payment.

## Can you use AdSense with another network?

Yes. Some managed networks want to be your only ad partner, such as [Newor Media](/ad-networks/newor-media)'s Elevate program and [Playwire](/ad-networks/playwire), so check their rules before you add one.

## Where can you go once you outgrow AdSense?

[Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) and [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric)'s Propel program take smaller sites with mostly US and other English-speaking readers. [Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) comes next. [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine) looks at yearly ad revenue instead of traffic.
