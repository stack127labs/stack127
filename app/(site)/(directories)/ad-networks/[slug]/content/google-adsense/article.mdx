## Is AdSense a good first ad network?

For many new sites it's the easiest start. There's no traffic minimum, and you set it up yourself in your AdSense account.

## Can you use AdSense with another network?

AdSense doesn't ask to be your only ad partner. Some managed networks do, so check their rules before you add one.

## Why do you earn less than the share Google shows?

The share applies after the advertiser platform fee. For ads bought through Google Ads, your cut of what the advertiser paid ends up lower than the headline share.

## How can you check if a site uses Google AdSense?

Look for Google AdSense in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Google AdSense.
