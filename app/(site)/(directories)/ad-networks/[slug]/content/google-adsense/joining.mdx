Sign up on the [AdSense start page](https://www.google.com/adsense/start/), after checking that your [country is open for sign-up](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13402307?hl=en).

No traffic number doesn't mean easy approval. Google reviews your content for originality, quality, and policy compliance, and it can turn down a new site that's thin or unfinished.
