## Who is Playwire a fit for?

Established sites and apps with original content, clean traffic, and readers in English-speaking countries. It suits a publisher ready to hand all ad slots to one partner.

## How much traffic do you need for Playwire?

At least 500,000 monthly pageviews, or 1,000 daily active users for an app. Its qualification guide gives other numbers, but those are advice on when Playwire pays off, not the entry rule. Quality matters too: it checks invalid traffic and your ad setup.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

1. HTTPS and a privacy policy on your site.
2. Room for at least three ads per device.
3. Invalid traffic at or below Playwire's limit.
4. A clean record with Google and major ad providers.

## Can you run other networks with Playwire?

No. Playwire says other ad network tags can't run with its script. Plan to move your ad slots over to it.

## What fits if you're below Playwire's traffic?

[Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) and [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric) take smaller sites with a large share of readers in the US and a few other English-speaking countries. [Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) starts at just over 1,000 monthly sessions.

## How can you check if a site uses Playwire?

Look for Playwire in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
