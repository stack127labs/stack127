## Who is Playwire a fit for?

Gaming sites, casual game portals, education and tool sites, entertainment, and travel, with original content and low invalid traffic. Apps and desktop games can apply too.

## Is 100,000 or 500,000 pageviews the real bar?

The FAQ's number is the stated rule. The 100,000 figure is Playwire's view of when it starts paying off. If you're between the two, ask, but don't expect approval.

## What do publishers report?

Mixed results near a million pageviews. In an [October 2026 Reddit thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/1wp5uz8/), one commenter with about a million monthly pageviews and 10% US traffic said Playwire worked well. Another, at a similar size with 25% US traffic, said they'd just been rejected. Neither gave the reason, so country share alone doesn't explain it.

## What fits if you're below Playwire's traffic?

[Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) and [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric) take smaller sites with most readers in the US and a few other English-speaking countries. [Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) starts at 1,000 Tier 1 sessions in 30 days.
