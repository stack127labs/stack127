## How much traffic do you need for Playwire?

Playwire's own pages give different answers, so check the Joining tab and ask its team. Quality matters too: it checks invalid traffic and your ad setup.

## Can you run other networks with Playwire?

No. Playwire says other ad network tags can't run with its script. Plan to move your ad slots over to it.

## Who decides where Playwire's ads go?

You do. Playwire recommends layouts, but publishers have the final say.

## How can you check if a site uses Playwire?

Look for Playwire in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Playwire.
