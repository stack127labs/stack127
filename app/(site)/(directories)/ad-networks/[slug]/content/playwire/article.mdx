## Who is Playwire a fit for?

Established sites and apps with original content and clean traffic, and some readers in the US. It suits a publisher ready to hand all ad slots to one partner.

## How much traffic do you need for Playwire?

Playwire's own pages give different answers, so check the Joining tab and ask its team. Quality matters too: it checks invalid traffic and your ad setup.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

1. HTTPS and a privacy policy on your site.
2. Room for at least three ads per device.
3. Invalid traffic at or below Playwire's limit.
4. A clean record with Google and major ad providers.

## Can you run other networks with Playwire?

No. Playwire says other ad network tags can't run with its script. Plan to move your ad slots over to it.

## What fits if you're below Playwire's traffic?

[Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) and [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric) take smaller sites with mostly US and a few other English-speaking readers. [Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) starts at just over 1,000 monthly sessions.

## How can you check if a site uses Playwire?

Look for Playwire in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
