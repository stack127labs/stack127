Playwire's pages disagree on traffic. Its FAQ asks for 500,000 monthly pageviews, or 1,000 daily active users for an app. Its qualification page says publishers are typically advised to have 100,000 pageviews and 20% US traffic.

You also need original content, invalid traffic at or below 7%, a privacy policy, HTTPS, and room for at least three ads per device. Apply through Playwire's onboarding form.
