Playwire's FAQ sets the traffic bar, with a separate one for apps. Its qualification guide gives other numbers, but those are advice on when Playwire pays off for you, not the entry rule. It looks for a good share of readers in English-speaking countries. More US traffic tends to earn more, but there's no US minimum.

You also need original content, invalid traffic at or below 7%, a privacy policy, HTTPS, and room for at least three ads per device. Apply through Playwire's onboarding form.
