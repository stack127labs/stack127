Playwire publishes two different traffic numbers for two different jobs. Its [FAQ](https://www.playwire.com/faq) sets the entry rule shown here, measured in Google Analytics. Its [qualification guide](https://www.playwire.com/qualification-criteria) suggests applying from about 100,000 monthly pageviews to get the full benefit. That's advice, not a confirmed lower entry rule.

The same guide links at least 20% US traffic to higher revenue. It's not a requirement. The FAQ also asks for a secure (HTTPS) privacy policy page and room for at least three highly viewable ads on each device.

The [apply page](https://www.playwire.com/apply) splits into websites, mobile apps, and desktop apps.
