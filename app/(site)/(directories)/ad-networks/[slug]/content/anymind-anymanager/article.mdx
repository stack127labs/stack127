## Who is AnyManager for?

Web and app publishers who want header bidding and pricing handled in one platform. AnyMind doesn't publish who qualifies, so contact its team.

## How does AnyMind set your share?

You agree on a fixed share before you start. Ask for it in writing, since AnyMind doesn't publish it.

## How can you check if a site uses AnyManager?

Look for AnyManager in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to AnyManager.
