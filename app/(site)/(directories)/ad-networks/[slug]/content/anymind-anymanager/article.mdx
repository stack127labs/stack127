## Who is AnyManager for?

Web and app publishers who want header bidding, price tuning, and analytics in one platform. AnyMind doesn't publish who qualifies, so contact its team.

## How does AnyMind set your share?

You agree on a fixed share before you start. AnyMind doesn't publish it, so ask for it in writing.

## What should you ask AnyMind before you sign?

1. What share do you keep, and is it the same for every ad format?
2. When are you paid, and what's the minimum?
3. Must AnyManager be your only ad partner?
4. How much notice do you need to give to leave?

## What if you want public terms first?

[Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum and publishes its share. [Ezoic](/ad-networks/ezoic) publishes a minimum share in its terms. [Sovrn](/ad-networks/sovrn) publishes its traffic bar and a $25 payment minimum.

## How can you check if a site uses AnyManager?

Look for AnyManager in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
