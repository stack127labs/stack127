## Who is AnyManager a fit for?

Web and app publishers in Asia, or with big Asian audiences, who want one partner for demand and analytics. Groups with several sites or apps get the most from that.

A small blog that wants to sign up today and see public pay terms won't find them here. [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) and [Ezoic](/ad-networks/ezoic) publish their share.

## What should you ask AnyMind before you sign?

1. What share do you keep, and is it the same for every ad format?
2. When are you paid, and what's the minimum?
3. Must AnyManager be your only ad partner?
4. How much notice ends the contract?
