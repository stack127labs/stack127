## Can you run APS with another ad network?

Yes, for UAM. It's built to run next to your other header bidding partners, so you don't have to drop them.

## How do you get access to APS?

Contact APS and ask. UAM is invitation only, and there's no public form. It suits sites that already use Google Ad Manager.

## What does the UAM fee mean for you?

APS takes its fee out of each bid before the auction runs. So the bids that compete for your ad space are already net of it.

## How can you check if a site uses Amazon Publisher Services?

Look for Amazon Publisher Services in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Amazon Publisher Services.
