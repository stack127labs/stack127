## Who is APS a fit for?

Established web publishers who run Google Ad Manager and header bidding, such as news and large content sites, and want more buyers competing. APS adds demand. It doesn't replace your main ad network.

If you don't use Ad Manager yet, start with [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) or a managed network that runs Ad Manager for you.

## How do you get a UAM invite?

Ask APS through the UAM page, and have your Ad Manager setup and current bidders ready to describe. Replies can be slow. One publisher reporting about 45 million monthly US pageviews [said on Reddit in 2022](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/v0ec6t) they'd heard nothing after about three weeks.

## What does the UAM fee mean for you?

The fee comes out of each bid before the auction. The bids competing for your ad space are already net of it. Compare final earnings, not bid prices, when you test UAM.

## Does the low minimum matter?

Yes for a test. Even a small first month of UAM earnings gets paid out instead of rolling over.
