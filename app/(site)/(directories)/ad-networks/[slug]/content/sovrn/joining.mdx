Create an account on the [Sovrn platform](https://platform.sovrn.com/account/signup), then start Ad Exchange onboarding. The traffic rule counts visits and must be strictly above the figure shown. Visits aren't the same as sessions or pageviews, so check how your analytics counts them.

The [approval guide](https://knowledge.sovrn.com/kb/what-is-required-for-sovrn-to-approve-my-property) asks for content published in the past four months, privacy and brand safety policies, and an audience that isn't children or only under-16s. You need an operational business domain and ads.txt ownership details, and resellers also need sellers.json.
