## Is Sovrn a fit for a small site?

It can be. The Ad Exchange doesn't set a traffic minimum, and its $25 payment minimum is lower than [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense)'s $100.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

A working domain, an ad system ID, and an ads.txt file you keep up to date. Those are the items Sovrn's approval guide lists.

## What's the difference between Sovrn's Ad Exchange and Sovrn Commerce?

The Ad Exchange sells ad space on your site. Sovrn Commerce is a separate affiliate product with its own payment terms. The details on this page are for the Ad Exchange.

## How does Sovrn pay?

On Net 45, in US dollars, with some options to get local currency. Pick a method other than wire if you want the lowest minimum.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share, and whether there's an exclusivity rule or contract length. Neither is on the approval or payment pages.

## How can you check if a site uses Sovrn?

Look for Sovrn in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
