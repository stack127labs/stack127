## Is Sovrn a fit for a small site?

Not for the Ad Exchange, below its visit rule. Above it, Sovrn can add demand to your current setup rather than replace it.

## What's the difference between the Ad Exchange and Sovrn Commerce?

The Ad Exchange sells ad space on your site. Sovrn Commerce is a separate affiliate product. A Commerce account doesn't mean Ad Exchange approval.

## What can delay approval?

Outside checks and manual review. Sovrn gives no fixed time. Stale content, missing privacy pages, or ads.txt records that don't match your account can all hold it up.
