## How does Opti Digital pay?

Opti Digital works on a revenue share, but the rate is not public. When you're paid depends on your setup.

With Managed Inventory, Opti Digital pays on Net 45. With Managed Account, it pays Net 60 for the demand it handles, and Google pays its own revenue on its 25-day cycle. Your order sets the exact terms.

The minimum, methods, and currency are not public.
