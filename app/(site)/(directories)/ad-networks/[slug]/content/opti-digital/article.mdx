## Who is Opti Digital a fit for?

Publishers and media groups that already use Google Ad Manager and want a partner to run it or add demand. A small blog without Ad Manager isn't the target.

## Managed Inventory or Managed Account?

Managed Inventory hands your ad space to Opti Digital, which collects and pays you. Managed Account keeps your own Google relationship, so Google pays you directly and Opti Digital pays for its share of demand. It suits a publisher who wants Google revenue to keep arriving directly.

## Can Demand Hub sit next to your current setup?

It's built to add demand, as in Opti Digital's [1XL case study](https://optidigital.com/resources/case-studies/how-1xl-unlocked-incremental-premium-demand-with-opti-digital/). Ask how it overlaps with your existing bidders before you switch it on.
