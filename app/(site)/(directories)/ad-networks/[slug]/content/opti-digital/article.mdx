## Who is Opti Digital for?

Publishers who want a partner to run Google Ad Manager. Its fit rules aren't public, so ask its team.

## What's the difference between Managed Inventory and Managed Account?

Both are ways Opti Digital runs your Google Ad Manager setup. They're separate commercial deals with different payment timing. With Managed Account, Google pays its share of revenue on its own schedule, so your money comes in two streams.

## Which setup gets you paid sooner?

Managed Inventory pays on Net 45. Managed Account pays Net 60 for the demand Opti Digital handles. Your order sets the exact terms.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share, the payment minimum, and how you're paid. Ask whether Opti Digital must be your only ad partner, and how much notice you need to leave. None of this is on its help pages.

## How can you check if a site uses Opti Digital?

Look for Opti Digital in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
