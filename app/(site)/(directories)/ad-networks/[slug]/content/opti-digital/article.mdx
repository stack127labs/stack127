## What's the difference between Managed Inventory and Managed Account?

Both are ways Opti Digital runs your Google Ad Manager setup. They're separate commercial deals with different payment timing. With Managed Account, Google pays its share of revenue on its own schedule.

## Who is Opti Digital for?

Publishers who want a partner to run Google Ad Manager. Its fit rules aren't public, so ask its team.

## How can you check if a site uses Opti Digital?

Look for Opti Digital in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Opti Digital.
