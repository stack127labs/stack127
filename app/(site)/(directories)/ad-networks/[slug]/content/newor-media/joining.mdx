## Who can join Newor Media?

Elevate, the entry program, has no stated pageview minimum. Your site must be in English, have a navigation bar, follow Google's ad policies, and have room for at least two ads per page. You need Google Ad Manager. Approval usually takes 24 to 48 hours.

The larger programs set traffic rules: Catapult at 60,000 US pageviews a month, and Premier at 420,000.
