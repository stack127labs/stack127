Elevate, the entry program, has no stated pageview minimum. Your site must be in English, have a navigation bar, follow Google's ad policies, and have room for at least two ads per page. You need Google Ad Manager. Newor also looks at where your visitors come from, but doesn't set a country mix.

The stated wait is how soon Newor usually replies, not a promise of approval. The larger programs, Catapult and Premier, set US pageview minimums.
