## Which Newor Media program fits your site?

Elevate if your site is small or new, since it has no pageview floor. Catapult and Premier are for sites with more US traffic.

## Does no minimum mean easy approval?

No. Manual review still turns sites down. In October 2026, one publisher [wrote on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/1vkm7no/skip_newor_media_by_any_means_possible/) that two blogs of about 10,000 monthly sessions each were rejected by Newor after AdSense and Journey accepted them, and another commenter reported a rejection at about 40,000. Neither gave a reason, so treat these as signs the review is real, not a hidden cutoff.

## What should you have ready before you apply to Elevate?

1. An English-language site with a navigation bar.
2. Room for at least two ads on each page.
3. Your own Google Ad Manager account.
4. A plan to remove other display networks.

## Can you run other ads with Newor Media?

Not other display networks. If you run [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) or another network now, it has to come off when Newor starts.

## What if you don't want an exclusive deal?

Compare [Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) and [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric), which also serve smaller sites. Ask each one which existing ads must come off before you sign.
