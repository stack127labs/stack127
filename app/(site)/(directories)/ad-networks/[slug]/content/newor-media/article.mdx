## Which Newor Media program fits your site?

Start with Elevate if your site is new, since it has no stated traffic bar. Catapult and Premier are for sites with more US traffic.

## Why are there two payment terms for Newor Media?

The FAQ and the platform agreement disagree. The signed agreement is your contract, so go by that, and ask Newor to confirm your dates.

## Can you run other ads with Newor Media?

Not other display networks on Elevate, which is exclusive. Read the agreement for your program before you add partners.

## How can you check if a site uses Newor Media?

Look for Newor Media in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Newor Media.
