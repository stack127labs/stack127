## Which Newor Media program fits your site?

Start with Elevate if your site is new, since it has no stated traffic bar. Catapult and Premier are for sites with more US traffic.

## What should you have ready before you apply to Elevate?

1. An English-language site with a navigation bar.
2. Room for at least two ads on each page.
3. Your own Google Ad Manager account.
4. Content that follows Google's ad policies.

Review usually takes 24 to 48 hours.

## Can you run other ads with Newor Media?

Not other display networks on Elevate, which is exclusive. If you run [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) or another network now, plan to remove it. Read the agreement for your program before you add partners.

## How do you leave Newor Media?

The platform agreement starts with a 90-day term, then renews. Give written notice at least 14 days before the term ends, or you roll into the next one.

## What if you don't want an exclusive deal?

[Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) and [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric) also take smaller sites, and neither states an exclusivity rule on its public pages. Ask to be sure.

## How can you check if a site uses Newor Media?

Look for Newor Media in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
