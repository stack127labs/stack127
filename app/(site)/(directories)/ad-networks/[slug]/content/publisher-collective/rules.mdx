## What rules does Publisher Collective set?

The partner terms give Publisher Collective the exclusive right to sell your ads worldwide. You can't change its ad tags, and you pass advertising leads to it.

You make requested site changes within seven days, and failing to reply within seven days can get the deal suspended. After a trial period, leaving takes three months' written notice. Weapons, porn, hateful, discriminatory, and indecent content isn't allowed.
