## Who is Publisher Collective a fit for?

Sites in technology, video games, hobbies and casual games, education, sports, or health. Sites in other niches need far more traffic to be considered.

## What happened to Snigel?

Snigel and Publisher Collective combined in 2025. Publisher Collective is the name publishers deal with, and Snigel is the technology behind it. Many sites still name Snigel's domain, snigelweb.com, in their ads.txt files, so we count them here.

## What do you give up?

Your ad sales. The exclusive representation clause, the ban on changing tags, and the duty to pass on leads matter most if you sell any ads yourself. The three-month notice period means switching later takes planning.
