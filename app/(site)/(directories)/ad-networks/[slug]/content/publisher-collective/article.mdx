## Who is Publisher Collective a fit for?

Sites that want a team to sell their ads and are happy to hand over the ad tags. There's no published traffic minimum, but you must own or have rights to your content.

## What happened to Snigel?

Snigel and Publisher Collective merged in February 2025. Publisher Collective now sells under its own name and keeps Snigel as its ad technology. Many sites still name Snigel's domain, snigelweb.com, in their ads.txt files. We count those sites under Publisher Collective.

## When does Publisher Collective pay you?

After the advertiser pays Publisher Collective. The partner terms don't set a fixed interval, so ask how long that usually takes. The €500 minimum is high, so a small site may wait several months for a first payment.

## What do you give up with Publisher Collective?

You can't edit its ad tags, and you may need to send advertiser leads its way. Read those terms closely if you also sell ads directly.

## What do you need to keep up with after you join?

Replies to Publisher Collective. You may need to update site materials within seven days, and the deal can be suspended or ended if you don't respond.

## How can you check if a site uses Publisher Collective?

Look for Publisher Collective or Snigel in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
