## When does Publisher Collective pay you?

After the advertiser pays Publisher Collective. The partner terms don't set a fixed interval, so ask how long that usually takes.

## What do you give up with Publisher Collective?

You can't edit its ad tags, and you may need to send advertiser leads its way. Read those terms closely if you also sell ads directly.

## What happened to Snigel?

Snigel and Publisher Collective merged in February 2025. Publisher Collective now sells under its own name and keeps Snigel as its ad technology. Many sites still name Snigel's domain, snigelweb.com, in their ads.txt files. We count those sites under Publisher Collective.

## How can you check if a site uses Publisher Collective?

Look for Publisher Collective or Snigel in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Publisher Collective.
