## Who is Revcontent a fit for?

News and editorial sites that want to test native recommendations on top of their display ads. It's not a replacement for a display network.

## Which payment method should you pick?

In the US, ACH. Outside the US, PayPal's minimum is far lower than a wire.

## How does Revcontent compare with Taboola and MGID?

[Taboola](/ad-networks/taboola) must be your only recommendation service while your deal runs. [MGID](/ad-networks/mgid) publishes a traffic rule. Revcontent has none but reviews every domain.
