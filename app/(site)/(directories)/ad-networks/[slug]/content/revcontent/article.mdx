## Who is Revcontent a fit for?

Sites that want content recommendation widgets and native ads. Revcontent doesn't publish a traffic minimum or region rule, but your site must follow its publisher terms and content policy.

## How is your Revcontent share set?

You negotiate it. Revcontent doesn't publish a standard rate, so compare its offer with what you earn now.

## What's the payment minimum at Revcontent?

It depends. The terms set one floor, and each payment method sets its own. Check your dashboard or order for the one that applies to you.

## Which payment method should you pick?

In the US, ACH has the lowest minimum. Outside the US, PayPal's minimum is far lower than an international wire.

## How does Revcontent compare with Taboola and MGID?

[Taboola](/ad-networks/taboola) must be your only recommendation service while your deal runs. [MGID](/ad-networks/mgid) publishes a regional offer with a share and pay day. Revcontent negotiates each deal.

## How can you check if a site uses Revcontent?

Look for Revcontent in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
