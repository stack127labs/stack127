PubPower's traffic guideline is approximate, not a strict cutoff. It doesn't publish which regions it accepts.

Your site must follow PubPower's content policy, which bars or restricts sexual, explicit, violent, illegal, gambling, and infringing content. Pop-ups and redirects aren't allowed.

To start, you add PubPower's ads.txt lines and install its ad tag. Its quick-start guide and FAQ give different review times, and weekends can take longer.
