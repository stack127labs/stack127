Create an account on the [PubPower registration page](https://apps.pubpower.io/register), add your domain, and wait for review. You add PubPower's ads.txt lines and install its ad tag.

The traffic figure comes from a [help page](https://help.pubpower.io/article/website-requirements) last updated around 2023, and it says "around", so treat it as old guidance, not a firm rule. The [content policy](https://help.pubpower.io/article/content-policies) bars or restricts sexual, violent, illegal, gambling, and infringing content, and pop-ups and redirects aren't allowed.
