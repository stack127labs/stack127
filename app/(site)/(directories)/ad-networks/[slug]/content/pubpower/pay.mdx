## How does PubPower pay?

If you earn more than $3,000 in a month, PubPower pays on Net 0. Otherwise it pays on Net 30.

The minimum depends on the method: $50 for Payoneer, $100 for PayPal, $100 to $1,000 for crypto, and $1,000 for a bank transfer. On the Essential package, your share is set for you and not published. On Advanced, bidders you bring yourself carry no revenue share. The payout currency is not public.
