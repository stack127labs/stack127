## Why do some PubPower sites get paid sooner?

PubPower pays sites above its monthly revenue line as soon as the month ends, with no waiting period. Other sites wait for the standard term.

## Which PubPower payment method should you pick?

Pick one with a minimum your earnings clear. Payoneer has the lowest minimum, and bank transfer the highest.

## How can you check if a site uses PubPower?

Look for PubPower in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to PubPower.
