## Who is PubPower a fit for?

Small and mid-size sites, especially anime and entertainment, that want fast payment and a simple setup. Sites with their own bidder deals can use the Advanced package instead.

## Can you run AdSense alongside PubPower?

Maybe. One owner of a seven-month-old anime site [wrote on Reddit in 2024](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/1dpv6e3/) that they ran both, and that PubPower's CPM came in below AdSense. Confirm it's allowed on your package.

## Which payment method should you pick?

One with a minimum your earnings clear. A small site on bank transfer could wait months for a first payment.
