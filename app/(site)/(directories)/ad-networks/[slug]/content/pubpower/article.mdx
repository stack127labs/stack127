## Who is PubPower a fit for?

Publishers who want fast payment. Sites that earn more than $3,000 a month are paid as soon as the month ends. PubPower doesn't publish who qualifies, so contact its team.

## Why do some PubPower sites get paid sooner?

PubPower pays sites above its monthly revenue line as soon as the month ends, with no waiting period. Other sites wait for the standard Net 30 term, which is still quicker than many managed networks.

## Which PubPower payment method should you pick?

Pick one with a minimum your earnings clear. Payoneer has the lowest minimum, and bank transfer the highest. A small site on bank transfer could wait months for a payment.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share, the payout currency, and whether PubPower must be your only ad partner. Ask how much notice you need to leave. None of these are public.

## How can you check if a site uses PubPower?

Look for PubPower in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
