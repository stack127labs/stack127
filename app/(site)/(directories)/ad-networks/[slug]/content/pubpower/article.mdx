## Who is PubPower a fit for?

Publishers who want fast payment. Sites that earn more than $3,000 a month are paid on Net 0, in the payment window early the next month. PubPower looks for around 100,000 visitors a month, and its review usually takes a few days.

## Why do some PubPower sites get paid sooner?

PubPower pays sites above its monthly revenue line on Net 0, in its payment window from the 1st to the 20th of the next month. Other sites wait for the standard Net 30 term, which is still quicker than many managed networks.

## Which PubPower payment method should you pick?

Pick one with a minimum your earnings clear. Payoneer has the lowest minimum, and bank transfer the highest. A small site on bank transfer could wait months for a payment.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your exact share, the payout currency, and whether PubPower must be your only ad partner. Ask how much notice you need to leave. None of these are public.

## How can you check if a site uses PubPower?

Look for PubPower in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
