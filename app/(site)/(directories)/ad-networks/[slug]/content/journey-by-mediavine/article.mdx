## How is Journey different from Mediavine?

Journey is for sites still growing, with a much lower bar to join. [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine)'s main program is for sites that already earn steady ad revenue. Journey's pay and rules aren't public, so don't assume they match Mediavine's.

## What does Mediavine check when you apply to Journey?

Your traffic sources, the countries your visitors come from, and who your readers are. It also wants original, brand-safe content made by people.

## How can you check if a site uses Journey by Mediavine?

Look for Journey by Mediavine in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Journey by Mediavine.
