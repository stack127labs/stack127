## Who is Journey a fit for?

Bloggers and small content sites that have started to get steady traffic. The bar is 1,000 premium sessions a month. Your content has to be original, made by people, updated, and brand safe.

## How is Journey different from Mediavine?

Journey is for sites still growing, with a much lower bar to join. [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine)'s main program is for sites that already earn steady ad revenue. Journey pays a 70% share, below the main program's tiers, on the same Net 65 timing. Its other rules aren't public, so don't assume they match Mediavine's.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

1. The Grow plugin installed and running for at least 30 days.
2. A clean record with Google, with no active AdSense or Ad Exchange issues.
3. A domain with about four months of history, which Journey says its ad partners expect.
4. A clear picture of your traffic: where it comes from, which countries, and who reads you.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Journey's payment minimum and payment methods aren't public. Ask for them, and ask whether Journey must be your only ad partner.

## What else fits a site this size?

[Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum. [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric)'s Propel program starts at 10,000 monthly pageviews for English sites.

## How can you check if a site uses Journey by Mediavine?

Look for Journey by Mediavine in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
