## Who is PubFuture a fit for?

HTML5 game sites and other gaming publishers past the pageview floor. Its public examples are about game sites, so a general blog has less to go on.

## What do PubFuture's case studies show?

A 2025 [case study about an HTML5 game site](https://blog.pubfuture.com/the-secret-to-400-more-revenue-for-h5-game-sites-pubfutures-winning-formula/1007/) reports a large revenue increase for one game site. It's PubFuture's own report about one site, not what yours should expect.

## What else should game sites compare?

[Playwire](/ad-networks/playwire), [Venatus](/ad-networks/venatus), and [NitroPay](/ad-networks/nitropay) also focus on gaming publishers.
