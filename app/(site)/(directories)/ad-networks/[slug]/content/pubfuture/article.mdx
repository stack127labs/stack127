## Is PubFuture a fit for a small site?

It may be. PubFuture doesn't publish a traffic minimum, so smaller sites can apply.

## Can you control which ads PubFuture shows?

Yes. PubFuture says you can block ads you don't want.

## How can you check if a site uses PubFuture?

Look for PubFuture in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to PubFuture.
