## Is PubFuture a fit for a small site?

Not a very small one. A 2025 PubFuture case study names 100,000 pageviews a month as its minimum. Its current pages don't list entry rules, so ask through its sign-up page if you're unsure.

## How does PubFuture pay compare?

Net 30 with a $50 minimum, which is quicker than the Net 60 many managed networks use. You can be paid by PayPal, Payoneer, bank transfer, or Bitcoin.

## Can you control which ads PubFuture shows?

Yes. PubFuture says you can block ads you don't want.

## How quickly will you get help?

PubFuture says its ad operations team replies within 24 hours.

## What should you ask before you sign?

Your share, whether PubFuture must be your only ad partner, and how much notice you need to leave. None of these are public.

## How can you check if a site uses PubFuture?

Look for PubFuture in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
