## Who is Concert for?

Established media groups with news, culture, or entertainment brands that advertisers already know. Membership is a sales partnership between companies.

A solo blog or small niche site isn't the audience. If you want an ad network you can apply to today, [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum, and [Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) and [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine) publish their entry rules.
