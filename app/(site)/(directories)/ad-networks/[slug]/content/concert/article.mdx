## What is Concert?

Concert is a publisher-led advertising marketplace run by Vox Media. The terms on its site are for advertisers who buy ads through it, not for publishers.

## Can any site join Concert?

Concert doesn't publish publisher rules or an application form. Contact its team to ask.

## What should you ask Concert before you sign?

Ask for your share, when you're paid, the payment minimum, and whether Concert must be your only ad partner. Ask who decides where ads go and how much notice you need to leave. None of this is public, so get it in writing.

## What if you want an open application instead?

Pick a network that publishes its entry rules. [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum. [Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) and [Mediavine](/ad-networks/mediavine) publish their entry rules, share, and pay day.

## How can you check if a site uses Concert?

Look for Concert in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
