## What is Concert?

Concert is a publisher-led advertising marketplace run by Vox Media. The terms on its site are for advertisers who buy ads through it.

## Can any site join Concert?

Concert doesn't publish publisher rules or an application form. Contact its team to ask.

## How can you check if a site uses Concert?

Look for Concert in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Concert.
