## How does Clickio pay?

Clickio bills monthly and pays around the 25th to 30th of the month payment is due. The help page gives one timing for everyone. The publisher agreement also offers a faster option, but only for demand partners that pay that fast. The minimum depends on how you're paid, and some methods can pay in your local currency.

There's no fixed share. Your commission depends on the rates and events each advertiser picks.
