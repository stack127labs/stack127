## Is Clickio a fit for a site outside the US?

It can be. Clickio sets its traffic bar by visitor region, with rules for readers across Europe, Latin America, and elsewhere. The bar is higher outside its main markets.

## What if your traffic is below Clickio's bar?

Clickio may still take your site if it already earns enough from ads. Revenue counts as well as pageviews, so mention your earnings when you apply.

## Can you keep other ad partners with Clickio?

Clickio's entry rules don't say it must be your only partner. Confirm what your agreement says before you add others.

## How can you check if a site uses Clickio?

Look for Clickio in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Clickio.
