## Is Clickio a fit for a site outside the US?

Yes, more than most managed networks. Clickio publishes separate guides for Italy, Spain, the rest of Europe, Portugal, Brazil, Colombia, Mexico, and the rest of the world. The guide rises outside the main English-speaking markets, so check the row for where most of your readers live.

## What if your traffic is below Clickio's guide?

Lead with your ad revenue. If you already earn enough from ads, Clickio may take the site anyway. Have a few months of earnings reports ready, since it can ask for three in a row.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

1. GA4 access, or a six-month traffic report.
2. An ads.txt file you can edit.
3. An IAB-approved consent tool on your site.
4. Original or licensed content that follows AdSense policies.

## Where else can a global site go?

[Publift](/ad-networks/publift) also looks at ad revenue first. [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum. [Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) and [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric) want most readers in a few English-speaking countries.
