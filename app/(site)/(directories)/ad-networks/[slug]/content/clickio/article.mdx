## Is Clickio a fit for a site outside the US?

It can be. Clickio sets its traffic bar by visitor region, with rules for readers across Europe, Latin America, and elsewhere. The bar is lowest for the UK, France, the US, Canada, and Australia. Sites in sanctioned countries can't join.

## What if your traffic is below Clickio's bar?

Clickio may still take your site if it earns enough from ads. Revenue counts as well as pageviews, so mention your earnings when you apply. Keep in mind the earnings rule can also apply after you join, not only at the start.

## What should you have ready before you apply?

1. GA4 access, or a six-month traffic report if Clickio can't verify your traffic another way.
2. An ads.txt file you can edit.
3. An IAB-approved consent tool on your site.
4. Content you own or have rights to, that follows AdSense policies.

## Can you keep other ad partners with Clickio?

Clickio's entry rules don't say it must be your only partner. Confirm what your agreement says before you add others.

## Where else can a smaller or global site go?

[Publift](/ad-networks/publift) looks at ad revenue first and works best with most readers in the Americas, Europe, or Australia and New Zealand. [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum. Networks like [Raptive](/ad-networks/raptive) and [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric) want most readers in a few English-speaking countries.

## How can you check if a site uses Clickio?

Look for Clickio in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
