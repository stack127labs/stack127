The traffic Clickio wants depends on where your visitors are. Sites with readers in the UK, France, the US, Canada, or Australia have the lowest bar, and other regions need more. A smaller site can still qualify if it earns enough from ads.

Your content must be yours or licensed, and it must follow AdSense policies. Adult, shocking, and racially intolerant content isn't allowed. Clickio also lists sanctioned countries it can't work with, including Cuba, Iran, Syria, Russia, and Sudan.

You'll add Clickio's ads.txt lines and use an IAB-approved consent tool. Clickio checks your traffic through GA4 or another independent source. If it can't, it asks for a six-month traffic report instead.
