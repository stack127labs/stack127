## Who can join Clickio?

The traffic Clickio wants depends on where your visitors are. Sites with readers in the UK, France, the US, Canada, or Australia have the lowest bar, and other regions need more. A smaller site can still qualify if it earns enough from ads.

You need six months of history, original content you have rights to, and good standing with Google's policies. Adult, shocking, and racially intolerant content isn't allowed. Clickio also lists sanctioned countries it can't work with, including Cuba, Iran, Syria, Russia, and Sudan.
