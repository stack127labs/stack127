## Is EthicalAds a fit for your site?

It suits developer and open source sites that want one clean ad instead of many. It won't fit a page that needs several ad slots, because EthicalAds must be the only ad on that page.

## Can you run EthicalAds with another network?

Yes, as long as they never share a page. EthicalAds lets you split your visitors with another network, or set a fallback ad.

## Where should the EthicalAds ad go?

Above the fold, on both desktop and mobile. That's what the policy asks for, along with a minimum click-through rate.

## How can you check if a site uses EthicalAds?

Look for EthicalAds in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to EthicalAds.
