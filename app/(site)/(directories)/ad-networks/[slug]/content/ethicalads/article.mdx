## Is EthicalAds a fit for your site?

It suits developer and open source sites that want one clean ad instead of many. It won't fit a page that needs several ad slots, because EthicalAds must be the only ad on that page.

## What should you check before you apply?

Make sure you have a spot for the ad above the fold, on both desktop and mobile. EthicalAds also expects a minimum click-through rate, so a spot readers actually see matters. There's no traffic minimum.

## Can you run EthicalAds with another network?

Yes, as long as they never share a page. EthicalAds lets you split your visitors with another network, or set a fallback ad.

## How does EthicalAds pay compare with AdSense?

You keep 70%, with a $50 minimum. [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) shows 80%, but that's after its platform fee, and it works out to about 68% for Google Ads demand. Its minimum is $100 in US dollars.

If your project already takes donations, EthicalAds can pay you through OpenCollective or GitHub Sponsors.

## What's similar to EthicalAds?

[Carbon Ads](/ad-networks/buysellads-carbon-ads) also runs one ad built to fit the page. Its pay terms aren't public, so you'd need to ask.

## How can you check if a site uses EthicalAds?

Look for EthicalAds in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
