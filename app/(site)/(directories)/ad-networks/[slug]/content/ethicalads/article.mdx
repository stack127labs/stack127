## Is EthicalAds a fit for your site?

Yes if your readers are developers and you want one clean ad, not many. It won't fit a page that needs several ad slots, or a general site with few technical readers.

## Can you apply below the traffic minimum?

You can ask, but don't count on it. EthicalAds considers exceptions for strong developer sites that are close or growing fast, and it decides each one itself.

## How does the share compare with AdSense?

[Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) shows a higher headline share, but that comes after its platform fee, and it works out lower for Google Ads demand. EthicalAds earns from fewer ad slots, so compare your total, not the percentages.

## What's similar to EthicalAds?

[Carbon Ads](/ad-networks/buysellads-carbon-ads) also shows one ad per page to developers. Its exclusivity covers your whole site, not one page, and it doesn't publish your share.
