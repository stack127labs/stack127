## Is PubNation still running?

Not on its own. Mediavine's help center says the migration to Mediavine is complete and PubNation is no longer offered. Some sites still name PubNation in their ads.txt files, so we keep listing them.

## Does a PubNation acceptance count with Mediavine?

Not for a new application. A former PubNation site should check which Mediavine program its account now sits in. Earlier acceptance doesn't prove you'd qualify for Mediavine today.

## What kind of sites did PubNation take?

Gaming, sports, tech, and entertainment sites, by its own description. One owner of a nearly nine-year-old gaming blog with 30,000 to 40,000 monthly sessions [said on Reddit in 2026](https://www.reddit.com/r/Blogging/comments/1s0ly5b/) they'd been accepted, earning about $621 the month before. That's history, not a guide to applying now.
