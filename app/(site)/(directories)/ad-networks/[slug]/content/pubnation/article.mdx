## How do you join PubNation?

PubNation doesn't publish how to apply. Contact the company through its website.

## What should you ask PubNation before you sign?

Ask for the share, when you'd be paid, the payment minimum, and whether it must be your only ad partner. Ask how much notice you'd need to leave.

## How can you check if a site uses PubNation?

Look for PubNation in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to PubNation.
