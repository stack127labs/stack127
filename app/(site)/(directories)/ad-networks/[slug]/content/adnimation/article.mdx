## Is Adnimation a fit for a small site?

Probably not the managed service, since it asks for substantial traffic. Adnimation's MAP product has no pageview minimum, but it's a separate program with its own terms.

## Can you leave Adnimation?

Yes. There's no lock-in contract, but you need to give notice. Ask how much before you sign, since it isn't public.

## What ads will Adnimation run on your site?

Display, video, and native or recommendation ads, with header bidding behind them. You can ask to change where they sit on your pages.

## How can you check if a site uses Adnimation?

Look for Adnimation in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker). We list a site here only when its current ads.txt or live ad setup points to Adnimation.
