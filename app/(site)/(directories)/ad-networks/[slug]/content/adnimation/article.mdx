## Who is Adnimation a fit for?

Original-content sites with a large audience: news, automotive, and other niches Adnimation names among its customers. Most of its customers have been US-based, including sites in other languages that serve US readers.

## Should a small site try MAP?

It's the route meant for you. MAP has no pageview floor, so a smaller site can apply and get reviewed. Its terms differ from the managed service, so don't count on Net 35 or the full team.

If MAP says no, [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum.

## How fast does Adnimation pay?

Faster than many managed services. Net 35 lands each month's money about five weeks after it ends, where [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric) and [Playwire](/ad-networks/playwire) use Net 60.
