## Who is Adnimation a fit for?

Sites with steady traffic that want display, video, and native ads run for them. Adnimation asks for substantial traffic but doesn't give a number, so contact its team if you're unsure.

It's not a fit for gambling, porn, or drug sites. Those are restricted.

## Is Adnimation a fit for a small site?

Probably not the managed service. Adnimation's MAP product has no pageview minimum, but it's a separate program with its own terms. Don't assume the managed service's pay terms apply to MAP.

For a small site, [Google AdSense](/ad-networks/google-adsense) has no traffic minimum, and [Journey by Mediavine](/ad-networks/journey-by-mediavine) starts at just over 1,000 monthly sessions.

## How fast does Adnimation pay?

Faster than many managed services. Net 35 means each month's money arrives about five weeks after it ends. Many networks on this list pay on Net 60, such as [Monumetric](/ad-networks/monumetric) and [Playwire](/ad-networks/playwire).

## Can you leave Adnimation?

Yes. There's no lock-in contract, but you need to give notice. Ask how much before you sign, since it isn't public.

## How quickly will you get help?

Adnimation says it replies in under two hours on average. You can reach it by email, phone, WhatsApp, Telegram, or Skype, which helps if something breaks on a busy day.

## How can you check if a site uses Adnimation?

Look for Adnimation in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our [ad network checker](/ad-network-checker).
