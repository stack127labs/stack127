## Are status codes on my domain a bad sign?

Usually not. A healthy domain normally carries at least one, and  `clientTransferProhibited` is the default lock most registrars apply precisely because it protects you from an unauthorised transfer. Seeing it is a good sign, not a problem to fix.

The ones actually worth acting on are the two hold codes, anything starting with `server`, and the lifecycle codes that mean a domain is on its way out. Everything else is either informational or something that clears on its own.
