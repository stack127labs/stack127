## Are status codes on my domain a bad sign?

Usually not. Every domain carries at least one, and `clientTransferProhibited` is a lock most registrars turn on by default to stop unauthorized transfers.

Look into these:

- `clientHold` or `serverHold`, because the site is offline.
- Any code starting with `server`, because only the registry can remove it, so find out why it's there.
- `autoRenewPeriod`, `redemptionPeriod`, or `pendingDelete`, because the domain is close to being lost.
