## What is the difference between client and server status codes?

This is the useful split. Codes starting with `client` were set by your registrar, which means there is usually a switch in your account that turns them off. Codes starting with `server` were set by the registry that runs the ending, and no amount of clicking in your account will move them. If you are stuck, that prefix tells you whether to look in your own settings or pick up the phone.

`client…` → your registrar, usually yours to change

`server…` → the registry, not yours to change
