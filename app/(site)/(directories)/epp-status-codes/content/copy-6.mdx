## Related tools

The [registration lookup](/domain-lookup) shows every status on a domain alongside the rest of its record. If you are specifically trying to move a domain, the  [transfer readiness check](/transfer-readiness)  works out which of these are actually in your way, and for the life cycle codes the [expiry checker](/domain-expiry) lays out the stages in order.

ICANN also publishes  [its own reference for these codes](https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/epp-status-codes-2014-06-16-en) , which is worth reading if you want the formal definitions rather than the practical ones.
