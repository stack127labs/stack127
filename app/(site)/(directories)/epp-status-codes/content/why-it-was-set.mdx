## Why was this status code set on my domain?

Why it was set. `server transfer prohibited` could be a trademark dispute, a court order, a registry security action, or a lock you asked for yourself and forgot about. The code carries no reason and the reason is not published anywhere public. Your registrar can usually find out.

Registries occasionally publish codes that are entirely their own invention and appear in no standard. Those are shown exactly as the registry returned them, marked as having no explanation available, rather than being matched to something plausible-looking. If you hit one, the registry that runs the ending is the only place it is documented.
