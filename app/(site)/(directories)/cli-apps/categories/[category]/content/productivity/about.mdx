## How do you choose a terminal productivity tool?

Start with the thing you do most in the terminal, then pick a tool for that.

- **Shell support.** Jumpers and finders hook into your shell. Check that they support yours, such as Bash, Zsh, or fish.
- **Config.** Session and dotfile managers live on their config. Check that you can keep it in Git and share it between machines.
- **Platform.** Check the Platform filter if you move between macOS, Linux, and Windows.
- **Health.** You'll use these every day for years, so prefer ones the Health filter marks Active.

## What should you keep in mind?

Add tools one at a time, and get used to each before the next. Dotfile managers can end up holding secrets, so check how they handle encryption before you push your dotfiles to a public repository.
