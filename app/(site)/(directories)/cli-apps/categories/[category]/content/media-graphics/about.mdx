## How do you choose a media tool?

Name the job first: convert, download, play, or view.

- **Formats and codecs.** Check that it handles the formats you need. Some builds leave out codecs for license reasons, so the version from your package manager may differ from the official one.
- **Downloaders** break when websites change. Prefer ones the Health filter marks Active, and update them often.
- **Images in the terminal** look best in a terminal that supports an image protocol, such as Kitty's or Sixel. Otherwise they fall back to colored blocks of text.
- **Platform.** Check the Platform filter for your system.

## What should you keep in mind?

Media commands can overwrite files without asking. Write the output to a new file until you trust the command. Only download media you have the right to keep.
