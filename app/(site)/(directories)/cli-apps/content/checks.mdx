## How are apps chosen and checked?

Each app is checked against its official repository or website, and we write its summary ourselves. The verified date on an app is when we last did that. Lists such as [Terminal Trove](https://terminaltrove.com/list/) and [awesome-cli-apps](https://github.com/agarrharr/awesome-cli-apps) help us find apps we missed, but their descriptions aren't copied.

When a fact isn't published, such as a license or a release date, the app shows "unknown" instead of a guess.
