## Do all app stores take 30%?

No. 30% is the standard rate at Apple, Steam, and Meta Horizon Store, but many stores charge less on early revenue, subscriptions, or small developers.

| Store | Lower rate | When it applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple App Store | 15% | Small Business Program members, and subscriptions after their first year |
| Google Play | 10% | Subscriptions under its new fee model, which is rolling out by region |
| Steam | 25%, then 20% | A game's lifetime sales above $10M, then above $50M |
| Epic Games Store | 0% | A game's first $1M each year, then 12% |
| Shopify App Store | 0% | An app's first $1M since January 2025, then 15% |

Each card lists the conditions, such as company size limits and regional rates.

## Why do some cards show no rate?

Xbox, PlayStation, and Nintendo set revenue shares in contracts they don't publish, so their cards say "Set by agreement". Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons let you sell paid extensions but don't run the checkout. You need your own payment provider; compare them in [Payments](/platform-fees/payments).
