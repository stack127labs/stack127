## Is a yearly plan or a royalty cut cheaper for distribution?

It depends on how much your music earns and how often you release. DistroKid and TuneCore charge a yearly plan from $24.99 and keep 0% of store and streaming royalties. CD Baby charges once per release, $9.99 for a single, and keeps 9%.

<Example title="One single earning $300 a year, at rates checked in September 2026">

CD Baby keeps 9%, which is $27 a year, plus the $9.99 release fee once. DistroKid's cheapest plan costs $24.99 a year and covers unlimited uploads.

</Example>

The more you release, the more a yearly plan covers. Withdrawal fees and paid extras can change the picture, so check the lines on each card.

## Do beat stores take a cut?

Several take 0% of your sales: BeatStars, which charges a plan instead, Airbit on every plan, and TRAKTRAIN on its Unlimited plan. SoundClick and Soundee keep 30% on their free plans and less on paid ones. BeatStars adds a 12% service fee to Marketplace orders, which you can choose to cover for your buyers.
