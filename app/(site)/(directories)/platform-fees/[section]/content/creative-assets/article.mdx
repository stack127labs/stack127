## How do I compare royalty rates here?

Cards show what the marketplace keeps, so a royalty reads the other way round. Adobe Stock pays contributors 33% on photos, so its card shows 67%. Fab pays 88% on product sales, so its card shows 12%.

## Why do subscription sites show no bar?

They pay from a shared pool, not a share of your price. Envato Elements, for example, puts 50% of its Individual and Teams subscription revenue in a pool and splits it between authors by downloads. What you earn depends on how often subscribers download your work, so there's no fixed cut to show.

## Does going exclusive pay more?

Sometimes. TurboSquid starts at 40% on its non-exclusive Basic plan and at 50% on SquidGuild, which needs your whole 3D catalog to be exclusive. Many marketplaces, including Adobe Stock, Shutterstock, and Creative Market, don't ask for exclusivity. Open a card to see its exclusivity terms.
