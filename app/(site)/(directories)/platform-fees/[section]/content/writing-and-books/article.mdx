## Why does KDP show 30% when it pays 70%?

Cards show what the platform keeps. KDP's 70% ebook royalty shows as 30%. On Amazon.com, the 70% rate needs a price from $2.99 to $12.99, and delivery costs come out of it. Outside those rules, the royalty is 35%.

## How are print book royalties worked out?

The printing cost comes off before you're paid, so the card's percentage isn't what reaches you.

<Formula title="Print royalty">
Royalty = list price × royalty rate − printing cost
</Formula>

KDP's royalty rate is 60% or 50% of the list price, or 40% through Expanded Distribution. IngramSpark pays the list price minus your wholesale discount, printing, and a 1.875% market access fee. To work out a KDP royalty with real printing costs, use the [KDP royalty calculator](/kdp-royalty-calculator).

## Is Substack cheaper than a 0% newsletter platform?

It depends on what you earn. Substack is free to start and keeps 10% of paid subscriptions, plus card fees. beehiiv and Ghost take 0% but charge a plan. Paid subscriptions on beehiiv start on its Scale plan, from $43 a month billed yearly, so Substack's 10% costs more once you take in about $430 a month.

Ghost prices its plans by members. Its Publisher plan is $29 a month billed yearly at 1,000 members.
