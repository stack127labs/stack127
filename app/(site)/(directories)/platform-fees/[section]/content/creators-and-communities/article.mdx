## How much of a membership payment do I keep?

Less than the platform fee alone suggests, because card processing comes on top.

<Example title="A $10 monthly Patreon membership, at rates checked in September 2026">

Patreon keeps 10% for new creators, which is $1.00. A US card payment adds 2.9% plus 30¢, which is 59¢. You keep $8.41.

</Example>

The fixed 30¢ weighs more on small payments. Patreon charges 5% plus 10¢ for processing on payments of $3 or less.

## How do freelance marketplaces charge?

Fiverr keeps 20% of every order, including tips, and holds your money for 14 days after an order completes. Upwork charges freelancers 0% to 15% per contract, plus 15¢ for each Connect you spend on proposals. Withdrawal fees vary by payout method on both.

## Do crowdfunding sites charge if I miss my goal?

Not on Kickstarter. It's all-or-nothing, so you pay no fees and get no money if the goal isn't met. If it is, Kickstarter keeps 5%, plus processing of 3% and 30¢ per pledge in the US. Indiegogo also waives fees on regular campaigns that miss their goal. Its Express campaigns have no goal and charge backers at checkout, so fees apply from the first pledge.
