## Should I sell on a marketplace or my own course platform?

A marketplace finds students for you and keeps a large share. A course platform keeps little or nothing per sale but charges a monthly plan, and you bring the students.

| Platform | Type | What it charges |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Udemy | Marketplace | Keeps 63% of a marketplace sale, but 3% when the student uses your instructor link |
| Teachable | Course platform | Starter plan at $39 a month, plus 7.5% of each sale |
| Kajabi | Course platform | Basic plan at $179 a month, and 0% of sales with Kajabi Payments |

Card fees are extra on Teachable and Kajabi.

## How do tutoring sites take their cut?

Most take a commission on each lesson, and some lower it as you teach more. Preply keeps 33% of your first 20 hours, falling to 18% after 400 hours. It also keeps the full price of each new student's first trial lesson.

Cambly works differently. It pays a fixed rate per minute, 17¢ for adult lessons, and doesn't say how much it keeps.
