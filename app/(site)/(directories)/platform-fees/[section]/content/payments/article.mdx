## Merchant of record or payment processor?

A merchant of record sells your product on your behalf, so it collects and pays sales tax and VAT for you. A payment processor only moves the money, and the tax stays your job. That's why merchant of record rates are higher.

<Example title="A $20 US card sale, at rates checked in September 2026">

| Service | Fee | Who handles sales tax |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stripe | 2.9% + 30¢ = 88¢ | You |
| Paddle | 5% + 50¢ = $1.50 | Paddle |
| Lemon Squeezy | 5% + 50¢ = $1.50 | Lemon Squeezy |

</Example>

Paddle's and Lemon Squeezy's fees include card processing. Lemon Squeezy adds 1.5% for international and PayPal payments and 0.5% for subscriptions.

## Which extra fees should I check?

The headline rate is for a domestic card. Check these before you compare:

- International cards: Stripe and PayPal each add 1.5% for US accounts.
- Currency conversion: Stripe adds 1%, and PayPal adds 3% to 4%.
- Payouts: standard payouts are free on Stripe, but instant payouts cost 1.5%.
- Your country: most processors set rates by country, and some only serve sellers in certain countries.
