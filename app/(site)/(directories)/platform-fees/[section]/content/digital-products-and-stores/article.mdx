## Is a 0% fee platform cheaper?

Only once you sell enough to cover its monthly plan. Gumroad has no plan but takes 10% plus 50¢ on direct sales. Sellfy's Starter plan costs $39 a month and takes nothing on sales up to $10,000 a year. Card processing at 2.9% plus 30¢ applies on both.

<Example title="Selling a $20 download in the US, at rates checked in September 2026">

| Sales a month | Gumroad fees | Sellfy Starter fees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | $33.80 | $47.80 |
| 40 | $135.20 | $74.20 |

Gumroad costs $3.38 a sale. Sellfy costs 88¢ a sale plus $39 a month, so it's cheaper from about 16 sales a month.

</Example>

Fees aren't the only difference. Gumroad is the merchant of record and handles sales tax for you. On Sellfy, you file and pay it yourself.

## How does print on demand pay you?

Most print-on-demand services charge a base cost to make and ship each item, and you keep what you add on top. That's why their cards say "Base cost" instead of a percentage.

Marketplaces work differently. Redbubble takes a fee from your earnings, 50% on a Standard account. TeePublic and Displate pay a fixed amount per item sold, such as $4 for a regular-price TeePublic Artisan tee.
