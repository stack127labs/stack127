Each fee is the standard rate for one domestic online card payment, for a seller based in the country you pick. It's shown in the provider's own currency.

A merchant of record sells to your customer for you and handles sales tax, so its fee is higher. A gateway costs less, but you're the seller, so you handle tax and disputes.

When a provider has no rate for your country, we show its worldwide rate and mark it unclear. Each card also lists where sellers can sign up.
