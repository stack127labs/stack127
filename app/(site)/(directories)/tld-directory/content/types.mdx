## What do the domain extension types mean?

The type is IANA's label for an ending. Generic endings can still restrict who can register, as `.edu` and `.gov` do.

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| generic | Endings such as `.com`, `.xyz`, `.edu`, `.gov`, `.biz`, and `.pro`. Brand endings, such as `.bmw`, are used only by their owner. |
| country-code | Two-letter endings for a country or territory, such as `.uk`, plus versions in other scripts, such as `.中国`, shown with their `xn--` form. Each sets its own rules. |
| infrastructure | `.arpa`, used for reverse DNS. You can't register a name under it. |
