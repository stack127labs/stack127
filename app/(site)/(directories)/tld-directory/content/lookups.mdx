## Why can't some domain extensions be looked up?

Our domain tools read registration data over RDAP, and some registries don't offer it. The Lookups column shows where each ending stands.

| Lookups | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Yes | The registry is in [IANA's RDAP directory](https://data.iana.org/rdap/dns.json). |
| Via our own list | The registry runs RDAP but isn't in IANA's directory. We added it after testing it with a real domain. The name under the label runs the service. |
| Cannot check | We know of no RDAP service for this ending, so our lookup and availability tools can't answer for it. |

A "Cannot check" ending may still have a WHOIS server or a lookup on the registry's website. Its page in [IANA's root zone database](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db) lists both. [Domain lookup](/domain-lookup) explains how RDAP and WHOIS differ.
