## Current notices

- The official [Exoscale blog](https://www.exoscale.com/blog/), [product changelog](https://changelog.exoscale.com/) and [status page](https://exoscalestatus.com/) show an active Compute service. I found no Compute shutdown or sunset notice. Recent platform notices cover encryption at rest for Compute and Block Storage (2 June 2026), VPC replacing Private Networks (28 October 2026), and a 30% A40 GPU price reduction effective 1 June 2026. VPC is a network product change, not a Compute retirement.
- Exoscale says it joined A1 Digital in 2017; an official A1 release later describes A1's acquisition of Exoscale. Exoscale is a trademark of Swiss company Akenes SA and a member of A1 Digital. Separately, Exoscale acquired cloudControl's team, technology and brand in 2016. These are ownership and historical acquisition notes, not a Compute rename or closure.
- The status page currently shows the zones and Compute operational. It records a significant CH-DK-2 Compute incident in January 2026 involving local-storage hangs on some hypervisors; affected workloads were moved and the incident was resolved. It also lists BG-SOF-1 network maintenance for 10-11 and 12-13 November 2026, with possible TCP session resets. See field 32.
