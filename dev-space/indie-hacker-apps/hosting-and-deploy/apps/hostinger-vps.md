## Lifecycle notice

Hostinger discontinued legacy OpenVZ VPS sales/renewals on March 1, 2026. Existing OpenVZ servers are scheduled to shut down on December 14, 2026; Hostinger says remaining servers and their data will then be deleted permanently. Migration to KVM requires a new server and a manual data transfer. This is a sunset of the old OpenVZ virtualization product. The current KVM VPS offer remains active. Official company newsroom and news-archive pages were searched for acquisition or company shutdown announcements; none appeared in the checked listings on 2026-11-06. [OpenVZ migration and end-of-life notice](https://www.hostinger.com/support/10469992-how-to-migrate-your-vps-from-openvz-to-kvm-at-hostinger/); [Hostinger newsroom](https://www.hostinger.com/newsroom); [Hostinger news archive](https://www.hostinger.com/blog/news/).

## Cost examples: our calculations

Calculated from the public prices checked on 2026-11-06. The fields' comparison input asks for normal monthly billing and no annual discount. Hostinger currently publishes 24-month introductory and 24-month renewal prices prominently, while the billing guide lists a 1-month renewal option but does not publish the current one-month KVM rate. I therefore show the exact 24-month published offer as a separate reference and do not present it as the normal monthly bill. All amounts below exclude applicable tax. Existing custom-domain registration is outside the hosting subtotal.

- **Shared KVM 1 inputs (global USD store):** 1 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, 50 GB disk and 4 TB monthly bandwidth; intro $6.49/month equivalent for 24 months; renewal $11.99/month equivalent for 24 months. Source: https://www.hostinger.com/vps-hosting?REFERRALCODE=innovarketing .
- **Our calculations:** introductory invoice `24 × $6.49 = $155.76`; renewal invoice at the currently published rate `24 × $11.99 = $287.76`. These are 24-month totals, not monthly billing.
- **Region and currency:** global Hostinger.com price in USD (available to US buyers and countries without a dedicated local store). Server region is separately selected from the KVM locations listed in field 16. Tax is added based on buyer location/account.

### A. Small commercial static site

- **Inputs:** 100 MB deployed files, 1,000,000 HTTP requests/month, 100 GB outbound/month, 20 builds of five minutes, private source repository and one custom domain.
- **Fit:** KVM 1 is the smallest documented VM. It can run a customer-configured static web server and customer-run builds. 100 GB is below the 4 TB plan allowance if outbound is counted toward it. Hostinger does not separately meter requests or build minutes.
- **Our calculation:** current 24-month promo subtotal `$6.49 × 24 = $155.76` upfront; current 24-month renewal subtotal `$11.99 × 24 = $287.76`. Exact one-month/no-discount cost is unknown, so this is not a compliant normal-month estimate. Applicable tax and any optional paid tools are extra.
- **Result:** technically compatible with the KVM model, but exact standard-month total is not published and bandwidth direction is not fully defined. Domain excluded as required by the workload rule.

### B. Small commercial app API

- **Inputs:** 1,000,000 requests/month, 100 ms active processor work/request, 250 ms elapsed/request, 256 MiB working memory, 100 GB outbound, same builds and domain as A; the server assumption is at least 1 vCPU and 1 GiB for 730 hours.
- **Capacity check:** KVM 1 provides 1 vCPU, 4 GB RAM and can remain running for the full prepaid term. The arithmetic input is `1,000,000 × 0.100 seconds = 100,000 active vCPU-seconds`, about `27.78 vCPU-hours`; this does not prove application throughput. CPU-credit throttling and the conflicting public throttling rules may affect sustained work.
- **Our calculation:** same KVM 1 published 24-month offer: `$6.49 × 24 = $155.76` upfront, renewing at `$11.99 × 24 = $287.76`. The exact price for the requested normal 1-month billing period is not published. No request, CPU-time or RAM-time charge is added because the VM is prepaid and those meters are not listed.
- **Result:** minimum resource sizing is met; actual workload fit/performance is unverified. The 100 GB transfer assumption is below 4 TB if outbound is counted, but traffic accounting direction is unresolved.

### C. App plus persistent data

- **Inputs:** B plus PostgreSQL with at least 1 GiB memory and 10 GB storage, daily backups retained seven days, and 10 GB/month app-to-database traffic.
