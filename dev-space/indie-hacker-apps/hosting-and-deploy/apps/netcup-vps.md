## Current status and notices

The VPS is actively offered to new customers. The official German server documentation says new VPS orders moved to Generation 12.5 and new tariffs on 22 September 2026; existing contracts were not affected by that change. The current product page displays VPS 500 through VPS 8000 G12.5. A VPS product page also showed a notice that provisioning may be delayed by elevated demand and asked buyers to use a support ticket.

The German news post dated 4 November 2026 announced netcup Apps in beta. The app catalog can install preconfigured apps such as Nextcloud, Immich, n8n, and WordPress from SCP without an additional app fee. The beta's available features and catalog can change, and regular support for netcup Apps is not available during beta; the company directs users to its forum.

I checked netcup's English and German blog/news pages and its status page. I found no VPS shutdown, netcup acquisition, or product-sunset notice. netcup's own pages describe Anexia as its parent company; this is a current ownership relationship, not a new acquisition notice.

## Cost examples: our calculations

Calculated 2026-11-06. To show a firm current bill, the examples use the VPS 500 G12.5 24-month option, public IPv4+IPv6, Nuremberg (NUE), and the product page's displayed German tax scope. The page gives €7.03/month for the plan, +€1.04/month for Nuremberg, €0 setup, and a 24-month billing period.
