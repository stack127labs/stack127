- **Locations:** the current price cards list Tokyo, but the general virtual-server documentation lists six locations and omits Tokyo. The plan-specific deployment selector is the source to check for the chosen model and current stock.
- **Bandwidth:** current Cloud VPS Gen 2 rate cards list 1 TB/month. The dedicated bandwidth docs clarify that this is egress only, that Europe/US and APAC traffic pools are separate, and that APAC uses its own €1.69/TB overage rate. Older general docs give broader plan ranges and omit Tokyo from overage examples; the newer bandwidth page is more specific.
- **Billing renewal:** payment docs say fixed-term service does not renew by default; Terms §17.3 say the order renews for the same term unless notice is given. Check the chosen Order and portal setting.
- **Scaling:** Cloud VPS marketing calls scaling “auto scale”; the product docs only describe a customer-triggered tier upgrade and say downsizing is unavailable. No autoscale trigger configuration or billing rule was found.
- **Backups:** current docs describe a paid VM Backup feature, including Cloud VPS in Lithuania. Terms §9.1 say Cherry does not provide backup services and puts responsibility on the customer. - **Tax:** Terms say tax is additional unless the order/rate schedule states otherwise. Prices and calculations are shown before applicable tax; no customer's tax jurisdiction was assumed.
- **News and ownership:** the official News archive, blog and changelog show current operations and product/partnership announcements. No acquisition, shutdown, or sunset notice was found; no separate newsroom page was identified.
