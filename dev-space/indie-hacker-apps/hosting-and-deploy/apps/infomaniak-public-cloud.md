## Cost examples

All figures below are our calculations from Public Cloud rates, in CHF, using 730 hours, for the Swiss Public Cloud regions dc3-a/dc4-a. The pricing page does not show separate rates by region. Rates are from the Public Cloud pricing page, not Infomaniak web hosting, Managed Cloud Server/VPS, kDrive, Database Service, Kubernetes, or Object Storage. The pricing page says its rates exclude VAT; the general terms state VAT treatment by billing address, so final invoice treatment should be confirmed. No vendor price estimator total is copied.

- **A — small commercial static site:** a1_ram2_disk20_perf1 at CHF 0.0064/hour + reserved public IPv4 at CHF 0.00457/hour. Our calculation: (0.0064 + 0.00457) × 730 = CHF 8.0081/month. Assumptions: one VM runs all month, 100 GB egress, one public IPv4, no paid backups or load balancer. Public Cloud generally lists VM bandwidth as free, subject to terms.
- **B — API, at least 1 vCPU and 1 GiB RAM for 730 hours:** Same 1-vCPU/2-GB flavor plus one public IPv4. Our calculation: (CHF 0.0064 + CHF 0.00457) × 730 = CHF 8.0081/month. Assumption: 100 GB egress; no request-count meter is listed for VM traffic. This does not test capacity for the stated request volume.
- **C — API plus PostgreSQL, 1 GiB database storage, 10 GB persistent data, daily backups for seven days:** Compatible as a self-managed VM, or with a separately billed managed MySQL service (not PostgreSQL). For the self-managed option, known VM+IPv4 subtotal using a2_ram4_disk50_perf1 is (CHF 0.01399 + CHF 0.00457) × 730 = CHF 13.5488/month. This is not a total: the public text pages do not expose the numeric block-volume rate, and backup size depends on stored data and retention. A daily seven-day backup amount cannot be priced exactly from the rates and storage inputs found.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Billing interval:** Public Cloud terms §3.4 and the billing FAQ say any started hour is billable; the pricing FAQ gives a proportional 15-minute example. These statements do not match. Examples use 730 full hours, so the arithmetic is unaffected.
- **VAT:** The public price and billing FAQ footer say prices exclude VAT. General terms say rates are VAT-inclusive for Swiss and EU billing addresses and generally not subject to VAT outside those areas, with exceptions. Public Cloud terms say they override general terms in a conflict but do not resolve this specific wording. Confirm the billing-country treatment before relying on a tax-inclusive total.
- **Block Storage amount:** French and English pricing pages show the Block Storage category, and official docs/help describe billing by GB, but a numeric rate did not appear in the public text extraction. Pages checked: French pricing, English prices, Public Cloud help center, block-storage operations, and quota docs. It is recorded as unclear, not as free or absent.
- **Currency and region:** Instance examples are the listed CHF prices for Infomaniak’s Swiss Public Cloud; docs list regions dc3-a and dc4-a. The ICU FAQ gives a CHF/EUR conversion but not a separate EUR instance rate card. Pricing-page rates do not specify a different rate per region.
- **Signup location:** Trial eligibility is explicitly limited to the countries listed in field 09. General new-account eligibility outside those trial countries was not published in the pages checked.
- **Lifecycle news check:** Official press releases, investor announcements, company news, product page, and docs were checked. The company reverse-merger/listing is recorded above; no Public Cloud acquisition, shutdown, or sunset notice was found. A 2026-01-27 press item says Public Cloud was expanded.
- **Not searched:** None of the unknown values above are based on a failed page load. No checkout, account sign-up, or login flow was used.
