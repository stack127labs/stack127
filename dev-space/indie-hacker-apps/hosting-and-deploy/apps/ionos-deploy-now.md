## Cost examples: our calculation

Calculation date: 2026-11-04. One solo commercial builder, ordinary monthly billing, one private source repository and one custom domain. No annual discount, temporary Membership discount, signup credit or optional paid support. Taxes are additional where applicable. Domain registration is outside the hosting subtotal, but a mandatory extra IONOS contract would need to be counted if required for this buyer.

**Sourced inputs:** regular Membership $4/month, Static project $2/month and PHP project $7/month; capacities 50 MB, 1 GB and 10 GB per deployment respectively: https://docs.ionos.space/docs/ (checked 2026-11-04). **Build assumption shared by A/B/C:** select a standard Linux x64 2-core GitHub runner, and assume the 20 five-minute builds represent 100 billable runner minutes, with no other account usage. Our calculation: `20 × 5 = 100 minutes`; `100 × $0.006 = $0.60` of the $12/month included compute allowance; incremental compute payment `max($0, $0.60 − $12) = $0`. Source: https://docs.github.com/en/billing/concepts/product-billing/github-actions (checked 2026-11-04). Workflow orchestration/deployment jobs and retries may consume additional time; they must fit within the unused allowance for that $0 incremental result to hold. Do not count all GitHub build services as unconditionally free.

Retained artifacts must also remain within the separate 500 MB allowance, and repository cache within its 10 GB allowance, for a $0 GitHub storage assumption. Twenty retained 100 MB copies would be `20 × 100 MB = 2,000 MB`, exceeding the artifact allowance. Actual billing depends on retention time and binary-unit measurements, not simply build count. Use workflow retention/cleanup deliberately; no unverified default retention is assumed. Rate and accounting source: https://docs.github.com/en/billing/concepts/product-billing/github-actions (checked 2026-11-04).
