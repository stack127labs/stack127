# Laravel Cloud

Official URL: [Laravel Cloud](https://laravel.com/cloud).

Lifecycle check, 2026-11-05: **Legacy queue clusters and the earlier managed-queue generation were scheduled to stop processing on September 30, 2026.** This date has passed. Current managed queues and Worker clusters are the documented migration destinations. Older managed queues use SQS, where unprocessed messages are retained for at most 14 days. [Queue cluster sunset](https://laravel.com/cloud/docs/workers.md); [Managed queue generation retirement](https://laravel.com/cloud/docs/queues.md).

**Redis by Upstash is deprecated and closed to new caches.** Monthly bandwidth caps have been enforced since October 1, 2026; higher existing-cache prices begin November 30, 2026. Laravel Cloud supports these caches until August 31, 2027 and says it will delete remaining caches afterward. Migrate to Laravel Valkey. [Retirement and billing notice](https://laravel.com/cloud/docs/resources/caches/redis.md); [Migration guide](https://laravel.com/cloud/docs/knowledge-base/migrate-upstash-to-valkey.md).

The main hosting product remains publicly offered to new buyers. No whole-platform shutdown, sunset or acquisition notice was found in the current offer, pricing, changelog and checked Laravel announcements. [Current offer](https://laravel.com/cloud); [Pricing](https://laravel.com/cloud/pricing); [Changelog](https://laravel.com/cloud/docs/changelog.md); [Flex and Starter announcement](https://laravel.com/blog/your-laravel-cloud-stack-now-scales-to-zero). These are bounded public checks, not a guarantee of future service.

Our app calculation:

    running seconds = 730 × 3,600 = 2,628,000
    raw rate multiplication = 2,628,000 × $0.00000496032 = $13.03572096
    selected compute charge = published monthly cap of $12
    known ongoing subtotal = $5 base + max($0, $12 usage - $5 credit)
                           = $12/month

The published cap controls. Finite displayed per-second precision can produce small differences at the cap boundary; do not substitute an uncapped 730-hour bill.

Define **F** as allowance actually earned this billing cycle, in GB. Define **U** as additional billable traffic beyond the workload's stated 100 GB outbound: inbound requests, any additional metered origin/edge path bytes, and unspecified external API/service traffic. No other apps contribute allowance here. The docs give an approximate per-second factor, without settling exact precision or accrual after the separate 28-day app charging cap. We retain F instead of inventing a 730-hour normalization. [Allowance accrual and traffic classes](https://laravel.com/cloud/docs/pricing.md); [Allowance accounting](https://laravel.com/cloud/docs/usage.md), checked 2026-11-05.

    A/B overage = $0.10 × max(0, 100 + U - F)
    A/B hosting formula = $5 + max(0, $12 + overage - $5)
                        = $12 + overage

A simplified illustration with **F = 100 GB and U = 0** gives $0 transfer overage and $12/month. It is not an exact quote for a real million requests: inbound bytes and all network paths are unspecified. **No exact complete A/B total is published here.**
