## Cost examples — our calculation

Calculated **2026-11-05** for one paying builder, public commercial use, normal monthly international USD billing, one custom domain, no credits, annual discount or optional support. Domain registration is outside the hosting subtotal. App compute is advertised in AWS US-East. No signup, deploy or load test was performed.

These are **known subscription subtotals, not complete workload totals**. The Docker guide says no bandwidth bill, while the terms permit metered bandwidth with no published numeric schedule. Tax inclusion is also not established. For all examples let **E(100 GB)** mean the unresolved monthly outbound charge, **N(10 GB)** the unresolved app-to-database network charge, and **T** any additional applicable tax. A zero value for E/N/T is not assumed. Sources checked 2026-11-05: [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/pricing); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docker-hosting); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/terms); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/about).

### A. Small commercial static site

Inputs: **100 MB deployed files; 1,000,000 requests/month; 100 GB outbound/month; 20 builds × 5 minutes = 100 build minutes; one private GitHub repository; one custom domain**. Static output runs in a generated Nginx container. Select **Always-On Small, 0.25 vCPU/512 MB, USD 12/month**, because the free tier does not support the required custom domain. Private GitHub source is supported without a separate SnapDeploy paid-repository tier. Sources: [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docs/supported-technologies); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/pricing); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docs/github); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docs/free-tier); checked 2026-11-05.

Our formula: **1 Small subscription × USD 12/month = USD 12/month known hosting subtotal**. Nginx/static delivery, private-source build workflow, image hosting, HTTPS, routing and basic logs/metrics are advertised platform features; each 5-minute build is within the 60-minute build timeout and Always-On removes the daily deploy cap. No separate mandatory builder, request, certificate or gateway tariff is published for these bundled features. Sources: [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docker-hosting); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docs/deployment); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/pricing); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docs/monitoring).

**Complete monthly total unresolved: USD 12 + E(100 GB) + T**, rather than claiming USD 12 is the cost of the complete workload. Expanded app disk/image fit and handling the specified traffic depend on the actual build and arrival pattern; the 100 MB site is not itself a tested Docker image. The custom domain is exempt from the documented shared-domain per-IP limit. No performance equivalence to a global static CDN is claimed. Sources: [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docs/scaling); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docker-hosting); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/terms).

### B. Small commercial app API

Inputs: **1,000,000 HTTP requests/month; 100 ms active processor work and 250 ms elapsed time/request; 256 MiB working memory; 100 GB outbound; the same 100 build minutes/private source/domain; 730 hours running**. Select **Always-On Medium USD 25/month**, with **1 vCPU and 2 GB RAM**. Small fails the required 1-vCPU capacity floor. Preserve the vendor's GB unit; 2 GB is above the scenario's 1 GiB minimum under either ordinary decimal or binary interpretation. Sources: [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/pricing); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docs/free-tier); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docker-hosting).

Our input arithmetic: **1,000,000 × 0.100 s = 100,000 processor-work seconds** and **1,000,000 × 0.250 s = 250,000 aggregate elapsed request seconds**. These are workload assumptions, not separately billed CPU/function seconds and not measured task throughput. Fixed monthly subscription covers the assumed full 730-hour month; no hourly rounding or division by CPU speed is invented.

Our formula: **1 Medium subscription × USD 25/month = USD 25/month known hosting subtotal**. Managed HTTP routing, basic logs/metrics, private-source builds, HTTPS and custom domain use are in the advertised bundle; no extra mandatory load-balancer/IP tariff is shown. **Complete monthly total unresolved: USD 25 + E(100 GB) + T**. Burst concurrency, request/body limits and end-to-end timeout are not established; actual app fit needs deployment testing. Sources: [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docker-hosting); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/pricing); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docs/monitoring); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docs/deployment); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/terms); checked 2026-11-05.

### C. App plus persistent data

Inputs: all of B, plus **one PostgreSQL database with at least 1 GiB memory and 10 GB storage; daily backups retained for at least 7 days; 10 GB/month app-to-database traffic**. Select **PostgreSQL Standard USD 49/month: 2 GB RAM, 0.5 vCPU, 10 GB storage, 100 connections**. Mini has only 5 GB disk and does not fit. Native add-on docs state daily backups with **30-day retention**, meeting the requested 7-day minimum; backup/restore usability was not tested. Sources: [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/addon-pricing); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docs/addon-services).

Our formula: **USD 25 Medium + USD 49 PostgreSQL Standard = USD 74/month known subscription subtotal**. Advertised daily backups and persistent EFS storage belong to the DB service; a normal app container is not used as a database disk. **Complete monthly total unresolved: USD 74 + E(100 GB) + N(10 GB) + T**. Private networking alone does not establish zero-charge app-to-DB traffic. There is no compulsory third-party PostgreSQL provider in this selection. Optional hourly backup arrangements/read replicas are not required for the stated workload. Sources: [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/pricing); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/addon-pricing); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docs/addon-services); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/terms).

A Git revert or previous app image does **not** restore PostgreSQL data. The provider's automatic backup restore procedure, restore fees and recovery objectives remain unpublished in the checked add-on docs, pricing and DB guide. Those unknowns matter operationally even though the advertised daily backup frequency/retention match the input. Sources: [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docs/deployment); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/docs/addon-services); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/addon-pricing); [Source](https://snapdeploy.dev/blog/managed-database-addons-postgresql-mysql-mongodb-mariadb).
