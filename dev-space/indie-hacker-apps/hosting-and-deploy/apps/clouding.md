## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Billing collection:** Current new-user FAQ and hourly guide describe prepaid balance and invoices at each top-up. The terms still describe monthly billing and end-of-period payment by card or PayPal. The account flow was not entered, so this remains a conflict.
- **Billing precision:** Current materials price servers per hour. A 2020 official article says per second. Current terms say a server is billed for each hour it exists, including while stopped. The exact rounding rule was not settled.
- **Minimum disk:** The live homepage shows a 5 GB configuration and price; the performance guide says a 25 GB disk is the minimum. The API resize example also uses 5 GB. Scenario A therefore shows both totals rather than choosing silently.
- **Data transfers:** The current privacy policy contains an outside-EU processing statement and US-provider list with old Privacy Shield dates. The current service terms say client-data transfers outside the EU require prior written permission. The policy's current provider list and scope are not clear.
