## Cost examples

All calculations below are ours, not vendor quotes. Currency is INR. The VM region is not attached to the price card; Compute docs list Delhi NCR and Jaipur, India. Tax inclusion is unresolved, so totals are not tax-adjusted.

### A. Small commercial static site

- Fit: Possible on a customer-managed VM. Cyfuture does not document managed Git builds, private-repository deploys, or preview sites for Compute.
- Inputs: FAQ entry plan ₹690/month, 1 vCPU, 1 GB RAM, 30 GB SSD, 5 GB transfer; 100 GB/month outbound; overage ₹5/GB.
- Formula: 100 GB outbound - 5 GB included = 95 GB overage. 95 × ₹5 = ₹475. ₹690 VM + ₹475 transfer = ₹1,165/month.
- Result: Our conditional subtotal is ₹1,165/month before any tax adjustment. It assumes the FAQ price and allowance apply to this workload; the pricing cards show a different first plan. Builds total 100 minutes and must run on the VM or elsewhere. Exact total remains uncertain because price-card scope, tax, and HTTPS certificate charges are not settled.
- Sources: https://cyfuture.cloud/pricing and https://cyfuture.cloud/docs/compute/cloud/overview (checked 2026-11-06).

### B. Small commercial app API

- Fit: The FAQ’s 1-vCPU/1-GB shape meets the stated server floor. A VM can serve the API, but capacity does not prove the required throughput.
- Inputs: Same ₹690/month plan and 5 GB transfer; 100 GB outbound; overage ₹5/GB; 730-hour month assumption.
- Formula: 95 GB overage × ₹5 = ₹475; ₹690 + ₹475 = ₹1,165/month. The vendor’s minimum hourly billing unit is one hour; this monthly calculation uses the published monthly price.
- Result: Our conditional subtotal is ₹1,165/month before any tax adjustment. The public pages do not establish the selected shape’s price as the current card entry, nor guarantee performance for one million requests. No per-request fee is listed for VM-hosted apps.
- Sources: https://cyfuture.cloud/pricing and https://cyfuture.cloud/docs/compute/cloud/overview (checked 2026-11-06).
