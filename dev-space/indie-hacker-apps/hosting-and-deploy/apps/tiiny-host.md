### A. Small commercial static site

Let **V** be the actual billable visit count. The workload's one million requests does not determine V: one visit may fetch many assets/pages. The 100 MB deployed file set likewise is not necessarily what each visitor downloads.

Our conditional account calculations, using the help articles' published allowances:

```text
Local build demand = 20 × 5 minutes = 100 minutes/month

If V <= 500,000 and actual counted bandwidth <= 100 GB:
  1 Pro account × US $38/month = US $38/month listed subtotal

If 500,000 < V <= 1,000,000 and actual counted bandwidth <= 200 GB:
  1 Pro Max account × US $89/month = US $89/month listed subtotal

The single 100 MB project fits either plan's published storage pool.
One custom domain and ordinary SSL are part of the selected plan workflow.
No extra teammate, paid builder, or new repository service is selected.
```

**Result: conditional $38 or $89 monthly hosting subtotals; no single exact complete total.** These are two stated plan cases, not a claim that every possible realization costs between those numbers. Actual V, effective Pro Max quota, bandwidth accounting, tax, and localized checkout amount must be settled. No unknown overage rate is assigned zero. If the visit/traffic caps are exceeded, the cost is not established by this calculation.
