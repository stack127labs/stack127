## Shutdown, sunset, and acquisition check

Alibaba Cloud ECS is active: its documentation was updated through November 4, 2026, and the official blog had an ECS disk-management post dated November 5. The company press room, service notices, and ECS announcements were checked; no ECS shutdown or acquisition notice was found. A November 2, 2026 notice retires four older instance families—re4, re4e, re6, and ebmre6. It does not close ECS or all instances in those families; check the notice and replacement schedule before choosing a listed type.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- ECS product marketing says 29 regions; the current ECS region guide lists 31 entries, including local and partner regions. Use the live region guide and purchase availability rather than the marketing count.
- The two international economy-instance cards show USD starting prices but no region. The detailed ECS pricing schedule timed out, and the calculator did not render static results. No rate was copied between regions. Official static examples provide Singapore compute rates and a China (Hangzhou) public-bandwidth example only.
- International prices are USD and exclude tax; final settlement may be in USD or local currency, and tax depends on the account billing country. The calculated examples stay in USD before tax.
- The international site excludes Chinese mainland entities and mainland phone numbers for registration. Chinese mainland resource purchases require real-name verification; international accounts cannot directly perform ordinary ICP filing. Public registration help does not enumerate every country restriction, and sanctions screening applies.
- ECS does not automatically back up disks. Snapshot setup and storage are separate; daily Postgres backup cost cannot be exact without stored bytes, size, and region-specific price.
- The ECS SLA page was updated September 4, 2026. It excludes free/trial usage and has claim deadlines, exclusions, and separate single- versus multi-zone calculations.
- Pages checked for closure/acquisition news: ECS release notes and product announcements, international service notices, Alibaba Cloud blog, press room, and Health Dashboard. The four-instance-family retirement is narrower than a product shutdown.
