### A. Small commercial static site

Inputs: 100 MB deployed files, 1,000,000 HTTP requests/month, 100 GB outbound, **20 × 5 = 100 build minutes**, one private repository and one custom domain.

Purchase assumption: one whole paid package. Formula: **1 × EUR 5 = EUR 5/month known subscription**. Tiny consumes 0.25 package, whose published allocation value is EUR 1.25/month; the unused purchased capacity is not a separate discount or refund.

Included line items: GitHub build/deploy tools, app hosting, normal traffic under fair use, HTTPS/custom domain, WAF and basic observability. No separate request, transfer, certificate, IP or gateway usage fee is stated for this route. The 100-minute build entitlement and direct private-source fit remain unverified, so **EUR 5 is a conditional hosting fee, not a confirmed complete workload quote**. Tax remains unresolved.
