## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Price presentation varies by term and the Linux 1 card conflicts with its full plan table.** The page showed €2.48/month and €2.48 renewal in a two-year card, while its detailed plan table showed €2.99/month and €2.99 renewal. The rest of the Linux tier table and the monthly/annual cards also show different first-term and renewal amounts according to term. Use the selected billing term's live values and recheck before purchase.
- **The product page's displayed discount label is not always the same as the plan-level card label.** For example, a page tab can say a family-wide discount while an individual card shows a different rounded discount. The published rates are reproduced as displayed, not recalculated.
- **Backups:** the old official FAQ's URL now redirects to the Help Center home. Its indexed official text states €1/month, backup schedules, and €29.99 restore, but a current 2026 help article only says one-time backups require additional payment and gives no rate. These old prices and schedules are therefore unclear, not confirmed.
- **Availability:** Terms provide a 99.95% service level with a credit formula; an uptime help page says 99.98% availability. The latter is not the contractual SLA. The company footer links an official status-update log at the billing portal.
