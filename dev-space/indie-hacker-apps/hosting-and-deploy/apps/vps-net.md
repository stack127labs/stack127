## Cost examples

All amounts below are **our calculations**, not VPS.NET quotes for these exact workloads. Use public monthly USD prices and exclude account credit as requested in the workload definition. Tax is excluded from arithmetic because the MSA says tax may be added and the signup checkout could not be inspected. The price pages do not state which datacenter each rate applies to.

### A. Small commercial static site

Inputs: 100 MB deployed files; 1,000,000 HTTP requests and 100 GB outbound/month; 20 builds of five minutes; private repository; one custom domain.

Candidate: V1-19 lists 0.5 GB RAM, 1 core, 25 GB NVMe and 2 TB bandwidth.

- Formula: one V1-19 monthly service = $5.50 to $6.05/month before tax.
- The deployed files fit within the listed disk. The 100 GB traffic amount is below the listed 2 TB bandwidth if the plan's bandwidth counter includes the same outbound traffic unit.
- VPS.NET does not provide a documented managed build meter or repository import flow. The builds need a customer-run build host or customer-managed tooling on the VPS.
- Result: known server subtotal $5.50 to $6.05/month; exact total is unknown because the two official plan pages disagree and build, traffic direction, domain certificate and tax details are not fully specified.

### B. Small commercial app API

Inputs: stateless HTTP app; 1,000,000 requests/month; assumed 100 ms active processor work and 250 ms elapsed per request; 256 MiB working memory; 100 GB outbound; same builds, private repo and domain as A. Capacity rule: at least 1 vCPU and 1 GiB RAM for 730 hours.

Candidate: V2-19 is the smallest listed tier with 1 GB RAM and 2 cores. Those labels appear to meet the stated floor but are not guaranteed equivalent to 1 GiB and 1 vCPU.

- Formula: one V2-19 monthly service = $11.00 to $12.10/month before tax.
- The plan's listed 3 TB bandwidth is above the workload's 100 GB, but outbound/aggregate accounting is not explained.
- Result: known server subtotal $11.00 to $12.10/month; exact total and technical capacity fit are unknown because plan pages disagree on price and VPS.NET does not define the core or RAM units against the workload floor. No app throughput is inferred from these specs.

### C. App plus persistent data

Inputs: B plus one Postgres database with at least 1 GiB memory, 10 GB storage, daily backup retained seven days and 10 GB/month app-to-database traffic. Database is self-managed on the VPS.

Candidate: V4-19 lists 4 cores, 4 GB RAM and 100 GB NVMe, leaving more nominal headroom for the OS, API and database than a 2 GB plan. Backup Protection starts at $5/month, but the plan-specific rate and seven-day retention are unknown.

- Formula using the official price listings: ($27.00 to $29.70 for V4) + backup from $5.00/month = from $32.00 to $34.70/month before tax.
- This range is not an exact quote: it combines two conflicting V4 price listings and a backup “from” price. The product's snapshot frequency and retention do not show that it supplies seven daily recovery points.
- Result: known lower-bound illustration from $32.00 to $34.70/month; total and compatibility with the exact daily-retention requirement remain unknown. Domain, external backup storage or paid third-party database services are not priced here.
