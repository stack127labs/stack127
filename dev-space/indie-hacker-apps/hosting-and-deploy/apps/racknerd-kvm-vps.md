- The current KVM table and cart agree on seven regular tiers. A RackNerd blog article still shows an older 512 MB price and disk size. The current product/cart pages are used above; the older blog copy is recorded as stale.
- The KVM page names 12 locations and says “France” without naming a city. The general datacenter page lists 20 locations across all RackNerd services. I use the KVM-specific list for KVM availability and do not infer that KVM is sold in all 20.
- Current specials show prices and Order Now links without quantity or expiry data. Older, dated promotion carts show zero available. The checkout configuration was not opened, so stock for current regular and special listings remains unpublished.
- Product and specials pages show annual or monthly listed prices, but do not name a separate renewal rate. Renewal invoices are generated 14 days before due date; the renewal help page does not confirm that promotion prices remain fixed.

- The KVM offering is an unmanaged virtual server. Runtime, framework, build, database, worker, preview, and app rollback fields often describe customer-managed software rather than RackNerd-managed product features. Those distinctions are stated in the affected rows.
- The AUP label in the footer resolves to the Terms page, while opening the standalone `/acceptable-use-policy` address failed. The prohibited-use rules were collected from the accessible Terms page.
