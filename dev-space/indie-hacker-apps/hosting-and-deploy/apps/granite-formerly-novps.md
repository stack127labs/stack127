## Cost examples — our calculation

Calculation date: **2026-11-05**. These are our calculations from public prices, not vendor-quoted totals. Native currency is **USD**, confirmed by pricing-page structured data. Tax inclusion is not stated; no currency conversion, tax estimate, introductory credit or annual discount is applied. One paying builder, one commercial project, monthly billing, custom domain; registration stays outside the hosting amount. No optional support, replicas or other apps are added.

Input sources, all checked 2026-11-05:

- [Pricing](https://granite.so/pricing) and [project billing](https://docs.granite.so/articles/2-pricing): Hobby USD 9/month, 15 shared usage credits, USD 1 per extra credit. Included credits expire each cycle. Extra credits are recurring purchases until reduced.
- [App/worker prices](https://docs.granite.so/articles/45-pricing): XS 1 vCPU/1 GiB, 5 credits per replica/month; hourly proration based on existence. This is the smallest nominal allocation meeting the server requirement.
- [PostgreSQL prices](https://docs.granite.so/articles/27-pricing): Medium 2 vCPU/2 GB RAM/45 GB disk, 24 credits/node/month; backup tariff USD 0.05/GB/month. The XS/Small rows say 1 **GB**, without a byte definition, so they are not treated as verified 1 **GiB**.
- [Registry prices](https://docs.granite.so/articles/40-pricing): USD 0.035/GiB/month average stored. [Project traffic policy](https://docs.granite.so/articles/2-pricing) explicitly says inbound, outbound and between-service traffic is not billed.
- [Source build workflow](https://granite.so/blog/granite-april-2026-update), [custom domain and SSL](https://docs.granite.so/articles/25-how-to-connect-a-custom-domain-on-granite), [database restore](https://docs.granite.so/articles/46-restore-from-backup) and [conflicting automatic-backup statement](https://granite.so/blog/railway-pricing-2026-what-web-worker-postgres-costs) establish the workflow and qualifications below. Build tariff and the exact requested backup window are unresolved.

Let U be the project's usage credits for a listed billing month, and E its selected recurring extra-credit purchase. Project invoice model: **USD 9 + E**, with at least enough E to cover usage beyond 15 credits. The mathematical minimum supplement is **max(0, U − 15)** at USD 1/credit; purchase increments are not published. Actual E can be higher and stays selected until changed. Do not charge USD 9 plus all resource prices without applying the included allowance.

Let R be average retained registry storage in **GiB** and K the unresolved build usage/cost expressed in the same credit equivalent, if applicable. Neither is silently assigned zero. The source repository's privacy entitlement, any external build service required, unpriced mandatory line items and tax treatment must be resolved before an exact all-in quote. A 100 MB site is not a 100 MB container-image-storage measurement.

### A. Small commercial static site

Inputs: 100 MB deployed files; 1,000,000 HTTP requests/month; 100 GB outbound; 20 builds × 5 minutes = **100 build minutes**; one private source repository; one custom domain.

Candidate route: serve files from an XS web container, with files baked into the image and platform custom-domain SSL. No separately mounted upload volume is needed for this candidate. This is a container-hosted static site, not a verified global static-site edge product.

Known listed-month compute usage: **5 credits**. Documented traffic fee for 100 GB: **USD 0**. Registry contribution: **0.035 × R**. Build contribution K is unknown; direct private-repository eligibility is not explicit in the checked integration instructions.

Formula for the known billing model: **U_A = 5 + 0.035R + K**; sufficient-credit model **9 + max(0, U_A − 15)** USD/month, subject to purchase increments and any other unpriced required costs. The known project minimum is **USD 9/month**, conditionally also the project invoice if all usage fits 15 credits and E = 0. **No exact complete workload total:** 100 build minutes and private-source workflow are not fully priced/verified, and retained image size is unspecified. Request throughput is untested.

### B. Small commercial app API

Inputs: 1,000,000 requests × 0.1 CPU-second = **100,000 CPU-seconds**; 1,000,000 × 0.25 elapsed seconds = **250,000 request-seconds**; assumed working memory 256 MiB; required server allowance at least 1 vCPU/1 GiB for **730 hours**; 100 GB outbound; same builds/private source/domain as A.

Candidate: one always-on XS replica, 1 vCPU/1 GiB, listed **5 credits/month**. Processor/request arithmetic describes the input, not the billing meter or proof that the instance handles the workload. Idle server hours remain billable. Traffic contribution is **USD 0** under the published rule.

Listed-month formula: **U_B = 5 + 0.035R + K**, then the same shared-credit formula as A. The public docs explain hourly proration and illustrate a 30-day month but do not give a universal hourly divisor for a 730-hour example. **USD 9 is the known monthly project minimum, not an exact 730-hour all-in quote.** Exact hourly normalization, source-build tariff and any unpriced required item remain unresolved. No speed or concurrency benchmark was performed.

### C. App plus persistent data

Inputs: all of B, plus one PostgreSQL with at least 1 GiB memory/10 GB disk, daily backup retained seven days, and 10 GB/month app-to-DB transfer.

Conservative database candidate: **Medium, 2 GB RAM/45 GB disk, 24 credits/month**, one primary node. This avoids silently equating the cheapest node's 1 GB label with 1 GiB. App XS adds 5 credits: **29 listed-month usage credits**. Current docs permit every compute size on Hobby; its 350 MiB app-volume cap does not limit the database's included 45 GB disk. Internal 10 GB transfer costs **USD 0** under the traffic rule.

Let B_avg be the average **GB of backups actually subject to billing**. Conditional usage formula: **U_C = 29 + 0.05B_avg + 0.035R + K**. Minimum-sufficient-credit algebra gives **USD 9 + (29 − 15) + 0.05B_avg + 0.035R + K = USD 23 + 0.05B_avg + 0.035R + K/month**, before any other unresolved required charge, purchase-increment rounding and tax adjustment. **USD 23 is a listed-month project-plus-compute baseline, not a complete total or an exact 730-hour quote.**

**Requested backup fit remains unverified.** PostgreSQL setup/restore docs do not publish a daily frequency with seven-day retention. PITR is bounded by the oldest available backup, not a stated seven-day promise. PG pricing bills scheduled/manual backups; the official blog says automatic system backups are free. Actual stored bytes, which automatic copies are billed, and backup frequency/retention remain unresolved. Do not assume seven full uncompressed copies or label the requested backup requirement satisfied. Extra restored databases and read replicas would incur their own charges.
