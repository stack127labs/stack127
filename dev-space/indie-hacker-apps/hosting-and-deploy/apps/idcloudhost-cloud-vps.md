## Cost examples

Prices are in Indonesian rupiah (IDR). The official price cards do not identify the region attached to each amount and do not say whether tax is included. Terms say 11% VAT/PPN; a later official tax notice says 12% from 2025-01-01. These are **our calculations**, not vendor quotes, and are pre-tax known subtotals only. No startup credit or optional support is included.

### A. Small commercial static site

- **Fit:** A VM can serve the files, but builds and repository access are customer-managed. One million requests and 100 GB outbound are not separately priced on the published unlimited-bandwidth offer.
- **Inputs and formula:** Basic Standard entry shape, 2 cores / 2 GB RAM / 20 GB storage: 1 × Rp 87,000 per month = **Rp 87,000/month**. The workload’s 20 builds × 5 minutes = 100 build minutes, which must run on the VM or external CI.
- **Result:** Our known VM subtotal is **Rp 87,000/month**. This is not an exact all-in total: Public IPv4 price and tax inclusion are not published, and domain registration is outside the listed VM plan.
- **Sources:** https://idcloudhost.com/pricing/ ; https://idcloudhost.com/blog/vps-unlimited-bandwidth/ ; https://idcloudhost.com/panduan/cara-install-waha-di-idcloudhost/ (checked 2026-11-06).

### B. Small commercial app API

- **Fit:** The 2-core / 2-GB entry shape exceeds the scenario’s minimum server floor of 1 vCPU and 1 GiB. VM capacity does not guarantee the requested throughput or response time.
- **Inputs and formula:** Basic Standard monthly rate: 1 × Rp 87,000/month = **Rp 87,000/month**. If billed hourly for an assumed 730-hour month: 730 × Rp 120/hour = Rp 87,600. The listed monthly rate is the lower known compute amount for this example.
- **Result:** Our known VM subtotal is **Rp 87,000/month**. The product lists unlimited premium bandwidth and no request meter, but the exact all-in amount remains unknown because IPv4 price and tax inclusion are not settled.
- **Sources:** https://idcloudhost.com/pricing/ ; https://idcloudhost.com/blog/vps-unlimited-bandwidth/ ; https://idcloudhost.com/tos/ (checked 2026-11-06).

### C. App plus persistent Postgres

- **Fit:** Postgres can be installed and maintained on a VM, or bought through the separate Managed Database product. The VM plan does not include a managed Postgres instance.
- **Inputs and formula:** Listed Basic Standard 2-core / 4-GB RAM / 60-GB storage shape: 1 × Rp 225,000/month = **Rp 225,000/month**. Optional Cloud VPS backups/snapshots cost extra according to resources used, but the rate and seven-day retention are not published.
- **Result:** Our known VM subtotal is **Rp 225,000/month**. This is partial, not an exact total; managed database pricing, backup cost/retention, IPv4 charge, tax inclusion, and any pricing treatment for the assumed 10 GB of app-to-database traffic are not published. The chosen 4-GB shape is a price example, not a workload performance guarantee.
- **Sources:** https://idcloudhost.com/pricing/ ; https://idcloudhost.com/tos/ ; https://idcloudhost.com/cloud-vps/ (checked 2026-11-06).

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Tax:** The terms state 11% VAT/PPN on transactions and service fees. An official December 2024 notice states 12% applies to transactions starting January 1, 2025. The pricing card does not say whether tax is included, so I did not adjust the calculations.
- **Entry price:** The homepage and an older Cloud VPS guide say the service starts at Rp 50,000/month; current itemized eXtreme plan cards begin at Rp 87,000/month. The lower offer has no public shape or matching price detail in the pages checked.
- **Regions:** A May 2025 Cloud VPS order guide said eXtreme was then only in South-JKT. A newer 2026 guide lists South-JKT01, West-JKT02, and Singapore SGP01. The current complete region matrix and region-specific price differences were not found.
- **SLA:** Cloud VPS is included in the terms’ SLA table. That table lists Epsilon as a Cloud VPS location but gives no matching percentage for Epsilon; it gives percentages for some data centers not listed in the Cloud VPS row. The public status uptime chart is platform-wide, not Cloud VPS-only.
- **Pricing and extra charges:** The Console pricing estimate was not opened because it is account-side. The public price cards do not settle Public IPv4 charges, backup rates, or tax inclusion. Cost examples therefore show known VM subtotals, not guaranteed invoices.
- **Backup wording:** Terms first contain a generic “VPS” backup exclusion, then specifically say Cloud VPS Console backups and snapshots are available as paid add-ons. I followed the product-specific Console clause and did not infer a free backup.
- **Trial and signup:** A 2025 guide describes a promo credit after a first top-up of at least Rp 55,000 if a promo code is active. It does not establish a current guaranteed trial, card requirement, or eligible-country list.
- **Other not-published items:** No public numeric account quota, CPU sharing designation, runtime architecture matrix, throughput cap, server-side app-log retention, managed Git build allowance, app-level rollback, or fixed support response time was found.
