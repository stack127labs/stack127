## Lifecycle and current status

OCI Compute is active and offered to new customers. I found no Compute shutdown, acquisition, or retirement notice for the requested `VM.Standard.E2.1.Micro`, `VM.Standard.A1.Flex`, or `VM.Standard.E5.Flex` shapes. I checked the Compute release notes, current shape list, OCI blog, Oracle News, and OCI service-change notices. The release notes show current Compute updates through October 22, 2026. The one upcoming Compute-specific change I found retires version 1 audit events for instance pools and cluster networks on June 30, 2027; it does not retire these shapes.

The current shape guide lists E5 Flex and A1 Flex. It also lists the E2.1.Micro as an Always Free micro shape. The broader E2 family is in the previous-generation section and has an old end-of-orderability date; that status does not remove the separately listed E2.1.Micro. Shape availability and host capacity can vary by region.

There are two different A1 free-use figures in Oracle's current materials. The Always Free account limit is 1,500 OCPU-hours and 9,000 GB-hours per month (2 OCPUs and 12 GB total). The Cloud Price List separately says a paid tenancy gets the first 3,000 OCPU-hours and 18,000 GB-hours free. I keep those offers separate below. After a Free Trial ends, paid trial resources are reclaimed unless the tenancy is upgraded; Always Free resources continue within their limits. Excess A1 resources in an unupgraded trial tenancy are disabled and then deleted after 30 days.

## Cost examples

All amounts below are **our calculations**, not Oracle quotes. They use 730 hours per month and USD pay-as-you-go API values fetched 2026-11-07. The requested commercial region is US East (Ashburn), `us-ashburn-1`; compute and storage rates below are USD. Egress uses the North America/Europe/UK meter. The first 10,240 GB/month of egress is free. Examples B and C assume paid use and that the Always Free volume allowance is already used or unavailable. Rates exclude tax, discounts, support add-ons, and separate products unless stated.

- **A — small commercial static site:** one Always Free E2.1.Micro VM, default 50 GB boot volume, one public IPv4, and 100 GB outbound. Compute, volume, public IP, and traffic are USD $0/month while the documented Always Free quotas are available and the account remains active. This is our calculation from published inclusions and the USD API. It excludes domain registration and any separately chosen Git or build service. The one-million-request performance target was not tested.
- **B — small commercial API:** E5 Flex, 1 OCPU plus 1 GB memory, running 730 hours; 50 GB balanced boot volume; 100 GB outbound. Compute = `730 × ($0.03 + $0.002) = $23.36`. Volume = `50 × ($0.0255 + 10 × $0.0017) = $2.125`. Public IPv4 = `$0`; 100 GB outbound is within the 10,240 GB free monthly allowance. Known subtotal = **USD $25.485/month**. This is our calculation and excludes domain, external builds, support add-ons, discounts, and tax. App capacity is untested.
- **A2 — small Ampere Linux test service:** A1 Flex with 1 OCPU and 6 GB memory for 730 hours uses 730 OCPU-hours and 4,380 GB-memory-hours. Both fit within the Always Free tenancy limits of 1,500 OCPU-hours and 9,000 GB-memory-hours, so compute is **USD $0/month** while those monthly allowances are available. A pay-as-you-go tenancy also has separate 3,000/18,000 free monthly A1 meter allowances. Storage beyond remaining Always Free volume capacity and egress beyond the free allowance are excluded.
- **C — API plus Postgres:** E5 Flex, 2 OCPUs plus 8 GB memory, running 730 hours; 50 GB boot volume plus a separate 50 GB database volume; 100 GB outbound; seven daily backups retained. Compute = `730 × (2 × $0.03 + 8 × $0.002) = $55.48`. Balanced volumes = `100 × ($0.0255 + 10 × $0.0017) = $4.25`. Public IPv4 = `$0`; outbound is within the allowance. Known Compute-and-volume subtotal = **USD $59.73/month**. This is our calculation. Five volume backups are included in Always Free; the exact storage cost for two additional backups depends on retained changed data and is not included. PostgreSQL resource fit and the 10 GB app-to-database transfer assumption are not benchmarked.

Scenarios B and C assume Linux, no paid image, no support add-on, no load balancer, no external domain cost, no discount, and no taxes. Builds are customer-managed. These subtotals include the stated Compute, block volumes, public IPv4, and outbound transfer only; they are not purchase quotes.
## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Currency correction:** the earlier `oracle.com/ca-en/cloud/price-list/` values are from Oracle Canada’s English price page and are CAD, not USD. The user-supplied `currencyCode=USD` API response fetched 2026-11-07 provides the USD rates used in the table and examples. The October 8, 2026 Global Price List PDF agrees with the USD API for E5, Block Volume, and North America egress, so the apparent rate conflicts are resolved.
- **Regional egress:** the USD API lists different rates by origin group after the common 10,240 GB monthly free tier: $0.0085/GB for North America/Europe/UK, $0.025/GB for APAC/Japan/South America, and $0.05/GB for Middle East/Africa. Ashburn uses the first group.
- **Public IPv4:** Oracle’s developer article explicitly says it does not charge for ephemeral or reserved public IPs, including reserved addresses not associated with an instance. The USD API’s 668 products contain no public IP/IPv4 meter. The networking guide describes ephemeral and reserved addresses and their assignment limits; other services such as NAT gateways and load balancers can still have separate charges.
- **Trial and Always Free:** the current Free Tier doc says 1,500 OCPU-hours and 9,000 GB-hours for an Always Free tenancy; the USD API says a pay-as-you-go tenancy has 3,000/18,000 free A1 hours. These apply to different account classes, not a single quota.
- **Free Tier inactivity:** Oracle’s FAQ says an account unused for 30 days may be suspended or terminated; another official expiration page refers to accounts active when used within the last 60 days. This conflict is retained.
- **Signup geography:** Free Trial signup help says some countries must contact Sales but does not list every affected country. I did not attempt signup or test a card.
- **Account-specific limits:** public service-limit docs describe defaults, but actual quotas and host capacity are tenancy/region-specific and were not read from an account.
## Field problems

- Field 05 asks for one cheapest plan, but OCI Compute is metered infrastructure rather than a fixed VM plan. E2.1.Micro is the free entry point; the other examples are named shape configurations. USD pricing uses the API snapshot, not Oracle Canada's CAD page.
- Field 09's exact country eligibility and accepted payment method vary by location. Oracle publishes a process but not a complete public list of countries where Free Trial is unavailable.
- Fields 05, 11, and 13 now use the USD price-list API; the former price conflicts were a CAD/USD mix-up. The exact total for Scenario C still excludes the incremental backup storage because the workload does not specify changed data retained per backup.
- Field 15's A1/E5 and free-tier examples are arithmetic, not application benchmarks. The E2 Micro can have low CPU capacity; the API and Postgres cases require a chosen capacity and customer-managed guest setup.
- Fields 18–31 describe VM infrastructure rather than a managed app platform. Frameworks, build pipeline, app database, HTTPS setup, release rollback, and guest logs are managed by the customer or another service.
- Field 17: a region selection is not a blanket residency promise for account metadata, operational data, or support records.
