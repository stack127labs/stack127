## Conflicts and unknowns

- The pricing page's selector controls whether amounts are displayed for 30 days, 24 hours, 60 minutes, or 60 seconds. Checking both 24-hour and 30-day displays resolves the apparent mismatch: object storage is CHF 0.001/GB/day or CHF 0.03/GB/30 days now, matching the dated notice; it rises to CHF 0.002/GB/day from 2027-01-01.
- Top-up VAT is explicit for Switzerland and the EU; the checked public pages do not give a full country eligibility list or every non-EU tax treatment.
- The page states the 100 GB/24-hour server allowance and CHF 2 per additional 100 GB, but does not explain how allowances pool across servers or how partial overage is rounded.
- The same selector also changes compute, SSD and Bulk volumes, snapshots, floating IPv4, own IP prefixes, and load-balancer amounts. The 24-hour prices for compute in the original record were correct. SSD volume, Bulk volume, snapshots, floating IPv4, and load-balancer rows had instead used 30-day display amounts as if they were per day; these rates and the scenario C subtotal are corrected here. The traffic-overage price remains per 100 GB above a daily threshold.
- Swiss workload infrastructure location is explicit in the Terms. The German-only privacy policy and public DPA materials do not map every administrative data category to a storage location.
- The public status page listed upcoming network work on November 9 and 16, 2026 and historical incidents. No reliability benchmark was calculated.
- GPU2 rates changed for new or resized GPU2 instances on September 1, 2026; ordinary Flex and Plus flavors were not part of that notice.
- Checked official News and Engineering Blog for acquisition, shutdown, and sunset notices. The only ownership event found was Cyberlink's announced 40% investment; no full acquisition or shutdown notice was found.
