## Current status and ownership

DeployHQ's core deploy automation service is active. Its website also lists two beta hosting add-ons: managed VPS hosting on DigitalOcean and static hosting on Cloudflare. These are separate offers; prices are currently free during early access and marked TBA after launch. DeployHQ's About page places it in the saas.group family. Its Terms and Privacy pages identify saas.group LLC as the legal operator. The service privacy page says its own servers are in UK data centres; this does not make DeployHQ the operator of a customer's target server.

I checked DeployHQ's About page, Blog, public changelog/feedback portal, saas.group's official site and Blog, and DeployHQ's status page for acquisition, shutdown, or service-retirement notices. I found no DeployHQ-specific acquisition or shutdown notice. The blog and docs are being updated, and the status service is current. The status page records a resolved dashboard/project-page incident on 2026-11-05; details are under field 32.
