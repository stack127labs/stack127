- **Scenario A — small commercial static site:** Compatible with a CloudPanel static HTML site. **Our calculation:** CE software fee = €0/month. Total = €0 + server + domain/DNS + network/delivery. The full monthly total is unknown because those rates come from the selected infrastructure and domain providers. The 20 five-minute builds are not a CloudPanel-hosted build allowance.
- **Scenario B — small commercial API:** Compatible with a Node.js, PHP, or Python site, but capacity at the scenario's request load is unverified. **Our calculation:** CE software fee = €0/month; provide a VM meeting at least 1 core, 2 GB RAM, and 10 GB disk, then add that provider's monthly server and transfer charges. Full total is unknown. If CloudPanel Managed is selected, add the selected plan fee (Starter €29/month, Business €49/month, or Pro €99/month) and the AWS bill charged separately to the customer's account.
