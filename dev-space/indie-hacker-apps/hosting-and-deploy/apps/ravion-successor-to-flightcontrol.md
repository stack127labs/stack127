# Ravion, successor to Flightcontrol

Official URL: https://www.ravion.com/
apps.md status: Active. Status is correct for Ravion; Flightcontrol remains available to existing customers while its migration tooling is incomplete.
Research start: 2026-11-07 07:06:40 UTC
Research finish: 2026-11-07 07:26:23 UTC
Minutes taken: 19.72

Transition: Ravion’s public site calls it Flightcontrol’s successor. The company says operating Flightcontrol showed it needed more control, flexibility, and operational depth, so it built a broader platform for developers and DevOps. The exact launch or effective transition date is not published on the official pages checked. Flightcontrol’s docs were last updated August 11, 2026 and already described Ravion as successor; they said one-click migration was not ready and told existing customers to keep using Flightcontrol. The current Ravion notice says existing customers’ dashboard and docs stay where they are. The old marketing domain redirects to Ravion; the old dashboard still serves a Flightcontrol login, and its status page remains available. Ravion’s migration guide says one-click import is planned, with manual migration steps available now. No acquisition or shutdown notice appeared in the checked company pages, blog, or changelog. The company site has a blog but no newsroom link.

Scope: Hosted Ravion control plane managing infrastructure in the customer’s AWS account; Team monthly billing used for the cost examples. AWS service charges are separate and billed by AWS. No signup, login, deployment, benchmark, or form submission was made. Prices are USD; tax treatment was not stated on the pricing page. Every source below was checked on 2026-11-07. Monthly and annual pricing controls were both selected on the pricing estimator.
