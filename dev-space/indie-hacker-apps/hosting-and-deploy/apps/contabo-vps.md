## Current status and notices

The apps.md row lists Contabo VPS as Active. Contabo is still advertising VPS plans and has public ordering instructions for new customers. The official status page checked on 2026-11-06 still listed a Customer Control Panel synchronization incident last updated on 3 November. It said new users might not be able to access the panel and new orders might be held. The page did not show a resolution in the copy checked, so current signup flow is not fully verified.

Contabo changed its VPS product lineup in 2026. The former Cloud VPS line is now called Core VPS; Performance VPS and Max Performance VPS are separate tiers. This record covers the current Core, Performance, and Storage VPS families. Max Performance VPS is sold as a VDS and is not counted as a VPS plan here.

The Contabo Company News archive and blog were checked for shutdown, sunset, or acquisition notices. I found none in those pages. The latest relevant notices say that selected entry-level VPS prices changed in 2026 and that a one-time setup fee began for Core VPS 4 and Storage VPS 10 orders on 3 November 2026. Absence from the pages checked does not prove that no notice exists elsewhere.
