## Cost examples

These are our calculations from public AceCloud prices, not vendor quotes. INR amounts below are Noida, India, before GST/tax unless noted. Where an input is unpublished or rate cards disagree, the exact total remains unknown.

### A. Small commercial static site

- Fit: Possible as a self-managed web server on a VM. Compute has no managed static-site build, private-repository deploy, preview, or certificate workflow.
- Inputs: S3a.medium, 1 vCPU/4 GB RAM; ₹1,349/month fixed plan or ₹2.31/hour; one public IP included; FAQ says no transfer fee for unmetered bandwidth.
- Formula: Fixed monthly compute = ₹1,349. Hourly alternative for 730 hours = ₹2.31 × 730 = ₹1,686.30.
- Result: Known compute subtotal is ₹1,349/month before tax on a monthly plan. Exact total is unknown because the selected boot-volume size/charge and any domain/TLS choices are not fully stated. Builds run on the VM or external CI; no AceCloud build-minutes price is published. The service terms give no numeric fair-use transfer threshold.
- Sources: https://acecloud.ai/cloud/compute/standard-instance/ ; https://acecloud.ai/pricing/ ; https://docs.acecloud.ai/knowledge-base/compute ; https://acecloud.ai/legal/terms-of-services-agreement/

### B. Small commercial app API

- Fit: S3a.medium is the smallest listed Standard VM that meets the required 1-vCPU/1-GiB floor. Vendor does not guarantee scenario throughput from flavor specs alone.
- Inputs: 730 hours; ₹2.31/hour on Noida INR rate card; ₹1,349 fixed monthly; 100 GB outbound listed as unmetered/no transfer fee; one public IP included.
- Formula: Hourly compute basis = ₹2.31 × 730 = ₹1,686.30. Monthly fixed option = ₹1,349.
- Result: Known compute subtotal is ₹1,349/month before tax if taking a monthly plan. Storage, domain/TLS configuration, and any add-ons are not included. No per-request price is published for VM-hosted apps.
- Sources: https://acecloud.ai/cloud/compute/standard-instance/ ; https://acecloud.ai/pricing/ ; https://docs.acecloud.ai/knowledge-base/billing/pricing-policies-and-best-practices

### C. App plus persistent Postgres

- Fit: Use the same S3a.medium app VM and a separate managed PostgreSQL service, or run Postgres yourself on a VM. Smallest managed tier shown is 2 vCPU/8 GB RAM, above the scenario minimum.
- Noida INR DBaaS table inputs: DB.2.8 is ₹9.77/hour or ₹5,704/month. App VM fixed monthly price is ₹1,349.
- Alternative PostgreSQL product page: small 2-vCPU/8-GB tier is ₹4.73/hour; region is not identified on that page. This differs from Noida DBaaS table for the same stated capacity, so it is not combined with the Noida quote.
- Separate DB storage input: general DBaaS page states $0.10/GB/month with no region stated. Our calculation for 10 GB is 10 × $0.10 = $1/month, kept in USD without conversion.
- Formula using Noida DBaaS table: app ₹1,349/month + DBaaS ₹5,704/month + storage $1/month. Mixed-currency subtotal; tax excluded.
- Result: Not an exact total for the requested workload. Managed PostgreSQL advertises hourly backups and point-in-time recovery, but retention of at least 7 days is not specified. Public DBaaS rates disagree, storage price is USD without region, and the table does not settle the 10-GB/7-day backup total.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Standard entry price: Standard table and current FAQ show ₹1,349/month for Noida S3a.medium; one fetched version of the general pricing FAQ showed ₹1,015/month. I could not establish whether the lower value is stale or a different configuration.
- Refunds: pricing FAQ describes usage-based monthly refunds and possible partial refunds on longer plans; billing policy says started fixed plans are non-refundable; legal refund policy allows limited discretionary cases.
- Trial credits: current FAQ says ₹20,000/about $200 and 30 days; billing-policy document says credits are added after first project order and expire after 7 days.
- Payment methods: current pricing FAQ, general FAQ, and billing policy list different methods/processors; country-by-country eligibility is not fully mapped.
- Region prices: detailed public VM tables are Noida/India/INR. The August 2026 company guide lists approximate hourly USD starts for Noida, Mumbai, and Atlanta without full VM dimensions. Product pages direct customers to detailed pricing or sales for Mumbai/Atlanta.
- Data-center scope: Standard FAQ says 10+ data centers, while current company/product pages identify 3 data centers and named regions. The complete Compute region list is unclear.
- Managed database rate: PostgreSQL page gives ₹4.73/hour for 2 vCPU/8 GB; Noida DBaaS table gives ₹9.77/hour and ₹5,704/month for DB.2.8. Storage and region scopes also differ.
- Transfer: pricing FAQ says unmetered/no transfer fee; service terms refer to fair-use limits without a numerical cap.
- Recovery: service terms say cancelled instance data cannot be recovered; Terms of Use allow a limited support-mediated recovery/export window after suspension or trial expiry.
- Status history: SLA mentions status links to be provided to customers; I did not find an official public status history page in the checked legal, product, FAQ, or newsroom pages.
- Calculator: public estimate page loaded but asks to create a project before configuring a resource. I did not create a project, log in, or submit anything.
