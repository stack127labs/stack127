No AI generation is required for these fixed workloads, so no external AI provider or optional token purchase is added. Included AI allowances are not treated as required build CPU.

### A. Small commercial static site

Inputs: 100 MB deployed files; 1,000,000 HTTP requests/month; 100 GB outbound; 20 builds × 5 minutes = **100 build minutes**; private repo; one custom domain.

Select **Plus**, the cheapest published paid custom-domain/always-on option:

- Monthly subscription = **1 × $10 = $10**.
- Published capacity: 1 vCPU, 1 GB RAM, 10 GB storage and 500 GB bandwidth.
- Declared site files are 0.1 GB if MB/GB are interpreted as decimal. Those file bytes and 100 GB outbound are below the stated plan allowances in isolation.
- Custom domain and TLS are included. Static output can be served from a persistent native runtime with eligible edge caching; no separate mandatory CDN purchase is stated.

Builds, dependencies, logs, retained artifacts, preview resources and other counted transfer may consume the shared allowance. A separate per-request fee is not listed, but a complete one-million-request/build entitlement is not explicitly priced. Native static support is documented; the selected plan has not been load tested.

**Known subscription component: $10/month. Full A expression: $10 + U_A; complete total unknown.** Free is excluded because this workload requires an own domain; that is not a claim that all commercial experimentation is forbidden on Free.

### B. Small commercial app API

Inputs: 1,000,000 requests/month; 100 ms active CPU and 250 ms elapsed/request; 256 MiB working memory; 100 GB outbound; A's 100 build minutes/private repo/domain. The comparison requires a server allocation with at least **one vCPU and 1 GiB RAM for 730 hours**.

Plus lists **1 GB**, without defining that as 1 GiB. The example runtime's memory_mb field does not settle the plan's entitlement units. Therefore select **Dev** as the conservative documented plan candidate: **2 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, 50 GB storage and 2 TB bandwidth for $50/month**. Four decimal GB exceeds 1 GiB. Configure one continuously running app with at least one vCPU and 1 GiB, leaving the remaining shared allowance for platform/application needs. Per-runtime allocation and available plan entitlements still need account verification.

- Monthly subscription = **1 × $50 = $50**.
- Workload active CPU = 1,000,000 × 0.100 = **100,000 CPU-seconds**.
- Sum of request elapsed time = 1,000,000 × 0.250 = **250,000 request-seconds**.

These request-time figures are workload inputs, not a per-function bill or proof of equal CPU performance. Dev is billed monthly; do not multiply its monthly fee by 730 hours. Always-on hosting is an operating mode, not an uptime promise.

**Known subscription component: $50/month. Full B expression: $50 + U_B; complete total unknown.** Plus's $10/month is a possible lower-price candidate only if the provider confirms that its 1 GB RAM meets the required 1 GiB allocation and all shared-resource conditions; it is not used as a verified qualifying plan.

### C. App plus persistent PostgreSQL and backups

Add to B: PostgreSQL with at least 1 GiB RAM and 10 GB data storage, daily backup retained seven days, and 10 GB/month private app-to-database traffic.

Use **Dev's $50/month shared plan** as a conditional capacity candidate, not two complete Dev plans:

- Allocate at least 1 GiB to the app and 1 GiB to PostgreSQL: **2 GiB** combined, below 4 GB even under decimal GB interpretation. Confirm per-service runtime allocation and overhead; no independent full plan allowance is granted to each node.
- Official PostgreSQL 15/16/17 templates have persistent storage; version 16 with pgvector is also available.
- For illustration only, assume 100 MB app files in addition to 10 GB database data: **10.1 GB** declared data. B itself does not specify a source/deployed-code size.
- Let **b** be the measured stored GB of one daily backup and **s** all other system/log/artifact storage. Seven equal-size copies require **10.1 + 7b + s GB** within the 50 GB allowance. That leaves **39.9 GB** for copies and overhead; if s were zero, b could be at most **5.7 GB**. This is a storage-budget illustration, not the vendor's backup format or schedule.

Pricing explicitly includes database backups in plan storage, but neither pricing nor storage/PostgreSQL/terms pages establishes a daily schedule, seven-day retention, consistency method or complete restore workflow. Workload C's backup policy is therefore **not verified**. Do not infer seven full copies, free extra storage, or a usable transaction-consistent restore from the inclusion claim.

Private app/database connectivity is documented. Its 10 GB traffic charge/allowance accounting is not defined; private is not automatically zero cost. Potential backup storage beyond plan, independent backup tooling, operations and any necessary higher tier remain unpriced.

**Known subscription candidate: $50/month. Full C expression: $50 + U_C; complete total and backup compatibility unknown.** A versioned PostgreSQL hostname selects a runtime deployment; it does not restore earlier database contents.
