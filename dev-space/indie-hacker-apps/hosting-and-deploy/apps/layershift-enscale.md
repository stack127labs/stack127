### Formula and required quantities

Let `R[n,h]` be reserved cloudlets for node n in hour h, `U[n,h]` consumed cloudlets, and `pR/pD` the applicable reserved/dynamic hourly prices including the provider's environment-level discount. A resource model is:

`compute = sum over nodes/hours of [R × pR + max(U − R, 0) × pD]`

Then:

`hosting = app compute + builder compute + database compute + disk + internet/private transfer + required public IP + required TLS + any required extra node/licence/service`

Actual metering/rounding, maximum resource settings and discount rules must be checked against the dashboard tariff. These symbols expose missing inputs; they do not imply that every meter has a public per-GB price or that stopping removes all charges.
