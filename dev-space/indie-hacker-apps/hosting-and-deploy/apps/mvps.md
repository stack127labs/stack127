## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Bandwidth:** FAQ/Terms describe throttling to 1 Mbps after the included monthly allowance is exceeded; the refund page also says usage above 1 TB is charged at €3/TB. The pages do not explain how these rules interact with each plan's included limit, so the charge is recorded without assuming when it applies.
- **Guaranteed link speed:** FAQ says 1 Gbps shared uplink and guaranteed 300 Mbps (200 Mbps for VPS One); Terms say 100 Mbps guaranteed (70 Mbps for VPS One). Cyprus speed is not guaranteed. No independent test was run.
- **VAT:** all published prices are VAT-exclusive. The exact added amount varies by buyer status and location; the public configurator showed 0% for its displayed India example, which is not a universal tax quote.
- **Data-center locations:** the official FAQ lists ten countries, but the public pages checked do not provide city names. The provider also calls the footprint “10+ locations” on some pages.
- **Blog/news check:** reviewed the latest official blog entries and the security-maintenance notice; no acquisition, product sunset, or company shutdown notice was found in those recent posts. The Oct. 16 reboot was completed. The status page showed operational components.
- **No public price found:** monthly or annual disk add-ons, snapshots beyond the included manual/automated backups, custom data-volume prices, and any tax rate for a specific buyer were not published on the pages reviewed.
