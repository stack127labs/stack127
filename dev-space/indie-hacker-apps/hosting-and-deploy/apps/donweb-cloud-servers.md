## Conflicts and unknowns

- Marketing states 99.9% availability; the Cloud Server terms provide a best-effort monthly 99.5% SLA with narrowly defined eligibility and future service credits. The contractual 99.5% governs the credit promise.
- Global USD pricing shows a discounted $2.64/month entry value against $4.85/month, but the global price page does not identify the matching VM size or say whether taxes are included. The Argentina page does state prices include IVA.
- DonWeb’s new-contract help says longer terms can get larger discounts. The renewal guide says Cloud plans retain the same monthly value across renewal terms. Neither page establishes how long the current promotional price lasts or its exact renewal amount.
- Premium backup pricing, global tax inclusion, private-cloud backup region, transfer overage price/exhaustion behavior, stopped-VM billing, and a complete country eligibility list were not published in the pages checked.
- I checked the official product pricing pages, Cloud Server help collection and individual install/access/network/backup/billing articles, Argentina and international terms, Argentina privacy policy, marketplace, status history, DonWeb blog, DonWeb News, and the company history page. The terms page body showed only navigation in direct page opens; the official indexed Argentina terms content exposed the clauses used here. The international terms and an alternate official terms host were also tried.
