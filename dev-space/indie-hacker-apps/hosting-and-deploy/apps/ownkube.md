## Cost examples: our calculation

Calculated **2026-11-05** from the official public sources below, all checked that date. One paying builder, one commercial product, monthly billing, one custom domain, no starter/promo credits, annual discount or optional support. Domain registration remains external. Prices in **USD**; current terms say the payment provider adds applicable tax, so tax is not silently included or estimated.

These calculations use **Ownkube Compute**, not buyer-owned AWS. Own-cloud Starter's USD 0 controller fee is not a substitute for an AWS infrastructure total.

### Shared inputs and method

- [Pricing](https://ownkube.io/pricing) and [plan/rate details](https://ownkube.io/docs/billing/plans): Personal **USD 5/month loads USD 5 wallet credit**, three custom domains, 500 build minutes. PAYG lacks custom domains and caps outbound at 50 GB/month, so it does not satisfy A/B/C even if compute fits.
- [Reserved boxes](https://ownkube.io/docs/billing/plans): Spark 1 **0.25 vCPU/0.5 GiB, USD 5/month**; Spark 3 **1 vCPU/2 GiB, USD 19/month**. Spark 3 is the smallest reserved box with at least 1 vCPU/1 GiB. Metered plan limits are ceilings, not fixed guaranteed allocations.
- [Builds](https://ownkube.io/docs/deployments/builds): **20 × 5 = 100 wall-clock build minutes**, within Personal's 500 allowance. No build overage for these stated minutes, assuming no other builds. Each five-minute build is below the paid 30-minute limit.
- [Regional traffic](https://ownkube.io/docs/deployments/regions): **100 GB × USD 0.02 = USD 2 Germany**; **100 GB × USD 0.13 = USD 13 Mumbai**. Inbound and same-region private transfer free.
- [Custom domain/TLS](https://ownkube.io/docs/networking/custom-domains): one host fits Personal's three; ordinary managed certificate issue/renewal included.
- [Postgres](https://ownkube.io/docs/data/postgres) and [configuration](https://ownkube.io/docs/deployments/configuration): DB 1 **0.5 vCPU/1 GiB, USD 8/month**; storage **USD 0.25/GB/month per instance**, selector capacities in **GiB**. Backups opt-in on Personal, included in storage, seven-day PITR. Physical backup frequency is disputed below.
- [Wallet usage](https://ownkube.io/docs/billing/usage) and [payment terms](https://ownkube.io/terms): service usage draws shared prepaid balance. The monthly plan purchase is a credit load, not a separate USD 5 surcharge on all usage it covers.

Let U be total monthly wallet usage for these resources. With no prior credit, Personal funds the first USD 5; further funding covers **max(0, U − 5)**, subject to minimum top-up **USD 5** and purchase rules. Do not quote **USD 5 + U** as the net cost of the same usage.

Cash paid and usage are different. For example, a USD 7 usage subtotal with a USD 5 plan load leaves USD 2 to fund; the minimum USD 5 top-up means **USD 10 cash added and USD 3 wallet credit left**, before tax and any other usage. That is our calculation, not a second mandatory USD 5 platform fee every month. Existing balance and later months change the timing of cash purchases.

**Monthly time basis:** published reserved prices are monthly, while usage lines expose box-hours and the platform meters time. An exact hourly divisor/partial-minute rounding for a listed monthly box price is not published in the checked pricing/usage pages. For **730 hours**, write the app box contribution as **P × 730 / H**, where H is the vendor's monthly reference hours if it prorates that way; H is not established here. The full listed-month figures below use the published monthly box prices, not an invented exact P/730 tariff.

### B. Small commercial app API

Inputs: **1,000,000 × 0.1 = 100,000 CPU-seconds** and **1,000,000 × 0.25 = 250,000 elapsed request-seconds**; **256 MiB assumed working memory**; required server allocation at least **1 vCPU/1 GiB for 730 hours**; 100 GB outbound and A's builds/private repo/domain.

Main comparable candidate: **Spark 3, 1 vCPU/2 GiB, USD 19 per listed month**, Personal plan, sleep disabled for the 730-hour assumption. Spark 1/2 fail the vCPU minimum. Reservation does not establish dedicated physical-core performance or prove request throughput.

Known listed-month formula:
- Germany: **19 + 0 build overage + (100 × 0.02) = USD 21 wallet usage**.
- Mumbai: **19 + 0 + (100 × 0.13) = USD 32 wallet usage**.

For exact 730-hour app time, the formula is **19 × 730/H + 2** in Germany, or **19 × 730/H + 13** in Mumbai, before unresolved required line items/tax. H is not published, so these are not exact 730-hour totals. Personal's USD 5 wallet load covers part of the usage, rather than being charged twice.

For context, the alternative usage meter would use **100,000/60 vCPU-minutes** and, if 256 MiB stays resident all 730 hours, **0.25 × 730 × 60 = 10,950 GiB-minutes**. At published approximate rates that gives **(100,000/60 × 0.00041) + (10,950 × 0.00014) = USD 2.216333… compute**, before overhead, rounding and traffic. This is **our assumption-based meter calculation**, not a fixed-allocation quote: provider-set requests, actual memory/CPU and minute granularity can differ. It does not replace Spark 3 as the server-allocation comparison.

**No verified complete workload total** for the same private-source/image-charge/time-basis reasons as A. Sleep savings are excluded from the main always-on candidate.

### C. App plus persistent data

Inputs: all B plus **PostgreSQL at least 1 GiB/10 GB storage**, **daily backup retained seven days**, and **10 GB/month app-to-DB traffic**.

Candidate: Personal, Spark 3, **one DB 1 at USD 8/month with 1 GiB**, same environment/region, no standby and DB sleep off. Backups explicitly enabled. The default offered **10 GiB** disk is enough capacity for the requested 10 GB; keep this native capacity label separate from the **GB** billing unit. No external database needed.

Let S be the storage quantity the vendor bills as **GB** for that selected 10 GiB volume. The docs do not define the mapping, so the storage charge is **USD 0.25 × S/month**, not silently USD 2.50.

Known listed-month formulas:
- Germany: **19 app + 8 DB + 0.25S storage + 2 outbound + 0 build overage + 0 same-region 10 GB transfer = USD 29 + 0.25S**.
- Mumbai: **19 + 8 + 0.25S + 13 = USD 40 + 0.25S**.

If the billing system counts that selector as ten billable units, an explicitly conditional illustration is **S = 10**, giving **USD 31.50 Germany or USD 42.50 Mumbai**. **This mapping is our illustration, not a confirmed vendor conversion or complete total.** An exact byte-based conversion is not assumed. Exact 730-hour box/storage proration, additional mandatory line items, private-source eligibility and taxes remain unresolved.

Seven-day PITR is documented, but daily physical-copy frequency is not settled: the database guide describes a weekly base backup and continuous changes; the configuration reference says daily backups. Workload C's literal daily-copy requirement therefore remains **unverified**. PITR may offer useful recovery inside seven days without proving seven independent daily snapshots. No contractual restore-time promise or benchmark is assigned. Restoring takes the database offline; app rollback does not undo its data/schema.

### Own-cloud mode

A/B/C above are hosted-platform candidates. For own-cloud Starter, **Ownkube fee = USD 0**, but **total = AWS EC2 + EBS/storage + IP/networking/traffic + backup/S3 and other required AWS services**. Production additionally charges **USD 5 × requested vCPU-months + USD 1 × requested GiB-months** to the Ownkube wallet. External AWS prices are not independently researched here, and no AWS instance was chosen or created; no own-cloud all-in total is offered. Vendor approximate October EC2 examples are leads, not proof of a current full infrastructure bill.
