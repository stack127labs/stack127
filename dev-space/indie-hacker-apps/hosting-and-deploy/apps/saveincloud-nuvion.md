## Conflicts and unknowns

- **BRL versus USD:** the Brazilian plan table shows R$ hourly and 730-hour monthly rates, while the FAQ on the same pt-BR product page says “from US$4/month.” The visible controls show product-family tabs and language selection, not a currency/period switch. The English price page also displayed R$; the USD FAQ quote is unexplained.
- **Transfer allowance:** the Nuvion FAQ says outbound transfer is free starting at 500 GiB/month depending on plan, and ingress is free. The network price page lists R$0.24/GB/month in both directions but does not say whether this is an overage rate. Exact per-plan allowance and billable traffic remain unresolved.
- **Billing scope:** the product FAQ says per-second VM billing with a 60-second minimum and monthly cap; the price FAQ describes monthly invoice cycles and the contract describes prepaid credits and a postpaid option. The public pages do not reconcile metering, invoice timing and whether the ceiling includes add-on meters.
- **Trial:** pricing advertises R$200 credits for new accounts for 30 days; About FAQ separately describes a one-time limited 14-day trial and R$50 activation reload. No page explains whether these offers combine.
- **Storage:** pricing mentions plan SSD storage in comparisons, but Nuvion's additional-prices section says block storage must be acquired. Default boot-disk size/inclusion is not settled.
- **Backups:** current Nuvion docs describe user-managed Backup Plans and separately billed storage; the general Virtuozzo contract dated 2025-02-05 also describes daily snapshots, seven-day retention and R$80 restores. Public pages do not confirm how that general clause applies to current Nuvion.
- **Tax and customer eligibility:** price/contract pages do not say whether rates include Brazilian taxes. Checked public pages do not publish card requirement, country eligibility or a sanctions list.
- **Data residency:** privacy policy names locations for SaveInCloud personal information, not a contractual residency guarantee for VM contents, logs or backups; no DPA/subprocessor list was found.
- **Archived VM billing:** the guide says archived VMs stop compute billing but does not say whether attached storage, IP or backup charges stop.
- **High Clock speed:** Nuvion introduction says 3.4 GHz; the price page and detailed VM guide say 3.6 GHz. I recorded the disagreement without further escalation.
- **Blocked pages:** no Cloudflare or bot check appeared. The web reader initially failed on several docs opened by direct URL; I reached those pages through the official docs navigation. The pt-BR privacy URL failed, so I used the official Portuguese policy URL found through the provider's own search result. No login, signup or form was attempted.
