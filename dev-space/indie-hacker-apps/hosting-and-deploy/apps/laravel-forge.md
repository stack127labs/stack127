## Cost examples and scope

The cost examples above use the required workload assumptions. Forge’s panel fee is exact, but total hosting bills are not: server, database, domain, and network prices depend on the selected provider, location, or dynamically priced Laravel VPS/managed resource. The rows give the exact Forge subtotal and identify what remains unpriced. All our calculations are in USD, exclude taxes, and use monthly Forge billing.

Laravel Forge has a newer-generation release and new billing options, but the public official blog/changelog pages checked did not disclose a dated old-versus-new Forge panel subscription price schedule. The verified dated change is the October 1, 2025 relaunch and Laravel VPS launch; the current pricing page shows monthly and annual prices. The terms effective date is October 1, 2025 and permits future price changes at the start of a later billing period.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Managed PostgreSQL backups:** March 17, 2026 blog says point-in-time recovery is available within seven days. Current managed database docs say continuous PITR is not available and recovery uses daily backups. Both official sources are listed in field 31.
- **Customer-server database versions:** database docs still show MySQL 8.0 and PostgreSQL 13, while the August 31, 2026 changelog says both were retired for new installations. Use the dated changelog for new installs; the docs need refresh.
- **Server monitoring:** current pricing lists server monitoring for all plans, while the monitoring guide says threshold-based CPU/disk/memory monitors are Business-only. The metrics page separately limits real-time charts to Laravel VPS, Hetzner, and DigitalOcean. Plan access needs confirmation.
- **Forge plan price history:** pricing, terms, blog, and changelog were checked. Current monthly and annual prices are public; a dated panel subscription increase/change log with the old prices was not found.
- **Laravel VPS and add-on prices:** the 2025 launch post said Laravel VPS starts at $6/month. Current docs direct users to a dynamic size/region calculation and use hourly billing. Current static server, database, cache, object storage rates, full VPS region list, and numeric fair-use bandwidth limit were not published in the public pages read.
- **Data residency:** the Laravel privacy policy describes US and cross-border processing, but does not provide a Forge-specific data-location commitment by data type. Trust Center DPA materials are access-controlled; no access request was made.
- **SLA:** pricing, support docs, terms, and status pages were checked. The public status page reports incidents and component status, but no Forge platform uptime SLA or service-credit schedule was found.
