## Current notices

- I found no IONOS VPS shutdown, sunset, or acquisition notice in the [IONOS US newsroom](https://www.ionos.com/newsroom/news/), [IONOS UK newsroom](https://www.ionos.co.uk/newsroom/news/), or the official [IONOS Server Guide](https://www.ionos.com/digitalguide/server/). IONOS says its name changed from 1&1 IONOS to IONOS in January 2020 and that it is part of the United Internet group; this is a company rename and ownership note, not a VPS shutdown or acquisition notice. Checked 2026-11-06.
- IONOS published a [2026 VPS price-adjustment notice](https://www.ionos.com/help/2026/vps-information-on-the-2026-price-adjustment/) for some existing, older VPS contracts. Affected customers receive an email and contract-specific new prices. The public notice does not list those individualized prices; it says customers who do not agree can cancel the affected contract with immediate effect. This notice does not establish the price for a new VPS+ order.
- The current offer is marketed as **VPS+**. The directory row says **IONOS VPS** and is marked Active. Its public offer pages have checkout links; I did not click through to order.

## Cost examples

All estimates below use the US storefront and USD. They are **our calculations**, not vendor quotes. The calculation assumes the crossed-out regular price is charged after month three; IONOS does not state that billing sequence in words on the public VPS+ page. Sales tax, domain registration, and external CI fees are excluded.

- **A. Small commercial static site:** VPS S+ base VM; 3 × $2 + 9 × $6 = **$60 for the first 12 months before sales tax**. 100 GB monthly transfer is within the advertised unlimited traffic allowance, up to 1 Gbit/s. Builds must run on the VM or a separate CI service.
- **B. Small commercial API:** VPS S+ is the smallest listed plan meeting the capacity rule (1 vCore, 2 GB RAM). 3 × $2 + 9 × $6 = **$60 for the first 12 months before sales tax**. This does not establish request throughput; builds and server operations remain customer-managed.
- **C. API plus PostgreSQL:** VPS S+ base is conditionally **$60 for the first 12 months before tax**, plus optional Cloud Backup at a starting rate of $0.065 per stored GB/month. The exact total cannot be calculated without the billable backup storage amount. PostgreSQL is self-managed on the VM, with at least 1 GiB RAM and 10 GB disk reserved from the plan.
