## Cost examples: our calculation

Calculated 2026-11-05 for one commercial solo builder, one private source repository and one custom domain, using normal monthly rates. No annual commitment, promotion, credits or optional support package. Domain registration and location-dependent taxes are outside the figures. None is an exact invoice total.

Inputs: [Velocity product and plans](https://www.cloudways.com/en/velocity.php) and [Cloudways pricing, Velocity section](https://www.cloudways.com/en/pricing.php) publish the normal server plans; [Velocity launch guide](https://support.cloudways.com/en/articles/15550368-how-to-launch-an-application-on-cloudways-velocity) gives the CDN excess and off-site backup prices; [Velocity server overview and disk changes](https://support.cloudways.com/en/articles/17662842-how-to-view-your-server-overview-in-cloudways-velocity) gives the 25 GB minimum selected disk. [Cloudways account payments](https://support.cloudways.com/en/articles/9652801-how-to-manage-your-cloudways-payments) establishes USD. [DigitalOcean service terms, Cloudways section 3](https://www.digitalocean.com/legal/tos-service-specific-terms) uses hourly increments, while the Velocity pricing FAQ describes daily proration. The public pages do not give Velocity's disk rate or an hourly-to-monthly divisor.

Define:
- H(P,730h): actual hosting charge for plan P over 730 hours. Advertised monthly bases are Starter $20 and Professional $30, but the exact 730-hour charge is unresolved.
- D(V): live-disk charge for selected capacity V GB, with V at least 25. Its rate and included allowance are unknown.
- B(S): charge for measured off-site backup storage S GB. The published unit multiplier is $0.033 × S, but Velocity's time basis, minimum, compression and incremental accounting remain unknown.
- X: any required build, private-repository or network fee not publicly resolved. X is unknown, not assumed zero.

### A. Small commercial static site

Inputs: 100 MB deployed files; 1,000,000 HTTP requests/month; 100 GB outbound; 20 builds × 5 minutes = 100 build minutes; one private repository and one custom domain.

Compatibility: a static bundle served through a Node start command fits the advertised workflow. Starter is the smallest published server plan. A private repository's exact entitlement and the build-duration/concurrency limits are not explicitly established. No separate request tariff was found; absence is not evidence of a universal $0 fee. [Velocity product and plans](https://www.cloudways.com/en/velocity.php); [Velocity launch guide](https://support.cloudways.com/en/articles/15550368-how-to-launch-an-application-on-cloudways-velocity); [Velocity deployment management](https://support.cloudways.com/en/articles/16187473-how-to-manage-deployments-for-your-application-on-cloudways-velocity).

Known calculation: CDN excess = $0.02 × max(100 − 250, 0) = **$0** using the current Starter quota. Even the conflicting help example's 100 GB quota gives $0 at exactly 100 GB; this does not resolve its quota mismatch. Starter's 3 TB server allowance also exceeds the stated 100 GB workload, but server/CDN accounting is not fully defined. [Velocity Cloudflare Enterprise controls](https://support.cloudways.com/en/articles/16162066-manage-cloudflare-enterprise-on-cloudways-velocity); [Cloudways pricing, Velocity section](https://www.cloudways.com/en/pricing.php).

Result: **advertised hosting base $20/month**, with managed HTTPS/CDN. Conditional full-month formula: $20 + D(25) + B(S_A) + $0 CDN excess + X. **Full total unknown.** There is no factual basis for treating all disk, backups and build activity as included in $20.

### B. Small commercial app API

Inputs: 1,000,000 requests/month; 100 ms active CPU and 250 ms elapsed per request; 256 MiB app working memory; 100 GB outbound; A's build, repository and domain requirements; always-on server for **730 hours**.

Selection: Starter's 2 vCPU and 2 GB RAM exceed the required minimum of 1 vCPU and 1 GiB. This is an allocation comparison, not a measured throughput promise. The active-CPU assumption gives 1,000,000 × 0.100 = 100,000 CPU-seconds of application work; Velocity does not publish a function CPU-second price, so that number is not converted into a usage charge. [Cloudways pricing, Velocity section](https://www.cloudways.com/en/pricing.php); [Velocity NGINX, PM2 and Redis controls](https://support.cloudways.com/en/articles/16160257-how-to-manage-services-for-your-server-on-cloudways-velocity).

Formula: H(Starter,730h) + D(25) + B(S_B) + $0 CDN excess + X. **Known advertised full-month base: $20; exact 730-hour hosting charge and full total unknown.** The unknown live-disk rate and proration mismatch prevent an exact quote. All traffic being dynamic does not create an independently verified per-request bill.

### C. App plus persistent data

Inputs: all of B plus native PostgreSQL with at least 1 GiB memory and 10 GB storage; daily backups retained seven days; 10 GB app-to-database traffic/month.

Selection: Professional, advertised $30/month, offers 4 GB server RAM and 2 vCPU. Our candidate allowance is 1 GiB for the app, 1 GiB for PostgreSQL, plus remaining capacity for OS, proxy, process manager, cache and builds. This is our sizing assumption: the product does not document an independently reserved 1 GiB database allocation. A 25 GB selected disk is a candidate for 10 GB database capacity plus app/system files; available usable space is not guaranteed. PostgreSQL is provisioned in the same Cloudways environment, not a separately priced DigitalOcean Managed Database. [Velocity PostgreSQL and external database connection](https://support.cloudways.com/en/articles/16188001-how-to-connect-and-manage-a-database-for-your-application-on-cloudways-velocity); [Velocity server overview and disk changes](https://support.cloudways.com/en/articles/17662842-how-to-view-your-server-overview-in-cloudways-velocity).

Formula: H(Professional,730h) + D(V_C) + B(S_C) + N(10 GB internal DB traffic) + $0 CDN excess + X. V_C must cover database, app and system use; 25 GB is only the minimum selectable starting point. N's charging rule is unknown. **Known advertised hosting base: $30/month; qualifying database allocation and full total unknown.**

Illustrative backup arithmetic only: seven independent full 10 GB copies would be 7 × 10 = 70 GB, and 70 × $0.033 = $2.31 at the published unit multiplier. **This is not a monthly backup quote or an assertion of seven full-copy billing.** Live data size, retained changed bytes, app files, compression, minimums and the rate's period are unresolved.
