## Cost examples

All prices are USD and exclude taxes per Hostwinds' terms. These are our calculations, not Hostwinds quotes. Managed Linux V1 is shown at the current $7.14/month product-page price; the page also crosses out $10.99 but does not say whether that is an introductory term or renewal. Unmanaged V1 shows $4.99/month without a crossed-out rate.

### A. Small commercial static site

- Inputs: 100 MB website, 1,000,000 requests, 100 GB outbound, 20 builds of 5 minutes, one private source repo, one custom domain.
- Selected plan: Unmanaged Linux V1, 1 CPU, 1 GB RAM, 30 GB SSD, 1 TB bandwidth, one included IPv4.
- Formula: $4.99/month VPS + $0 known overage within the listed 1 TB allowance = $4.99/month before tax.
- Result: **$4.99/month before tax**, excluding separately registered domain and external CI. The customer installs and maintains the web server; workload performance is untested.

### B. Small commercial app API

- Inputs: 1,000,000 requests/month, assumed 100 ms processor work and 250 ms total elapsed time per request, 256 MiB working memory, 100 GB outbound, same builds and domain as A.
- Selected plan: Managed Linux V1, 1 CPU, 1 GB RAM, 30 GB SSD, 1 TB bandwidth, for the directory's 730-hour capacity assumption.
- Formula: current page rate $7.14/month + no listed per-request/CPU-second meter = $7.14/month before tax, assuming traffic stays within the 1 TB allowance.
- Result: **$7.14/month before tax** at current public product-page rate. If the crossed-out $10.99 rate applies later, the price would be $10.99/month; order page was inaccessible and renewal treatment is unconfirmed. No throughput benchmark was run.

### C. App plus Postgres

- Inputs: Scenario B plus a 10 GB Postgres database on the same VM and daily backups retained for 7 days.
- Selected plan: Managed Linux V2, 1 CPU, 2 GB RAM, 50 GB SSD, 2 TB bandwidth, at $14.29/month; backup add-on is $1/month plus $0.03 per GB of stored backup data.
- Formula: $14.29 VPS + $1.00 backup base + ($0.03 × B GB stored) = $15.29 + $0.03B per month before tax. If stored backups total exactly 10 GB, our calculation is **$15.59/month before tax**.
- Qualification: Backups default to nightly and 60-day retention; frequency and retention can be changed. Actual backup size is for the full server and incremental changes, so a 10 GB database alone does not determine the bill. $15.59 is conditional, not a guaranteed total.
