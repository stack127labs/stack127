## Conflicts and unknowns

- Non-Japanese customer signup eligibility remains unresolved. The basic agreement refers to residence in a “designated country” without listing countries in the checked version; signup help assumes domestic contact details and asks overseas-phone users to contact support. No signup was attempted.
- Entry prices vary by zone. The smallest Standard example is ¥1,540/month in Ishikari, while Tokyo Zone 2 lists ¥1,760/month. Check the selected zone before quoting a price.
- The base server page advertises high maximum configurations, while per-project quotas can be much lower. The 300 GB memory quota and account/zone server caps govern provisioning unless Sakura approves an increase.
- Sakura states customer disks and archives are stored in Japan; the pages checked do not make a complete location commitment for account metadata, logs, and all processors.
- Data transfer has no per-GB fee in the shared offer but has a 1.6 TB monthly per-server guideline and possible throttling. This is not unlimited guaranteed throughput.
- Sakura’s terms and support pages are primarily Japanese. English product summaries have less detail; Japanese manual, help, pricing, and agreement pages were used for the applicable terms and limits.
