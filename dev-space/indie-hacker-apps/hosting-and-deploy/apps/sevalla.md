# Sevalla

**Brand transition:** Kinsta launched the separate Sevalla brand in 2024. Its February 2, 2026 notice moved app/database/static-site management from MyKinsta to Sevalla, with services continuing under Kinsta. No current full-platform shutdown or acquisition notice was found in the official sources searched. Sources: https://sevalla.com/blog/a-fresh-contender-joins-the-paas-market/; https://kinsta.com/changelog/paas-moving-to-sevalla/; https://sevalla.com/about/ (checked 2026-11-05).

**Current migration and sunsets:** new companies use Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI); existing Google Cloud companies are moving in waves, including database/disk companies throughout November 2026. Legacy public TCP/database endpoint hostnames and ports stopped on October 15. Nixpacks is deprecated; new Git apps use Ingot from October 29, while existing Nixpacks apps continue. No final Nixpacks removal date is announced. Sources: https://docs.sevalla.com/platform/oci-migration; https://sevalla.com/changelog/; https://docs.sevalla.com/applications/builds/overview (checked 2026-11-05; migration/changelog rechecked before saving).
