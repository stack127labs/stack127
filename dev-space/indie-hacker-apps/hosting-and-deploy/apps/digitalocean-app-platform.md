## Cost examples — our calculations

These are our calculations from the cited published prices, not vendor quotes or performance tests. All three use one builder, an ordinary commercial product, one existing authorized private Git repository, one custom domain, monthly self-service billing, no annual discount, no startup credit and no optional paid support. Domain registration and location-dependent tax are excluded. Prices are USD; the billing API explicitly identifies invoice currency and the terms exclude applicable taxes from the displayed prices. Sources: https://docs.digitalocean.com/reference/api/reference/billing/; https://www.digitalocean.com/legal/terms-of-service-agreement (checked 2026-11-04).

Use a stable component name for a full billing cycle. For the 730-hour server allocation, assume a calendar month with at least 730 hours, not 730 hours split across two monthly billing cycles. App Platform's first-28-days rule caps the normal component bill; renaming a component can restart that counter. Transfer allowance also accrues with lifetime, up to the full monthly amount. Sources: https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform/details/pricing/; https://docs.digitalocean.com/platform/billing/bandwidth/ (checked 2026-11-04).

The native source builder, platform-managed images, basic delivery, standard runtime log view, custom-domain attachment and managed HTTPS are used. No external CI build, paid registry, external retained-log service, dedicated egress pair, NFS share or extra preview deployment is selected. The price page includes the free-tier features in paid plans, and the product FAQ describes no hidden build/image-storage charges; no separate standard build-minute charge is listed. This is the published-model estimate, not proof of arbitrary unlimited build usage. Sources: https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing/app-platform; https://www.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform; https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform/how-to/view-logs/; https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform/how-to/manage-domains/ (checked 2026-11-04).

### A. Small commercial static site

Inputs: 100 MB deployed output; 1,000,000 HTTP requests/month; 100 GB outbound/month; 20 builds × 5 minutes = 100 build minutes; one private Git repository and one custom domain. Choose one of the first three static-only apps and the ordinary native builder. It can contain the static component without a paid runtime container. Source: https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform/details/pricing/; https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform/how-to/manage-static-sites/; https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing/app-platform (checked 2026-11-04).

For this calculation GB and MB in the supplied workload are decimal. The vendor bills binary GiB:

- 100 GB × 1,000,000,000 bytes/GB ÷ 1,073,741,824 bytes/GiB = 93.13225746154785 GiB.
- Full-month allowance: 1 GiB for this static-only app.
- Billable transfer = max(0, 93.13225746154785 − 1) = 92.13225746154785 GiB.
- Transfer charge = 92.13225746154785 × $0.02 = $1.842645149230957.
- Static app/base, native builds, deployed static-file serving, custom HTTPS and standard route: $0 within the published free static-app offer. The App Platform price model does not add a per-HTTP-request charge to these inputs.
- Our calculated monthly hosting total: $1.842645149230957 before tax, displayed as approximately $1.84 after rounding to two decimals.

Sources for the billed inputs: https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform/details/pricing/; https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing/app-platform; https://www.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform (checked 2026-11-04). This assumes no other apps consuming the team's pooled allowance and no extra static-only app fee. The general agreement permits charging end users; no free-static commercial ban was found. Source: https://www.digitalocean.com/legal/terms-of-service-agreement, section 4.4 (checked 2026-11-04).

Compatibility: suitable in the documented static/private-repository/custom-domain model. Each five-minute build is under the one-hour timeout. A hard deployed-file total size cap, monthly build-minute ceiling and build-concurrency quota were not established, so this is not a tested capacity guarantee for the exact output/traffic. Sources: https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform/details/limits/; https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform/how-to/manage-static-sites/; https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform/details/pricing/ (checked 2026-11-04).
