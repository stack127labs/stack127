# bunny.net Edge Scripting

**Status and lifecycle:** active public script-hosting offer. The [runtime notice](https://bunny.net/docs/scripting/runtime), updated October 14, 2026, says bunny.net will continue operating and maintaining its customized Deno-based runtime despite Deno winding down active development. Existing scripts and new development/deployment continue; future runtime plans will be communicated in advance. No Edge Scripting shutdown date is announced there.

No product shutdown, sunset or acquisition announcement was found in bounded checks of the [company blog](https://bunny.net/blog/), [News archive](https://bunny.net/blog/tag/news/) and [changelog](https://bunny.net/docs/changelog?tags=Edge+Scripting). Current whole-product GA status was not independently established. Shared company/security material is kept brief below; the [current CDN notice](https://status.bunny.net/incidents/p6gf1by9kr7m) is not evidence of a script-runtime breach.

## Cost examples — our calculations

Calculated on 2026-11-05 from the sources checked that day. Use one paying builder, normal monthly usage after trial, no promotional credits, no annual discount and no optional support plan. All bunny.net amounts retain the published **$** symbol; no currency conversion is made. Taxes are excluded under the [tax policy](https://bunny.net/docs/billing/sales-tax).

Edge Scripting does not use Magic Containers' CPU-hour, RAM-hour, volume or Anycast tariff. Its own execution meters are CPU and executed requests, with separate CDN bandwidth. Sources: [scripting pricing](https://bunny.net/docs/scripting/pricing) and [delivery pricing](https://bunny.net/docs/cdn/pricing).

### Metering formula

Let **N** be total executed script requests across this otherwise unused account in the calendar month, and **T** be aggregate active CPU-seconds. For positive execution usage:

`Scripting = $0.20 × ceil(N / 1000000) + $0.02 × max(1, ceil(T / 1000))`

This follows the [published increment rules](https://bunny.net/docs/scripting/pricing): the first executed request starts both components; the increments are pooled across scripts. The monthly scripting minimum is $0.22, not an included free million requests. Another execution after exactly 1,000,000 enters a second request increment. Tests/other scripts can therefore change the result.

The **platform account minimum** is `max($1, applicable account usage)`, rather than an extra $1 added to each script or zone. A standalone script's Pull Zone makes the active-zone condition relevant. Source: [account billing](https://bunny.net/docs/billing/add-funds).

For delivery, choose **CDN Standard with all 100 GB served in Europe/North America**:

`100 GB × $0.01/GB = $1`

This is a separately billed delivery component. CPU/request rates have no published region multiplier, and execution follows the supported global PoP routing rather than a reserved Frankfurt app server. Source: [CDN rates](https://bunny.net/docs/cdn/pricing) and [execution locations](https://bunny.net/docs/scripting/regions).
