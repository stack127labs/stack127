## Cost examples — our calculation

Calculated 2026-11-05 in the published currency, KRW. These are monthly **hosting-capacity subtotals**, not complete price quotes. Use one paying builder, normal monthly billing, one private source repository and one custom domain; exclude domain registration, startup credits, annual discounts and optional support. No checkout or load test was performed. Tax inclusion and explicit commercial eligibility remain unresolved.

Inputs for every example: [pricing](https://cloudtype.io/pricing), [resource subscriptions](https://docs.cloudtype.io/ko/developers/resource.md), [private repositories](https://docs.cloudtype.io/ko/developers/private-repo.md), [domain connection](https://docs.cloudtype.io/ko/developers/domain.md), checked 2026-11-05. Hobby's 100GB monthly allowance and 500 build minutes cover the **stated** 100GB delivery and 20 × 5 = 100 build minutes; whether every required charge is included is not established. A build artifact must also fit the 5GB image cap. No separate paid source-host subscription is documented as a prerequisite for SSH private-repository deployment.

### A. Small commercial static site

Inputs: 100MB deployed files; 1,000,000 HTTP requests/month; 100GB outbound; 20 five-minute builds; private repository; custom domain. The [HTML guide](https://docs.cloudtype.io/ko/developers/html.md), checked 2026-11-05, provides the deployment path. Pricing says a static website with no assigned CPU/RAM uses a service slot without consuming memory. The minimum Hobby memory subscription enables the paid custom-domain entitlement; no additional static-service CPU subscription is selected.

**Our calculation:** 1 × ₩6,600 Hobby subscription = **₩6,600/month published capacity subtotal**. Builds: 20 × 5 = 100 minutes ≤ 500. Delivery: 100GB = the paid included allowance; assumed no other metered traffic in the space.

This is not a complete commercial-workload total. A million monthly requests do not establish peak rate or actual load capacity; request/gateway fee inclusion, final tax treatment and commercial-use wording remain unresolved. Published traffic and build allowances do not settle every component's price. Persistent runtime app files are unnecessary for this immutable static-site input; ordinary app disk mounts must not be assumed.

### B. Small commercial app API

Inputs: stateless HTTP app; 1,000,000 monthly requests; 100ms active processor work, 250ms elapsed time and 256MiB working memory per request; the same 100GB delivery, build/repository/domain assumptions as A. Quote an always-on allocation of at least 1 vCPU and 1GiB RAM for 730 hours. The [Node/container deployment guides](https://docs.cloudtype.io/ko/developers/node.md), checked 2026-11-05, document continuous service allocation rather than function-time billing.

Pricing describes Hobby memory as both 0.5GB and 512MB per increment, without defining the byte basis. Two increments give the marketed nominal 1GB configuration; whether that satisfies the field's 1GiB condition is unresolved. Three increments give 1,536 printed MB, exceeding 1GiB under either usual decimal-MB or binary-MiB interpretation. This conservative purchase is not asserted to be the smallest qualifying purchase.

**Our calculation, nominal configuration:** (2 × ₩6,600 memory) + (1 × ₩6,600 vCPU) = **₩19,800/month published capacity subtotal**.

**Our calculation, conservative memory selection:** (3 × ₩6,600 memory) + (1 × ₩6,600 vCPU) = **₩26,400/month published capacity subtotal**.

The subscription itself activates Hobby; do not add another ₩6,600 base fee on top of the memory subscription. The 730 hours represent one normal monthly billing cycle for comparison; Cloudtype does not quote an hourly CPU/RAM charge. Do not multiply monthly subscriptions by 730. Subscriptions must be deployed as subscribed resources to avoid the free daily stop.

The assumed active work is 1,000,000 × 0.100 = 100,000 processor-seconds; this is a workload input, **not a Cloudtype billable quantity or a virtual-core performance conversion**. Peak concurrency, sustained throughput and memory overhead have not been tested. Exact minimum qualifying allocation and a complete bill remain unresolved for the same tax/commerce/unitemized-component reasons as A.

### C. App plus persistent data

Inputs: B plus one PostgreSQL service with at least 1GiB memory, 10GB persistent storage, daily backups retained seven days and 10GB/month app-to-database traffic. Use the same environment for private service-name access; sources are the [database guide](https://docs.cloudtype.io/ko/developers/database.md), [PostgreSQL guide](https://docs.cloudtype.io/ko/developers/postgres.md), [connection guide](https://docs.cloudtype.io/ko/developers/connect.md) and pricing, checked 2026-11-05.
