## Cost examples: our calculation

Calculated **2026-11-05**, in **USD**, the currency explicitly returned by the [public pricing feed](https://cloady.com/api/billing/pricing). One paying builder, one otherwise empty paid workspace, a commercial product, an existing custom domain, normal monthly pricing, no trial/credits/annual discount or optional support. Use Germany (eu1) for the stated placement assumption; prices are documented as identical across regions, but stock is not guaranteed. Domain registration remains outside hosting.

Public tax inclusion and the buyer's invoice tax are not established. Amounts below are the published list-price estimates; no tax rate or currency conversion is invented. No exact tax-inclusive invoice is claimed.

Shared inputs: [hosting/pricing guide](https://cloady.com/docs/hosting), [capacity model](https://cloady.com/docs/concepts), [private Git/source deployment](https://cloady.com/docs/getting-started), [CLI private-repository support](https://cloady.com/docs/cli), [paid/free build rules](https://cloady.com/docs/free-tier) and [domains](https://cloady.com/docs/ui), all checked 2026-11-05. Private GitHub repositories are explicitly supported after connecting the workspace integration. Paid capacity includes native builds/deployments, HTTPS, basic logs/tools and network traffic without usage fees; no external build service is required by the documented route.

The five exact price anchors are known. The prose says non-anchor totals cost between the covering anchor and its predecessor, but it does not supply a complete equation. The example Medium calculator's displayed per-resource rates are not applied universally. We use exact anchors for A/B and a bounded estimate for C.

### A. Small commercial static site

Inputs: 100 MB deployed files, 1,000,000 HTTP requests/month, 100 GB outbound, **20 × 5 = 100 build minutes**, one private repository and one custom domain.

Select a custom static web-server/source app at the **Nano anchor: 0.5 vCPU, 1 GB RAM, 5 GB SSD**. The 5 GB allowance exceeds the stated 100 MB files. Use the private Git route so the local-folder upload's 50 MB/3000-file ceiling is not mistaken for a 100 MB build-output limit.

Formula: **1 × USD 3.00/month = USD 3.00/month list-price hosting estimate**.

Line items: reserved CPU/RAM/disk are included in that anchor; paid network, 1,000,000 requests without a per-request meter, native private-source builds, custom-domain HTTPS and basic logs/metrics have no separate listed usage charge. No compulsory paid gateway/IP/certificate add-on was identified for the web route. Tax treatment remains unresolved; domain registration is outside hosting.

A free workspace has only 15 GB outbound/month, so **100 GB outbound alone exceeds the current free offer**. Its million-request and actual CPU/memory limits also must not be confused with paid capacity. The chosen paid workspace therefore avoids relying on a free or temporary allowance.

Compatibility is documented at the hosting/build/network level, subject to acceptable use and region capacity. No load test proves peak throughput or that this is the absolute cheapest custom resource shape. Backups are not required for replaceable source-built static output.

### B. Small commercial app API

Inputs: 1,000,000 requests × 0.100 CPU-seconds = **100,000 CPU-seconds**, or **27.777… CPU-hours** of assumed work. Aggregate elapsed request time is 1,000,000 × 0.250 seconds = **250,000 request-seconds**. Working memory is 256 MiB, but the server comparison requires at least **1 vCPU and 1 GiB allocation for 730 hours**, plus 100 GB outbound, the same 100 build minutes, private repository and custom domain.

Choose the smallest published qualifying price anchor, **Small: 1 vCPU, 2 GB RAM, 50 GB SSD**. The 2 GB nominal memory clears 1 GiB even under a decimal reading. This deliberately retains the source's GB units; the extra disk is part of an exact known price point. A leaner custom allocation could cost less, but its exact bill is unestablished.

Formula: **1 × USD 5.00/month = USD 5.00/month list-price hosting estimate**. Do not multiply the subscription by 730 or apply a CPU-hour charge to 27.777… hours: paid billing is reserved declared capacity, including idle/stopped time.

Native private builds, paid traffic, ordinary request handling, HTTPS and basic logs/tools are included rather than separate usage-priced items. Tax treatment is unknown. No gateway or IP surcharge is added without evidence.

Nominal allocation is compatible, but CPU speed, request-body/concurrency/timeout constraints and actual million-request performance remain unverified. The removed serverless guide's old time limits are not applied to this long-running API. Its assumed actual CPU work would also exceed the free tier's four-hour budget, independently of outbound traffic.

### C. App plus persistent data

Inputs: B plus a PostgreSQL service with at least 1 GiB memory and 10 GB persistent storage; daily backup retained seven days; 10 GB/month app-to-database traffic.

Use **one custom Compose application containing two services**, an API and PostgreSQL, with internal-only database access. Allocate 1 vCPU and **2 GB RAM to each service** for a combined **2 vCPU and 4 GB RAM**. Reserve a **10 GB persistent database volume**, with the stateless app rebuilt from its source/image. Configure the database mount explicitly rather than relying on container-local storage.

The official [PostgreSQL template](https://cloady.com/templates/postgres) and [Compose source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Cloady/templates/main/postgres/docker-compose.yml) demonstrate the image, persistent mount and credentials. Its small default allocation is not used as the comparison's database size. A customer Compose declaration with larger reservations is an assumed deployment configuration under the documented model, not a claim that the default template already meets the workload. No native HA/PITR/automatic patching contract is inferred from the catalogue's “managed” label.

Pricing bound: the combined CPU/RAM exceeds Small (1 vCPU/2 GB) and fits Medium (4 vCPU/8 GB/200 GB). The docs place a total in the range between that covering anchor and the one below it. Let **P** be the app's monthly resource price. Conservative outer bound: **USD 5 ≤ P ≤ USD 15**. This is a broad bound, not an exact interpolation or claim that either endpoint is the quoted price for this custom shape.

Enable managed backups for this **whole combined application**. Because this is the only app in the workspace, its own resource price is P; no per-app share of a nonlinear multi-app bill needs guessing.

Formula: managed backup surcharge = **0.20 × P**. Resource plus managed-backup range = **1.20 × P = USD 6 to USD 18/month**, before any unestablished tax adjustment or extra backup/export dependency. This is **our conditional range**, not a verified full workload total.

The included paid-network statement has no separate per-GB bill for the stated 100 GB public and 10 GB internal traffic. Internal DB access uses service name/expose rather than an internet-published port. No private-network performance or universal internal TLS guarantee is inferred.

Backup fit remains incomplete. Daily managed snapshots retain **seven successful restore points**, not a guaranteed seven-calendar-day recovery window. Privacy explicitly rejects that time guarantee. The docs also do not establish PostgreSQL-consistent snapshots or a native logical-dump/PITR method. If exact seven-day database-consistent recovery needs an additional export/archive service, its cost is unknown and is **not included in USD 6 to USD 18**. A code rollback neither restores database rows nor reverses schema migrations.
