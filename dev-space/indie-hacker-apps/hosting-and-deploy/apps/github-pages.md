## Cost examples: our calculations

Calculation date: 2026-11-04. Use the approved workload inputs, one paying builder, private source, an existing custom domain, monthly billing and no credits/annual discount/optional support. Taxes and domain registration are outside hosting subtotals. These calculations do not validate an intended commercial use or measure capacity.

### A. Small commercial static site

Inputs: 100 MB deployed files, 1,000,000 requests/month, 100 GB outbound/month, 20 five-minute builds, private source and one custom domain. Static delivery can represent the file-based part. The policy restricts general business/transaction/SaaS hosting; donations/crowdfunding are limited permitted examples. The unspecified “commercial product” is not enough to establish a permitted site purpose. Source: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-for-additional-products-and-features#pages (checked 2026-11-04).

Technical limits: 100 MB is below the published 1 GB site ceiling; 100 GB/month reaches the stated soft bandwidth threshold. The one-million request count is not a verified throughput guarantee. A custom workflow avoids the soft hourly build count, but the deployment timeout still applies. Source: https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits (checked 2026-11-04).

Free Pages requires public source, so it does not satisfy the fixed repository requirement. Pro has the correct personal-account eligibility, but the current exact public price was not verified. To make the known plan component reviewable, the following is a **conditional Team illustration**, not the cheapest verified commercial solution: assume one licensed builder, an organization-owned private repository, no other account usage and acceptance of a one-seat monthly purchase at the advertised rate. Sources: https://github.com/pricing , https://docs.github.com/en/get-started/learning-about-github/githubs-plans and https://docs.github.com/en/billing/how-tos/manage-plan-and-licenses/manage-user-licenses (checked 2026-11-04).

Our calculation:

- Plan component: `1 assumed licensed user × $4 USD/user/month = $4 USD/month`.
- Build input: `20 × 5 = 100 minutes`.
- Under the Pages-free standard-runner statement: additional eligible runner compute `$0`.
- Under private-runner allowance accounting: `100 × $0.006 = $0.60 USD` of the $18 Team included compute amount; `max(0, $0.60 - $18) = $0` additional compute, assuming no other shared usage.

Billing interpretation sources: https://docs.github.com/en/billing/concepts/product-billing/github-actions and https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/creating-a-github-pages-site (checked 2026-11-04). The conflict matters for a busy account, even though both interpretations give zero additional compute for this isolated example. The 100 minutes is assumed to cover the specified work; any separate deploy job/setup time may add runner usage. No exact additional duration is invented.

Storage assumption: use the upload action's one-day artifact retention and spread the 20 builds across separate days; retain at most two adjacent 100 MB outputs, no cache or other packages/artifacts. Our bound is `2 × 100 MB = 200 MB`, below Team's 2 GB artifact allowance, giving `$0` excess artifact storage under that assumption. If the generated archive is larger or other shared usage consumes the allowance, recompute from actual hourly storage. Sources: https://github.com/actions/upload-pages-artifact and https://docs.github.com/en/billing/reference/product-usage-included (checked 2026-11-04).

Custom-domain attachment and automatic TLS are documented; domain purchase is excluded. Source: https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/securing-your-github-pages-site-with-https (checked 2026-11-04).

**Result:** $4 USD/month is the conditional one-seat Team plan component, with $0 additional compute and artifact storage under the stated assumptions. No unconditional A total is published: commercial eligibility, current minimum purchase/actual plan choice and service at the soft delivery threshold are not fully settled. Do not describe this as unrestricted commercial hosting for $4, or as a $0 private-repository website. An external CDN suggested after quota pressure would be another selected/priced service, not an assumed free addition.

### B. Small commercial app API

Inputs: 1,000,000 HTTP requests/month, 100 ms active CPU and 250 ms elapsed/request, 256 MiB working memory, 100 GB outbound, plus A's builds/domain.

Compatibility: no. Pages has no visitor-request app runtime. Server-side language execution is explicitly unsupported; the published site consists of generated files. The two-processor/8 GB build runner cannot serve the API for 730 hours or allocate per-request memory after deployment. Sources: https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/creating-a-github-pages-site and https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/runners/github-hosted-runners (checked 2026-11-04).

Our calculation result: **not applicable as a Pages-hosted API; no monthly total**. CPU/memory-time multiplication would price a product that is not offered here. Hosting the API elsewhere creates a different split application with an external backend, not completion of B by Pages alone.

### C. App plus persistent data

Inputs: B plus Postgres with at least 1 GiB RAM and 10 GB storage, daily backups retained seven days and 10 GB/month app-to-database traffic.

Compatibility: no. The app runtime is already missing. Pages' Git repository, published artifact and Actions job are not a managed Postgres service. An external database still requires a separately hosted API/application layer. Sources: https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages and https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/creating-a-github-pages-site (checked 2026-11-04).

Our calculation structure for a redesigned application, **not a priced C estimate**:

`eligible static frontend plan + separately selected API host + external Postgres capacity + daily backup retention + backend/database transfer + compulsory extras`

No external provider/configuration is selected for an incompatible original workload. Those components remain unpriced. C's actual Pages hosting subtotal/total is not applicable. Source history or a Pages redeploy does not restore external database data.
