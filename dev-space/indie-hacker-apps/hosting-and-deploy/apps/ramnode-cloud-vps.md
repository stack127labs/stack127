## Cost examples: our calculations

Calculations use the public USD prices checked on 2026-11-06. The displayed monthly price is the cap for a VM running continuously; listed hourly amounts are rounded. Tax and domain registration are excluded. Prices are not differentiated by compute region on the public table.

### A. Small commercial static site

- **Inputs:** 100 MB deployed files; 1,000,000 requests and 100 GB outbound per month; 20 builds of five minutes; private source repo; one custom domain.
- **Plan:** Lite 1 GB, 1 core, 15 GB NVMe, 500 GB bandwidth. The Lite public page lists EWR, LAX, and NLX; IPv4 is an additional $2/month.
- **Our calculation:** VM $2/month + IPv4 $2/month = **$4/month**. 100 GB is within the 500 GB listed allowance. No managed build or request meter is listed. The customer runs the build and deploys from SSH/CI.
- **Result:** Compatible with the published VM resources for the stated small site, before tax. Domain purchase and any external CI bill are excluded.

### B. Small commercial app API

- **Inputs:** 1 million requests/month; 100 ms active processor time and 250 ms elapsed time per request; 256 MiB working memory; 100 GB outbound; same build/domain assumptions as A. Required server minimum is 1 vCPU and 1 GiB for 730 hours.
- **Plan:** Lite 1 GB has one virtual core and 1 GB RAM. It is listed at $2/month IPv6-only plus $2/month for IPv4.
- **Our calculation:** $2 VM + $2 IPv4 = **$4/month**, subject to the monthly cap. The 100 GB outbound assumption is within the 500 GB listed allowance.
- **Result:** Meets the specified size threshold. The page does not promise app throughput, and the VM's 1 GB RAM must also host the OS and app; this is not a performance guarantee.

### C. App plus persistent data

- **Inputs:** workload B plus PostgreSQL with at least 1 GiB RAM and 10 GB storage; daily backup retained seven days; 10 GB/month app-to-database traffic.
- **Plan:** Lite 2 GB, one virtual core, 20 GB NVMe, 500 GB bandwidth, with IPv4; add a 10 GB NVMe block volume for a separate database disk.
- **Our calculation:** VM $3/month + IPv4 $2/month + 10 GB volume × $0.085/GB-month = **$5.85/month known subtotal**. Daily backup storage is listed at $0.10/GB-month.
- **Backup example:** if seven complete 10 GB backup copies each occupy 10 GB of billed storage, our hypothetical formula is 7 × 10 GB × $0.10 = $7/month, giving **$12.85/month**. This is not a vendor quote or an exact total: RamNode does not publish the actual billable storage, copy/deduplication behavior, or fixed seven-day retention. The supported schedule and backup billing need to be confirmed for the chosen account.
- **Result:** PostgreSQL is self-managed. The exact workload total cannot be established from public docs because backup retention and billed image size are not specified.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Uptime terms:** Cloud onboarding advertises a 99.99% SLA; linked terms say uptime is not guaranteed; an older RamNode pre-sales knowledgebase page states a 99.98% VPS guarantee. The current Cloud VPS SLA and any service credit are not clear from those official pages.
- **Cloud funding minimum:** the Cloud overview says add at least $3; the billing FAQ says a $10 minimum initial PAYG credit. The current credit promotion starts at $10, but that does not resolve the two guides' minimum-funding mismatch.
- **Cloud instance CPU allocation:** the getting-started page calls VM CPU/resources dedicated or guaranteed; the AUP says VPS cores and disk I/O are shared and can be reduced for disruptive load.
- **Region availability:** Cloud data-center guide lists ATL and SEA alongside EWR, LAX and NLX, while the current Lite/product pricing text says Atlanta or Seattle is coming soon and lists only EWR/LAX/NLX for Lite. Do not assume every tier is orderable in every listed location.
- **Bandwidth overage units:** bandwidth documentation describes $0.004 per GB per hour over allocation; the pricing extras table describes $0.004 per GB per month, billed hourly. No overage example is used in our calculations.
- **DDoS coverage:** product feature copy says DDoS protection is included on all instances; the terms describe shared filtering and say filtered IPs are separately ordered with no guarantee. The included protection scope is not defined.
- **Lite IPv4:** sales copy says Lite is IPv6-only with a $2/month IPv4 add-on, while the general Cloud IPv6 page says all instances include one IPv4. The Lite price calculation uses the tier-specific sales text and flags the conflict.
- **Account team size:** quota page lists five team members per account; add-users guide says support can add as many as needed and describes self-service teams as under development.
- **Not published, not assumed absent:** exact region-specific VM prices, local checkout amounts and taxes, card/identity requirements for every buyer, complete sanctioned-country list, precise hourly rounding, floating-IP rate, account-wide hard spending cap, managed build/Git deployments, fixed backup retention and actual billable backup size, per-flavor processor model, full-service data-residency commitment, and managed application log retention/export.
