## Conflicts and unknowns

- The Italian price list says O1I1 starts at €1.99/month plus VAT. The detailed Aruba Cloud VPS table and Spanish-language VPS page list €2.49/month plus VAT. I used the detailed product-page price for calculations and left the discrepancy visible.
- The exact retail IPv4 price for Starter was not published. An Enterprise-only price list shows €0.70/month for an additional IPv4, but it cannot safely be applied to a retail VPS order.
- The startup programme is mentioned in service terms, but the linked presentation returned 404 and the only article found with a credit amount dates from 2018. Current availability and amount remain unclear.
- The Cloud Backup page advertises a separate service, but its help documentation excludes Cloud VPS. The VM's free snapshot lasts only 48 hours and one snapshot can exist at a time. The seven-day daily-backup scenario therefore needs a customer-run or external solution; I did not invent its cost.
- VPS page promotes Italy and a location selector, but I could not verify a complete current VPS-specific list of countries or which sizes are available in each location.
- I tried the Italian detailed VPS page, Italian help page, Italian price list, and Italian terms route before using English documentation and the Spanish product page as supporting sources. The price list loaded; some Italian detailed pages did not.
- No full public app runtime matrix, managed framework deployment, public historical status page, guaranteed ordinary-ticket response time, or workload-level performance results were found in the checked pages.
