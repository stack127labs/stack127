## Cost examples

Prices are the current public EUR prices displayed for TransIP's `.eu`/Dutch VPS product. The order page labels them excluding VAT; totals below are therefore **EUR/month ex VAT**, with the consumer VAT discrepancy left unresolved. These are our calculations, not vendor quotes.

### A. Small commercial static site

- Selected plan: V1, one shared or dedicated vCPU, 1 GB RAM, 100 GB NVMe, one IPv4, one IPv6, and one snapshot.
- Inputs: 100 MB website files; 1,000,000 requests; 100 GB outbound; 20 builds × 5 minutes; one private repo; one custom domain.
- Formula: `V1 €5/month + €0 listed egress charge + €0 required gateway/IP charge = €5/month ex VAT`.
- Builds must run on the VM or an external CI service; TransIP does not supply a managed build pipeline. A domain is not included, and the customer installs/configures HTTPS. Traffic is unlimited under fair use; requests and workload capacity were not tested.
- Result: **€5/month ex VAT**, excluding a separately purchased domain or external CI.

### B. Small commercial app API

- Selected plan: V1, the smallest published configuration with at least 1 vCPU and 1 GiB RAM, running for the required 730 hours.
- Inputs: 1,000,000 requests; 100 ms processor work and 250 ms elapsed time per request; 256 MiB working memory; 100 GB outbound; same builds and domain as A.
- Formula: `V1 monthly package €5 + €0 request/CPU-time meter + €0 listed charge for 100 GB traffic = €5/month ex VAT`.
- Result: **€5/month ex VAT**, before any separately purchased domain/CI. This is fixed monthly pricing, not an hourly charge. The workload's throughput is untested; the listed RAM is the VM total, not a promised application working set.

### C. App plus PostgreSQL

- Selected plan: V2, one shared or dedicated vCPU, 2 GB RAM, and 100 GB NVMe; PostgreSQL is self-managed on the same VM.
- Formula: `V2 €10/month + automatic backups €5/month + €0 listed egress meter = €15/month ex VAT`.
- Result: **€15/month ex VAT** for the stated server and backup add-on. The add-on makes backups every 4–6 hours and weekly; published retention is 9 recent points (up to 36–54 hours) plus five weekly points. It does **not** match the requested seven daily backup points. The VM has 100 GB disk for the 10 GB database, but app/database capacity and performance are untested. Consumer VAT and any domain are excluded.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **V1 price:** Current `.eu` order page and `.nl` VPS page show €5/month. A TransIP comparison/help page at `https://www.transip.nl/knowledgebase/3814-wat-is-een-performancevps` still shows €7.50. I used the live product/order offer for current prices and recorded the discrepancy.
- **VAT display:** Current price pages say ex VAT/BTW, while Article 6.1 of TransIP's terms says consumer prices include VAT and business prices exclude it. The displayed price is recorded as published; the final consumer checkout amount was not tested.
- **SLA scope:** A VPS help page says 99.99% uptime generally, but the 99.99% availability arrangement's product table names PerformanceVPS, SandboxVPS, and BladeVPS, not the new VPS line. The new line's contractual service-credit eligibility is unsettled.
- **Data locality:** Product page says VPS data is held in Netherlands datacenters. Offsite backups only say geographically separate; exact country and location are not published. The DPA is accessed through the account control panel, so its terms were not inspected.
- **Signup limits:** Checked the public order/product pages, terms, and privacy policy. A complete country eligibility, identity/card requirement, and full payment-method list is not published there. No signup was attempted.
- **Not published after checking product/order pages, docs/help, terms, privacy, and add-ons:** no managed app builds/previews, request/runtime quotas, managed database/queue/auth, app-log retention, account spend cap, or cross-provider disk image export. Those are not treated as “no charge” or universal technical impossibility where the customer can self-manage the VM.
- **Status fetch:** Official help confirms TransNOC is the public status service, but the direct `https://transnoc.nl` fetch failed in the research tool. Its incident history was not checked.
- **Legacy plans:** BladeVPS and PerformanceVPS remain separately listed on legacy pages; their pooled traffic, disk, backup inclusions, and prices were not copied to the new V1–V5 packages. The 2025 launch announcement said they would remain orderable “for the time being.”
