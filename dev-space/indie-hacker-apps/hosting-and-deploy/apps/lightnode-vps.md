## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Prices by location:** Washington, Frankfurt, and Singapore public cards showed identical prices. The final logged-in console quote and all other locations were not checked; no rate is carried over to unreviewed data centers.
- **Hourly/monthly billing:** the Start $0.012/hour rate multiplied by 672 hours is $8.064, higher than the displayed $7.71 monthly rate. The FAQ promises a monthly cap but does not explain this arithmetic.
- **Availability wording:** marketing claims show 99.95%, 99.99%, and 99.9% across homepage/product/Washington pages. No public contractual SLA page was found.
- **Data center count:** the current “Live now” page lists 41 nodes; homepage/product marketing says 44. Bucharest, Toronto, and Quito are not in the current active list; their present status is not explained.
- **Not published in reviewed docs:** exact traffic overage and pay-by-bandwidth rates, data-disk and snapshot prices, tax treatment, complete eligible-country/sanctions list, data residency by data type, and post-closure data-retention period.
- **News check:** the official News archive page and current notice were reviewed. Iraq's temporary internet shutdown notice was specific to the node and dates shown; it was not evidence of LightNode shutdown or acquisition. I did not find a separate linked corporate newsroom/blog feed.

## Field problems

- The price page combines a location-specific configuration, traffic/bandwidth choice, and an hourly/monthly switch. Public prices were directly compared for three locations; region prices for the remaining active nodes require the console and were not guessed.
- “TB” is shown without a decimal/binary definition. Traffic overage and bandwidth-mode prices are account-console values, so workload totals that exceed the included bundle cannot be exact from public evidence.
- The docs index exposed an SLA navigation label without a readable SLA page. Marketing uptime figures therefore remain separate from a contractual uptime guarantee.
- The location directory currently lists 41 nodes while other official marketing pages say 44. I preserved the difference instead of assuming three nodes were retired.
