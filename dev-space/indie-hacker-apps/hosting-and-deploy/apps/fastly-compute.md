**Active self-service offer; no whole-product shutdown, sunset or acquisition notice found in the official pages searched.** An April 7, 2026 announcement opened credit-card self-purchase of Compute, KV Store, Secret Store and Fanout and introduced current request/vCPU pricing. Python support is beta, and the separately promoted framework integrations in Fastly Labs have experimental qualifications.
