## Current product and news check

Convox is an active platform family with two distinct deployment modes: the open-source Convox Rack installed in a customer cloud account, and Convox Cloud, which runs on Convox-managed infrastructure. The hosted Convox Console manages Racks and provides workflows. Do not combine the Rack's cloud-provider bill with Console subscription fees or with Convox Cloud machine/database prices.

The Terms of Use identify Convox, Inc. as the Console operator; the About page does not identify a parent company. The About page, blog, current GitHub repository/releases, and docs Changes/Releases pages were checked for a shutdown, acquisition, or license-change notice. I found no such notice in those pages. The blog showed a recent post dated 2026-11-05, and the Rack repository had commits dated 2026-11-05 and 2026-11-03. The repo's latest release is 3.26.0, dated 2026-11-05. The footer’s “Updates” link points to the blog; a separate `/updates` newsroom URL returned “Page Not Found.”
