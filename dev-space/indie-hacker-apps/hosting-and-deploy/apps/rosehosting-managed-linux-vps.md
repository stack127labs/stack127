## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Managed scope and security responsibility:** Support pages offer security hardening, automated updates, and help across native Linux server software. The Terms say the customer remains solely responsible for server and account security. The operational help is clear, but ultimate legal responsibility stays with the subscriber.
- **Transfer wording:** Plan cards say “unmetered”; the AUP and Terms qualify that phrase with reasonable-use limits and possible service action for excessive traffic. No fixed overage rate or traffic cap is published.
- **Annual billing:** The public pricing page offers monthly billing and a discounted monthly equivalent paid 12 months upfront. General Terms also mention bi-yearly billing, but no bi-yearly public VPS price was available. The current order flow was not opened.
- **Expired promotion:** A $1/month promotion remains at a “Sale Finished” URL. That page explicitly says the sale ended, so it was not used as the current price.
- **Backups:** Weekly offsite backup is included. Daily or a second weekly backup is optional, but the public pages do not give its price, retention, or offsite location. Terms still place data-preservation responsibility on the customer.
- **Status history:** RoseHosting links to a public status page, but its rendered text did not expose incident or historic uptime details. The written SLA was available and checked.
- **Subprocessors:** The DPA says the current subprocessor names and countries are listed at the official subprocessor URL. That page returned an internal error, and the official search index showed only the DPA reference; the list could not be verified.
