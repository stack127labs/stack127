## Current notices

- Civo Compute is actively offered. Its Compute product and pricing pages are live, the company blog had posts through 5 November 2026, and the newsroom had October 2026 announcements. I found no notice that Civo or Compute is closing or being acquired.
- The newsroom announced the Civo Indonesia Region in Jakarta on 23 October 2026. It offers virtual machines and is operated locally by Arupa Cloud Nusantara. Civo's status page lists Compute/JKT1 as operational. Older region documentation, last updated 24 September 2026, omits JKT1. The newer announcement and status page are the current evidence for Jakarta.
- Jakarta customers are billed in Indonesian rupiah through Arupa. Civo's public SKU price table is in USD and has no city label; it does not give a per-instance Jakarta price. Do not use the USD table as an IDR quote.
- Civo's public newsroom announced that it acquired Kubefirst in March 2024. A later Civo blog describes the acquired product as Konstruct (formerly Kubefirst) and says the acquisition occurred in 2025. These official Civo pages disagree on the year and name; the acquisition concerns a separate developer-platform product, not Civo Compute. I found no acquisition of Civo itself or shutdown notice for Compute.
- Civo's Compute page says instances can be stopped and advertises paying only for what is needed. Its billing documentation says stopped instances remain charged at the full hourly rate until deleted. The billing documentation controls the cost notes below.
- The apps.md row says status Active and home region “Not checked.” This research confirms the active status and checks the regions below; I did not change apps.md.

## Cost examples: our calculations

Calculated on 2026-11-06. Each example models LON1 (London, UK), because the official region matrix explicitly lists CPU Compute there. The single Civo USD price table does not publish a London-specific SKU rate, so these are planning calculations using that table, not a city-specific quote. VAT is excluded where applicable. No startup credit, annual commitment or optional paid add-on is included. Domain registration is excluded.

### A. Small commercial static site

Inputs: 100 MB of deployed files, 1,000,000 HTTP requests and 100 GB outbound/month, 20 five-minute builds, one private repository and one custom domain.

- One xSmall instance: USD $0.007440/hour × 730 hours = $5.4312/month.
- Published inbound/outbound transfer tariff: free; the legal fair-use qualification remains unresolved.
- The 100 MB site fits in the included 25 GB root disk by capacity. Civo does not build or deploy the site for the customer; builds run in the VM or an external CI system.
- Result: our known server-plus-transfer estimate is $5.4312/month before VAT. This does not prove that xSmall has enough capacity for the request rate; workload performance is not verified. The public price table does not publish a specific region quote.

### B. Small commercial app API

Inputs: 1,000,000 requests/month; assumed 100 ms active CPU and 250 ms elapsed/request; 256 MiB working memory; 100 GB outbound; same builds and domain as A. The field's VM comparison requires at least 1 vCPU and 1 GiB RAM for 730 hours.

- Small instance: 1 core, 2 GB RAM and 25 GB NVMe.
- Formula: USD $0.014881/hour × 730 hours = $10.86313/month before VAT.
- The workload input represents 100,000 assumed CPU-seconds, but Civo charges this VM by the hour. No throughput equivalence is inferred from the vCPU count.
- Published transfer tariff is free; the legal fair-use qualification is unresolved. A public IP is the default, but the price table does not show a reserved-IP tariff.
- Result: $10.86313/month is the known instance charge. It is not a full city-specific quotation or benchmark; check any selected IP/network options and exact regional billing before purchase.

### C. App plus persistent data

Inputs: B plus PostgreSQL with at least 1 GiB memory and 10 GB storage, daily backups retained seven days, and 10 GB/month app-to-database traffic.

- Small VM: USD $0.014881/hour × 730 = $10.86313/month.
- Smallest single-node managed database: 4 GB RAM, 2 cores, 40 GB NVMe at USD $0.059524/hour × 730 = $43.45252/month.
- Known VM + database subtotal: $10.86313 + $43.45252 = $54.31565/month before VAT. Prices use the global USD table and do not establish an LON1-specific rate.
- Civo documents automatic backups for its managed database, but the pages checked do not state daily cadence or seven-day retention. The documented VM snapshot API is for configured private regions; public Compute availability and pricing are unclear.
- Result: the required seven-day daily backup could not be priced or verified, so $54.31565 is a known subtotal, not an exact scenario total. The database-to-app transfer rate is not separately stated; general transfer is advertised free subject to field 11.
