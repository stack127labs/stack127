## Status and company notices

OVHcloud VPS is active and open for new deployments. The company launched its VPS 2027 range on June 17, 2026. No official notice found that OVHcloud VPS is shutting down, being sunset, or has been acquired. The company newsroom does report acquisitions of Seald (January 26, 2026) and Dragon LLM (March 25, 2026); these notices do not describe a change in VPS ownership. ## Cost examples: our calculations

Workload inputs are from the approved examples in `fields.md`: one paying builder, commercial product, a domain already owned, monthly use, no credits, and no annual discount. No account, deployment, or benchmark was used. Exact monthly no-commit prices for the chosen data centre were not exposed by the public configurator text. For context only, the UK storefront’s **12-month** VPS-1 configuration for **UK Erith** is £39.72 ex tax/12 months = **£3.31/month**; UK France Gravelines and Germany Limburg were also shown at £3.31/month, while Madrid Local Zone was £4.00/month. These commitment prices are not passed off as the requested no-annual-discount estimate. Source: https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/vps/configurator/?brick=VPS%2BModel%2B1&planCode=vps-2027-model1&pricing=upfront12&processor=+&storage=40__SSD__NVMe&vcore=2__vCore

### A. Small commercial static site

Inputs: 100 MB deployed files; 1,000,000 HTTP requests and 100 GB outbound per month; 20 builds of 5 minutes; one private repository; one custom domain.

- **Compatibility:** Can be hosted by serving the built files from a customer-configured web server on VPS-1. No OVH-managed build service is included. 100 GB is within the advertised unlimited transfer for standard locations and below the smallest APAC allowance.
- **Known configuration:** VPS-1, 2 vCores/4 GB RAM/40 GB NVMe; customer manages source checkout, build, web server, DNS, and TLS.
- **Our calculation for the available committed quote:** `£39.72 ex tax ÷ 12 months = £3.31/month` for VPS-1 in UK Erith on the configurator’s 12-month term.
- **Comparable result:** **Unknown** at the fields.md no-annual-discount basis. Build provider, custom domain registration, and labor are outside the VPS price.

### B. Small commercial app API

Inputs: 1,000,000 stateless HTTP requests/month; assumed 100 ms active processor work and 250 ms elapsed time per request; assumed 256 MiB working memory; 100 GB outbound; same build/domain/repository assumptions as A.

- **Compatibility:** Raw VPS supports a self-managed API. VPS-1 is the smallest listed configuration and exceeds the workload’s minimum server floor of 1 vCPU and 1 GiB RAM.
- **Known configuration:** VPS-1 in UK Erith; no separate request or memory-time charge was found. The 100 GB egress is within the normal advertised traffic allowance.
- **Our calculation for the available committed quote:** `1 × (£39.72 ÷ 12) = £3.31/month ex tax` on the 12-month term.
- **Comparable result:** **Unknown** at monthly billing with no annual discount. Actual fit at one million requests was not benchmarked; builds run on the VM or an external CI service.

### C. App plus persistent data

Inputs: scenario B plus PostgreSQL with at least 1 GiB RAM and 10 GB storage; daily backup retained seven days; 10 GB/month app-to-database traffic.

- **Compatibility:** The customer can self-manage PostgreSQL on the VM. VPS-1 offers 4 GB RAM and 40 GB disk, but the combined app/database fit is untested; the OS and both services share the VM.
- **Known backup option:** Premium automatic backup offers seven rolling daily VPS restore points. It excludes extra disks and does not establish a transaction-consistent PostgreSQL dump or off-site database backup.
- **Our calculation for the available committed quote:** `VPS-1 (£39.72 ÷ 12) + Premium backup (£0.96/month) = £4.27/month ex tax` for UK Erith at the 12-month configurator selection. The £0.96 add-on quote is UK storefront pricing; it was displayed in the same 12-month configurator.
- **Comparable result:** **Unknown** under no annual discount, and a full cost cannot be stated because the exact monthly no-commit price and any required DB-consistent/off-site backup are not published in the checked pages. A separate managed PostgreSQL product starts at US$7.69/month on the Worldwide options page, but the public starting price does not confirm the required 1 GiB/10 GB configuration and is not mixed into the UK quote.
