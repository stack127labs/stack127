## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Billing period:** Pricing and billing docs say no subscription and hourly metering; Terms still say monthly or annual subscription billing. The public price page has no annual toggle or annual amount. The monthly plan equivalents are listed above; exact hourly divisor and rounding are not public.
- **Backups:** Server setup guides recommend provider backups and show Google Cloud/DigitalOcean snapshot workflows. The Terms mention separately contracted ServerPilot backup services but public plan/docs pages do not explain an available add-on or price, so that point remains unclear.
- **Status and changelog:** The blog has dated product and security notices through 2026-11-04. The attempted official `/changelog/` fetch failed and no separate newsroom or public status page was linked in the official pages checked.
- **Data processing:** Privacy Policy says ServerPilot stores collected information in the United States and may collect server configuration, traffic, logs, and file contents. The official subprocessor page linked by the policy did not load in this check.
- **Operating system life:** Ubuntu 20.04 remains on the ServerPilot connection list although Ubuntu standard security support ended in April 2025. ServerPilot's Ubuntu guidance points to Ubuntu Pro for EOL release security updates.
- **Plan features:** Economy excludes dashboard log viewer and metrics; Business and First Class include progressively more monitoring. No hard cap on app count was published, but each app incurs its plan's app fee.
