## Cost examples: our calculations

Calculated 2026-11-04. One builder; public commercial project; existing custom domain; private personal source repo owned by that builder; normal monthly credit plan; no annual, promotional, startup or trial credits; no support package, Preview Server, paid AI, image transforms or optional build concurrency. Domain registration outside hosting total. Tax added according to billing location; agreement excludes tax from fees. Sources checked 2026-11-04: https://www.netlify.com/pdf/self-serve-subscription-agreement.pdf ; https://docs.netlify.com/manage/accounts-and-billing/billing/manage-payments/ . Git source/provider costs are outside Netlify's invoice; no separate mandatory source-provider fee established for the single owner's personal repo.

The published input does not specify whether all 20 builds become production releases. Select **20 successful published production deploys**, each with the stipulated five-minute build. Current credit billing prices production deploys, not build minutes. If some are only previews or failures, adjust deploy credits downward; associated compute/traffic remains. Use current self-serve rates from https://docs.netlify.com/manage/accounts-and-billing/billing/billing-for-credit-based-plans/how-credits-work/ and pack USD prices from https://docs.netlify.com/manage/accounts-and-billing/billing/billing-for-credit-based-plans/buy-credit-packs/ . No prior unused pack balance assumed.

Let `P` be the actual monthly Personal base and `Q` the actual 3,000-credit Pro base. Public pricing displays Personal $9/$12/$15 and Pro $20/$25 without enough conditions to choose: https://www.netlify.com/pricing/ . Calculations below add USD packs **only conditional on the selected base being billed in USD with the stated allowance**. Otherwise retain separate currency components rather than convert. Do not use old indexed Pro tier prices to optimize the estimate. Higher tiers exist, but their current price was not established.

Credit purchase formulas for total credit usage `C`, no carryover and no other add-ons:

- Personal: `P + $5 USD × ceil(max(0, C − 1000)/500)`.
- Pro base: `Q + $10 USD × ceil(max(0, C − 3000)/1500)`.

Whole pack purchase is rounded up explicitly; this does not assert invoice duration rounding. Pack remainders carry over without expiry, so these are first-cycle cash purchases, not a steady-state per-credit amortization. The next month's spend can differ with carried balance. Auto recharge must be enabled, or enough packs bought under the documented pack route, to avoid exhaustion pausing service.

### A. Small commercial static site

Inputs: 100 MB deployed assets; 1,000,000 HTTP requests/month; 100 GB delivered outbound; 20 × five-minute successful production builds; one domain and private repo. Select static assets without runtime Functions. All 1M requests/100 GB are the **whole project's metered delivery**, including any selected previews/redirects; extra team traffic is outside this workload.

- Deploys: `20 × 15 = 300 credits`.
- Outgoing delivery: `100 GB × 20 = 2000 credits`.
- Requests: `(1,000,000/10,000) × 2 = 200 credits`.
- Total stated metered usage: `C_A = 300 + 2000 + 200 = 2500 credits`. No separate current build-minute meter or ordinary deployed-file storage meter is listed in the complete credit schedule. This refers to deploy assets, not Database/Blob uploads. Certificates included in ordinary domain feature: https://docs.netlify.com/manage/domains/secure-domains-with-https/https-ssl/ . Deployment history follows https://docs.netlify.com/deploy/manage-deploys/manage-deploys-overview/ .
- Free 300 cannot complete the full workload. Personal needs `ceil((2500−1000)/500) = 3` packs: **P + $15 USD**. Pro 3,000 credits covers it: **Q**.
- If applicable bases are confirmed USD, Personal's displayed variants produce **$24/$27/$30 before tax**; Pro's displayed variants produce **$20/$25 before tax**. These are conditional alternatives, not verified prices for three countries or a guaranteed lowest monthly plan.

The 100 MB output is not evidence that any individual large file is accepted.
