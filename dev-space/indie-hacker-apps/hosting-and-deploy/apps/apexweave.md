## Cost examples — our calculation

Calculated 2026-11-05 in **USD**, as explicitly identified by the public product catalogue. Use one paying builder, normal monthly billing, one private source repository, a commercial workload and an own domain. No trial credit, annual discount, optional support package, domain-registration fee or currency conversion is included. Tax inclusion and any mandatory setup charge remain unknown.

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: [catalog](https://apexweave.com/api/order/products), [price](https://apexweave.com/pricing), [app](https://apexweave.com/app-hosting), [git](https://docs.apexweave.com/#git-deployment), [runtime](https://docs.apexweave.com/#runtime-stack), [build](https://docs.apexweave.com/#settings-build), [domain](https://docs.apexweave.com/#domain-management), [backup](https://apexweave.com/blog/database-backup), [dbjs](https://apexweave.com/build/assets/DatabasesPage-CHWnVwAf.js), [termsjs](https://apexweave.com/build/assets/TermsPage-smzK7V5U.js). The catalogue is the unauthenticated product feed used by the vendor's public pricing/app/database pages. No private account endpoint was queried.

These are **subscription-fee components, not full comparable monthly totals**. Let **U_A**, **U_B** and **U_C** represent any required but unpriced transfer, build, extra storage/backup, operations, setup and tax components. They may be zero, but the public sources do not establish that. No bandwidth allowance, per-GB excess tariff or build-minute budget was located, so a one-million-request/100-GB bill cannot be completed.

### A. Small commercial static site

Required inputs: 100 MB deployed files; 1,000,000 HTTP requests/month; 100 GB outbound; 20 builds × 5 minutes = **100 build minutes**; one private repo and custom domain.

Native static:latest uses NGINX. Select **AppForge Starter** as the smallest paid technical candidate: **0.5 CPU, 512 MB RAM, 10 GB NVMe storage**. Custom domain, free SSL, private Git and auto-build workflow are listed. A separate mandatory gateway/IP purchase is not published.

- Monthly app subscription = **1 × $5 = $5**.
- 100 MB files = **0.1 GB** if decimal file units are assumed, well below the listed 10 GB disk by themselves.
- System/dependency files, retained builds/logs and app uploads also require capacity; their storage accounting is not fully documented.
- Starter is marketed for side projects/testing, while larger plans explicitly mention businesses. No Starter-specific commercial prohibition was found, but the short terms do not fully define commerce/ads/affiliate permissions.

No permanent free total is used because trial/expiry wording conflicts. An illustrated quick deployment is not proof that 100 build minutes or the specified traffic quantity is included. The allocation was not benchmarked for one million requests.

**Known subscription component: $5/month. Full A expression: $5 + U_A; complete total and exact traffic/build fit unknown.**

### B. Small commercial app API

Required inputs: stateless HTTP app; 1,000,000 requests/month; 100 ms active CPU and 250 ms elapsed/request; 256 MiB working memory; 100 GB outbound; A's private repo/builds/domain. Directory comparison assumes at least **one vCPU and 1 GiB RAM for 730 hours**.

Select **AppForge XL** as the smallest nominal matching app tier: **one advertised CPU core, 2 GB RAM and 30 GB NVMe disk**, compared with Pro's 0.75 CPU/1 GB. Even decimal 2 GB exceeds 1 GiB. The advertised core is a container allocation, not a dedicated-physical-core or equal-throughput guarantee; exact physical tenancy/architecture is not published.

- Monthly app subscription = **1 × $15 = $15**.
- Stated active CPU input = 1,000,000 × 0.100 = **100,000 CPU-seconds**.
- Stated summed request elapsed time = 1,000,000 × 0.250 = **250,000 request-seconds**.

Request-time inputs are not a function-duration charge. The model is monthly service pricing, so the fee is not multiplied by 730. Treat this as one ordinary monthly subscription and the comparison's 730-hour capacity assumption; stopping a container has no published automatic subscription-fee waiver.

Private Git, auto-build workflow, domain and SSL are documented. Request/connection ceilings, delivery quantities and exact build entitlements remain unpriced.

**Known subscription component: $15/month. Full B expression: $15 + U_B; complete total unknown.** Capacity is a nominal documented allocation, not measured app performance.

### C. App plus persistent PostgreSQL

Add PostgreSQL with at least 1 GiB memory and 10 GB data, daily backups retained seven days, and 10 GB/month private app/database traffic.

**No database tier can be verified to meet the memory requirement from the public catalogue.** Database XL advertises 1 GB but returns memory_limit 512; Database XXL advertises 2 GB but also returns memory_limit 512, with no public schema explanation. Do not assume the feature prose overrides the numeric quota or vice versa.

For a transparent price illustration only, select **Database XXL's advertised 2 GB/50 GB disk plan**, not as a verified qualifying configuration:

- AppForge XL subscription = **$15/month**.
- Database XXL subscription = **$8/month**.
- Combined listed subscription fees = **$15 + $8 = $23/month**.

Database storage is separate from the app's 30 GB. The required 10 GB database data fits the advertised 50 GB disk, but backup storage may have its own inclusion rules. If seven equal compressed daily copies of size **b GB** were stored inside that same allocation with **s GB** system/temporary overhead, the illustration would require **10 + 7b + s ≤ 50 GB**. This is our conditional storage formula, not the provider's documented backup format or quota method.

Product page says daily snapshots retained seven days; backup guide says daily managed-DB backups retained thirty days and PITR included. Both nominally cover seven days, but eligibility, consistency, usable-copy guarantees and billing are not reconciled. Exact recovery semantics were not tested.

Internal endpoints are advertised, yet there is no published charge/allowance rule for the specified 10 GB private traffic. A private address does not by itself establish zero-cost transfer. The documented dump/import path can support independent copies, but external storage and any scheduled backup process have no complete public tariff.

**Conditional listed-fee subtotal: $23/month. Full expression: $23 + U_C; qualifying database memory, exact backup policy and complete monthly total unknown.** App rollback redeploys an older commit and does not restore historical PostgreSQL data.
