### C. App plus persistent data

Add one **self-managed Postgres database**, at least 1 GiB memory, 10 GB active database storage, daily backups retained seven days, and 10 GB/month app-to-database traffic.

Select **CPX32, NBG1**, four shared vCPUs, **8 GB RAM / 160 GB primary disk**, plus the optional daily seven-slot disk Backup service. This is an explicit resource increase over B, rather than pretending Postgres is a free managed add-on. Budget at least **1 GiB for Postgres** and 256 MiB for the API, leaving capacity for the OS, caches and build/backup work. Database memory configuration, SQL updates, authentication and monitoring are operator-managed.

For storage, budget 10 GB active data plus **seven complete logical exports at an assumed 10 GB each**, with remaining space for OS/app/index/WAL growth. Export size is a planning assumption, not a guaranteed bound on every 10 GB database. The larger disk avoids fitting active data, seven uncompressed copies and OS into a marginal 80 GB server.

Postgres `pg_dump` produces a consistent single-database export even while users access it; roles/global objects need separate handling, and its own docs warn it is not the general solution for every production backup strategy: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/app-pgdump.html, checked 2026-11-06. Our proposed workflow is daily completed exports, retained seven days, with incomplete output kept separate until completion and restoration tested. This was **not implemented or tested** during research.

Enable the daily full-primary-disk Backups so these completed exports also have off-server copies. Hetzner chooses the automatic backup window and does not guarantee a live disk image is consistent. The design therefore preserves completed logical exports rather than assuming raw live Postgres files are always recoverable. Do not rely on unfinished exports; schedule and verify against the selected backup window and available disk space. Sources: https://docs.hetzner.com/cloud/servers/backups-snapshots/overview/, https://docs.hetzner.com/cloud/servers/backups-snapshots/faq/, https://www.hetzner.com/legal/terms-and-conditions/, checked 2026-11-06.
