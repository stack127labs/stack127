Calculated 2026-11-05, in the catalogue's native **USD**. These are conditional hosting subtotals, not complete quotes. One paying commercial builder, normal monthly subscriptions, no credits, annual discounts or optional support. Existing custom-domain registration stays outside hosting. Tax inclusion and the buyer's applicable tax are not established; no tax rate or currency conversion is assumed.

### A. Small commercial static site

Inputs: 100 MB deployed files, 1,000,000 requests/month, 100 GB outbound, 20 builds × 5 minutes = **100 build minutes**, one private repository and one existing custom domain.

Formula: known hosting subscription = **1 × $3 = $3/month**. Full workload cost = **$3 + U_A**, where U_A is the unpriced private-source/build route and any required charges not resolved by the public tariff, plus applicable tax. **No exact total.** A separate necessary gateway/IP/SSL fee was not found, but the tariff does not establish every possible additional meter.
