## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Price-card scope:** product cards say “for reference only.” Billing docs include example rates but say live ECS pricing details are authoritative and regional. The international pricing calculator did not expose stable numeric rate tables through public fetch, so only card reference prices and known billing components are recorded.
- **Region prices:** verified card values are specific to AP-Singapore and CN-Hong Kong. No price was copied to other listed international regions. Region availability and flavor availability can differ.
- **Free offer:** the international free-offer catalog is dynamic. No ECS recurring free allowance was established; limited-time promotions should be checked against the buyer's account and region.
- **Signup country coverage:** account country, billing address, sanctions and payment method constrain access, but the checked international account/help pages do not provide a complete country-by-country ECS eligibility table. No signup was attempted.
- **Data residency:** the privacy statement describes customer content location choice and cross-border handling, but it does not establish that all support, account, billing, log and backup data remain in the selected ECS region.
- **News/blog access:** official Huawei Cloud press releases and blog index were accessible and checked. Guessed older paths `/intl/en-us/press-releases.html` and `/intl/en-us/blogs/` failed to load; the active international paths are `/intl/en-us/news` and `/intl/en-us/about/blogs.html`. The parent Huawei newsroom was also checked. No relevant shutdown/acquisition notice was found.

## Field problems

- Field 05 asks for the cheapest complete bill. ECS reference cards show compute-only figures, while required disk and public-network prices are dynamic and regional; no all-in “starting price” is claimed.
- Field 09 trial/payment terms change by country and offer. The public international offer page is dynamic and account-specific; the limited-time offer catalog is not evidence of an always-free tier.
- Field 19 framework features are controlled by the customer-installed runtime and app. ECS does not publish managed framework adapters, so framework-specific SSR/streaming claims are not applicable to the service itself.
- Field 22 protocols are guest-OS configured, while EIP/security-group constraints are separately documented. ECS does not publish managed WebSocket or request-duration guarantees.
- Field 32 has different single-AZ and multi-AZ SLA values. Only the single-AZ service-credit tiers are included here; the multi-AZ percentage is not turned into an assumed credit.
- The international product page provides several flavor cards but not every region/flavor combination. The exact price calculator could not be extracted from the public page; no login or purchase console was used.
