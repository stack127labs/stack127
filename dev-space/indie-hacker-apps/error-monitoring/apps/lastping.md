## Cost examples

All amounts below are **our calculations**, not vendor quotes. The advertised individual plan is $0, with no monitor cap; terms still permit rate-limiting or account suspension for use beyond normal monitoring. Counts use the field-list's 30-day month.

- **Scheduled jobs:** 1,440 pings per job/month = 24 runs/day × 30 days × 2 signals (start and success). Small: 5 × 1,440 = 7,200 pings, $0/month. Growing: 25 × 1,440 = 36,000 pings, $0/month. Busy: 100 × 1,440 = 144,000 pings, $0/month. No numeric job/ping quota is published; all three costs assume fair use.
- **HTTP uptime:** 43,200 runs per endpoint/month = 60 runs/hour × 24 hours × 30 days. Small: 5 × 43,200 = 216,000 runs; growing: 25 × 43,200 = 1,080,000; busy: 100 × 43,200 = 4,320,000. The monitor charge is $0/month for each individual workload. The vendor does not publish probe-location count, custom-domain status-page support or a numeric HTTP run cap, so this does not establish that every comparison condition is available.
- **Status pages:** The page feature is free for an individual, but only one public page per project is documented. Page-subscriber counts, email updates and custom domains are not documented. So $0 is the known page-feature charge; the 100-, 1,000- and 10,000-subscriber scenarios cannot be fully priced or confirmed as supported.

## Field problems

- The field list has no AI-agent-monitoring group. I recorded agent spans, metrics, logs, budgets and retention under C14/C20/C22, A01/A02 and L01/L02. These are agent-only signals, not general application APM or log search.
- The official source repository is MIT licensed but contains CLI/MCP clients, not a complete self-hosted service. C06 applies to that client code; H01–H03 are not applicable to the hosted platform.
- The status-page cost scenario assumes subscriber email and custom-domain support. LastPing documents public/private pages and included page counts, but does not document those assumptions. Its $0 page cost should not be compared as a complete subscriber-page bundle.
