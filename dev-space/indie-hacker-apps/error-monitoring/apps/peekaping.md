## Cost examples — our calculation, estimated

These compare the fixed editorial workloads. They are **our calculation, estimated**, not a quote or a tested capacity claim. The MIT self-hosted software has **0 software-license fee**; the project does not assign that zero a currency. No exchange rate, trial credit or external provider's free allowance is assumed.

### Uptime

Inputs: **5 / 25 / 100 HTTP endpoints**, checks every **60 seconds**, **one probe location**, **30 days**, email alerts, and **one own-domain status page**.

Formula: scheduled runs per endpoint = 30 × 24 × 60 = **43,200**. Runs = endpoints × 43,200 × locations.

| Endpoints | One-location scheduled runs | Three-location scheduled runs | Software-license fee | Complete recurring total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| 5 | 216,000 | 648,000 | 0 | Unknown |
| 25 | 1,080,000 | 3,240,000 | 0 | Unknown |
| 100 | 4,320,000 | 12,960,000 | 0 | Unknown |

Three locations mean three separately configured probes, not three instances sharing one token. Retried checks and confirmation activity can add runs. No vendor per-run rate was found for the self-hosted release. A 60-second interval meets the released 20-second minimum; the timeout may be at most 60 × 0.8 = **48 seconds**. Own-domain page configuration and SMTP integration exist, but their domain, server and mail-provider costs are not included in the zero.

Total formula = **software fee 0 + compute + persistent database/storage + backups + network/egress + domain/DNS/TLS operations where charged + mail-provider charges**. A three-location setup also needs remote-probe compute and network capacity. Workload-specific CPU/RAM/disk sizing is not published, so an exact host configuration or full monthly total cannot be justified.
