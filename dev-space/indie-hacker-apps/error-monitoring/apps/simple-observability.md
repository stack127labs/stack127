## Scope and evidence

The apps.md URL points to Simple Observability at simpleobservability.com. The product combines server metrics and logs, HTTP endpoint checks, alerting, and scheduled-job monitoring. The standard platform is hosted. Its BYOS offer keeps raw telemetry in a customer-managed store while Simple Observability still runs the interface and alert engine; BYOS is not a fully self-hosted platform.

The agent repository is marked GPL-3.0. The Terms say the hosted platform's software and intellectual property belong to Chunk SASAU. The Privacy Policy identifies Chunk SASAU as a registered company in Casablanca, Morocco. No private person's name is included.

Pricing is shown as “$3 per month per server.” The vendor does not identify the currency code or say whether VAT is included. The free plan's duration and commercial-use eligibility are unstated. Annual subscriptions are allowed by the Terms, but the pricing page lists only the monthly rate. The price page directs businesses above 10 servers to request custom pricing, although the slider displays a maximum of $150. Examples stay within the clearly published 10-server range.

I checked the homepage, pricing, docs, Terms, Privacy Policy, data-residency page, BYOS page, GitHub agent repository and releases, and the company blog. I searched official pages for acquisition, shutdown, and sunset announcements. No such notice was found. The newsroom and changelog URLs did not load. The Terms and Privacy pages were read; their last update dates are shown below where applicable.
