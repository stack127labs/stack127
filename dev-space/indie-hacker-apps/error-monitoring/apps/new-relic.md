**Free tier, ingest and users:** Free includes **100 GB/month shared across telemetry, one Full Platform user and unlimited Basic users**, with no credit card. Exceeding the free data limit stops ingest **and platform access** until an upgrade or the next month. Paid Original Data is **USD 0.40/GB beyond 100 GB**; Data Plus is **USD 0.60/GB beyond 100 GB**, with an additional EU-region surcharge. These are processed/stored bytes, rounded up to whole GB monthly. On Standard, the **first Full Platform user is USD 10/month promotional** and additional Full Platform users are **USD 99 each/month**, up to five. The one free Full Platform user does not remain free when applying Standard's paid seat table. Basic, Core and Full Platform users have different product access. An alternative compute model replaces user licensing with consumption charges; its Core Compute price was not established here. Sources: https://newrelic.com/pricing/free-tier (checked 2026-11-05; source updated Not shown) ; https://newrelic.com/pricing (checked 2026-11-05; source updated Not shown) ; https://docs.newrelic.com/docs/accounts/accounts-billing/new-relic-one-pricing-billing/new-relic-one-pricing-billing/ (checked 2026-11-05; source updated Not shown) ; https://docs.newrelic.com/docs/licenses/license-information/usage-plans/new-relic-usage-plan/ (checked 2026-11-05; source updated Not shown) ; https://docs.newrelic.com/docs/licenses/license-information/product-definitions/new-relic-one-pricing-definitions/ (checked 2026-11-05; source updated Not shown) ; https://docs.newrelic.com/docs/accounts/accounts-billing/new-relic-one-user-management/user-type/ (checked 2026-11-05; source updated Not shown) ; https://newrelic.com/pricing/compute (checked 2026-11-05; source updated Not shown).

**Retention boundary:** Original Data logs default to **30 days**, distributed Span data to **eight days**. Data Plus defaults are **120** and **98 days** respectively. However, **APM error stack traces, TransactionTrace and SqlTrace default to eight days under both data options**. Longer APM-error event history must not be advertised as equally long stack-trace detail. Source: https://docs.newrelic.com/docs/data-apis/manage-data/manage-data-retention/ (checked 2026-11-05; source updated Not shown).

### Performance
