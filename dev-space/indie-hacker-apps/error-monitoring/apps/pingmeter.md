## Cost examples — our calculation, estimated

These are **our calculations, estimated**, using the live public plan table checked **2026-11-05**: https://pingmeter.com/#pricing . Check-frequency, multi-location, confirmation and page claims are from https://pingmeter.com/ and https://pingmeter.com/presskit.php . No third-party price or trial allowance is used.

### Uptime detection baseline

**Inputs:** one builder; 5, 25 or 100 separate endpoint monitors; **60-second** HTTP checks; one requested probe location; full **30-day** month; email outage alerts; one own-domain status page. No SMS/calls, paid credit top-ups, extra sub-users or recovery actions are required for the arithmetic.

**Run formula:** 30 × 24 × 60 = **43,200 scheduled requests per endpoint**. Baseline runs = endpoints × 43,200. Actual failure-confirmation or extra requests cannot be calculated because the public page does not state retry count/cadence. Pingmeter publishes monitor-based plans, not a per-request rate; these run counts explain the workload rather than inventing request charges.

| Workload | Baseline scheduled requests | Smallest advertised matching cadence/capacity | Detection subscription calculation | Full required scenario |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 endpoints | 5 × 43,200 = **216,000** | Basic: 50 monitors, 60-second checks | 1 account × $12.99/month = **$12.99/month** | **Unknown**: one-location selection/charging and own-domain page entitlement/setup not defined |
| 25 endpoints | 25 × 43,200 = **1,080,000** | Basic: 50 monitors, 60-second checks | 1 account × $12.99/month = **$12.99/month** | **Unknown**, same qualifications |
| 100 endpoints | 100 × 43,200 = **4,320,000** | Standard: 100 monitors, 60-second checks | 1 account × $36.95/month = **$36.95/month** | **Unknown**, same qualifications |

Free's 10-minute and Starter's 5-minute intervals do not match the workload, even when their monitor count is sufficient. Basic's 60-day history and Standard's “Unlimited” history cover the example's 30-day duration as advertised; raw export, account deletion and backups remain separate unknowns. Email has a checkmark on all plans.

These are **detection subtotals**, not verified complete prices. “Under your own subdomain” does not resolve whether a customer-owned DNS domain, managed TLS, a certain page count or paid page entitlement is included. Status pages have no plan-comparison row. No required page charge is silently assigned zero.

### Three locations

With three independently scheduled probe locations, required baseline checks would be:

- 5 endpoints: 216,000 × 3 = **648,000/month**.
- 25 endpoints: 1,080,000 × 3 = **3,240,000/month**.
- 100 endpoints: 4,320,000 × 3 = **12,960,000/month**.

**Three-location price is unknown.** The homepage advertises simultaneous multi-location scans, but supplies no selected-location allowance, per-location multiplier, minimum required probe count or billable-check definition. It is not defensible to multiply the subscription by three, assume three locations are included, or assume one location can be selected independently. Extra confirmation scans are also unspecified.
