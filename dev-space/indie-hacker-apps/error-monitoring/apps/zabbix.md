**Lifecycle and ownership check:** Zabbix is active. Zabbix 8.0.0 LTS was released on 2026-11-05. I checked the official release list, product blog, pressroom, release notes, and support policy pages for a shutdown, sunset, or acquisition notice; I found none. The pressroom identifies Zabbix SIA as a Latvia-based company, with headquarters in Riga. The product row is broadly accurate, though Zabbix is a general monitoring and observability platform rather than a dedicated exception tracker.

**Scope:** The main record below covers the free, self-hosted Zabbix server, frontend, agents, and proxies. Zabbix Cloud and paid support are described separately at the end. Those offers do not change the self-hosted license or product limits.

The official site describes Zabbix as open-source monitoring software for infrastructure and services. The software is licensed under GNU AGPLv3 from version 7.0 onward. The documentation itself has separate use terms and is not under AGPLv3.

## Self-host cost examples

These are **our calculations**, not Zabbix quotes. The official sizing guide provides CPU/RAM starting points; AWS pages provide the cited on-demand compute and gp3 storage rates. Each estimate assumes 730 VM hours and a 100 GB gp3 volume. Small: `$0.096 × 730 + 100 × $0.08 = $78.08/month`. Medium: `$0.192 × 730 + 100 × $0.08 = $148.16/month`. The first number in each formula is compute and the second is disk. These subtotals exclude backups, network transfer, taxes, any separate database host, and operator time. The AWS disk rate can vary by region. Zabbix advises workload benchmarking; these sizes are starting examples, not performance promises.

## Field problems

- C11/H02 asks for comparable cost examples, but Zabbix cannot provide an infrastructure total without an operator-selected provider, region, database layout, and retention. I give two transparent compute-plus-disk examples and leave large-installation total cost unpublished.
