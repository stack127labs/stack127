## Cost examples: our calculation, estimated

These are estimates of **workload and the project license component**, not complete hosting quotes. MIT grants the software without a fee and does not set a per-monitor/per-run recurring charge. No currency conversion is involved: zero license fee has no invoice currency. Hosting, persistent storage, backups, traffic, domains/DNS, SMTP/SMS/calls, remote-browser services, optional Globalping, taxes and operator labor are not priced. Labor is excluded, not assumed free.

No validated CPU/RAM/disk sizing for these workloads was established in the official install/image/runtime material. Selecting an arbitrary cheap VPS would imply unsupported capacity, so no host rate is attached and **all complete recurring totals remain unknown**. There is no first-party annual subscription discount to calculate.
