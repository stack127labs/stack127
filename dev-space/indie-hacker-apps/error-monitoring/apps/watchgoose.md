## Cost examples

These are **our calculations**, not vendor quotes. They use monthly EUR pricing before VAT and the field list's 30-day month.

- **Scheduled jobs:** 1,440 pings per hourly job/month = 24 runs/day × 30 days × 2 signals (start and success). Small: 5 jobs = 5 checks and 7,200 pings; Free includes 10 checks, so the check-based price is **EUR 0/month**. Growing: 25 jobs = 25 checks and 36,000 pings; Startup includes 100 checks, so **EUR 17/month**. Busy: 100 jobs = 100 checks and 144,000 pings; Startup fits exactly at **EUR 17/month**. These estimates price the check allowance only. Free lists 200 ping-log entries and Startup 1,500; the vendor does not explain whether those numbers are per account or per check, or how long the event history lasts, so full-history fit is unknown.
- **Status pages:** one public page per project is included on every plan. The page feature itself has no separately listed price, but subscriber notification support, subscriber caps and custom-domain support were not found. The page fee is therefore known as included; totals for 100, 1,000 or 10,000 subscribers cannot be calculated as complete bundles.

## Field problems

- Watchgoose combines a cron heartbeat monitor with a basic public status page. The status-page fields apply to that bundled communication feature; they do not make Watchgoose a general uptime checker.
- The pricing page gives ping-log **entry counts** without saying whether each count is per account or per check or how long entries remain. I kept that count separate from a time-based retention claim and marked its scope unclear.
- The plan's fixed check price does not depend on the number of heartbeat requests in the public table. Cost examples therefore show the price for check capacity, while noting that full event-history retention is not established.
- A complete hosted service license and self-host requirements do not apply because no self-hosted service or server source is offered in the reviewed official material. The company's Open Source support program is a customer benefit, not a server license.
