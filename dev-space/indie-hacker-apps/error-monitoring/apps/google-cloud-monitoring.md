## Status, sunset, acquisition and product changes

Cloud Monitoring is an active, hosted Google Cloud service. Google acquired Stackdriver in 2014 and retired the Stackdriver brand in favor of Google Cloud Operations in 2020; that is historical ownership and renaming, not a current acquisition or a shutdown of Monitoring. The latest release notes checked include 2026 updates. No whole-product sunset or acquisition affecting Cloud Monitoring was found in the product pages, Monitoring release/deprecation notes, Google Cloud Blog, Google Cloud newsroom, or Google Cloud Press Corner.

The legacy Monitoring agent reached end of support on 2026-09-28. Its migration assistance runs through 2027-09-28. This is a collection-agent retirement; the hosted Monitoring service remains active. Ops Agent, Prometheus and OpenTelemetry paths are documented. ## Product and price boundary

Cloud Monitoring collects, stores and charts metrics; monitors application and infrastructure health; and provides dashboards, SLOs, alerting, public/private uptime checks and synthetic tests. It can display application metrics and link to traces, but Cloud Trace, Cloud Logging and Error Reporting are separate Google Cloud products and their storage, prices and limits are not included here. Monitoring does not provide grouped exception issues or a customer-facing status page.

This is a hosted service. Ops Agent, Prometheus collectors, OpenTelemetry SDKs and test functions may run in the customer's environment, but none is a self-hosted Cloud Monitoring backend.

**Price region:** Workload examples below use **us-central1 (Iowa)** where a region is needed to describe the monitored resource or synthetic function. Google publishes Cloud Monitoring's USD rates without regional price rows; the rates below are not region-specific quotes. Uptime-check probe locations are listed separately because probe geography affects the number and location of executions, not the published per-execution rate. Monitoring is a global product, but time-series storage depends on the monitored resource's valid location, zone or region labels. [Pricing](https://cloud.google.com/products/observability/pricing) · [Monitoring data regionality](https://docs.cloud.google.com/monitoring/docs/region-support)

## Cost examples: our calculations, not quotes

All examples use USD public list rates checked 2026-11-06. **The Monitoring rate card does not publish regional price differences.** Where a resource or function location helps define the scenario, it is **us-central1 (Iowa)**. Exclude taxes, negotiated discounts, other Google Cloud products, logging/tracing, notification-provider fees and any usage from other projects or billing-account workloads unless stated.

### Byte-metered metrics

Inputs are monthly metric volumes of 1 GiB, 10 GiB and 50 GiB. Use binary units: 1 GiB = 1,024 MiB. Assume one unused allowance of 150 MiB per billing account and that all paid usage remains in the first tier at $0.2580/MiB.

Formula: max(metric MiB − 150 MiB, 0) × $0.2580.

| Monthly volume | Formula | Our calculation |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 GiB | (1,024 − 150) × $0.2580 | $225.49 |
| 10 GiB | (10,240 − 150) × $0.2580 | $2,603.22 |
| 50 GiB | (51,200 − 150) × $0.2580 | $13,170.90 |

### Managed Prometheus or eligible OTLP samples

Inputs are 1 million, 10 million and 50 million monthly samples. The first band is $0.06 per million samples and has no free sample allowance.

Formula: sample millions × $0.06.

| Monthly samples | Formula | Our calculation |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 million | 1 × $0.06 | $0.06 |
| 10 million | 10 × $0.06 | $0.60 |
| 50 million | 50 × $0.06 | $3.00 |

### Monitoring API reads

Inputs are 1 million, 10 million and 50 million returned time series from qualifying non-console read calls. Assume no other usage has consumed the first 1 million free returned series that month.

Formula: max(returned series − 1,000,000, 0) ÷ 1,000,000 × $0.50.

| Returned series | Formula | Our calculation |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 million | max(1M − 1M, 0) × $0.50/M | $0 |
| 10 million | (10M − 1M) × $0.50/M | $4.50 |
| 50 million | (50M − 1M) × $0.50/M | $24.50 |

### Public uptime checks

Inputs: 5, 25 or 100 endpoint configurations; one execution every minute for 30 days; each configuration runs in USA's three regions (us-central1, us-east4 and us-west1); all share one project's 1 million free regional executions. A 30-day month has 43,200 one-minute intervals.

Formula: max(endpoints × 43,200 × 3 − 1,000,000, 0) × ($0.30 ÷ 1,000).

| Endpoints | Monthly regional executions | Our calculation |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 5 | 648,000 | $0 |
| 25 | 3,240,000 | $672 |
| 100 | 12,960,000 | $3,588 |

### Synthetic monitors

Inputs: 1, 5 or 20 test flows, each run every 5 minutes for 30 days (8,640 executions per flow). Assume one unused 100-execution billing-account allowance and $1.20 per 1,000 additional runs.

Formula: max(test flows × 8,640 − 100, 0) × ($1.20 ÷ 1,000).

| Test flows | Monthly executions | Our calculation |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 8,640 | $10.248 |
| 5 | 43,200 | $51.72 |
| 20 | 172,800 | $207.24 |

These are synthetic execution fees only. The function's Cloud Run/Cloud Run functions, network, storage and Cloud Logging charges are excluded. Request count by itself cannot price traces or an instrumented application; those separate products or measured metric-byte/sample quantities are needed for a total.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Future alert charges:** The current pricing page announces alert-policy fees no sooner than 2027-09-01. They are not included in costs as of 2026-11-06.
- **Price region versus data region:** The published Monitoring price table does not provide regional price rows. Monitoring docs separately describe resource-based time-series storage and probe execution locations. These claims describe different things, not a rate conflict.
- **Uptime result retention:** Checked the public uptime-check guide, uptime charts and metric-retention reference. They do not clearly publish a separate retention period for uptime-check results, so the row remains unclear.
- **Data residency:** The regionality guide gives technical storage rules, while the general data-residency terms index does not establish a selectable Cloud Monitoring residency commitment. No broader contractual guarantee is inferred.
- **Product split:** Google Cloud Logging and Error Reporting are distinct directory entries; Cloud Trace pricing and trace retention are also out of scope here.
- No apps.md row error found. apps.md was not changed.

## Field problems

- **C07/C08/C10/C11:** “Free” is not one plan. Metric bytes, Prometheus samples, API reads, uptime executions and synthetic executions have separate meters, scopes and allowances. Cloud Monitoring list prices do not publish region-specific rate tiers.
- **C12/C13:** Billing allowances, technical quotas and cost notifications behave differently. Budget notifications are not a hard cap, and quota failures are not a universal spending limit.
- **C14/C21:** Metric retention varies by metric domain. Uptime-result retention and point-level deletion are not clearly documented in the pages checked; descriptor deletion is not immediate data erasure.
- **C22/C23:** Technical storage placement is resource-based, and the general Cloud data-residency index does not provide a Monitoring-wide region choice. No customer-selected region guarantee is stated.
- **A01–A03:** Cloud Monitoring provides application-health metrics but not a bundled request-tracing or real-user-monitoring meter; Cloud Trace is a separate product.
- **U05:** HTTPS certificate expiry is documented; domain registration expiry was not found after checking the uptime guide, uptime metrics and pricing.
- **E01–E04, S01–S03, J01–J02 and H01–H03:** These groups describe exception tracking, status pages, job heartbeats and self-hosting. They are not Cloud Monitoring offerings.
