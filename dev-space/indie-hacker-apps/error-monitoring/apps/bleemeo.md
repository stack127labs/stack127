## Cost examples

All amounts below are **our calculations**, in EUR, excluding VAT, using the published monthly reference rates and a 30-day month. Server plans meter hourly and invoice monthly. They exclude discounts, taxes, and any usage not listed in the formula.

- **Starter server monitoring:** 5 × €4.99 = €24.95; 25 × €4.99 = €124.75; 100 × €4.99 = €499.00.
- **Professional server monitoring:** 5 × €10.99 = €54.95; 25 × €10.99 = €274.75; 100 × €10.99 = €1,099.00.
- **Uptime checks on Starter:** assume one paid server and 5 monitors included per account. 5 monitors: €4.99 total; 25 monitors: €4.99 + 20 × €1.01 = €25.19; 100 monitors: €4.99 + 95 × €1.01 = €100.94.
- **Logs:** at the English-page rate of €0.50/GiB, 5 GiB = €2.50 and 25 GiB = €12.50 if evenly spread under the 1 GiB/day limit. For 100 GiB, unit-rate arithmetic is €50.00, but that workload exceeds the daily limit and the actual acceptance or bill is not published; the exact total is unknown.

## Field problems

- Uptime monitoring and customer status pages are both relevant but distinct; status pages show monitored application health and do not provide the underlying probe themselves.
- Community Edition is a separate, complete self-hosted stack. The apps.md deployment cell is marked “Unknown” and its description only mentions a hosted agent, so the listing understates the available deployments.
- Several docs and legal pages have older publication dates; the French terms are from 2017 and the privacy policy from 2021. The current English pricing page and the older French table conflict on log price.
- Error tracking, APM tracing, real-user monitoring, and cron-heartbeat fields were marked not applicable because the official product scope reviewed does not describe those products. Glouton can parse logs and run service checks, but neither is an exception tracker or missed-job monitor.
