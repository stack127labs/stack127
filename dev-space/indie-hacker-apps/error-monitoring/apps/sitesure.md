## Cost examples

**Our calculation, estimated.** Checked **2026-11-05**. Preserve the published **$** symbol; no currency conversion or assumption that it means USD. Monthly rates are used. No annual offer was found. These are monitor-count estimates, not vendor quotes or exact complete workload totals.

Rate source: https://sitesure.net/pricing . Paid plans advertise the same feature list and unlimited users. The Free plan's two monitors do not cover any 5/25/100-monitor scenario, and trial capacity is excluded.

- Petite: **$20 + max(0, N − 5) × $2**.
