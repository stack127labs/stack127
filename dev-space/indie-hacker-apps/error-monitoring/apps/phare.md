### Uptime: one location

**Inputs:** 5, 25 or 100 HTTP endpoints; every **60 seconds**; one selected region; full **30-day** month; one builder; email alert to that registered organization member; one public own-domain status page. Use no additional unrelated monitoring, AI generations, phone alerts, private agent or paid external integrations. Set incident and recovery confirmations to **1** for this baseline, with direct nonredirecting URLs and no extra failure-confirmation requests assumed. This is a comparison input, not Phare's recommended reliability configuration.

Phare meters one event per HTTP/TCP/ICMP check. Multiple regions multiply events. Hobby's **100,000** events, even including its **10%** emergency buffer, do not cover the smallest 216,000-event workload.

**Formula:**

- Runs per endpoint = 30 × 24 × 60 = **43,200**.
- Events E = endpoints × 43,200 × selected regions.
- Full-month Scale price = **€5 + max(0, E − 500,000) × €0.00001**.
- Additional-event authorization must be high enough for max(0, E − 500,000). Unused authorized quota is not charged. Do not treat the temporary free buffer as recurring paid capacity.

| Workload | Scheduled events | Included / additional billed events | Formula | Estimated monthly price, before tax |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | ---: |
| 5 endpoints, 1 region | 5 × 43,200 = **216,000** | 216,000 within 500,000; 0 additional | €5 + 0 × €0.00001 | **€5.00** |
| 25 endpoints, 1 region | 25 × 43,200 = **1,080,000** | 500,000 included; 580,000 additional | €5 + 580,000 × €0.00001 | **€10.80** |
| 100 endpoints, 1 region | 100 × 43,200 = **4,320,000** | 500,000 included; 3,820,000 additional | €5 + 3,820,000 × €0.00001 | **€43.20** |

Email alerts to the builder and a public page/custom domain carry no separate published Phare fee. Registering/renewing the domain is excluded. This workload does not require customer subscriber-email delivery. Thirty-day data is within the normal one-year monitoring retention, subject to the published account/deletion conditions; no archive or raw-export equivalence is implied.
