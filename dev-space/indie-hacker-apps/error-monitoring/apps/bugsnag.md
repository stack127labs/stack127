## Scope and evidence

This replaces the earlier group-run research. Attempted all 27 core fields, four error fields, three performance fields and three self-host fields. General log storage, external uptime probes, synthetic browser runs, public status pages and missed-job scheduling were not established as product families in the checked product guides. A BugSnag service-status page is not a customer status-page product.

Every row has a unit, plan, edition, deployment, signal, source URL, checked date and note. `SaaS` means the standard hosted service at app.bugsnag.com; `Enterprise SaaS` means the separately documented instance at app.bugsnag.smartbear.com. `On-premise` means the licensed customer-operated backend. `SDK` means client code, not that backend. Unless a note supplies a date, `source_updated_at` is not stated on the source. Checked dates are UTC dates. Multiple URLs preserve claims with different scope or explain a bounded unknown.

Only the owner's six states are used. `not_published` means not found in the named checked sources. `unclear` means a published statement leaves its scope or application unresolved. `check_failed` means retrieval did not establish the value, rather than proving it is unavailable from the vendor. Calculations are labelled separately. No signup, login, form submission, installation, ingestion, alert delivery or support-quality test was performed.
