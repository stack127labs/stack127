### Uptime: one regional group as a stated proxy

Required workload: 5 / 25 / 100 HTTP endpoints, one location, 60-second polling, 30-day month, email alerts, 30 days of monitoring detail and one own-domain status page.

Pingdom explicitly does not allow pinning an individual uptime probe/city. The following uses **one selected regional probe group per endpoint**, rotating about ten servers. It is a qualified proxy for the single-location input, not a claim that the exact fixed-probe scenario is supported.

Healthy scheduled checks = endpoints × 30 × 24 × 60 = endpoints × 43,200. Failure-confirmation requests can add traffic; they are not an established per-run fee.

History caveat: reports describe uptime history since creation and transaction history as forever. That is not a guarantee of complete indefinite raw response bodies/screenshots. The public Test Result Log export is limited to 10,000 lines per download. The raw-artifact retention/availability distinction remains visible.
