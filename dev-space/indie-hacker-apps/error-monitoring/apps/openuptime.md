## Cost examples: our calculation, estimated

All hosted rates are **USD per workspace per month**, with monthly billing. No annual rate, tax amount, paid overage, extra credit pack or currency conversion is invented. The relevant constraints are **both active monitors and executed outbound requests**. An outbound request uses a credit whether it passes or fails. Status-page and alert email allowances are shared.

### Uptime: one region

Inputs: **one builder, 5 / 25 / 100 HTTP endpoints, 60-second checks, one selected region, 30 days, email alerts, one own-domain public status page**. No other scheduled monitors, regional diagnostics or manual checks. Keep notification email within the selected plan's allowance and use an existing owned domain.

Intervals per endpoint: `30 × 24 × 60 = 43,200`.

| Endpoints | Required checks/30 days, our calculation | Selected plan | Active-monitor / check allowance | Estimated recurring subscription |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 5 × 43,200 = **216,000** | Lite | 50 monitors / 300,000 checks | **$5/month** |
| 25 | 25 × 43,200 = **1,080,000** | Starter | 300 / 2,160,000 | **$19/month** |
| 100 | 100 × 43,200 = **4,320,000** | Team | 1,000 / 6,480,000 | **$49/month** |

Formula: select the lowest published plan whose **interval floor, monitor cap, request allowance and paid own-domain entitlement** all fit; no per-monitor fee is added. Email room is **2,000 / 15,000 / 60,000** notifications/month respectively. The input has no specified outage count, so unlimited email delivery is not implied; excess email stops. Free cannot meet a one-minute interval or managed own-domain requirement.

Rates/conditions checked **2026-11-05**: https://openuptime.app/pricing ; https://openuptime.app/docs/regions ; https://openuptime.app/integrations/email ; https://openuptime.app/docs/api .

The single selected region can be Auto or one configured non-Auto region. No guaranteed current hosted city list was established from the public docs; bundled Vercel sample locations are not substituted for it. Billing-period reset anchoring and variable calendar-month length are not fully specified. The fixed calculation uses the requested **30 days**. Manual tests, other monitors and extra regions spend additional credit.

**Why monitor count alone misleads:** 50 Lite monitors all checked every minute would need `50 × 43,200 = 2,160,000` credits, **7.2 times** Lite's 300,000. The sustained 30-day maximum is `floor(300,000 ÷ 43,200) = 6`, as the pricing table states. Free's three five-minute monitors need `3 × 30 × 24 × 12 = 25,920` checks in 30 days, but `3 × 31 × 24 × 12 = 26,784` in 31 days, above 26,000. These are our calculations, not a guarantee of a calendar-period reset model. Source: https://openuptime.app/pricing .
