> **Scope note:** This is PingPong by Bluecap, a hosted status-page and uptime-monitoring service at the URL in `apps.md`. It checks website URLs, tracks SSL certificate expiry, monitors selected third-party services and accepts custom metrics through an API. It is not an error tracker, log platform, APM or self-hosted project.

- **Free incidents and maintenance:** the pricing page says “unlimited incidents” and “Unlimited Scheduled Maintenance” in headings, but its detailed comparison lists 20 incidents and 5 maintenances per month for Free. It does not explain which values control.
- **Paid prices:** the page shows monthly prices and says the first three months are discounted, but does not give the later regular price.
- **Plan comparison:** checkmark cells are not represented in the text extraction. I used the named plan summaries and help pages for confirmed features, and did not assign other features by plan.
- **Policy age:** terms are dated 2020; privacy, DPA and SLA are dated 2021. The DPA still refers to Privacy Shield frameworks for data transfers. No legal-currentness conclusion is made.
- **Data location:** terms say the Site is hosted in Ireland; the product page says status pages use AWS in multiple regions. The reviewed pages do not identify where each monitoring record is stored or offer customer region selection.
- **Backups:** the status-page feature page says hourly backups; the security page says daily backups. I found no clarification.
- **Release and company news:** no public changelog, blog or newsroom was found. The direct `/blog/`, `/news/` and `/changelog/` paths were inaccessible, so no release date is reported.
- **Monitoring rules and history:** check timeouts, retries, failure quorum, precise uptime-history retention, monitor overage behavior and automatic monitor-to-status-page incident publishing are not specified in the docs reviewed.
