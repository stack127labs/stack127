## Cost examples — our calculation, estimated

All examples are **our calculation, estimated**. They are not vendor quotes or load-test results. The AGPL self-hosted software has **0 software-license fee**, with no charge currency assigned by Kuvasz. Host prices retain the original **$** amounts, **GiB**, **vCPU** and billing conditions from DigitalOcean; no exchange rate or trial credit is used.

### Selected operating setup

Assumption for each single-location example: one **DigitalOcean Basic Regular bundled Droplet**, **2 vCPUs, 4 GiB RAM, 80 GiB SSD, 4,000 GiB monthly transfer**, hosting Kuvasz and PostgreSQL together. The published monthly cap is **$24.00**, with listed hourly rate **$0.03571**. Use one complete 30-day billing month and ordinary bundled-plan billing, not a v5 uncapped configuration. DigitalOcean states per-second billing and a minimum charge of 60 seconds or $0.01, whichever is higher.

The official Kuvasz sizing guide recommends **768 MB for Kuvasz**, **at least 1 CPU**, and reports **512 MB database allocation** for up to 1,000 mixed monitors in its benchmark. Our selected host leaves additional memory and CPU room for PostgreSQL and the operating system. It is a conservative example, not a verified minimum: the official benchmarks ran on an **Apple M1 Pro**, with mixed intervals/types and concurrent readers, so their CPU results are not a cloud-vCPU performance guarantee.

Formula:

- Software = **0**.
- Selected compute/storage/transfer bundle = **$24.00/month**, assuming included resources suffice.
- Basic weekly system backup = $24.00 × 20% = **$4.80/month**.
- Known selected subtotal = 0 + $24.00 + $4.80 = **$28.80/month**.
- Three independent copies = 3 × $28.80 = **$86.40/month**.

The complete bill is **not exact**. Add any domain/DNS/SMTP or alert-provider charges, excess transfer, extra backup/archive storage, taxes and maintenance labour. A system disk backup is not a tested database-consistent restore; database backup procedure and recovery testing remain work for the operator. No region was chosen or service purchased.
