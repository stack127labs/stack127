## Cost examples — our calculation, estimated

These are **our calculation, estimated**, not a vendor quote or tested capacity claim. MIT self-hosted software has **0 license fee**; CheckCle does not assign a currency to that zero. All host prices retain DigitalOcean's original **$**, **vCPU** and **GiB** units. No conversion, promotional credit or assumed free email delivery is used.

### Chosen main-server setup

The quickstart publishes **minimum 1 vCPU / 500 MB RAM / 2 GB disk** and **production recommendation 2+ vCPUs / 2–4 GB RAM / 10 GB+ SSD**. Its claimed experience with **70+ checks, 15 servers and 60+ certificates** lacks an interval/retention/load-test recipe; it does not guarantee 100 targets, all regional workloads or a given cloud CPU.

Assumption: one **DigitalOcean Basic Regular bundled plan**, **2 vCPUs, 4 GiB RAM, 80 GiB SSD, 4,000 GiB transfer**, hosting the app and embedded PocketBase data together. Published monthly cap **$24.00**, listed hourly rate **$0.03571**. Assume a complete 30-day billing month within that monthly cap, not DigitalOcean's uncapped v5 option. The provider states per-second billing with minimum **60 seconds or $0.01, whichever is higher**.

Formula:

- Software-license fee = **0**.
- Selected main host = **$24.00/month**.
- Basic weekly system backup = $24.00 × 20% = **$4.80/month**.
- Known main-server + backup subtotal = **$28.80/month**.

This subtotal covers selected main infrastructure, using freshly researched host rates and CheckCle's broad hardware guidance. It excludes domain/DNS, SMTP and other notification-provider charges, remote agent nodes, extra disk/backup storage, excess transfer, taxes and maintenance labour. Host snapshots do not establish a database-consistent backup or tested restore. The app's v1 production-use warning still applies.
