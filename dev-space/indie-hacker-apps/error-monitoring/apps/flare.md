Flare provides hosted error tracking, logs and performance monitoring. Hobby costs $9/month for one project and user. A builder monitoring three apps needs at least Standard at $29/month. Errors, spans and log lines have separate monthly quotas; reaching a quota stops that signal. Default error spike protection also discards bursts before they exhaust the allowance.

The trial, plan-change and cancellation terms have material inconsistencies. The current offer says a 10-day no-card trial and immediate prorated plan changes, while the terms contain other trial lengths and next-period changes. The public DPA also needs to be read beside the terms' EU-processing claim. These differences remain visible below.

## Cost examples: our calculation, estimated

These are separate workloads, not a combined usage bill. All $ amounts retain the pricing page's symbol; the ISO currency was not established. Taxes, external Vonage SMS and any separately negotiated terms are excluded. No exchange rate is used. ### Error tracking

Inputs: one builder, three separately monitored app projects, 30 days of error detail, and 5000 / 50000 / 500000 accepted error occurrences per month. No paid replay or AI add-on is assumed. Repeats count individually; this is not the number of grouped issues.

Formula: choose the lowest published plan with at least three projects and enough monthly error occurrences. Subscription price is fixed within its allowance. An excess does not have a published per-event charge; collection stops until reset or upgrade.

| Busy: 500000 reports | Growth's 300000 is insufficient; Premium: 500000 ≤ 2000000; 1 × $279 | $279/month |
