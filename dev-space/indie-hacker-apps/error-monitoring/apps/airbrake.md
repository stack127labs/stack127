## Cost examples — our calculation, estimated

All rate sources checked 2026-11-04 UTC. Calculations preserve `$`, published units and the monthly term. Taxes are additional under https://www.airbrake.io/terms. No currency conversion is made.

### Errors

Inputs: one builder, three projects, 5,000 / 50,000 / 500,000 error occurrences per month; 30 days of detail; no paid replay or AI. Select Dev feature package ($0), a paid error tier, and free APM Tier 0 with no performance traffic. Projects/users fit the current pricing cards. Rates and allowances: https://docs.airbrake.io/docs/overview/packages_and_plans/; entry offers/projects: https://www.airbrake.io/pricing.

Formula: **feature-package cost + error-tier base + max(0, errors − included quota) ÷ 10,000 × tier's excess rate**. Comparison considers the relevant published tiers, with usage caps high enough to accept the submitted volume.

| Workload | Inputs and arithmetic | Result and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5,000 errors | Dev $0 + Tier 1 $19 + max(0, 5,000 − 25,000) ÷ 10,000 × $6.50 | $19/month, our calculation, estimated. Free error plan cannot be used for this comparison because current free detail/quotas are unresolved. |
| 50,000 errors | $0 + $19 + (50,000 − 25,000) ÷ 10,000 × $6.50 | $35.25/month if rates apply proportionally, our calculation, estimated. Fractional 10K billing-block treatment was not explicitly published. Tier 2's quota covers it at $59/month without excess. |
| 500,000 errors | $0 + Tier 3 $129 + (500,000 − 300,000) ÷ 10,000 × $3.65 | $202/month, our calculation, estimated. Tier 4 instead covers the count at $299/month. Tier 1 with excess would be $327.75 and Tier 2 $259, under the same proportional arithmetic. |

These are conditional recurring **volume** estimates. The plan-age windows cover at least 30 days, but extremely large groups lose roughly 25% of occurrence detail while still consuming paid quota: https://docs.airbrake.io/docs/faq/error-reduction-in-very-large-groups/. Therefore complete detail for every submitted occurrence is not guaranteed by these prices. Exact billing rounding, unusually large groups and configured intake caps can change fit. Error and APM excess are separate; a cap on one meter cannot substitute for a cap on the other.

Annual illustration only: if the advertised 10% discount applies exactly to the $19 base for a full year, **12 × $19 × 0.90 = $205.20/year**, equivalent to **$17.10/month**, our calculation, estimated. The site displays $17/month and docs say approximately 10%; this is not an exact quoted annual checkout total. No excess usage, taxes or paid package are included. Sources: https://www.airbrake.io/pricing and https://docs.airbrake.io/docs/overview/packages_and_plans/.

### Performance

Inputs: 1 million / 10 million / 50 million requests/month, 10% selected, two app services on two 1-vCPU hosts in total, 14-day traces. Selected request counts are **100,000 / 1,000,000 / 5,000,000**. The illustrative ten spans × 1KB do not determine this count-metered service's price, and no byte conversion is used.

Airbrake counts route, query and queue measurements, rather than publishing a generic distributed-span bill. Browser/server measurements across two services may produce more than one billable item per selected request. No host charge is stated in https://docs.airbrake.io/docs/faq/apm-faq/. The API uses aggregated counts/timings: https://docs.airbrake.io/docs/devops-tools/api/. The two hosts therefore do not supply a per-host add-on charge, nor do they establish trace storage.

For transparent arithmetic only, calculate the **APM component** with one counted route event per selected request, no queries/jobs/additional service records and Dev feature package $0. No paid error-tier base is included; whether a particular APM-only checkout requires another subscription was not verified. Docs rate formula is **APM base + max(0, counted events − included events) ÷ 100,000 × APM tier rate**.

| Route-only case | Tier 1 APM-component arithmetic using docs | Quota-covering APM alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100,000 events | $19 + $0 = $19/month | Tier 1 at $19/month includes 750K. |
| 1,000,000 events | $19 + (1,000,000 − 750,000) ÷ 100,000 × $0.19 = $19.475/month | Tier 2 at $59/month includes 3M. |
| 5,000,000 events | $19 + (5,000,000 − 750,000) ÷ 100,000 × $0.19 = $27.075/month | Tier 3 at $129/month includes 10M. |

Every result in that table is **our calculation, estimated and conditional**, for the APM component only, not a complete invoice. Values retain arithmetic precision rather than silently rounding. At five million events, Tier 2 plus its documented excess would be **$59 + 2,000,000 ÷ 100,000 × $0.15 = $62/month**, which is higher than the Tier 1 calculation. A larger quota is not automatically the cheapest plan if the unusually low documented excess rates apply.

However, https://www.airbrake.io/pricing displays **$5 per 10K on-demand performance events**, while https://docs.airbrake.io/docs/overview/packages_and_plans/ gives the per-100K rates above. The calculator's extracted default tier/quota text does not resolve that disagreement. Do not select the lower rate as the current payable rate. Free APM's seven-day history also fails 14 days. Paid performance retention is long enough for metrics, but raw trace retention and the requested sampling/span workload are not established. **An exact price for the full performance scenario remains unresolved.**
