### Monthly uptime comparison

Inputs: **5, 25 or 100 HTTP endpoints**, checks every 60 seconds, one fixed location, a 30-day month, email alerts and one public status page on the builder's own domain. Assume each endpoint consumes one website monitoring slot, consistent with adding one URL in the setup guide. No extra browser flows, SMS or third-party integration subscriptions are included.

Formula:

`scheduled checks per endpoint = 30 × 24 × 60 = 43,200`

`nominal scheduled checks = endpoint count × 43,200`

| Endpoints | Scheduled checks in 30 days | Lowest fitting published monthly plan | Estimated monitoring-only subtotal | Full comparable total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 5 × 43,200 = **216,000** | Business, up to 25 websites, one-minute checks | **USD 21/month** | **Unknown / incomplete.** One fixed location and an included own-domain status page are not established. |
| 25 | 25 × 43,200 = **1,080,000** | Business, up to 25 websites, one-minute checks | **USD 21/month** | **Unknown / incomplete**, for the same location/status-page limitations. |
| 100 | 100 × 43,200 = **4,320,000** | Agency, up to 100 websites, one-minute checks | **USD 75/month** | **Unknown / incomplete**, for the same location/status-page limitations. |

Startup's **USD 7/month** covers five websites but runs every **five minutes**, so it does not meet this comparison. Its own nominal 30-day workload at that interval would be `5 × 30 × 24 × 12 = 43,200` scheduled checks across all five URLs. It is not silently substituted for a 60-second workload.

The published price meter is website tier and interval, not requests. Email/webhook notifications are unlimited on these plans. There is no published per-check rate or retry overage amount to add. This does **not** settle what happens if a user adds websites above quota; enforcement, auto-upgrades and overage policy are not published.

These counts are schedules, not an exact count of HTTP network operations. The check first uses HEAD, can retry with GET, follows redirects and can perform additional failure-confirmation attempts. Exact fallback/failure frequency is not supplied by the workload. The detailed guide says three retries after an initial failure at 30-second intervals, while the feature page says “triple checks.” No physical-request total is guessed.

For a nominal **three-location** workload:

`endpoint count × 43,200 × 3`

The resulting scheduled run counts would be **648,000**, **3,240,000** and **12,960,000**. These are workload arithmetic only. The official sources do not establish selectable locations, number of locations per check or a three-location rate. **There is no exact native three-location estimate**; neither three copies of a plan nor a three-times price is assumed.

No WatchSumo own-domain status-page price or entitlement was found. Its footer's SumoStatus link leads to a separate product being prepared. An external status-page subscription cannot be priced as zero. Monitoring-history duration is also unpublished; a “last month” summary is not 30 days of individual response detail. The 30-day calculation period above is an input, not a retention guarantee.
