## Cost examples — our calculation, estimated

Published monthly tiers are used as steps. Do not interpolate a per-event rate. Formula: choose the smallest advertised tier whose monthly intake is at least the submitted error count; estimated volume-only bill = that tier's monthly price. ## Conflicts and unknowns

All sources in this section were checked 2026-11-04 UTC.

- **Hosted retention:** https://www.bugsink.com/dpa/ specifies a fixed 60-day raw-event period. https://www.bugsink.com/docs/ingestion-rate-limits-and-retention/ describes selective retention and possible multi-year retention without clearly separating every statement by deployment. https://www.bugsink.com/ publishes stored-event caps. Hosted DPA gives the relevant legal duration; the sources do not explain whether selective pruning can remove hosted detail earlier, or how all three descriptions fit together. The file records the conflict rather than treating a count cap as days.
- **Monthly quota outcome and cost controls:** checked https://www.bugsink.com/managed/, https://www.bugsink.com/docs/ingestion-rate-limits-and-retention/, https://www.bugsink.com/docs/settings/ and https://www.bugsink.com/register/75K/. Fixed pricing is advertised, but automatic upgrades, grace, usage warnings, monetary caps and exact managed rejection rules are not established. Self-host defaults are explicit and configurable; they cannot supply hosted defaults.
- **Billing and buyer terms:** checked current https://www.bugsink.com/terms-and-conditions/, https://www.bugsink.com/dpa/, https://www.bugsink.com/privacy-policy/, the managed page and both registration paths. Current terms cover self-host software and reference historic terms only for versions through 1.0.x. Annual totals, tax treatment, hosted cancellation/refunds, trial conversion and a complete eligible-country list remain unresolved. The free registration explicitly says no credit card; the paid trial does not publish equally clear card/renewal terms in the retrieved page.
- **Premium Support currency:** https://www.bugsink.com/ says $15/user/month; https://www.bugsink.com/register/premium-support/ says €15/user/month. Neither checked page explains a regional currency choice. Preserve both; no silent conversion or cheaper-price selection.
- **Native maturity:** https://github.com/bugsink/bugsink/releases/tag/2.6.2 says experimental; https://www.bugsink.com/blog/glitchtip-vs-sentry-vs-bugsink/ calls the same version production-ready. https://github.com/bugsink/bugsink/issues/82 confirms completed minidump work and continued feature opt-in. https://github.com/bugsink/bugsink/issues/20 gives a narrower Apple limitation. ## Field problems

- **C11/C14:** fixed retention days and complete raw-detail retention need to be distinguished. Selective count-based storage cannot be reduced to a days number, even where a hosted legal policy also gives a duration.
- **C06/C08:** a free main license, a source-available noncompete condition and a separately restricted ee directory are different facts. A single free/open-source boolean would mislead readers.
- **C12/C13:** operator-configurable defaults are useful for self-hosting, but do not prove hosted quotas, permissions or financial caps.
- **C16/E02:** accepting an SDK event does not guarantee native crash symbolication or source context. These need platform-specific exceptions. “Production-ready” and “experimental” remain conflicting vendor labels for 2.6.2.
- **H02:** throughput benchmark hardware is not a comparable hosting bill. There is no basis for a current exact total without storage, backup and provider assumptions.
- **C27/states:** fields.md uses unknown/derived names; this file uses only the six states requested by the owner. Calculations retain their estimate label. Nonmatching product-family fields are excluded rather than padded with not_applicable rows.
