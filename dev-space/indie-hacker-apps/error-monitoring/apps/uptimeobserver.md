### Scheduled jobs

**Required editorial inputs:** 5, 25 or 100 jobs; hourly runs; distinct start and success signals; 30-day month; email failure alerts. Required signal count = jobs × 24 × 30 × 2 = **jobs × 1,440 pings/month**, giving **7,200 / 36,000 / 144,000** pings.

Preserve both and direct an affected user to support; this review does not assume an upgrade is the intended fix. Generic addresses/distribution lists are documented for the email integration.
- **Broad marketing versus plan-specific features — resolved cautiously:** SSL/domain and status marketing pages use “Included in Every Plan.” The actual Free pricing icons cross out expiry and custom domains; plan-specific support docs say paid-only. Use those specific tables/guides, retaining the marketing caveat. Text-only feature lists alone would be misleading.

These are product limitations, not controlled test results.
- **Retention/export/deletion:** read plans, monitor/heartbeat/lifecycle, FAQ, MCP, privacy, terms and GDPR pages. No numeric operational-history retention, archival tier, complete raw export, export rate or backup-purge schedule found. MCP offers programmatic monitoring-data access without a complete export-tool/schema inventory. Policy portability/erasure is not an account backup format. - **Data terms:** GDPR page asserts no outside-EEA transfers; privacy allows protected jurisdiction transfers. Exact storage regions, choices, DPA and named subprocessors were not located in those policies/links. UK/US probe addresses are execution locations, not enough to infer where databases/backups reside. No blanket compliance certification or current no-transfer guarantee was assigned.
- **Subscriber and component entitlements:** product page advertises customer emails, but public prices do not commit subscriber numbers or extra rates. - **Job signals:** heartbeat guide documents interval/grace, first-ping Pending state and metadata storage. Lifecycle refers to cron schedules, but exact cron expression/timezone/DST controls are not described. No separate run-start signal/max-runtime/failure route or complete output retention is established. **success:false still refreshes health**; failure requires omitting the heartbeat.

- **Lifecycle/update source:** announcement/news archives and current support docs were read; no separate platform changelog discovered. No shutdown/acquisition notice found is not an operational or future-continuity guarantee.
