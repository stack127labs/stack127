## Cost examples

**Our calculations, estimated, not quotes.** Currency is EUR; published prices exclude VAT. Monthly figures below are consumption equivalents for exactly 30 days, not a monthly subscription or the cash amount charged each month. No conversion, trial credits, SMS, third-party subscription, extra confirmation allowance or labor is included.

For a pack costing `P` with `C` credits:
- `credit rate = P / C`.
- `consumption equivalent = credits actually consumed × P / C`.
- `cash for k whole packs = k × P`, with remaining credits carried as a balance.
- For the deliberately chosen smallest-pack purchase basis, `k = ceiling(required credits / 200,000)`. This is not claimed to minimize cash across mixed pack sizes.

The two displayed unit-price bases are the smallest listed purchase (€5/200,000) and the largest (€500/60,000,000). Intermediate packs are in the facts table. A cheap €500-pack consumption equivalent does **not** mean a customer can pay that fraction as an entry bill.
