## Cost examples — our calculation, estimated

Prices below retain the published $ symbol. An ISO dollar currency and tax treatment were not found. These are conditional subscription-base estimates, not completed checkout quotes. ### Errors

Inputs: one builder, three web apps on three domains, 5,000 / 50,000 / 500,000 reports per month, 30 days of error detail, no paid replay or AI extras. For this calculation only, assume one report consumes one displayed error unit and the apps fit the published unlimited-domain model. Backend service/project counting remains undefined.

Formula: choose the smallest displayed monthly tier with allowance at least the report count, then use that plan's tier price. The pricing code ties the error, replay and visitor labels to the same selected quota; it does not say these are one combined pool.

| 50,000 | 50,000 | $29 | $35 | Same plan retention at this selected tier. |
| 500,000 | 500,000 | $79 | $85 | Same plan retention at this selected tier. |

Indie's exact compliance with a fixed 30-day minimum is unclear because the vendor says one month.
