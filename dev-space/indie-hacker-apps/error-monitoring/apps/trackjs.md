### Required workload and the missing input

The directory fixes one builder, three apps, 30 days of error detail, and 5,000 / 50,000 / 500,000 reports per month, without paid replay or AI extras. TrackJS prices **page views**, so those error counts alone do not determine the subscription. A busy site can have few errors; a small site can have a noisy bug.

Business is the first published standard tier satisfying three separate applications and normal 30-day error retention. Starter has only one app and ten days; Professional has five apps but fifteen days. Keeping selected Starred/Shared examples does not give every report the required history.

For the estimates below, we add these **editorial assumptions**, not claims about typical usage:

- Browser page views are 100,000 for Small, 1,000,000 for Growing and 5,000,000 for Busy, counted as TrackJS agent initializations.
- All three apps share one Business starting subscription. Three production apps consume three of its ten listed application slots. No extra staging/test keys are assumed.
- No account minute receives more than 90 eligible errors, no browser agent sends more than ten in a second, and agent deduplication does not suppress a report we need to keep.
- Reports fit the under-100KB requirement, pass ignore/validation rules and reach the service. No installation or retained-count verification was performed.
- No custom higher traffic allowance, special sponsorship/discount, bespoke retention or optional paid feature is purchased. There is no published per-error charge to multiply into the bill.
