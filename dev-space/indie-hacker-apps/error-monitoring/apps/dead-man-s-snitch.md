## Current status and ownership

Dead Man's Snitch has current public hosted plans and a public status page. The status page showed all systems operational when checked. I found no current shutdown, sunset, or acquisition announcement in the product blog, status page, Terms, or Privacy pages. The latest post shown on the product blog was the Microsoft Teams integration update dated 2024-06-27; I found no separate newsroom or changelog page linked from the product site.

The current Terms name Dead Man's Snitch, LLC as the service provider and say its intellectual property belongs to Dead Man's Snitch and its licensors. The Privacy page also names Dead Man's Snitch, LLC, and its change log says it was updated to that entity on 2025-01-01. A 2013 Collective Idea post announced that company acquired the product. The FAQ still calls it a Collective Idea product, so that ownership statement appears stale. The LLC's parent or members are not named in the official pages checked.
