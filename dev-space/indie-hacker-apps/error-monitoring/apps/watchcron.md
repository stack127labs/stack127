## Current status and ownership

WatchCron's current homepage and pricing page advertise a permanent free plan and paid subscriptions. Their Start free and Start monitoring links advertise new customer intake; I did not submit a form or create an account. The homepage currently says all systems are operational, but the separate status endpoint at https://status.watchcron.com/ could not be fetched. I did not independently test monitoring, alerts, or uptime.

The Terms and Privacy Policy identify the operator as a private individual, but do not state the operator's country. I do not repeat the person's name. The Terms choose Ukrainian law; this does not establish the operator's home country.

I found no WatchCron acquisition, shutdown, or sunset notice in the official homepage, About, Terms, Privacy, and blog items checked. The newest relevant blog item I found is dated Oct 24, 2026. It reports that competitor Cronhub shut down June 30, 2026; it is not a WatchCron shutdown notice. The site's navigation links to a Blog described as news and longer reads. I found no separate newsroom or changelog link in the navigation checked.

This is not a thin-vendor case. The site exposes a pricing page, About page, Terms, Privacy, Refund Policy, a docs index, numerous monitor and feature pages, use-case pages, and a blog. ## Cost examples

All estimates below are **our calculations** from the public monthly prices and allowances, before taxes. Annual total is not calculated because the current plan page only shows the lower monthly equivalent billed yearly.

- **Scheduled jobs:** 1 job × 24 runs/day × 30 days × 2 signals (start and success) = 1,440 pings per check/month. Free allows 100, Starter 1,000, Pro 5,000. Pro is the first plan that fits. At 5, 25, or 100 externally run jobs, each scenario fits Pro's 250-check allowance: **$19/month**. Cloud Cron executions are not included in this scenario.
- **Uptime:** The monitor-count calculation is Free **$0/month** for 5 endpoints, Starter **$7/month** for 25, and Pro **$19/month** for 100. This assumes the 60-second setting is available; the pages do not publish an exact interval range. It excludes a customer-owned-domain status page, which WatchCron does not offer, so the full stated scenario is unsupported.
- **Status pages:** Free permits two projects, so 1 page / 100 subscribers and 1 page / 1,000 subscribers fit at **$0/month**. Three pages require at least Starter's five-project allowance: **$7/month**. Subscribers are unlimited. These are WatchCron-hosted public pages, not own-domain pages, so the full stated status-page scenario is unsupported.
