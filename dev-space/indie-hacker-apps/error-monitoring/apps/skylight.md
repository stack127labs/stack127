## Cost examples

All are **our calculations, estimated and not quoted**. The public calculator rate schedule uses USD. Formula: tier base price plus each whole million above the included level times that tier's marginal rate, rounded up by million. Our calculations: 1M requests selects S at $20; 10M selects M at $100 + 5 × $10 = $150; 50M selects XL at $370. Annual equivalents simply multiply by 12 and do not represent discounted annual plans. Annual payment is arranged by prepaid credits; no discount is published.

The common performance scenario's two services, two one-vCPU hosts and one builder do not change the published request meter. Request totals are treated as account-wide across owned apps. Skylight does not expose the requested 10% sampling control, so full monthly request counts are priced. Its 45-day retention exceeds the scenario's 14 days. Taxes and negotiated pricing above 1B requests/month are excluded. Logs and errors are not assigned costs because Skylight does not publish them as ingested product signals.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Agent license:** Tilde's support page calls its Ruby agent open source; the repository LICENSE.md grants limited use and verbatim copying but prohibits modification and most other exploitation. I leave the labeling conflict visible.
- **Collaborators:** Current account docs allow unlimited collaborators and GitHub access; Terms of Service last updated March 4, 2019 say users must not permit third parties to access or use the service. The legal text may be stale, but no public amendment resolving this was found.
- **Background jobs:** Worker guide documents Sidekiq, Delayed::Job and ActiveJob support for agent 5.0.1; FAQ still describes background jobs as an Insider beta. Public docs do not clarify whether all accounts have access.
- **Trial conversion:** Pricing and terms confirm a free month/no-card trial, but do not say whether trial accounts automatically convert or how the recurring free allowance applies afterward.
- **Data portability and deletion:** MCP provides selected aggregate reads, but I found no bulk export instructions. Terms permit backups to remain until ordinary deletion, without a deadline.
- **Data location:** I checked support/security information, FAQ, privacy statement and terms; they do not state a storage region or link a DPA.
- **News and lifecycle:** Skylight and Tilde newsroom routes returned 404; the official product blog and Tilde pages were checked instead. Skylight's guessed changelog route returned 404; I checked the Ruby-agent repository changelog. No shutdown, acquisition or sunset notice was found in available official pages checked.
- **Policy freshness:** Terms and privacy notice both show March 4, 2019 as last-updated/effective date. Newer product claims are recorded separately rather than treated as edits to those legal documents.

## Field problems

- Skylight appears in this error-monitoring directory as an APM product, not an error tracker. Its current FAQ explicitly distinguishes it from error-tracking software.
- The field list asks for sampling in the APM group, but Skylight describes request aggregation rather than percentage sampling. I recorded the all-request and aggregation behavior in A02 and the scenario caveat.
- Pricing renders its rate table in the browser. The public /plans/default data endpoint supplied the five tier rates and thresholds; it was read without an account or form submission.
- Trial-to-paid behavior and card requirement for continuing on the recurring free allowance are not fully described publicly.
- Some support pages appear old and internally inconsistent with other current product and legal pages. I kept the conflicts instead of treating a help page as a formal terms amendment.
