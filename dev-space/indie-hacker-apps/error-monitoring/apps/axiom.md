## Cost examples: our calculation, estimated

Rate sources, all checked 2026-11-04: https://axiom.co/pricing and https://axiom.co/pricing/llms.txt. Byte definition: https://axiom.co/docs/reference/optimize-usage. Plan counts: https://axiom.co/docs/reference/limits. Retention control: https://axiom.co/docs/reference/datasets.

Choose one builder, no add-ons, no purchased compute commitments, no separate metric dataset, no external alert delivery charges, no tax. Estimate each family independently, not their sum. Assume decimal GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes for workload arithmetic. Assume all retained data remains Frequent Access; no cold-tier discount. Query allowances include manual, dashboard, API and monitoring queries. Actual query compute is memory allocated × runtime, not simply bytes scanned.

For these sub-tier Cloud workloads, use:

`monthly USD = 25 + 0.12 × max(0, ingest_GB − 1,000) + 0.20 × max(0, query_GB_hours − 100) + 0.03 × max(0, compressed_retained_GB − 100)`

This formula applies only while data loading and query usage remain in their first paid tiers. Personal has no paid-overage assumption: USD 0 only if loading <=500 GB/month, queries <=10 GB-hours/month, compressed storage <=25 GB, retention <=30 days, and other limits fit.

### Logs

Required inputs: 5 / 50 / 250 raw GB/month, 30-day searchable retention, one builder, no archive. To make the unspecified query and compression inputs explicit, assume **10 GB-hours of total monthly query compute** in every scenario and **95% compression**, meaning stored bytes are 5% of uncompressed input. This is an estimate assumption, not a compression guarantee. Assume steady uniform ingestion, a 30-day month and a 30-day retention period, so retained storage equals one month's compressed loading.

| Scenario | Uncompressed loading | Storage formula/result | Query compute | Personal result | Cloud formula/result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small | 5 GB/month | 5 × 0.05 = 0.25 GB | 10 GB-hours/month | Estimated USD 0/month | 25 + 0 + 0 + 0 = USD 25/month |
| Growing | 50 GB/month | 50 × 0.05 = 2.5 GB | 10 GB-hours/month | Estimated USD 0/month | 25 + 0 + 0 + 0 = USD 25/month |
| Busy | 250 GB/month | 250 × 0.05 = 12.5 GB | 10 GB-hours/month | Estimated USD 0/month | 25 + 0 + 0 + 0 = USD 25/month |

Personal costs are conditional on free commercial eligibility and actual compute/storage fit. At busy usage, 10% stored size yields 25 GB, exactly the allowance; worse compression exceeds it. If total query compute rises to 250 GB-hours/month, Personal no longer fits; Cloud estimate is `25 + (250−100) × 0.20 = USD 55/month`, holding all other inputs as above. This is why raw log volume alone is not enough to publish a universal free-price claim.

### Performance

Required inputs: 1 million / 10 million / 50 million requests/month; two app services on two 1-vCPU hosts; one builder; 10% sampling; 14-day retention; ten spans per sampled request. Assume **1 KB = 1,000 bytes of metered uncompressed data per span**, including its share of serialization overhead. This interpretation is deliberate: a 1 KB application span before export could become a larger metered event. Assume 95% compression and total query compute of 10 GB-hours/month. SDK/OTel instrumentation is the chosen setup; the hosts are workload context, not a priced Axiom host unit.

| Scenario | Raw byte formula | Loading | Steady retained-storage formula | Personal result | Cloud result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small | 1,000,000 × 0.10 × 10 × 1,000 | 1 GB/month | 1 × 0.05 × 14/30 = 7/300 GB | Estimated USD 0/month | 25 + 0 + 0 + 0 = USD 25/month |
| Growing | 10,000,000 × 0.10 × 10 × 1,000 | 10 GB/month | 10 × 0.05 × 14/30 = 7/30 GB | Estimated USD 0/month | 25 + 0 + 0 + 0 = USD 25/month |
| Busy | 50,000,000 × 0.10 × 10 × 1,000 | 50 GB/month | 50 × 0.05 × 14/30 = 7/6 GB | Estimated USD 0/month | 25 + 0 + 0 + 0 = USD 25/month |

Two services can share a trace dataset with `service.name`; this avoids inventing a per-service license charge. This is not a guarantee that instrumentation has no CPU/network cost. Whole-application profiling, extra logs, derived metrics and external hosting bills are excluded. Exact costs stay unknown until exported span size, query usage and compression are measured. Personal commercial eligibility remains unclear.

### Error-report workload and other families

For 5,000 / 50,000 / 500,000 error reports/month, **cost unknown**. Axiom has no established published per-exception meter or complete issue-tracker feature set. A formula would need `report count × serialized bytes/report`, compressed retained size and query compute; report size was not specified in fields.md. Three apps can fit three Personal datasets, but that alone does not price their reports. Do not substitute log/trace cost for dedicated error-tracker functionality.

Uptime, browser checks, status pages and heartbeat-job costs are not applicable to the researched product offer. No matching dedicated product was established. A monitor querying error/no-data events is not proof of an external probe, browser runner, subscriber status page, or start/end job protocol. No hosting-cost estimate was calculated for BYOC because sizing and annual platform price are unpublished in checked sources.

## Conflicts and unknowns

All sources in this section checked 2026-11-04.

- **C23 region eligibility:** https://axiom.co/docs/reference/limits lists US for both plans. https://axiom.co/docs/reference/edge-deployments lists US East and EU Central, with extra edges available via add-ons. Event data and query execution stay at selected edge; account operations stay in US. Record EU capability, leave exact Personal/Cloud eligibility unresolved. Multi-edge add-on fee not found in checked pricing.
- **C15 users:** https://axiom.co/docs/get-help/faq calls Cloud users unlimited, while https://axiom.co/docs/reference/limits specifies a 1,000-user soft cap. Use the numeric limit with the published ability to request an increase, not an unconditional unlimited label.
- **C14/L03 access-tier timing:** https://axiom.co/docs/reference/optimize-usage moves data after 30 and 90 consecutive unqueried days. https://axiom.co/pricing/llms.txt gives age bands of 0 to 60, 60 to 120, and 120+ days, while its prose uses a typical-query-window model. The assumptions are not fully reconciled. Our 14/30-day estimates use Frequent Access only.
- **C11/L03 storage allowance example:** rate card https://axiom.co/pricing/llms.txt says first 100 GB of compressed storage is free, but its 12-month example subtracts 100 GB from each month's compressed ingestion before multiplying by retention. That differs from subtracting one 100 GB allowance from the full retained inventory. Do not extend that example to exact long-retention totals without clarification. Our sample footprints are below the allowance under either interpretation.
- **Query usage:** rate card describes GB-hours as roughly a scanned-GB analogy. The exact definition at https://axiom.co/docs/reference/optimize-usage is allocated memory multiplied by execution time. Do not calculate a bill from scanned GB alone.
