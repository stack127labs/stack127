## Cost examples

**Our calculation, estimated.** Checked **2026-11-05**. Preserve the published **$** symbol; no currency conversion or undocumented tax is included. Estimates assume steady workload, one primary account, no other active checks, no trial capacity, one-minute intervals unless stated and no paid replay/diagnostic add-on guessed.

Monthly monitor cost for peak-active count N:

- **Personal:** $10 + max(0, N − 5) × $0.50.
- **Professional:** $25 + max(0, N − 200) × $0.40.
- **Premiere:** $80 + max(0, N − 200) × $0.25.

Source: https://nodeping.com/pricing.html . Base subscription is prepaid; monitor overage and sub-minute peak fees appear on the **following invoice**. These are recurring workload amounts, not necessarily the first bill. Only enabled checks count, but disabling later does not erase an earlier peak. No peak beyond the stated workload is assumed. Sources: https://nodeping.com/faq.html ; https://nodeping.com/terms.html .
