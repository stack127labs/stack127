## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Free versus trial:** Free Forever with monthly allowances is on the current plan table and the September 2026 release. The same pricing page's FAQ and an August 2026 Marketplace post still describe a 14-day unlimited-ingestion trial. Whether a trial remains separate, and whether it converts to PAYG or Free Forever, is not stated.
- **Billing basis:** Pricing calculator calls the data volume uncompressed logs/metrics/traces. Its FAQ says daily measurements use data actually stored, excluding pipeline drops. This may describe the same post-filter volume at an uncompressed rate, but the public wording does not fully define the calculation.
- **Regions:** Hosting FAQ says hosted services default to AWS/Azure in the US; GDPR page says personal data may be stored in AWS in the US, Europe, Canada and India. These pages do not map regions to telemetry products/accounts. Confirm the region in the order or BYOC terms.

Its software license and complete price are not public. Upgrade, backup, and restore runbooks were not found on the on-prem page, prerequisites, release notes or security page.

- **Synthetic/browser billing:** Free allowances and unit rates are published. The checked docs do not define rounding for partial 5,000-check or 1,000-browser-run units; usage estimates are rate-based calculations, not exact invoices.
- **No-shutdown search:** Checked the official blog, product-updates archive, press-release/news page, terms and changelog through the latest listed 2026-11-04 release. No shutdown, sunset or acquisition notice was listed. This does not establish private ownership history.
