## Cost examples

All examples are **our calculation, estimated**, not quotes. Original currency is **USD**. Terms explicitly exclude applicable taxes. Every scenario has **one builder**. “Free” means the project's seat/base subscription; paid monitors and telemetry require a payment method even on Free. Email delivery is advertised free; SMS/WhatsApp/voice, AI, replay and supplier costs are excluded unless named. No trial credit or currency conversion is assumed.

### Uptime: 5, 25 and 100 endpoints

Inputs: 60-second HTTP checks, one location, 30 days, email alerts and one own-domain status page. Published Free features include the page, custom domain/SSL and email alerts; active monitors are **$1 each/month**.

`nominal checks/endpoint = 30 × 24 × 60 = 43,200`

`estimated monitor component = endpoints × $1 + $0 Free base`

| Endpoints | Nominal one-location checks/month | Estimated recurring monitor/base component |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 5 | 5 × 43,200 = **216,000** | **$5/month** |
| 25 | 25 × 43,200 = **1,080,000** | **$25/month** |
| 100 | 100 × 43,200 = **4,320,000** | **$100/month** |

These exclude the external domain purchase and tax. No explicit numeric public page-resource ceiling was found, so the common-page fit is not an installed/tested result. Advanced on-call/API/secret features may add a paid seat tier. A turned-off non-Manual monitor remains billable; do not treat pausing it as deleting its charge.

For three configured probes, nominal checks are **648,000**, **3,240,000** and **12,960,000**. Billing docs price the monitor rather than each check/probe, and no additional public per-probe rate was found, so the published monitor component remains **$5/$25/$100**. Website retries count **after the first attempt**: default usually 3 retries means up to **4 attempts** for eligible errors, with one-second waits and full timeout per attempt.
