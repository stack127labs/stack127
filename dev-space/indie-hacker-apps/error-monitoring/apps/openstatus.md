### Uptime with one common status page

Inputs: **5, 25 or 100 HTTP endpoints**, 60-second checks, one location, 30 days, one builder, email alerts and one own-domain page. For a useful common-page comparison, **every endpoint is represented by its own component**. Hosted email is a documented recipient-address channel; no customer SMTP subscription is specified as a prerequisite.

`nominal runs per endpoint = 30 × 24 × 60 = 43,200`

`monitor packs = ceil(max(0, endpoints − included monitors) ÷ 10)`

`subscription subtotal = base monthly price + monitor packs × 15`

| 5 | 5 × 43,200 = **216,000** | Starter: 20 monitors / 20 components / 1 page | **30 USD** |

| 100 | 100 × 43,200 = **4,320,000** | Scale: 50 monitors / 500 components, plus 5 ten-monitor packs | 500 + 5 × 15 = **575 USD** |

Monitor packs do not increase the component allowance. For 25 endpoints, Starter plus one pack costs **45/month** but still has only 20 components, so it does not fit the page held constant. For 100 endpoints, Starter plus eight packs costs **150/month** but retains the same page constraint; Pro plus five packs costs **175/month** but only has 50 components. Those are monitor-capacity subtotals, not equal alternatives to the all-endpoint common-page workload.
