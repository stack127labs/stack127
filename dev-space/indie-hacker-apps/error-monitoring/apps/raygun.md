## Cost examples — our calculation, estimated

All monetary results below are **our calculation, estimated**, in USD before taxes. These are recurring list-price configurations, not quotes, verified checkout totals or claims to be the cheapest possible reservation. Rate sources, checked 2026-11-04: https://raygun.com/pricing and https://raygun.com/documentation/accounts-billing/billing/. Sampling source: https://raygun.com/documentation/product-guides/apm/apm-settings/sampling/.

The common formula is:

Monthly total = chosen monthly base price + max(0, billed events − reserved events) × that product's on-demand rate.

No currency conversion, annual discount, bonus-product offset, replay/AI extra, SAML add-on, usage cap, SMS/call service or retention extension is silently included. Standard plans have unlimited apps and members. Filtering, spike protection and an enabled cap can reduce accepted data, so an invoice calculation is not a guarantee every submitted event is retained. The estimates assume the stated events pass validation and are accepted.

### Errors

Inputs: one builder, three applications, 5,000 / 50,000 / 500,000 accepted error instances per month, at least 30 days of error detail, no paid replay or AI extras. All named hosted plans have 180-day error retention, so no retention purchase is needed.

| Workload | Chosen monthly plan | Included allowance | Formula, our calculation | Estimated result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small: 5,000 errors/month | Crash Reporting Basic | 100,000 errors/month | 60 + max(0, 5,000 − 100,000) × 0.001 | USD 60/month |
| Growing: 50,000 errors/month | Crash Reporting Basic | 100,000 errors/month | 60 + max(0, 50,000 − 100,000) × 0.001 | USD 60/month |
| Busy: 500,000 errors/month | Crash Reporting Team, uncapped | 200,000 errors/month | 120 + (500,000 − 200,000) × 0.001 = 120 + 300 | USD 420/month |

For context, the same 500,000 accepted errors on unmodified Basic would cost 60 + 400,000 × 0.001 = **USD 460/month**, while Business's 1,000,000 included errors costs **USD 600/month**. These comparisons only cover the published base reservations. An extra reserved block can change the best choice; its exact price was not established.

A Team hard-cap example is 120 + 40 = **USD 160/month**, but it stops at 200,000 reserved errors. It therefore **fails the 500,000-error completeness requirement**. Adding a cap does not buy the missing 300,000 events. Basic plus the USD 40 cap is not an evidenced offer.

Assume a supported APM runtime/host and one billed request trace per sampled request across the two services. The standard 30-day trace retention meets the requested window.

- 1,000,000 × 0.10 = 100,000 traces/month.
- 10,000,000 × 0.10 = 1,000,000 traces/month.
- 50,000,000 × 0.10 = 5,000,000 traces/month.
