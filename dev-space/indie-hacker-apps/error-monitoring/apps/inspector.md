## Product and price boundary

This record covers Inspector's hosted application monitoring service: instrumented requests, jobs and commands; exceptions; transaction and segment performance; notification channels; and the related REST API. It does not treat the Neuron AI framework as a separate Inspector monitoring offer. Inspector's client libraries run with the customer's application; they do not provide a self-hosted Inspector backend.

Prices on the current pricing page are in **EUR**. Monthly and annual prices are published. The page says annual billing gives two months free. Transaction quotas reset monthly. Costs below use the first listed plan that covers the example's monthly transaction volume; they do not convert currency or include overage.
