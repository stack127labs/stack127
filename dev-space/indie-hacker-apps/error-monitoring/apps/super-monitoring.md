## Field problems

- **C11 requires explicit unsupported conditions.** Five endpoints fit a quoted plan count, but strict one-location selection is not established. Twenty-five/hundred endpoints exceed public tiers. Five-minute full transactions are unsupported. Unknown totals remain unknown beside known bundle prices.
