## Cost examples: our calculation, estimated, not quoted

Use monthly billing first, one builder, no paid AI/governance or Audit Trail, no SMS/phone delivery, no log archiving, and no currency conversion. Amounts retain the source's **$** symbol. An explicit product currency code and complete tax treatment were not established from the checked page text.

### Logs: 5, 50 and 250 GB/month

Required retention is **30 searchable days**. Basic's seven days and Standard's seven/fifteen days do not satisfy it. Public Pro rows give:
- 1 GB/day, 30 days: stored $4.90/GB, listed capacity price $5.00/day.
- 5 GB/day, 30 days: stored $3.40/GB, listed capacity price $17.50/day.
- Received raw logs: $0.10/GB, separately described by the usage guide.

Assume a 30-day month, uniform daily ingestion, one Logs App, no transformation-induced size change, and **received GB = stored GB**. These are our workload assumptions. GB means the vendor's billable data unit, not compressed archive GB; no silent raw/compressed or decimal/binary conversion.
