## Product and editions

Elastic Observability is the logs, metrics, application performance, and user-experience solution built on Elasticsearch and Kibana. Elastic sells two hosted choices: **Elastic Cloud Hosted**, where the customer chooses and pays for deployment resources; and **Elastic Cloud Serverless**, where Logs Essentials or Complete features are priced by usage. Elastic also offers the **self-managed Elastic Stack**, with a free Basic subscription and paid self-managed subscription tiers. The Basic tier, the default Elastic License 2.0 distribution, the optional source-code licenses, and paid commercial subscription entitlements are distinct. Hosted services do not grant the customer a self-managed software license. [product overview](https://www.elastic.co/docs/solutions/observability) · [deployment and pricing comparison](https://www.elastic.co/pricing/) · [serverless feature comparison](https://www.elastic.co/docs/solutions/observability/observability-serverless-feature-tiers) · [self-managed subscription comparison](https://www.elastic.co/subscriptions)

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Hosted Standard generally lists APM, but the 2026 feature-matrix footnote excludes APM when Standard is purchased through AWS Marketplace. Both claims are retained under C26; the Marketplace restriction is specific to that purchase route.
- The product page states 450+ integrations while current docs state over 400. No exact count is used.
- Marketing's “as low as” Serverless rates are below the first-band rates shown in the live detailed table. Cost examples use the displayed first band for AWS us-east-1, not the volume-discount minimum.
- An exact Cloud Hosted bill is not calculable from data volume alone. The checked Hosted pricing, usage calculator guidance and billing pages require resource configuration and region inputs.
- A public per-seat/user maximum, domain-expiry check, APM error-fix regression workflow, universal check interval, session replay and self-managed paid list price were not published in the pages named in their respective rows.
- No separate public customer status-page product or scheduled-job heartbeat product was identified; the vendor's own Cloud status page and Heartbeat network checks are described separately.

## Field problems

- **C06:** “license” can refer to default distribution, source-file choices, Basic subscription or paid support/feature entitlements. Those are split rather than collapsed into one label.
- **C07/C11:** Cloud Hosted is resource-based, while Serverless is usage-based and self-managed license pricing is quote-only. The cost calculations are only comparable Serverless log scenarios.
- **C14:** rollover is not retention; Serverless defaults to indefinite storage unless the user sets lifecycle deletion.
- **Uptime:** the old Uptime app is deprecated; current synthetic monitoring lives in Synthetics. Keep version and deployment caveats with the old app.
- **S01–S03 and J01–J02:** these are not separate customer status-page or scheduled-job-monitor offers. Vendor status communications and ordinary telemetry are not substitutes for those products.
