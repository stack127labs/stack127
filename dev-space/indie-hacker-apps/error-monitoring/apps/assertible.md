**Inputs:** one builder; 5, 25 or 100 simple HTTP endpoint tests; every 60 seconds; one requested location; a 30-day month; email failure alerts; one own-domain status page. One main request per test, no auth/setup/teardown/link-check requests, redirects or extra confirmation requests in the baseline. No trial credit is included. ## Field problems

- **C11:** endpoint count alone does not determine Assertible's web-service count.
