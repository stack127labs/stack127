## Cost examples — our calculation

All figures below are **our calculation, estimated, not quoted**. Prices keep the **$ symbol exactly as published**. The checked pricing and billing terms do not explicitly establish the invoice's ISO currency code, so we do not convert or silently label them USD.

The advertised Cloud rate is **$0.39 per GB ingested**. CSA sections 1.4–1.5 and 5 describe a monthly billing cycle, rounding ingestion to the nearest **0.1 GB**, invoicing monthly in arrears and taxes charged separately. Section 4.1 says charging is solely for ingested GB, while its definitions and section 5 also refer to Order-defined Usage Metrics. No separately verified current public query-scan/export tariff or minimum invoice was established. These are rate-card ingestion estimates, conditional on that standard rate applying to the account.
