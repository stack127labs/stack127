## Cost examples

All examples are **our calculation, estimated**, not quotes. Currency stays **EUR**. Published prices **exclude VAT**, so totals below are before applicable VAT. No exchange rate, trial credits, Vonage delivery rate or unlisted overage is invented.

### Uptime at the required 60-second interval

Inputs: **5, 25 or 100 HTTP/HTTPS URLs**, one builder, checks every 60 seconds, one fixed location, a 30-day month, email alerts and one own-domain public status page. Each endpoint is assumed to consume one website/monitor slot, consistent with the URL-based resource.

Formula:

`nominal scheduled runs per URL = 30 days × 24 hours × 60 checks/hour = 43,200`

`nominal scheduled runs = URL count × 43,200`

| URLs | Nominal scheduled runs/month | Published fitting plan | Estimated native bundle subtotal, before VAT | Fixed-location comparability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 5 × 43,200 = **216,000** | Starter, 5 websites | **EUR 6/month** | Exact one-fixed-location operation is not documented. |
| 25 | 25 × 43,200 = **1,080,000** | Growth, 25 websites | **EUR 30/month** | Exact one-fixed-location operation is not documented. |
| 100 | 100 × 43,200 = **4,320,000** | Scale, 100 websites | **EUR 90/month** | Exact one-fixed-location operation is not documented. |

The API explicitly allows a **60-second** interval on the check; all paid sizes offer a minimum of 30 seconds. Slowing the interval to one minute does not change a published website-capacity price. The plan includes the API, unlimited team members, email integration and one status page per monitored website with own-domain support. One own-domain single-site page therefore does not require a separate published page subscription.

These are **native bundle subtotals**, not an exact equivalent to the specified fixed-origin workload. The product describes global origins and a second-location verification after failure. No user region selector, fixed-one-probe contract or normal origin-rotation schedule is published in the checked API/settings docs.
