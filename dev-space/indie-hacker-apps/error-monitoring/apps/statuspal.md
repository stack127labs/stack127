StatusPal is a hosted status-page and incident-communication service with URL monitoring. The two live editions need separate limits: Classic is the established product, while Next is the newer product with separate Free and Pro offers. The company’s current service page lists both editions’ services as operational. The Next pricing headline says “Unlimited pages,” but the plan details list one Free page and five Pro pages; this record uses the explicit plan details and flags the mismatch.
