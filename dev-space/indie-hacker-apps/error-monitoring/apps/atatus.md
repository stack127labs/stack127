## Status and scope

Atatus is an active, broad observability service under the NamLabs Technologies Pvt Ltd name shown in its site footer. Its current pricing, product, documentation and 2026 What's New pages remain live. I found no acquisition, shutdown or sunset notice in the company blog, What's New page, or searches of the official site; the attempted `/newsroom/` URL did not load, so that path could not be checked. Its offers include hosted and private on-premises/BYOC deployments. Error monitoring is one feature alongside APM, logs, browser/mobile RUM, infrastructure and synthetic uptime checks.

The `apps.md` row says “hosted + on-premises option.” Official pages substantiate on-premises, private cloud, air-gapped and BYOC deployment for the broader platform, including error tracking. The on-premises offer is enterprise-sales-led. Its comparison table says fixed annual contract, unlimited users and unlimited retention, but does not publish a price or minimum requirements.
