## Product and pricing boundary

CloudWatch is AWS's hosted monitoring service for metrics, alarms, dashboards, logs, traces, application performance, synthetics and real-user monitoring. It also supports on-premises and other-cloud sources through agents and OpenTelemetry. The control plane and stored CloudWatch data remain AWS services. The CloudWatch Agent and AWS Distro for OpenTelemetry are optional customer-run collection software, not a self-hosted CloudWatch service.

CloudWatch prices are metered across separate functions, including log ingestion, storage and queries; custom metrics; alarms; Synthetics; RUM; Application Signals; and X-Ray traces. AWS rates vary by Region. Unless a row says otherwise, the example rates and calculations below use **US East (N. Virginia), us-east-1**, the region named in AWS pricing examples. They are not a quote for another region or for an entire AWS application. EC2, Lambda, S3, SNS, Data Firehose, data transfer, paid support, and other AWS products keep their own charges. [CloudWatch overview](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonCloudWatch/latest/monitoring/WhatIsCloudWatch.html) · [CloudWatch pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/cloudwatch/pricing/) · [AWS regions and endpoints](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/general/latest/gr/cw_region.html)

## Cost examples

All prices below are **our calculations**, not AWS quotes. Region for each example: **US East (N. Virginia), us-east-1**. AWS says CloudWatch prices vary by Region.

### Logs: standard-class ingestion only

Assumptions: monthly log ingestion is 5, 50 or 250 GB; first 5 GB of log ingestion are free; the first paid Standard rate is $0.50/GB. Formula: max(monthly GB − 5 GB, 0) × $0.50. Excludes compressed storage, Logs Insights scanning, vended-log rates, data protection, delivery/export, and other AWS services.

| Monthly log volume | Formula | Our calculation |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Small: 5 GB | max(5 − 5, 0) × $0.50 | $0/month |
| Growing: 50 GB | max(50 − 5, 0) × $0.50 | $22.50/month |
| Busy: 250 GB | max(250 − 5, 0) × $0.50 | $122.50/month |

### Logs: Essentials-class ingestion only

Same inputs and free allowance. Formula: max(monthly GB − 5 GB, 0) × $0.25. Essentials is the lower-ingest class with a reduced feature set; storage and Logs Insights query charges remain the same as Standard.

| Monthly log volume | Formula | Our calculation |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Small: 5 GB | max(5 − 5, 0) × $0.25 | $0/month |
| Growing: 50 GB | max(50 − 5, 0) × $0.25 | $11.25/month |

### RUM and Synthetics examples

After any trial, the pricing page's N. Virginia RUM example rate of $1 per 100,000 web events gives: 100,000 events = $1; 1,000,000 = $10; 10,000,000 = $100. The first-time free trial is 1 million web events/account and is not assumed in these ongoing figures.

For Synthetics, at the N. Virginia example rate of $0.0012 per run, 100 runs = $0 of canary-run fees after the monthly 100-run allowance; 1,000 runs = (1,000 − 100) × $0.0012 = **$1.08**; 10,000 runs = (10,000 − 100) × $0.0012 = **$11.88**. These are canary-run fees only. Lambda execution, CloudWatch Logs, S3 artifacts and custom metrics can add charges.

### CloudWatch Omni separately

This is a separate Omni analysis example, not a standard CloudWatch console/dashboard quote. Region: **US East (N. Virginia)**, the Omni pricing page region. Assumptions: 10 GB of log ingestion at $0.50/GB, 2 GB of spans at $0.35/GB, 1 GB of OTel metric ingestion at $0.50/GB, 30-day storage for logs/spans, 15% compressed storage ratio as used in AWS’s worked example, 70 GB of log/span scans, and no trial credits or remaining Always Free usage. Formula: (10 × $0.50) + (2 × $0.35) + (1 × $0.50) + (12 × 0.15 × $0.03) + max(70 − 5 × (10 + 2), 0) × $0.005 = $6.304, or **$6.30/month** after rounding to cents. This excludes AgentCore evaluations and usage allowances/credits that may apply to an eligible account. [Omni pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/cloudwatch/omni/pricing/)

### Application Signals and traces

There is no honest traffic-only total without the signal counts created by inbound requests, outbound dependencies, SLO metric periods, transaction-search indexing, query volume and X-Ray sampling. AWS's pricing page publishes those meter formulas and worked examples; estimate each meter for the app's actual request topology and select the customer's Region in AWS Pricing Calculator.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Evidently retirement date:** AWS's blog says October 17, 2025, while the guide history says October 16, 2025. This is a historical date conflict for the retired Evidently feature only.
- **Free Tier:** AWS account credits and account-plan closure are separate from CloudWatch Always Free meters. Accounts created before and after 2025-07-15 have different Free Tier programs.
- **Regional pricing:** The worked figures are N. Virginia examples. No price is generalized to another Region.
- **CloudWatch total cost:** Metrics, alarms, logs, APM, traces, RUM and Synthetics use different meters. AWS service charges for Lambda, S3, SNS, data transfer and other products are excluded.
- **Log class naming:** Current docs use Essentials; older pages still say Infrequent Access. AWS documents this as a rename with unchanged features/pricing, not a price conflict.
- **No unified project/seats plan:** CloudWatch uses AWS accounts, IAM identities, resource quotas and account billing, not a public seat-based application plan.

## Field problems

- **C07/C11:** “CloudWatch price” is not one unit price. Logs examples are intentionally limited to ingestion; storage and query costs need more workload detail. Synthetics/RUM/APM use separate units and are not bundled together.
- **C08/C09:** The CloudWatch Always Free metering allowances, time-limited feature trials and AWS new-account credits must be kept separate. New AWS Free plan accounts may close after six months or credit exhaustion unless upgraded.
- **C13:** Budget alerts do not prove a hard cap on CloudWatch usage. No CloudWatch-specific billing stop was found in the reviewed pricing and billing pages.
- **C14/C21:** Retention and deletion differ by logs, metric periods, RUM events, canary results and S3 copies. Expiry marking can precede physical deletion.
- **C06/H01–H03:** The Agent and ADOT Collector are licensed collection code; they do not make the managed CloudWatch backend open source or self-hostable.
- **E01–E04:** Error signals and replay exist, but the product does not use a grouped exception-issue lifecycle as a tracker does.
- **U05:** Checked the product overview, pricing and canary guide. Native TLS/domain-expiry warnings are not documented; custom canary scripting is not counted as a built-in feature.
- **S01–S03 and J01–J02:** No customer-owned status-page tool or dedicated scheduled-job heartbeat product was found in the CloudWatch product/pricing scope.
- No apps.md row error found. The research corrects the row's broad “uptime” wording by identifying it as Synthetics canaries. apps.md was not changed.
