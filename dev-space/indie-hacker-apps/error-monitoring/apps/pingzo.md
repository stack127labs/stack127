## Field problems

- **C11:** A subscription amount can be calculated while the full workload is unsupported or unverified. Keep the monitor/job allowance assumptions and missing location, start-event or subscriber conditions beside every amount.
- **C15/C18:** Administrative seats, on-call responders, recipients and chat members are different scopes. “Unlimited responders” does not define every workspace permission or seat.
- **U03/C23:** Probe geography, regional marketing, storage residency and billing currencies are different facts. A local node or GDPR claim does not identify database location.
- **U04:** “Three strikes” is weaker than the published 3-of-5/30-minute algorithm. Do not substitute consecutive failures, region quorum or instantaneous detection for the actual wording.
- **S02:** Operator alerts and status-page subscriber delivery use different audiences. A WhatsApp quota cannot stand in for subscriber email allowance.
- **A03/C16/C20:** RUM visit limits, heartbeat SDKs and custom failure strings must not be expanded into trace sampling, native crash SDKs, grouped errors or full browser transactions.
- **C27:** A newer live legal page supersedes stale search-cache text. A recovered malformed HTML extraction does not count as check_failed. A public pricing-region response is evidence for the returned regional prices only.
