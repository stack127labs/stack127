## Cost examples: our calculation, estimated

Use monthly billing, one builder and the supplied workloads. Each family is calculated independently against one organization's shared pool. No taxes, debug/release uploads, competing signals or external notification/provider charges are included. These are not vendor quotes or tested service configurations.

Rate sources, checked 2026-11-04: [plans](https://glitchtip.com/pricing/) and [on-demand billing](https://glitchtip.com/documentation/on-demand-billing/). They publish `$`; a subscription currency code was not separately established. The published SaaS bands are used despite the version/deployment caveats below.

For a plan with monthly base `B`, included events `Q`, and total billed events `E`, let `x = max(0, E − Q)`:

`estimated cost = B + min(x, 1,000,000) × 0.00017 + min(max(x − 1,000,000, 0), 9,000,000) × 0.00015 + max(x − 10,000,000, 0) × 0.00012`.

This requires enabled on-demand billing and a high enough cap. Prices are per event, so no rounding to whole 1,000-event blocks is assumed. Cap exhaustion restores throttling and therefore does not deliver the complete requested data. Annual totals are omitted because the annual-toggle prices were not retrievable.

### Error tracking

One builder, three apps, 30-day detail. Paid unlimited projects/users and 90-day advertised event retention fit. Each occurrence is one event. Other signals and upload MB are set to zero explicitly.

| Errors/month | Selected plan | Our formula | Our estimated monthly result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5,000 | Small, 100,000 included | 15 + 0 | $15 |
| 50,000 | Small, 100,000 included | 15 + 0 | $15 |
| 500,000 | Medium, 500,000 included | 50 + 0 | $50 |

At 500,000, Small plus public on-demand rates would be `15 + 400,000 × 0.00017 = $83`, so Medium is cheaper among these published choices. The 500,000 case leaves no allowance for symbol uploads; include actual uploaded MB before treating it as an all-in bill.

### HTTP uptime

Requested inputs: 5/25/100 endpoints, sixty-second checks, thirty days, one location, email alerts and one own-domain status page. Our requested checks are `N × 30 × 24 × 60 = N × 43,200`. Conditional billable events use `checks ÷ 10` from the billing guide.

| Endpoints | Requested checks | Conditional events | Selected public-plan arithmetic | Monitoring-only estimate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 216,000 | 21,600 | Small: 15 | $15 |
| 25 | 1,080,000 | 108,000 | Small: 15 + 8,000 × 0.00017 | $16.36 |
| 100 | 4,320,000 | 432,000 | Medium: 50 | $50 |

For three independent full-frequency locations, multiply check counts by three: 648,000 / 3,240,000 / 12,960,000 checks, or 64,800 / 324,000 / 1,296,000 conditional events. Corresponding public-plan arithmetic is $15 Small / $50 Medium / `50 + 796,000 × 0.00017 = $185.32` Medium with excess. This is workload sensitivity only; a selectable three-location service was not established.

**Exact full-workload totals remain unknown.** Released software supports sixty-second intervals, but hosted plan-specific limits, probe geography, monitor caps and SaaS own-domain status pages were not established. A consecutive-failure threshold uses scheduled checks in the reviewed software, rather than a documented extra retry tariff; additional billed confirmation runs are not invented. The uptime guide's competing one-request/one-event statement would materially change the bill.
