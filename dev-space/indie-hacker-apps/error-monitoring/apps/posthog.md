PostHog offers exception monitoring within a broader developer platform. Error events have their own meter and are not also charged as Product Analytics events. Logs and general application traces share a separate GB ingestion meter; recordings, exports and destination triggers have other meters. Free and PAYG share allowances, but a card-backed PAYG organization supports more isolated projects. This matters for one developer with three apps.

## Cost examples — our calculation, estimated

All amounts below are USD, excluding taxes. These are calculations from published rates, not vendor quotes or observed invoices. Use monthly PAYG, without prepaid discounts, startup credits, premium platform packages, AI or paid replay. Do not sum the separate workload families without recomputing the shared logs/traces allowance.

### Error reports

Inputs: one builder, three unrelated apps in **three projects**, 30 days of detailed errors, and 5,000 / 50,000 / 500,000 ingested exceptions per month **combined across the organization**. PAYG has USD 0 base, six projects and sufficient event retention; it requires adding a card for multiple projects. The no-card Free plan's one project does not provide this same isolation. Ordinary app analytics are excluded; billed exceptions are not billed again as Product Analytics events.

Rate function for the requested volumes:

`E(N) = min(max(N − 100,000, 0), 225,000) × 0.000370 + max(N − 325,000, 0) × 0.000140`

The lower USD 0.000115 band begins above 10 million and is not reached here.
