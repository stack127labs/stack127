# Sorry

- **Official URL:** https://www.sorryapp.com/
- **apps.md row:** `Lead` / `active` (listed 2026-11-04); the row describes a hosted status page for outage, maintenance and service communication.
- **Shutdown, sunset, acquisition:** No acquisition, shutdown or sunset notice was found in the official blog, product updates or service history checked. The service page is operational and had a product update on 2026-11-04. A Sorry™ Audiences blog post dated 2026-09-21 said the feature was still in development and targeted an October launch; the post still says “coming soon,” and no launch notice was found in the current update feed.
- **Research start:** 2026-11-06 11:10:20 UTC
- **Research finish:** 2026-11-06 11:25:35 UTC
- **Minutes taken:** 15.25

Sorry™ (also styled SorryApp) is a hosted status-page and incident-communication service. It does not monitor a customer’s infrastructure itself; it can turn external monitor alerts into status-page notices. The company’s live pricing page lists a $99/month base page with 1,000 subscribers and five team members, plus add-ons. Its current seven-day trial claim conflicts with an older Help Centre article that says 14 days. The public Terms and Privacy pages returned only the site navigation/footer in the research view, and the linked DPA opened a Google Drive sign-in screen, so those policy details could not be verified.
