## Status, rename, and ownership check

Monoscope is the current product name. Its December 17, 2025 year-in-review post says it rebranded from APIToolkit as the product grew beyond API request monitoring into logs, metrics, traces, and error tracking. The old `apitoolkit.io` address redirects to Monoscope. The current site and blog are active; the blog index lists a post dated October 30, 2026, and the GitHub repository shows release v0.6.31 dated October 10, 2026. I found no official acquisition, shutdown, or sunset notice in the blog, changelog, site, or repository pages checked. The public changelog's latest entry is dated January 20, 2025, so it is older than the current blog and release activity. The official site links a press kit, but its `/brand` page returned 404; I found no separate newsroom.

The site identifies Past 3 Technologies UG as the company behind the product. The product and company pages checked do not publish a founder/operator name here.

Sources checked for product changes: [rename post](https://monoscope.tech/blog/monoscope-2025-year-in-review/), [current blog index](https://monoscopehq.com/blog/), [changelog](https://monoscopehq.com/changelog/), [current homepage](https://monoscopehq.com/), [about page](https://monoscopehq.com/about/), [old APIToolkit domain](https://apitoolkit.io/), [GitHub releases](https://github.com/monoscope-tech/monoscope/releases), and [repository](https://github.com/monoscope-tech/monoscope).

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Free seats:** current pricing says unlimited team members; the repository README says one Free user. The current pricing page is newer and plan-specific, but both claims are retained above.
- **Cloud+S3 price:** current pricing and S3 docs say `$199/month` on top of `$29` Cloud (20M events included). Older official FAQ/cached pricing copy described `$199` as the starting total and 100M events included. The current plan table and storage guide were preferred for calculations.
- **Storage architecture:** current pricing separates vendor-hosted Cloud, Cloud+customer-S3, and self-hosted. Older README wording suggests both Cloud and self-hosted use customer S3; current Compose quickstart instead includes local TimescaleDB. Production architecture for all self-host deployments remains unclear.
- **Request bodies:** current product/pricing pages say bodies are included, while the Jan 23, 2025 privacy policy describes bodies as optional and metadata-only by default. Defaults may differ by SDK; verify the chosen SDK configuration.
- **Regions and DPA:** current pricing says US or EU Cloud region selection and says GDPR DPAs are signed. The older privacy policy says EU infrastructure and says Enterprise customers can request a DPA. Current pricing is newer, but public DPA terms were not found.
- **Trial:** a “30 day free trial” footer remains in terms/privacy, while current pricing promotes a permanent Free plan. No separate trial auto-charge/card conditions were published in the pricing, terms or privacy pages checked.
- **Not published after checking:** buyer-country eligibility; ISO currency code and annual billing; hard spend cap; project/recipient limits; a full SDK version matrix; source-map upload workflow; mobile support matrix; probe regions, interval range and timeout; certificate/domain checks; bulk export limits; selective single-event deletion; self-host sizing, running-cost inputs, backup/restore runbook and support response times.
