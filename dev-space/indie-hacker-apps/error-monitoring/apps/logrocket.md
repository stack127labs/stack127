## Cost examples — our calculation, estimated

Required inputs: **one builder, three apps, 30 days of full error detail**, 5,000 / 50,000 / 500,000 reports per month, no separately purchased replay/AI extras.

The published charging unit is **captured sessions**, not reports. Let:

- **E** = submitted error reports/month.
- **R** = average captured reports per error-bearing recorded session.
- **Z** = additional recorded sessions without those errors.
- **S = E ÷ R + Z**, provided those session/report distributions are actually measured.
- Estimated charge = selected plan price for **S** + contracted seat/retention/add-on charges + optional overage sessions × account overage rate + applicable taxes.

This formula is our calculation. No value of R or Z is supplied by the field-list workload, and privacy/quota/sampling can change capture. A user/session is not the same as a unique issue group or a monthly active user.
