Official URL: https://robotalp.com/

**Product changes and sunsets found first:** Robotalp's official migration notice says the rebuilt service launched **November 2, 2026**. Monitoring is now organized around website properties, with a fixed bundle of robots and plan-set schedules. Regular scheduled checks no longer use credits. Credits are for manually requested scans.

SMS, weekly reports, Dr. Alp emails and the response-time chart are gone. Old API keys and credit balances were reset. History before January 1, 2026, raw check results and response-time history did not migrate. This is a major product migration, not a full-service shutdown.

**Availability and ownership:** The rebuilt hosted service, Free plan and paid upgrades are documented as available. No current whole-service shutdown, sunset or acquisition notice was found in the checked current docs, migration notice, company page, pricing or legal pages and targeted official-domain searches. The company page describes an independent, investor-free team. Privacy's hypothetical business-transfer clause is not an acquisition announcement.

## Cost examples

All examples below are **our calculation, estimated**, not quotes. Original dollar amounts and periods are preserved. No exchange rate, private checkout amount, unpublished scan rate or old per-check credit tariff is assumed. Taxes and separate integration licenses are excluded where a published amount is shown.

### Scheduled uptime checks

Inputs: **5, 25 or 100 distinct public website domains**, 60-second HTTP checks, a 30-day month, email outage alerts and one own-domain public status page. Each endpoint is assumed to be a separate website so the input can be compared to property quotas. Several paths under one domain are not independent properties in this product.

Formula for one nominal fixed location:

`scheduled runs = websites × 30 days × 24 hours × 60 checks/hour = websites × 43,200`

| Websites | Nominal one-location runs/month | Current plan fit | Estimated recurring total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 5 × 43,200 = **216,000** | Solo has 5 properties, one member, 60-second availability and own-domain support. | **Unknown.** Current Solo subscription price and numeric notification allowance are not public. Fixed one-location operation is unsupported. |
| 25 | 25 × 43,200 = **1,080,000** | Team has 25 properties, 10 members and 60-second availability. | **Unknown.** Current Team price and exact page/notification quotas are not public. Fixed one-location operation is unsupported. |
| 100 | 100 × 43,200 = **4,320,000** | Agency has more than Team's 25 properties, but the exact cap is not given. A special plan may be needed. | **Unknown; plan fit unconfirmed.** Do not assume Agency covers 100 or multiply an old robot price by the number of websites. |

The known **additional scheduled-run credit requirement is zero**: the current plan's regular measurements do not use credits. This does not make the subscription or full scenario free. Manual “Scan now” requests are excluded. The plan fixes the regular schedule, and the same credit balance is not an allowance for billable scheduled runs.

For a nominal three-location comparison, `websites × 43,200 × 3` gives **648,000**, **3,240,000** and **12,960,000** runs. These are arithmetic workload counts only. Robotalp does not let the user choose one or three regions, and the current docs do not publish the normal per-check region count. There is **no exact native one-/three-location cost comparison**. Immediate failure confirmations run from every region, but current docs do not give an exact confirmation-run count; all scheduled measurements remain outside scan-credit metering.

Email notification usage depends on outages, recoveries, reminders and recipient routing, which the uptime workload does not quantify. Current numerical message caps are not public. A capped workspace stops sending notifications when its quota runs out while monitoring continues. One public status page does not add a subscriber-email entitlement.

### Browser flows and manual scans

For **1, 5 or 20 full browser flows** every five minutes in a 30-day month:

`full-flow executions = flows × 30 × 24 × (60 ÷ 5) = flows × 8,640`

A property's manual scan costs the sum of the robots that actually run; already-running and ownership-blocked scans are skipped without charge. - **C07/C10/C11:** A public legacy-generation rate card is not enough when the current product names, charging meter and quotas have changed. Keep observed amounts under the older edition; keep current totals unresolved. Source-rounded annual equivalents must not be used to reconstruct annual invoices.

Do not convert robot counts to websites or use a current HTTP page check as proof of API/port/browser-flow support.

- **C21:** “Delete” can mean archive, reversible deletion request or irreversible workspace closure. Record each behavior and its limits instead of a universal “data can be deleted” boolean.
- **C27:** The six user-approved states replace the draft states in fields.md. Cost arithmetic is labeled “our calculation, estimated” in values/notes, rather than introducing a derived state. Not-published claims are bounded to the named sources read.
