## Product and price boundary

This record covers DeadBro's hosted Rails APM and error-monitoring service, including its bundled website checks, public status pages, application logs, background-job monitoring, and plan-gated server metrics. The `dead_bro` gem runs in the customer's Rails application and sends data to DeadBro's hosted service. The site calls this an open-source agent, but the public repository has no license file in its root listing; I could not confirm an exact license. The gem requires a DeadBro account, and I found no self-hosted monitoring backend.

Prices are in EUR. Monthly prices and usage limits are listed on the current pricing page. Annual billing is advertised as 20% off, but the FAQ's “pay for 10 months, get 12” works out to 16.67% off a 12-month total; the page does not show annual totals. Estimates below use monthly plans and the lowest plan that covers the example request volume. No currency conversion is used.

## Cost examples

These are **our calculations**, not vendor quotes. They assume steady monthly usage, no additional apps or seats beyond the example, and a plan cap that covers the full monthly request count. The vendor pools tracked requests across an account and pauses tracking when the daily share is exhausted; it publishes no overage rate.

- **Small:** 40,000 tracked requests/month. Free includes 50,000/month. `€0/month`.
- **Growing:** 1,500,000 tracked requests/month. Starter includes 2,000,000/month. `€19/month`.
- **Busy:** 8,000,000 tracked requests/month. Growth includes 10,000,000/month. `€79/month`.

Website-check slots are separate from request volume: Free includes 1 URL, Starter 5, Growth 25, and Pro/Enterprise unlimited. Annual costs are omitted because the page's annual-discount wording and arithmetic conflict.
