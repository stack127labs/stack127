All prices retain the published **$** symbol. No ISO currency code, annual Montastic discount, tax rate or tax-inclusive total was established. Product pages specify monthly subscriptions. Corporate terms say fees are prepaid and generally non-refundable; they do not supply extra-unit rates. These are recurring subscription estimates, not vendor quotes or tested configurations.
