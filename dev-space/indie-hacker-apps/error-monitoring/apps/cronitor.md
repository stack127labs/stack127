## Cost examples

All totals below are **our calculations**, not Cronitor quotes. They use the published `$2 per monitor/month` and `$5 per user/month` Business rates. They exclude taxes, discounts, SMS, and any unlisted overage.

### Cron jobs

Assume one builder, one job monitor per scheduled job, no other monitors, and no status-page branding add-on.

| Workload | Inputs and formula | Our calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Small | 5 jobs fit the Hacker allowance of 5 monitors; one user is included. | **$0/month** ongoing. |
| Growing | 25 jobs, 1 user: `(25 × $2) + (1 × $5)`. | **$55/month** Business. |
| Busy | 100 jobs, 1 user: `(100 × $2) + (1 × $5)`. | **$205/month** Business. |

Job executions are not a separate public billing unit; the monitor and user counts drive these examples. Business includes 12-month data retention. The free plan has no stated retention period.

### Uptime checks

Assume HTTP checks every 60 seconds, one probe region, 30 days, one user, and one own-domain status page. One endpoint requires 43,200 checks/month; 5, 25, and 100 endpoints therefore require 216,000, 1,080,000, and 4,320,000 checks/month. The published normal-check price is per monitor; no separate run or region charge was found. A 60-second interval requires Business because Hacker's fastest interval is 5 minutes. The status-page guide maps an own domain/logo to the $25 branded-page add-on.

| Workload | Inputs and formula | Our calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Small | 5 monitors, 1 user, 1 branded page: `(5 × $2) + $5 + $25`. | **$40/month** Business. Monitor/user subtotal is $15/month; branded own-domain page adds $25/month. |
| Growing | 25 monitors, 1 user, 1 branded page: `(25 × $2) + $5 + $25`. | **$80/month** Business. Monitor/user subtotal is $55/month; branded own-domain page adds $25/month. |
| Busy | 100 monitors, 1 user, 1 branded page: `(100 × $2) + $5 + $25`. | **$230/month** Business. Monitor/user subtotal is $205/month; branded own-domain page adds $25/month. |

### Status pages

Assume one builder. Business includes one page and 500 subscribers; Enterprise includes unlimited pages and subscribers but has custom terms/features. A branded page is $25/page/month. The pricing table does not say whether subscriber limits are per page or account-wide, and does not publish an overage rate.

| Workload | Inputs and formula | Our calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Small | 1 page / 100 subscribers; Business one user plus branded page: `$5 + $25`. | **$30/month** if the branded add-on is needed; 100 subscribers fit within Business's 500. The 50-subscriber Hacker limit is too small. |

Status pages fed by Cronitor monitors also incur the monitor charges shown in the cron-job or uptime examples. The free basic status page is included with the Hacker plan, subject to its 50-subscriber limit.

- **C13 bill controls:** The docs clearly describe monitor quotas, but do not say whether a customer can set a monthly dollar cap or a spend alert that stops charges. I kept these as not published rather than infer from usage billing.
- **C14 retention:** Business gives a 12-month headline and the job/uptime pages give 12-month product details; free and Enterprise signal-by-signal retention is not stated. RUM FAQ uses plan-dependent wording but no current plan table by retention period.
