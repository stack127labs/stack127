### Uptime: 5 / 25 / 100 HTTP endpoints

Inputs: HTTP check every 60 seconds, one location, email alerts and one own-domain status page. A thirty-day month contains **43,200 minutes**.

| Endpoints | Scheduled-check formula | Scheduled HTTP executions | Plan calculation | Known monthly plan subtotal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 5 × 43,200 | 216,000 | Pro $69; 5 ≤ 300 monitors | **$69** |
| 25 | 25 × 43,200 | 1,080,000 | Pro $69; 25 ≤ 300 monitors | **$69** |
| 100 | 100 × 43,200 | 4,320,000 | Pro $69; 100 ≤ 300 monitors | **$69** |

Basic $19 has sufficient count and one-minute checks for all three, but **does not include an own-domain status page**. Pro is the lowest published plan with that feature. No status-page add-on rate is published, so Basic plus an invented cheap domain add-on is not used.

**Full-scenario qualification:** No selectable one-location policy, exact source region/count or scheduling behavior was established. Therefore $69 is the documented bundle subtotal for the count/interval/domain conditions, **not a guaranteed exact match to the geographic condition**. No additional-location fee is established, but absence of a fee mention does not prove free geographic control.

Each detected HTTP failure causes an immediate confirmation request. With R confirmation requests during the month, actual HTTP requests = scheduled executions + R. Failure frequency is not specified, so R is unknown. Plans publish monitor-count allowances, not a per-run tariff; no retry surcharge or execution-overage rule is published in the checked pricing/terms.
