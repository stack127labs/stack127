## Cost examples — our calculation, estimated

All prices are **USD**, as current terms section 14 explicitly states. These are estimated recurring prices from public rates, not quotes or tested invoices. Inputs: one builder, three apps represented as three Errors projects, at least 30 days of raw error detail, monthly billing, no paid replay or AI extras. Standard monthly allowance is sufficient in each chosen tier, so optional overage stays off.

Sources, checked 2026-11-04: https://realuptime.io/pricing ; https://realuptime.io/errors ; https://docs.realuptime.io/api ; https://realuptime.io/privacy ; https://realuptime.io/terms .

### Three error workloads

Formula: choose the lowest monthly Errors plan with **event allowance ≥ reports**, **project allowance ≥ 3**, and **raw-event retention ≥ 30 days**.

| Workload | Chosen plan | Included allowance | Retention/projects | Formula | Estimated recurring cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5,000 reports/month | Errors Pro | 100,000 events/month | 90 days / 10 projects | 1 × USD 23.99 + USD 0 overage | USD 23.99/month |
| 50,000 reports/month | Errors Pro | 100,000 events/month | 90 days / 10 projects | 1 × USD 23.99 + USD 0 overage | USD 23.99/month |
| 500,000 reports/month | Errors Business | 750,000 events/month | 180 days / 50 projects | 1 × USD 58.99 + USD 0 overage | USD 58.99/month |

Free includes enough monthly events for 5,000 reports, but its 14-day raw retention and two-project cap fail these held conditions. Combining apps into fewer projects would not fix the retention condition.

A report is an event, not a grouped issue. Larger accepted context does not bill as several events. The estimate assumes accepted reports conform to SDK size, quota and traffic rules; it is not a promise to ingest an arbitrary burst at once. Taxes, third-party notification-provider bills, paid AI features, separate Monitor/Status purchases and unspecified seats beyond the owner are excluded. The account owner is sufficient for the one-builder workload; Errors-only additional team entitlements remain unclear.

### Optional overage comparison

Published paid rate: **USD 0.50 per 1,000 events above allowance**, enabled only if the customer opts in and chooses a sufficient monthly budget.

For the busy workload on Pro, our calculation would be:

- Extra reports: 500,000 − 100,000 = 400,000.
- Overage: (400,000 ÷ 1,000) × USD 0.50 = USD 200.00.
- Total: USD 23.99 + USD 200.00 = **USD 223.99/month**, requiring at least USD 200 of available overage budget.

Business at USD 58.99 includes those 500,000 reports and is cheaper for that workload. This comparison uses an exact multiple of 1,000; rounding/proration of incomplete 1,000-event units is not established and is not invented. A budget cap limits usage charges by refusing/counting additional events when no permitted allowance remains. It does not cap the fixed plan or other products.

### Annual billing

| Plan | Twelve monthly payments | Published annual total | Monthly equivalent, our calculation | Saving, our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Errors Pro | 12 × USD 23.99 = USD 287.88 | USD 239.90/year | USD 239.90 ÷ 12 ≈ USD 19.99/month, rounded to two decimals | USD 47.98/year |
| Errors Business | 12 × USD 58.99 = USD 707.88 | USD 589.90/year | USD 589.90 ÷ 12 ≈ USD 49.16/month, rounded to two decimals | USD 117.98/year |

These are annual prepaid charges, not the ability to pay the displayed monthly equivalent monthly. Exact saving fraction is two months out of twelve, or 16⅔%. Overage remains separate.

RealUptime Complete's 15%/20% bundle discounts are excluded because the workload buys only Errors. The pricing page's first-20-customers 50% promotion is excluded because remaining eligibility was not established. No promotion is treated as a guaranteed current price.

### Other workload families

RealUptime Errors does not ship tracing/APM: Sentry transaction items are accepted and discarded, not stored or billed. Its supported message/event intake and breadcrumb context do not establish a GB searchable-log product. Therefore the 1M/10M/50M backend-request and 5/50/250 GB log workloads have no comparable Errors price here.

HTTP/agent/browser/heartbeat checks and public status pages belong to separately priced products. Their uptime, synthetic, job and status-subscriber scenarios are outside this Errors record, rather than being assigned a zero price or silently bundled. See the explicit separation at https://realuptime.io/errors and https://realuptime.io/pricing ; protocol exclusions are at https://realuptime.io/kb/point-your-sentry-sdk-here and https://docs.realuptime.io/api .
