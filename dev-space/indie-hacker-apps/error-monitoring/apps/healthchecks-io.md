## Cost examples

These are **our calculations** from the official hosted plan limits and prices. Workload: 5, 25, or 100 jobs; each runs hourly for a 30-day month and sends both a start and a success ping. That is `jobs × 24 runs/day × 30 days × 2 signals` (1,440 pings/job/month). Healthchecks.io prices by number of checks, not monthly ping volume, so ping totals do not change the plan price. These workloads are far below the API's stated 5-pings-per-minute per-check limit.

| Workload | Plan selected | Formula | Our calculation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small: 5 jobs | Hobbyist; 20 jobs included | 5 ≤ 20 jobs; 5 × 1,440 = 7,200 pings/month | **$0/month** |
| Growing: 25 jobs | Business; 100 jobs included | 25 > 20 free/Supporter jobs; 25 × 1,440 = 36,000 pings/month | **$20/month**; annual option $192/year ($16/month as published) |
| Busy: 100 jobs | Business; 100 jobs included | 100 ≤ 100 jobs; 100 × 1,440 = 144,000 pings/month | **$20/month** at the plan limit; above 100 jobs, Business Plus is $80/month up to 1,000 jobs |

The $5/month Supporter plan does not increase the 20-job limit. Self-hosted total costs are not estimated because no official hardware sizing or fixed deployment shape is published.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Annual terms:** the current pricing page offers annual Business and Business Plus subscriptions, but the terms last updated May 9, 2018 say billing cycles are monthly. I used the current plan-specific prices and kept the mismatch visible.
- **Object storage region:** the FAQ says hosted data are stored in Germany, while the April 2026 blog says large ping bodies moved to self-hosted S3-compatible storage but does not state that server's country. The blog also describes off-site daily backups of those objects, kept for 30 days. I did not assign a country to that object store.
- **Notifications beyond included credits:** exact conversion and additional cost for SMS, WhatsApp, and calls, including what happens after monthly credits run out, were not stated on the pricing or notification pages.
- **Trial/commercial rules:** pricing, FAQ, and terms were checked; no separate time-limited trial terms or explicit Hobbyist commercial-use rule was found.
- **Data export and filtering:** the Management API documents per-check access and deletion, but I found no full-account archive format or automatic ping-body secret/PII masking description in the API, privacy, or log docs.
- **Newsroom/acquisition:** the About page and official blog were checked, including its current October 2026 post and the historical 2021 ownership post. No separate newsroom or current acquisition/shutdown notice was found. This is a bounded search, not a claim about undisclosed ownership changes.
