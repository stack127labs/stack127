### AWS Serverless is a different calculation

Serverless bills separate indexing/search **OCU-hours** and **stored GB-months**. Our raw monthly GB does not determine consumed OCU time or stored index volume.

- **Classic:** published first-collection floor is normally **2 OCUs**, with a **1 OCU** dev/test option without redundant standby nodes; actual minimum can vary by collection/data. Additional compute scales with work.
- **NextGen:** current pricing says it can scale indexing/search to zero after **10 minutes** of inactivity. Collection-group minimum/maximum settings are generation-specific.
- **Both:** separately billed stored data and optional services prevent "compute can be zero" from becoming a free ongoing log service.

No Classic rate/minimum is silently reused as a NextGen quote, and no idle-time assumption is inserted for the fixed workloads. A 30-day Serverless time-series lifecycle can be configured, but deletion occurs on a documented best-effort schedule after the minimum period; that timing can affect stored-volume bills.
