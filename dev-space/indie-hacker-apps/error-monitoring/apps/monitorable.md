## Scope and evidence

Monitorable is a hosted Linux server and container monitoring service. Its agent reports host metrics, Docker container health, systemd service state, and physical disk SMART health. It does not probe websites from outside. The agent is open source under Apache-2.0, but the complete hosted service is not offered as a self-hosted edition.

The current homepage was live and showed Lite, Pro, Scale, and Enterprise plans. Pricing is in USD. Annual amounts and monthly equivalents are recorded separately. Monitorable says taxes, if any, are calculated and collected by Creem at checkout; it does not say whether VAT is included. There is no published price for fleets above 150 servers.

I checked the homepage pricing and FAQ, the official docs for installation, requirements, metrics, alert rules, notification channels, data history, and troubleshooting, the privacy policy, and refund/cancellation policy. I attempted the terms, changelog, blog, newsroom, source repository, release page, and app sign-in routes. Those routes did not load in the web check. The privacy policy identifies the operator as an individual entrepreneur, but the terms page that gives the legal identity and address did not load. No private person's name is included here.

A stale official homepage snapshot in search results still says paid plans are not on sale. The direct homepage copy crawled more recently shows working paid-trial links and says plans renew until canceled. I used the newer page for current pricing and noted the stale snapshot below. I did not sign up, log in, or submit a form. The sign-in route could not be verified.
