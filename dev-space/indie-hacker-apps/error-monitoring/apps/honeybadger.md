## Scope and evidence

Each row records its plan, edition, deployment, signal, exact source URL, and checked date. `source_updated_at` is not stated unless the note gives a visible source date or release month. The checked date is not a publication date. A confirmed row confirms its stated value only; it does not imply that a feature works on every plan or runtime.

`not_published` means no answer was found in the named sources checked. `check_failed` means retrieval prevented a check. `unclear` means readable evidence leaves the scope or meaning unresolved. `conflicting` preserves different official claims. Calculations below use the task's workloads and are our estimates, not vendor quotes. Prices retain the published `$` symbol because a subscription currency code was not established.

No account, login, form submission, installation, API call with customer credentials, ingestion, delivery test, or support request was made. The docs describe expected behavior; it was not tested. This overwrites the earlier group research with a fresh source review.

## Cost examples: our calculation, estimated

All arithmetic uses the supplied editorial workloads. These are not vendor quotes, not claims of cheapest configuration, and not a combined bill. The same bundle can cover several families; adding the base subscription once for every family would double-count it. Use monthly billing. Taxes, delivery fees, optional replay services, custom contracts and unpriced upgrades are excluded or left unresolved. No currency conversion is made.

### Errors: 5,000 / 50,000 / 500,000 reports per month

Inputs: one builder, three projects, 30-day detail, Team $26/month, 50,000 included reports, 90-day advertised error retention, opt-in overage rate $0.0003/report. Rates and allowances: [plans](https://www.honeybadger.io/plans/), checked 2026-11-04. Quota counts occurrences, not grouped errors: [quotas](https://docs.honeybadger.io/resources/quotas/), checked 2026-11-04.

Our formula: `26 + max(0, reports − 50,000) × 0.0003`.

| Reports/month | Included | Extra reports | Our estimated monthly result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5,000 | Within 50,000 | 0 | $26 |
| 50,000 | Within 50,000 | 0 | $26 |
| 500,000 | 50,000 | 450,000 | $26 + $135 = $161 |

The $161 estimate requires enabled overage billing. Without it, enforced throttling begins at allowance and processing stops at 125%. A higher quota tier may cost less; the unavailable selector prices prevent finding the cheapest valid tier. Developer is not substituted for the small scenario because its 15-day retention misses the requested 30 days. The exact preservation guarantee for every raw notice in a recurring issue remains unverified.

Business sensitivity, using its own published rate: `80 + max(0, reports − 50,000) × 0.0006` gives $80 / $80 / $350. This is a separate plan calculation, not an added fee on top of Team.

Annual equivalents, our calculation: Team `286 ÷ 12 = $23.833333…/month`; Business `880 ÷ 12 = $73.333333…/month`. Annual totals remain $286 and $880. Relative to twelve base monthly payments, savings are `26 × 12 − 286 = $26` and `80 × 12 − 880 = $80`. No annual add-on or overage discount is assumed.
