## Scope and evidence

Attempted all 50 field IDs: C01 to C27, E01 to E04, U01 to U05, L01 to L03, A01 to A03, S01 to S03, J01 to J02 and H01 to H03.

Product identity: one Sentry product with hosted plans (Developer, Team, Business, Enterprise) and a self-hosted edition under the FSL source-available license. Hosted and self-hosted facts are separate rows. Plans, add-ons and self-hosting are not merged.

Method: official pages were downloaded over HTTPS with a browser User-Agent on 2026-11-23. The pricing page's own schema.org price data and its plan-table check marks were read from the page. Docs were read as page text. No account, login, signup, form, installation, event ingestion or alert delivery was tested.

Money is USD. Prices exclude sales tax; the pricing docs say so. Calculations are our estimates, not quotes.

Hosted source: https://sentry.io/pricing/ and https://docs.sentry.io/pricing/. Self-hosted source: https://develop.sentry.dev/self-hosted/ and https://github.com/getsentry/self-hosted.

## Cost examples: our calculation, estimated

Use monthly billing where the page offers it. Team is $29 per month billed monthly; Business is $89 per month billed monthly. Annual prices are shown separately. Each family is calculated for one builder and the workload in fields.md. No taxes, Seer, notification fees or other charges are included.

Errors (5,000 / 50,000 / 500,000 a month, 30-day detail):

- 5,000 errors: Developer, $0. Exactly fills the 5k allowance; Developer has no PAYG, so more errors are dropped.
- 50,000 errors: Team, $29. Exactly fills the 50k allowance.
- 500,000 errors: Team, $29 + 50,000 errors at $0.0003625 + 400,000 errors at $0.0002188 = $134.65 estimate. The band rule is unclear (C12). If one band rate applied to all extra errors instead, the figure would be lower; that reading was not calculated here.

Uptime (5 / 25 / 100 HTTP endpoints, checked every 60 seconds, email alerts):

- Each endpoint needs one uptime monitor. Developer includes one, with no PAYG, so it cannot cover these workloads.
- Team: $29 base + (endpoints − 1) extra monitors at $1 each.
- 5 endpoints: $29 + 4 × $1 = $33 a month.
- 25 endpoints: $29 + 24 × $1 = $53 a month.
- 100 endpoints: $29 + 99 × $1 = $128 a month.
- Every row is conflicting: the pricing page says $1 per uptime alert, and the docs say $1 per monitor. The docs price monitors, not check runs, so 60-second checks add no separate charge in the pages read.
- The status-page item in the scenario is not offered; no status page is included.

Logs (5 / 50 / 250 GB a month, logs only):

- Team: $29 base; 5 GB included; $0.50 per GB beyond that. 5 GB = $29. 50 GB = $29 + 45 × $0.50 = $51.50. 250 GB = $29 + 245 × $0.50 = $151.50. Team query window is 14 days.
- Business: $89 base; 5 GB included; $0.50 per GB beyond that. 5 GB = $89. 50 GB = $89 + 45 × $0.50 = $111.50. 250 GB = $89 + 245 × $0.50 = $211.50. Business query window is 30 days, which matches the 30-day scenario.
- The GB unit is not defined as raw or compressed, so every row is unclear.

Performance (1M / 10M / 50M requests a month, 10% sampling, 10 spans per sampled request, Team):

- 1M requests: 100,000 sampled requests × 10 = 1,000,000 spans. Within 5M included: $29.
- 10M requests: 1,000,000 sampled × 10 = 10,000,000 spans. 5,000,000 extra × $0.0000020 = $10. Total $39 under the inside-band reading.
- 50M requests: 5,000,000 sampled × 10 = 50,000,000 spans. 45,000,000 extra × $0.0000020 = $90. Total $119.
- The band rule is unclear. Ten spans per request is the scenario's assumption, not a Sentry figure.

Jobs (5 / 25 / 100 jobs, each run hourly, start and success check-ins), Team:

- Each job is one cron monitor. Team includes one; each extra is $0.78 per billing period.
- 5 jobs: $29 + 4 × $0.78 = $32.12 a month.
- 25 jobs: $29 + 24 × $0.78 = $47.72 a month.
- 100 jobs: $29 + 99 × $0.78 = $106.22 a month.
- The docs price monitors, not check-ins. Hourly start and end check-ins (1,440 per job a month) are not priced separately in the pages read. Our sums are unclear.

Browser checks: not offered. Status pages: not offered. Self-hosted running cost: not published; not calculated.

## Conflicts and unknowns

All sources in this section were checked 2026-11-23.

- **Uptime unit.** [Pricing page](https://sentry.io/pricing/) lists "1 uptime monitor +$1.00/uptime alert additional". [Pricing docs](https://docs.sentry.io/pricing/) list "Uptime Monitors... $1.00/monitor", and [monitor quota guide](https://docs.sentry.io/pricing/quotas/manage-cron-monitors/) describes PAYG budget paying for extra monitors per billing period. The per-monitor reading is used for the sums above; the per-alert wording is kept as a conflict.
- **Uptime timeout.** [Uptime docs](https://docs.sentry.io/product/uptime-monitoring/) say the timeout is configurable up to 30 seconds, and also say each request has a 10-second timeout threshold. Neither is resolved here.
- **Free plan terms.** [Pricing page](https://sentry.io/pricing/) calls Developer free. [Terms section 17](https://sentry.io/legal/terms/) limits No-Charge Products to a designated period, default 30 days, and caps liability at US$50. The terms do not name Developer, so the ongoing free plan is not confirmed.
- **Errors and spans PAYG bands.** [Pricing docs](https://docs.sentry.io/pricing/) list per-unit rates by volume band. They do not say whether a band's rate applies only to units inside that band or to all units once the band is reached. Both 500,000-error and performance rows are unclear for this reason.
- **Logs query window and event lookback.** [Logs guide](https://docs.sentry.io/product/explore/logs/) says query window is 7, 14 or 30 days by plan. [Pricing page](https://sentry.io/pricing/) says 30-day lookback on Developer and up to 90 days on Team and Business. These are different signals; both are kept.
- **Seer pricing change.** [Pricing docs](https://docs.sentry.io/pricing/) state that Seer moves to usage-based credits on December 8, 2026. Seer is outside every scenario here.
- **Self-hosted license label.** [GitHub repository metadata](https://api.github.com/repos/getsentry/self-hosted) reports NOASSERTION, while the LICENSE.md file is FSL-1.1-Apache-2.0. The file is used.
- **Support.** The plan table lists email support on all plans. Terms say paid support runs through the support page or email. No response time is published.
