## Scope and evidence

Attempted **40 distinct field IDs**: C01 to C27, U01 to U05, L01 to L03, S01 to S03 and J01/J02. Hyperping provides external checks, full Playwright browser scripts, scheduled job start/completion pings, system metrics, application Logs, on-call/incident response and customer status communication. Complete self-hosting, application exception/source-map tracking and backend APM tracing were not established, so E/A/H groups are outside this product scope. Browser traces are synthetic run artifacts, and trace IDs in log lines do not establish a stored distributed-tracing product.

Read current UI pricing and its official machine-readable pricing.md, linked plan-specific docs/API definitions, public terms/privacy/DPA/security/subprocessor/SLA pages, project/seat quota rules, check/region/IPv6/confirmation/browser/SSL/domain/maintenance guides, cron guide/API/product FAQ, server agent requirements, full log source/search/alert documentation, and status-page builder/components/subscribers/incident/migration guides. Lifecycle notices were checked first; the unmaintained v1 notice was followed into the current migration instructions.

Pricing.md supplies the actual monthly and yearly totals and an explicit Logs alpha-before-metering condition; rounded annual equivalents must not be multiplied into a bill. The dedicated cron product FAQ resolves a quota detail not explicit in the generic API: each healthcheck counts as one shared monitor. Projects do not multiply quotas; account limits span projects.

The evidence preserves three material current-source conflicts: yearly unused-month refunds versus terms' fourteen-day restriction; seven-day versus ninety-day backup-deletion statements; Pro versus Business TV/auto-refresh entitlement. Some update labels lag the content they contain, so the report copies dates rather than guessing when every paragraph changed. Hyperping's competitor-pricing widget is not proof about other apps and was not used for their facts.

Every fact row has exact URLs, checked UTC date, source date where stated, unit, plan, edition, deployment, signal and an individual note. Source dates follow URL order. Only the six requested states are used. not_published means not established in the named checked sources. All arithmetic remains **our calculation, estimated**, with limitations beside the result. No signup, ingestion, installation, private API access or delivery/performance testing occurred.
