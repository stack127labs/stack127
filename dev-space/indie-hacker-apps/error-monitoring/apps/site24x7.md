## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Identity:** Current Site24x7 pages still sell observability while the announcement places it under OpManager Nexus. Preserve the announcement and current commercial labels; do not infer shutdown or silently duplicate the cloud offer as an unrelated app.
- **Seats, locations and channels:** Current pricing cards versus their own matrix disagree on multi-user support. Free-plan comparison versus current Web Uptime pricing disagree on paid locations and SMS. The polling article describes random single-location Web Uptime polling. These remain open after reading website setup, location profiles, notification profiles, the pricing FAQ and outage logic.
- **Frequency:** The pricing FAQ says poller frequency is fixed at five minutes; website setup specifically permits ten/fifteen-second polls on eligible packs. Secondary frequency is one hour in the current plan matrix versus thirty minutes in the FAQ/outage article. Do not promise the faster value without a plan-specific answer.
- **Retention:** General history-years labels do not specify raw data. APM traces thirty days versus plan-specific spans seven/fifteen days may describe different data. Read APM metrics, configuration, licensing and Capture All Traces; the mapping was not found. Free raw retention is still not clear.
- **Logs:** Signup cohorts have different retention factors. Current pricing presents both retention-specific rates and weighted license consumption. The example exposes two smallest-workload totals rather than presenting one as exact. Read log-type settings, retention, quota and add-on worked examples. Archive storage and reindex pricing remain unspecified.
- **StatusIQ:** Standalone Free excludes custom domain; paid Site24x7 Basic includes it. Blue's USD 50/month annual display implies USD 600/year, while its guide says USD 599. Monthly examples avoid that discrepancy. Subscribers are notified about incidents, not arbitrary component-only changes.
- **Missing error/job controls:** The JS errors, APM metrics/configuration/milestone and cron/heartbeat/failure endpoint pages did not establish custom error fingerprints/merge, automatic fixed/regressed issue state, complete mapping storage quotas, dropped-error counters, full captured job output or a per-run history limit.
- **Billing and privacy gaps:** No universal monetary cap, current-pack SMS allowance/rate card or complete buyer-country exclusions found in the pricing, credits, subscription and terms pages. Data-region mapping is not a promise every buyer can pick every region. No account was used to resolve purchase-only details.
