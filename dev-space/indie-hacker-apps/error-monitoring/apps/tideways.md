## Field problems

- **C07/C11/A02:** “Sampling 10%” can mean dropping whole monitored requests or selecting detailed traces. Only the first reduces the request meter, and it loses errors too. Both interpretations are shown.
- **C11/E01/C14:** Error reports are not the billing unit. Three app allowances and a 30-day lookback do not establish storage of every individual error occurrence.
- **C14/C26:** Aggregated history and selected historical traces are distinct from raw detail; old plan names need a boundary.
- **C05/C06/H fields:** A proprietary hosted backend, installed commercial components and an archived Apache client have separate scopes.
- **J01/J02:** These are telemetry heartbeat rules, not a complete cron-expression scheduler with a dedicated start/end ping API.
- No field-list edit is proposed. Country can be enriched to Germany; no apps.md identity/availability error was established.
