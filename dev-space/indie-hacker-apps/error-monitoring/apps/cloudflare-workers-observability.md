## Product and pricing boundary

Workers Observability collects logs, request metrics, traces, and grouped runtime failures for Cloudflare Workers. It is a Cloudflare-hosted feature of the Workers platform, not a self-hosted service or a general-purpose monitor for applications running on other platforms.

Cloudflare announced unified byte-based Observability pricing effective **December 1, 2026**. Until November 30, the Workers plans' event-based Workers Logs and tracing rates still apply. The new Observability allowance is pooled by Cloudflare account across Workers logs and traces, Cloudflare Traces, Issues, and several other Cloudflare datasets. It is separate from Workers compute charges. The Workers Paid plan's $5 monthly minimum is a runtime plan price, not an Observability subscription fee. [Workers Logs pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/observability/logs/workers-logs/) · [Workers tracing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/observability/traces/workers-tracing/) · [Unified Observability pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/observability/pricing/) · [Workers platform pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/)

## Cost examples: our calculation

These are **our calculations**, not Cloudflare quotes. They use the announced Unified Observability Paid tier beginning December 1, 2026, USD, a 30-day billing cycle, and no telemetry from other Cloudflare products. They exclude any unknown fixed account fee, Workers runtime subscription/usage, taxes, external destination charges, and data not included in the stated workload.

The ingestion input is **billable, pre-compression GB received by Cloudflare**, not an assumed number of log events or requests. Assume data is received evenly each day and all data remains queryable for the standard seven days. Under those assumptions, estimated storage is monthly ingestion × 7/30. This is only a storage estimate; real billing uses the maximum queryable GB stored each day.

| Workload | Assumed input | Paid ingestion calculation | Estimated storage calculation | Our calculation |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | ---: |
| Small | 5 GB/month | max(5 - 50, 0) × $0.25 = $0 | 5 × 7/30 = 1.17 GB-month; under 10 included | $0/month |
| Growing | 50 GB/month | max(50 - 50, 0) × $0.25 = $0 | 50 × 7/30 = 11.67 GB-month; (11.67 - 10) × $0.10 = $0.17 | $0.17/month |
| Busy | 250 GB/month | (250 - 50) × $0.25 = $50.00 | 250 × 7/30 = 58.33 GB-month; (58.33 - 10) × $0.10 = $4.83 | $54.83/month |

The Free tier is not a monthly bucket: it stops account-wide ingestion above **0.5 GB on a day**. With even daily distribution and no other telemetry, 5 GB/month averages 0.17 GB/day and fits; 50 GB averages 1.67 GB/day and 250 GB averages 8.33 GB/day, so those workloads exceed the Free daily cap. The charge for a complete Free-tier dataset above the cap is not meaningful because Cloudflare stops ingesting new data. The Paid totals above also depend on the assumed seven-day storage window and do not include any separate Workers Paid $5/month runtime fee. [Unified pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/observability/pricing/) · [Workers plan price and included runtime](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/) · [Usage-based billing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/billing/understand/usage-based-billing/)

Before December 1, logs and traces use event counts, so these GB examples do not estimate current event-based charges. For example, one request may create an invocation log plus one or more console logs or trace spans. Cloudflare's current Workers Logs docs show a two-log-per-request example; tracing sampling and event count are workload-specific. The Workers Paid $5 minimum is the compute platform plan. It must not be presented as a flat Workers Observability price.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Upcoming pricing transition:** Through November 30, Workers Logs has separate Workers Free and Workers Paid event quotas; Cloudflare announced the pooled, byte-based Observability meter from December 1. The unified allowance also pools other Cloudflare signals, so an account's non-Worker data can use it.
- **Separate product prices:** Workers runtime is priced separately. Workers Paid is $5/month minimum. Workers Logpush uses a separate request-based charge, and standard Logpush exports have separate GB charges. Do not combine those into the cost of storing and querying Workers Logs.
- **OTel export after December 1:** The October 2025 tracing post states the current 10-million-export-event allowance and $0.05/million overage for Workers Paid. The current export, Logpush, and unified pricing docs do not say whether that OTel-specific event rate remains after Dec 1.
- **Observability Paid base price:** The pricing table publishes its usage allowance and overage rates but not a separate fixed monthly base price. The Workers $5 compute minimum is not used to fill that gap.
- **Plan mapping:** The new pricing page says Free, Paid, and Enterprise, and states that Free/Pro/Business customers move on Dec 1 and Enterprise moves at renewal. It does not give a full mapping from every existing Workers plan to a standalone Observability tier.
- **Data residency:** The DPA and general Metadata Boundary FAQ were checked, but neither names a selectable or default storage region for Workers Observability data. The Metadata Boundary's general customer-log wording is not treated as proof of product-specific region controls.
- **Historical Baselime notice:** Cloudflare announced an expected standalone Baselime sunset toward end of 2024. The official product/news pages checked do not confirm the actual final service date. Workers Observability is live.
- **Pricing example version:** An older indexed copy of the official pricing page displayed incorrect storage arithmetic ($0.80 and $7.05). The direct page updated Oct 25, 2026 shows $1.00 and $7.25; the newer direct version is used.

- **C10-C11:** Cloudflare publishes a paid Observability usage allowance but not a fixed Observability base price. The workload examples calculate the telemetry component only and state the storage assumptions.
- **C14:** Metrics history, log/trace retention, and issue occurrence detail have different periods. Issue automation history is not issue data retention.
- **C21:** OTel export's post-December price is not stated, and disabling persistence is different from deleting data already stored.
