## Current status and ownership

CronAlive is advertising an ongoing free plan, paid subscriptions, and a 14-day Pro trial. Its homepage signup link opened the public registration route at https://app.cronalive.com/register; I did not submit the form or create an account. This is evidence that the vendor is taking signups, but I did not create a customer account.

The live service status is **not independently confirmed**: Terms link to https://status.cronalive.com/, but that page could not be fetched in the research tool. The current product pages, pricing, docs, and terms remain available. The Terms say the service is operated by CronAlive, an individual entrepreneur. The vendor does not identify the operator's country in the Terms or Privacy Policy, so this file does not name a private person.

I found no shutdown, sunset, or acquisition announcement in the official product pages, Terms, Privacy Policy, or the blog listing checked. The blog's newest visible entry is dated 24 August 2026. I found no separate newsroom or changelog link in the site navigation checked. These are bounded searches, not proof that no notice exists elsewhere.

## Cost examples

### Scheduled jobs

**Our calculation**, monthly billing, 30 days:

- Inputs: 5, 25, or 100 jobs; each runs once an hour and sends two pings, one at start and one on success.
- Formula: jobs × 24 runs/day × 30 days × 2 pings/run = jobs × 1,440 pings/month.
- 5 jobs: 7,200 pings/month; 5 checks fit Free, with its 10-check and 50,000-ping allowances; **$0/month**.
