Official URL: https://gigapipe.com/

**Status, shutdown, sunset, acquisition and rename check:** Active open-source project and hosted offer. The old qryn repository resolves to Gigapipe and the README explicitly says "formerly known as qryn." The site identifies the earlier cLoki project as starting in 2018. The latest public release is **v5.6.1, published 2026-11-03**. No shutdown, sunset or acquisition announcement was found in the official sources checked. A generic acquisition/assignment clause in the terms is not an acquisition announcement. The site's news page stops at an older v4.1.1 entry, so it is not used as the current release number. **Price and retention conditions:** Pro is **€599 monthly**, or **€479/month equivalent billed yearly**. It advertises up to 5 TB/month ingestion, yet the FAQ says unlimited ingestion within infrastructure resources. Enforcement of that boundary remains unresolved. Base storage is **360 GB NVMe**, with extra storage **€100 per TB per month**. Storage/compression estimates are not guaranteed retention periods. ## Cost examples

These are **our calculations, estimated, not quoted**. They use the currently advertised Pro monthly base, not trial credits or the older metered qryn Cloud tariff. All checked source dates below are 2026-11-05.

### Logs

Inputs: one builder; 5, 50 or 250 **raw GB sent per month**; a 30-day month; 30 days of searchable log detail; no archive. Other telemetry is excluded from the illustrative volume. Pro includes 360 GB physical NVMe; actual stored bytes include compression, labels, indexes and other database overhead. The vendor's approximate capacity claims do not specify a guaranteed overhead allowance.

Formula: monthly Pro subscription = **€599 + (€100 × n)**, where n is the number of additional 1 TB NVMe units explicitly ordered. With **n = 0**, the conditional base is **€599**. There is no published current per-GB ingestion or query-scan multiplier to add.

| Workload | Raw input over the 30-day history | Our calculation with no storage add-on | What prevents an exact qualifying total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small | 5 GB/month | €599 + €100 × 0 = **€599/month conditional base** | Current 30-day searchable-retention entitlement and full stored footprint are not specified. |
| Growing | 50 GB/month | €599 + €100 × 0 = **€599/month conditional base** | Same unknowns; raw ingest and physical stored bytes are different measures. |
| Busy | 250 GB/month | €599 + €100 × 0 = **€599/month conditional base** | Same unknowns; shared telemetry, overhead and headroom can consume disk. |

All three raw monthly volumes are below the advertised 5 TB/month figure. At an illustrative **1:1 raw-to-stored payload ratio**, retained log payload alone would be 5, 50 and 250 GB, each below 360 GB. That is our deliberately simple storage assumption, not a measured ratio, guaranteed fit or a complete disk requirement. We do not substitute the vendor's roughly 6× compression claim for a tested workload.

If one additional TB is explicitly needed, the same published formula gives **€599 + €100 × 1 = €699/month**. There is no evidence that the add-on is automatically ordered on exhaustion. Exact totals meeting all retention/storage conditions remain **unknown** pending plan confirmation and sizing.

Annual base, separately: **€479 × 12 = €5,748/year**, versus **€599 × 12 = €7,188/year** for twelve monthly base payments. Calculated saving: **€1,440/year**, before taxes and extras. No currency conversion or rounding is used.
