## Conflicts and unknowns

- **PAYG on Pro:** the pricing page, checked/crawled more recently, says both Pro and Business can enable PAYG. The Beats & Usage guide, last updated May 11, 2026, says Pro cannot. Cost calculations use the current plan-specific pricing page; ask Drumbeats to confirm the docs update.
- **Paid 30-second uptime checks:** pricing advertises 30 seconds, while uptime docs list 1 minute as the shortest accepted interval. The 60-second cost scenario is documented; do not assume 30-second checks work.
- **Cron expression syntax:** the technical Cron guide says six-field expressions are rejected, while the newer Cron product page says five- and six-field expressions are supported. I left the accepted syntax unresolved.
- **Keyword checks:** the April 8 uptime changelog listed keyword verification as coming soon. The current monitor-type and uptime guides do not say whether it later shipped.
- **Custom domains:** status-page marketing says a customer domain can use CNAME. Pricing and Trust Center describe vanity slugs under Drumbeats. The linked [custom-domain guide](https://drumbeats.io/docs/status-pages/custom-domains) returned a cache miss, so the CNAME claim remains unresolved.
- **Status-page subscribers:** pricing gives subscriber limits and status-page marketing offers RSS/Atom, but older comparison copy says there were no visitor subscriptions. The linked [email-subscriptions guide](https://drumbeats.io/docs/status-pages/subscriptions) returned a cache miss. Email delivery and the definition of the subscriber counter remain unclear.
- **Project capacity:** Free and Pro project counts are public; the Business project count is not shown in the current plan summary.
- **More than two uptime locations:** Europe and beta US East are the only locations described. The requested three-location price cannot be calculated.
- **Data-processing agreement:** Privacy describes Drumbeats' agreements with its providers, but no public customer DPA was found in the Terms, Privacy, pricing or Trust Center pages.
- **News and shutdown check:** I checked the [official blog archive](https://drumbeats.io/blog), the recent [BullMQ monitoring article](https://drumbeats.io/blog/monitor-bullmq-workers-heartbeat), homepage, Terms, Privacy and public status page. The BullMQ article was published November 3, 2026. The blog has product/changelog posts and no separate newsroom link was found. No shutdown/acquisition notice appeared in those checked pages.
