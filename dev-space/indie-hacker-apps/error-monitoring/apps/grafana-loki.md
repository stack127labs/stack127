**Status, shutdown, sunset and acquisition check:** Loki OSS and Grafana Cloud Logs are active. GitHub's latest stable release is **v3.7.9, published 2026-10-20**. No shutdown of either was found in the official pages checked. Important related notices:

- **Promtail:** standalone agent reached end of life **2026-03-02**, with no future support or updates. Use Alloy or another supported collector. ## Cost examples

All examples are **our calculations, estimated, not quoted**. Currency remains **US dollars**. No exchange rate, trial credit or silent rounding is used.

### Hosted logs: 30-day searchable history

Fixed inputs: one builder; 5, 50 or 250 raw GB/month; a 30-day month; 30-day searchable log detail; no archive; no paid AI, enterprise plugins or IRM; no other Cloud products consuming additional paid usage.

For the arithmetic only, assume the submitted raw volume maps one-for-one to billable processed GB, and that no Adaptive Logs rule drops data, so **P = W = V**. P is processed volume, W is written volume, V is our raw input. This does not assert a byte-perfect vendor definition for label/metadata overhead, GB versus GiB, or compressed object-store space.

Current displayed starter rates are:

- Platform fee: **$19/month**.
- Process: **$0.050 per GB**.
- Write: **$0.400 per GB**, with **50 GB written allowance** explicitly documented.
- Retain: **$0.100 per GB per additional 30-day retention increment** beyond paid 30-day minimum. The example has **zero** extra increments.
- Query: assume **Q ≤ 100 × W** per stack, so no excess query volume. Otherwise the numerical overage rate was not found and a full total cannot be calculated.

Sources: https://grafana.com/pricing/ (checked 2026-11-05; source updated Not shown) ; https://grafana.com/docs/grafana-cloud/platform/pricing-and-usage/logs/ (checked 2026-11-05; source updated Not shown) ; https://grafana.com/docs/grafana-cloud/platform/cost-management-and-billing/analyze-costs/reduce-costs/logs-costs/control-query-usage-costs/ (checked 2026-11-05; source updated Not shown).

The literal billing-guide interpretation used for the first estimate charges all processing:

**Estimated monthly cost A = $19 + ($0.050 × P) + ($0.400 × max(W − 50, 0)) + $0 extra retention + $0 excess queries.**

Because the pricing card says 50 GB ingested is included, also show an explicit alternative assumption that the same first 50 GB are exempt from processing:

**Estimated monthly cost B = $19 + ($0.050 × max(P − 50, 0)) + ($0.400 × max(W − 50, 0)).**

B is an assumption for the unresolved allowance wording, not a second confirmed tariff. These two calculations are not a guaranteed invoice range; tiers, measured bytes and additional services can change the amount.

| Small | 5 GB/month | max(5 − 50, 0) = 0 | 500 GB/month | $19 + $0.25 + $0 = **$19.25/month** | $19 + $0 + $0 = **$19.00/month** |

The Pro platform fee is charged once in these examples, not once per product or app. One builder fits the included three visualization users. Raw byte intake, written bytes, query scan and compressed disk bytes are distinct measures.

**Exact totals are not established.** These use public rates labelled "starts at," assume no volume discount, and leave the processing-allowance ambiguity visible. Query usage is bounded explicitly for the example; actual query volume is otherwise unknown. ### Self-hosted logs

Software license charge is **zero**. Comparable complete cost is:

**Loki compute + durable chunks/index storage + collector + optional Grafana/Alertmanager + backup storage/operations + network and object-store request charges.**

Use the same raw 5/50/250 GB monthly input and 30-day retention. Daily raw averages are:

- 5 ÷ 30 = **1/6 GB/day**, approximately **0.17 GB/day**.
- 50 ÷ 30 = **5/3 GB/day**, approximately **1.67 GB/day**.
- 250 ÷ 30 = **25/3 GB/day**, approximately **8.33 GB/day**.

Decimal daily figures are explicitly rounded to two places. These averages fall below the documentation's rough **20 GB/day** monolithic guidance, but bursts, cardinality, query load, replicas and storage overhead still determine sizing. The official CPU/RAM table is for distributed clusters and does not establish the tiny instance needed for these scenarios.

An exact hosting bill therefore stays **unknown**. No arbitrary machine price, compression factor or replication assumption is inserted. Labor is excluded from infrastructure arithmetic but remains an operating cost. Configure 30-day retention deliberately, protect the API and plan independent backups; default indefinite retention is not a disk budget.

## Field problems

- **C01/C05/C06:** Loki, Grafana UI, Alloy, Cloud Logs and GEL are related components/offers, not interchangeable editions with one license and feature set.
- **C03/C26:** Collector EOL, community chart maintenance, commercial edition sunset and future major-version removal have different scopes. They should not become one "Loki is discontinued" flag.
- **C07/C11/L01/L03:** One "price per GB" field is inadequate for separate processing, writing, extra retention and query scanning. Allowances must identify their dimension; an unresolved allowance cannot produce an exact total.
- **C14:** Plan entitlement, operational retention default, query lookback and customer archive lifecycle need separate values. A storage default is not an extra contractual day.
- **C15:** Active users, tenants, streams, apps, dashboards and recipients are different units. Do not derive a project count from stream/cardinality limits.
- **C17/C18/C22:** Collection filters, backend alert evaluation, notification routing and paid incident response live in different components. Available integrations do not prove included delivery credits or automatic masking.
- **H02/H03:** A rough monolithic ingestion threshold is useful deployment guidance, not a CPU/RAM quote or a tested recovery guarantee.
- The approved states are used throughout, including for calculations whose exact total remains unresolved. fields.md was not edited.
