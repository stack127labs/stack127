# Auth apps: discovery list

Checked on 2026-11-04. This is a research candidate list, not a recommendation or completed app research.

Auth means signing people in and managing their app accounts. A library runs inside code you own. A server runs as a separate service. Hosted means the vendor operates it. “Server + hosted” includes both routes; the license still needs checking. A backend bundles login with other app services. A passkey uses a device or security key instead of a password. SSO means using one login across apps.

Each row has one primary type so counts do not overlap. Related modules share a row where they are one product family. Different managed Keycloak vendors remain separate purchases. Cross-directory notes mean “also consider there”; they do not mean the product processes payments itself.

- **active:** official product pages or project documentation were readable and still presented an auth offer. This does not prove signup, service uptime, security, or ongoing maintenance.
- **gray:** the site exists, but scope, packaging, solo-developer fit, or a known transition needs an owner decision.
- **check again:** an official source exists, but operating status or current integration details could not be confirmed well enough.

The date column records the public-source check. A “check again” date is an attempted confirmation, not a claim the service works. No accounts were created and no login flows were tested.

“Not checked” under region means headquarters, service availability, and data location were not researched. These are different facts. “You choose hosting” is a deployment note, not a headquarters claim.

Discovery codes link to the source and query log in [notes.md](notes.md#discovery-sources). E means a seed from the researcher's ecosystem knowledge followed by an official-site check; it is not an independent endorsement. The brief supplied some initial names. Competitor pages, reviews, and threads supplied names only. Inclusion reasons below come from official pages.
