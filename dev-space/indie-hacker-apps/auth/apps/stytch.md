## Cost examples — our calculation

These are our calculations, not quotes from Stytch. Checked **2026-11-04**. Prices use the original dollar amounts, treated as USD under the [current terms' invoicing default](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/legal/tos). No conversion or negotiated discount is applied.

The cheapest published route is **Pay as you go**. Use one production project and one developer seat. Each person has one account/member identity in this example. Users registered before the month and authenticate on at least two days. No AI agents, M2M tokens, device fingerprints, SCIM, SMS, WhatsApp or paid support. Branding stays standard. For the email/password-or-social choice, use existing social accounts so the baseline does not depend on an unverified per-email rate. For B2B organizations without SAML, use supported Google/Microsoft OAuth. Existing organization memberships are already in place: invitation capability is required, but this example sends no new invitation messages in the measured month.

**Input evidence:** the [pricing page](https://stytch.com/pricing) publishes the base, seats, connection allowance and overage; the [pricing-change notice](https://changelog.stytch.com/announcements/2024-11-22-updated-self-serve-pricing) explicitly names both products' 10,000-user allowance. [Role assignment](https://stytch.com/docs/multi-tenant-auth/enterprise-ready/rbac/assigning-roles-to-members), [B2B UI](https://stytch.com/docs/multi-tenant-auth/build-auth/overview) and [Admin Portal](https://stytch.com/admin-portal) establish the required organization/invitation/role capabilities. No paid authentication-feature tier is added.

Let U be the month's distinct billable active identities in the selected project. Let C be SSO plus SCIM connections. Let Rconsumer(n) be Stytch's current charge for n excess Consumer MAUs, including any actual volume brackets. That function could not be recovered from the interactive rate card; it is deliberately not replaced with an old advertised cents-per-user rate.

Known monthly formula:

`listed auth charge = $0 base + user overage + max(C − 5, 0) × $125 + $0 organization fee + selected compulsory add-ons`.

| Scenario | Inputs and selected route | Formula — our calculation | Result | Limits of result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Consumer 1,000 | Consumer Pay as you go; U=1,000; C=0 | $0 + $0 user overage + $0 connections + $0 selected add-ons | **$0/month listed auth charge** | Within the named free allowance; not a total app operating cost. |
| Consumer 10,000 | Consumer Pay as you go; U=10,000; C=0 | $0 + $0 + $0 + $0 | **$0/month listed auth charge** | Uses exactly the published allowance. |
| Consumer 50,000 | Consumer Pay as you go; U=50,000; C=0 | $0 + Rconsumer(50,000−10,000) | **Total unknown; 40,000 excess MAUs** | Current marginal rate/volume brackets were not readable. |
| Consumer 100,000 | Consumer Pay as you go; U=100,000; C=0 | $0 + Rconsumer(100,000−10,000) | **Total unknown; 90,000 excess MAUs** | Do not extrapolate a rate from a different product, edition or old announcement. |
| Business A | B2B Pay as you go; U=1,000; 10 orgs ×100 members; C=1 SAML; no SCIM | $0 + $0 users + max(1−5,0)×$125 + $0 orgs + $0 add-ons | **$0/month listed auth charge** | One member identity per person; all connections used for a full month. |
| Business B | B2B Pay as you go; U=10,000; 100 orgs ×100 members; C=10 SAML; no SCIM | $0 + $0 users + (10−5)×$125 + $0 orgs + $0 add-ons | **$625/month listed auth charge** | Five excess connections. No partial-month calculation or inactive-connection exemption assumed. |

These figures exclude taxes, the developer's hosting/database, domain registration, external observability storage, staff time and any external provider charges. They also exclude billable optional services because the scenarios do not use them. Standard Stytch email sending exists, but a separate public email rate/allowance was not established; using the chosen social-login path makes no claim that every possible email flow costs zero. Adding $99/month branding or $1,000/month Premium support is optional. Enterprise pricing is quote-only; no annual-prepayment discount is silently used.

The advertised unit is monthly active identity, not login requests. The full rules for multiple projects, repeated human identities across organizations, agent activity and shared allowances remain unresolved. These baselines avoid those cases. They must not be used as a general billing estimator for arbitrary account layouts.

- **F08 and F11:** the text view of an interactive calculator is incomplete. A readable zero-base card cannot establish a marginal usage rate. Keep separately the known allowance, the failed numeric-rate extraction and the calculable excess quantity.
