## Cost examples: our calculation

These are our calculations, not quoted customer bills. The consumer baseline is one production app, one developer dashboard seat, existing users returning on at least two days, with social login and no customer organizations, SSO, SMS or paid extra security. Google is chosen. Email delivery is not compulsory for that chosen sign-in method; if email verification, recovery, invitations or email MFA are added, the builder's SMTP bill is separate.

The known identity meter is $0 per active user. The platform still bills database resources shared with the rest of the app. Use Q for the workspace's measured monthly query tokens and S for billable storage in the price card's GB units. The baseline does not establish Q, S, storage averaging, concurrency, or application workload.

- Free: $0 + max(Q − 50,000,000, 0) / 1,000,000 × $0.06 + max(S − 1 GB, 0) × $1.50/GB.
- Pro: $15 + max(Q − 150,000,000, 0) / 1,000,000 × $0.05 + max(S − 5 GB, 0) × $1.00/GB.
- Scale: $350 + max(Q − 500,000,000, 0) / 1,000,000 × $0.04 + max(S − 50 GB, 0) × $0.75/GB.

Rate inputs checked 2026-11-05: https://github.com/niledatabase/niledatabase/blob/main/www/app/pricing/_components/Free.tsx, https://github.com/niledatabase/niledatabase/blob/main/www/app/pricing/_components/Pro.tsx, https://github.com/niledatabase/niledatabase/blob/main/www/app/pricing/_components/Scale.tsx, https://github.com/niledatabase/niledatabase/blob/main/www/app/pricing/_components/faqs.tsx.
