## Field problems

- **Dates and evidence status need room for conflicts.** F48 has a concrete source-date mismatch and a conditional policy extension. Stable-tag source facts remain confirmed where they can be read directly. No runtime compatibility or security guarantee was invented.
- **Optional assurance and accessibility searches are bounded.** A public policy or form template cannot replace an independent report. Sources checked for the seven not_published rows are listed above.
