Ory Network is the vendor-hosted service. Kratos is the account/login server, Hydra is the OAuth2/OIDC token server, and Polis is the enterprise SSO broker. Apache-licensed core and commercial Ory Enterprise License (OEL) features are separated below. Signup and running deployments were not tested.

## Cost examples: our calculations

Prices preserve the vendor's $ notation. No currency conversion, startup discount, negotiated discount or tax assumption is applied. These are calculated authentication subtotals, not guaranteed invoices or whole-app running costs.

### Inputs and arithmetic

The public monthly Production base is $70. The pricing page initially renders its annual $770 value; its embedded data explicitly contains `monthly: 70` and `yearly: 770`. Growth's embedded card and both comparison contexts show $9,350/year; this research did not find a separately purchasable monthly Growth base. Checked 2026-11-04: https://www.ory.com/pricing.

Production's credit is $21/month, shared across service usage. Its rates are $0.14/aDAU, $0.007/client-credentials token and $0.000090/permission check. Credit applies to usage, not the base fee, and unused credit expires. Usage is billed monthly even if the base is annual. Checked 2026-11-04: https://www.ory.com/pricing and https://www.ory.com/docs/console/usage-billing.

Our formula for a full-month Production subscription is:

```text
aDAU = sum of daily unique active users / days in the month
usage = $0.14 × aDAU + $0.007 × M2M tokens + $0.000090 × permission checks
calculated monthly auth subtotal = $70 + max(0, usage − $21)
```

The fixed consumer baseline says each existing user returns on at least two days. That does not settle aDAU. For the priced sub-scenario, every user uses Identities APIs on exactly two distinct days in a 30-day month, with no activity on other days, no extra accounts, no client-credentials tokens and no permission checks. This is an explicit lower-activity example, not a universal price for that MAU count. Login, recovery and session/API activity on additional days may increase the meter. Definition checked 2026-11-04: https://www.ory.com/pricing.

Traffic is spread across the month so all shared rate buckets are respected, including Production's POST-login bucket of 60/minute and 10/second, and session-check bucket of 1,400/minute and 134/second. Monthly user counts do not establish peak capacity. The highest example needs 200,000 user-days spread across the month, rather than all 100,000 users arriving together on two days. Limits checked 2026-11-04: https://www.ory.com/docs/guides/rate-limits-project and https://www.ory.com/docs/rate-limits.json.

Production is the cheapest published monthly plan supporting this consumer configuration. Its calculated auth subtotals are also below Growth's $9,350/12 annual-base equivalent at all four example counts, before adding Growth usage. The Developer tier cannot be substituted because it has no production environment. The annual-base division is our comparison arithmetic, not a monthly Growth purchase offer. Checked 2026-11-04: https://www.ory.com/pricing.
