## Cost examples: our calculation

These compare the current publicly listed Outseta platform bill. They are not total website or business running costs. **Currency remains the page's original $ symbol; an explicit ISO invoice currency was not established from the subscription pricing/terms.** No currency conversion is applied.

All four consumer cases use one production app, one developer owner, monthly billing and email/password or Google. Every person registered before the month and returns on at least two days. Because Outseta charges for contacts, the explicit sub-scenario is **exactly N total stored contacts, with no additional inactive users, leads or newsletter-only contacts**. Each member has a free product and the business processes **$0 membership revenue** during the month.

Formula: **monthly platform tier + successful membership payment volume × Outseta transaction percentage + compulsory extras**. At zero payment revenue, percentage transaction charges are zero. No billing period is converted into MAU pricing and no historical plan, annual discount, referral offer or negotiated rate is substituted.

Included in the plan presentation: authentication, CRM/email tools, developer seats, API/webhooks and human support. No mandatory recurring message volume follows from the returning-password/social-login inputs. The actual transactional send allowance and overage rate were not found, so no invented per-email rate is added. Passwordless/MFA campaigns would need their own message-volume assumptions.

Excluded: applicable taxes, website/Webflow/WordPress hosting, custom application/backend/database, domain registration, external auth providers, optional automation and developer time. Stripe processing and the v3 announcement's additional Stripe Billing subscription percentage matter if the app collects revenue; they do not produce a revenue-based charge in these zero-revenue examples.

The business cases preserve the requested organizations, memberships, roles and incoming customer SAML. A contact-only platform base is shown as a known component, never as the complete usable business price. Public evidence does not establish a native incoming-SAML offer; the documented custom external-auth path has an unknown provider/integration cost.
