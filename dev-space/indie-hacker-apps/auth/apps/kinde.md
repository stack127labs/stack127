- F13 must separate ordinary organizations from advanced organizations and unique organization-scoped roles. Basic role assignments/invitations should not force an unnecessary higher plan.
- F20/F38: fully anonymous signup and custom application guest state are different; absence of a guest-upgrade guide is not proof every custom flow is impossible.
- F23/F29/F30 combine features whose documentation has different apparent freshness. Record conflicts at feature level, not one blanket support yes.
- F35 does not apply to the managed auth server; client SDK operations are separate.
- F45 needs endpoint and concurrency scope; no single numerical rate could be established from the public policy.
- F48 requires a per-SDK release, not a single version for the whole hosted product.
