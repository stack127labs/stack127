## Field problems

- Hosted trials, seats/projects, MAU quotas, residency plans and uptime SLAs do not fit this embedded library. Their not_applicable rows describe scope, not missing hosted-product research.
- Goth's `Provider`, provider `Session`, `gothic.Store` and the application's own signed-in account session are different things. Combining them would overstate persistence, logout and device control.
- Bundled provider adapters can differ materially. Google refresh support, GitHub verified-private-email retrieval, microsoftonline refresh constraints and Azure tenant selection are recorded separately.
- A project README/provider list can lag code or upstream services. Deprecated Google+ is not treated as current Google support. No provider count, live compatibility or official identity-service endorsement is inferred.
- The fixed business baseline requires SAML plus account organization features. Generic OIDC/custom interfaces do not satisfy it. Free software cost also does not settle infrastructure or developer work.
- The allowed six states override older state words in fields.md. Cost calculations use confirmed for the known software subtotal and not_applicable for unsupported complete business scenarios.
