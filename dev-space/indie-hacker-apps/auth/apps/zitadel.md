## Cost examples — our calculations

All inputs checked **2026-11-04**. These are our calculations from published units, not vendor quotes. Full fixed baselines cannot produce one exact total because they specify **at least two active days**, rather than an exact number of billable days, and do not settle management API traffic or service-user activity.

### Billing inputs and formula

ZITADEL's **monthly sum of DAUs** is the sum of unique active users on every day, including token refreshes and service-account authentication. It is not average daily users and is not MAU. A user authenticating on two separate days contributes two units. Source: https://help.zitadel.com/how-is-daily-active-users-calculated (checked 2026-11-04).

For Cloud Pro, let:

- **D** = total monthly billable user-days, including any additional service-user days.
- **I** = monthly rounded-up average of activated external IdPs across instances.
- **R** = billable authenticated management requests.
- **A** = billed customer Portal administrator seats.
- **C** = custom instance domains.
- **X** = 1 when the optional extended support/SLA package is bought, otherwise 0.

Our calculation from the published calculator:

`Pro monthly auth subtotal = US$100 + US$50 × max(0, ceil((D − 25,000) / 2,500)) + US$100 × max(0, ceil((I − 3) / 5)) + US$100 × max(0, ceil((R − 500,000) / 500,000)) + US$20 × max(0, A − 3) + US$50 × max(0, C − 1) + US$999 × X.`

The base, domains and support package are upfront; DAUs, IdPs, management requests and administrators are usage-based at period end. Each excess block is rounded up. Sources: https://zitadel.com/pricing/detail, https://zitadel.com/_next/static/immutable/chunks/1rrc1d59tf8y6.js, https://help.zitadel.com/pricing-and-billing-of-zitadel-services, https://help.zitadel.com/what-is-an-external-identity-provider, https://help.zitadel.com/management-api-requests-billing (checked 2026-11-04).

The actual official calculator was not submitted or manipulated. Its public JavaScript was read to resolve the hidden block sizes and rounding. The current source specifies **2,500 DAUs/block**, **5 external IdPs/block** and **500,000 management requests/block**. It replaces totals above **US$3,000/month** with a sales-quote prompt; we preserve that condition rather than calling its hidden arithmetic a confirmed offer.

All four baseline groups have one production app and one developer Portal seat. Users registered before the month and return on at least two days. No customer organizations, business SSO, SMS or paid extra security.

The following **priced sub-scenario** fixes the missing usage inputs:

- Each existing user has authentication/refresh activity on **exactly two days** during the billing month, with no other active days.
- No additional service-user or administrative authentication days are added. If the chosen integration creates those, include them in D; the table is conditional on this input.
- Total billable management API traffic stays **within 500,000/month**. Native OAuth/OIDC traffic and billable Session/User/Admin API traffic must be measured separately.
- Use ordinary existing-user email/password login, with no new signup, reset, contact verification or message-triggering account changes in the measured month.
- Production SMTP is configured externally. Pro includes one custom domain; no extra domain or support package is purchased.

Pro is the lowest publicly priced plan covering the stated user-day volumes and production-sender setup.
