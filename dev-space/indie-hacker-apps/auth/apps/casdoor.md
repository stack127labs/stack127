## Cost examples — our calculation

All six are our calculations from public inputs, not vendor quotes or a live checkout. Use one continuously running production product app and one builder. All users registered before the month and return on at least two days. Consumer scenarios use email/password, without customer organizations, business SSO, SMS or paid security. Standard Cloud subscriptions charge per instance, with no per-MAU/per-connection charge. The larger published MAU figures are guidance, never throttled, and do not establish measured capacity.

There is no perpetual free Cloud plan. The US$4/30-day trial credit is excluded. Month-to-month subscriptions are used; annual totals US$80/US$200/US$450 and their rounded monthly headlines are not substituted. PAYG depends on running minutes and is shown separately in F11. Taxes and external email/SMS/IdP fees are excluded. The existing-password return pattern does not require a message at every login; recovery, changed contact details, mailed invitations and optional MFA can add delivery costs.

Eligibility caveat: the published Basic/Premium organization caps do not explain whether the mandatory `built-in` administrator organization consumes a slot. Ordinary app users belong to another organization. We therefore show the exact known Basic/Premium billing arithmetic conditionally, rather than assume an exemption. Enterprise has unlimited organizations and supplies a public US$59/month alternative. The business baseline uses separate accounts per customer organization; it does not require one user account to join several organizations.

For all six self-host scenarios, the Apache core software license charge is $0 and native account/organization/role/invitation/SSO capabilities are documented. SQL/compute/storage/SMTP/Redis where needed, backups, provider bills, support and operator work are additional and unestimated. $0 software is never presented as a $0 running total.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Lifecycle check came first.** Searched official domains for shutdown, sunset, retirement and acquisition; opened [home](https://casdoor.ai/), [cloudHome](https://www.casdoor.com/), [blog](https://www.casdoor.com/blog), [readme](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/casdoor/casdoor/master/README.md), [repo](https://api.github.com/repos/casdoor/casdoor) and [release](https://api.github.com/repos/casdoor/casdoor/releases/latest). No Casdoor product shutdown/sunset/acquisition notice was found. Repository metadata says not archived/disabled and v4.25.0 is recent. The old [oldDomain](https://casdoor.org/) redirects to casdoor.ai. Privacy language about a hypothetical future merger/acquisition is not evidence that one happened. The README does explicitly say the previous Ant Design console in web-old is no longer built/served; the current frontend replaces it.
- **Organization quota accounting affects the cheapest exact scenario.** [core](https://casdoor.ai/docs/basic/core-concepts/) says built-in is mandatory and cannot be deleted, and ordinary app users are in other organizations. [pricing](https://www.casdoor.com/pricing) advertises Basic 1 / Premium 10 / Enterprise unlimited organizations; [b2b](https://www.casdoor.com/blog/multi-tenant-authentication) repeats the customer-tenant model but does not define whether built-in consumes a quota slot. Read those sources plus [org](https://casdoor.ai/docs/organization/overview/), [sharedApp](https://casdoor.ai/docs/application/shared-application/) and [invite](https://casdoor.ai/docs/invitation/overview/). We do not invent an internal-object exemption. Consumer Basic billing arithmetic is $9 at every user count and business A Premium arithmetic is $25, but capped-plan eligibility remains unclear. Enterprise at $59 is a published unlimited-org alternative; business B therefore has an exact published subscription result. No member-per-org quota was found either; no unstated extra-member tariff is added.
