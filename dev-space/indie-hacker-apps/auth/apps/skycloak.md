## Cost examples: our calculation

These are **our calculations**, not vendor quotes or a cluster-capacity test. They preserve the six inputs in fields.md and monthly USD billing.

The billing formula is:

**Monthly hosted fee = base subscription + additional cluster slots by size + US$49 × extra custom domains + selected paid add-ons.**

There is no user/MAU multiplier or separate native SAML/organization multiplier. Price sources: https://skycloak.io/pricing/; https://skycloak.io/docs/getting-started/subscription-plans/. Checked 2026-11-04.

Common assumptions and limits:

- One production app and one builder seat. All users existed before the month and return on at least two days. No SMS, SCIM, paid extra security, optional custom JAR or extra domain is required for the password/social baseline.
- The **Small-fit sub-scenario** assumes one included Small cluster can hold the accounts and serve their actual traffic and configuration. Under that assumption the cheapest published native-core subscription is Developer at US$29/month. Unlimited users does not establish unlimited concurrent requests, memory, database size or resilience.
- The fixed user counts do not provide peak requests, concurrency, session/token workload or resource sizing. Cluster docs call Small a one-site development size, Medium a two-site staging size and Large a three-site production recommendation. They do not supply an enforceable user-count-to-size table. No prohibition on all production use of Developer was found, but capacity for these baselines was not established. Sources checked: https://skycloak.io/docs/features/clusters/; https://skycloak.io/docs/features/billing/; https://skycloak.io/docs/faqs/; https://skycloak.io/acceptable-use-policy/. Checked 2026-11-04.
- Therefore the primary baseline totals remain **unclear**, with a known US$29 base-fee candidate and an explicit conditional Small-fit result. The table does not present US$29 as a verified usable 100,000-user production configuration or silently use Launch's marketing comparison as proof.
- Business A/B require native Organizations enabled, member invitations and role mappings, plus application enforcement of the organization context. The vendor organization guide demonstrates 26.2.4 membership/IdP routing; the versioned upstream 26.8.1 guide explicitly documents organization-group roles/invitations. Current deployable versions are behind the management API's key requirement, so the complete configuration/version must be checked before treating the candidate plan as usable. Sources: https://skycloak.io/blog/multitenancy-in-keycloak-using-the-organizations-feature/; https://www.keycloak.org/docs/26.8.1/server_admin/index.html; https://skycloak.io/docs/api/cluster-versions/. Checked 2026-11-04.
- Builder workspace invitations are not counted as the customer's 10/100 organizations. Each business scenario uses customer organizations in one realm, rather than 10/100 paid clusters. No SCIM or native per-SAML connection fee is added.
- SMTP and invitation/reset/verification delivery remain separate. Email/SMTP docs show own-provider configuration but no universal free app-user send allowance. The baseline does not specify sends or provider; it cannot produce an exact external email bill. Existing password/social logins do not inherently require a fresh reset/invitation message every return. Sources: https://skycloak.io/docs/features/email/; https://skycloak.io/docs/guides/smtp-configuration/. Checked 2026-11-04.
