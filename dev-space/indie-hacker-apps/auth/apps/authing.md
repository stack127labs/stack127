Authing has a public free developer offer and paid customer-authentication plans. It also sells workforce identity and licensed private deployment. This record focuses on hosted customer accounts, with private deployment recorded where the fields require it. Signup, payment and provisioning were not tested.

The Chinese original pages were read. The pricing page's public JavaScript bundle supplies its interactive B2C/B2B capacity prices and plan-card text. Several plan rights conflict with the comparison table. Those contradictions remain visible below. Amounts retain the site's ¥ notation; no currency conversion, tax assumption or annual discount is applied.
