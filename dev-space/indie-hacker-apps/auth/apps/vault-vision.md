Vault Vision publicly offers hosted authentication for custom apps and Webflow, a development sandbox, monthly paid plans and a quoted Enterprise offer. Public docs and code remain available, but their dated maintenance evidence is old. No shutdown notice was found on the pages read. Signup, checkout and production provisioning were not tested.

The actual **Pricing** navigation link leads to the homepage's plan section, so that homepage is the exact pricing source. Current cards say Launch $25/month for up to 2,000 users and Growth $95/month for up to 100,000. Older official articles describe different Professional-plan limits. “Users” is not publicly defined as MAU. The six cost examples retain that uncertainty and show what can be calculated under an explicit total-registered-user sub-scenario.

## Cost examples — our calculation

All four consumer baselines use one production app, one developer dashboard owner, password login, and already-registered users who return on at least two days in the month. They have no customer organizations, business SSO, SMS or paid security add-ons. Registration and initial verification occurred before the modeled month. Ordinary password returns do not themselves require an email challenge; optional resets, verification and MFA sends are excluded. No public required production sender fee was established.

Because the card never defines its counted user population, each consumer row adds a controlled sub-scenario: one tenant, exactly the stated number of total registered users, no other inactive/stored users and no extra tenants. This shows the published subscription component without assuming that inactive users are free. It does not establish an exact invoice for any app with only its MAU count known.

All examples use monthly dollar prices as printed, without currency conversion, grants, trial entitlements, negotiated discounts or assumed annual savings. Taxes, the builder's app hosting, optional sender services, external Webflow/Airtable/Stripe charges and staff time are separate and not estimated. Business examples require membership, invitations and roles in addition to SAML. A separate authentication tenant is not automatically a complete customer-organization feature.
