## Cost examples — our calculation

Consumer baseline: one production app, one builder, existing distinct users who log in during the month and return on at least two days. Password login uses an app-written user lookup/endpoint, the module's Scrypt verification and session helper. No customer organizations, business SSO, SMS or paid extra security.

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: MIT module licence, https://github.com/atinux/nuxt-auth-utils/blob/v0.6.1/LICENSE ; password helpers, https://github.com/atinux/nuxt-auth-utils/blob/v0.6.1/src/runtime/server/utils/password.ts ; session helpers, https://github.com/atinux/nuxt-auth-utils/blob/v0.6.1/src/runtime/server/utils/session.ts ; module requirements, https://github.com/atinux/nuxt-auth-utils/blob/v0.6.1/README.md .

Let H be Nuxt server/compute/network cost, D the persistent user/credential database and any optional challenge/revocation store, E configured delivery cost, and O backups/monitoring/operations. **Total = 0 module licence charge + H + D + E + O.** The comparison inputs do not determine those operating charges. No deployment benchmark, currency conversion or hosting/developer-time estimate is made.

“0” means no module licence charge, not a published hosted plan in an invented currency. Account recovery, email verification and notifications require additional app/provider work. Optional passkeys need credential storage and peer dependencies; replay-resistant challenges require app-managed storage/consumption.
