## Cost examples — our calculation

All six examples use the approved `fields.md` baseline: one production app, one builder seat, existing registered users returning on at least two days in the month. Every listed user is an MAU under Descope's definition. Consumer examples have no customer tenants or business SSO. Business A has 10 active customer tenants with 100 members each and one configured SAML connection. Business B has 100 active tenants with 100 members each and 10 configured SAML connections. Both require memberships, invitations and basic roles; neither needs SCIM, FGA, the hosted Admin Portal or the paid SSO Setup Suite.

**Billing basis:** paid figures below are monthly equivalents under annual billing, not independently purchasable month-to-month subscriptions. Pro's annual base is **12 × $249 = $2,988**. Growth's annual base is **12 × $799 = $9,588**. A public monthly-only price was not located. All dollar figures are **USD**, before tax. These are calculations from public standard rates, not an invoice quote. Sources, checked **2026-11-04**: https://www.descope.com/pricing ; https://www.descope.com/terms.

**Pricing conflict:** Pro's plan card and detailed rate table both say **10,000 MAUs**, while the same page's overage FAQ example says **7,500**. Main calculations use the matching card/table pair. The alternative FAQ reading is shown below so the discrepancy is not silently resolved. No order or private billing screen was available to establish the actual contracted allowance. Source, checked **2026-11-04**: https://www.descope.com/pricing.

Use the builder's own production Google/OAuth app for ordinary users. Descope's default Google app is testing-only, not an allowed production dependency. Social sign-in does not require a delivered Descope email in this steady-state baseline. For the SAML users, use the specified customer connections. Sources, checked **2026-11-04**: https://docs.descope.com/auth-methods/oauth ; https://docs.descope.com/auth-methods/sso ; https://docs.descope.com/how-to-deploy-to-production.

The formula for the two public paid tiers is:

`base + max(0, MAU − included MAU) × $0.05 + max(0, active tenants − included tenants) × $1 + max(0, configured SSO connections − included SSO connections) × $50`

Inputs from the plan cards/rate table: Pro **$249 / 10,000 MAU / 35 tenants / 5 SSO**; Growth **$799 / 25,000 MAU / 100 tenants / 10 SSO**. Free supports **7,500 MAU / 10 active tenants / 3 SSO** without overages. These are separate meters. All quantities refer to a month's steady usage, except that every configured SSO connection counts even if idle. Source, checked **2026-11-04**: https://www.descope.com/pricing.
