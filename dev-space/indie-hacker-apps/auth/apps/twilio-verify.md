## Cost examples — our calculations

The four consumer baselines use one app and one builder, with existing users returning on at least two days, email/password or social login, no SMS, no organizations and no SSO. Verify alone cannot support these whole-account baselines. Both business baselines are also unsupported. They are recorded explicitly rather than assigned a misleading zero price.

For the separate **US SMS** illustration, our added assumptions are: **2 approved OTP checks per user per month; 1 outbound single-segment SMS per check; 100% completion; no resends or fallback; standard pay-as-you-go; steady-state use after one-time credits are consumed**. Two checks is an explicit modelling choice, not something inferred from two returning days. The app/account/session service is external and unpriced. ## Field problems

- **F02, F12–F15, F21, F25–F28:** the approved scope is a verification add-on. Account-system scenarios, social providers, app sessions, business workspaces and SSO/SCIM prices cannot be filled as if Verify were a full identity platform. Those conditional product fields are not applicable. This does not make hosted commercial/operational questions inapplicable.
- **F08, F11, F17:** a user, a verification, a message attempt, an SMS segment and a successful check are different units. Custom-code attempts, Push denials and SNA carrier-completed mismatches have different charging rules. Any later cost interface needs those inputs and a country/channel/carrier distinction.
- **F16, F27, F40:** Twilio staff organizations/users and account-user import are infrastructure administration, not customer organizations or app-user migration. Verify Entity IDs/factors have a separate purpose. API pagination is not a total Service/user cap.
- **F39–F42, F46:** short-lived verification resources, report lookup windows, minimum PII time-to-live, content deletion, billing retention, backups and regional workload processing are separate promises. A single retention or EU-residency checkbox would hide material limits.
- **F47–F49:** API credits, carrier delivery, preview exclusions and support response targets have different scopes. Enhanced paid-edition SLA and premium support prices must not be presented as standard Verify guarantees.
- **apps.md:** no factual row correction identified. The existing country/channel warning remains useful; a complete universal country-eligibility matrix was not established.
