## Cost examples — our calculations

The consumer baseline requires one production app, one builder and **1,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 / 100,000 existing monthly users**, returning on at least two days, using email/password or social login without SMS. Verify alone cannot supply that account/session baseline. The two business baselines also require organizations, membership, invitations, roles and SAML that this add-on does not supply. They are **unsupported**, not zero-cost plans.

For a separate **US SMS verification illustration**, our explicit assumptions are: **2 successful OTP checks/user/month; 1 outbound single-segment SMS/check; all users complete; no retries, fallbacks or failed attempts; default Verify Conversion; one app and one builder; trial credit already used**. Two checks is our modelling choice, not something implied by two returning days.

Let U = users; S = successful checks = 2U; M = outbound single-segment US SMS attempts = 2U. Let r = the account's US SMS delivery rate and C = applicable carrier/related charges. Neither r nor the mapping of the actual managed Verify sender to public carrier tables was established.

**Our calculation:**

- USD platform subtotal = **S × 0.06084 = U × 0.12168**.
- EUR platform subtotal = **S × 0.052 = U × 0.104**.
- Full Conversion bill = platform subtotal + M × account's US SMS rate + applicable carrier/related charges + taxes and any chosen paid extras.
- **Exact full total remains unknown.** The published 'as low as' US LVN SMS headline is not substituted as a guaranteed Verify route price. Trial credits, negotiated discounts, optional support/reporting, external account-platform fees and developer time are not silently deducted or estimated.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Trial recipient limit:** account support says registered number plus **4 additional test numbers**; general Messages onboarding says **up to10 destinations** in DEMO mode. - **Redact versus Auto-redact price labels:** technical docs call **Redact API free**, while automatic redaction is paid.
