## Cost examples: our calculation

All inputs and source pages below were checked 2026-11-04 UTC. These are our calculations, not sales quotes. Original dollar symbols and monthly units are preserved; the public tariff does not explicitly identify its ISO currency. The US$ liability-cap clause in the terms is not used to fill that gap.

For each scenario there is one production app and one builder dashboard seat. Every distinct app user registered before the month and returns on at least two days. No paid extra security, SMS, machine-to-machine tokens or SCIM is added. Consumer uses email/password or social; business requires native organization membership, invitations, basic roles and the specified SAML connections. No custom-role design is required.
