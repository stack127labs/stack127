# Databases and backend: candidate apps

Research date: 2026-11-04. Step 1 only: candidate discovery and official-site checks. Prices and detailed features are not finished research.

211 candidates: 155 active, 55 gray, 1 check again. This is a broad discovery inventory, not a claim that every product worldwide has been found.

## How to read this list

- **active**: the official page or official repository opened and still presents usable software or a service. This is a public-site check, not a signup, purchase, uptime test, or recommendation.
- **gray**: the official page opened, but category fit, maintenance, licensing, or a lasting solo-builder entry needs a decision or more research.
- **check again**: direct verification was blocked, or a current issue needs another check. These rows must not enter a public active count yet.
- The date is the date of this research session. For check-again rows it records an attempt, not a successful confirmation. Search snippets alone do not qualify as direct confirmation.
- One row per selectable product or closely related product family. A multi-engine provider stays one row; named storage, vector, and workflow products with separate jobs can have separate rows. Do not describe this count as a count of unique companies.
- A database **engine** is the software that stores and queries data. Self-hosting means you run and maintain it yourself. A library runs inside your own app.
- Home markets are left unknown unless the opened official page states something useful. Company location, database regions, and a legal promise to keep data in a country are different facts.
- Discovery codes link to the source log in [notes.md](notes.md#discovery-sources). **P** = task prompt; **K** = known-name coverage seed, then official-site check; **O** = official link, redirect, or related official product. The other codes name independent discovery sources. They do not prove competitor facts.
- The reason column is a short scope decision, based on the linked official page. It is not a finished feature comparison. Lifecycle evidence is linked in [notes.md](notes.md#shutdowns-rebrands-and-acquisitions).
