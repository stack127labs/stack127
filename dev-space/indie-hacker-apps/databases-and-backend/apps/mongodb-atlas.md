## Shutdown, sunset, acquisition and plan changes

Checked 2026-11-05. Atlas Core remains a current public cloud offer. No Atlas-wide shutdown or acquisition notice was found in the official product, pricing, retirement, terms and status pages checked; this is a bounded search result, not a promise about future availability. [Product](https://www.mongodb.com/products/platform/atlas-database), [Pricing](https://www.mongodb.com/pricing), [Cloud Status](https://status.mongodb.com/).

**M2, M5 and Serverless support ended January 22, 2026**, after new creation stopped in February 2025. Existing deployments were migrated to Free/Flex/Dedicated according to the migration rules. **App Services reached EOL September 30, 2025**, including Device Sync/SDKs, Data API, GraphQL, Static Hosting and HTTPS Endpoints; its app-user authentication/Rules stack must not be included in this listing. Database/scheduled triggers and their Functions survive. The pricing page still contains legacy tier/App Services rows. [Current tier notice](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/billing/atlas-flex-costs/), [dated App Services notice](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/api/doc/atlas-app-services-admin-api-v3/), [deprecation scope](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/app-services/deprecation/), [stale pricing tables](https://www.mongodb.com/pricing).

Other lifecycle points: Atlas Infinite is **public preview**, Free backup subscriptions are **private preview**, and Automated Embedding docs contain a preview/non-production warning. The legacy BI Connector reached EOL in **September 2026**. MongoDB's official annual filing reports discontinued Atlas Data Lake support; no exact retirement date is assigned here. [Infinite](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/infinite/atlas-infinite-landing/), [Free backups](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/backup/cloud-backup/free-cluster-backup/), [Automated Embedding](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/vector-search/indexes/vector-search-type.md), [BI Connector replacement/lifecycle](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/sql-interface/), [annual filing](https://investors.mongodb.com/static-files/2e35ada9-36d6-4ea2-a03a-bbba3b559770). All checked 2026-11-05.

## Cost examples — our calculation

All examples are our calculations, checked 2026-11-05. These are proposed workloads, not measured benchmarks. Currency is USD for direct self-service billing. The comparison deployment is **AWS us-east-1 (Northern Virginia)**. Flex rates are the vendor's generic schedule for supported AWS/Azure/GCP regions, applied to this verified location; they are not a separately quoted local tariff. Taxes, paid support and optional services are excluded unless stated. Sources: [Flex rates](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/billing/atlas-flex-costs/), [region support](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/reference/amazon-aws/), [USD billing](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/reference/alert-conditions/), [fees/taxes](https://www.mongodb.com/legal/terms-and-conditions/cloud).

### Small document app and idle project

Inputs: one production database, 1 GB total stored including normal indexes for 730 hours, 100,000 reads, 20,000 writes, 10 peak connections and 5 GB internet output. Free cannot hold 1 GB. Flex has 5 GB capacity and 500 connections; assume this workload stays within the base 100-ops/sec tier throughout each billed hour. Monthly request totals alone cannot prove that performance assumption.

Formula: 730 hours × USD $0.0110/hour = **USD $8.03 Flex cluster subtotal**. Outgoing cluster transfer adds $0 under the Flex inclusion. The published nominal $8/month label is retained in the facts table; it is not exactly $0.0110 × 730. If utilization enters higher tiers, use each tier's actual billed hours; the cluster charge is capped at $30/month. This ceiling excludes independently billed triggers, model inference, support and other services. Sources: [Free capacity](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/reference/free-shared-limitations/), [Flex limits](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/reference/flex-limitations/), [Flex billing](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/billing/atlas-flex-costs/).

Idle variant: retained 1 GB, no connections or operations, 730 hours. Flex cannot pause, so the same formula gives **$8.03 cluster subtotal**. Free's zero-dollar storage allowance would still be too small. Eligible Dedicated clusters can pause, retaining storage charges, then automatically resume after 30 days; an indefinitely free paid archive is not supported. [Pause rules](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/pause-terminate-cluster/).

Backup/HA variants: Flex has three-node replication and eight retained daily snapshots; these are distinct forms of protection. Its backup guide does not give a separate Flex snapshot tariff, so no unverified backup line is added to the subtotal. The optional Free-backup preview has formula min(30 days × $0.10/day, $3 monthly cap) = **$3** for an eligible smaller Free cluster; it does not make the 1-GB example eligible for Free. Sources: [Flex backup](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/backup/cloud-backup/flex-cluster-backup/), [Free backup preview](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/backup/cloud-backup/free-cluster-backup/), [backup tariff](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/billing/additional-services.md).

Dedicated paid path: an exact regional total is incomplete. Formula is **730 × p(AWS us-east-1, tier/configuration) + configured-storage charges + backup + route-specific transfer + optional services**. The general pricing card's $0.08/hour and $56.94/month do not name a cloud/region, and the read-only calculator did not expose a computed regional quote. They are not used to manufacture a Northern Virginia total. For an AWS us-east-1 snapshot amount B measured in GB-days, the published conversion gives **B × ($0.14 × 12/365)**; B cannot be inferred from 1 GB of logical data because snapshot volume/history differ. Sources: [calculator/public region metadata](https://www.mongodb.com/pricing/calculator), [configuration and backup units](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/billing/cluster-configuration-costs.md), [unlocated headline price](https://www.mongodb.com/pricing).

Optional private-network variant, AWS us-east-1 only: 1 endpoint × 730 hours × $0.0450/hour = **$32.85 Atlas endpoint subtotal**, plus $0.0120 × capacity units and AWS's own interface-endpoint charges. This is not part of the public-network Flex estimate, and Flex does not support private endpoints. [PrivateLink rates](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/billing/additional-services.md).

### Small job workload

Original inputs: 10,000 jobs/month, each 1 second active, 0.5 vCPU and 0.5 GB memory, peak 5 concurrent, no waiting/retries. **The workload does not fit the 350-MB Function cap**, and Atlas does not provide a selectable 0.5-vCPU trigger worker. An exact comparable total is therefore unavailable. [Function limits](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/atlas-ui/triggers/functions/).

Adapted illustration only: 10,000 trigger executions, 1 second each, measured at 256 MB, no retry/wait or external output. Formula: 10,000 × 1,000 ms × (256/32) = **80,000,000 weighted compute-ms = 22.222… compute hours**. Even if all run in one day, these counts are below the project's daily 25-hour and 50,000-request allowances, provided no other project usage consumes them. Trigger request/compute charge is then **$0**; cluster costs and external services remain separate. After the daily allowance is unavailable, the published per-ms formula would give **80,000,000 × $0.000000005 = $0.40 compute**, plus 10,000/1,000,000 × $2 = **$0.02 requests** in the first marginal request tier. The bulk-hour price is not exactly equivalent, so $0.42 is labelled formula-based arithmetic, not a reconciled invoice guarantee. Transfer would add the applicable daily excess × $0.12/GB. Rates have no cloud/region differentiation in the trigger guide. [current daily allowances/rates](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/billing/triggers/).

Retry/wait variant: one additional full execution per event doubles those inputs to 20,000 requests and 160,000,000 weighted ms, or 44.444… compute hours. If all occur on one otherwise-unused day, requests remain below 50,000 while compute exceeds 25 hours by 19.444… hours. Using the per-ms formula, max(160,000,000 − 25 × 3,600,000, 0) × $0.000000005 = **$0.35 compute arithmetic**. This assumes every retry actually executes in full; no universal retry count/duration contract is established. Waiting can change measured runtime or hit the 300-second cap, so no zero-cost durable-wait behavior is assumed. Daily distribution matters; monthly counts cannot be offset against a monthly free pool. [formula/free tier](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/billing/triggers/), [failure/resume behavior](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/atlas-ui/triggers/database-triggers.md).
