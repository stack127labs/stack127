### Attachment adaptation of the file workload

Cloudant is not a general object store, but it supports small document attachments. An adaptation of the approved workload would hold **100 GB total billed document/index/attachment data for 30 days = 720 hours**, perform 100,000 writes and 1 million reads, and transfer 100 GB out. Individual attachments must fit the **10 MB** limit, and the app must supply authenticated delivery and a protection strategy that does not rely on unsupported attachment backup.

Using only the **Classic FAQ's unlocated rate** as an illustration:

`max(100 GB − 20 GB included, 0) × 720 hours × $0.0014/GB-hour = $80.64`

This is **our calculation of an illustrative storage subtotal**. The rate's reference region is not stated; it is not a current Dallas quote and is not assigned to Gen 2. It excludes reserved capacity, internet transfer, backups, request bursts and taxes. If 100 GB describes only attachment payloads, document/index overhead increases billed data. No exact file-store total can be published.
