## Cost examples — our calculation

**No new app can buy or start these scenarios.** The [Chinese](https://docs.leancloud.cn/sdk/announcements/sunset-announcement/) and [English sunset notice](https://docs.leancloud.app/en/sdk/announcements/sunset-announcement/) closed new registrations/apps on January 12, 2026. The arithmetic below is for an already-existing app before January 12, 2027. It is not a recommendation, a new-customer quote or a guarantee of continued operation after shutdown.

All calculations checked 2026-11-04. Primary edition: international `us-w1`, documented as North America in [FAQ](https://docs.leancloud.cn/sdk/start/faq/) and [CLI region list](https://docs.leancloud.cn/sdk/engine/cli/). China figures are separate RMB calculations, not currency conversions. Rates come from the [international pricing table/FAQ](https://leancloud.app/pricing/) and [China pricing table/FAQ](https://www.leancloud.cn/pricing/); allocation rules from [international Engine platform](https://docs.leancloud.app/en/sdk/engine/platform/) and [Engine FAQ](https://docs.leancloud.app/en/sdk/engine/faq/).

**Time and units.** Use a 31-calendar-day existing-app billing window for the database/backend/jobs/idle examples. A continuous 730-hour allocated workload reaches 31 dates if it starts at midnight: 30 × 24 + 10 = 730 hours. Daily peak billing therefore uses **31 billed days**, not 730 ÷ 24 rounded down. This avoids silently fitting 730 hours into a 30-day month. The file scenario retains its required 30-day window. Daily free delivery does not roll over; downloads are assumed uniform. For numerical transfer examples only, explicitly assume **1 GB = 1,000 MB**, so 33 MB = 0.033 GB; vendor binary/decimal accounting was not established. No other silent MB/GiB conversion is used. Taxes and independently purchased domains/CDNs are excluded because the full treatment/cost is unverified.

**Business floor.** International qualifying daily charges are max(actual eligible daily usage, **$5**); China max(actual eligible daily usage, **RMB 30**). File capacity and file delivery are added outside the floor. They are not part of the $5/RMB30 minimum. The floor is applied daily, not once to an uneven month's aggregate. Uniform distribution below keeps each known eligible day below the floor. Unknown meters may change totals.

**Retry/wait variant — our calculation.** If 10% of 10,000 jobs receive one retry, attempts = 10,000 + 1,000 = **11,000**. At one active second plus two waiting seconds inside each execution, active = **11,000 seconds**, waiting = **22,000 seconds**, total = **33,000 aggregate execution seconds**. Each attempt's 3 seconds is below the documented 15-second response limit. This does not solve the concurrency-one constraint, and timed-out code may continue running. With unchanged daily worker size/count, there is no supported per-second deduction from the allocated-instance bill; extra data/API/transfer work can still charge. Queue default backoff is a separate 60,000 ms between attempts, not two seconds of active worker waiting. See [Queue](https://docs.leancloud.cn/sdk/engine/functions/cloud-queue/), [Functions](https://docs.leancloud.cn/sdk/engine/functions/guides/) and [instance billing](https://docs.leancloud.app/en/sdk/engine/platform/).

**If recurring file allowances are unavailable — our calculation.** Same international 30-day 100 GB file case: 100 × $0.001 × 30 + 100 × $0.10 = **$13.00** before requests, database metadata, CDN and any Business floor. China domestic HTTPS, with the 10 GB capacity allowance unavailable: 100 × RMB0.16 + 100 × RMB0.36 = **RMB52.00**. These are marginal capacity/delivery figures, not a current no-allowance subscription tier or converted currency. A Business database is already billed from the first GB/request at the above rates; there is no unverified free Business storage allowance subtracted.

**Backup/availability variants.** The [security guide](https://docs.leancloud.cn/sdk/storage/guide/security/) documents 7-day backups and Business restore, but pricing's Developer backup wording differs; PITR/extra backup tariff was not established. [Engine platform](https://docs.leancloud.cn/sdk/engine/platform/) recommends two standard instances for failover. At the smallest sizes over 31 daily peaks, two workers cost **2 × 31 × $1.6 = $99.20** internationally or **RMB124** domestically before applying any eligible Business floor. That is engine HTTP failover, not an extra LeanStorage replica price or a guarantee of meeting job throughput. No complete priced cross-region database HA variant was found.
