## Cost examples

All examples are our calculations, not vendor quotes. Prices are USD, before applicable taxes, and assume standard PAYG at the current $7.99/TB-month list rate.

- **1 TB stored for one full month, with 100 GB egress:** 1 TB × $7.99/TB-month + $0 egress + $0 API requests = **$7.99**. This assumes the 100 GB download stays within the free-egress rule.
- **Idle paid account with zero active storage for one month:** 1 TB minimum × $7.99/TB-month = **$7.99**. Delete the account to stop future minimums.
- **1 TB held for 30 days, then deleted:** 30 active days + 60 timed-deleted days = 90 billable days; 1 TB × $7.99/month × 90/30 = **$23.97** total storage billing before tax.

## Conflicts and unknowns
