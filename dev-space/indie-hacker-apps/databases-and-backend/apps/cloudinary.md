## Cost examples

The amounts below are our calculations, not Cloudinary quotes. Credits are shared across storage, transformations, and delivery. Cloudinary's public price cards show a dollar sign but do not state a currency code; amounts are kept as shown. Taxes and paid add-ons are excluded.

1. **Free, one meter only:** 25 credits × 1,000 image transformations = 25,000 transformations; alternatively, 25 × 1 GB = 25 GB storage, 25 GB image bandwidth, or 25 GB video bandwidth. These are alternatives and cannot be added together as separate allowances.
2. **Free, mixed image workload:** 5 GB stored + 10,000 image transformations + 5 GB delivered image bandwidth = 5 + 10 + 5 = 20 credits. That fits in the 25-credit Free pool, leaving 5 credits, assuming no add-ons or other usage.
3. **Above Free:** 10 GB storage + 100,000 image transformations + 50 GB image bandwidth = 10 + 100 + 50 = 160 credits. This fits within Plus's 225 credits. The listed Plus commitment is $99 per month, or $89 per month on an annual subscription; the source does not label the currency code. External CDN/origin costs and taxes are excluded.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Plan names are not presented consistently. The current billing guide names four self-service paid tiers; the pricing page shows Plus and Advanced cards alongside Free and Enterprise, while the compare FAQ supplies amounts for Advanced Extra and Pro and has inconsistent wording about the number of plans. This file uses the current billing guide's names and the FAQ's explicit amounts, and does not infer additional tiers.
- The dollar currency code is not printed on the plan cards. The exact Pro PAYG overage price, Enterprise price, and dollar spend cap were not found after checking pricing, billing, usage, and terms pages.
- The Free credit system is shared. Image bandwidth is 1 GB per credit; video bandwidth is 1 GB per Free credit and 2 GB per paid self-service credit. Video transformation processing has a separate per-second rate that depends on output type and resolution.
- No inactivity pause rule or fixed backup-version retention period was found in the pricing, billing, usage, or backup docs. The 30-day cancellation restore period is an account-recovery rule, not a stated backup retention period.
- Search for acquisition and shutdown notices covered the Cloudinary newsroom, blog archive, product billing/docs, and public status page. The latest acquisition notice found was the 2022 Indivio acquisition; no current shutdown or sunset notice appeared in those sources.
- The Privacy Policy says uploaded content is deleted following termination or deactivation, while billing documentation describes a 30-day restore period after account cancellation. The pages do not define the timing relationship for every termination case, so the record keeps that point unclear rather than treating the restore window as a universal retention guarantee.
