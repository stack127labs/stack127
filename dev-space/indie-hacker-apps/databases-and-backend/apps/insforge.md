## Cost examples — our calculation

These are our calculations, not InsForge quotes or performance tests. Checked 2026-11-04. Use one Cloud project in `eu-central` (Frankfurt), month-to-month Pro, 730 allocated backend hours, no other projects consuming organization allowances/credit, and no optional Custom Compute container, model calls or site builds unless stated. [Region article](https://insforge.dev/blog/insforge-multi-region) says equal regional pricing. Keep the published `$`, GB and MB units; no currency or GB/GiB conversion is made. Tax is extra under [Terms, Fees and Taxes](https://insforge.dev/terms).

**Shared price inputs.** [Pricing](https://insforge.dev/pricing) and [Pricing FAQ](https://docs.insforge.dev/pricing.md) publish Pro $25/month, 8 GB database, 100 GB files, 250 GB egress, 100,000 MAU and 100,000 function calls, plus one $10 monthly backend-compute credit across the organization. Choose Micro, 2 vCPU/1 GB RAM, $0.0134/hour. Compute = 730 × $0.0134 = **$9.782**; net after credit = max($9.782 − $10, 0) = **$0**. Known subscription + compute subtotal = **$25**. Micro is a sizing assumption, not proof it handles these workloads. Nano's smaller advertised rate is $0.0067/hour: 730 × $0.0067 = $4.891, also covered by the credit, so choosing Nano does not lower this subtotal. Printed $5/$10 monthly compute displays are not substituted for hourly arithmetic.

Annual Pro instead requires $240 upfront; its advertised $20/month equivalent gives a **$20 equivalent monthly known subtotal** under the same credit assumptions. It is not a monthly payment option. These are ordinary paid paths after Free no longer fits, using recurring paid-plan inclusions, not trial discounts.

- **Model access:** [Gateway](https://docs.insforge.dev/core-concepts/ai/overview.md) promotes project proxy/key hiding; [AI REST](https://docs.insforge.dev/sdks/rest/ai.md) deprecates proxy routes and recommends direct OpenRouter access using a provisioned key.
