## Lifecycle and access notices

Managed PostgreSQL was announced in **June 2026** and remains offered on the current product/docs pages. The lifecycle-first official-site search did not identify a product-wide shutdown, sunset, acquisition or former name requiring a successor entry. Sources: https://sliplane.io/blog/jun-15-2026-changelog, https://sliplane.io/managed-postgres, https://docs.sliplane.io/databases/overview/ (checked 2026-11-05). This is a bounded current-status finding.

**The free app-server demo is not a free managed database.** The managed product explicitly has no free tier. Terms treat demo/evaluation resources as temporary and converted to paid only by a clearly marked paid order. Sources: https://sliplane.io/managed-postgres (FAQ), https://sliplane.io/terms (Sections 2.2/4.7–4.9; checked 2026-11-05).

**Pausing does not stop billing.** To stop managed database resource charges, export needed data and delete the database. A PITR restore creates another hourly-billed database; the source remains. Sources: https://docs.sliplane.io/databases/pricing/, https://docs.sliplane.io/teams/billing/, https://docs.sliplane.io/databases/backups/ (checked 2026-11-05).

**Germany Confidential** is an isolated dedicated-only option requiring a separate signed agreement. Its coverage excludes logs, builds, environment variables and S3 buckets; its name is not a blanket legal-compliance badge. Source: https://docs.sliplane.io/more/confidential-region/ (checked 2026-11-05).

## Cost examples — our calculation

Checked 2026-11-05. Use **Germany**, standard shared **Starter**, **1 vCPU / 1 GB RAM / 10 GB included disk / 100 connections**, before VAT. The official table also applies to Finland; no regional currency conversion is made.

### Small SQL/document app

Approved inputs: **one production SQL database, 1 GB stored, 100,000 reads, 20,000 writes, 10 peak connections, 5 GB Internet transfer and 730 hours**.

- **Hourly, no commitment:** rounded billed hours = ceil(730) = 730. Compute = 730 × €0.03253 = **€23.7469**, or **approximately €23.75**, explicitly rounded to cents.
- **Storage:** 10 GB is included. If the stated 1 GB of data plus engine files/indexes/WAL fit within that allocation, **€0 additional storage**. The application data size is not treated as identical to physical database occupancy.
- **Native database egress:** the included-egress policy gives **€0 additional charge** for this 5 GB example, subject to fair use. No unrestricted bulk-transit allowance is inferred.
- **Backups:** seven-day PITR is included on every tier, so there is no separately listed backup fee in this base case. Restored copies and optional third-party logical backups are separate.
- **Known hourly database component:** **€23.7469 before VAT**, conditional on the workload fitting 1 GB RAM/1 shared vCPU and included disk. The ten client connections fit the published 100-connection limit. The request counts do not add a per-query tariff, but they do not prove throughput/latency suitability.
- **Monthly commitment comparison:** **€19 for one committed month**. This is its published fixed commitment price, not an hourly tariff multiplied by 730 and not proof that every calendar month has 730 hours.
- **Annual commitment comparison:** **€190 for one committed year**. For comparison only, €190 / 12 = €15.833333… per month, **approximately €15.83** rounded to cents. That comparison is not a purchasable monthly price or a claim about an unstated annual payment schedule.

No managed document-native engine is included in this entry; PostgreSQL JSON/extension support is not relabelled as another hosted database.

### Small vector search app

Inputs: **100,000 vectors × 1,536 float32 values**, **1 KiB metadata/vector**, **100,000 searches**, **10,000 updates** and **5 GB transfer**.

Our raw payload arithmetic:

- Values: 100,000 × 1,536 × 4 bytes = **614,400,000 bytes**.
- Metadata: 100,000 × 1,024 = **102,400,000 bytes**.
- Sum: **716,800,000 bytes = 0.7168 decimal GB**, using 1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes.
