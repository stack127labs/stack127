## Evidence rules

The task's six states override the vocabulary in `fields.md`. `confirmed` means supported by the linked official source. `not_published` means not found in the named bounded search, not proof of absence. Calculations below are ours. All checked dates use UTC. Notes identify product, plan, source title/section and limitations.

Firebase is a collection of services. Project-level Firebase quotas and Google Cloud billing-account quotas must not be multiplied by apps, users or buckets. Spark, Blaze no-cost allowances, SQL trials and Google Cloud welcome credit are different offers.

The [Firebase terms map](https://firebase.google.com/terms) assigns Firestore, Functions, Storage, Auth and SQL Connect to Google Cloud terms; Hosting/Realtime Database have Google APIs/Firebase paid terms and Firebase processing terms. The corresponding policy and processing agreements were reviewed. This record does not infer compliance from a data center location.

Prices preserve their original units and conditions. Cost examples use USD Iowa/us-central1 on-demand rates without tax or committed-use discounts. Raw scenario GB are explicitly treated as decimal bytes; converted GiB values are shown. Cloud Storage pricing defines its own GB as binary GiB. Legacy Firebase bucket GB labels are kept as published.

## Cost examples — our calculation

These are modeled costs, not vendor quotes or measurements. Rates were checked on 2026-11-04: [Firestore](https://cloud.google.com/firestore/pricing), [Firestore billing rules](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/pricing), [Cloud Storage](https://cloud.google.com/storage/pricing), [Cloud free allowances](https://docs.cloud.google.com/free/docs/free-cloud-features#cloud-storage), [request-based Cloud Run](https://cloud.google.com/run/pricing) and [Cloud Tasks](https://cloud.google.com/tasks/pricing). The Firebase plan guide explains [Blaze and shared billing](https://firebase.google.com/docs/projects/billing/firebase-pricing-plans).

Assumptions: one project; Iowa resources; normal internet downloads to US destinations; 30-day month; no trial credits, tax, phone SMS, SAML/OIDC, paid support, frontend hosting/CDN or image processing. Retained storage uses 720 hours (30×24), with no always-on database compute allocation. `[x]+ = max(x,0)`.

Conversion: `1 decimal GB=1,000,000,000 bytes/2^30=0.931322574615478515625 GiB`. This conversion is ours; vendor prices remain per their published units. Displayed long decimal money results below are arithmetic outputs, not promised invoice precision.

Let `S` be total billed Firestore GiB including document/index overhead. Let `R_d/W_d` be billable reads/writes on Pacific-time day `d`, including additional index/rules/listener work. For an eligible Standard database, the core formula is:

`720×$0.000205479×[S−1]+ + ($0.03/100,000)×sum_d[R_d−50,000]+ + ($0.09/100,000)×sum_d[W_d−20,000]+ + $0.12×[internet_GiB−10]+`

This covers the specified normal destination/first paid transfer tier. Deletes, recovery, another region and additional services have separate terms. A monthly read count cannot establish daily free-tier eligibility.

### 1. Small database app

Inputs: 1 GB total **billed** database/index data, 100,000 single-document reads, 20,000 single-document writes, 10 peak client connections and 5 GB downloads over 30 days. Reads are direct document gets; no listeners, security-rule dependent-document reads, index scan surcharges, backups or PITR.
