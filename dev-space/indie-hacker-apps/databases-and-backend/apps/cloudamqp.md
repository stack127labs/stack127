## Notices

84codes AB operates CloudAMQP. Its current terms cover the managed RabbitMQ and LavinMQ services. CloudAMQP is active: its changelog lists product and region updates through October 2026, and 84codes' 2026 newsroom lists current company news. I found no CloudAMQP shutdown or acquisition notice in the product changelog, CloudAMQP news posts, or 84codes newsroom. The 84codes newsroom does report that CloudKarafka, CloudMQTT, and ElephantSQL were retired starting January 28, 2025; that notice says 84codes was focusing on CloudAMQP and LavinMQ.
