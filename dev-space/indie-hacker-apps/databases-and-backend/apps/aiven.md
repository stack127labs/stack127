Scope: Aiven’s managed-service catalog, with service-specific free offers and entry prices. Detailed SQL, connections, recovery and vector examples use **Aiven for PostgreSQL**. Aiven Runtime is separate container compute, not included database execution. Other engines are not assigned PostgreSQL limits. Aiven for Flink is in sunset and unavailable for new creation; this does not mean the whole Aiven platform has retired.

The hosted plans and sales-enabled BYOC are distinguished. This public-source record is not an account quote, performance test or complete comparison of every engine/region/size.

## Field problems

- **Aiven is a catalog.** A flat per-app field cannot capture every engine/region/tier without misleading reuse. Core free offers and entry prices are separated; deeper database, recovery and vector facts use PostgreSQL. Kafka/Runtime facts are scoped when relevant.
- **Rounded cards and exact rates differ.** Pricing uses GB labels while public catalog resources use MB keys; both are preserved without guessing a binary/decimal conversion. A raw capacity is not guaranteed application payload.
- **Free is indefinite pricing with operating restrictions.** Inactivity power-off, EOL and deletion rules are separate from trial expiry. Free services also fall under Beta terms.
- **Runtime is separately billed compute.** It is not included in a PostgreSQL service, and its application-auth gate is not a complete consumer backend bundle. Current fixed-hour billing and announced future usage billing must stay separate.
- **Storage fields do not fit database disk.** The seven file fields are explicitly not applicable to a native app-file module in this scoped offer; customer code/external buckets remain possible. This is not a claim that arbitrary application uploads are impossible.
- **Job systems differ.** SQL pg_cron, Kafka topic retention and Runtime workers cannot share one invented queue TTL or per-invocation bill. Customer code must supply much of the delivery/retry design.
- **Recovery metrics differ.** Backup count, PITR history, node replication, restore time and content/file protection cannot be collapsed into “backups included.”
- Allowed states follow the owner’s later instructions rather than the older state names in `fields.md`. C22 describes evidence quality.

No `apps.md` row error found. “SQL and managed databases”, active status and `hosting and deploy` fit the current catalog. Its instruction to keep engine-specific plans separate is respected by the explicit PostgreSQL detail scope and separate named-service rows. Only this app record was written.
