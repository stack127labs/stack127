Temporal has two editions here: the MIT-licensed server that you run, and Temporal Cloud, a paid hosted service. Temporal Cloud coordinates Workflows; in the standard deployment, your Workers run on compute you provide. Its Cloud usage bill covers Actions and Workflow History storage, plus the selected support plan. Treat worker hosting as a separate cost.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The pricing page says Cloud starts at $50 per million Actions and advertises volume pricing down to $25/M. The linked pricing documentation gives the marginal tier table for Business, Enterprise, and Mission Critical. I use that newer detailed table for tier boundaries and note the pricing-page headline as the starting rate. The docs say the page has a calculator, but the web text view does not expose whether its inputs are sliders; no monthly/annual switch was visible in the page text.
- The pricing page says no base monthly fee for Pay-As-You-Go. The pricing docs clarify that Developer support adds 10% of Cloud consumption, while Business is the greater of $500/month or 10%. These describe support-plan fees, not contradictory Action rates.
- Cloud serverless Workers appear in current changelog entries as preview/pre-release; their infrastructure pricing is not given in the core Cloud pricing table. Treat Worker compute as a separate provider/add-on quote.
- A dedicated egress price, a public card requirement for signup, an inactivity deletion rule, and a general log-retention period were not published on the pricing page, Cloud docs, Terms, or Privacy pages checked. Their row notes name the pages searched.
- Temporal's October 2026 blog says the Oso security team joined Temporal. The post does not say Temporal acquired all of Oso or that an existing product was discontinued.

## Field problems

- **C13:** “Action” is a family of server-side operations, not a Workflow run count. A single Schedule execution can create three Actions; retries, heartbeats, Queries, Fairness and Provisioned Capacity can add usage. The row spells out these rules; usage estimates from Event History can be off by up to 5%.
- **C13/C21:** The displayed dollar sign is supported as USD by the pricing documentation's USD storage table and its statement that one Temporal Credit equals $1 USD. Do not infer another currency or silently round the calculation.
- **C17:** GCP regions have a temporary new-namespace restriction in three listed regions as of the check date. Region availability is not uniform across providers or replication modes.
- **H01:** The Docker Compose sample is explicitly a starting example; it is not a production hardware profile. Resource needs depend on the workload, and no universal CPU/RAM floor was found.
- **C21:** No idle dollar total is stated because open Workflow storage, retention, separate Worker hosting, and rate limits depend on the actual workload and plan.
