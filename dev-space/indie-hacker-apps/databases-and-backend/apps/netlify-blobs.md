## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Blob billing detail:** Netlify's 2023 beta announcement says there were no Blobs costs during beta, while its July 2026 comparison says it draws from the shared account credit pool. The current credit-meter docs list general compute, bandwidth, requests, and deploy meters but no Blobs storage or operation conversion. I use the newer comparison for current billing and leave the exact Blobs conversion unknown.
- **Plan pricing display:** The credit-plan docs give `$0`, `$9`, and Pro from `$20` per month. The live pricing page displayed multiple dollar values in its plan widget without labeling the regional basis in the page text. I report the documentation values as Netlify plan prices, retain `$`, and do not claim a currency code or region.
- **Commercial use:** Netlify's Free-plan launch post says commercial projects are allowed, while the current Self-Serve Agreement says the service is for internal business purposes. I do not resolve this legal wording.
- **Blob data lifecycle:** The product docs distinguish deploy-specific cleanup from site-wide stores that persist until manually expired or deleted. The privacy statement and service agreement do not give an exact deletion interval for all Blob bytes after account closure.
- **Pages tried for Blobs pricing/limits:** Blobs docs and API/requirements, current credit-plan comparison, pricing page, pricing FAQ, credit-meter guide, billing FAQ, official Blobs-versus-Vercel comparison, launch post, and changelog. These show the shared-credit statement and per-object ceilings but no standalone GB price, total quota, operation rate, or Blob-specific meter formula. The 2023 launch post's beta-free statement conflicts with the newer shared-credit explanation.
- **Shutdown/newsroom check:** I checked Netlify's blog, its About/press releases page, and its changelog for acquisition, shutdown, or sunset notices. No current notice or separate newsroom was found. The current Blobs Beta label remains visible in the plan docs.

## Field problems

- Credit plan subscription prices are not Blob storage rates. The plan table includes Blobs but does not state a Blob-specific storage quota or map Blob operations to credits.
