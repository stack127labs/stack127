## Cost examples

The us-east-1 prices below come from the official AWSQueueService Price List offer, published 2026-09-11 and fetched 2026-11-07. They are our calculations, not AWS quotes.

- **Our calculation — 1.2 million Standard request units in one month in us-east-1:** (1.2M − 1M free) ÷ 1M × $0.40 = **$0.08 USD**. Assumes payload blocks at or below 64 KB and no transfer charges, KMS, S3 Extended Client use, or Fair Queue requests.
- **Our calculation — 1.2 million FIFO request units in one month in us-east-1:** (1.2M − 1M free) ÷ 1M × $0.50 = **$0.10 USD**. Assumes payload blocks at or below 64 KB and no transfer or KMS charges.
- **Our calculation — idle SQS queue with no API requests:** $0 USD in SQS request charges because SQS has no minimum fee. Excludes any other AWS resources.
