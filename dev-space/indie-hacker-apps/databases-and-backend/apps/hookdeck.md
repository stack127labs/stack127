## Conflicts and unknowns

- Event Gateway's current pricing page has three incompatible usage-rate presentations: `$0.33 per 100k`, `$1.00 per additional 100k`, and FAQ examples of `$0.30` per 10,000 events. I kept the row `conflicting` and did not choose one as the billable rate.

- The pricing page advertises a 14-day team-invite trial; Terms describe a first-time-user 30-day highest-plan trial. The exact relation between those trials is not stated.
- No Event Gateway region selector or exact storage region was found in pricing, docs, privacy, or the subprocessor list. Privacy says EEA data may be transferred outside the EEA.
- No universal Outpost payload ceiling was found. NATS has a documented default of 1 MiB, adjustable by the operator; AWS EventBridge rejects entries above 1 MB. These are transport/destination limits, not a product-wide Outpost cap.
- No Event Gateway egress rate or Outpost managed transfer rate was found in pricing, docs, or Terms. No self-hosted infrastructure total can be exact without a deployment size and provider.
- No acquisition or shutdown notice was found in official company pages, blog, changelog, or Terms checked. This is limited to those sources and the check date.
