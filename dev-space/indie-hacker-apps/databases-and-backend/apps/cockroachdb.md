## Cost examples — our calculations

All inputs were checked on 2026-11-05. These are our calculations, not vendor quotes. The ordinary paid reference is Continuum Standard, AWS us-east-1, 2 provisioned vCPUs for 730 hours. The pricing page uses a 2-vCPU reference, but does not prove this is the universal minimum or sufficient capacity for these workloads. No trial credit is subtracted from a recurring price. Tax is excluded under the [SaaS Terms, Fees](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/cloud-terms-and-conditions/).

Rate sources: [current pricing, AWS reference and trial FAQ](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/pricing/); [Consumption Table, AWS us-east-1 and transfer tables](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/pdf/Consumption_table_pricing.pdf). Decimal scenario GB is explicitly converted: `g = 1,000,000,000 / 1,073,741,824 = 0.931322574615478515625 GiB/GB`. Monetary results below are rounded to cents and marked partial. Let `B` be actual backup GiB-month and `N` additional billable internal/cross-region transfer; both remain unknown. [Managed Backups](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/cockroachcloud/managed-backups) explains why retention, change history and backup frequency affect B.

### 1. Small SQL/document app

Inputs: one database, 1 GB retained for the month, 100,000 reads, 20,000 writes, 10 peak connections, 5 GB internet transfer, 730 hours. Formula:

`2 × 730 × 0.092 + g × 0.32 + 5g × 0.09216 = $135.047176666259765625`.

**Known paid subtotal: $135.05/month**, plus `0.023B + N`, taxes and any selected add-ons. Request counts cannot establish CPU sizing, query latency or backup volume. This is not an exact smallest-plan total. Both current editions offer managed recovery, but retained backup bytes remain a separate billable input. A PCR/extra-region variant additionally needs a standby/topology configuration, capacity and routes; no exact high-availability variant is priced.

Legacy eligible Basic, a different offer: [Plan a Basic Cluster](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/cockroachcloud/plan-your-cluster-basic) publishes the combined allowance and rates. Formula `max(0, 0.20 × R/1,000,000 + 0.50 × g − 15)`, where R is measured total billable RU including relevant background/network work. R is unknown, so the full result is unknown; it can be $0 only if combined eligible usage stays within the shared credit. Do not equate 120,000 SQL operations to 120,000 RU. Without the allowance, omit the subtraction.

### 2. Full app backend

Inputs: the SQL workload plus 1,000 monthly active users, 1 GB files, 100,000 file reads, 10,000 uploads, 20 GB total internet transfer and 10,000 function calls. For a concrete function input, assume 1 second and 512 MiB each: `10,000 × 1 = 10,000 execution-seconds`; this is our workload assumption, not a CockroachDB worker allocation.

**Complete backend total unknown.** Database cost follows case 1 only for its database portion. Authentication, upload storage/delivery and function hosting were not established as bundled services in the [current feature reference](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/continuum/feature-reference.md). Which provider serves the extra 15 GB is unspecified; do not price all 20 GB as CockroachDB egress. External email/SMS, model services and worker bills are not included.

### 3. Small file store

Inputs: 100 GB hot storage for 30 days, 100,000 writes, 1 million reads and 100 GB internet transfer; objects retained for the month.

**Not applicable as a standalone file-store quote.** [BACKUP](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/stable/backup.md) writes database backups to separately supplied object storage; it does not sell app buckets. [BYTES](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/stable/bytes.md) columns are SQL values. Formula would require the chosen object's storage + requests + retrieval + delivery rates; those provider inputs are absent. Do not multiply this workload by database storage rates.

### 4. Small vector search app

Inputs: 100,000 vectors, 1,536 float32 numbers each, 1 KiB metadata each, 100,000 searches, 10,000 updates and 5 GB internet transfer.

Our raw-input calculation: `100,000 × (1,536 × 4 + 1,024) = 716,800,000 bytes = 0.667572021484375 GiB`. This is a workload payload calculation, not measured stored or indexed size. [VECTOR](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/stable/vector.md) and [Vector Indexes](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/stable/vector-indexes.md) establish the query/index capability.

Let P be actual billable logical GiB-month. Under the same illustrative cloud configuration, formula `134.32 + 0.32P + 0.4291534423828125 + 0.023B + N + model costs`. **Total unknown**: P, compute sufficiency, backup history and embeddings/reranking are not established. Do not treat raw vector bytes as the whole index bill or invent a free model quota.

### 5. Small job workload

Inputs: 10,000 jobs, 1 second each, 0.5 vCPU, 0.5 GB RAM, peak 5, no waiting/retries. Our requested work is `10,000 active seconds = 5,000 vCPU-seconds + 5,000 GB-seconds`. One retry per job doubles that active work; adding 10 seconds' waiting per job adds 100,000 waiting-seconds whose billing depends on the worker provider.

**Not applicable to a general worker runtime here; total unknown if built with external workers.** [CDC](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/stable/change-data-capture-overview.md) and [BACKUP](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/stable/backup.md) describe database jobs, not arbitrary job machines. Invocation/orchestration, worker hosting, logs, transfer and retry/wait rates are missing; database vCPU pricing cannot be substituted for a nonexistent published worker allocation.

### 6. Idle project

Inputs: the 1 GB SQL data retained for 730 hours, zero client queries and zero internet downloads; keep the illustrative 2-vCPU cluster provisioned.

Formula `2 × 730 × 0.092 + g × 0.32 = $134.618023223876953125`. **Known paid subtotal: $134.62/month**, plus backup storage and other retained or internal activity. The [Pricing FAQ, idle clusters](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/pricing/) explicitly keeps provisioned compute billable.

Legacy Basic alternative: `max(0, 0.20 × R_idle/1,000,000 + 0.50g − 15)` under [Basic billing](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/cockroachcloud/plan-your-cluster-basic); zero client queries does not establish zero background RU. Eligible self-hosted [Enterprise Free](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/stable/licensing-faqs) has a $0 software-license line, but infrastructure + backups + operations remain unknown. This is not a $0 hosted alternative.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Regional conflict:** [Continuum FAQ](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/continuum/faq.md) marks GCP southamerica-east1 available to Standard; the [consumption table](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/pdf/Consumption_table_pricing.pdf) has dashes for its Standard compute/storage columns. This record excludes that location from the confirmed Standard list.
- **Cloud generations:** [current pricing](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/pricing/) and [legacy pricing](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/pricing/legacy/) address different organizations. Legacy $15 credits and legacy capacity limits are not assumed available to a new Continuum customer. The [older free-trial docs](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/cockroachcloud/free-trial) describe throttling and accrued charges during a grace period, while the current FAQ says paused. The current offer is used for the new-user trial; the exact account determines legacy behavior.
- **Idle architecture versus billed host:** [architecture](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/blog/continuum-architecture/) describes SQL pods/virtual workloads scaling to zero; [Continuum FAQ](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/continuum/faq.md) still charges the host plus virtualization. That does not justify a zero idle cloud invoice. The pricing table's Standard “from $0.057/vCPU-hour” is unscoped; the explicitly regional us-east-1 reference is used here rather than assuming the lower headline applies there.
