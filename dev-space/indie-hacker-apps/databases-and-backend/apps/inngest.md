## Conflicts and unknowns

- The pricing comparison lists Enterprise concurrency as 500 included, while the Enterprise card says custom concurrency and the limits page also says custom. I used **custom** in the plan limits row and left the discrepancy visible.
- The current pricing page shows Pro execution overage as `$50 per 1 million` in its comparison table, but its FAQ describes tiered rates between `$0.000050` and `$0.000015` per execution without the tier thresholds. The table rate is a displayed reference; exact marginal Pro cost above included usage is unresolved. The `$` currency code is not named on the page.
- The pricing page's actual calculator includes sliders for runs, average `step.run()` calls, scores, queue depth, and realtime connections; seats, concurrent steps, and workers use increment/decrement inputs. I inspected the live pricing UI. It showed monthly prices and no annual/monthly switch.
- Inngest counts one function run and each step execution. Its FAQ says retries increase usage, but the pricing, retry, and Insights pages do not provide an exact billing rule per failed attempt. No retry multiplier is assumed in the cost example.
- The Cloud plan table provides trace/log retention, but does not specify a separate retention period for queued, unexecuted event payloads or exact Cloud account-deletion timing. I checked limits, pricing, Privacy, and Terms for those items.
- The press page is a press kit; no distinct newsroom page was found. The lifecycle search covered the company blog, changelog, press kit, Terms, repository, and releases as of 2026-11-06.
