**Lifecycle and company news:** Storj announced an agreement to be acquired by Inveniam on October 22, 2025. In a July 26, 2026 restructuring announcement, Storj Labs, Inc. said it had filed Chapter 11 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Northern District of West Virginia (Case 5:26-bk-00512). The announcement says Inveniam had acquired Storj, the company would keep operating in the ordinary course, and it did not expect service interruptions. Storj's FAQ says it is not shutting down and operations, service, and the network are running normally. On November 6, 2026, the public status page showed all listed systems operational. I found no shutdown or sunset notice in the current official blog and newsroom pages. Chapter 11 is a material financial restructuring; the continued-service statement is Storj's own current public position, not a guarantee of future continuity. ## Cost examples — our calculation

Checked 2026-11-06. Current public rates are in USD and use decimal units. Both tiers charge egress at $7/TB. The active-account monthly floor is $5, not a credit. Applicable U.S. sales tax is excluded because it depends on customer location.

**Small workload:** Store 100 GB for a full month and download 100 GB. Standard raw use is `100 GB × $7 / 1,000 GB + 100 GB × $7 / 1,000 GB = $0.70 + $0.70 = $1.40`. The $5 monthly floor makes the invoice **$5 USD before applicable tax**. Advanced raw use is `$1.00 storage + $0.70 egress = $1.70`, so its invoice is also **$5 USD before applicable tax**.

**Idle storage:** Keep 100 GB stored for a full month with no downloads. Standard storage is `100 GB × $7 / 1,000 GB = $0.70`; Advanced is `$1.00`. Each remains below the active-account floor, so the monthly invoice is **$5 USD before applicable tax**. This assumes stored data remains in the account; an empty account has separate suspension and deletion rules.
