# Neo4j AuraDB / Neo4j

## Shutdown, sunset, acquisition and edition changes

Checked 2026-11-05. Neo4j AuraDB, self-hosted Community and self-hosted Enterprise remain active offers. **Neo4j 4.4 long-term support ended November 30, 2025**. The current release archive lists **2026.10.1**; **5.26 is LTS**. No whole-product shutdown or acquisition notice was found in the official product, pricing, release, changelog, legal and status sources checked. [Release archive](https://neo4j.com/release-notes/), [upgrade guide](https://neo4j.com/docs/upgrade-migration-guide/current/), [Aura status](https://status.neo4j.io/).

The **legacy transactional HTTP API is deprecated from 5.26**, replaced by Query API, and is not available on Aura. Vector query procedures are deprecated from 2026.04 in favor of SEARCH; the old vector quantization.enabled setting is deprecated from 2026.06. Aura multiple databases became GA on September 30, 2026 for Business Critical/VDC, while Business Critical AWS private endpoints remain preview. [HTTP API](https://neo4j.com/docs/http-api/current/), [vector-index notices](https://neo4j.com/docs/cypher-manual/current/indexes/semantic-indexes/vector-indexes/), [changelog](https://feedback.neo4j.com/changelog), [private-endpoint preview](https://neo4j.com/docs/aura/security/private-endpoints/).

**License/migration finding:** Aura self-serve terms and the current Cloud Addendum restrict use of open-source Community Neo4j during the agreement and for **36 months afterward**, subject to the applicable agreement/order wording. The paid Software Addendum has a similar restriction for the **same Project**. These are separate contractual facts from Community's GPLv3 license; an export feature does not establish unrestricted migration to Community. [Aura self-serve restrictions](https://neo4j.com/legal-terms/aura-selfserve-terms/), [Cloud Addendum](https://neo4j.com/legal-terms/cloud-agreement/), [Software Addendum](https://neo4j.com/legal-terms/software-agreement/), [Community GPL](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/neo4j/neo4j/2026.10/LICENSE.txt).
