## Cost examples — our calculation

Checked 2026-11-06. Use the published €5 per rounded-up 250 GB block after the first 1 GB average team allowance. Prices exclude VAT. Sliplane says requests and ingress/egress are not billed.

**Small app workload:** 100 GB average stored across the team's buckets for the billing month, 1 million API requests, and 100 GB downloaded. Formula: `ceil((100 GB - 1 GB) / 250 GB) × €5 = 1 × €5 = €5/month before VAT`. The request and transfer components are €0 under Sliplane's published tariff.

**Idle app retaining files:** At the same 100 GB average storage with no requests or transfer, the storage charge remains `ceil((100 GB - 1 GB) / 250 GB) × €5 = €5/month before VAT`. If the team stores 0 GB, the published 0–1 GB tier is €0 for storage. Both examples exclude VAT and any separate Sliplane products. These are our calculations, not vendor quotes.

Rates and allowances: https://docs.sliplane.io/object-storage/pricing/ ; request/transfer charges: https://sliplane.io/object-storage .
