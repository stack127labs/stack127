## Cost example

**Our calculation:** A Hatchet Cloud PAYG workload with 2 million task runs and 1.5 million events in one 30/31-day billing cycle costs `$32.50` for those two meters: 1 million overage runs × `$30` per million + 0.5 million overage events × `$5` per million. It assumes storage, logs, users, workers, and other usage stay within their listed allowances. The site does not name the currency code, so the result keeps its displayed `$` symbol. Worker hosting, tax, and any storage/log overage are excluded.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Currency:** Hatchet's pricing page uses `$` for prices, but the pricing and terms pages checked do not name USD or another currency code. The price rows preserve `$` and do not convert it.
- **Active storage period:** The plan table says task inputs and outputs are held for 24 hours and gives 10 GB included on the card tier. The pricing estimator also says the first 10 GB “each day” is free. The public page does not explain whether this means a daily allowance or a concurrent/rolling storage cap.
- **Log and trace overage unit:** The comparison table describes included ingestion as monthly ingested volume, while the displayed overage rate is per GB-hour. The pricing page does not explain the conversion or average-storage calculation; no total log overage estimate is made.
- **Cloud retention scope:** Pricing states 1 day, 7 days, or 90+ days for data queryable in dashboard/API. The privacy policy separately says workflow execution logs may be retained up to 90 days. The pages describe different scopes; the plan's queryable retention is used for plan comparisons.
- **Unpublished after search:** I checked official pricing, terms, privacy, product docs, and repository pages for the currency code, Cloud data-transfer rate, hard spend cap, Cloud inactivity/deletion behavior, task payload maximum, future schedule horizon, full Cloud export, deletion timing for task payloads after account closure, and minimum PostgreSQL/hardware versions. The facts table names the pages tried for each unknown.
- **Changelog access:** The docs site's Changelog link did not load via the page fetch. The GitHub engine releases and official Helm chart changelog were used as alternatives; both showed active maintenance.

## Field problems

- The pricing page mixes GB-hour overage rates with daily or monthly included-volume descriptions for active storage and logs. It does not publish enough billing detail to calculate those overages exactly.
- The docs Changelog and Trust Center pages did not return readable content in the web fetch. Release notes, the official chart changelog, pricing, and the privacy policy were still available and checked.
