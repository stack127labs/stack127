### Small SQL app: standard paid path

Inputs: one production MySQL database; 1GB stored for a full month; 100,000 reads and 20,000 writes; ten peak connections; 5GB internet transfer; 730 hours of desired availability. Express allocates two CPU cores, 2GB RAM, 60GB SSD and 60GB SATA backup space, with a FAQ default of 500 connections. Formula: one Express subscription on the 1mo term = **USD 6.99 for the billing month**. No per-query or 5GB traffic fee is added under the published plan. Do not multiply this monthly price by 730 hours. The storage and connection inputs are below published allocation/default figures, but operation counts do not predict CPU, temporary-space use or latency.
