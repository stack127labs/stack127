## Cost examples: our calculations

Scenario: 100,000 vectors; 1,536 float32 values each; 1 KiB metadata each; 100,000 searches; 10,000 updates per month; 5 GB stated internet transfer. Pinecone's billable index size includes IDs and metadata, and actual query RU depends on namespace size.

| Example | Inputs and formula | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approximate stored payload | 100,000 × (1,536 × 4 bytes + 1,024 bytes metadata + assumed 8-byte ID) | Our calculation: 717,600,000 bytes = 717.6 MB decimal, about 0.7176 GB. Excludes index overhead; this is not a measured billable index size. |
| Read units at the minimum | If one namespace is no larger than 1 GB: 100,000 searches × minimum 0.25 RU/query | Our calculation: at least 25,000 RU. At the Standard range of $16 to $18 per million, about $0.40 to $0.45. Rate endpoint is not mapped to a specific cloud/region, and larger namespace size raises RUs. |
| Writes and transfer | 10,000 updates × minimum 5 WU/request = 50,000 WU minimum; 5 GB egress versus Standard's 100 GB allowance | Our calculation: write charge floor about $0.20 to $0.225 at the Standard $4 to $4.50/M range, if each update stays at its minimum. Egress overage is $0 if the 5 GB is Pinecone egress; actual writes may cost more because old/new record sizes are unknown. |
| Monthly Standard plan | Minimum usage applies to the combined actual monthly usage | Our calculation: $50 minimum if total metered use is below $50; otherwise the bill is actual use. Exact total is incomplete because update sizes, final index size, and whether the stated transfer is Pinecone egress are not specified. |
| Builder option for this scenario | $20/month flat; estimated 0.7176 GB payload, 25,000 minimum RU, at least 50,000 WU, and 5 GB egress against its 10 GB monthly egress quota | Our calculation: likely fits the published Builder caps under those assumptions and costs $20/month. Oversized updates or index overhead could raise usage; excess Builder quotas are blocked, not billed. |

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Pricing lists a 99.95% Enterprise uptime SLA, while the linked Database SLA limits contractual coverage to a qualifying Enterprise advance commitment with an upfront payment and at least a 12-month term. The legal addendum controls its coverage conditions.
- The pricing page says free support is included on Builder and Standard; the linked 2024 Support Policy says support is not included by default unless a paid tier or qualifying commitment applies. The applicable plan/order wording needs confirmation.
- Public pricing publishes read/write rate ranges, says the exact rate varies by cloud and region, and does not pair range endpoints with a named cloud-region. I kept the range intact and did not assign its endpoints to AWS, GCP, or Azure.
- The public pricing text and cost guide explain that egress, import, backup/restore, cross-region restore, and model usage are separately metered, but exact current rates for those items were not exposed in the checked official page text. I checked the Pricing page, calculator, and cost guide.
- The current SDK region enum lists more regions than the Starter comparison table. I treated the Starter row as AWS `us-east-1` and the SDK list as current paid serverless availability; cloud and region still cannot be changed in place after index creation.
- Pinecone's official Blog and Newsroom were checked for acquisition, shutdown, sunset, and product-retirement notices. I found no Pinecone Database shutdown or acquisition notice. I did find the September 4, 2026 SaaS Marketplace retirement and the restriction on creating legacy pod indexes after August 18, 2025. The company blog and newsroom show active product updates, including full-text GA and current Database releases.
- I checked the MSA, Website Terms, Acceptable Use Policy, Privacy Policy, DPA, service-specific supplemental terms, Support Policy, Database SLA, BYOC responsibilities, and security measures. The Website Privacy Policy says it does not govern personal data Pinecone processes for service customers; the MSA/DPA apply to customer data. Exact data-deletion timing after the MSA's retrieval window and backup retention were not published in the checked pages.
- Pinecone Local is covered by a separate EULA. It is not evidence that the managed database can be self-hosted in production.
