## Cost examples — our calculation

These are **our calculation**, checked **2026-11-05**, not vendor quotes or workload benchmarks. The paid example is **AWS N. Virginia, us-east-1, USD**, using the current detailed Operational pricing table. No currency conversion is used. The 730-hour month is the directory comparison assumption; real billing is by clock hours touched. Applicable taxes, optional services and application-host bills are additional. Sources: [regional configuration prices](<https://www.couchbase.com/pricing/>), [billing meters and rounding](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/billing/billing.html>), [credit/fee conditions](<https://www.couchbase.com/CapellaServiceFees/>) and [configuration requirements](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/clusters/databases.html>).

### Small document app

Inputs: one production database, 1 GB stored for the month, 100,000 reads, 20,000 writes, 10 peak connections, 5 GB internet outbound, 730 on-hours. Assume SDK access, one Basic paid node, no Data API or Private Endpoint, and that the workload fits the selected node's memory/CPU/disk. Reads/writes are not individually multiplied by a per-operation tariff. Monthly counts and connection count do not prove the peak load fits.

The listed one-node 2-vCPU/8-GB configuration includes a 50-storage allocation in the price table; you do not buy just 1 GB of disk for this workload. The provider page describes a 50-GB minimum, while general billing uses GiB for disk. Preserve those units and verify the deployed configuration rather than making an invisible conversion.

- **Basic running resource subtotal:** `730 × USD 0.15 = USD 109.50/month`.
- **Same listed node with Developer Pro:** `730 × USD 0.19 = USD 138.70/month`.
- **Complete total:** `node subtotal + actual backup storage charges + transfer charges + any enabled add-ons + tax`. This is **incomplete**, because the public transfer/backup units and plan-credit rates do not yield an unambiguous exact bill for the scenario.
- **Idle but still on:** the same resource subtotal continues. Zero app queries do not release the provisioned node.
- **Turned off:** `off-hours × regional OFF rate + retained backup/storage/add-on/transfer costs`. The exact OFF rate was not obtained publicly from the pricing, billing or fee terms. It is not zero, and the paid cluster turns back on after 30 days; a permanently dormant always-free paid resource is not established.

Free-tier evaluation could have a **USD 0 service subtotal** while it remains eligible and within its limits, but its evaluation-only legal conditions, 72-hour shutdown and 30-day deletion make it a different comparison from the paid production example. Free data storage is publicly inconsistent (8 versus 10 GB). Exact free request/transfer/user quotas were not established, so 5 GB outbound is not assumed unlimited or guaranteed free. See [free lifecycle and migration](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/get-started/create-account.html>), [Preview/free-use conditions](<https://www.couchbase.com/capella-terms/>) and [paid OFF behavior](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/clusters/off-on-database.html>).

### Backup and high-availability variants

- **Bucket backup price input:** the AWS N. Virginia public page quotes USD 0.07 per GB/month; if a measured **1 vendor-billed GB-month** archive uses that rate, `1 × USD 0.07 = USD 0.07`, giving **USD 109.57** for the Basic resource plus that illustrative archive, before transfer/tax. This is **not** a claim that 1 GB of source data produces exactly 1 GB of billed backups. Full/incremental cycles, retention, compression and download files change backup storage, and billing docs instead describe GiB metering.
- **Cloud Snapshot input:** 0.14 **credits** per GB/month in the public AWS example. `measured snapshot storage × 0.14 × applicable USD-per-credit` is the formula. The public credit amount is not silently relabelled USD 0.14/GiB. Snapshot incrementality, backup-copy regions and plan-specific credit purchase rates remain variables.
- **Three-node example:** the current AWS table lists a 3-node 4-vCPU/16-GB/80-storage-per-node configuration. The Developer Pro resource arithmetic is `730 × USD 1.22 = USD 890.60`; Enterprise is `730 × USD 1.96 = USD 1,430.80`. These are conditional listed-configuration subtotals, with replicas/AZ choices to be configured and backup/cross-zone/region transfer added. A Basic three-node configuration is still single-AZ; merely adding nodes does not create datacenter fault tolerance.
- Two independently deployed regional clusters also require both clusters' regional rates plus XDCR traffic and destination indexes. No global replicated total is quoted from a single-region table.

The detailed prices differ from unscoped plan headlines and some older cached renderings. The opened current regional rows are recorded; console/order pricing should resolve a purchase. Backup inputs and limits: [bucket backup types and retention](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/clusters/backup-restore.html>), [snapshot storage and regional copies](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/clusters/cloud-snapshots.html>), [billing units](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/billing/billing.html>). Availability inputs: [AZ and service placement](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/clusters/databases.html>) and [formal SLA](<https://www.couchbase.com/CapellaSLA/>). No backup is assumed solely from a plan's ability to configure backups.

### Full backend and file workloads

**Full app backend:** add 1,000 monthly active users, 1 GB of attachments, 100,000 file reads, 10,000 uploads, 20 GB total internet transfer and 10,000 short function calls. Capella App Services provides authentication/sync and document attachments, but adds running App Service nodes, a load balancer and processed data to the Operational cluster bill. Its first **1 GiB per clock hour** load-balancer-processing allowance is a different meter from internet egress. Node shape, region, per-hour traffic distribution and any external identity/email/SMS services remain unspecified. Eventing also needs a suitable node shape, so the cheapest small-database node cannot be reused as if it ran all functions. The total is **incomplete**. **File-store comparison:** 100 GB retained for a 30-day month, 100,000 writes, 1 million reads, 100 GB outbound, files retained for the month. This exceeds the published free storage figures, and a 50-GB paid allocation alone does not satisfy it. Attachment files must be at most **20 MB** each. A provisioned operational cluster with enough data/replica/index storage plus App Services and transfer would be needed; public sources do not provide a complete independent hot-file tariff or exact request/egress total. This is **not an exact object-store cost**. ### Vector and job workloads

**Vector workload:** 100,000 vectors × 1,536 float32 values, 1 KiB metadata each, 100,000 searches, 10,000 updates, 5 GB internet outbound. Raw payload arithmetic is `100,000 × (1,536 × 4 + 1,024) = 716,800,000 bytes`. This is **not** billed JSON/index size. JSON/base64 representation, index choice, full-vector persistence, quantization, replicas and query filtering change resources.

The 1,536 dimensions fit the documented Search Vector limit. A paid Search Service on a single node needs the 4-vCPU/16-GB shape rather than the cheapest 2-vCPU/8-GB shape. Its AWS Basic regional row is USD 0.29/hour, so the **conditional resource subtotal** is `730 × 0.29 = USD 211.70`, before storage expansion, backup, transfer and model work. Capacity for this dataset/traffic is not measured. Composite/Hyperscale indexes have different placement/training choices; do not treat every index as one flat per-vector tariff.

External embedding APIs add their own bills, or AI Data Plane adds workflow/model meters. Native Hyperscale reranking uses full vectors and extra database work; it is not the same as a priced external semantic-ranker call. AI model/token/cache rates require additional regional model inputs. The complete vector total is **incomplete**. See [Search Vector dimensions](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/vector-search/create-vector-search-index-ui.html>), [index choices](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/vector-index/use-vector-indexes.html>), [node/service requirements](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/clusters/databases.html>), [native vector reranking](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/vector-index/hyperscale-reranking.html>) and [AI billing/promotion dates](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/billing/billing.html>).

**Jobs:** 10,000 one-second jobs with 0.5 vCPU and 0.5 GB memory are not a published Eventing machine reservation or per-invocation plan. A suitable provisioned Eventing node/service group runs JavaScript handlers; source/metadata writes, timer state, HTTP calls and concurrency affect sizing. If a 4-vCPU/16-GB AWS Basic node can host this workload, the same **USD 211.70/730-hour resource subtotal** is an example, with data/backup/transfer added. The fixed 0.5-vCPU/0.5-GB execution isolation, actual capacity and full total are **not established**. Deduplicated mutations also mean 10,000 document changes need not yield 10,000 handler calls. See [function resource settings](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/eventing/add-eventing-functions.html>), [mutation handling](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/eventing/eventing-language-constructs.html>) and [persistent timer behavior](<https://docs.couchbase.com/cloud/eventing/eventing-timers.html>).

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Free data storage:** AWS/Azure provider references say 10 GB, while GCP and the general Free pricing card say 8 GB. These figures are recorded separately, with no invented universal quota.
- **Prices and scope:** the current opened detailed regional rows differ from headline starting prices. Some older vendor search-cache renderings also show different rounded/Enterprise values. Cached snippets were leads; the current opened page supplies the recorded rows. The unscoped headlines remain unclear instead of being declared a second identical-config tariff.
- **Backup and transfer units:** pricing uses GB and mixes USD with credits; billing describes GiB metering and typical credits/GB networking examples. Those were checked against the Fee Addendum, which points to the control-plane consumption rate. No exact conversion or universal USD-per-credit rate was assumed.
- **Missing exact values after search:** free operation/transfer/user/client quotas; hosted connection entitlement; branch quotas; OFF rates; complete attachment/model tariffs; general Eventing retry/failed-message retention; exact function-log retention; and a physical deletion deadline. The fact rows identify the relevant docs and legal pages searched.
- **Legal conditions matter:** the MSA explicitly restricts free/Preview evaluation use, and the fee addendum gives broader no-access deletion rights than the free-cluster workflow alone. The privacy policy excludes customer content for which Couchbase acts as processor; the DPA governs that processing. Region selection is not treated as a legal compliance certificate.
- **Access failures:** direct corporate-page requests returned HTTP 403, but official product, price, acquisition and legal pages were recovered through the web reader. Several guessed docs paths returned the generic documentation home page; current paths were located from the official documentation index. These failures are recorded as `check_failed`, not turned into missing-feature claims.
- **No live account checks:** no signup, login, form submission, private account API, benchmark or restore test was performed. This is public-document evidence, not measured performance or verified application compatibility.
