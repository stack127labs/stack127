## Cost example — our calculation

**Scenario:** One paid OCI tenancy, one month, US East (Ashburn), `us-ashburn-1`; 100 GB average Standard storage, 100,000 non-delete requests, and 100 GB outbound internet transfer. Assumes the tenancy has not used its free storage/request/transfer allowances elsewhere. Uses Oracle's global USD public-cloud list prices; no tax, other OCI services, replication, versioning, archive retrieval, or paid storage tools.

| Input | Formula | Our calculation |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Standard storage | (100 GB − 10 GB free) × USD $0.0255/GB-month | USD $2.295 |
| Requests | (100,000 − 50,000 free) ÷ 10,000 × USD $0.0034 | USD $0.017 |
| Outbound internet transfer | 100 GB is within the first 10 TB/month allowance | USD $0.000 |
| **Total** | USD $2.295 + $0.017 + $0.000 | **USD $2.312/month** |

This is our calculation, not an Oracle quote. Oracle lists storage and transfer in GB; I have not converted GB to GiB. Oracle says public-cloud prices are uniform across regions, while outbound transfer over the free allowance is priced by origin-region group. Taxes and unrelated OCI use are excluded.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Trial storage allowance wording:** The Always Free page says a trial account with credits gets 10 GB each in Standard, Infrequent Access, and Archive, while also saying the first 20 GB in a Free Trial does not consume the USD $300 credit and trial storage is unlimited while credits remain. The same page clearly states that a post-trial Always Free-only account has 20 GB combined. I kept the account states separate and did not add the trial statements together as a single 30 GB promise.
- **Inactivity:** Oracle says a free account remains active if used within the last 60 days, but I did not find a clear Object Storage object-retention rule triggered solely by inactivity. I checked the free-promotion expiry, Always Free resource, and Object Storage lifecycle pages.
- **Cross-region replication transfer price:** Oracle's Object Storage replication guide says data is replicated asynchronously and the overview lists replication-related usage charges, but neither page nor the global price list provided a distinct per-GB network price for this replication path. I did not use public internet egress rates as a substitute.
- **Estimate page:** The official Object Storage cost-estimator route opened as a page shell but exposed no calculator fields or results. The Oracle global price-list PDF, Cloud Price List, Object Storage FAQ, Archive FAQ, Always Free docs, and billing docs supplied the rates and allowances used here.
- **No product closure notice found:** I checked the Object Storage release notes, OCI blog, Oracle News/press newsroom, and Oracle Investor News. The absence of a notice in those pages is not proof that no other notice exists.

## Field problems

- C06's broad “commercial use” question is not answered as a product-specific yes/no in the public FAQ. The standard US Cloud Services Agreement describes internal business use and bars third-party service access or commercial exploitation except as permitted by the order. A public app's end-user access model depends on the customer's applicable agreement and order.
- The Oracle global price list uses GB, while some technical limits use GiB or TiB. Values are left in their source units; no conversions are implied.
