## Lifecycle, sunset and acquisition notices

The umbrella service is operating. No whole-service shutdown or parent acquisition notice was found in the official product overview, current version policy, release notices and legal pages reviewed. Engine-specific retirements are listed below. This is a bounded search, not a guarantee about future availability. Checked 2026-11-09. Sources: [Managed Databases](https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/databases/index.html.md), [version support](https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/databases/version-support-policy/index.html.md), [Terms](https://www.digitalocean.com/legal/terms-of-service-agreement).

- **New PostgreSQL/MySQL Standard clusters are changing now.** From 2026-10-15, accounts that have never created either PostgreSQL or MySQL are restricted to 1/2/4 GiB Standard plans without standby or read-only nodes. From 2026-11-30 the same applies to all accounts creating new clusters. Having created either engine previously, even if deleted, postpones the restriction until November 30. Existing clusters remain exempt, including resizing, forks, restores and adding nodes. Advanced gains Basic Shared CPU 8–64 GiB under the notice; some limits pages disagree, as recorded below. Checked 2026-11-09. [Plan-change notice](https://docs.digitalocean.com/release-notes/upcoming/dbaas-plan-changes/index.html.md).
- **MySQL 8.0 retired on 2026-10-30.** Forced upgrades to 8.4 start in maintenance windows and can disconnect clients even with high availability. Checked 2026-11-09. [MySQL end-of-life notice](https://docs.digitalocean.com/release-notes/upcoming/mysql-8-eol/index.html.md).
- **PostgreSQL Standard 14 reaches end of support on 2026-11-12.** New creation stopped on May 12. Checked 2026-11-09. [Version policy](https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/databases/version-support-policy/index.html.md).
- **New Redis-based Managed Caching cluster creation ended on 2025-04-30 00:00:00 UTC.** The API reference gives the exact cutoff; the launch post gives adjacent April 29/30 dates. Existing Redis clusters are not stated to have been shut down. This record covers current Valkey. Checked 2026-11-09. [Database API reference](https://docs.digitalocean.com/reference/api/reference/databases/) and [Valkey launch](https://www.digitalocean.com/blog/introducing-managed-valkey).

Scope: the six-engine managed service, with PostgreSQL/MySQL Standard versus Advanced separated where behavior changes. DigitalOcean Spaces, App Platform, Functions and Serverless Inference are separate products. The inventory's “hosting and deploy” overlap is reasonable; no apps.md row correction is proposed. Classification is our judgment based on the [engine overview](https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/databases/index.html.md), checked 2026-11-05.
