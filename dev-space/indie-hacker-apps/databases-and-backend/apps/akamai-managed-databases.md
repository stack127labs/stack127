## Cost examples — our calculations

Checked: 2026-11-04. These are our calculations, not Akamai quotes or measured capacity tests. Reference region: Frankfurt, Germany (`eu-central`), using the published Global SQL price table and public type catalog. The [regions](https://api.linode.com/v4/regions) lists Managed Databases capability there; this does not guarantee current stock of every generation or Valkey LA access. Currency: USD; taxes and application-host bills excluded.

Inputs: [nanode](https://api.linode.com/v4/databases/types/g6-nanode-1) gives Shared SQL one-node US$0.024/hour, US$16 monthly cap and three-node US$0.055/hour, US$37 cap. [g6](https://api.linode.com/v4/databases/types) gives G6 Dedicated 4 GB one/two/three-node US$0.102/0.214/0.307 per hour and US$68/143/206 monthly caps. [billing](https://techdocs.akamai.com/cloud-computing/docs/understanding-how-billing-works.md) says round service usage up to a whole hour and apply each published monthly cap. [billingfaq](https://techdocs.akamai.com/cloud-computing/docs/billing-faqs.md) specifies binary storage/network-unit conventions; price-table GB labels are retained here. No annual discount or promotional credit is applied.

The transfer total needs a route. [network](https://techdocs.akamai.com/cloud-computing/docs/network-transfer-usage-and-costs.md) exempts database transfer for customers using IPv6 and documents same-region/private-network exemptions. [manage](https://techdocs.akamai.com/cloud-computing/docs/aiven-manage-database.md) warns about public IPv4, while [pricing](https://www.akamai.com/cloud/pricing/databases) gives US$0.005/GB overage. For **5 chargeable vendor-GB after all applicable allowances**, our calculation is `5 × US$0.005 = US$0.025`. For a documented exempt IPv6 path the database transfer line is US$0. A separate app server can still have its own network charges. The general all-route total is therefore not exact. If “5 GB” means 5,000,000,000 decimal bytes instead of five binary vendor-GB, the conditional fee is `5,000,000,000 ÷ 1,073,741,824 × US$0.005 = US$0.023283064365386962890625`; no silent GB/GiB conversion is applied.
