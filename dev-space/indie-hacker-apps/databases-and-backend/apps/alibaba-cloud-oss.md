## Conflicts and unknowns

- The international price page lists posted product prices in USD. Payment terms allow account settlement in USD, INR, or MYR; INR and MYR settlement uses a transaction-time exchange rate. This is a difference between display currency and payment currency, not a contradiction in the listed OSS rate.
- Storage plans are labeled “Global Regions.” Pay-as-you-go storage, outbound traffic, and request tables use region selectors. The accessible text shows the selector set to China (Hong Kong), but other displayed price tables do not retain their region labels. I use the Hong Kong rates only where the page identifies that selection and leave the rest unmapped.
- I tried direct official pricing page region-query URLs for Singapore and Hong Kong, but the web tool could not open those variants. The international pricing page itself and official regions/endpoints list were accessible. This prevents a full same-price regional grouping for pay-as-you-go rates; it does not make the selected Hong Kong values unknown.
- The public OSS overview gives availability for a multi-AZ design, while the international paid-service SLA lists separate targets by storage class. The service-specific SLA is used for the contractual target; the overview figures remain a description of the multi-AZ design.
- OSS product terms are linked through the current legal index. The Product Terms page's web text is sparse, but the official search-indexed copy gives the OSS hourly metering term. I also checked the Membership Agreement, International Website Terms of Use, Payment and Tax Terms, International Privacy Policy, and OSS-specific SLA.
- No complete country signup eligibility list or full account-level data deletion timetable was found on the official pages checked. Bucket deletion and account-data deletion are separate actions.
- No shutdown or acquisition notice was found on the OSS product-announcement page, release notes, English press room, or OSS blog tag checked.
