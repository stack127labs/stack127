- Current status: active service, with an open incident. On 2026-11-06, Hetzner reported that Object Storage in FSN1 was partially degraded for specific customer buckets; some requests could fail or time out. The incident was still under investigation when checked.
- Scope: Hetzner's managed S3-compatible object storage. Prices here are for Object Storage itself, not Hetzner Cloud servers. Buckets can be placed in FSN1 (Falkenstein, Germany), NBG1 (Nuremberg, Germany), or HEL1 (Helsinki, Finland). Base pricing is account-wide across those locations. EUR examples state whether German VAT is included.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The English and German current pricing FAQs show the base hourly charge, but the English page omits the monthly cap amount and the current extra-storage numeric rate. The German page gives a monthly price of €7.72 including 19% VAT and displays egress at €1.19/TB including 19% VAT.

- The product page's backup copy mentions server-side encryption, but the technical FAQ says objects are not encrypted at rest by default and identifies SSE-C as the available mode. I use the technical FAQ for the default and note the product-page wording as a conflict.
- The 2024 launch announcement's extra-storage price is older than Hetzner's notice that a product-wide price adjustment took effect on 2026-04-01. I do not present that launch figure as current.
