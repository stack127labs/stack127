# Cloudflare Workers KV

**Current status and retirement notice:** standard Workers KV is an active hosted offer on Workers Free and Paid. Legacy REST paths under `/workers/namespaces/` had an announced October 15, 2026 stop date; integrations should use `/storage/kv/namespaces/`. This is an API-path retirement, not a product shutdown. No whole-product sunset or acquisition notice was found in the inspected official overview, pricing and KV changelog. Sources: [Overview](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/), [Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/pricing/), [legacy route deprecation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-07-15-kv-legacy-namespace-routes-deprecation/) and [KV changelog](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/product/kv/), checked 2026-11-05.

**Edition and location note:** KV Instant is a separate private beta with much smaller namespace limits. Standard KV jurisdiction controls became generally available October 2, 2026; they restrict durable storage, while cached copies can remain outside the jurisdiction. Sources: [Getting started, Instant](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/get-started/), [Limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/limits/), [jurisdiction GA notice](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-10-02-kv-jurisdictions-ga/) and [Data location](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/reference/data-location/), checked 2026-11-05.

## Cost examples — our calculations

Rates and limits below were checked on 2026-11-05. Use a **standard KV namespace with eu jurisdiction**, not KV Instant. KV publishes global USD rates rather than a city-specific tariff. The EU constraint covers durable storage; cached data can remain elsewhere. Assume account allocations are otherwise unused. Sources: [KV pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/pricing/), [Workers pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/), [KV limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/limits/) and [Data location](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/reference/data-location/).

### Small key-value application

Adapt the approved small-database workload to key-value access: one production namespace, 1 GB stored for a full month, 100,000 single-key reads, 20,000 single-key writes, ten peak application clients and 5 GB internet transfer. Assume **1 GB is provider-reported billable KV storage**, including whatever storage accounting applies; it is not a conversion from 1 GB of raw payload. No key value exceeds 25 MiB, writes to the same key remain at most one per second, and operations incur no extra list/delete calls or retries. Ten clients are HTTP callers rather than ten SQL sessions. This eventual-consistency service is not a substitute for the reference application's transaction requirements. Sources: [Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/pricing/), [Limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/limits/) and [How KV works](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/concepts/how-kv-works/), checked 2026-11-05.

For application delivery, assume one calling Worker request per KV operation, averaging 1 CPU-ms/request, with logs disabled. That is our workload assumption, not a measured property of KV. External REST access would remove that Worker mapping but introduce separately sourced caller hosting costs. Sources: [KV FAQ, outside Workers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/reference/faq/) and [Workers pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/), checked 2026-11-05.

| Component | Our formula and scope | Our result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid KV reads | 100,000 compared with 10 million/month included | USD 0 additional |
| Paid KV writes | 20,000 compared with 1 million/month included | USD 0 additional |
| Stored data | 1 provider-reported GB for one month; first 1 GB included | USD 0 additional |
| Namespace | One, within first 1,000/account | USD 0 additional |
| Lists/deletes | Assumed zero | USD 0 additional |
| Internet transfer | 5 GB; KV explicitly has no egress charge | USD 0 KV egress |
| Calling Worker | 100,000 + 20,000 = 120,000 requests; 120,000 × 1 = 120,000 CPU-ms | Within Paid 10 million requests / 30 million CPU-ms |
| Paid subscription | One account × USD 5/month | USD 5 |
| Conditional Paid total | USD 5 subscription + USD 0 KV and caller overages | **USD 5/month before tax** |

This is our calculation from [KV Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/pricing/) and [Workers Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/), checked 2026-11-05. The subscription is counted once for the account. The same plan can host other products, but their usage is not assumed free or included in this workload. Taxes are excluded under [Self-Serve terms, 4.4](https://www.cloudflare.com/terms/), checked 2026-11-05.

**Free variant:** use a 30-day operational month and distribute the 100,000 reads across days at no more than 3,334/day; distribute 20,000 writes at no more than 667/day. These are below 100,000 reads/day and 1,000 writes/day. Assume every day's total account use stays within all Free quotas and the namespace's complete stored size remains at or below 1 GB. The calling Worker stays below its distinct 100,000 requests/day and 10 CPU-ms/invocation, given the assumed 1-ms profile. **Our conditional total is USD 0/month**, including KV's explicit zero egress charge. A 1 GB raw payload with uncounted keys/metadata could fail the storage fit, and 20,000 writes concentrated on one day would fail Free even though the monthly total is small. Sources: [KV Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/pricing/), [Limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/limits/) and [Workers Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/), checked 2026-11-05.

The 730-hour always-on assumption adds no allocated KV compute fee: KV's tariff lists operations, storage and namespace count rather than instance-hours. No automatic backup or high-availability add-on is priced into either total. A managed backup/restore entitlement was not established; a user-managed export has additional operations and storage costs. Sources: [Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/pricing/), [API](https://developers.cloudflare.com/api/resources/kv/) and [Wrangler KV commands](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/reference/kv-commands/), checked 2026-11-05.

### Idle project

Retain the same provider-reported 1 GB in one standard namespace for a month, with zero reads, writes, deletes, list calls and Worker requests. No expiration is configured.

- Free: USD 0 operations + USD 0 storage within 1 GB + USD 0 subscription = **USD 0/month** under the stated eligibility/cap assumptions.
- Paid: USD 5 account minimum + USD 0 operations + USD 0 storage above the included 1 GB + USD 0 extra namespaces = **USD 5/month before tax**.

These are our calculations from [KV Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/pricing/) and [Workers Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/), checked 2026-11-05. No fixed inactivity purge deadline was established in the docs or [terms](https://www.cloudflare.com/terms/), [Developer terms](https://www.cloudflare.com/service-specific-terms-developer-platform/) and [DPA](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/), checked 2026-11-05. These estimates are conditional costs for retained data, not a promise of indefinite retention.

### What changes the bill

For a named write-heavy variant, hold storage at 1 provider-reported GB and increase writes to **2 million/month**, still within the same-key rate limit. Excess write count = 2 million − 1 million included = 1 million; 1 million / 1 million × USD 5 = **USD 5 extra KV writes**. Keep 100,000 reads and the assumed 1-ms calling Worker profile: 2.1 million Worker requests and 2.1 million CPU-ms remain within Paid allocations. **Conditional total = USD 5 account minimum + USD 5 KV writes = USD 10/month before tax.** This uses whole excess billing units because KV-specific fractional-unit rounding was not established. Sources: [KV Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/pricing/), [Limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/limits/) and [Workers Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/), checked 2026-11-05.

Bulk read/write transport does not change the billable key count. A 100-key bulk read counts as one external-service operation against the documented per-Worker-invocation limit, but 100 KV read operations for billing. Missing-key reads and administrative inspection also consume operations. Sources: [KV Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/pricing/), [Read API](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/api/read-key-value-pairs/) and [KV FAQ](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/reference/faq/), checked 2026-11-05.

Additional namespace example, separate from the workloads above: 1,050 namespaces held for a full month require approval above the default creation limit. (1,050 − 1,000) × USD 0.10 = **USD 5/month extra namespaces**, plus the USD 5 Paid account minimum and any operation/storage fees. This is our calculation, also illustrated in the vendor's current [KV pricing FAQ](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/pricing/); creation rules are in [Limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/limits/), checked 2026-11-05.

### Scenarios outside this scoped product

A complete 1,000-MAU backend, 100 GB file-store service, managed vector-search app or 10,000 executed jobs has no total here. KV can hold application bytes and schedules, but the scoped service does not itself supply the requested login flows, S3 file delivery, similarity indexes or job execution. Additional Workers execution, Queues, R2, Vectorize/model providers or external hosting need their own workload/rates. A Queues event integration is not bundled job execution. Sources: [KV overview](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/), [KV API](https://developers.cloudflare.com/api/resources/kv/), [Event subscriptions](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/event-subscriptions/) and [KV pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/platform/pricing/), checked 2026-11-05.
