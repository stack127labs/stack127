## Cost examples — our calculation

Prices are the published Cloudflare R2 USD rates checked 2026-11-06: https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/ . These examples cover R2 only. They exclude tax, other Cloudflare products, and source-provider charges. Internet egress from R2 is $0 in both classes.

1. **Standard workload:** 100 GB-month stored, 2 million Class A requests, 15 million Class B requests, 50 GB direct internet egress, one bucket. Assume the account has not used its Standard free allowance. Calculation: `(100 GB-month − 10 free GB-month) × $0.015 + (2M − 1M free) × $4.50/M + (15M − 10M free) × $0.36/M + 50 GB egress × $0 = $1.35 + $4.50 + $1.80 + $0 = USD $7.65/month`.
2. **Infrequent Access workload:** 100 GB-month stored for at least 30 days, 1 million Class A, 5 million Class B, 20 GB retrieved, and 100 GB direct internet egress. IA has no free allowance. Calculation: `100 × $0.01 + 1M × $9.00/M + 5M × $0.90/M + 20 × $0.01 + 100 × $0 = USD $14.70/month`.
3. **Empty idle bucket:** one empty bucket and no requests, with the account below 100,000 buckets. Ongoing R2 usage is USD $0/month in this example. Any Workers or unrelated Cloudflare subscription charges are excluded.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Cloudflare product sources updated at different times:** the current R2 pricing page (last updated October 30, 2026) is used for rates and allowances; the R2 S3 compatibility matrix (October 14, 2026) is used for API support. Older blog pricing claims were not used where the current pricing page is available.
- The R2 price table does not publish separate storage or operation prices for the location hints or jurisdictions. It also does not publish a separate upload-ingress or cross-region transfer rate. I checked the pricing, upload, migration, and data-location pages.
- R2 docs say to complete checkout to add an R2 subscription, and Cloudflare's billing profile docs say a payment method is needed to purchase services. I did not test the dashboard. The pricing page does not state a universal R2-specific card-only requirement; supported billing methods are broader than cards.
- An exact object-deletion schedule after account closure was not found. Cloudflare's privacy policy covers personal information and is not treated as an object-retention schedule. Payment failure has a separate 30-day conditional deletion warning in the billing policy.
- The R2 Enterprise SLA is not generalized to self-serve R2. The public SLA page is specifically titled Enterprise Subscription SLA.
- The official newsroom index was checked, but the acquisition notice was found on Cloudflare's acquisitions-tagged blog. The Deno team joining notice is unrelated to R2.
