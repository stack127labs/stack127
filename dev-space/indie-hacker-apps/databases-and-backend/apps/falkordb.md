### Small vector search app

Inputs: 100,000 vectors, **1,536 float32 values per vector**, **1 KiB = 1,024 bytes of metadata per vector**, 100,000 searches, 10,000 updates and 5 GB internet transfer per month.

Our raw-payload calculation is:

`100,000 × [(1,536 × 4 bytes) + 1,024 bytes] = 716,800,000 bytes`.

This excludes nodes/edges/properties, HNSW graph links, indexes, allocator overhead, replica copies and embedding/reranking generation. It is **not** billed index size. The 1,536 dimensions fit the documented 1–4096 range; the payload cannot fit the 100 MB Free tier. A 2 GB Startup instance would have a **$146/730-hour allocation subtotal only if the complete graph/index fits its 1.5 GB dataset allowance** and the workload meets performance needs. Actual required size and total cost remain unknown.
