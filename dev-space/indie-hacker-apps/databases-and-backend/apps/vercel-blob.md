## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Current Pro billing:** The 2025 Blob GA blog lists fixed Pro allowances (5 GB storage, 100,000 simple operations, 10,000 advanced operations, and 100 GB transfer). The later Pro credit change and current pricing page show a $20 shared usage credit; the September 2026 Flat Rate CDN notice includes Blob Data Transfer inside the team’s shared 1 TB capacity. The current model is used above.
- **Private storage maturity:** The dedicated private-storage guide still carries a Beta label and a March 2026 update date. The newer June 30, 2026 changelog says Private Blob, signed URLs, and OIDC are generally available; that newer notice is used.
- **Not published after search:** Hobby card requirement; inactivity-based Blob deletion/pause; separate object retrieval fees; minimum object size/retention period/early-delete charge; S3-compatible endpoint or operation matrix; Blob backup/replica placement; exact object purge period after deletion; built-in versioning/undelete/object lock; and an exact Blob-specific uptime-credit commitment. ## Field problems

- **C06:** Public pricing does not say whether a card is required for initial Hobby registration. No signup form was submitted.
- **C10/C15:** Pro usage credit, Flat Rate CDN, and regional PAYGO prices are different mechanisms. A single “included Blob quota” would misstate the current plan.
- **C17:** Vercel publishes 20 Blob storage regions and 20 delivery hubs, but its privacy notice says processing can occur in other jurisdictions. A selected store region is not treated as a complete residency guarantee.
- **F05:** “S3-backed” appears in Vercel material, but the official client interface is its own SDK/CLI. No customer-facing S3 endpoint or full compatibility matrix was found.
- **F07:** Durability/availability claims do not establish version history, point-in-time recovery, or a customer-selectable replica.
