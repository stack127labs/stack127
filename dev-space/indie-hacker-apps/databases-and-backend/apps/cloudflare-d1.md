## Current status and retirement notices

Checked **2026-11-05**. Core D1 is active and generally available; the official announcement dates GA to April 1, 2024. No complete-product shutdown or parent acquisition notice was found in this bounded official search. [Current overview](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/), [GA announcement](https://blog.cloudflare.com/building-d1-a-global-database/), [current notices](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/product/d1/).

- **Old alpha databases are retired.** Official pages differ on the query cutoff: August 22 versus August 23, 2024. Alpha backup access ended July 1, 2025. The old migration walkthrough remains online, but its alpha-backup commands no longer provide a current recovery route. [Migration guide](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/alpha-migration/), [release history](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/release-notes/).
- **Read replication remains Beta.** This is a different stage from generally available core D1; the agreement’s Beta terms apply. [Replication guide](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/best-practices/read-replication/), [Agreement §5](https://www.cloudflare.com/terms/).
- **Free daily caps:** binding/REST queries fail at the daily row caps from September 1, 2026; stored data remains intact. [Notice](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-09-01-d1-free-tier-limit-enforcement/).
- **Deleted-database recovery:** undelete is limited to seven days, as announced October 23, 2026. [Notice](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-10-23-undelete-seven-day-window/).
- **Query response bound:** the 50 MB result limit took effect November 4, 2026; REST import/export operations are exempt. [Notice](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-10-30-query-result-size-limit/).
- **Current paid database-count pricing:** 100,000 databases included, then USD 0.10/database/month, even empty or idle. Current account creation cap is 1,000,000; older limits and preview search results are not used. [Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/pricing/), [limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/limits/).

## Cost examples — our calculation

Checked **2026-11-05**. Currency **USD**, excluding tax. Location assumption: a newly created primary with **`us` jurisdiction**; the public tariffs do not list a regional surcharge. This is a pricing assumption, not an account deployment. Sources: [location](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/configuration/data-location/), [D1 tariff](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/pricing/), [Workers tariff](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/), [tax clause §4.4](https://www.cloudflare.com/terms/).

**General formula:** let `R` and `W` be actual metered rows read/written, `S` billed GB-months and `N` whole-month databases. With otherwise-unused account allowances:

`D1 usage = max(R − 25,000,000,000, 0)/1,000,000 × $0.001 + max(W − 50,000,000, 0)/1,000,000 × $1 + max(S − 5, 0) × $0.75 + max(N − 100,000, 0) × $0.10`

`Workers Standard = $5 + max(requests − 10,000,000, 0)/1,000,000 × $0.30 + max(CPU-ms − 30,000,000, 0)/1,000,000 × $0.02`

The minimum is once per account; do not add it twice when D1 and functions share that account. These full-month calculations do not establish unreported proration or invoice rounding. Include any additional product charges. Source definitions: [D1 meters](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/pricing/), [query versus row counts](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/reference/faq/), [Workers Standard](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/).

### 1. Small SQL app

Inputs: one production database; **1 GB total billed storage**, including indexes; 100,000 app reads, 20,000 app writes, ten peak app connections and 5 GB transfer; data retained all month. A 730-hour allocated-instance charge is inapplicable to D1.

**Free:** the requested single database exceeds the **500 MB/database** cap. The 5 GB account allowance does not remove it. Splitting across databases would change the workload and transaction scope, so no free equivalent is priced. [Capacity limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/limits/).

**Paid, conditional worked mapping:** assume observed total meters end up at `R=100,000`, `W=40,000` (for example, single-row writes plus one affected index, with all other billed work included in these totals), one database and 1 GB-month. Assume 120,000 Worker requests at **10 ms CPU/request**, no other account use, and ordinary included logging only:

`CPU = 120,000 × 10 = 1,200,000 ms`

`D1 = $0; Workers = $5 + $0 request excess + $0 CPU excess; known total = $5/month before tax`

The input-to-meter mapping is **our assumption**, not a measured guarantee. Real scans, write-related reads, migrations, retries and indexes change it. If the full 1 GB meant logical payload excluding indexes, storage also changes. Ten simultaneous app clients are not ten simultaneous connections in one Worker invocation; no performance benchmark was run. [Meter definitions](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/pricing/), [binding concurrency](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/limits/), [throughput caveats](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/reference/faq/).

**After included usage is consumed elsewhere in the account:** under the same metered mapping, marginal D1 resource cost is:

`100,000/1,000,000 × $0.001 + 40,000/1,000,000 × $1 + 1 × $0.75 = $0.7901`

That is **USD 0.7901 before invoice rounding**, not a new zero-allowance plan. If the 120,000 Worker requests/1.2 million CPU-ms are also all above their included grants:

`Worker excess = 0.12 × $0.30 + 1.2 × $0.02 = $0.036 + $0.024 = $0.060`

`marginal D1 + Worker usage = $0.8501`

The shared **USD 5 account minimum** still exists, but should not be allocated twice to a project when already paid. As an illustrative allocated total, `$5 + $0.8501 = $5.8501`, plus the other workload that consumed the allowances. The actual invoice depends on account-wide use. No additional database-count charge applies to this one-database example within its count grant.

**Backup variant:** included Paid Time Travel retains 30 days; no added history/restore fee. Independent long-term exports require separate Workflows/R2 costs. [Recovery](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/reference/time-travel/).

**Read-replica variant:** Beta replication adds no separate replica storage/compute fee, so the same meters yield the same conditional subtotal. A write-HA/failover package or recovery-time SLA is not established. [Replication pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/best-practices/read-replication/).

### 2. Full app backend

Inputs: scenario 1 plus 1,000 MAU, 1 GB independent files, 100,000 file reads, 10,000 uploads, 20 GB total transfer and 10,000 function calls.

For an explicitly illustrative function profile, each call lasts **one elapsed second**, uses **10 ms CPU**, and requests **0.5 GB memory**. D1 does not allocate that worker size. No price is assigned to an unsupported or unverified memory configuration.

`full total = D1 + application authentication + file storage/operations + compatible function host + transfer + any email/SMS`

The D1 component remains subject to scenario 1’s metered mapping. [Workers CPU/request pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/) is not a GB-second quote, and elapsed duration alone does not determine CPU. Auth, R2/files and other services must be selected separately. The full total is **unknown**. [Product roles](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/storage-options/). Email/SMS costs and delivery are not included.

### 3. Small file store

Inputs: 100 GB average hot files for 30 days; 100,000 writes, one million reads, 100 GB transfer; objects retained throughout.

`file total = GB-months + object operations/retrieval + delivery`

**Not a D1-only workload.** Database BLOBs are not an independent object-store API. Cloudflare identifies R2 separately, so no D1 storage price or D1 egress exemption is assigned to this file-store total. No total until the object service, class and delivery method are selected. [Storage-product map](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/storage-options/).

### 4. Small vector search app

Inputs: 100,000 vectors × 1,536 float32 values; 1 KiB metadata/vector; 100,000 searches, 10,000 updates and 5 GB transfer.

`raw bytes = 100,000 × (1,536 × 4 + 1,024) = 716,800,000 bytes = 0.7168 decimal GB`

This is **our raw-payload calculation**, excluding indexes/row overhead. It does not fit one Free database’s 500 MB cap; more importantly, D1’s documented extensions do not establish native approximate vector indexing. [D1 extensions](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/sql-api/sql-statements/), [capacity](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/limits/).

Cloudflare points to Vectorize for vector search. Storing arrays/BLOBs in D1 alone does not establish a vector-search implementation, query meter, latency or model budget. `total = chosen vector index + query/update use + model generation/reranking + optional SQL metadata`; **unknown** until those inputs exist. [Product roles](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/storage-options/).

### 5. Small job workload, including retry/wait variant

Inputs: 10,000 jobs × one active second, requested 0.5 vCPU/0.5 GB memory where supported; peak five concurrent; no retries/waits in base.

`base active work = 10,000 × 1 = 10,000 aggregate seconds`

D1 is **not a worker host or job orchestrator**. Queue/orchestration, compatible worker compute, logs and database access need separate selection. `full total = worker + orchestration + D1 + logs + transfer`; **unknown**. A SQL query timeout or a Workers CPU-ms rate is not a quote for the requested machine. [Product separation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/storage-options/), [Workers pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/).

Retry/wait variant: 10% of jobs receive one extra one-second execution; each original waits five seconds:

`active = 10,000 + 1,000 = 11,000 seconds`

`waiting = 10,000 × 5 = 50,000 seconds`

`aggregate active + wait = 61,000 seconds`

This is aggregate work/wait, not elapsed service uptime. D1 charges resulting row work/storage, not these job seconds. D1’s automatic read retries and app-managed write retries do not guarantee exactly-once job side effects. [Query retry behavior](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/best-practices/retry-queries/). Worker/orchestrator waiting and retry costs remain outside this incomplete estimate.

### 6. Idle project

Inputs: retain the same single 1 GB database all month; zero queries, requests or jobs.

`D1 = $0 row work + $0 storage excess + $0 database-count excess = $0`

`Paid account minimum = $5; conditional standalone-account total = $5/month before tax`

If the account’s storage inclusion is already consumed elsewhere, keeping this extra 1 GB adds `1 × $0.75 = $0.75/month`; illustrative allocated sum is **USD 5.75**. Idle databases above the count grant remain charged separately. [D1 idle/count/storage meters](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/pricing/), [account minimum](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/).

Free cannot retain this **one 1 GB database**. A smaller eligible Free database may have no plan charge, but no fixed inactivity-deletion deadline or guaranteed never-delete rule was established in the inspected [FAQ](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/reference/faq/), [terms](https://www.cloudflare.com/terms/), [Developer Platform terms](https://www.cloudflare.com/service-specific-terms-developer-platform/), [Privacy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) and [DPA](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/). Export data you need before explicit deletion or closure.
