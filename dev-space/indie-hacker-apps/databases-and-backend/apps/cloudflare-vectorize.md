## Cost examples: our calculations

Vectorize's rates are account-level and do not vary by region on the published price table. The Vectorize page uses `$` without an ISO currency code; the separate Workers plan page explicitly labels its $5 minimum as USD. These examples are product usage estimates, not vendor quotes.

|---|---|---|
| Small vector search app, Workers Paid | 100,000 vectors × 1,536 float32 values each; 100,000 searches/month; 10,000 updates/month; one index; 1 KiB metadata per vector; 5 GB internet transfer. Query dimensions = (100,000 stored vectors + 100,000 searches) × 1,536 = 307.2M. Stored dimensions = 100,000 × 1,536 = 153.6M. Query charge = (307.2M − 50M included) × $0.01/M = $2.572. ## Conflicts and unknowns

- **V1 versus V2 limits:** current Limits (updated Oct 30, 2026) says V2 permits 20 million vectors/index. The January 23, 2026 product changelog said 10 million at that time. The current Limits page is newer; the V1 limits remain separately listed. The transition docs say V1 is deprecated, existing operations remain functional, but no shutdown date for existing V1 indexes is stated.
- **V1 migration:** the transition page describes manual transition and says automated migration was planned in older material. The current docs still provide the legacy transition guidance; no automatic-migration status or V1 end-of-service date was found. Checked the [transition guide](https://developers.cloudflare.com/vectorize/reference/transition-vectorize-legacy/), [Vectorize changelog](https://developers.cloudflare.com/vectorize/platform/changelog/), and [product changelog](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/product/vectorize/).
- **Free overage:** searched the [Vectorize pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/vectorize/platform/pricing/), [limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/vectorize/platform/limits/), and [Cloudflare self-serve terms](https://www.cloudflare.com/terms/). They do not state whether free use hard-stops, bills, or changes plan after an included quota is exceeded.
- **Data location and account eligibility:** searched the Vectorize overview, index creation docs, terms, and [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). The pages establish global service language and general privacy terms, not a Vectorize index region choice or country eligibility list.
- **Backups and deletion:** searched Vectorize API/list docs, pricing, terms, and privacy policy. The API supports listing, getting, deleting vectors/indexes, and migration. No automatic backup schedule, recovery window, account-closure purge deadline, or deletion certificate is stated for vector data.
- **Blog/newsroom:** checked the [Vectorize blog tag](https://blog.cloudflare.com/tag/vectorize/) and [Cloudflare press releases](https://www.cloudflare.com/press/press-releases/). The newer AI Search launch is a separate service; no Vectorize sunset/acquisition notice appeared in those lists.

## Field problems

- Vectorize requires a Workers Free or Paid account. The account-plan fee and Worker execution costs are separate from Vectorize dimension and index charges; they are shown separately in the table and examples.
- The price table uses `$` without an ISO currency code or region. I kept its currency symbol and did not label Vectorize's rates USD.
- Free quota behavior after exhaustion, index data location, automatic backup retention, and final data-purge timing were not specified in the official pages searched. These remain unknown rather than being treated as no fee, no retention, or global-region choice.
- Vectorize and AI Search overlap in Cloudflare's AI product family, but AI Search has its own managed storage, hybrid search, and pricing. Its rates and features were excluded from Vectorize values.
- The Vectorize event subscription feature publishes index lifecycle events through Queues; it is not a per-vector realtime change stream or offline sync system.
- `fields.md` combines generic database and vector fields. I used D fields for index semantics and operational limits, then V fields for dedicated vector limits and search behavior. File and job modules and the self-host field do not describe this managed vector service and are marked not applicable.
