Source: https://docs.sevalla.com/platform/oci-migration.md (status / databases sections; checked 2026-11-05).

**Retired endpoints:** old `<cluster>.proxy.sevalla.app` hostnames/ports 30000–30999 were disabled October 15, 2026. Use the current external hostname/port from the dashboard. Sources: https://docs.sevalla.com/databases/connect.md and https://docs.sevalla.com/platform/oci-migration.md (legacy endpoint sections; checked 2026-11-05).

The lifecycle-first official search identified these transitions, rather than a portfolio-wide shutdown or a new owner replacing Kinsta. Current docs/pricing/status remain accessible. This is a bounded finding. Sources: https://docs.sevalla.com/databases/overview.md, https://docs.sevalla.com/billing/pricing.md, https://status.sevalla.com/ (checked 2026-11-05).

## Cost examples — our calculation

Checked 2026-11-05. Use **new OCI Frankfurt, eu-frankfurt-1**, 730 running hours, before tax, no other paid app resources. The published database table has no regional multiplier. Rates/placement: https://docs.sevalla.com/platform/locations.md, https://docs.sevalla.com/billing/pricing.md, https://docs.sevalla.com/billing/overview.md. Backup inputs: https://docs.sevalla.com/databases/create.md, https://docs.sevalla.com/databases/backups.md, https://docs.sevalla.com/object-storage/overview.md.

### Small SQL/document app

Approved inputs: **one production database, 1 GB stored, 100,000 reads, 20,000 writes, 10 peak connections, 5 GB Internet transfer, 730 hours**.

- **DB1 price floor:** $5 for 0.25 CPU/0.25 GB RAM/1 GB total disk. The disk includes engine files, metadata, logs and indexes, so the scenario's full 1 GB logical app data is not a verified fit. MySQL/MariaDB also have an explicit DB1 production-memory warning.
- **Conditional PostgreSQL DB2 runtime:** 730 × $34 / 730 = **$34** for 0.5 CPU, 2 GB RAM and 5 GB disk. Query CPU/RAM, actual physical disk and connection fit remain untested. Limits publishes 100 connections for OCI DB1 PostgreSQL, but does not supply every DB2 client ceiling.
- **Native database transfer:** the published no-charge internal/external policy gives **$0** for 5 GB. The app-domain bandwidth meter is not applied to this database TCP connection.
- **Reads/writes:** no per-SQL-operation meter appears in the database tariff. Counts do not prove the selected size performs adequately.
- **New OCI backup bucket:** let **B** be the average of the bucket's hourly peak stored GB over these 730 hours. Storage = **$0.02 × B**. Conditional database-plus-backup component = **$34 + $0.02B**. Actual dump size/compression/occupancy is unknown, so **an exact all-in total is not established**.
- **Explicit occupancy illustration:** if measured backups occupy exactly 7 GB at every hourly peak throughout the period, 7 × $0.02 = **$0.14**, total **$34.14**. This is our hypothetical steady-state occupancy, not a claim that every 1 GB database produces seven 1 GB dumps or reaches steady state instantly.
- **MySQL/MariaDB alternative:** same $34 DB2 runtime plus actual bucket cost; DB2 or larger is the documented production recommendation. Their semantics/memory behavior are not assumed identical to PostgreSQL.

Overhead/limit sources: https://docs.sevalla.com/databases/overview.md, https://docs.sevalla.com/platform/limits.md; native traffic/size rates: https://docs.sevalla.com/billing/pricing.md. No MongoDB/document-native managed engine is in this product list.

- **Eligible $20 credit:** if the whole credit remains valid and unused by other services, max(0, $34 + $0.02B − $20) = **$14 + $0.02B** before tax; at assumed B = 7, **$14.14**. Sources: https://sevalla.com/get-started/, https://docs.sevalla.com/billing/overview.md.

Inputs: **100,000 vectors × 1,536 float32 values**, **1 KiB metadata/vector**, **100,000 searches**, **10,000 updates**, **5 GB database transfer**.

- Metadata: 100,000 × 1,024 = **102,400,000 bytes**.
