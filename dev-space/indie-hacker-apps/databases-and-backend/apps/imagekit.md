- **apps.md status:** `active` (row checked 2026-11-04)
- **Scope:** Hosted media storage and delivery, image/video processing, and digital asset management (DAM). ImageKit also connects to a customer-owned bucket or server; that origin storage remains separate from ImageKit's Media Library.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Currency:** plans show `$` but the plans, billing guide, and terms do not name a currency code or regional pricing table. Values and calculations retain `$` without assuming USD.
- **Video transformation storage:** the pricing FAQ says video transformations add to storage, while the pricing-parameters guide says internal image/video transformation cache is not charged. The pages do not define the boundary. Persistent DAM outputs should be budgeted as storage; the exact treatment of internal video cache remains unclear.
- **Image limits:** the plan table and general transformation guide disagree on some image input/output limits. They may describe distinct upload and processing checks, but the pages do not explain the distinction. Keep the table values separate and confirm if a workload depends on a boundary value.
- **Original Cache Storage:** docs say the Enterprise option is metered, but no public rate or allowance was found in plans, pricing docs, or the feature guide.
- **DAM-only extensions cap:** the price table renders the DAM Pro default cap as “undefined units.” The pricing guide says the account can show paid-plan limits and support can adjust them; it does not settle the public DAM-only cap.
- **SLA:** a marketing page states 99.9%, and the terms refer to an SLA, but I did not find public coverage, measurement, exclusions, or credit terms in the plans, terms, or security pages.
- **Deletion:** the Terms provide a 30-day download window after termination, then permit deletion; the Privacy Policy says content is deleted after account deletion without giving an exact schedule.
- **Pages tried for inactivity/retention:** plans, pricing parameters, billing and subscription, Terms, Privacy Policy, asset versioning, and DAM upload/overview. They do not publish an inactivity timer, a minimum storage duration, or a precise post-termination deletion date.
- **Newsroom:** I checked the official blog and searched the official site for newsroom/press and acquisition, shutdown, or sunset notices. No separate newsroom page or such announcement surfaced. The Terms' 30-day discontinuation clause is contractual language only.

## Field problems

- The plans page has two separate pricing selectors. Its first visible plan table is Complete Media Processing + DAM; the official indexed page also exposes a separate DAM-only table. Their free storage amounts and delivery scope differ, so values are split by plan type.
- “Bandwidth,” storage, Video Processing Units, Extension Units, seat count, domains, purge requests, and original-cache storage are separate meters. Transforming an image is generally not charged per variant, while video processing and AI/extension operations can use separate units.
- The general transformation limits and plan comparison do not align on all image sizes. They are kept as separate sourced records with the disagreement noted rather than silently merged.
- Some list prices use `$` with no published currency code. No currency conversion was made.
