## Conflicts and unknowns

- The product homepage summarizes the Free Tier as “5 GiB” and “55,000 operations.” The detailed Free Tier table specifies 5 GB-months, 5,000 Class A operations, and 50,000 Class B operations. I use the detailed table and preserve its units; the operation total is the same.
- Current regional prices are shown for Iowa (`us-central1`) only. They should not be treated as prices for every region, dual-region, multi-region, or zone.
- The pricing page is the source for current rates. The pricing-change page includes older announced changes and SKU mappings but does not show a clear effective date beside every changed price. Where historical text and current tables differ, this file uses the live pricing table and labels the selected location.
- No Cloud Storage idle deletion interval or product-specific monthly minimum was found after searching the pricing page, storage FAQ, lifecycle docs, Free Tier page, and Google Cloud Terms.
- The official Cloud Storage blog index, Google company-announcements page, and product release notes were checked for acquisition or closure notices. No such notice appeared in those pages during this review.
