## Cost examples: our calculations

Use AWS US East (Ohio), `aws-us-east-2`, one root branch, no replica/extra branch, no history/snapshots in base variants, no private network or AI Gateway. Assume stated data retained for a full month. Rates: [Neon plans](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/plans), Price/Compute/Storage/Functions/Object Storage/Public network transfer, checked 2026-11-04. Meter definitions: [Usage and cost calculations](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/usage-calculations), checked 2026-11-04. All formulas are our calculations, not vendor quotes. Tax treatment and invoice rounding are unresolved.

| Scenario | Inputs and formula | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small SQL app, Free | 1 GB database, 100,000 reads, 20,000 writes, 10 peak connections, 5 GB egress; Free allows up to 100 CU-hours. Read/write counts do not determine compute. | Incomplete. Conditional $0 if all usage and eligibility fit Free; not a guaranteed workload price. |
| Small SQL app, Launch | Let C = monthly CU-hours. C × $0.106 + 1 GB-month × $0.35 + max(5 GB − 500 GB, 0) × $0.10. | $0.35 + $0.106C before tax and backups/history. C unknown. |
| Always-on SQL variant | Additional assumption: 0.25 CU sufficient for 730 hours; sleep disabled. 0.25 × 730 = 182.5 CU-hours; 182.5 × $0.106 + $0.35 = $19.695. | $19.695 unrounded arithmetic, not evidence that compute meets workload. The 0.25 CU/730-hour illustration comes from [pricing, Low load](https://neon.com/pricing), checked 2026-11-04. |
| Idle SQL, Launch | Already asleep entire month; same 1 GB, zero requests, no retained backups/history. 0 × $0.106 + 1 × $0.35. | $0.35 accrued; invoices below $0.50 not collected per Price section. Do not call storage free or infer later balance treatment. |
| Full backend, Launch | SQL scenario plus 1,000 auth MAU, 1 GB files, 100,000 file reads, 10,000 uploads, 20 GB total egress, 10,000 function calls. Let A/W = active/waiting Capacity-Hours. $0.35 + $0.023 + $0.106C + $0.10A + $0.025W + 10,000/1,000,000 × $0.60 + max(20 − 500, 0) × $0.10. | Idle full backend, Launch | Same 1 GB database and 1 GB files, zero execution/transfer, no history/backups. 1 × $0.35 + 1 × $0.023. | Conditional $0.373 accrued subtotal, below collection threshold. F03 minimum-storage rules unresolved. |

Full-backend Free would exceed its 5 GB egress/project/month at 20 GB, so cannot promise uninterrupted $0 hosting. Query counts cannot determine CU-hours. Function duration/active-waiting split are absent from the generic approved backend scenario, so A/W remain unknown. The generic job scenario's 0.5 vCPU/0.5 GB does not match published fixed 2048 MiB and an unstated vCPU allocation; no comparable job total invented. Model costs, external email/SMS, migration source-provider costs and backup/history variants remain separate. File-storage minimum size/duration is unknown, so backend totals are conditional.
