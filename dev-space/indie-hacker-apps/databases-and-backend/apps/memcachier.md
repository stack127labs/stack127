### Idle/free recovery and changing plans

For a free cache at zero activity, the known recurring plan price remains **$0**; after **30 days of inactivity** it becomes inaccessible until re-enabled in dashboard Settings ([Disabled Caches](https://www.memcachier.com/documentation/disabled-caches)). The docs do not guarantee that all stored values survive this interval. The calculation is not a promise of a continuously live free application or durable retained data.

Moving from free/development to production **clears the cache**, even though other plan changes generally preserve it ([plan-change exception](https://www.memcachier.com/documentation/upgrading-downgrading)). Refill cost, extra upstream database traffic and client/cloud network charges during a refill are unknown. A reasonable application must tolerate cache misses and repopulate from its authoritative data source. Paid Heroku add-ons do not stop their plan accrual merely because the application is idle or its compute sleeps; deprovisioning removes access and starts the provider's several-day in-memory removal process ([retention/destruction](https://www.memcachier.com/legal/security)).

### Other reference workloads

- **Full backend:** the reference adds 1,000 monthly active users, 1 GB files, 100,000 file reads, 10,000 uploads, 20 GB internet traffic and 10,000 function calls. No native app-user/file/function bundle is established by [the product overview](https://www.memcachier.com/documentation/getting-started), [plans](https://www.memcachier.com/pricing) or [cache-only policy](https://www.memcachier.com/legal/security). Their providers and execution duration/RAM are unspecified. **No full-backend total** is supported.
- **100 GB hot file store:** a 100 GB cache plan is not a durable object/file bucket. The 1 MB combined object limit and no-cache-backup policy also differ materially from file storage. **No applicable native file-store total** is offered.
- **Vector app:** the 100,000 × 1,536-FLOAT32 vector scenario, metadata, 100,000 searches and 10,000 updates does not fit a documented native vector-search/index API here. Cached embeddings would still need an external search engine/models. **No native vector-query total** is offered; ordinary cache capacity is not a vector-index price.
- **Jobs:** 10,000 one-second jobs, 0.5 vCPU/0.5 GB worker RAM and five concurrent workers require another execution host. Our timing calculation is **10,000 × 1 = 10,000 active worker-seconds**. One full retry each would make **20,000 active seconds**; 30 seconds' wait each adds **300,000 waiting seconds**. Whether waiting/retries consume billed compute depends on that external host/framework. MemCachier can cache outputs/counters at its capacity price, but supplies neither the worker shape nor a native durable job queue. **No all-in job total** is supported.

A free cache, paid cache, durable database, model service and worker host solve different parts of an app. None of the missing components is entered as zero.
