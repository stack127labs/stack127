## Conflicts and unknowns

- The Free cluster page calls its included support “Standard support.” The pricing/support pages describe Free support as community or best effort and reserve the support portal for paying customers. I recorded this as a conflict.
- Pricing publishes resource-based hourly Cloud billing, while exact vCPU, RAM, disk, backup, GPU, and model rates are only available from the interactive Cloud calculator/console. The calculator page opened but returned a JavaScript-required message, so I did not invent a paid quote or attach a price to an unverified region.
- Qdrant Cloud's legal agreement allows invoice currencies of EUR and/or USD by order, while payment docs show marketplace Resource Usage Units. The applicable Cloud Service Order or marketplace invoice determines the actual currency.
- Pricing says EU customers can select EU regions, but that does not establish that every customer data copy, support record, or subprocessor stays in the chosen region. The DPA and privacy policy were checked; no blanket residency guarantee is claimed.
- Cloud paid clusters can be manually suspended, but the public automation and billing pages did not say which charges stop. The calculator's exact suspension/proration treatment remains unknown.
- The Cloud create-cluster page says Free clusters have “Standard support”; pricing and support pages instead indicate community/best-effort access. Confirm the actual support entitlement before relying on ticket response times.
- Qdrant's status page recorded a Cloud UI/login incident on 2026-11-02; it said existing database clusters and API operations continued working.
- The company site has a blog and “What's New” navigation, but I did not find a separate newsroom. I checked the blog and status page for company acquisition, service retirement, and shutdown announcements.
- The general website Terms are dated 2021-12-10 and define the website service. I also read the current Cloud Service Agreement, Privacy Policy, and Cloud DPA; Cloud customer data terms are recorded from those service-specific pages.
