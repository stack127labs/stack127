## Cost examples: our calculations

These adapt the approved small SQL/document workload into a key-value workload. They do not imply SQL queries or transactions can be moved unchanged. Region: AWS `us-east-1`, one database, no optional read regions, no Prod Pack, backups, search, vectors or paid support. Use 1 GB average logical data for a full month, 100,000 single GET commands and 20,000 single SET commands, 10 peak TCP connections and 5 GB external transfer. Assume each operation fits request/record limits; connection-cap fit remains unverified. No additional maintenance, SDK retry, transaction or TTL commands assumed. Rates/allowances: [Redis Pricing](https://upstash.com/pricing/redis), PAYG details, storage, bandwidth, database-count and Fixed tables; checked 2026-11-04. Replication scope: [Replication](https://upstash.com/docs/redis/features/replication), checked 2026-11-04.

Let S be daily-average billed storage in GB across all billable replicas/regions. Its mapping from the 1 GB logical dataset is not established. Let T be total billable bandwidth, including any internal replication traffic. The scenario fixes internet traffic, not every component of T.

| Scenario | Inputs and formula | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free, 1 GB workload | 120,000 commands/month fits 500,000 allowance, 5 GB internet fits 10 GB, but 1 GB data exceeds both conflicting 250/256 MB limits. | Free does not fit this data size. Not a $0 alternative for the stated workload. |
| PAYG active command subtotal | (100,000 GET + 20,000 SET) / 100,000 × $0.20. No read regions, so no cross-region write multiplier. | $0.24 command subtotal. Not a complete invoice. Billing rounding/minimum not established. |
| PAYG active formula | $0.24 + storage_charge(S) + max(T − 200 GB, 0) × $0.03/GB + any unestablished minimum invoice. First 10 databases have no count fee. | Incomplete. If S ≤ 1 GB, published free storage allowance implies $0 storage. Above 1 GB, pricing wording does not clearly distinguish $0.25 × S from $0.25 × (S − 1); do not choose silently. |
| PAYG idle, 1 GB logical data | Zero commands, no new writes/traffic assumed; retained data still occupies replicas. 0 × $0.20 + storage_charge(S). | Incomplete storage-only total; do not promise $0. Paid terms reserve deletion after 3 idle months, so zero-request retention is not indefinite. |
| Fixed 1 GB, active or idle | One primary/no optional read region; published 1 GB data and 100 GB bandwidth tier; no command-count charge. $20/month + 0 × $10/read-region. | Conditional $20/month before tax/add-ons if dataset and all metered bandwidth fit tier. Ten peak TCP connections still need a current limit. Fixed remains billed while idle. |
| Prod Pack variant | Add $200/database/month to whichever eligible paid base plan applies. Fixed 1 GB: $20 + $200. | $220/month base plus tax/any other charges. Latest daily backup included; older retained backups have an unknown rate here. |

No free allowance for commands carries into paid plans. The free storage/bandwidth specifically listed for PAYG is separate. No CPU-hour conversion is needed for plain GET/SET billing. Backup-inclusive PAYG total remains incomplete because backup rates and billable replica storage are unresolved. Free inactivity archival also prevents promising an unchanged live endpoint for an idle month. No total for standalone Upstash Vector, QStash, Blob or a full backend bundle is implied.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Free capacity: [pricing](https://upstash.com/pricing/redis), checked 2026-11-04, gives 250 MB in plan cards/FAQ/all-plans table and 256 MB in the comparison. Search results also show an older 256 MB version. Treat current direct-page inconsistency as unresolved, not a unit conversion.
- Free command quota: [Redis FAQ](https://upstash.com/docs/redis/help/faq) retains 10,000/day, while [pricing](https://upstash.com/pricing/redis) and the [March 11, 2025 change](https://upstash.com/blog/redis-new-pricing) say 500,000/month. All checked 2026-11-04. Prefer current price schedule provisionally and keep conflict visible.
- Inactivity: [FAQ](https://upstash.com/docs/redis/help/faq) promises archival after at least 30 idle days with recovery; [terms](https://upstash.com/trust/terms.pdf), Resource Inactivity, reserve deletion after one week Free/three months paid. Checked 2026-11-04. Archive retention and precedence not established.
- Deletion: [terms](https://upstash.com/trust/terms.pdf) mention six-month cancellation and possible immediate deletion/backups, [privacy](https://upstash.com/trust/privacy.pdf) describes profile deletion within 30 days subject to retention, and [account FAQ](https://upstash.com/docs/common/account/faq) says data unrecoverable.
