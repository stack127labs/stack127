**Current status and licensing notice:** Restate is active. Its server repository's current license is Business Source License 1.1 (BSL 1.1), not an OSI open-source license. It permits production use for the licensee's own services and internal deployments, but bars running a public Restate platform for third parties. The license history dates the BSL change to 2023-08-07. Each licensed release converts to Apache License 2.0 four years after release.

No shutdown or acquisition notice was found. Restate's 2026-09-30 post reports a $20 million Series A, which is funding, not an acquisition. ## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Free Cloud allowance:** the current pricing comparison/FAQ says 100,000 actions/month; the Cloud marketing page and 2025 public launch post say 50,000. The pricing table is used for current plan values, but the disagreement remains recorded as conflicting.
- **Self-host license wording:** the pricing page calls self-hosted Restate open source under a permissive license. The repository's current BSL 1.1 LICENSE explicitly says it is not an open-source license; it permits the licensee's own production use and bars a public Restate platform for third parties. The repository license controls the license description.
- **Cloud pricing and terms:** the current pricing page lists paid monthly plans and action overages. The public Terms still say Cloud use is free. The pricing table is recorded for current plan amounts, but the legal-document conflict remains unresolved.
- **Availability:** the pricing table advertises plan-level SLA percentages, while the general terms say availability cannot be guaranteed. No separate public SLA document was found in the pages checked.
- **Cloud gaps:** the checked public pages do not settle what happens after Free exhausts its allowance, exact region IDs, Cloud's maximum request payload, billing for replayed retries, exact Cloud data deletion timing, or Cloud egress rates. The relevant pages checked are named in each row.

## Field problems

- C10 is used for paid plan allowances as well as free quotas because the schema groups plan limits; each plan is named in its row.
- The phrase “open source” on the Pricing page conflicts with the current BSL license. The repository LICENSE says BSL 1.1 is not an open-source license.
- The pricing page has calculator sliders/fields and volume presets but no visible monthly/annual switch. It shows a dollar sign but does not identify an ISO currency code or tax treatment.
- Enterprise throughput is printed as “Up to 100,000s actions/sec”; it cannot safely be normalized to an exact number.
- BYOC has capacity-based examples but no self-service standard rate card, so those examples are not mixed into Cloud's per-action plans.
- The Trust Center page returned no readable lines when opened. Its official indexed text exposed a broad US/EU hosting statement, subprocessors, security controls, and dated updates. No private report was opened.
