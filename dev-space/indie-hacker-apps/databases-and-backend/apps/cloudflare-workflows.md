## Notices

Workflows is generally available. The product changelog, the Workflows blog tag, Cloudflare's acquisitions blog tag, and the press-release newsroom were checked; no Workflows shutdown, sunset, acquisition, or retirement notice was identified. Recent product changes include jurisdiction controls on October 27, 2026, a paid-plan instance-retention default change on September 10, and new step and stored-state billing from August 10. Cloudflare's newest Workflows-tagged blog items reviewed were published August 4, 2026 and describe its use in developer tooling. Python Workflows and dashboard workflow diagrams are still described as beta features; that does not make the Workflows service itself beta.

Workflows is available on both Workers Free and Workers Paid. It has its own step and persisted-state usage meters, while requests and CPU use Workers' shared pricing. Workers Paid has a separate $5 USD per-account monthly minimum. The Workflows charge is not a Queue charge.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The current Workflows Limits table and Cloudflare's April 2026 scale update set Paid concurrency to 50,000 running instances per account. A prose example later on the current limits page still mentions 10,000; the newer table and scaling update were used, and the stale prose is noted in J02.
- The current pricing and limits pages say new Workers Paid Workflows retain instance state for 7 days by default. Workflows created before the September 10, 2026 change keep their earlier 30-day default. The pricing page's linked retention note and current limits page clarify this plan/date difference.
- The Developer Platform service-specific terms list Workers and several related products but do not name Workflows separately. The file records the exact general Developer Platform clauses without claiming that the page answers every Workflows-specific legal question.
- Cloudflare does not state on the pages checked what happens when a Free account exhausts its daily billed-step allowance.
- The status page reviewed had no distinct Workflows component. No Workflow-specific uptime commitment was found in the reviewed support and product pages.

## Field problems

- The product is a workflow service built on Workers, so requests and CPU are shared Workers meters while steps and persisted state are additional Workflows meters. The file keeps those costs separate.
- The Workflows billing definition counts sleep and event-wait step operations, while the per-Workflow maximum-step limit excludes `step.sleep`. These are separate billing and execution-limit rules.
- Workflows state retention is not the same as metrics retention: state and logs have plan-specific instance retention, while analytics metrics are kept for 31 days.
