Sources: [2024 notice](https://redis.io/blog/redis-adopts-dual-source-available-licensing/), [2025 notice](https://redis.io/blog/agplv3/), [license overview](https://redis.io/legal/licenses/); checked **2026-11-05**.

## Cost examples — our calculations

Checked 2026-11-05. These are our calculations, not Redis quotes or measured performance. Cloud comparison location: **AWS us-east-1 (N. Virginia)**, USD before taxes, where the selected plan/features are offered. The public calculator did not yield a valid numeric quote in this read-only retrieval. The USD 5 Essentials headline has no verified region/size association; it is **not** substituted as the us-east-1 1 GB price. Redis Software licensing and external hosting are separate.

### Small database: one full month

Inputs: one production database, 1 GB retained data, 100,000 reads, 20,000 writes, 10 peak connections, 5 GB internet transfer and 730 provisioned hours. Requests/month do not determine peak ops/sec, CPU, object overhead or all network traffic.

- **Recurring Free path:** does not fit. Required 1 GB exceeds 30 MB. Ten connections fit the 30-connection ceiling, but the capacity mismatch already rules it out. A smaller learning prototype within every Free limit has USD 0 Redis plan charge; that is a different workload, and Free persistence/HA/TLS restrictions still matter. Sources: [Free creation](https://redis.io/docs/latest/operate/rc/databases/create-database/create-free-database/), [limits](https://redis.io/docs/latest/operate/rc/subscriptions/view-essentials-subscription/essentials-plan-details/), [TLS](https://redis.io/docs/latest/operate/rc/security/database-security/tls-ssl/).
- **Paid RAM Essentials candidate:** a 1 GB plan with replication leaves 512 MB for the dataset, so it cannot hold 1 GB of unique data. The next current nominal size is 2.5 GB. Our calculation: 2.5 GB ÷ 2 = **1.25 GB nominal dataset allocation with replication**, before usable-data overhead. A 2.5 GB RAM plan offers 2500 connections, 4000 ops/sec and 400 GB monthly total network allowance. This is a sizing candidate, not proof the workload fits after key/index/allocator overhead. No-HA 1 GB is also not guaranteed to fit exactly 1 GB of payload plus overhead. Sources: [HA sizing](https://redis.io/docs/latest/operate/rc/databases/configuration/high-availability/), [current plans](https://redis.io/docs/latest/operate/rc/subscriptions/view-essentials-subscription/essentials-plan-details/).
- **Essentials formula:** regional selected-plan charge + applicable backup-destination storage/requests + external client-network charges + any applicable extras/taxes. Using hourly billing would require the full-precision hourly rate and rounding; multiplying the displayed rounded USD 0.007 by 730 is not a reliable tariff. **Result: incomplete**, because the selected us-east-1 plan quote and complete extras are unknown. Sources: [calculator](https://redis.io/pricing/calculator/), [billing report](https://redis.io/docs/latest/operate/rc/billing-and-payments/cost-report/), [backup destinations](https://redis.io/docs/latest/operate/rc/databases/back-up-data/), [fees](https://redis.io/legal/cloud-tos/).

The report documents a minimum-price adjustment, but the exact application of a Transaction still controls. With 730 hours, the minimum component alone is **USD 200 before other charges**; an exact total is unavailable. The first-USD-200 promotion is excluded from the normal recurring comparison because its complete conditions were not established. Sources: [minimum/promotion](https://redis.io/pricing/), [RBU report](https://redis.io/docs/latest/operate/rc/billing-and-payments/cost-report/), [contract fees/network](https://redis.io/legal/cloud-tos/).

Include all relevant client ingress/egress and other billable routes without double-counting the specified 5 GB. Essentials capacity allowances are not Pro egress discounts. External worker/app providers can charge separately. Sources: [plan bandwidth](https://redis.io/docs/latest/operate/rc/subscriptions/view-essentials-subscription/essentials-plan-details/), [quota guide](https://support.redislabs.com/api/v2/help_center/en-us/articles/27315881501202.json), [Pro network reporting](https://redis.io/docs/latest/operate/rc/billing-and-payments/cost-report/).

### Small vector search application
