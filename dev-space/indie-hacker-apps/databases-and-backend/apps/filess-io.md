## Lifecycle, shutdown and acquisition check

The public service and documentation are operating. No whole-service shutdown, sunset, retirement or vendor acquisition notice was found in the official documentation, blog, terms or official-domain notice searches. The privacy policy's hypothetical merger clause and an article's example of a customer's acquisition are not acquisition announcements. Checked 2026-11-05. Sources: [documentation](https://docs.filess.io/), [blog](https://docs.filess.io/blog/), [Privacy](https://filess.io/privacy-policy/).

**Production-use conflict:** Dedicated is promoted for production, while Terms §2.6 describes the service as providing non-production databases. Architecture also mentions lightweight production on Shared. No explicit edition exception resolving this was found. Do not mark production eligibility confirmed. Sources, checked 2026-11-05: [architecture](https://docs.filess.io/docs/architecture/shared-vs-dedicated/), [Terms](https://filess.io/terms-of-service/).

Other material conflicts concern prices, Shared placement/IP filtering, monitoring inclusion and Shared SLA availability. These are retained below. The public pages do not justify treating a pricing card as a complete production contract.

Scope: managed databases first, split Shared/Dedicated. Related hosting volumes and one-shot containers are included only where the official hosting article establishes relevant file/job features. They are not automatically included in database plans. The apps.md “hosting and deploy” overlap is reasonable; no inventory row correction proposed. This scope/classification is our judgment based on [architecture](https://docs.filess.io/docs/architecture/shared-vs-dedicated/) and [Hosting](https://docs.filess.io/blog/docker-compose-on-kubernetes-filess-hosting/), checked 2026-11-05.
