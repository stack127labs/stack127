## Cost examples — our calculation

All rates below are the published Spaces rates checked 2026-11-06: https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/spaces/details/pricing/ . Amounts are USD. Examples exclude applicable tax, paid support plans, and other DigitalOcean products.

1. **Standard, below transfer allowance:** 300 GiB-month stored, 400 GiB outbound. Calculation: `$5 base + (300 − 250) GiB × $0.02/GiB + max(400 − 1,024, 0) GiB × $0.01/GiB = $6.00/month`. Assumes one subscription and no special transfer exemption.
2. **Standard with CDN traffic:** 300 GiB-month stored, 1,200 GiB total outbound including CDN/origin transfer. Calculation: `$5 + 50 GiB × $0.02/GiB + (1,200 − 1,024) GiB × $0.01/GiB = $7.76/month`. Assumes no free regional Droplet traffic.
3. **Cold known-meter subtotal:** 100 GiB-month stored and 250 GiB retrieved, assuming the average-daily-use waiver is 100 GiB. Calculation: `100 GiB × $0.007/GiB + (250 − 100) GiB × $0.01/GiB = $2.20`. This is not an exact full bill because the public docs do not clarify whether an account with only Cold buckets pays the USD $5 Standard subscription base; it also excludes outbound bandwidth and tax.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Bucket deletion interval:** The Spaces Limits page, last verified 2026-09-03, says a bucket name cannot be reused during a one-week pending-destruction period. The Destroy page, last verified 2026-07-13, says deletion takes at least two weeks and can take longer for a large bucket. I use the newer limits page for the name lock, but the actual deletion interval remains conflicting.
- **Cold-only base price:** The detailed pricing page says the USD $5 base is for a Standard Storage subscription, then lists Cold rates and shared bandwidth. The current product pricing page shows a USD $5 starting amount and Cold as an additional price, but does not plainly say whether an account with only Cold buckets also owes the base. The Cold-only example is therefore a known-meter subtotal, not an exact bill.
- **Pricing hierarchy:** The detailed Spaces pricing page (verified 2026-10-26) is used for USD/GiB rates and the average-daily retrieval waiver. The product marketing page uses rounded GB language and a shorter “once per month” Cold retrieval explanation; it is not used to replace the detailed pricing rule.
- **Account closure retention:** The Terms allow content destruction at termination, while the Privacy Policy's 90-day deletion statement concerns account data/personal information. Neither page states the exact object-data deletion time after account closure.
- **Company news check:** DigitalOcean Blog and newsroom were checked through their currently visible latest posts (November and September 2026, respectively), along with the Spaces release notes and product page. I found the Paperspace acquisition notice, but no Spaces shutdown, acquisition, or sunset announcement.
- **Data location wording:** The GDPR FAQ says customer data will remain in the selected region unless DigitalOcean informs the customer otherwise. The August 2026 Terms allow storage/transfer in other regions unless agreed otherwise in writing. The newer Terms are used; the difference is recorded as a conflict.
- **No region-specific price table:** The pricing page lists one rate per meter, while the availability page lists region support. A per-region price difference is not published in those pages.

## Field problems

- C12 does not state the minimum total invoice for Cold-only usage, because the published base price is named as a Standard subscription while Cold has separate storage and retrieval prices.
- C19 deletion guidance conflicts on whether the pending-destruction/name-lock interval is one week or at least two weeks. The newer limits page is used for the name-lock note; no exact object purge time is claimed.
- C11 has no inactivity expiry interval. Spaces has no compute that can pause, but billing suspension can still affect the account and stored objects.
- C05 is optional and was not useful for assessing Spaces; company home-market context is not substituted for bucket region or data-residency terms.
- F05's “S3-compatible” label needs a partial-compatibility note. Versioning and bucket policies work through the API, while several AWS S3 features and cross-region copy behavior differ.
- F06: a free built-in CDN is not a free-transfer promise. CDN origin/edge transfer counts against the Spaces allowance; Cold Storage cannot use CDN.
