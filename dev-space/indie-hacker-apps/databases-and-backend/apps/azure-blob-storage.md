## Main access tiers

| Tier | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| Hot | Online access for frequently read or modified data; higher capacity rate and lower access costs than cooler tiers. |
| Cool | Online access for infrequently used data; 30-day minimum in GPv2; lower capacity cost with higher access and retrieval charges. |
| Cold | Online access for rarely used data with fast access; 90-day minimum in GPv2; higher access and retrieval charges. |
| Archive | Offline storage; rehydrate before reading, potentially up to 15 hours; 180-day minimum; supported with LRS, GRS, and RA-GRS. |
| Smart | Automatically starts Hot, then moves eligible inactive blobs to Cool after 30 days and Cold after 90 days; no Archive tier; eligible blobs below 128 KiB remain Hot. |

Tier descriptions: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/storage/blobs/access-tiers-overview ; https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/storage/blobs/access-tiers-smart ; https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/storage/blobs/archive-rehydrate-overview (checked 2026-11-06). Smart tier is available for supported GPv2 standard accounts with ZRS/GZRS/RA-GZRS; it is GA in supported public regions and preview in Azure Government and China 21Vianet. It can charge a monitoring fee for eligible blobs over 128 KiB.
