## Conflicts and unknowns

- Egress: the pricing headline says downloads up to 3× “active storage volume” are free. The detailed pricing FAQ says 3× “total storage volume” and gives the 100 TB stored / 300 TB downloaded example. I use the FAQ's more specific wording and retain the wording difference here.
- Regions: IDrive's locations page and developer guide say 15 regions and name 15 places. The current endpoint URL table lists 20 region codes, including five “-2” or additional locations. The docs do not explain the mismatch; use the unique endpoint displayed for the bucket in the account.
- Free offer: pricing says “Try with 10 GB Free,” but e2 pricing FAQs and terms do not state a duration, card requirement, or whether it continues as a free tier. The separate startup offer is clearer: 100 TB for qualified applicants, with payment after exceeding the allowance, but no term is stated.
- S3 support: a third-party-app page says “100% bit-compatible,” while the general FAQ lists specific unsupported S3 operations. The operation list is the safer compatibility boundary.
- Public access: older object help describes public objects, while newer official integration guides say no new public buckets have been allowed since July 21, 2026. I treat the newer dated notice as current for new projects.
- Replication billing: the product documents same- and cross-region replication but do not say how replica storage or transfer is billed. The price impact is therefore not calculated.
- Status monitoring: no public e2 incident dashboard was linked from product, support, FAQ, legal, or company-news pages reviewed.

## Field problems

- C09/C10: the 10 GB offer is advertised as a trial but has no published duration or complete plan limits. The conditional 100 TB startup offer has an application gate and no published time limit.
- C17: the marketing region count (15) and endpoint list (20 codes) are not reconciled. Prices themselves are published as one USD rate, with no region-specific matrix.
- F04: API object-name maximum (1,024 characters) and web-console object-name maximum (254 characters) differ by interface; the FAQ does not explain the difference.
- F05/F06: compatibility and access docs include broad claims or older instructions that are narrower than later notices. The new-public-bucket date is included so old sharing steps are not presented as current.
- F07/C15: public pages do not explain replication's storage and egress billing. No exact replication total is claimed.
