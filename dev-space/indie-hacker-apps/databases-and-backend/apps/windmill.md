Windmill runs scripts, scheduled jobs, workflows, APIs, and internal apps. It is available as Windmill Cloud and as software you operate yourself. Community Edition is free to self-host, while Enterprise adds a commercial license and business features. Windmill Cloud has a free plan, a seat-and-execution Team plan, and a dedicated Enterprise Cloud service billed by seats and worker compute units.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Windmill pricing was checked with its interactive **Cloud / Self-hosted / White Label** mode switch, **Monthly / Annually** period switch, plan variant buttons, and worker/seat range sliders. Monthly Cloud Team showed $10 per builder seat and $5 per half-seat operator; annual showed $120 and $60. Cloud Enterprise showed $40/$20 per month per full/half seat, $100/month per CU, and a $600/month core package. Self-host Enterprise showed $20/$10 per month per full/half seat and $50/month per CU. The visible 16% annual discount applies to Enterprise, not Team seat pricing.
- Cloud Team Terms confirm that extra executions are billed but do not publish the rate. Pricing, Terms, and the upgrade guide were checked; the overage unit price remains unknown.
- Cloud Enterprise Cloud audit history conflicts: pricing says unlimited retention, while the audit guide says 60 days in-product and up to one year in cloud storage, extendable on request. This file keeps the more specific audit guide detail visible and leaves the discrepancy explicit.
- The official Windmill status page could not return service data during the check. No uptime condition is inferred.
- A shared Cloud job timeout maximum and Cloud Free behavior after the 1,000 execution allowance were not stated in the pages checked.
- The `apps.md` category emphasizes jobs/events and understates Windmill's internal-app and developer-platform roles; the official URL and active status are correct.

## Field problems

- Cloud Enterprise and self-host Enterprise both use compute units, but the prices differ and the worker pricing has a separate Cloud core-package charge. The pricing page selector defaults to self-hosted; copying its first visible rates would misstate Cloud.
- The pricing page's Cloud Enterprise audit row and the dedicated audit-log guide use different retention descriptions.
- “Execution” is a time-based billing unit, not simply one API request or one workflow run. A longer job can consume multiple execution units; flow wait time is excluded.
- The self-hosted Community Edition page calls Windmill open source, while the root license explains that published CE binaries bundle AGPL/Apache code with proprietary code covered by restricted CE terms. These scopes must stay separate.
