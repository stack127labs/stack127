## Conflicts and unknowns

- Price tables are shown by storefront and currency, while bucket placement is selected from physical regions. The pages do not state that a storefront's displayed currency maps to one physical region. I label each rate by its price-page market and do not convert currencies.
- The US price page displays USD rates for 1-AZ classes but does not expose the full multi-zone archive table. The worldwide USD table is used for 3-AZ and archive prices; both pages were checked.
- Singapore's price page states a dated free-egress exception through 2026-12-31. A post-2026 rate is not published on the page checked.
- Current product docs describe Object Storage 2.0 as a private-beta backend project. The product page and setup docs continue to present the current service as available; no retirement date was found.
- Separate cross-region replication transfer charges, a lasting free storage quota, a complete country eligibility list, and a universal first-response support time were not published on the specific pricing, docs, legal, and support pages cited above.

## Field problems

- C06: Public pages do not give one complete country eligibility matrix. The US trial is specifically limited to eligible US Public Cloud customers.
- C15: APAC egress is free only through a stated end date; the later tariff was not published. A separate cross-region replication transfer price was not located.
- C17/F01: Price-page storefront and currency are not the same thing as physical storage location. Rates are labeled by storefront, while region choice and class availability are taken from the region and class docs.
- C20: SLA values are stated with contract exclusions; support response time is not universal across support levels.
- F05: “S3-compatible” does not mean complete AWS S3 parity; use the official compliance matrix for a specific operation.
