## Cost examples

Both totals below are **our calculations**, not Tigris quotes. They use the current main pricing page's Global/default placement and its stated USD rates. They assume a full 30-day month, average Standard usage, an active 5 GB monthly Standard allowance, no taxes, and no object-notification events.

- **Small active bucket:** 10 GB average Standard storage; 20,000 Class A requests; 100,000 Class B requests; 100 GB egress. Formula: (10−5) GB × $0.02/GB-month + (20,000−10,000) ÷ 1,000 × $0.005 + (100,000−100,000) ÷ 1,000 × $0.0005 + 100 GB × $0 = **$0.15/month**.
- **Idle storage:** 10 GB average Standard storage with no requests or events. Formula: (10−5) GB × $0.02/GB-month = **$0.10/month**.

These estimates exclude taxes and any separate compute or CDN charges. The older alternate pricing page's explicit multi-region replication rate is not included; confirm that mode with Tigris before estimating non-default replica placement.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Acquisition:** Dropbox signed a definitive agreement to acquire Tigris Data Inc. on 2026-11-03 and announced it on 2026-11-05. The announced close is expected in Q4 2026 subject to conditions. The product and status pages remained live, and the status page reported full operation on 2026-11-06. No shutdown date or completed ownership transfer was announced in the official material checked.
- **Multi-region prices:** The current main pricing page describes Global/default placement and says the price is the same across geographies with no per-region charge. The alternate `site.tigrisdata.com/pricing` page says additional explicitly selected regions add $0.02/GB-month each. I use the current main pricing page for Global/default pricing; the cost of an explicitly selected multi-region configuration should be confirmed.
- **Terms versus current prices:** The official service agreement is dated 2023-12-13 and says the service is currently free, while the current pricing page publishes paid rates. The current pricing page is used for rates; the terms page should be reviewed for an update.
- **Direct SaaS use:** The standard service terms say the service is for internal use and restrict providing a product or service to third parties. Tigris separately advertises its Partner Integrations API for platform storage. A solo builder storing end-user files should confirm which agreement applies.
- **Blog and legal-page access:** The official Tigris blog index returned an unsupported Markdown content type, and the current About-page links to legal pages returned 404. The official legacy terms and privacy URLs were found through Tigris's own indexed legal results. Several new documentation pages also returned unsupported Markdown, so the accessible feature and pricing pages were used where they independently support the fact.
