## Cost examples: our calculations

Use the approved small-file-store scenario: 100 GB average stored for a full 30-day month, 100,000 writes, 1 million reads, 100 GB ordinary internet egress, files kept for the month. One classic consumption account in US West, one private bucket, single-part PutObject/GetObject requests within limits, no old versions, extra replicas, unfinished parts, Class D notifications, migration appliance, image processing, premium support or CDN. Assume billing GB/TB are decimal, explicitly 1 TB = 1,000 GB; that conversion was not independently verified in a current B2 billing specification, so outputs stay conditional. GB is not silently converted to GiB.

Inputs: [current main pricing](https://www.backblaze.com/cloud-storage/pricing), Billing FAQ/fundamentals/egress, checked 2026-11-04; [transaction pricing](https://www.backblaze.com/cloud-storage/transaction-pricing), Classes A/B/C/D, checked 2026-11-04. The latter has a conflicting old storage rate. These are provisional calculations using the main page, not vendor quotes or verified invoices. Taxes, invoice minimum and rounding unresolved.

| Scenario | Inputs and formula | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small file store, recurring free allowance | Storage: max(100 − 10, 0) GB / 1,000 GB per TB × $6.95/TB/30 days. Writes/reads: 100,000 Class A + 1,000,000 Class B, free. Egress: max(100 − 3 × 100, 0) GB × $0.01/GB. | Conditional $0.6255, unrounded arithmetic before tax/invoice minimum. Complete baseline meter subtotal under assumptions, but not final publishable quote because F01 price conflict remains. |
| Small file store, allowance unavailable | Normal rate subtotal without applying 10 GB: 100/1,000 × $6.95 + $0 API calls + $0 egress. | Conditional $0.695 per 30 days, unrounded. This is a comparison variant, not a claim B2 normally removes its recurring allowance. |
| Idle project, same retained data | Same 100 GB for 30 days; zero calls/egress. max(100 − 10, 0)/1,000 × $6.95. | Conditional $0.6255. Idle does not remove storage charge. No compute charge invented. |
| Fully free storage-size variant | 10 GB retained for 30 days, no notifications; egress at most 3 × 10 = 30 GB; Class A/B/C calls free. | Conditional $0 baseline. This changes the workload and is not the 100 GB scenario. Account/public-bucket eligibility still applies. |
| Download-heavy variant | Same 100 GB storage, 1,000 GB direct internet egress: max(1,000 − 300, 0) × $0.01 = $7 transfer; add $0.6255 storage. | Conditional $7.6255 before tax/rounding, showing why route matters. Through eligible CDN partners B2 egress may be free; partner charges excluded. |

If Class D event notifications are enabled, daily event counts are needed. Formula before unresolved block-rounding: sum over days of max(calls_day − 2,500, 0) / 10,000 × $0.004. Monthly request totals cannot apply a daily free allowance correctly without that distribution. Class D is excluded from the baseline and cannot be capped. Replication adds destination storage; old versions/unfinished parts increase average byte-hours. No total compares B2 alone with a full database/auth/functions backend.
