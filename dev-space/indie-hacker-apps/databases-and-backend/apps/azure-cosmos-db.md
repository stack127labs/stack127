# Azure Cosmos DB

**Lifecycle notices checked first:** MongoDB vCore was renamed **Azure DocumentDB on 2025-11-18**; see [official release notes](<https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/documentdb/release-notes>). **Cosmos DB for PostgreSQL is on a retirement path and is not recommended for new projects**; see [official product updates](<https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cosmos-db/postgresql/product-updates>). These notices do not announce retirement of the RU-based Cosmos DB service researched below. The [limits page](<https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cosmos-db/concepts-limits>) also marks legacy attachments as being deprecated; the [backup guide](<https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cosmos-db/continuous-backup-restore-introduction>) identifies Synapse Link as deprecated. Their historical pricing labels are not new general file-storage or analytics recommendations.

Inputs: one NoSQL production database/container, 1 GB of **billable data plus indexes** retained for the full month, 100,000 reads, 20,000 writes, 10 peak connections, 5 GB internet outbound, one East US region, no availability-zone premium, default periodic backup or free-storage 7-day continuous backup. The storage assumption is stronger than “1 GB raw documents”: if indexes add storage beyond 1 GB, add USD 0.25 per extra GB/month.

- **Manual, no free-tier discount:** assume the entire request pattern fits a new 400-RU/s resource without exceeding a hot-partition limit. Throughput = `400 / 100 × USD 0.008 × 730 = USD 23.36`. Storage = `1 × USD 0.25 = USD 0.25`. Subtotal **USD 23.61/month**, also **USD 23.61 when idle** while the resource/data remain. The query counts/connections do not prove the 400-RU/s peak assumption; if it fails, this is not a supported total.
- **Eligible Cosmos free tier, provisioned:** `max(400 − 1,000, 0) / 100 × 0.008 × 730 + max(1 − 25, 0) × 0.25 = USD 0` database throughput/storage subtotal. This requires creation-time opt-in, an unused free-tier entitlement and the resource fitting available throughput. Other containers/regions share the account discount. Paid backup/restore or other meters are not automatically free.
- **Autoscale, no free discount, Tmax 1,000:** minimum scaled capacity 100 RU/s. If every region/partition stays at that floor throughout every hour: `100 / 100 × 0.012 × 730 + 1 × 0.25 = USD 9.01/month`. If the resource scales to 1,000 RU/s in every hourly billing period: `1,000 / 100 × 0.012 × 730 + 0.25 = USD 87.85/month`. These are conditional examples of the hourly scaling rule, not a prediction from monthly operation counts.
- **Serverless, no Cosmos free tier:** let `R` be actual read RUs and `W` actual write RUs. `USD 0.25 × (R + W) / 1,000,000 + USD 0.25 × 1 GB`. The exact active subtotal is **incomplete** until item sizes, indexes, query shape and consistency are known. Illustrative assumptions only: 100,000 approximately 1-KB point reads at 1 RU each, plus 20,000 writes measured/assumed at 5 RUs each, give `(100,000 + 100,000) / 1,000,000 × 0.25 + 0.25 = USD 0.30/month`. **5 RUs per write is our example input, not a universal vendor rate.** Idle with zero requests is `0 + 1 × 0.25 = USD 0.25/month` before paid backup extras.
- **Internet transfer for this 5-GB scenario:** USD 0 under the Cosmos-specific first-5-GB table, assuming the allowance remains available. It also fits the general Bandwidth page's 100-GB allowance. This small example therefore does not need to resolve the conflicting larger-egress schedules. Inter-region traffic is not included.

The 400-RU/s resource bills for reserved capacity rather than multiplying monthly RUs by the serverless rate. Strong/bounded-staleness reads can double read RUs; query reads are not necessarily point reads. Measure the application's request charge before choosing a capacity plan.

Same **East US, USD** rates, same 1-GB billable account data assumption:

- Default periodic two-copy storage: **USD 0 extra backup storage**, with snapshot recovery rather than arbitrary-point recovery. If a configuration keeps `B` GB/month of **additional** periodic backup data, add `B × 0.10` for LRS, `B × 0.125` for ZRS or `B × 0.20` for RA-GRS. **Vector workload:** 100,000 vectors, 1,536 float32 components, 1 KiB metadata each, 100,000 searches, 10,000 updates, 5 GB outbound. Raw arithmetic is `100,000 × (1,536 × 4 + 1,024) = 716,800,000 bytes`. This is **raw payload**, not measured billed JSON/index storage.
