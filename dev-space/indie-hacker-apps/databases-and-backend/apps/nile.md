## Cost examples

**Our calculations**, checked 2026-11-05, using Nile's hosted serverless workspace. Select the documented region **AWS_US_WEST_2** for comparison, subject to the [workspace's actual available-region list](https://www.thenile.dev/docs/api-reference/databases/list-available-regions). The pricing page gives no regional rate differences. No account was created to verify a region or invoice.

**Currency:** the official page prints **$** without explicitly identifying its ISO currency code. That original notation is preserved. No currency conversion or tax assumption is added.

Let **T** be monthly *billable query tokens across the workspace* and **S** be its *billable storage GB*. The [pricing page](https://www.thenile.dev/pricing) defines query tokens as abstract CPU/memory consumed by queries. Its operation-count calculator is an estimate, not a published invariant that every read/write has the same resource cost. No workload was run to measure T; storage averaging/rounding, tax and invoice-minimum treatment were not established.

Published base/compute/storage formulas:

- **Free:** $0 + $0.06 × max(0, T − 50,000,000)/1,000,000 + $1.50 × max(0, S − 1).
- **Pro:** $15 + $0.05 × max(0, T − 150,000,000)/1,000,000 + $1.00 × max(0, S − 5).
- **Scale:** $350 + $0.04 × max(0, T − 500,000,000)/1,000,000 + $0.75 × max(0, S − 50).

These formulas show the published components before any unresolved billing increments, tax, transfer or optional-service costs. An overage on Free does not automatically require a $15 subscription upgrade.

### 1. Small SQL app

Inputs: one production database; **1 GB stored** for a full month; **100,000 reads**; **20,000 writes**; **10 peak connections**; **5 GB internet transfer**; **730 hours** if always-on compute were required.

Ten connections are below the published Free/Pro connection ceilings. Nile's shared serverless compute has no public 730-hour allocated-instance rate to multiply. The read/write counts do not establish T.

Assuming **S = 1 billable GB**, our calculation is:

- Free base/compute/storage = **$0.06 × max(0, T − 50,000,000)/1,000,000**. It equals **$0** if T stays within 50 million.
- Pro base/compute/storage = **$15 + $0.05 × max(0, T − 150,000,000)/1,000,000**. It equals **$15** if T stays within 150 million.

**Result: conditional $0 Free or $15 Pro for the published base/compute/storage components; exact total incomplete.** T, 5 GB egress pricing and any required protections are not known. No fixed query-token conversion or universal permission to run production on Free is asserted. The production-oriented paid path has the plan/SLA qualifications in C06/C20.

Backup variant: [paid support-assisted recovery](https://www.thenile.dev/docs/support/backup_restore) exists, but current backup retention, download, restore completion time and extra fees are not established. Do not add pricing's “coming soon, one week” as a live entitlement or price Free backups at zero. A protected-production total is **incomplete**.

High-availability/read-replica variant: **incomplete**. The public material does not define a current orderable replica topology, node count or corresponding price. A logical second database is not a separately proven standby/failover system, and the [dedicated API](https://www.thenile.dev/docs/api-reference/databases/create-a-dedicated-compute-instance) supplies no verified public machine rate.

### 6. Idle project

Inputs: retain **1 billable GB**, zero requests and zero active job execution for a month; assume **T = 0** for the listed query meter.
