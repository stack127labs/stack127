## Notices

- Pub/Sub is an active, fully managed Google Cloud service. Its release notes list updates through October 1, 2026, and the Google Cloud blog lists current Pub/Sub features. I found no Pub/Sub acquisition or service shutdown notice in the release notes, product page, or company blog pages checked.
- Pub/Sub Lite is deprecated, closed to new customers since September 24, 2024, and is scheduled to turn down on January 31, 2027. The newer “Choose Pub/Sub or Pub/Sub Lite” guide gives that date. The pricing page still says March 18, 2026, a past date; I use the newer guide and record the conflict below.
- Recent changes include AI Inference single-message transforms (generally available), a remote MCP server (generally available), and direct Bigtable subscriptions (Preview). These are Pub/Sub updates, not Pub/Sub Lite features.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Pub/Sub Lite shutdown date:** The current migration guide says January 31, 2027. The Pub/Sub pricing page still says March 18, 2026, and that date has passed. I use the newer migration guide.
- **Free-tier units:** The product and Free Tier pages show “10 GB”; the detailed Pub/Sub pricing page says exactly 10 GiB of Message Delivery Basic SKU throughput per billing account per calendar month. I use the detailed billing unit for calculations.
- **Bigtable export price:** Bigtable subscriptions are Preview, but no Pub/Sub-specific price was published on the pricing, Bigtable subscription, or release-note pages checked.
- **Data transfer:** Pub/Sub says region-crossing traffic follows VPC rates. Exact cost depends on source and destination region; the table gives representative rates, not an exhaustive matrix.
- **Signup payment method and eligibility:** The Free Tier, product pricing, and Google Cloud terms pages do not specify a universal card requirement or complete country eligibility list. No signup form was submitted.
- **Pub/Sub-specific spend cap:** Pricing, quotas, and general billing-budget pages do not say whether a hard spend cap is available specifically for Pub/Sub.
- **DPA retrieval:** The direct Cloud Data Processing Addendum URL timed out in one open attempt. I used Google's indexed official copy with `?authuser=0`, which exposed sections 6.1–6.2 on deletion.

## Field problems

- C05 is provider-wide rather than Pub/Sub-specific. The file records Google's contracting entity and global region footprint without treating service regions as a company headquarters.
- C16 has quota controls and general billing budgets, but no public Pub/Sub-specific hard spend-cap statement.
- C21's throughput calculation assumes the stated 1 TiB is eligible Basic SKU volume in one billing account and that the full 10 GiB free amount is unused that month.
