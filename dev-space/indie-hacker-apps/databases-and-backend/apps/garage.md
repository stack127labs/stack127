## Cost examples — our calculations

Garage is self-hosted software, so these are not vendor quotes. The product has no published managed storage, request, egress, or backup tariff in the pages checked.

**Small file store.** Inputs from fields.md: 100 GB average hot storage for a 30-day month, 100,000 writes, 1 million reads, and 100 GB Internet transfer. For a three-copy layout, our raw replica-payload calculation is **100 GB × 3 = 300 GB**, before metadata, block overhead, compression, or deduplication. The operator also needs compute for the chosen node count, storage beyond this payload, Internet transfer, monitoring, and a separate backup destination. Garage adds no published per-request bill, but no host, region, or network price was selected, so the total monthly amount is **incomplete and unknown**.

**Idle project.** Keep the same data and send no requests for a month. The stored replica payload remains approximately **300 GB before overhead** in the three-copy example. Disk, backup retention, and any host charges continue under the operator’s infrastructure provider. If all nodes are stopped, the service is unavailable. The monthly total is **incomplete and unknown**, not zero.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The official Garage S3 compatibility page lists supported endpoints, but its update history stops at 2022-05-25 and it warns that entries may be outdated. I report what the official matrix states and do not treat it as a conformance test of v2.5.0.
- Garage's general S3/configuration docs list path-style and virtual-host access, but its Nextcloud integration guide says it supports only path-style access. I kept this as a client-setup conflict because the docs do not show dates that settle it.
- No universal object-size maximum was published in the Garage docs. I checked Quick Start, configuration, S3 compatibility, known issues, official downloads/releases, and Deuxfleurs’ available terms page. The terms page applies to Deuxfleurs-hosted services and does not answer a Garage object limit.
- The official product download page and docs have no Garage pricing table. The Deuxfleurs homepage’s voluntary contribution guidance is for the association’s separately hosted services, so I did not use it as Garage software pricing.
- The upstream Forgejo repository and release-note links were blocked by robots.txt in the research browser. I used the official Garage release list for the release date and the Deuxfleurs organization’s GitHub mirror for LICENSE, README, GOVERNANCE, and SECURITY. The mirror labels itself as a mirror and identifies Forgejo as the upstream source.
- The Deuxfleurs blog index’s September 2026 item reports organizational reshaping intended to continue the association’s mission. The direct article fetch returned a cache miss. The blog index, release list, and Garage homepage showed no Garage shutdown or acquisition notice.
- No standalone Garage privacy policy was found. Garage is software that the operator runs and controls; the Deuxfleurs legal notice and terms describe the association’s sites and hosted services, not the user’s independent Garage data.
