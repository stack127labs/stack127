**Shutdown, sunset and ownership notices:** UnivaPay's current service is active. Bank Pay-family wallets closed new applications on 15 December 2025 and are scheduled to stop on **20 December 2026 at 23:59**; this does not shut down ordinary bank transfers or the whole provider. The old cp.ccps.jp online system had a published complete-migration deadline of **31 March 2025**, including wrapper integrations; the new system does not carry every old method forward. These are product/system-specific sunsets. Sources: https://univapay.com/notice/20251216_ginkopay/; https://docs.univapay.com/docs/migration/overview/; https://univapay.com/ (checked 2026-11-05).

## Cost examples — our calculations

Checked 2026-11-05. Amounts remain in JPY. These are one-time online payments, not installments or MCP. No conversion to another currency is made.

**Ordinary online cards cannot be given a numeric all-in example from public rates.** For one ¥1,000 sale, the sale percentage component is `1,000 × quoted percentage`. Add the contracted combined processing fee, or separately charged authorization/capture operations, then applicable fixed/monthly-minimum fees, options, taxes and bank payouts. Failed checks/attempts can add fees. The public page does not publish the necessary numeric inputs or rounding rule. Sources: https://univapay.com/price/; https://univapay.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/upc-kameiten-kiyaku_20250701.pdf (checked 2026-11-05).

The separate China online-link campaign publishes setup ¥20,000 excluding tax, monthly ¥0 for three months then ¥3,000 excluding tax, and processing at 2.5%. The campaign is limited to its own inquiry/application route. The standard transfer charge is ¥400 excluding tax. The examples below calculate **only those published components**. They are not complete bank-proceeds quotes: the page does not explicitly label tax treatment of the 2.5% component, and additional contracted fees, if any, remain unconfirmed.

| Scenario and inputs | Formula — our calculation | Published-component result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| China-link campaign, first month: ¥100,000 gross wallet sales; one transfer; setup included | percentage = 100,000 × 0.025 = 2,500; subtotal = 20,000 setup + 0 monthly + 2,500 + 400 transfer | ¥22,900, plus applicable taxes and any additional agreed charges. |
| Same campaign, month four: ¥100,000 gross; one transfer; setup already paid | subtotal = 3,000 monthly + (100,000 × 0.025) + 400 | ¥5,900, plus applicable taxes and any additional agreed charges. |
| Same campaign, first three months: ¥100,000 gross each month and one transfer each month | subtotal = 20,000 + (3 × 0) + (300,000 × 0.025) + (3 × 400) | ¥28,700 across three months, plus applicable taxes and any additional agreed charges. |
| Same campaign, first year fixed setup/monthly components only | 20,000 + (3 × 0) + (9 × 3,000) | ¥47,000 excluding tax, processing, transfers and any additional charges. |

Input sources: https://univapay.com/easy-link-payment/; https://docs.univapay.com/docs/faq/individual/settlement/ (checked 2026-11-05). No new campaign application was submitted, so public pricing is not treated as a binding merchant quote. No per-payment rounding is needed for these example amounts; the public general rounding rule remains unverified.

The monthly-minimum article contains an **illustrative contract**, not a standard offer: ¥3,000 minimum, 3% sale fee and a ¥10 processing line (its examples use ¥11). Its fee-floor mechanism is recorded, but those sample amounts are not substituted into the general-card calculation. Source: https://blog.univapay.com/getsugakusaitei-fee/ (checked 2026-11-05).

They cannot be merged into one global direct-charge list. Reviewed all three product/API explanations and the MCP supplement.
