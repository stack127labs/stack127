## Cost examples

These are our calculations from Culqi's currently published Online and Link tariff, checked 2026-11-06. They do not include currency conversion or a negotiated lower rate. Do not add PEN and USD amounts into one total.

- Domestic card, PEN 100 sale: PEN 100 × 3.44% = PEN 3.44 variable commission, plus USD 0.20 fixed per online sale.
- Domestic card, PEN 50 sale: PEN 50 × 3.44% = PEN 1.72; because it is below PEN 87.72, the published minimum commission is PEN 3.50, plus the separately listed USD 0.20 fixed fee.
- International card, USD 100 sale: USD 100 × 3.99% = USD 3.99 variable commission, plus USD 0.20 fixed fee, or USD 4.19 before any unresolved tax treatment.

The current Culqi tariff says commissions are inafectas to IGV. BCP's Culqi product page and Culqi's published contract annex say + IGV. A merchant should confirm the fee treatment in the current CulqiPanel quote or contract before relying on a net amount.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Fee tax treatment: Culqi's current pricing page labels the commissions inafectas to IGV; its help article says issuer commission tax applies only for some nonfinancial cards; BCP's product page and the published contract annex show + IGV. Kept the current Culqi tariff's rate, but marked the tax result conflicting.
- Fixed USD 0.20 on local methods: the tariff says it applies to all online sales, while the fee help page lists it for cards and Yape only. Marked exact stacking for PagoEfectivo, wallets, bank methods, agents and Cuotéalo unclear.
- Buyer-country coverage: international card pricing is published, but there is no complete buyer-country list in the pricing, methods, docs or agreement searched.
- Seller-country coverage: Peru is documented. The reviewed sources do not list other signup countries.
- Card and PagoEfectivo minimum/maximum transaction values: the official transaction-limits page links a PNG table, but text extraction did not read it. Direct image retrieval failed due to the environment's DNS/network restriction, so the detailed card limits are check_failed. Wallet and Cuotéalo limits were available in their text docs.
- Business allowlist: the contract requires a declared business line and lawful activity, but the checked agreement, developer docs and help pages do not publish a full allowed/prohibited-category table.
- Sources searched for shutdown or sunset information: Culqi's company timeline, the official blog updates page, current pricing and developer docs. The timeline documents ownership changes and current BCP affiliation; no closure notice appeared in these pages.

## Field problems

- F35 transaction limits are partly presented as an image rather than readable text; the image retrieval failed, so do not treat the card limit as zero or absent.
- Official online fee pages use different IGV wording. The current Culqi tariff is the source for the headline rate, but the applicable IGV on every fee component is not settled by public pages.
- Culqi publishes no full seller-country list, buyer-country matrix, category allowlist, universal account cap, payout minimum, standard reserve rate, or standard closure release time. These are marked not_published where relevant, with the pages searched attached to those rows.
