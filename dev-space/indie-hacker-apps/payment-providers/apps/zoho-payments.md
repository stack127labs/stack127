## Field problems

- F03/F04: country-level “registered business” language coexists with Individual onboarding. Legal incorporation wording in India's current terms is materially narrower; it cannot be normalized away.
- F08/F09: software downloads and intangible services need a clear country-specific policy decision, not a generic claim that a gateway supports SaaS. US subscriptions over one year are explicitly prohibited by its policy; automatic renewal alone does not clarify how that restriction applies to a particular agreement.
- F11: the US currency FAQ preserves obsolete currency codes. They are recorded as published, not independently endorsed as operational. Zoho Checkout is narrower than standalone Payments.
- F19 to F28: MDR, platform fees, convenience fees, sales tax, tax on fees, mandate events, merchant settlement and salary transfers are different costs. Combining them gives a false headline fee.
- F33/F35: customer transaction time, escrow credit, bank settlement and activation are different clocks. India payout overview has imprecise “two business days, i.e. T+1 or T+2” wording; the method breakdown is retained.
- F37/F40: inbound card migration is documented; it does not prove an outbound export right. Privacy retention is purpose/legal-duty based, so a fixed deletion date is not supplied.
- No proven apps.md row error found. The “India, also US offer” label is broadly correct, but it should not imply matching product eligibility or fees.
