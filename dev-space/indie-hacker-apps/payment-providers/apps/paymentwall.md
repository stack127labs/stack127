## Cost examples — our calculations

Checked/calculated on 2026-11-05. No public card or local-method percentage/fixed fee was verified for a defined merchant country. The linked Fee Schedule returns 404. Therefore these examples do not invent a complete payment cost.

### Processing scenarios with missing prices

Let `p` be the quoted processing percentage, `f` the fixed per-payment fee, `x` an applicable international/FX charge, `b` a Billing surcharge and `w` an allocated payout cost. Each variable needs an account- and country-specific quote. Source for the existence of separate components: [Terms of Service](https://www.paymentwall.com/en/terms); checked 2026-11-05.

| Scenario | Inputs and formula | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic sale of 10 local currency units | `10 × p / 100 + f`; p/f not published for a defined local seller/card | Unknown full processing cost |
| Domestic sale of 100 local currency units | `100 × p / 100 + f`; p/f not published | Unknown full processing cost |
| International 100-unit sale | Domestic-versus-replacement rate rule and cross-border charge are unknown; do not automatically add two rates | Unknown full processing cost |
| Converted 100-unit sale | Method charge plus the applicable conversion stage/spread; exact rate, stage and fee-currency conversion are unknown | Unknown total and net bank receipt |
| 100 subscription sales of 10 units | `100 × (10 × p / 100 + f) + b + w`, only once the quote defines what b/w include | Unknown total; gross sales = 1,000 currency units |
| Refund of a 100-unit sale | Original processing fee remains charged; additional refund/network/FX costs follow the agreement/fee schedule | Unknown numeric total; not free merely because the sale is reversed |

No tax on provider fees, required method component or external merchant-account charge is silently set to zero. SpiderPipe underlying-provider fees can remain separate from Paymentwall's own charges. Inputs searched: [Brick](https://www.paymentwall.com/products/brick), [PayAlto](https://www.paymentwall.com/products/payalto), [Subscriptions](https://www.paymentwall.com/products/subscriptions), [original-fee retention](https://www.paymentwall.com/support/refunds-and-reversals/are-there-any-fees-for-refunding-a-transaction), [agreement](https://www.paymentwall.com/en/terms); checked 2026-11-05.

### Known VAT-report component

The contract states EUR 0.05 per relevant end-user transaction for EU-incorporated sellers, or USD 0.05 for sellers incorporated elsewhere. This is the calculation/report tool's charge in covered territories, not the sales tax itself, not a processing rate and not proof of country eligibility. The statement does not establish how an unincorporated individual is classified. Source: [Terms of Payment, tax-calculation clause](https://www.paymentwall.com/en/terms); checked 2026-11-05.

| Seller scope | Sale amount | Our calculation for this component only | Component as share of sale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EU-incorporated; relevant covered transaction | EUR 10 | EUR 0.05 | `0.05 / 10 × 100 = 0.50%` |
| EU-incorporated; relevant covered transaction | EUR 100 | EUR 0.05 | `0.05 / 100 × 100 = 0.05%` |
| Non-EU-incorporated; relevant covered transaction | USD 10 | USD 0.05 | `0.05 / 10 × 100 = 0.50%` |
| Non-EU-incorporated; relevant covered transaction | USD 100 | USD 0.05 | `0.05 / 100 × 100 = 0.05%` |

For 100 relevant transactions, `100 × 0.05 = EUR 5.00` or `USD 5.00` in the applicable contract currency. These currencies were not converted or combined. Complete processing/Billing/payout totals remain unknown.

### Reserve and minimum-payout effects

Assume the public initial 5% rolling reserve applies to an approved account and there are no previous reserve credits. This is a cash hold, not a non-refundable fee. Source: [rolling reserve and payout clauses](https://www.paymentwall.com/en/terms); checked 2026-11-05.

- **USD 10 gross:** `10 × 5% = USD 0.50` withheld; `10 − 0.50 = USD 9.50` remains before unknown processing, tax-report and other deductions.
- **USD 100 gross:** `100 × 5% = USD 5.00` withheld; at most USD 95.00 remains before the other deductions. This is not a promised bank payout.
- **USD 1,000 gross:** `1,000 × 5% = USD 50.00` withheld; at most USD 950.00 remains before other deductions. The reserve's remaining balance starts monthly release six months after the processing month, subject to changed risk terms and actual losses.
- **Threshold illustration:** if the account's '$100' threshold and a $100 gross sale use the same dollar currency, a 5% reserve leaves at most `$100 − $5 = $95` before fees. That single sale cannot reach the normal $100 net threshold without other amounts or an approved exception. The public threshold does not explicitly code the dollar currency, so no universal USD/EUR account interpretation is assumed.

The bank-arrival estimate of three to seven business days begins after payout is sent. It must not replace month-end Net 30/45/60/90 collection delays, the payout batch, threshold wait or any reserve. Sources: [collection/payout terms](https://www.paymentwall.com/en/terms), [minimum and exceptions](https://www.paymentwall.com/support/payouts/how-can-I-receive-my-payout), [bank arrival help](https://www.paymentwall.com/support/payouts/how-much-time-does-it-take-to-receive-a-payout); checked 2026-11-05.

## Field problems

- F01/F39 need a whole-company versus product/dependency distinction. Terminal3/FasterPay, the regional platforms and Coinbase's transition must not be mistaken for Paymentwall-wide acquisitions or shutdowns.
- F03 is hard to apply: a US-labelled public URL still uses EU-only eligibility. The listing's 'Global' discovery label is too broad to answer whether a particular seller can join.

- F14/F15 need API/edition scope: Checkout, Brick and TokiDoki have different methods, periods and recovery controls. Native proration/metering cannot be inferred from subscription editing.
- F19–F28 cannot yield a reliable total without an account quote. The Fee Schedule's broken public link is a real evidence gap. Historical or third-party rate cards were not used as proof.
- F25 uses incorporation scope for a EUR/USD fixed tax-report fee. It does not clearly define the fee treatment of every individual/sole proprietor.
- F34 uses a dollar sign without an ISO code, while method limits elsewhere use explicit EUR/BRL/SGD. That ambiguity is retained rather than silently normalised.
- F36/F44 have a real public initial reserve. It is conditional/changeable and affects cash availability; it must not be counted as a permanent processing charge.
- C01–C05 apply only to a separately connected, currently unverified crypto route. Treating them all as not_applicable would hide an actual published connector; treating its old instructions as current would overstate evidence.
- No definite app-name, official-URL or active-status error was found in apps.md. Its gateway category fits, but current software route and non-EU signup still need offer confirmation.
