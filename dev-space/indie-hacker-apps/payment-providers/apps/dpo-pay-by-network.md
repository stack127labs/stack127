## Owner, rebrand, and sunset checks

**Current owner:** Network International LLC owns DPO Group / DPO Pay. Network International completed its acquisition of 100% of DPO Group on 29 September 2021 for USD 291.3 million. Network International and Magnati merged in October 2025 under a Brookfield-led consortium; the merged company operates as Network International LLC. The DPO Pay service continues within that group.

**Name history:** the product began as 3G Direct Pay, became Direct Pay Online, and consolidated as DPO Group in 2018. DPO Pay later became the merchant-facing product name. A 2024 DPO post said “DPO Pay is now Network” and described DPO Pay as a product under the Network brand. The newer 2026 rebrand post describes a phased rollout in Kenya, Uganda, Botswana, Namibia, and Zambia. A Network newsletter says the rebrand went live in Uganda, Zambia, and Namibia during 2025 after regulatory approval. Current docs, agreement links, portal, and product pages still use “DPO Pay by Network” or “DPO Pay”; current merchants are told they keep their DPO Pay login, service, settlements, and support during transition. The rollout sources differ in date and country coverage, so the rebrand is recorded as ongoing rather than complete everywhere.

**Product sunset:** DPO Card stopped receiving settlements on 15 November 2023 and stopped working on 8 December 2023. This was the merchant virtual/prepaid card, not DPO’s payment gateway. The official FAQ says unused balances were returned to the merchant account and card access lasted six months after decommissioning.

The current DPO blog, PR archive, official Network newsroom, product pages, and DPO Card FAQ were checked for closure, sunset, or acquisition news. No notice of a shutdown of the core payment service was found. ## Cost example

**Our calculation — Kenya Easy Scan QR only:** on a KES 1,000 card payment, the published 2% rate gives `KES 1,000 × 0.02 = KES 20` in provider commission, before VAT. On a KES 1,000 M-PESA or Airtel Money QR payment, the published 0.5% rate gives `KES 1,000 × 0.005 = KES 5`, before VAT. The Easy Scan page says the customer pays no fee unless the merchant adds a markup. These rates do not establish DPO’s standard online-gateway price. Source: https://dpogroup.com/products/easy-scan/ (checked 2026-11-06).

No standard online-gateway transaction-cost example can be calculated from public prices: domestic/international card, local-method, conversion, recurring, and payout rates are account-specific or not published. No missing price was assumed to be zero.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Merchant of record:** FAQ calls Network the merchant of record in one answer, but also says the merchant bears chargeback losses; the PSP agreement describes the merchant as selling its own goods. The public sources do not settle the legal meaning.
- **Rebrand timing:** the 2025 newsletter says rollout went live in Uganda, Zambia, and Namibia during 2025; the 2026 blog lists a rollout across Kenya, Uganda, Botswana, Namibia, and Zambia. Current merchant docs still use DPO Pay names. This points to phased regional changes, but exact completion dates by market are not published.
- **Pricing:** the official fee/help pages and agreement direct sellers to their account Application or portal Commercial Terms. No public standard rate table was found for the nine country offers. The separate Kenya Easy Scan product does publish QR rates.
- **Limits:** card/account-specific amounts are not public. The MNO Advisory publishes indicative wallet limits by operator and warns customer-tier rules can reduce them.
- **Signup country list:** DPO advertises 20+ African countries and has nine country offer pages, but does not publish a current complete seller-country eligibility list. Global shopper reach is not used as seller eligibility.
- **Legal entities:** the current Exhibit A is linked but could not be read in this interface. Local office names were not substituted for contracting entities.
- **Pages checked for not-published tax service coverage:** FAQ, legal-agreements index/agreement, ecommerce page, product overview.
- **Pages checked for not-published minimum payout and payout currencies:** FAQ, agreement, commercial-terms help, and payment-method/currency docs.
- **Pages checked for unlisted subscription billing controls:** recurring/bulk page, tokenisation guide, and API docs.
- **Shutdown/news check:** DPO blog and PR archive, Network newsroom, current product pages, and the separate DPO Card decommission FAQ. No core gateway shutdown notice was found.

- F03: “20+ countries” refers to operating presence; the website does not provide a full current seller-signup list. Nine local merchant offer pages are currently visible.
