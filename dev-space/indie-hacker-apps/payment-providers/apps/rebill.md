## Conflicts and unknowns

- The registered-company-only signup FAQ conflicts with the merchant contract's definition of merchant as a natural or legal person. The public signup path returned 403, so the practical acceptance rule and form availability need confirmation from Rebill.
- Rebill's local market price pages show 8 calendar days for some accreditation rows while their FAQs say balances can be requested after 2 business days. Argentina has additional method-specific differences between its table and FAQ. Rebill's webhook docs distinguish accreditation from available balance, but the public price pages do not reconcile all timings.
- The general USD schedule is for cross-border collection and includes separate transaction and conversion charges. Do not apply it to the local-currency schedules or combine it with the local card rates.
- Brazil has a local legal affiliate and local payment methods, but the public detailed Brazil fee table is in the USD cross-border pricing schedule. Current terms say Rebill do Brasil does not act as local acquirer/sub-acquirer in Brazil's payment system.
- Local price pages give distinct refund and dispute charges. The general USD schedule separately lists USD 2 per refund and USD 15 per dispute; an account's negotiated terms may differ.
- The merchant terms allow a 7% rolling reserve for 30 days when a processor requires it, a 90-day transaction-specific hold and a post-termination hold up to 180 days. They are separate conditions.
- A complete seller-domicile list, product-category restriction list, buyer-country blocklist, minimum payout, per-transaction limits, fee for inactive account, PCI scope and card-token portability were not published in the pages checked.
- The pricing page shows local amounts with `$` for Mexico, Chile and Colombia. I retained each country's currency context and did not convert values.

- F04: The public pricing FAQ says registered companies only, while current contract language defines a merchant as a natural or legal person.
- F07: The public signup link is live, but its application host returned 403 to the research fetch. No signup or form submission was attempted.

- F33: Price-table accreditation times and FAQ balance-withdrawal times do not align on several local pages. The record preserves both.
