# PayHere

Shutdown, sunset and acquisition check: no current shutdown, sunset or acquisition notice was found in the official product, help and news sources checked. PayHere does disclose a historical cyberattack on 2 April 2022 and a 36-hour service outage. Payment/payout processing resumed on 3 April; the merchant portal was fully restored on 6 April 2022. The later apology acknowledges compromise of merchant, customer and payment data, including personal information and masked card numbers/expiry dates. Full card numbers were not stored/exposed. These are historical provider disclosures, not a current shutdown notice. ## Conflicts and unknowns

- Partial refunds: the refund guide, updated 2024-06-11, explicitly denies them; the Refund API, updated 2026-09-04, gives a partial-refund amount parameter. The newer API may differ from the portal, but the sources do not explain that difference. Both are retained rather than choosing a universal yes/no. Sources: https://support.payhere.lk/refund-process; https://support.payhere.lk/api-&-mobile-sdk/refund-api.
- Unregistered seller access: the current application guide allows home-based/freelance businesses and personal local bank accounts. The older official onboarding blog expressly limits unregistered businesses to HelaPay. The guide does not say that all payment methods are available to those categories. The old HelaPay signup link now redirects to the homepage; this does not settle card eligibility. Sources: https://support.payhere.lk/application-process; https://blog.payhere.lk/your-online-hotline-easy-guide-to-join-payhere/.
- Donations: the application guide forbids donation collection; the terminology page still mentions a donation as a payment example. The explicit onboarding restriction is the safer planning assumption, but the contradiction is preserved in F09. Sources: https://support.payhere.lk/application-process; https://support.payhere.lk/terminology.
- Payout clocks: general help says daily T+2, refund help says two bank days, the instalment article says T+2 bank working days, and agreement §4.4 says within three days from authorization without defining the day type. These may describe compatible ordinary cases, but weekend/holiday arrival and authorization/capture exceptions cannot be merged into a universal calendar-day promise. Sources: https://support.payhere.lk/general-questions; https://support.payhere.lk/refund-process; https://blog.payhere.lk/pay-now-pay-later-pay-easily-payhere-instalments/; https://www.payhere.lk/legal/; https://support.payhere.lk/hold-on-card.
- Merchant refund accounting: both help and legal terms describe returning processing charges to the customer. They do not expose the merchant's debit/credit entries. Buyer reimbursement and a merchant fee credit are different questions. Sources: https://support.payhere.lk/refund-process; https://www.payhere.lk/legal/.
- Fees not fully published: pricing, limits, USD payout help and the agreement were checked for tax on fees, fixed/minimum transaction charges, international issuer-only pricing, payout fees and dispute fees. No complete public schedule for those components was located. Monthly and transaction percentages are not treated as all-in prices. Sources: https://www.payhere.lk/fees/; https://support.payhere.lk/limits-and-fees; https://support.payhere.lk/usd-payouts; https://www.payhere.lk/legal/.
- Thresholds and reserves: general payout help, USD help, terminology and the agreement were checked. Release Level and holding time are defined, but no numeric minimum payout, universal reserve percentage or reserve release period is given. Sources: https://support.payhere.lk/general-questions; https://support.payhere.lk/usd-payouts; https://support.payhere.lk/terminology; https://www.payhere.lk/legal/.
- Billing and tax: recurring, subscription-management and token-charging docs were searched for native seats, credits, proration, tax calculation/filing and tax-invoice support. Merchant-supplied amounts and tax line items are not evidence of these managed services. ## Field problems

- F04/F12: allowing an unregistered seller to apply does not prove access to card payments. Legal form and enabled payment method need separate values.
- F20/F21/F22: premium card brand, foreign-issued card and foreign charge currency are different triggers. Only the published triggers were priced.
- F26: the sources describe the buyer's full refund but do not clearly describe the merchant's original processing-fee credit.
- F31/F32/F35: USD payout is a separate account edition with one eligible bank, its own transaction limits and LKR monthly fees. Treating it as a generic currency toggle loses these conditions.
- F33: T+2, bank days, legal settlement and the capture date need separate clocks. A single payout-days number is insufficient here.
- F37: API retrieval is useful evidence of record access, but not evidence of bulk export or saved-card portability.
- F39/F41: current public operation and provider security claims do not erase the disclosed 2022 incident or establish an independently verified current certificate.
- C01–C05: conditional crypto fields do not apply to this documented fiat offer. Their state does not imply an explicit crypto-business prohibition.
