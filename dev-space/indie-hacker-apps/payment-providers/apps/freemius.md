## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Seller and buyer countries:** [Supported Countries](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/selling-with-freemius/supported-countries/) distinguishes checkout and maker payouts, but its positive payout list includes Crimea/'Sevastapol' while its exclusion section blocks sanctioned Ukrainian regions. It also contains duplicates/old labels. The listed 190+ reach is retained as a provider claim, not a cleaned eligibility count. Full owner-residency and bank-country conditions remain unpublished after checking verification, payouts and seller terms.

- **Approval:** [Pricing](https://freemius.com/pricing/) advertises immediate/five-minute selling; [verification](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/selling-with-freemius/verification/) says some products need review and to check back in a few days. The [signup guide](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/getting-started/creating-an-account/) confirms free account creation only. No exact approval guarantee or complete public identity/beneficial-owner/website checklist was found.

- **Growth and legacy pricing:** [Detailed pricing](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/getting-started/our-pricing/), the pricing page and [pricing announcement](https://freemius.com/blog/new-freemius-pricing-2026/) publish progressive volume bands. [Pricing FAQ](https://freemius.com/help/faq/pricing/) instead describes tailored Growth pricing without a fixed table. Existing sellers can remain on legacy pricing; a future account's applicable Growth arrangement must be confirmed. Conditional calculations below do not settle this conflict.

- **Gateway and fee scope:** [Vendor Terms Exhibit A](https://freemius.com/terms/vendor/) supplies best-effort, changeable gateway figures. The [currency guide](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/selling-with-freemius/multi-currency/) supplies a European-card example; the pricing guide gives an average rather than a universal actual rate. No complete card-class/acquiring/country matrix, fee-tax schedule or negotiated caps was found. Freemius's share is on the full tax-inclusive transaction per the pricing FAQ.

- **Methods and presentment:** [May 2025 release](https://freemius.com/blog/freemius-release-notes-may-2025/) limits iDEAL to hosted checkout and plans wider availability. [Checkout overview](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/checkout/), [checkout configuration](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/checkout/integration/freemius-checkout-buy-button/) and Sales FAQ do not confirm rollout completion, Apple/Google Pay activation or a local-method tariff. [July 2026 notes](https://freemius.com/blog/freemius-release-notes-june-2026/) say newer-currency conversion was through Stripe with PayPal support in progress; [September notes](https://freemius.com/blog/freemius-release-notes-august-2026/) add CHF/RSD without closing that method-specific gap.

- **FX and payouts:** [Earnings/payout guide](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/selling-with-freemius/your-earnings/) and the currency guide explain separate USD/EUR/GBP configurations and automatic USD settlement of other presentment currencies. Vendor terms default to USD unless agreed otherwise and pass through partner conversion/transfer costs. A complete destination-currency code list, numeric FX matrix, non-USD payout floors and guaranteed receiving-bank arrival were not found.

- **PayPal payout charges:** [My Account FAQ markdown](https://freemius.com/help/faq/my-account.md) says US transfers are free and non-US transfers cost 2% capped at USD 20. Exhibit A instead gives US per-segment charges and non-US verified/unverified segmentation. Direct retrieval of the ordinary FAQ URL returned an empty body; its official markdown version was readable. The two fee versions are retained, not collapsed into a universal payout-wide cap.

- **Refunds:** [Refund Payment](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/selling-with-freemius/refund-payment/) permits partial refunds and card/PayPal windows of 365/180 days, while [Sales FAQ](https://freemius.com/help/faq/sales/) still says full-only/30 days. September release notes corroborate partial refunds. The vendor contract also limits percentage-fee reversals by time, with overlapping day-30 wording; pricing summaries omit that condition. The fixed fee and gateway commissions remain non-refundable.

- **Disputes:** the detailed pricing guide describes USD 15 to USD 20 processor fees on a loss; Exhibit A lists per-chargeback figures. These pages and Sales FAQ do not give a complete counter-dispute/returned-on-win schedule. No universal zero fee on a win is inferred.

- **Billing and checkout scope:** [Prepaid-credit guide](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/saas/usage-based-billing-credits/) makes the app maintain balances and consumption. [Plan setup](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/saas/saas-plans-pricing/) and [API index](https://freemius.com/help/api/) do not establish native postpaid metering, credit expiry or automatic overage invoices. Hosted/configuration docs do not publish own-domain hosted checkout terms. A custom recovery or success URL is a different capability.

- **External-sale licence hosting:** [Current checkout FAQ](https://freemius.com/checkout/) directly shows 6.1% + 50¢ for external sales with Freemius-hosted licences. A search excerpt showed the older 7% figure; direct retrieval and a fresh page open agreed on the newer figure. [EDD migration docs](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/migration/migrating-from-edd-to-freemius/) separately say no cut of legacy gateway renewals that only sync licences. New external licence hosting must not be conflated with that migration exception.

- **Migration:** [Migration page](https://freemius.com/freemius-migration/) and My Account FAQ promise business-data export/API access. EDD docs distinguish licence imports, renewal syncing, Stripe payment migration and unsupported PayPal account-to-account migration. No outbound saved-card/token/mandate procedure, conditions or fee was found in these pages, API index or vendor terms.

- **Closure and data:** vendor terms give notice and post-termination obligations, including a separately scoped three-month reserve. [Product Retirement](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/selling-with-freemius/product-retirement/) is customer-transition guidance, not account cancellation terms. [Privacy policy](https://freemius.com/privacy/) and [DPA](https://freemius.com/terms/dpa/) permit appropriate retention and deletion requests without publishing one universal erasure or final-liability-hold deadline.

- **Support, status and assurance:** [Support guide](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/getting-started/where-to-get-help/) publishes email/Slack and Slack hours, but no complete support-language list or contractual response SLA. The [official status page](https://status.freemius.com/) showed a data-fetch error on both attempts; it cannot establish uptime. PCI statements in pricing/Sales FAQ were recorded as provider claims; no current independently checked audit/licence was established from the legal/privacy/security wording.

## Field problems

- F03/F05/F10 require separate maker, owner, bank and buyer dimensions. The provider's duplicate/contradictory country list should not be normalized into an exact clean count without review.

- F20 to F25 must separate the platform percentage/fixed fee, gateway fee, tax-inclusive base, WordPress feature add-on, legacy plans and external-sale licence hosting. A headline 3.8% is not a complete processing price.

- F14/F15 must separate iDEAL initiation from SEPA renewals, prepaid-credit collection from application-managed consumption, and trial payment details from an upfront trial charge.

- F26 mixes refund capability, payment-provider windows and fee return. Current detailed docs and published FAQs disagree on different parts, so one field-level yes/no would hide material limits.

- F28/F32/F34 require partner/destination scope. Free Freemius payouts do not mean free bank/PayPal/Wise conversion, and USD 100 must not silently become EUR 100 or GBP 100.

- F33/F36/F44 need the sales-cohort delay, transfer funding, accelerated AI arrangement, rolling reserve and closure reserve kept separate. None alone guarantees a first or final bank-credit date.

- F37 needs separate evidence for business-data export, legacy renewal syncing, inbound payment migration and outbound payment-credential portability. Only the last route remained unpublished.

- F39 live status could not be checked because its public page did not expose data in either retrieval. This must not become an outage allegation or an uptime percentage.

- F41 is optional and distinguishes explicit provider security statements from independent proof. C01 to C05 are not triggered merely because approved blockchain-related software may be sold.
