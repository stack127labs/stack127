# WayForPay

- **Official URL:** https://wayforpay.com/uk
- **Status in apps.md:** active (row checked 2026-11-04)
- **Research start:** Fri Nov 6 23:36:44 UTC 2026
- **Research finish:** Fri Nov  6 23:54:59 UTC 2026
Minutes taken: 18.25
- **Current status / owner notice:** Both the Ukrainian and Czech WayForPay routes are live. The Ukrainian merchant agreement names Financial Company Way For Pay LLC; the Czech terms name WAYFORPAY s.r.o. I found no official acquisition, shutdown, sunset, licence-loss, or sanctions action notice in the company blog/news pages checked. The NBU's 2026 inspection plan lists the Ukrainian company for Q4; the plan is not an inspection result or a sanctions finding. Public ultimate ownership was not found.
- **Price-page check:** The Ukrainian site and Help fee page show a standard 2% success fee, no setup fee, and no plan, period, or currency selector. Help says business rates may be individual. The Czech site has no public fee selector or general price list; its terms put rates in a seller-specific List of Rates and signed point-of-sale specification. Feature articles advertise 1.5% for two Czech products, but do not state VAT treatment or fee currency.
- **Scope:** Ukrainian merchant gateway for resident FOPs and legal entities; separate Czech WAYFORPAY s.r.o. merchant route where terms are individual. The merchant sells to the buyer. Foreign-buyer card acceptance is not the same as foreign-seller eligibility.

## Cost example

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Ukraine payout Help says next banking day; both current Ukrainian sample merchant agreements allow up to three business days and include a UAH 500 accumulated payout threshold. The signed merchant agreement controls the seller's actual terms.
- Czech terms are an independent WAYFORPAY s.r.o. route. They set prices, settlement currency and settlement frequency through the merchant's List of Rates and point-of-sale specification. Public seller-country and legal-form eligibility are not stated.
- Czech Shop and QR Terminal articles say 1.5% per successful sale, but do not say whether that fee is in CZK/EUR/USD or whether VAT is included. General Czech card rates remain unpublished publicly.
- Ukrainian public 2% pricing does not state VAT treatment. The Ukrainian contract samples and current fee page were checked.
- Public ownership/shareholder information was not found in Ukrainian/Czech About and terms pages or the official blog/news pages checked. Legal operators are identified above; no private person's name is included.
- WayForPay lists the Ukrainian operator in the NBU's Q4 2026 inspection plan. The plan does not state an inspection result, licence action or sanctions outcome.
- The A-Bank “Pay in parts” Help text renders its six-payment merchant fee as “8,%”. Its fee image could not be read by the web fetch, so that one value remains unclear. Other installment schedules are transcribed from text on their individual official pages.
