## Cost examples

The percentage examples show the remainder before plan fees, setup and settlement. A settlement batches payments: the published LKR 10 charge is not charged once for each payment unless each is settled separately.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Setup fee: the price is consistently LKR/Rs.1,500, but the public pricing says pay after bank document approval. Onboarding says pay before application/agreement and promises a refund if card-network clearance fails. Current IPG §30 says paid setup/subscription fees are non-refundable. The exception and collection stage remain unresolved. Sources: https://onepay.lk/pricing; https://docs.onepay.lk/guide/getting-started; https://www.onepay.lk/agreement.html.
- Premium cards: current IPG Annexure 2 calls Amex/Diners/Discover 3.50% an MDR across plans. The pricing page calls its listed 3.5% an additional fee above standard processing. A 3.5-point surcharge would be materially different from a full 3.5% rate. Both sources are retained. Sources: https://www.onepay.lk/agreement.html; https://onepay.lk/pricing.

## Field problems

- F04/F05/F06: a blank document-table cell is not an express exemption. Keep individual eligibility, registration documents, bank ownership and residency separate.
- F11/F21/F22/F32: foreign buyer, foreign-issued card, charge currency and settlement currency are different dimensions. The provider's pages currently mix them.
- F14/F15/F24: Card on File charging, SDK subscriptions and no-code billing should not be flattened into one native billing feature set. Saved cards also have a monthly fee.
- F19/F35: subscription-waive-off volumes and monthly collection ceilings differ. A fee-waiver number is not a charge limit.
- F20/F26/F27: premium-card rates, ordinary refunds and chargebacks have different fee wording. Retaining commission on a chargeback does not prove commission is retained on every ordinary refund.
- F28/F33: per-settlement fees depend on batching. Processing date, bank working days and bank arrival are separate stages.
- F37/F42: report APIs and payment status are useful, but do not establish card-token portability, native downloads or software licence delivery.
- F39/F40/F44: the agreement page mixes versions/products. Current IPG terms were identified by the version3 container; POS-only reserve/termination terms stay outside this file.
- C01–C05 remain conditional and do not apply to this fiat offer.
- No apps.md row error was found. Its Sri Lanka gateway/recurring-billing description and distinction from Vietnam match this research.
