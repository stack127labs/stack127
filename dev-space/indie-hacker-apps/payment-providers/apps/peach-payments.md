## Shutdown, sunset, acquisition and security notices

- **Active; no provider-wide shutdown found.** Checked the [official blog](https://www.peachpayments.com/scale/), [newsroom](https://www.peachpayments.com/media/), company history and developer release notes. The status page reported [all systems operational](https://status.peachpayments.com/) at the time of this check. This does not guarantee uninterrupted service.
- **Ownership and acquisitions:** the disclosed group entity is Baobab Payments GmbH trading as Peach Payments. [Apis Growth Fund II led the funding completed in 2023](https://www.peachpayments.com/scale/peach-payments-concludes-us30m-funding-deal/); its [portfolio lists the investment](https://www.apis.pe/portfolio/our-portfolio/peach-payments/). Current controlling ownership percentages were not published in the sources reviewed. The [company history](https://www.peachpayments.com/about/) describes PayDunya as acquired in 2025; the [3 April 2025 original announcement](https://www.peachpayments.com/scale/peach-payments-acquires-west-african-payments-gateway-paydunya/) had been subject to closing. PayDunya's six-country offer is not treated as the same merchant signup/pricing offer.
- **Retired methods:** [dated release notes](https://developer.peachpayments.com/docs/payments-api-release-notes) remove Nedbank Direct EFT on 21 July 2026, FinChoicePay on 26 June 2024 and the older Instant EFT on 29 January 2024. Historical marketing and status-component labels still mention some retired methods. This is separate from Nedbank card acquiring.
- **Integration change:** [new custom integrations should use Peach Orchestration](https://developer.peachpayments.com/docs/product-portfolio-overview). Classic Payment Links, Payment Page, extensions and other product docs remain relevant; this is not a provider-wide shutdown.
- **Public PCI evidence needs renewal:** the [currently linked certificate](https://www.peachpayments.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Peach-Payments-Certificate-2025.pdf) was issued 4 November 2025 and expired **3 November 2026**. The [legal hub](https://www.peachpayments.com/legal/) and [certificate page](https://www.peachpayments.com/legal-doc/pci-compliance-certificate/) still claim compliance. No replacement certificate was found in official-site searches. Expired public evidence does not by itself prove that the provider is currently non-compliant.

## Scope and evidence rules

Core seller offers are South Africa, Kenya and Mauritius. Wider group reach and buyer coverage do not establish additional self-service signup countries. The linked July 2025 merchant agreement is a **South African contract and schedule**; its Rand charges and legal entity are not copied to Kenyan or Mauritian accounts.

Growth and Enterprise are separated where they differ. Numeric method prices below are South African Growth advertised prices unless explicitly labelled otherwise. Country tables for Kenya and Mauritius generally require quotes. All published pricing is kept in its original currency; South African fees exclude VAT. A combined displayed method price already containing R1.50 is not given another R1.50 by default. Where the agreement itself appears to require a different fixed total, that difference is recorded.

States describe the particular scoped claim in each row. Unknown rows identify the official pages searched. No account was opened, and no private quote or merchant-specific schedule was obtained.

## Cost examples — our calculations

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: [current fees](https://www.peachpayments.com/fees/), [South African MSA schedule](https://www.peachpayments.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Peach-MSA-Automated-Onboarding-Updated-on-22-July-2025.pdf) and [SARS confirmation that the standard VAT rate remains 15%](https://www.sars.gov.za/media-release/practical-implication-on-finance-ministers-decision-to-reverse-vat/). These examples use the **entire charged payment amount** as the percentage base, not a price before the merchant's own sales tax.

Assumptions: South African Growth, one successful debit per sale, no refund/dispute, no own-acquirer extras, no optional risk points, messaging, verification or paid consulting. These are calculations from public inputs, not an account quote. VAT is a tax on Peach's fees here. Figures with more than two decimal places show the exact arithmetic followed by an explicitly rounded cash illustration; actual invoice rounding may differ.

| Scenario and inputs | Formula | Result — our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One R100 local 3DS card payment | R100 × 2.95% + R1.50 | R4.45 excluding VAT; × 1.15 = R5.1175 including VAT, rounded to **R5.12**. R94.8825 remaining before the merchant's own taxes, rounded R94.88. |
| One R500 local 3DS card payment | R500 × 2.95% + R1.50 | R16.25 excluding VAT; R18.6875 including VAT, rounded **R18.69**. R481.3125 remaining, rounded R481.31. |
| One R500 international card payment | R500 × 3.50% + R1.50 | R19.00 excluding VAT; **R21.85** including VAT; R478.15 remaining. The foreign rate replaces the local rate. |

### Differences that can be resolved by date or product scope

1. **Old setup/Payflex prices:** the [16 June 2025 merchant-account guide](https://support.peachpayments.com/support/solutions/articles/47001098859) lists R250 setup and other older examples. The current [fee page](https://www.peachpayments.com/fees/) and [22 July 2025 onboarding agreement](https://www.peachpayments.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Peach-MSA-Automated-Onboarding-Updated-on-22-July-2025.pdf) both give zero setup for the standard route. The older guide is still useful for route-dependent onboarding estimates, with its age made explicit.
2. **Country tabs:** extracted pricing HTML contains all three country tables. The ZAR 2.95% Growth offer is South African, not a Kenyan rate merely because a country query parameter was present. Kenya and Mauritius quote account/card/local-method fees by volume.
3. **Settlement conditions:** current pricing explicitly says Growth daily settlement starts after two weeks of trading; generic T+1 agreement/help text describes the standard daily process. The exact first-two-week schedule is not published there. Own merchant-account bank cycles are separate. Older weekly examples, including the [2023 launch article's Mobicred exception](https://www.peachpayments.com/scale/daily-settlements/), are not silently substituted for current general terms; any surviving method-specific exception needs the seller's applicable method terms.
4. **Method editions:** classic PayJustNow POS-only and classic PayPal support do not define every new Orchestration capability. New integrations use their own [method table](https://playground.peachpayments.com/operate/payment-methods); the [classic table](https://developer.peachpayments.com/docs/pp-payment-methods) remains necessary for older products.
5. **MoneyBadger refund information:** the older [August 2025 FAQ](https://support.peachpayments.com/support/solutions/articles/47001280105-moneybadger-faq) speaks broadly about wallet details. Current method docs limit the initial-address requirement to Bitcoin Lightning and name Bybit among wallets not needing that address. The [method terms](https://www.peachpayments.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/MoneyBadger-Terms-Conditions.pdf) also allow a support-assisted collection route after payment; that does not make the API's initial-data requirement disappear.
6. **Privacy dates:** the linked policy's filename contains Feb-2026, but its body says **updated February 2025**. The body date is recorded; the filename is not treated as a new effective date.
