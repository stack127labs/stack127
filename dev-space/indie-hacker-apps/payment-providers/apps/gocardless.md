## Field problems

- **F20/F21:** these ask for card fees. They are not applicable to this bank-payment provider; the full bank-payment prices, including international conditions, are under F23.
- **F03/F10/F11:** seller jurisdiction, payer bank location, scheme access and currency are different lists. The January product document's “non-Eurozone” country labels are retained only as provider categories.
- **F19/F23/F27:** named plans have multiple legal fee editions. Public prices can be collected, but the exact private account's applicable edition cannot be inferred from a plan name alone.
- **F23/F22:** an international fee's fixed component is billed in the payer currency using the provider's published table. Copying the seller's GBP/USD amount onto a EUR collection would be wrong.
- **F15/F29/F30/F42:** payment collection, tax on provider fees, buyer invoicing and product delivery are separate functions. A subscription/invoice use case is not proof of tax filing, usage measurement or automatic access delivery.
- **F33/F36/F44:** instant confirmation is not bank receipt or guaranteed final settlement. A no-routine-reserve claim must not erase the contract's risk/security rights.
- **C01–C05:** these conditional fields do not apply to the researched fiat offer. Restricted crypto business activity is not the same question as crypto payment acceptance.
