**Current status:** Active. Tpay is the payment brand of Krajowy Integrator Płatności S.A. (KIP). The current Tpay company profile and press room show active product and business updates. I found no acquisition, shutdown, or service-sunset notice in the official blog and press pages checked. Bank Pekao S.A. reports a 38.33% shareholding in KIP in its 2025 group report; it is a significant minority shareholder and strategic partner, not identified there as sole owner. The Tpay sources checked do not identify Ailleron or the Polish Payment Standard (PSP) as the current parent. PSP is named as the operator behind BLIK recurring payments. The former brand Transferuj.pl changed to Tpay.com in 2015; Tpay remains the current service.

Scope: Tpay’s online payment gateway, payment links, recurring card/BLIK collection and available online payment methods. Tpay is the payment intermediary; the merchant sells and fulfils the underlying product or service. The separate TpayGO phone-terminal service is outside the online fee comparison. All prices below are the current public Polish online plans in PLN. The live offer does not say whether displayed amounts include VAT. Tpay’s current terms require VAT invoices for commissions and other charges, but do not state that the public rate amounts are net or gross. I have left that tax-inclusion question unpublished rather than guessing. No account or form was submitted and no private people are named.

All 45 non-crypto fields, including optional fields, and all five crypto-conditional fields were attempted. Tpay is a conventional payment provider, so the crypto fields do not apply. Public registration supports Polish businesses; foreign companies may be reviewed, but Tpay does not publish a list of accepted foreign seller countries. The current offer page is used for plan prices. An older 2026 package PDF and older support material show some different BLIK and foreign-payout amounts, noted below where they matter.

## Cost examples

Our calculation for a PLN 100.00 domestic online bank-transfer payment:

- Inputs: PLN 100.00 payment; Starter rate 1.59% + PLN 0.39; no refund or payout fee included.
- Formula: 1.59% × PLN 100.00 + PLN 0.39 = PLN 1.59 + PLN 0.39.
- Result: **PLN 1.98 Tpay fee** before any account-specific charges. The published offer does not say whether this amount includes VAT.

For comparison, the same PLN 100.00 transfer on Business at 0.99% costs PLN 0.99 in transaction fees. If at least one transaction occurs in that calendar month, add the PLN 99 service fee. VAT inclusion for those displayed amounts is not stated.
