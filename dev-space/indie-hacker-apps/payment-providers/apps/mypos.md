## Conflicts and unknowns

- The current country pricing pages do not use a single European online rate. France, Germany/Austria, Belgium and the Netherlands publish One/Plus/Pro account plans; other checked country pages publish pay-per-transaction rates, often in local currency. I kept the country schedules separate and grouped only countries whose own pages displayed matching online terms.
- The UK pricing page says EEA-issued card-not-present payments for UK merchants are charged as non-EEA after Brexit. That affects the rate class for a UK seller.
- Online transaction and plan fee pages do not say whether VAT is included. Hardware-store wording that terminal prices exclude VAT does not establish the VAT treatment of processing fees.
- The service agreement states a default 10% e-commerce reserve, up to one year or longer when legally necessary, but also allows account-specific variation. The developer help page says some accounts may not have a reserve.
- Recurring card payments require support activation and a merchant-built billing process. No built-in subscription-management feature set was found.
- myPOS credits processing proceeds to its e-money account within seconds; separate external-bank transfers may carry account/country/plan fees.
- Pages checked for restricted products and business types: public acceptance policy, service terms, online payment and developer pages. No full product-by-product approval table for SaaS, downloads, AI services, adult, crypto or crowdfunding was found.
- Pages checked for VAT and tax treatment: local pricing plans, account fee schedules, service terms and online checkout pages. Service-fee VAT inclusion and tax filing coverage were not stated; the online store terms only explain VAT on hardware orders.
- Ownership/news check used myPOS newsroom and company history pages. The official pages document Advent International joining in 2024 and myPOS's later acquisitions; no shutdown or later sale notice was found.

## Field problems

- Country pricing pages use different account plans, card groups and local fixed-fee currencies. The file groups only identical checked schedules; it does not infer rates for unreviewed markets from a neighboring country.
- VAT inclusion is not stated for the public online processing and plan fees. Every listed fee records that unknown explicitly.
- The default e-commerce reserve is material, but a specific seller's reserve may be different or absent under its account terms.
- Chargeback initiation amounts shown in some account tariffs can refer to disputes on the myPOS business card. They are not treated as a merchant acquiring chargeback fee without an explicit statement.
- The country list comes from current product-market selectors plus service eligibility terms. Applicants still need local residence/registration and individual approval.
