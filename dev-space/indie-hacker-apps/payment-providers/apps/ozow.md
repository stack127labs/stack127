## Shutdown, sunset, acquisition and ownership check

The official website, current product pages, terms, company blog and products-and-partnerships blog were checked for an Ozow shutdown, sunset or acquisition notice. No such company-wide notice appeared in the pages searched. The blog lists product and partnership updates through 6 August 2026; a separately labelled newsroom was not found in the official navigation. The May 2026 Happy Pay and April 2026 Lula announcements describe partnerships, not acquisitions. Ozow's legal terms identify **Ozow (Pty) Ltd**, company number `2013/214663/07`; the public pages checked do not identify its current controlling owner or ultimate shareholder. ## Conflicts, differences and unresolved points

1. **Monthly fee:** the public pricing page and FAQ say no monthly subscription/setup fee, but the card product FAQ says monthly service fees may apply. No public rate or scope resolves the difference. Both statements are retained in F19.
2. **Settlement timing:** package pricing advertises next-day settlement; current Hub docs say settlement varies by method and merchant arrangement. The card guide says weekly, BNPL says weekly, voucher usually next day and can take up to three days, while Pay by Bank has daily weekday/Saturday times. The broad marketing claim is not treated as a guarantee for all methods. Merchant FAQ's “same day or standard EFT” wording is also less specific than the method guides.
3. **Card bands:** public domestic-card rates are clear as amounts and percentages, but Ozow does not define what the displayed R0–R1,000,000 ranges measure or their period. It also does not explain the next rate above R1,000,000. Each tier is therefore `unclear`, not silently interpreted as monthly volume.
4. **AVS trigger:** pricing says AVS is required for high-risk-category merchants on the first successful transaction per unique bank account. Merchant terms describe designation for high-risk PayShap merchants, cached valid results and an expiry determined by Ozow. The R0.50 fee excluding VAT applies per actual check; the precise repeat-check trigger depends on the cached result/account rules.
5. **API deprecation:** Payments API docs are deprecated but still supported, have no announced end-of-life date and do not accept new merchants. One API is current. This is a product migration issue, not an Ozow shutdown.
6. **Crypto details:** Ozow publishes the crypto payment path and ZAR merchant settlement, but not a readable supported-asset/network or wallet list, conversion rate/spread, or crypto-specific refund and payment-expiry rules. Ozow's public price does not guarantee an account-specific price because merchant terms allow a separate written price.
7. **Unknowns searched:** category approval, buyer country coverage, currency conversion, recurring billing add-ons, invoices/tax IDs, closure and release timing, reserve amounts, exports/payment-data portability, approval timing, and crypto asset/network/refund details were checked against the URLs in their respective rows. Missing information is not treated as a zero fee or a “no” feature.
