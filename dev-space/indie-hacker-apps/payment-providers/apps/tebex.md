## Cost examples

These are our calculations, checked 2026-11-04. The checkout examples use the fee guide's native $ notation as a common unit, with a $0.40 PayPal fixed fee, zero sales tax, no conversion, no special agreement and no Plus plan cost. They do not establish that the same fixed charge is $0.40 in every accepted charge currency. No dollar-to-GBP or other currency conversion is performed.

For tax-free price G, platform rate p, PayPal gateway rate r = 2.49% and fixed fee f = $0.40:

- Platform fee = G × p.
- Gateway fee = G × r + f.
- Transaction fees = platform fee + gateway fee.
- Creator net before payout/plan costs = G - transaction fees.

The platform fee and gateway fee are separate deductions. Both percentage components use the same tax-free transaction amount in these examples.
