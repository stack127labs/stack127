# Fat Zebra

Shutdown, sunset and acquisition check: No whole-provider shutdown notice was found in the current product, pricing and news pages reviewed. Fat Zebra acquired Pin Payments on 11 December 2024 and SecurePay from Australia Post on 1 April 2025. Its SecurePay announcement promised service continuity. These are acquisitions, not proof that either acquired brand or the core Fat Zebra service shut down.
