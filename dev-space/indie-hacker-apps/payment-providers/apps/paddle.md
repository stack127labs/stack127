## Conflicts and unknowns

All pages below were checked on 2026-11-04. An unknown means the cited pages did not settle that component; it does not prove the feature or charge does not exist.

- **Dispute threshold and evidence procedure:** the specific chargeback help and the seller handbook disagree on both the percentage/measurement and whether sellers supply extra evidence. Both are preserved as `conflicting` rows. Neither page gives an effective date resolving the mismatch. Sources: https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/risk-prevention/understanding-chargebacks-with-paddle; https://www.paddle.com/seller-guides/seller-handbook.
- **Ordinary refund cost:** the API/changelog prove that a retained-fee field exists, not that every original fee is retained in every refund. Following the changelog's “how fees are calculated” link leads to a handbook without the promised general refund-fee explanation. Read the full/partial refund guide, help article and adjustment definition; no universal retained percentage/fixed fee or separate ordinary refund fee was settled. Tax-only adjustments have an explicit fee-return rule. Sources: https://developer.paddle.com/changelog/2025/retained-fees-payout-totals/; https://www.paddle.com/seller-guides/seller-handbook; https://developer.paddle.com/build/transactions/create-transaction-adjustments/; https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/your-customers/how-do-i-issue-refunds; https://developer.paddle.com/api-reference/adjustments/create-adjustment/.
- **Payout wording:** payout help opens with a broad no-extra-fees statement and then lists SWIFT/FX exceptions. The detailed exceptions agree with the route-specific fee structure, so the file records them instead of calling every payout free. Source: https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/get-paid/is-there-a-fee-taken-for-payouts.
