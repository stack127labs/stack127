Current site footers say “Plisio, Inc.” An official Plisio article published in May 2026 says “UK PLISIO LTD #12374919”; UK Companies House records that company as dissolved on 2021-06-29. Exact-name searches of official regulator domains (FCA, FinCEN, FINTRAC, and BCSC) did not surface a clear Plisio entry or enforcement notice. The FCA Register search page and FinCEN MSB search tool were opened, but their interactive query fields could not be used through the web reader. This does not establish that no registration or enforcement record exists. Treat the current legal operator, its country, and licensing status as unresolved.

## Cost examples

Our calculation, using a $100-equivalent invoice before any network fee or tax:

- Gateway API at 0.5%: `$100 × 0.005 = $0.50` equivalent processing fee.
- White Label at 1.5%: `$100 × 0.015 = $1.50` equivalent processing fee.
- USDT BEP-20 Gateway API at 1%: `$100 × 0.01 = $1.00` equivalent processing fee; White Label at 2%: `$100 × 0.02 = $2.00`.

These are our calculations from Plisio's published rates. The actual fee is charged in crypto, asset-specific minimums may apply, and blockchain network fees are separate. The examples assume the customer pays the processing fee; Plisio's store settings allow the fee to be passed to customers.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Custody and settlement:** The standard balance, cold-storage, and withdrawal docs indicate Plisio account custody. A May 2026 official editorial article says Plisio is non-custodial and routes funds directly to a merchant wallet. The API and FAQ pages were checked; no matching direct-wallet configuration was found. I left this as conflicting.
- **Legal operator:** Current site footer says “Plisio, Inc.”; linked terms do not identify a company or country; a Plisio editorial page names UK PLISIO LTD #12374919, which Companies House says dissolved in 2021. The relationship is unproven. I checked the Plisio terms, footer, Companies House record, FCA Register, and FinCEN MSB search.
- **Identity checks:** Current FAQ/security claims say no proof of identity is required; the linked AML/KYC PDF allows risk-triggered identity checks. The public pages disagree on whether any identity check can occur.
- **Fees and minimums:** The current English fee schedule omits APE and LoveBit; the official Polish translation lists both. The older send/receive minimum FAQ includes a different ETH minimum from the current fee table and only covers some assets. The fee table is used for merchant invoice minimums; payout minimums remain incomplete.
- **Settlement speed:** “Instant transfers” is marketing copy, while blockchain settlement depends on confirmation and network conditions. No guaranteed payout schedule or time-to-bank was found.
- **Not published after checking:** No seller-country allowlist, seller legal-form matrix, routine merchant KYB checklist, fiat bank payout route, recurring billing, tax service, statutory tax invoice, public sandbox, universal refund treatment, complete payout minimum schedule, data export/migration route, reserve percentage, or account approval estimate was found. I checked the pricing page, API docs, terms, AML/KYC PDF, FAQ, integrations page, and official blog where each topic applied.
