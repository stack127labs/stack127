## Cost example

Our calculation for one UAE domestic-card sale of AED 1,000: 2.6% × AED 1,000 + AED 1 = AED 27. The June 29, 2026 Business-fees article says the fee includes 5% VAT, so no extra VAT is added in this example. An international card or non-AED currency adds 1.5% × AED 1,000 = AED 15, giving AED 42 total under that help-page schedule. This assumes the surcharge is applied once when either condition applies; the public page does not say the 1.2% rate markup stacks as a separate charge when the seller requests non-AED.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The UAE Business-fees help article (June 29, 2026) says the 2.6% + AED 1 and extra 1.5% fees include 5% VAT. The Fees page (January 6, 2026) says displayed fees are subject to 5% VAT. The newer business-specific help page is used for the cost example; the difference remains visible.
- Fees page schedules cash-outs as free currently and AED 1 from November 1, 2026. That effective date has passed, but the table still labels AED 1 as future; the September 24 cash-out help page says Ziina does not charge a cash-out fee. Current treatment is unresolved.
- For a non-AED seller request, the help page says the FX rate includes a 1.2% markup; the merchant fee article also says non-AED payments add 1.5%. The pages do not clearly say whether these are both applied as separate costs or how the rate markup appears in settlement.

- Bank arrival estimates differ: business marketing says 1–2 business days for transfers under AED 25,000; cash-out help says almost instant for Ziina Card accounts and about 12 hours for other business accounts.
- Owner/shareholders beyond the licensed legal operator Ziina Payment LLC are not disclosed in About, terms, privacy or contact pages.
- Public unknowns after checking current docs, help, fees and terms: foreign seller eligibility, buyer-country blocks, full approval list by product type, seller recurring-billing support and method limits, custom checkout domain, statutory tax-invoice handling, fixed chargeback fee, complete FX stacking rule, customer card-token migration and split marketplace payouts.
