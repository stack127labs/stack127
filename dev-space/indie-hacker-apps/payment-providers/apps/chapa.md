## Scope and current status

This record covers Chapa's current public payment platform, while keeping the newer `chapa.global` API and marketing pages separate from the Ethiopia-focused `chapa.co` merchant agreement and older API docs. The latest official company notice found is dated November 3, 2026. It calls Chapa Financial Technologies S.C. a POS and payment gateway operator and schedules a shareholder meeting for November 28, 2026. The site and signup route are live. I found no official shutdown, sunset or acquisition notice in the company news or blog pages checked.

The new site describes global payments and lists Canada, Uganda and Ethiopia on pricing/contact pages. The public merchant agreement still defines its territory as Ethiopia and requires a local bank account. Public evidence does not settle whether businesses registered outside Ethiopia can onboard to the newer global offer. A global buyer-payment claim is not treated as proof of foreign-seller eligibility.

All 45 general fields and all five crypto-conditional fields were attempted. One row is recorded per scoped fact; the old static Ethiopia method table is kept separate from the current API's country-and-currency-specific method catalog. No account was created or accessed.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Foreign sellers:** Ethiopia is the explicit territory in the merchant agreement and it requires a local settlement bank. Pricing/contact pages show Canada, Uganda and Ethiopia, while the global signup page uses “wherever you are”; the signup capture did not load its country list. The public sources do not establish that a foreign-incorporated business can open a live account. Pages checked: pricing, contact, merchant agreement, terms, signup, v2 quick start and payout-bank docs.
- **Foreign cards:** the current pricing page advertises 1% per successful international transaction, but it does not specify a card-only rate or issuer-country matrix. The August 2022 terms require a pre-approved partner program when expected international card sales exceed 20% of total sales. No later policy explains how those terms apply to the newer global offer.
- **Currencies:** v2 quick start lists four charge currencies; legacy v1 sample lists two; marketing pages say 25+ or 140+ currencies without defining whether those are charge, presentment or settlement currencies. Method-level availability can depend on the authenticated account.
- **Pricing details:** no public fixed card fee, method-specific local fee, payout rate, refund-fee treatment, subscription surcharge or universal FX spread was found. The merchant agreement refers to an attached account pricing schedule, but the public agreement page does not expose it.
- **Language check:** Amharic-language searches for Chapa payment/pricing/legal material and official `chapa.co` Amharic URLs did not surface a richer official Amharic page. English pricing, terms, developer docs, help center, blog and company news were checked.
- **Company news:** official news list and blog were checked for shutdown, sunset and acquisition notices. None surfaced; latest news item is the November 3, 2026 shareholder-meeting notice. This is a search result, not a guarantee no notice exists elsewhere.

## Field problems

- The newer global site/API and Ethiopia-specific agreement/legacy docs are live at the same time. Their seller-country and currency scopes are not mapped together publicly; the file keeps each edition/source separate.
- The `payment-methods` and payout-bank v2 endpoints require a merchant API key for the live enabled-method matrix. Their public docs explain the fields and limits, but cannot show a complete account-specific international list without sign-in.
- “International transaction” on pricing is broader than “international card sales” in the terms. They are recorded separately; their relationship remains unresolved.
- Public documentation sometimes uses API amounts in integer cents/minor units and the legacy method table in ETB. Values are kept in their published units and not converted across currencies.
