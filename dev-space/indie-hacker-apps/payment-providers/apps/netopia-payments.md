**Current status:** Active Romanian online payment processor operated by NETOPIA FINANCIAL SERVICES S.A. Investor Innova Capital lists NETOPIA Group as an active portfolio company and reports an 80% stake. NETOPIA’s official blog records the 2023 majority-stake sale; I checked its current blog/news archive and found no shutdown or later acquisition notice. The company announced live RoPay checkout and a wider BNR licence in 2026. “NETOPIA Payments” is the processor; mobilPay Wallet is a separate listed product, and some legacy API/support paths still use “mobilPay.”

Scope: Online card processing, card wallets, Link2Pay, recurring payments, instalments, SMS payments, RoPay and marketplace split payments. Card terminals and the separate mobilPOS product are excluded. The current card tariff is on the Romanian registration/pricing page and says it applies from 1 August 2026. I checked the page and the card service page for currency or plan switches: no such selector appeared; the card rates shown are in RON, with a volume-negotiated option. A February support contract still displays the previous 0.99% card rate; the newer published tariff is recorded below. NETOPIA does not publish a complete non-Romanian seller-country list.

## Cost examples

These are our calculations from public prices. Processing fees exclude VAT (TVA); no tax rate is added. The card example assumes an ordinary successful RON card payment, no Shopify-specific agreement, no refund and no payout-transfer fee.

- **RON card payment of RON 500:** 1.19% × RON 500 + RON 0.30 = **RON 6.25 before TVA**.
- **Orange SMS payment of EUR 5:** the published Orange fee is **EUR 1.20 before TVA**. This is NETOPIA’s fee, not the buyer’s EUR 5 charge.
- **Orange SMS payment of EUR 2:** 55% × EUR 2 = **EUR 1.10 before TVA**.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The current card tariff page shows 1.19% + RON 0.30 from 1 August 2026. A February 2026 support offer and contract appendix show the older 0.99% + RON 0.30. The newer tariff is used for standard card costs.
- The latest interbank-fee guide gives the current RON middle bracket as RON 1,000–4,999.99 for RON 6, while the February contract/card offer shows RON 1,000–49,999.99. I use the later dated guide and record the older threshold in the row note.
- The current page lists the instalment range and issuer-dependent pricing but no current bank-by-bank rate matrix. An older February table has detailed bank rates; I did not treat it as the current schedule.
- NETOPIA does not publish a complete foreign seller-country, foreign bank-account, or international settlement eligibility list. The public onboarding path is built around Romanian business registration and a RON bank statement.
- The current public card page does not state a currency or plan selector. It lists RON prices; the API’s EUR/USD/GBP support requires legal approval. No foreign-currency processing markup was published.
- The public pages do not settle whether the old 0.35% Shopify surcharge still stacks with the August 2026 rate, or whether card-processing fees are returned for an in-platform refund.
