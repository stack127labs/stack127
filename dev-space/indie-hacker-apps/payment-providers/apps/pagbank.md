## Cost examples

Our calculations use a R$100.00 customer payment, the published rate and no seller-specific discount, tax, shipping, dispute or installment surcharge. They are not PagBank quotations.

- **Checkout credit card, 14-day receipt:** R$100.00 × 4.99% + R$0.40 = **R$5.39 fee**; calculated remainder **R$94.61**.
- **Checkout credit card, 30-day receipt:** R$100.00 × 3.99% + R$0.40 = **R$4.39 fee**; calculated remainder **R$95.61**.

- **Link campaign, eligible R$100.00 single-installment credit sale during promotion:** R$100.00 × 0% = **R$0.00 fee**, if the account has enrolled/qualified and the campaign rate applies. Promotion dates, eligibility and rate validity through 2026-12-31 are in the F20 notes.
