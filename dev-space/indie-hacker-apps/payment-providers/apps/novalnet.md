## Cost examples

- **Our calculation — €100 Visa/Mastercard transaction at the displayed EUR base rate:** €0.24 transaction fee + (€100 × 1.70%) = **€1.94**. This uses the visible German price-table value, assumes the listed 1.70% applies to the full €100, and excludes any VAT because the page does not say whether VAT is included. It does not include setup/monthly fees or any account-specific terms.
- **Our calculation — €100 non-EU/commercial Visa/Mastercard transaction using the German table:** €0.24 + (€100 × 2.90%) = **€3.14**. The English page’s separate “1.5% surcharge” wording is not added here; see conflicts below.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Card-country surcharge:** The current German pricing page gives commercial and non-EU Visa/Mastercard a total displayed rate of €0.24 + 2.90%. The English `/payment-plans/` page instead says a 1.5% surcharge applies to commercial/business and non-EU cards, without stating whether this adds to the 1.70% base. The German EUR table is the clearest locally tailored figure, but the cross-page wording remains unresolved for sellers using other region/currency selector settings.
- **Other fixed price:** `novalnet.com/products/fees-and-prices/` says “from €49.90” but does not identify a fee period/type. The dedicated German Instant plan clearly shows €99 setup and €25 monthly; I have not treated €49.90 as the same charge.
- **Selector options:** English and German pricing pages were opened and the Region/Currency selectors were checked. The page extraction exposed the controls but not their option lists or alternative rates, so only the displayed EUR table is recorded.
- **Tax on provider fees:** German price table, English payment-plans page and general fee page checked for MwSt/VAT; no statement on whether amounts include VAT was found.
- **Owner:** Official imprint, company overview and blog/news pages identify Novalnet AG as the operator but do not publish a parent/shareholder statement. No private person is named.
- **Seller-country eligibility:** The regional page describes payment reach, not a seller-country allowlist. Compliance page lists 40 high-risk and 35 prohibited jurisdictions. FAQ says applicants are generally considered but does not provide a complete accepted-country table.
- **Contract terms:** FAQ, imprint, privacy notice and the public plugin licence were checked. The full payment-services agreement is signed at onboarding and is not published as a universal merchant contract; therefore tax allocation, standard dispute fees and post-closure fund release cannot be settled publicly.
- **Crypto:** Novalnet’s payment-method catalogue lists BitPay, while the pricing page bars cryptocurrency/Bitcoin businesses. These can refer respectively to a buyer payment method and a prohibited merchant category, but Novalnet does not publish asset/network, custody, conversion or refund details. Treat crypto capability as unclear beyond the catalogue listing.
- **Shutdown/acquisition check:** Official English blog, German magazine/press pages and current product/pricing pages checked. No acquisition, shutdown or feature sunset announcement found; the official blog includes a 2025 Visa-acquirer announcement and current 2026 posts.

## Field problems

- Price selectors did not expose their country/currency option values in the page text. Prices are therefore scoped to the visible EUR schedule.
- No public merchant contract was found, so contract-only fees, tax allocation and closure-fund rules stay unknown.
- Novalnet’s official directory lists a very large number of payment methods, including legacy-looking entries. I recorded the methods needed for the fee comparison and separately flagged BitPay because it affects the crypto-only fields.
- The owner field distinguishes legal operator from ultimate ownership. The official pages confirm Novalnet AG as operator but do not state the ultimate owner.
