# NAPS

- Official URL: [NAPS Morocco](https://naps.ma/)
- apps.md row: active; gateway; Middle East and North Africa; Morocco. The row describes a dedicated online merchant acceptance offer.
- Product researched: NAPS ePay for website/app checkout and NAPS ePay Link for payment links, both offered by the Moroccan payment provider NAPS SA. Physical TPE prices are excluded.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Current ePay fees:** the current NAPS ePay page opened on 2026-11-07 lists 1.65% for national cards, 3.2% for international/premium cards, 1 DH processing per transaction, 6,900 DH HT setup, and 2,900 DH yearly. A search preview had older 1.75%/3% figures; the opened page and its FAQ today agree on 1.65%/3.2%, so those are recorded as current. The page does not clearly mark the 1 DH fee, annual price, and percentages HT/TTC.
- **Older ePay price card:** the still-public older NAPS page lists 1.98% national, 1.68% under a Bank Al-Maghrib condition, 3% international, and a first-year-free subscription. Those values are not carried over to the current Go card. It separately lists Premium setup/subscription prices, but the current Premium card does not show a price.
- **Payment links:** the current link product is a distinct offer: 3,900 DH HT setup, 990 DH HT per year, 1 DH transaction processing, 1.75% national and 3% international. These figures are not substituted for ePay Go.
- **Foreign sellers and currencies:** public pages target Moroccan merchants and require local business records/RIB but do not publish a foreign seller list, charge currencies, payout currencies, or a foreign-bank exception.
- **Merchant legal terms:** I checked the official client flow, its linked merchant terms URL, the public NAPS terms page, and the privacy page. The linked merchant terms page timed out; the accessible older terms are for prepaid-card holders, not merchant acquiring.
