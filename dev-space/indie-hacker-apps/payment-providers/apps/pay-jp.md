## Cost examples — our calculations

Checked 2026-11-05. These examples use a Japan merchant, JPY one-time sales, no refunds/disputes unless stated, and the ordinary published plans. Plan fees and bank-transfer fees include tax; card-processing fees are consumption-tax exempt. Round each processing fee down to whole yen, as the help states. No currency conversion is performed.
