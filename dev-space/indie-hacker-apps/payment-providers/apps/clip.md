## Cost examples

These are our calculations from Clip’s public online fee information, not quotes. Inputs are one successful MXN 1,000 online card payment and, in the second example, a three-month MSI payment. The fee calculation assumes Clip applies the published percentage to the MXN 1,000 transaction amount; the public fee pages do not spell out whether a merchant’s separately charged buyer tax changes that base. IVA shown here is 16% of Clip’s fee components. Examples exclude catalog-specific SAT tax withholdings, account-specific rates, issuer fees, and reader hardware.

- Regular card sale: 3.6% × MXN 1,000 = MXN 36.00 Clip fee; 16% IVA on the fee = MXN 5.76; total fee = **MXN 41.76**, leaving MXN 958.24 before other deductions.
- Three-month MSI sale: (3.6% Clip fee + 4.57% bank surcharge) × MXN 1,000 = MXN 81.70 before IVA; IVA = MXN 13.07; total fee and surcharge = **MXN 94.77**, leaving MXN 905.23 before other deductions. Clip’s calculator rounds the total rate including IVA to 9.5%.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Recurring seller eligibility:** Clip actively markets automated subscriptions and has dedicated recurring-payment terms, but the general service terms list recurring-payment practices among excluded activities. The public pages do not say which subscription businesses are accepted.
- **Refund timing:** the general terms say merchant refunds may be requested within 90 days; the help article gives 60 days for Visa/Mastercard/other and 180 days for American Express. Checkout pages also say refunds through the app are same-day only. The interface and card-brand difference is not explained.
- **Payment limits:** Pagos a Distancia has clear lower limits: MXN 10,000 per transaction, MXN 50,000 daily and 20 links daily. Checkout pages say new accounts start at MXN 5,000 and some accounts can reach MXN 200,000. The plugin help page has an older MXN 20,000 figure after KYC. General terms state a MXN 99,999 single-payment cap, which conflicts with the current MXN 200,000 Checkout FAQ.
- **Testing:** the developer homepage advertises sandbox/test data, but the FAQ says Checkout Transparente and subscription APIs have no sandbox or test cards. It does not identify the product scope of the newer sandbox claim.
- **Payout estimate:** PAD terms say next business day; plugin help says 24 hours including weekends and holidays. The terms permit Clip to change payout frequency with notice and allow bank delays/risk holds.
- **Currencies:** standard Mexican terms say MXN; an older redirected-Checkout doc says MXN or USD. The current v2 API schema does not give a complete supported-currency list, so current USD acceptance is not confirmed.
- **Taxes:** Clip says catalog sales by individual sellers can have SAT withholdings, but the current product page does not give rates. A detailed rate table found in an older catalog blog post is not treated as current. No tax filing service was found for general Checkout transactions.
- **Ownership:** contracting entities and management are public, but a current controlling owner or cap table was not found. Investors are not treated as owners without an official ownership statement.
