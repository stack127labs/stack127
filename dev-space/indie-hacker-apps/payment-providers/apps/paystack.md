## Ownership, shutdown checks and current restrictions

Stripe acquired Paystack in October 2020. On 20 January 2026, Paystack announced The Stack Group as a new parent and a proposed ownership structure still pending regulatory approvals. The announcement is not evidence that those approvals have since completed. Sources: https://paystack.com/blog/news-updates/paystack-joining-stripe; https://paystack.com/blog/news-updates/10-years-of-paystack-and-the-beginning-of-the-stack-group (checked 2026-11-05).

No provider-wide shutdown or sunset notice was found after checking the official blog, News & updates section and developer changelog, plus official-site searches for shutdown, sunset and discontinuation. These checks establish what was found, not a promise about future service. Sources: https://paystack.com/blog; https://paystack.com/blog/category/news-updates; https://paystack.com/docs/changelog/api/ (checked 2026-11-05).

**Current method outage:** Nigeria Direct Debit is listed as a major outage, investigating, on the status page checked during this research. The incident was posted 5 November 2026 at 14:56 UTC and affects both existing linked-account charges and new bank links. Source: https://status.paystack.com/ (checked 2026-11-05, rechecked 19:17:54 UTC).

**Market access:** Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya and South Africa are established signup markets. Côte d’Ivoire has operating services but unclear general access beyond the private-beta wording in help. Egypt explicitly remains a private-beta application; Rwanda's page asks for interest ahead of launch. Sources: https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2130562; https://paystack.com/ci/; https://paystack.com/egypt/; https://paystack.com/rwanda/ (checked 2026-11-05).

Separate consumer/banking brands and physical Terminal hardware are outside scope. Egyptian beta prices are recorded without presenting Egypt as generally available.
