- **Argentina credit or prepaid, ARS 100,000:** ARS 100,000 × 2.6% = **ARS 2,600 fee before IVA**. Our calculation from the 2.6% + IVA rate.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The current pricing page calls its large-business plan Enterprise; the FAQ still says Advanced. I used Enterprise as the current name.
- The service-cost terms do not state whether the Essential subscription rate replaces or stacks with the base payment-method fee.
- The FAQ says merchants maintain a separate commercial relationship with an invoice issuer; an older help article says Mobbex can set up Facturante without an existing account. Confirm the account-specific arrangement.
- The current terms and pricing establish an Argentine seller setup. The FAQ's phrase “Argentina and the region” does not list countries where foreign merchants may sign up.
- Enterprise minimum billing is shown as $70,000,000 per month without a currency code. I retained the symbol as published.
- The status page timed out when checked. Current plans, account signup and September 2026 product updates show an active offer, but live component uptime was not verified.
- Exact international-card surcharges, QR/DEBIN/cash method prices, FX charges, payout fees, minimum payout, per-transaction limits, reserve schedule, post-closure retention and PCI certificate scope were not found in the pages listed in the relevant rows.
