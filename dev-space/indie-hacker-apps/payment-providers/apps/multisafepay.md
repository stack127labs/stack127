## Conflicts and unknowns

- **General pricing vs Visa page:** the Dutch tariff page requires a personal quote and says no setup/monthly charges. A separate Visa page advertises €0.20 + 1.5%. It is treated as a distinct published Visa offer, not a rate for every seller or card method.
- **Ownership vs product name:** MultiSafepay became wholly owned by Ant International and integrates with Antom, but its public agreement and merchant pages remain MultiSafepay. No public rename or shutdown notice found. Alipay+ is a payment method, not a new product name.
- **Merchant country whitelist:** EEA support is stated, but public signup pages do not supply an exhaustive seller-country list. EU business bank and legal business checks are explicit.
- **Method pricing:** public pages publish no per-method numerical fee schedule for local bank, wallet, prepaid or BNPL methods. Those fees can vary and need a merchant quote. The rows group methods by the fact that public rates were not published; they do not claim the methods share one contracted fee.
- **VAT:** Dutch terms say stated prices exclude BTW. Invoice docs explain VAT depends on whether MultiSafepay collects the method payment; non-collecting methods can show zero VAT because VAT is handled via the card scheme. No single VAT-inclusive total applies to every method and merchant country.
- **POS:** documented POS products include SmartPOS, traditional C-TAP and Android Tap to Pay; Tap to Pay remains in pilot. Hardware purchase/rental prices are not public and are separate from online transaction rates.
- **No universal reserve or chargeback amount:** terms permit risk-specific deposits and fee pass-throughs. The partner payout fee is €0.50 per payout, but it should not be applied to ordinary merchant accounts.

## Field problems

- F03 is the EEA service region, not a verified whitelist for every merchant country. The specific signup list is not public.
- F04 uses company-registration and business-account requirements; the public pages do not settle every sole-proprietor or unregistered individual case.
- F11 charge and payout currencies differ. Keep the method-specific matrix separate from the smaller payout list.
- F23 method prices are quote-based; grouped rows describe where no public number was found, not an identical fee.
- F29 and F30 separate the merchant’s tax obligations from VAT on MultiSafepay fees and any invoice feature.
- F40 has a contractual release deadline for security deposits after termination, but no fixed duration for risk holds while an account is live.
- F41 names the Dutch provider and its regulator; group ownership is separate from the entity handling the merchant contract.
