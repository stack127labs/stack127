## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Seller market and MENA wording:** The home page markets Moyasar in Saudi Arabia and MENA, but the current FAQ says service focuses on Saudi Arabia. The merchant terms describe requests to accept payments from foreign-issued cards; that is a buyer-country approval flow, not evidence that merchants outside Saudi Arabia can sign up. Saudi Arabia is the only confirmed standard seller market.
- **Direct versus aggregation/platform payouts:** Direct merchant terms say payment is transferred within seven working days after the balance reaches SAR 100. The aggregation guide says twice weekly on Monday and Thursday; platform terms specify Sunday, Tuesday, and Thursday. Each page names a different account/contract scope, so I recorded them separately.
- **Google Pay:** The developer overview lists Google Pay for web, while the detailed payment-form method list does not include it in the same selectable-method list. I could not find a separate setup and live-eligibility page, so the exact route remains unclear.
- **Pricing:** English and Arabic FAQs say sales provides prices based on business needs. The public terms confirm agreed service fees, VAT on those fees, and a possible accelerated-payout fee, but do not publish the ordinary transaction schedule.
- **Company-news dates:** The blog listing dates the Major EMI announcement October 25, 2026; the article body uses an October 26 dateline. Both are after the research start date and confirm the current license update.
- **Termination notice wording:** The merchant terms say Arabic controls if the language versions differ. The English merchant termination clause and its Arabic wording do not make the normal notice procedure fully clear; I marked it unclear rather than choosing one interpretation.
- **Newsroom check:** The official `/resources/newsroom/` page contains featured third-party media coverage. I also reviewed the company's blog archive, About page, current product pages, and the October 2026 license announcement. No shutdown, sunset, acquisition, or rebrand notice appeared.
- **Pages checked for missing onboarding and tax details:** English and Arabic FAQ, merchant terms, platform terms, privacy policy, current product pages, invoice docs, and the developer overview. The support helpdesk returned a 502, so it was not used as proof of a negative fact.
