## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Seller location and bank eligibility:** [Current country page](https://fungies.io/global-availability/) has 121 entries: our count is 105 standard, 11 Preview and five EUR-only. [Availability help](https://help.fungies.io/getting-paid/global-availability.md) and [payout guide](https://help.fungies.io/getting-paid/payouts.md) contain older/different currency and minimum tables. The [verification guide](https://help.fungies.io/workspace-verification.md) and [Seller Terms](https://help.fungies.io/legal/seller-terms-of-use) do not publish a full owner-residency, beneficial-owner percentage, ID-format or country/entity document matrix. Countries not listed are not guessed into either allowed or prohibited status.

- **Standard rate versus private conditions:** [Pricing](https://fungies.io/pricing/), [Platform Fees](https://help.fungies.io/for-game-developers/platform-fees.md) and [FAQ](https://help.fungies.io/other/faq.md) say 5% + $0.50 with processing included and no standard international, subscription, method, dispute or provider-payout surcharge. [Main Terms](https://fungies.io/terms-conditions/) and Seller Terms still allow disclosed account fees, partner costs and liability deductions. The main SaaS part also contains a $300 overdue-account reinstatement clause. The exact applicable order form was not accessed.

- **Fee base and non-USD amount:** [Tax-inclusive Pricing](https://help.fungies.io/tax-inclusive-pricing.md) and its [USD order screenshot](https://help.fungies.io/images/content/d1jeysasorso1cjk8oyd-image.png) show USD 2.99 subtotal, USD 0.69 tax, USD 0.65 fee and USD 2.34 income. Our arithmetic matches 5% of the pre-tax subtotal plus USD 0.50, rounded to cents. These examples do not supply a universal contractual base, tax-on-provider-fee rule, non-USD fixed-fee convention or fee-cap matrix. Pricing and both public seller contracts were also checked.

- **Methods, currencies and renewal scope:** [Customer Payment Methods](https://help.fungies.io/getting-paid/customer-payment-methods.md), [method overview](https://fungies.io/payment-methods/), [hosted method guide](https://help.fungies.io/for-saas-developers/hosted-checkout-more-payment-methods.md) and [current developer method guide](https://docs.fungies.io/guides/payment-methods.md) were read. Country/currency/order/activation conditions matter, and subscriptions use separate invoice settings. Link is unavailable for subscription checkouts. The [OpenAPI schema](https://docs.fungies.io/api-reference/openapi.json) enforces one catalog currency per workspace; its 150-code enum is not a verified all-method availability table.

- **Usage and proration:** [Usage tutorial](https://help.fungies.io/tutorials/saas-developers-with-pay-as-you-go-products-usage-based.md) says one item per extra charge, but [current charge reference](https://docs.fungies.io/api-reference/subscriptions/charge-subscription), [Subscription resource](https://docs.fungies.io/core-resources/subscription.md) and schema permit up to 20. The supplied unitPrice must exceed 100 minor units in the current schema even though its description says minimum 100. [Upgrade guide](https://help.fungies.io/upgrading-or-downgrading-plans-with-api.md) describes automatic proration while the [API schema](https://docs.fungies.io/api-reference/subscriptions/update-subscription) defaults to none; that is an API configuration distinction, not a promise that every call prorates. Metering, credit balance and consumption remain the app's work.

- **Refund cost:** [Refund API](https://docs.fungies.io/api-reference/orders/refund-order-payment) adjusts the platform fee after refunding an eligible Stripe order, without publishing the exact returned components. Seller/main terms default to non-refundable fees unless agreed/required otherwise. The [refund help](https://help.fungies.io/for-end-users/refunds-and-chargebacks.md), FAQ, pricing and [buyer terms](https://fungies.io/buyer-terms-of-service/) do not resolve a complete retained-fee/refund-action tariff or exact bank timing. No zero-cost refund is assumed.

- **Disputes:** Public pricing says no per-dispute fee; legal terms allocate processor/assessment costs and any disclosed administration fee to the seller except Fungies-caused losses. Applicability and returned-on-win treatment need account-specific clarification. Main Terms give five Business Days for evidence; Help Center Seller Terms give seven, in each case shortened by the communicated deadline. Neither public version is silently discarded.

- **Payout rails, currencies and floors:** Payout guide gives initial 7-to-14-day scheduling, country exceptions, manual/instant estimates and possible alternative-currency fees. [PayPal Settings](https://help.fungies.io/getting-paid/customer-payment-methods/paypal-settings.md) separately says Wednesday weekly payouts. The current standard payments pages say daily bank payouts, so one route must not be imposed on every method. The linked original tables remain the source for other countries; no single reconciled worldwide bank minimum is claimed.

- **FX and reserves:** The seller contracts permit provider spreads/costs at conversion, separate refund/dispute rate exposure, risk reserves and security holds. Pricing/payout docs do not supply a numeric complete FX/instant/alternative-currency route tariff or a universal reserve percentage/duration. Daily dispatch is not a promise of immediate cleared funds or a maximum closure hold.

- **Domain and testing limits:** [Current custom-domain guide](https://docs.fungies.io/developers/custom-domain/overview.md) allows supported DNS providers/flattening; [older root-domain guide](https://help.fungies.io/store-settings/set-up-custom-domain/migrating-your-domain-to-cloudflare.md) says Cloudflare is mandatory from 14 October 2024. [WooCommerce guide](https://help.fungies.io/integrations/woocommerce.md) still mentions an older staging host; its product-sync limits and staging instructions are not assumed to apply universally to the current platform.

- **Export and migration:** [CSV reports](https://help.fungies.io/getting-paid/transactions-reports.md) and [dashboard page](https://fungies.io/platform/merchant-dashboard/) establish business/transaction exports. Seller contracts and [DPA](https://fungies.io/data-processing-addendum/) do not establish outbound card/token/mandate portability. [Contact](https://fungies.io/contact-us/) offers migration discussions, not a public saved-subscription transfer route, guaranteed timeline or universal free price.

- **Closure and retention:** Seller Terms state ordinary 30-day written notice, while the main SaaS clauses have billing-cycle and Business Days variants. Order-form scope matters. Final proceeds may remain subject to liabilities/reserves; a fixed final-release or export-access deadline is not given. - **Crypto offer:** [Dedicated crypto help](https://help.fungies.io/getting-paid/customer-payment-methods/crypto-and-stablecoins.md) lists eight eligible merchant countries but no token/network matrix. None of these, the buyer terms, refund API or webhook docs supplies exact chain confirmations, wrong-network/underpayment/expiry handling, on-chain custody, conversion locks, crypto refund rules or network fees. Generic market discussion and linked third-party crypto services are not used as Fungies proof.

- **Support and assurance:** Contact, Help Center, API/docs and both legal forms were checked. They give email/Discord and qualified service-level wording, not a universal support-hours/language/response or uptime SLA. No separate live status page or current independent PCI/licence attestation was established from these pages. [October 2026 changes](https://help.fungies.io/other/product-changelog.md) support current activity, while the 2027 platform/API v1 section explicitly describes future work.

## Field problems

- F02/F18 must separate the legal MoR role from the required connected payout account. Stripe onboarding does not make the seller the legal MoR, and a method-specific PayPal account must not be applied to every checkout.

- F03/F05/F10 must keep general seller eligibility, Preview/EUR-only payout conditions, crypto merchant eligibility, owner location and buyer restrictions separate. The full 121-location seller list is grouped by its actual conditions.

- F11 needs verified payment-method/currency evidence. A schema enum, selected display currency or sample checkout is not a worldwide live acceptance matrix.

- F15/F35 must distinguish the catalog's representable minor-unit price from a gateway payment minimum and from the extra-charge endpoint's stricter unitPrice rule. The one-item tutorial and 20-item current schema genuinely disagree.

- F20/F26/F27 need the public standard pricing and legal/account exceptions alongside each other. A worked fee example supports its calculation, not every private agreement; 'adjusted fee' is insufficient to invent a refund-return amount.

- F31 to F34 combine payout rails, first-payout delay, settlement, dispatch, arrival and bank minimums. The two country tables disagree; this record keeps clear examples and material conflicts rather than inventing a fully reconciled bank-minimum dataset.

- F37/F40 need transaction export, saved-payment portability, cancellation, final settlement and processor/controller data retention kept separate. A CSV export or deletion right does not guarantee payment credentials can move.

- F41 records provider security statements separately from independent certification/licence proof. F43 separates approved third-party payouts and Aflink creator payments from a general native split-payment product.

- C01 to C05 apply because Fungies advertises crypto checkout. Token names without network/contract pairs are incomplete; accepting crypto does not prove crypto payouts or seller custody.

- Marketing number counters can render as zero before loading. Static pricing text, actual docs and dated current changes were used; the explicitly future 2027 platform is not treated as live.
