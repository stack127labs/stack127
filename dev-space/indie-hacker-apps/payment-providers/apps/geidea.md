## Cost examples

Our calculations for a Saudi online merchant under the published Card-not-Present schedule dated 17 August 2025. These use one transaction and exclude monthly/setup charges unless shown.

- Credit card sale of SAR 1,000: (SAR 1,000 × 2.5%) + SAR 1 transaction fee = SAR 26 before VAT. VAT calculation: SAR 26 × 15% = SAR 3.90. Total processing cost = SAR 29.90. This is our calculation from the published rate; the merchant agreement controls the actual offer.
- Mada sale of SAR 1,000: SAR 1,000 × 1% = SAR 10 before VAT. SAR 10 × 15% VAT = SAR 1.50. Total processing cost = SAR 11.50. This is our calculation.
- Monthly gateway charge: SAR 99 + 15% VAT (SAR 14.85) = SAR 113.85. One-time setup: SAR 499 + 15% VAT (SAR 74.85) = SAR 573.85. This is our calculation; no annualization or bank fee is added.
