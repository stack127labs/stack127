## Field problems

- **Variable pricing:** F20 needs a margin plus actual wholesale/network cost. An average effective rate or Canadian illustrative card table is not a universal price.
- **Country/currency scope:** US USD, Canadian CAD and Canadian configured USD card processing need separate treatment. Seller location, bank location, buyer location and card issuer country are different eligibility questions.
- **Billing versus approval:** a recurring-payment feature does not override restricted-business review or mean subscription collection is free. Card retries, ACH retries, recurring invoices and Recurring Payments plans have different behavior.
- **Fee Saver responsibility:** the pass-on fee is paid by the buyer only in eligible flows. Its refund terms can put that cost back on the merchant; a zero seller-card headline is not a general zero-cost plan.
- **Tax wording:** adding a configured tax rate is different from keeping rates current, deciding obligations, validating tax IDs and filing/remitting returns.
- **Limits and funding:** a payment-page setting, approved account limit, bank-fee cap, Faster Deposits batch threshold and minimum payout are distinct values.
- **Moving providers:** exported tokens/customer CSV files are not portable full card data. Migration needs the documented secure process and destination-provider compliance.
- **Crypto applicability:** no native crypto offer was established in the method docs. C01 to C05 are assessed as not applicable to this product, rather than assigning unsupported crypto features.
- **Evidence states:** a confirmed row applies only to its scoped statement. Private/account-specific terms remain unknown; cost rows and retrieval notes are not additional fact rows.
