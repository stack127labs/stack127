# Wave Payments

Acquisition and service notices (checked 2026-11-05): H&R Block completed its acquisition of Wave on 2019-07-01. No whole-product shutdown or sunset notice was found in the official product, pricing, Help Center and status pages reviewed. Wave remains active for US and Canadian businesses. Wave announced its regional focus in November 2020 and discontinued product/customer support outside those countries; older foreign bookkeeping accounts do not establish payments eligibility. A new online-payments experience is being rolled out: notified merchants must supply updated details within 45 days to avoid a payment-processing pause. Instant payouts are currently unavailable in that new experience.
