## Scope and evidence

The apps.md row points to Taiwan. This record covers its online gateway, payment-facilitator collection, Link Pay, Shopify and relevant add-ons, with published offline differences separated. It also records material differences for the separate Japan offer. Generic HKD/MYR acquirer routes do not establish open seller signup in Hong Kong/Malaysia.

Traditional Chinese pricing, application/refund/account FAQs, terms/privacy, API guides and the published merchant/Shopify manuals were read. Japan's own pricing, terms, onboarding and privacy pages were checked. The Taiwan FAQ initially renders collapsed headings; its actual answers were recovered from its public static page module, [faqdata](https://www.tappaysdk.com/_nuxt/777bc92.js), and are cited alongside the [faq](https://www.tappaysdk.com/taiwan-zhtw/help/faq/payment-facilitator) page. No account, login, payment or form submission was used.

All facts were checked on 2026-11-05. Taiwan terms/privacy say updated 30 March 2026; Japan terms say last revised 3 January 2023 and its privacy policy is established 1 October 2022. The published Portal manual is version 1.0.9, March 2024; the Shopify manual includes a 3 July 2024 revision. Older manual statements are not silently substituted for current tariff changes.

Taiwan quoted prices are in TWD and exclude 5% business tax unless a specific source says otherwise. Withdrawal tax treatment is separately unresolved. Japan price pages contain mixed-country blocks; no Taiwan tax or TWD fixed fee is assumed to apply to Japan.
