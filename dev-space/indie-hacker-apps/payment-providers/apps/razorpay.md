## Cost examples: our calculation

Checked 2026-11-04. These are illustrative fee-only calculations, not a complete payout quote. Assume an Indian merchant, INR 1,000 successfully captured on an ordinary domestic card, outside the 90-day welcome offer, no custom contract, no currency conversion, no instant settlement, no refund or dispute, and no other checkout add-ons. INR 1,000 is the total payment charged, including any sales tax the merchant chose to include. Merchant sales taxes are not computed here.

- Processing input: 2% of captured payment, https://razorpay.com/pricing/ (checked 2026-11-04).
- Fee-tax input: 18% GST on platform fee, https://razorpay.com/solutions/e-commerce/ (checked 2026-11-04).
- One-time formula: INR 1,000 × 0.02 = INR 20 processing; INR 20 × 0.18 = INR 3.60 fee GST; total INR 23.60; remaining after those fees INR 976.40. Effective fee for these components: 2.36%. No rounding needed.
- Subscription input: current advertised 0.5% add-on per subscription payment plus base platform fee and GST, https://razorpay.com/subscriptions/ (checked 2026-11-04; limited-time offer with no visible expiry).
- Subscription formula: INR 1,000 × (0.02 + 0.005) = INR 25; INR 25 × 0.18 = INR 4.50 GST; total INR 29.50; remaining after these components INR 970.50. Effective fee for these components: 2.95%. This does not establish that the chosen card/mandate is eligible or that the offer lasts throughout a subscription.
- International total: not calculated. Exact card qualification, FX spread, payout terms and possible reserves remain unresolved. An “up to 3%” headline cannot supply a complete comparable net amount.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Settlement timing: https://razorpay.com/pricing/ says typically T+1 or Instant; https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/settlements/faqs says domestic T+2 working days from capture and international T+7 working days. Use approved account schedule. These are different from a guaranteed first payout or bank-arrival promise.
- Settlement currency: the settlement FAQ and subscription FAQ say INR universally; https://razorpay.com/blog/what-is-an-eefc-account/ and https://razorpay.com/newsroom/?p=4741 describe the newer five-currency EEFC exception. This is a separately activated offer, so preserve standard-versus-EEFC scope rather than replacing INR with a universal multi-currency claim.
- Currency counts: international docs say 160+, https://razorpay.com/accept-international-payments/ says 135, and https://razorpay.com/solutions/saas/ says 92. Full currency list did not appear in the fetched docs. No complete code list or universal count is asserted.
- Seller geography: https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/international-payments/faqs answers a question about companies outside India with “Yes” followed by “businesses registered in India.” That wording is ambiguous. https://razorpay.com/accept-international-payments-from-india/ clearly describes overseas settlement without an Indian entity, but no complete permitted seller-country list was found. - Pricing: https://razorpay.com/pricing/ and https://razorpay.com/subscriptions/ have limited introductory offers without full exposed eligibility/expiry terms. The former also separates zero UPI MDR from a technology fee. RuPay zero MDR is not treated as proof of no platform fee.
