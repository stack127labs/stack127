## Acquisition, sunset and migration notices checked first

- **DOKU acquisition: 2022.** senangPay's own history calls it a DOKU company following that acquisition. The Malaysian product is now branded senangPay-DOKU, while its agreement identifies SimplePay Gateway Sdn. Bhd. as the operator. DOKU is the parent/shared platform; this record does not reuse the Indonesian DOKU offer's fees, registration countries or limits. <https://guide.senangpay.com/about-senangpay>; <https://guide.senangpay.com/about-doku>; <https://senangpay.com/terms-of-agreement/>
- **Active service; the earlier legacy-platform migration is complete, while the announced DOKU Malaysia platform launch is still pending.** The official sunset page says senangPay successfully migrated to senangPay-DOKU. Existing payment links redirect to the new checkout; current Malaysian signup/pricing remain public. No whole-product shutdown notice was found in these official pages. <https://guide.senangpay.com/sunsetted-products-and-packages>; <https://senangpay.com/doku-migration-faq/>; <https://senangpay.com/>
- **Retired/unavailable offerings matter:** Stripe Advanced/Multicurrency, Advance Plus, various old packages, senangTap, pay2phone and the old terminal product are sunsetted. ShopBack Pay was discontinued via senangPay on **17 March 2026**; Google Pay is unavailable until further notice. Current pricing/migration notices also limit JCB, Boost and tokenisation. Do not treat old tables or future rollout as active functionality. <https://guide.senangpay.com/sunsetted-products-and-packages>; <https://senangpay.com/pricing/>; <https://senangpay.com/doku-migration-faq/>
- **Important unresolved conditions:** current guide versus agreement settlement timing, Atome availability/rate and expanded refund rollout disagree. The legal page also contains old contract/package material after the numbered agreement. These remain labelled below. <https://guide.senangpay.com/senangpay-doku-settlement-schedule>; <https://senangpay.com/terms-of-agreement/>; <https://guide.senangpay.com/atome-bnpl>; <https://guide.senangpay.com/how-to-request-a-refund-for-senangpay-doku-merchants>
- **Closure risk:** numbered agreement section 14.6 says pending payments are forfeited to senangPay as liquidated damages on provider termination. This severe published clause is retained; no guaranteed routine final-funds release is assumed. <https://senangpay.com/terms-of-agreement/>

Scope: the Malaysian online senangPay-DOKU Starter, Advance and quoted Enterprise offer, with optional Malaysian BNPL/IPP and related Catalog/FlexiBill features. Offline 2C2P softPOS and retired Stripe/terminal packages are kept outside the online rate baseline. “Confirmed” means a claim is directly supported by the linked official source, not that a private account, regulator register or payment was tested. KYB means business verification; SSM is the Malaysian business registry, ROS the societies registry, MDR the processing rate, and IPP bank instalments.
