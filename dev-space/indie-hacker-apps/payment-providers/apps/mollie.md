## Conflicts and unknowns

- Seller versus buyer availability: https://help.mollie.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002116105-Can-I-use-Mollie-s-services-in-my-country supplies seller-base countries. Complete buyer/card-country exclusion matrix was not found in restrictions, agreement or method/currency docs. A supported currency is not proof a country’s cards are permitted.
- Registration is required, including for sole traders. A sole-trader personal-bank exception is not unregistered-person eligibility. Full legal-form and owner-residence matrices remain unknown after checking creation and bank-account support.
- Pricing scope: https://www.mollie.com/en/pricing and https://www.mollie.com/nl/pricing expose different product/country context. Only the explicit Netherlands online schedule supplies price examples. In-person Pro/monthly fees and optional business bank subscription were not carried into core online pricing.
- Fixed-cost exception: minimum monthly invoices apply to some higher-risk industries. The public article’s EUR 50 is an example; actual account minimum is communicated individually. Missing inactivity-specific pricing is unknown, not zero.
- Multi-currency scope: https://help.mollie.com/hc/en-us/articles/5546871220370-What-are-multi-currency-payouts lists 13 currencies and 12 additional balances with volume-based eligibility. Netherlands pricing FAQ still says up to seven extra currencies. Preserve this count/version mismatch; do not promise multi-currency payout access for every new seller.
- Settlement delays: current en-us pending-balance help says iDEAL next business day and SEPA debit 9 business days; https://help.mollie.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115002540294-How-long-does-it-take-to-process-a-payment says card 5 business days and SEPA debit 10 business days. Account/product/locale differences were not resolved. Payout scheduling and bank arrival are separate waits. Revenue Day and Mollie Business Account have special rules and eligibility.
- Payout pricing: current en-us payout help states some daily/manual payout charges, while the en-gb article https://help.mollie.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115000010065-When-do-I-receive-my-money-from-Mollie says all frequencies free. Current Netherlands pricing and en-us article support the recorded paid exceptions. Keep market/locale scope and check the actual account plan.
- Disputes: the new Dispute Portal page explicitly scopes its fee handling to beta customers and gives Klarna-specific tariffs. The EUR 25 Klarna rate is not the universal card chargeback fee. Generic card fees and every returned-on-win exception were not established. SEPA debit EUR 10 has its own official source.

- Additional costs: searched Netherlands pricing, recurring guide, recurring support, currency-conversion help, invoicing and agreement. Numeric FX fee, universal recurring/billing surcharge, optional fraud/tax add-on tariff and exact closure fee were not established. Missing prices are not zero.

## Field problems

- F03/F05 need company base, owner residence and bank-country rules separately. IBAN possession alone is insufficient.
- F08 bundles distinct sales types; a broad ecommerce/SaaS claim cannot establish permission for each one.
- F14 should distinguish the method used to set up consent from the method used for later automatic debits.
- F15/F24 should distinguish a gateway API, RCUR and paid third-party billing integrations. They have different features and economics.
- F19 needs account/industry minimum-invoice exceptions, not only a monthly plan price.
- F20/F21 require seller market and EEA/card-class tiers. A foreign EEA card does not necessarily get the non-EEA rate.
- F26/F27 need method/product scope. PayPal refunds and Klarna beta disputes cannot define all-method charges.
- F28/F32 need primary versus additional balance and volume eligibility. Same-currency payout can still have a fee.
- F33 needs pending-to-available, schedule and bank arrival separately, including locale/version conflicts.
- F41 combines contracting entity, licensing and security audit scope. A provider licence statement does not establish a dated certification.
- F44 cannot provide an account-specific reserve using generic public terms; unknown is expected.
