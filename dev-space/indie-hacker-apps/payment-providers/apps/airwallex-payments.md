Terms used below: a wallet is the Airwallex balance holding settled funds. Like-for-like means a payment settles in the same currency. A reserve is money kept back temporarily for risk. Capture collects an authorised payment. MCC means merchant category code. Merchant of Record (MoR) means the legal seller to the buyer.

## Cost examples — our calculations

There is no sales tax, currency conversion, Billing, Tax add-on, dispute, refund, extra gateway attempt, custom URL or outbound bank transfer. EEA domestic means a consumer card; Australia uses the new cohort. Country and card-origin rates replace one another.

Formula: `processing fee = payment amount × percentage / 100 + fixed gateway fee`. Effective fee: `fee / payment amount × 100`. These are calculations from public standard prices, not account quotes. Australia prices include GST; UK/EEA prices exclude VAT, and SG/HK/US schedules exclude applicable taxes as stated. Unknown tax on the provider's fee is not added.

### Monthly plan effects — our calculations

- **US, 500 domestic USD 10 sales:** `500 × (10 × 0.028 + 0.30) = USD 290` on USD 5,000 gross, or `290 / 5,000 × 100 = 5.80%`. Explore monthly fee is USD 0. Inputs: [US fee schedule](https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/terms/fee-schedule), [US pricing](https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/pricing); checked 2026-11-05.
- **UK, ten domestic GBP 100 sales, no other deposits and end balance below GBP 10,000:** `10 × (100 × 0.013 + 0.20) + 19 = GBP 34` on GBP 1,000 gross, or 3.40%, before any applicable VAT on fees. At 100 such sales, GBP 10,000 processing volume meets the deposit test: `100 × 1.50 + 0 = GBP 150`, or 1.50%. Assumes the published waiver applies to that billing cycle; excludes first-month promotions. Inputs: [UK pricing](https://www.airwallex.com/en-uk/pricing), [UK fee schedule](https://www.airwallex.com/en-uk/terms/fee-schedule); checked 2026-11-05.
- **Australia, ten domestic AUD 100 sales, no other qualifying deposits and end Wallet/Yield balance below AUD 10,000:** `10 × (100 × 0.0165 + 0.30) + 29 = AUD 48.50` on AUD 1,000 gross, or 4.85%. At 50 such sales, AUD 5,000 processing volume meets the deposit test: `50 × 1.95 + 0 = AUD 97.50`, or 1.95%. Excludes trial promotions and assumes the published cycle waiver applies. Inputs: [AU pricing](https://www.airwallex.com/en-au/pricing), [AU fee schedule](https://www.airwallex.com/en-au/terms/fee-schedule); checked 2026-11-05.

### Billing, FX and reversals — our calculations

- **New-cohort US USD 100 subscription:** `100 × 0.028 + 0.30 + 100 × 0.005 = USD 3.60`, or 3.60%, before optional Tax. With Automatic Tax Calculation on that same zero-sales-tax transaction: `3.60 + 100 × 0.004 = USD 4.00`, or 4.00%. No second 0.50% usage-product charge is assumed. Inputs: [US fee schedule](https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/terms/fee-schedule), [US pricing](https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/pricing); checked 2026-11-05.
- **UK merchant, USD 100 international Visa/Mastercard charge settled like-for-like to USD:** method percentage `100 × 0.0315 = USD 3.15`; foreign-currency settlement `100 × 0.01 = USD 1.00`; gateway GBP 0.20. Published components total **USD 4.15 plus GBP 0.20**, before fee-currency conversion or applicable taxes/network charges. A single-currency total is **unknown** without the actual fee FX rate. Do not also add the 2% acceptance-conversion fee when this transaction settles like-for-like. A later wallet conversion is a separate event only if performed. Inputs: [UK fee schedule](https://www.airwallex.com/en-uk/terms/fee-schedule), [FX documentation](https://www.airwallex.com/docs/payments/payment-operations/reporting/fx-in-payments), [settlement-versus-conversion rules](https://help.airwallex.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4407760528793-What-are-foreign-currency-fees-on-my-Airwallex-Online-Payments); checked 2026-11-05.
- **US refund of a USD 100 sale:** the new request fee is USD 0.30 plus any network charges, and cross-currency cases can add refund-date FX. A complete original-sale-plus-refund net cost is **unknown** because the reviewed sources do not establish a blanket return/retention rule for original processing/Billing/Tax fees. Inputs: [US fee schedule](https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/terms/fee-schedule), [refund documentation](https://www.airwallex.com/docs/payments/payment-operations/manage-payments/refunds); checked 2026-11-05.
- **AI-assisted US dispute recovery:** recovered USD 100 gives `min(100 × 25%, 200) = USD 25` success fee; recovered USD 1,000 gives `min(1,000 × 25%, 200) = USD 200`. These are separate from the initial USD 15 dispute fee and any network charges. No refund of those initial fees is assumed. Input: [US fee schedule](https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/terms/fee-schedule); checked 2026-11-05.

Local-method totals outside the explicit WeChat cases cannot be calculated from the retrieved tables. Neither sales tax nor an unknown payment-method component is silently treated as zero.
