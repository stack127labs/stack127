- No official shutdown notice or general current fee-change notice was found in the Shopify product, help, changelog and newsroom pages searched. Product changes found are listed at the top: 15-country European launch, local-method expansion, USDC launch, Meta Pay retirement, and Fall '26 methods.

## Field problems

- F10 is inherently method-specific: Shopify publishes seller availability and method-specific buyer countries, but not a universal buyer-country list covering all cards and all issuers.
- F20–F23 vary by region, plan, card classification, currency and negotiated Plus contract. This file records the US plan rates and several representative regional schedules; one rate should not be copied to every supported country.
- The checkout price selector exposes recurring plan/country differences. Capturing every local-method price in all 40 seller countries would exceed the comparison scope; check the exact market page and Shopify Admin before signup.
- F30 invoices can depend on jurisdiction and app. Shopify order paperwork is not itself proof of a compliant tax invoice in every country.
- C02: the public USDC documentation explains wallet claiming and the payment terms identify processors, but they do not fully state custody for an unclaimed USDC balance.
