## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Current pricing and older pricing:** use the current 2026 pricing page for plan fees, limits and included payouts. The 2025-01-01 commercial PDF has older Startup/Comfort/Supreme values and says its Starter ceilings are monthly. Those older figures are not carried into the current offer. Current pricing does not state the Starter threshold period.
- **First-month offer:** the developer Fees page says all transactions are 0% in the first month, then a plan must be selected. The current pricing page describes a free Starter threshold but does not explain whether the first-month offer is additional, included or still active. The interaction is unclear.
- **Hold timing:** current plan pricing lists 72 hours for Comfort, 48 hours for Supreme and 24 hours for Elite. The product terms dated 2024-07-01 say the last transactions are held for 48 hours generally. The current plan table is used for tier values; actual withdrawal and bank-arrival timing remains unpublished.
- **Seller geography:** specific merchant terms limit sellers to Algerian nationality/residency or Algerian-law entities/contracts. A registration selector lists the United States as “coming soon.” The company describes wider international expansion as planned for 2027; this does not establish current seller eligibility outside Algeria.
- **Payment methods and currency:** terms say DZD transactions and receipts; current methods are CIB and EDAHABIA. Visa and Mastercard are marked coming soon. The API balance endpoint lists DZD, EUR and USD wallets, but the docs do not reconcile those wallets with this DZD merchant offer or describe conversion and payout.
- **Legal operator:** Chargily Pay terms identify CHARGILY EURL as the E-Supplier; the Algerian pricing footer says Chargily Algeria EURL, while the global footer says Chargily Inc. The relationship is not explained in the reviewed material. The official company page reports the 2019 change from Nessplus Telecom to Chargily; no acquisition was identified.
- **Privacy / legal routes:** the specific merchant terms page is available at `/business/pay/term`. The footer-linked `/business/privacy`, `/business/term`, `/dz/business/privacy`, `/dz/business/term` and `/business/cookie` routes and the `/company/legal` notice route returned 404 or a cache-miss error. No accessible Chargily Pay privacy policy or general merchant closure policy was found.
- **Pages searched for unpublished fees or limits:** current English/French/Arabic pricing pages, merchant terms, merchant help pages, product and method pages, registration, API docs index, checkout create/object docs, balance docs and support pages. The public sources do not settle international-card pricing, FX, charge minimum/maximum, payout method, payout currency, minimum payout, refund treatment of original fees, recurring billing or payout bank-arrival SLA.
- **Blog and newsroom check:** the French company newsroom was reviewed through its latest February 2026 post and older entries. No shutdown, sunset or acquisition notice was found. `https://chargily.com/blog` and `https://chargily.com/company/blog?lang=fr` could not be accessed/found; no separate blog archive was identified in the reviewed company navigation.
