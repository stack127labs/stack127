## Shutdown, sunset, acquisition and ownership notices

- Active online offer. No provider-wide shutdown or online-product sunset found after checking the [blog](https://www.yoco.com/za/blog/), the [company page's Press and media section](https://www.yoco.com/za/about/), product pages and targeted official-site searches. The [status page](https://status.yoco.com/) reported fully operational, including online payments, payouts and developer services. This is a point-in-time observation.
- [Yoco acquired Dyner.ai on 28 May 2026](https://www.yoco.com/za/blog/dyner-joins-yoco/). Dyner is a restaurant operating-system business; this is not an announcement that Yoco was acquired or its gateway retired.
- [Current main terms](https://a.storyblok.com/f/111633/x/5a9f3cd9a5/yoco-main-t-cs-oct-2026.pdf) name Yoco Technologies Proprietary Limited as operator. The [company history](https://www.yoco.com/za/about/) names Series C investors, including Dragoneer; it does not establish current controlling shareholding.
- New online-only merchants normally pay **R199 including VAT before review**. Main terms clause 6.4 exempts active/onboarding accounts before **2 November 2026**, qualifying device purchase/registration and expressly exempt channels. Paying does not guarantee approval. See the [current online signup guide](https://support.yoco.help/en/articles/884286-how-to-sign-up-with-yoco-online-only) and contract exceptions.
- **Gateway does not support automatic subscription/recurring billing**, according to the [official integration guide](https://support.yoco.help/en/articles/109553-how-to-integrate-yoco-with-your-online-store). Buyer BNPL instalments and repeated invoices are different features.

## Scope

Online Gateway/Checkout API, Links, Page and Invoices, for a South African seller. Core, Plus and Pro costs are separated; local card tiers use the rolling three-month sales average. Online/in-person volume can combine for tier eligibility, but card-machine rates are not used as online rates.

Hardware is optional. The single hardware example below is kept separate and excluded from online cost calculations. There is no assumed purchase of a card machine, POS bundle or mobile payment SDK. Transaction fees exclude VAT; merchant-plan monthly prices and the R199 signup charge include VAT. No currency conversion is performed in this research.

The [October 2026 main terms](https://a.storyblok.com/f/111633/x/5a9f3cd9a5/yoco-main-t-cs-oct-2026.pdf), [September 2026 payment terms](https://a.storyblok.com/f/111633/x/523c4cd452/yoco-payment-services-t-cs-september-2026-docx.pdf) and current detailed [plan/fee help](https://support.yoco.help/en/articles/270145-all-about-yoco-plans-and-fees) supply current contractual and tariff context. The prohibited-business PDF's filename says September 2026 but its body footer says January 2025; its currently linked content is recorded without inventing a newer effective date.

## Conflicts, resolved differences and unknowns

1. **Online versus in-person fees:** the [pricing page](https://www.yoco.com/za/pricing/) highlights in-person debit rates. The [detailed fee table](https://support.yoco.help/en/articles/270145-all-about-yoco-plans-and-fees) explicitly separates online prices and fixed R2 charges. Online rates in this record come from that table, not from a generic headline or a guessed 0.5 percentage-point uplift.
2. **Setup fees:** current online page, signup guide and main terms acknowledge the new charge. Generic free-tools/no-monthly-fee wording does not waive a new applicant's R199 review fee. Exemptions, non-approval risk and duplicate/incorrect-charge refunds are preserved.
3. **BNPL total remains unclear:** [Payflex](https://support.yoco.help/en/articles/716444-adding-payflex-to-your-online-store) and [Happy Pay](https://support.yoco.help/en/articles/743086-adding-happy-pay-to-your-online-store) say their fees are charged over and above Yoco online payment fees. The plan matrix lists 5.25% + R5 but does not explicitly identify the additional base category for every BNPL sale. The named tariff is confirmed; a universal all-in total is not.
4. **Nightly Instant availability remains conflicting:** the dedicated Instant article gives 23:00–00:00 queuing. The Fast comparison lists 23:30–00:15 unavailability. Both show only a relative update date, so there is no clear basis to declare one newer. Both windows are retained; no claim of uninterrupted instant banking is made.
5. **Payout minimums:** the general guide says R10; the paid-plan guide says balance exceeds R50 for the first monthly payout after subscription deduction; Instant requests require R50. These scopes are kept separate. The general guide also supplies first-payout estimates, so first timing is not marked unpublished.
6. **Refund product differences:** general App/API/link refunds do not explicitly remove the Invoice FAQ's limitation. The general refund page combines in-person/online guidance, so bank/debit timing caveats are preserved without imposing a universal online refund guarantee.
7. **Support:** the current dedicated help page distinguishes daily human service from after-hours AI support. Generic 24-hour service marketing is not treated as 24-hour human staffing.
