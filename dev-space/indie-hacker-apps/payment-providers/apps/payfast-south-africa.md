## Owner, retirement checks and current restrictions

**Current owner:** Payfast is the South African ecommerce product of Network International. Payfast's 7 May 2024 rebrand announcement says Network acquired it in 2021; the product and merchant integrations continue. Network's 1 October 2025 announcement places the merged Network/Magnati group under a Brookfield-led consortium, operating as Network International LLC. The South African merchant contract still names PayFast (Pty) Ltd, registration 1999/017441/07. Sources: https://payfast.io/blog/payfast-is-now-network/; https://www.network.ae/en/about-us/press-and-media/news/network-international-and-magnati-merge-to-create-the-leading-fintech-across-the-middle-east-africa; https://payfast.io/company-information/; https://payfast.io/legal/general-terms-conditions/ (checked 2026-11-05).

**Retired methods:** Bitcoin payments ended at midnight on 20 July 2019. Manual Instant EFT for Standard Bank ended 18 May 2020; manual EFT for other banks and direct cash deposits ended 30 June 2020. These notices do not retire automated Instant EFT or current SCode/Mukuru cash networks.
