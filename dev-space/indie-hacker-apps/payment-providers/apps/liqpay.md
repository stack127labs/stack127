# LiqPay

- **Official URL:** https://www.liqpay.ua/
- **apps.md status:** active (row last checked 2026-11-04)
- **Current status / owner:** Active online acquiring product of PrivatBank. PrivatBank is 100% owned by the state of Ukraine, represented by the Cabinet of Ministers. No LiqPay acquisition, shutdown, or product sunset notice was found in the official LiqPay blog and PrivatBank news archive checked.
- **Research start (UTC):** 2026-11-06 23:55:41
- **Research finish (UTC):** 2026-11-07 00:13:24
- **Minutes taken:** 17.72
- **Pricing page selector check:** The current tariff page has no seller-country, currency, or billing-period selector. It shows a Standard tariff and an Individual tariff by application; the visible language switch is not a price switch.
- **Pricing scope:** Ukraine-registered FOPs (sole proprietors) and legal entities. Current LiqPay tariff: 1.3% on Ukrainian cards and 2% on foreign cards. The seller’s settlement is in UAH. VAT inclusion is not stated.
- **Research scope:** Public official LiqPay, PrivatBank, Ministry of Finance and National Bank material only. No account was created and no forms were submitted.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Current card fee:** LiqPay’s dedicated tariff page and merchant pricing block show 1.3% for Ukrainian cards and 2% for foreign cards. A lower FAQ block on the same PrivatBank acquiring page and a bank PDF still state 1.5% for all cards. A PRRO product page also displays 1.5% and 1% figures. I used the current dedicated LiqPay tariff as the controlling public price and treated the older/general copy as stale. Sources: `https://www.liqpay.ua/tariffs`, `https://privatbank.ua/business/business-connect-liqpay`, `https://privatbank.ua/get/table/59/pdf`, `https://privatbank.ua/business/handbook/prro_liqpay`.
- **PrivatBank account:** Current onboarding says a settlement IBAN at any bank works and the activation FAQ says a seller without a PrivatBank business account can sign the LiqPay agreement without opening one. Current formal LiqPay terms say the payment requisites must match the client’s current account at the Bank. I marked this conflict instead of treating the newer setup instructions as overriding the formal terms. Sources: `https://www.liqpay.ua/information/instructions/registration`, `https://www.liqpay.ua/information/handbook/activation`, `https://conditions-and-rules.privatbank.ua/view-content-516/`.
- **Buyer-country list:** The FAQ links a restricted-country list, but its endpoint returned HTTP 405. The FAQ and separate payer page were checked; the email-domain list is not a substitute for the full country list.
- **Installment seller cost:** The FAQ gives a fee that varies by installment count plus acquiring but does not publish the full current schedule. The Instant Installment method has no separate merchant service fee, but still has acquiring cost. Buyer monthly financing rates are listed separately.
- **Taxes:** The current acquiring fee pages do not say whether processing fees include VAT. PRRO can fiscalize eligible sales but does not make LiqPay the seller or tax filer.
- **FX:** LiqPay documents UAH/EUR/USD charge currencies and UAH settlement, but the public pages checked do not specify the FX rate basis or markup.
