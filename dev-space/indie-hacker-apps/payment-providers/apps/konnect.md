## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Monthly charge:** the current pricing page says no subscription or monthly fee. The undated terms say a payment account has a flat monthly contribution but do not give its amount. No source establishes which contract text is newer, so F19 is `conflicting`.
- **Payout method/timing:** current site FAQ says transfers are automatic and points to dashboard-specific timing. The terms describe merchant-ordered transfers to a registered bank account, executed by the end of the next provider business day. They do not give one general settlement cycle, so F33 is `conflicting`.
- **Approval timing:** the FAQ says account activation generally takes minutes; integration docs give 1 to 48 hours for setup and activation. These are estimates and may describe different stages; no guaranteed SLA was found.
- **Cards:** current pricing names Visa and Mastercard, while terms also define cards by CB network. A separate current CB acceptance statement was not found.
- **FX and settlement:** API lists TND, EUR and USD and says non-TND payments are automatically converted. The FX rate source/markup, conversion fee, balance currency and payout currency were not published. Reviewed API currency and response docs, pricing, FAQ and terms.
- **Eligibility and limits:** public seller-country, owner-residency, bank-country, charge-minimum/maximum and collection-limit tables were not found. Reviewed homepage, pricing, terms, onboarding docs, API initiation and no-code payment-link docs. Foreign-card acceptance is buyer reach, not proof of foreign seller eligibility.
- **Refund/dispute costs:** terms cover card reversal and refund routes, but no public refund fee, original-fee return rule, chargeback fee or seller evidence deadline was found. Reviewed terms, pricing and dashboard transaction guide.
- **Licensing:** Konnect calls itself a BCT-certified facilitator. The BCT facilitator framework is public, and OIF links a facilitator list, but its embedded PDF could not be fetched here. No Konnect-specific certificate number was verified. This does not establish lack of approval.
- **Product scope:** “all sectors” is broad marketing. No public prohibited-industry schedule or product-by-product acceptance matrix was found. Reviewed terms, privacy and product pages.
- **Newsroom:** official blog and press-coverage page were searched for shutdown, sunset, acquisition and rebrand notices. The only material change notice found was the visual identity refresh. The status board reported all listed systems operational.
