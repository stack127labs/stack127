**Current status:** Quickpay is operating under its own name as Quickpay ApS, part of Unzer since 2021. Unzer announced the acquisition in January 2021 and confirmed completion on 30 September 2021. Quickpay’s newsroom and blog were checked for acquisition, merger, shutdown, or retirement news; no Quickpay shutdown or service retirement notice was found. Its official status page showed all systems operational when checked. The legal terms page still says it took effect on 1 June 2016; see the notes below.

**Scope:** Quickpay is a payment gateway. The merchant needs a separate acquirer or payment-method agreement; acquirer and method-provider prices, settlement, and approval are separate from Quickpay’s gateway charges. The public regional price pages have four region choices (Denmark, Europe/EUR, Norway, Sweden). Their visible prices are monthly and per transaction; no billing-period switch or annual price was shown. The four pages state prices exclude VAT/moms.

## Cost example

**Our calculation, Denmark:** one DKK 100 card payment using the public starting card rate, with no optional methods or add-ons.

- Quickpay transaction charge: DKK 0.25.
- Acquirer charge: 1.35% × DKK 100 = DKK 1.35; the stated DKK 0.75 minimum does not bind; add DKK 0.22 fixed.
- Total variable charge: DKK 0.25 + DKK 1.35 + DKK 0.22 = **DKK 1.82**, before VAT.
- The DKK 99 monthly Merchant plan fee is separate. This is our calculation from the public listed prices; the actual acquirer rate may be higher or otherwise differ under the merchant’s agreement.

Sources: https://quickpay.net/dk/prices-dk/ (checked 2026-11-07).
