## Field problems

- F03/F05 must distinguish company location, the country that issued each owner's/director's ID, residence and bank location. “Global” cannot serve as a seller eligibility value.
- F20/F21 use “domestic” and “international,” but Dodo's public rates use US buyer/card location. Merchant incorporation alone does not select the US price. India’s “15c” notation cannot be normalized without its fee currency.
- F02/F18/F29 need an offer boundary: Dodo MoR and BYOP have different legal sellers, accounts, taxes, disputes and payout paths. BYOP is separated only where a field's value changes.
- F22/F26/F32/F33/F34 need separate checkout FX, settlement-wallet conversion, bank conversion, refund mode, payout initiation and bank arrival. Linked-wallet thresholds use face-value currencies rather than the default combined USD test.
- F19 and F40 cover two different inactivity rules: the MSA's possible three-month dormancy charge and the dashboard's 180-day archive prerequisite. Neither is a fixed reserve-release period.
- C01–C05 apply because Dodo accepts stablecoins even though sellers receive fiat USD. No seller crypto-wallet custody or self-hosted node requirement is inferred.
