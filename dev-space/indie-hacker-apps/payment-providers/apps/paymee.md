## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Current status:** apps.md says `active`. Paymee's site currently advertises Business products and pricing, while its 14 February 2024 official statement said its operations were paralyzed and full resumption awaited case closure and release of frozen funds. The public blog/press archive has no later resolution. Current processing is therefore unclear, not confirmed active or formally shut down.
- **Separate undated technical notice:** Paymee says payment processing had slowed after a spike in payment requests and would return to normal within five days. The page has no visible publication date, so it cannot resolve the later/current status question or be aligned confidently with the frozen-funds notice.
- **Regulatory status:** Paymee's 2024 statement describes the BCT inspection as finding it acted as a facilitator, rather than as a payment institution. BCT Note 2024-05 sets general bank-partnership, seller KYC, fund segregation, reporting and <=48-hour leveling guidelines for facilitator arrangements. It does not confirm Paymee's current bank partner, individual licence status or current funds access. The OIF's official facilitator-list PDF could not be fetched, so its list entry was not verified.
- **Commercial terms:** no public merchant agreement, seller restricted-business list, merchant refund/dispute rules, payout schedule, reserve policy, termination/fund-release clause, tax allocation, or bank-account eligibility table was found. The official privacy policy displays 2021/2022 date text and is not a substitute for merchant terms.
- **Pricing detail:** the published 1.9% and 2.9% tariff has no stated effective date, explicit fee base, fixed fee, tax-on-fee treatment, international-rate stacking rule, conversion rate/cost, refund retention or payout cost. We kept the domestic and international rates separate and did not infer currency from the separate consumer-wallet FAQ.
- **Pages searched for unpublished business terms:** `Tarifs`, `Passerelle de paiement`, `Paylink`, `Checkout Page`, the three developer guides, `Créer un compte Paymee Business`, `Qui sommes-nous ?`, `Politique de confidentialité`, `FAQ`, `Contact`, and the official blog/press categories. Official-domain searches for merchant “conditions générales”, “CGU”, refund terms and restricted-business terms did not surface a public merchant agreement.
- **Newsroom check:** Paymee's official blog, Blog page 2, “Communiqué de Press” category, both French and Arabic 2024 crisis statements, and the technical notice were checked. No separate newsroom archive or later shutdown/acquisition/resumption post was found.
