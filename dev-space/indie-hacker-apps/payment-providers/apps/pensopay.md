**Current status:** Pensopay is active under PENSOPAY A/S and is part of Visma. Visma became owner in 2022. The current Danish news page and official company pages were checked for acquisition, merger, shutdown, and product retirement notices. No Pensopay shutdown or product-sunset notice was found. Pensopay’s 9 August 2022 Shopify article explains an earlier Quickpay link: Shopify had required Pensopay to use Quickpay’s competing Shopify app; Pensopay then launched its own app so Shopify payments no longer passed through a third-party provider. A current help article still says WooCommerce subscription payments use Quickpay’s gateway as the technical processor. These are separate connections.

**Scope:** Pensopay is a gateway plus an onboarding route to partner acquiring agreements. Clearhaus is its primary acquirer; Clearhaus or another contracted acquirer moves the card funds and sets the acquiring agreement. Pensopay does not itself act as the merchant’s acquiring bank. The gateway plans and acquirer charges stack, but the public pricing page and acquiring fee guide describe the same 1.25% standard consumer-card rate; do not count that percentage twice.

**Pricing-page switches checked:** The Danish page at /priser/ displays DKK; the English page at /en/pricing/ displays EUR. They are separate locale pages, not a currency selector. Both offer monthly subscription or pay-as-you-go, allow plan changes month to month, and show no annual billing switch. Their plan figures differ by page; they are recorded as shown and not converted. The pages do not say whether the Pensopay plan amounts include VAT. Pensopay’s help article says acquiring-bank fees are typically VAT-exempt, which does not establish the VAT treatment of Pensopay’s own subscription or transaction charges.

## Field problems

- The label “gateway plus acquiring” can imply Pensopay is the acquiring bank. It is not: Pensopay arranges a partner acquirer, usually Clearhaus; the acquiring contract and settlement are handled by that acquirer.
- Pricing-page rates use simplified wording (“1.25% for acquiring”), while the acquirer fee guide adds distinctions for business cards and foreign cards. The example uses one consumer card rate only.
- EUR and DKK prices are shown on separate localized pages without a clear country-to-price mapping. Plan prices are retained in their displayed currencies.
- Several third-party method fees differ between Pensopay’s top-level plan page and the more detailed method catalogue. Material conflicts are retained in F23 instead of choosing a rate without evidence.
- The general terms page is dated 2015 and may not represent the current contract. Its clauses are quoted with that age caveat.
