# PayU India

Shutdown, sunset and acquisition check: No whole-provider shutdown notice was found in the current product, pricing, onboarding, policy and company sources reviewed. PayU remains a Prosus payments business. The proposed BillDesk acquisition did **not** complete: Prosus says the conditions were not met by 30 September 2022 and the agreement automatically terminated in October 2022. ## Cost examples — our calculation

Checked: 2026-11-05. Inputs: an Indian merchant on the public standard service-fee model; customer transaction amount as shown; processing charged at 2% for ordinary domestic Visa/Mastercard or 3% for the named premium/EMI/international class; 18% GST on the gateway fee. No GST input-credit recovery is deducted. These examples calculate the published processing component, not a complete quote for optional services, refunds, disputes, settlement upgrades, conversion or product sales taxes. Sources: https://payu.in/pricing/; https://payu.in/gst-calculator/.

Formula: processing fee = customer transaction amount × processing rate. GST on fee = processing fee × 18%. Total processing deduction = processing fee + GST on fee. Amount after that deduction = transaction amount − total processing deduction.

| Scenario | Inputs | Formula | Result — our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small domestic Visa/Mastercard sale | ₹100 customer charge; 2% processing; 18% GST on fee | ₹100 × 0.02 = ₹2.00; ₹2.00 × 0.18 = ₹0.36 | ₹2.36 processing deduction; ₹97.64 after this deduction; effective processing cost 2.36%. |
| Domestic Visa/Mastercard sale | ₹1,000 customer charge; 2% processing; 18% GST on fee | ₹1,000 × 0.02 = ₹20.00; ₹20.00 × 0.18 = ₹3.60 | ₹23.60 processing deduction; ₹976.40 after this deduction; effective processing cost 2.36%. |
| Amex/Diners/EMI or international-card processing component | ₹1,000 customer charge; 3% processing; 18% GST on fee | ₹1,000 × 0.03 = ₹30.00; ₹30.00 × 0.18 = ₹5.40 | ₹35.40 processing deduction; ₹964.60 after this deduction; effective processing cost 3.54%. International FX, if applicable, remains additional/unknown. |
