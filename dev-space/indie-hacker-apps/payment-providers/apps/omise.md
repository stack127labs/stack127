## Service and name-change notices

**Omise returned from the Opn/Opn Payments brand on 18 March 2025.** The [official rebrand notice](https://www.omise.co/en/post/omise-unveils-bold-rebranding-and-ai-powered-innovations) says service continues uninterrupted. No provider-wide shutdown or sunset notice was found in official company, policy and service-notice searches. [Current status](https://status.omise.co/) remains available with operational components and partner maintenance notices. Checked 2026-11-05.

**Acquisition scope:** the [15 November 2022 acquisition announcement](https://www.omise.co/en/post/opn-enters-us-market-with-acquisition-of-merchante-en) says Opn acquired MerchantE, which would retain its own US brand. This record covers the documented Thailand, Singapore, Malaysia and Japan Omise gateway, rather than treating US group operations as eligibility for the same account. Sources: [live-account requirements](https://docs.omise.co/how-do-i-enable-live-account) and [settlement-country rules](https://docs.omise.co/currency-and-amount), checked 2026-11-05.

**Integration notices:** Omise publishes a [library/certificate compatibility notice](https://docs.omise.co/library-update) and an [October 2026 PHP security notice](https://docs.omise.co/php-sec-update-oct-2026). Old certificate-pinned libraries can fail after certificate renewal; the PHP notice applies to merchant-hosted PHP systems and distinguishes those from hosted/Shopify flows. These are integration conditions, not a gateway shutdown. Checked 2026-11-05.
