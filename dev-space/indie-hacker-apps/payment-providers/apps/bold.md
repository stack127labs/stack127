# Bold

- **Official URL:** https://bold.co/
- **apps.md status:** active
- **Product scope:** Bold is a Colombia-founded payment provider with separate Colombia and Peru seller offers. This record focuses on online links, buttons and API payments. Card-reader hardware prices are in a separate section and are not included in online processing fees.
- **Current status and company news:** Active. Bold's official press room has 2026 company releases and its Academia has 2026 posts. I checked both and found no Bold shutdown, sunset or acquisition notice. Bold Perú's tariff page says merchants who migrated from VendeMás keep the pricing arranged with their account executive; the public pages describe a migration to the Bold brand, not a completed corporate acquisition.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Bold is currently a two-country branded offer, with separate Colombian and Peruvian contracts and payment rules. The Peru legal agreement's entity RUC (20601443651) differs from the footer on the current tariff page (20602370497); I left the legal identity unresolved.
- The Peru tariff page documents migration from VendeMás and says existing merchants keep their individually agreed rates. The checked official press room and Academia did not publish an acquisition notice, so this file records a migration/brand transition, not a corporate acquisition.
- Colombia's tariff selector displays monthly bands up to COP 10 million and COP 10–20 million, but the checked public extraction only exposed the first band. The COP 10–20 million online card values are check_failed; do not treat first-band rates as universal.
- Colombia's API product page mentions recurring automation, while its API FAQ says merchants need an external system to manage/tokenize payer details and call the real-time API. This file records API-based recurring charges as conditional and does not treat Bold as a subscription manager.
- Colombia's online page presents three settlement-speed/bank options with different processing fees. They are kept as separate transaction-pricing options, not collapsed into one rate. Official developer guides disagree about Shopify plugin status.
- Colombia's anti-fraud guarantee is conditional and has an individualized monthly cap. The link guide's COP 10 million headline does not replace the account-specific limit in the agreement.
- Public prices are before Colombia IVA/withholdings and Peru IGV. Foreign card rates and merchant-specific risk/volume limits may differ from the standard public schedule.
- Colombia's general help page gives a higher COP 30 million sales range than the link-specific COP 10 million maximum; they have different channel scope, so only the lower link cap is applied to link payments.
