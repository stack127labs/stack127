## Cost examples

**Our calculation — successful domestic online card payment, EGP 1,000.00**

- Inputs: EGP 1,000.00 sale; published card rate 2.75% + EGP 3.00; VAT 14% on the Kashier fee only.
- Formula: (EGP 1,000 × 2.75%) + EGP 3 = EGP 30.50 processing fee; EGP 30.50 × 14% = EGP 4.27 fee VAT; total Kashier fee and VAT = EGP 34.77.
- Result: estimated net before other account-specific adjustments = EGP 965.23.
- Sources: [Kashier pricing](https://www.kashier.io/pricing).

**Our calculation — successful domestic online card payment, EGP 100.00**

- Inputs: EGP 100.00 sale; published card rate 2.75% + EGP 3.00; VAT 14% on the Kashier fee only.
- Formula: (EGP 100 × 2.75%) + EGP 3 = EGP 5.75; EGP 5.75 × 14% = EGP 0.805; total fee and VAT = EGP 6.555.
- Result: estimated net before other account-specific adjustments = EGP 93.445.
- Sources: [Kashier pricing](https://www.kashier.io/pricing).

The examples cover online card acceptance only. POS hardware and in-person processing, wallet/kiosk rates, installments, international-card pricing, payout pricing and enterprise terms are separate or quoted by sales.

- **Apple Pay price:** Its developer guide says Apple Pay settles over the card rail, while the current pricing page lists Apple Pay among wallet methods with sales-quoted rates. The public pages do not resolve the merchant fee; recorded as unclear.
- **Activation timing:** the current Arabic homepage says 24–48 hours for eligible merchants; the contact page says most accounts go live next working day. These are estimates, not promises.
- **Refunded original fee:** pricing says refunded transactions are not charged, but the refund documentation and merchant terms do not say whether fees already deducted on the original transaction are returned.

- **Unpublished payout timing and limits:** I searched the merchant agreement, payout and settlement docs, transaction session API, InstaPay docs and current pricing. Individual payout schedules are in the merchant dashboard; universal minimum payout and general transaction caps are not published.

## Field problems

- Country onboarding is not published as a country matrix. Egypt is the only explicitly named merchant market; the terms' general reference to where a business is organized does not prove foreign onboarding is open.
- Kashier's documentation contains both online collection and merchant-to-recipient payout APIs. The file treats settlement to the merchant separately from those outbound transfer tools.
- The pricing page treats Apple Pay as a sales-priced wallet method while the developer guide says it uses the card rail; a direct sales answer is needed to establish its merchant rate.
- VAT arithmetic can produce fractional piastres, as in the EGP 100 example. The example preserves the exact calculation and does not assume Kashier's rounding rule.
- No fee or country distinctions outside the Egypt-facing offer were supported by official public material, so those values remain unknown rather than being inferred from worldwide buyer marketing.
