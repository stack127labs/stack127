Scope: UK online payment gateway, hosted checkout, payment links and virtual terminal. Card-terminal rental and transaction pricing from in-person terminal bundles are excluded. takepayments sells the gateway service and introduces merchants to a separate acquiring bank; it is not a merchant of record. Its site also offers a direct Worldpay Ecommerce route, which has a separate Worldpay contract and is not combined with takepayments pricing here.

Current owner and status: takepayments Limited is an active UK company and part of Global Payments. Companies House identifies Global Payments Acquisition UK Limited as holding 75% or more of shares and voting rights, with the right to appoint or remove directors. Global Payments completed its acquisition of Worldpay on 9 January 2026; this is a group transaction, not a takepayments shutdown or rebrand. The current takepayments blog and product pages remain active; no closure/sunset notice was found. Its older company name was Payzone UK Limited (changed in 2019). No private people's names are included.

## Field problems

- F18–F28: takepayments is an introducer/service layer for acquiring; each merchant's acquiring bank can set separate rates and rules. Do not treat a terminal quote or one acquirer's schedule as an online gateway standard.
- F19/F29: “No set-up fee” is a broad company claim, but online and acquiring fees are individually agreed and VAT treatment is not public.
- F33: Next-working-day settlement claim has an explicit caveat for ecommerce pages, links and virtual terminal; obtain the channel-specific schedule.
- F40: The online service contract and acquirer/terminal agreements are separate. The former has a 12-month minimum and 90-day notice; any acquiring exit charge depends on the signed terms.
- F41: FCA authorization on the company site is not evidence that every gateway feature is FCA-regulated; the FCA page limits stated scope to consumer hire for specified terminal rentals.
