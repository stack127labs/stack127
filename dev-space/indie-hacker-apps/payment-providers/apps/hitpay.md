## Service notices and scope

**Card migration:** merchants outside Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines were required to move to their own Stripe Standard connection by **5 June 2026**. The notice warns of interrupted card acceptance if migration is incomplete. The current guide prices that route as Stripe's regional fees plus a 0.5% HitPay application fee. Source: [Standard Connect migration and pricing](https://docs.hitpayapp.com/connections/stripe/standard-connect), checked 2026-11-05.

No provider-wide shutdown, sunset or acquisition/rename notice was found in official searches and change notices. HitPay remains active; [status](https://status.hitpayapp.com/) showed services online, last updated 2026-11-05 at 12:10 UTC. [Company/licence information](https://hitpayapp.com/sg/license-registrations) identifies Mobiedge as a wholly-owned HitPay Malaysia subsidiary; no acquisition date was established. The [change log](https://docs.hitpayapp.com/changelog) has current releases, including UPI (INR) on 30 October 2026. All checked 2026-11-05.

This record covers HitPay payment acceptance and billing. Main published price markets are Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines. Other-country Stripe-connected card accounts, cross-border local methods, specialized USD banking and supplier remittance have different terms. Seller signup lists conflict; see F03. Stripe Managed Payments is a separate product and is not treated as this connected gateway route.

## Field problems and index notes
