**Regulatory and operating status:** Coinremitter's public merchant site, current API docs and blog were live on 2026-11-07. The MAS Investor Alert List includes **CoinRemitter** from 2026-10-09 and shows the same Singapore address and website named by Coinremitter's cookie policy. MAS describes the list as covering entities that may be wrongly perceived as licensed or authorised, or related investment offers; its CoinRemitter entry does not state a specific allegation, licence finding, penalty or enforcement action. I found no company response to the listing. Coinremitter's terms separately say its services are unavailable to people and businesses in Singapore, although its privacy and cookie policies identify the operator as Coinremitter Pte Ltd., Singapore. The older API documentation was scheduled to stop working after 2025-06-30; the company directs users to its current API docs at api.coinremitter.com. I found no acquisition or service-shutdown notice in the company blog or product pages checked.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The current fee page and FAQ say processing and transaction charges apply when funds are withdrawn; the terms say the 0.23% processing fee is deducted from each transaction before settlement. Treat the exact point of deduction as unresolved until a merchant confirms it in its account or contract.
- BTC minimum incoming payment is 0.00001 BTC on the general fee table/current supported-currency API, but the BTC-specific gateway page says 0.0001 BTC. This is material because the page says under-minimum payments are not credited.
- The general fee chart pairs fixed fees with Gas Station options for BNB, ETH, USDT ERC20, USDT TRC20 and USDC ERC20. Current coin-specific pages clarify the paired values; see F28. The USDT ERC20 page has a coin-label typo, recorded in that row note.
- Coinremitter's FAQ says no ID is needed at signup, while terms/privacy say business and beneficial-owner information is collected and verification/KYC documents may be requested. The distinction between initial registration and later review is not fully specified.
- The seller-country claim says “over 130 countries” without naming them. Singapore is expressly excluded. No other full seller or buyer country list was found in the official availability, terms, FAQ or API pages checked.
- MAS lists CoinRemitter on its Investor Alert List from 2026-10-09. The public entry does not state the reason or a specific enforcement action. I checked the MAS Investor Alert List and Financial Institutions Directory, plus Coinremitter's terms, risk warning, privacy/cookie pages and announcement blog; no licence number, penalty notice or company response was located.
- No separate minimum withdrawal amount, fiat payout, reserve duration, marketplace split flow, bulk export/migration tool or account approval estimate was published in the fees, API, terms, FAQ and support pages listed above.
- Coinremitter states that the merchant receives crypto in an internal wallet and later withdraws it. This differs from marketing wording that suggests coins go directly to the merchant's own wallet; the terms and wallet docs establish the internal-wallet stage.

## Field problems

- **F28/F35:** Coin-specific pages clarify the Gas Station withdrawal-fee pairs. F35 is about payment/account limits; the table publishes minimum customer payments, not minimum withdrawals. These have been kept separate. The BTC and USDT TRC20 minimums conflict across current product pages.
- **F03/F06:** Marketing says broad country support and no signup KYC; terms impose a Singapore exclusion and permit later verification. A complete country list and precise verification stage are not published.
- **F41:** The MAS alert is an official regulator record but does not spell out a specific finding for this entry. It is recorded as an alert, not described as proof of an enforcement action or licence decision.
- **C01:** TCN is a testnet coin, so it is listed for integration coverage but not counted as a normal merchant payout asset.
