- Product researched: Revolut Business Merchant account acceptance: online gateway/checkout, hosted payment links and invoices, Revolut Pay, and in-person hardware where available. The regular Revolut Business account and its plan fees are separate from merchant transaction fees.
- apps.md status: active
- Acquisition, licence and shutdown check: Revolut's newsroom and blog are active in 2026. The company announced a French banking licence in August 2026 and a conditional US national-bank charter approval in September 2026; the French Business account migration is planned, while merchant eligibility and terms still identify Revolut Bank UAB for the current EEA service. New Zealand Merchant terms say service remains in testing and invite-only. I found no official shutdown or acquisition of this merchant-payment product. Revolut's announced acquisition of Banco Cetelem in Argentina concerns a separate banking expansion.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The New Zealand Merchant agreement says the product is invite-only testing, even though some localized eligibility pages list New Zealand among supported countries. I treated the local agreement as the actual access limit.
- The Australia eligibility article lists Iceland, while the Singapore version omits Iceland and New Zealand. The NZ terms separately explain its invitation status; the Iceland discrepancy is unresolved.
- The official Merchant fee page says the Business account plan is separate. This file records merchant transaction charges and terminal costs separately from banking plan prices.
- Revolut’s France all-plans fee page is already published but explicitly takes effect on 2 December 2026. I did not use its future SEPA Direct Debit or Revolut Pay Business/Pro rates as current prices.
- Revolut's French bank licence and future customer migration are official, but the current French payment-processing terms on the checked date still name Revolut Bank UAB and its French branch. The replacement agreement effective 30 November 2026 was not yet in force on the research date.
- The United States has a US-incorporated Business banking offer and conditional bank-charter news, but the Merchant eligibility list excludes the US and no US merchant fee table/entity was found. No US price was inferred.
- Public pages do not state whether online processing fees include VAT/GST in every market. UK terms explicitly say applicable taxes are excluded; hardware tables separately show VAT/GST.
- Original card-processing fee retention on refund, exact country-wide bank payout corridors, saved-card portability and category-by-category digital product approval were not settled by the public pages checked.
- Official newsroom and blog pages were checked for acquisitions, licence changes and shutdown notices. Revolut's acquisition of Banco Cetelem in Argentina is a separate banking expansion; no Revolut Business Merchant shutdown notice was found.

## Field problems

- Eligibility articles are localized and have a small Iceland/New Zealand list difference. New Zealand's own terms are clearer: invitation-only testing.
- The US Business account and bank-licence news do not establish access to the Merchant acquiring product. The US is absent from the published Merchant signup list.
