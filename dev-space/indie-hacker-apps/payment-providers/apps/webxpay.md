## Cost examples

Examples calculate only the published processing percentage. Subscription/activation fees are additional. Fee tax, any unpublished fixed/minimum charges, refund/dispute costs and bank deductions are unknown, so the remainder is not guaranteed all-in proceeds. Rs. is kept as printed in the Sri Lankan pricing source.
