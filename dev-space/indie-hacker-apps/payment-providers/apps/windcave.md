## Field problems

- F03–F06 need a distinction between direct acquiring, bank-connected gateway service and method-specific buyer countries. Office locations and legal definitions do not prove open signup.
- F19–F28 cannot yield a dependable numeric online cost without the merchant offer. A historical MSF average excludes the certainty and account scope needed for a signup comparison.
