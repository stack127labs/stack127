## Conflicts and unknowns

- The public monthly fee bands are based on cumulative invoice volume; separate compliance tiers set daily and annual account limits. These are different thresholds.
- The pricing table has no visible switch and does not disclose its exact conversion rule for non-USD invoices. Its FAQs mention the question but do not expose an answer in the accessible page. The exact conversion formula and high-risk surcharge remain not published.
- The current settlement-options page (2026-10-07) allows one fiat currency plus up to five crypto currencies. The June application guide says one fiat plus one crypto; the newer settlement page is used.
- The 2026-10-29 supported-asset table and 2026-10-31 payment-method article include newer assets and networks than the API invoice reference. The exact API support for every new token/network pairing needs confirmation.
- The status page’s component list says BitPay.com and Payments have a major outage while the API invoice and settlements are operational. The visible incident list only identifies sandbox maintenance; the page does not settle merchant-checkout impact.
- The public US agreement requires a non-refundable application fee but does not publish the amount. Pricing and public help pages were checked for a number.
- Sole-trader eligibility is not clear from the agreement, application instructions, or compliance page. No universal legal-form list was found.
- BitPay’s press page and company blog were checked for acquisition, licence/regulatory, and shutdown news. The material current notice found was the 2026-07-16 MiCA authorisation for BitPay B.V.; no acquisition or shutdown notice was found on those pages.

## Field problems

- F04 does not say whether sole traders can apply in each currently open country. The public terms require a duly organized entity, but do not settle every local legal form.
- F08 does not provide an allow/deny table for SaaS, downloads, ebooks, courses, and memberships. These remain subject to business review and the prohibited-business policy.
- F11’s pricing currency list points to a live exchange-rate list rather than a fixed currency table. Currencies can change; no values were converted.
- F17 and C01 have an old API reference beside newer support pages. The newer support pages describe current checkout assets, but not every token-to-network mapping in the API.
- F39 status reporting has conflicting component labels on one official page; it is not safe to infer a live production checkout outage from the aggregate label alone.
