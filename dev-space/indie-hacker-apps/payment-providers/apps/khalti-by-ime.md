## Cost examples: our calculation

No complete numerical merchant-processing quote can be calculated from the public sources. Merchant terms specify a one-time API fee and a percentage on successful transactions but publish neither amount. Gateway FAQs say prices are personalized. The annual fee is disputed between the merchant terms and FAQ. Sources: https://khalti.com/info/terms/merchant/; https://khalti.com/payment-gateway/; https://blog.khalti.com/khalti-payment-gateway/ (checked 2026-11-05).

Let A be the contracted one-time API/setup amount, r the merchant processing percentage, Y any applicable annual maintenance amount, and f any additional fixed processing component. A, r, Y and f are not all publicly established. A missing fixed component is not assumed to be zero.

| Scenario | Inputs | Formula: our calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One NPR 1,000 merchant sale | Registered/approved Nepal merchant; quoted percentage r and any fixed component f unknown | Processing = 1,000 × r / 100 + f | Numerical merchant processing fee and net receipts unknown |
| First 100 successful sales | 100 × NPR 1,000 = NPR 100,000 gross; setup A; unknown fee components | Known structure = A + 100,000 × r / 100 + 100 × f, plus applicable tax/add-ons | Numerical total unknown; annual maintenance must also be confirmed if the period reaches year two |
| Published KPG tariff component | One transaction at an assumed NPR 1,000 sale amount; tariff shows Rs 5 or Rs 10, without payer/selection rules | 5 / 1,000 × 100 = 0.5%; 10 / 1,000 × 100 = 1% | Rs 5/Rs 10 corresponds to 0.5%/1% of this example amount. These are only the listed tariff components, not the merchant's all-in fee. |
| Standard merchant bank withdrawal | NPR 2,000 eligible merchant funds; FAQ's advertised free merchant transfer | 2,000 × 0 = 0 provider withdrawal charge | NPR 0 advertised provider bank-transfer charge; prior processing/tax deductions remain separate |

The Rs 5/Rs 10 KPG input is from https://khalti.com/info/charges/ (last updated 10-Jul-2026). The free merchant transfer claim is from https://khalti.com/payment-gateway/; https://blog.khalti.com/khalti-payment-gateway/. VAT applies to published tariffs where required, but these sources do not give all applicable numerical tax/stacking rules (checked 2026-11-05).

The 3% domestic and 5% international Visa/Mastercard rates on the tariff page apply to customer wallet loading. They are not used as merchant acquiring fees. The consumer bank-withdrawal fee and issued-card NPR 1,000 chargeback handling fee are also different product scopes. Source: https://khalti.com/info/charges/ (checked 2026-11-05).
