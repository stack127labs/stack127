**Current status and changes:** OpenNode's product and status pages were live on 2026-11-07. Its status page showed payments, Bitcoin nodes, API, and BTC withdrawals operational. A 2025 filing in the Prime Trust bankruptcy litigation alleges OpenNode used Prime Trust for payment rails and custody in 2023; those are allegations in a complaint, not findings about current operations. The official press room and company-news pages had no acquisition or shutdown notice; their latest company item was dated 2022-09-13.

This example excludes any separate payout fee, miner/network fee, and exchange-rate movement.

- The newer **2026-10-02 Supported currencies** help page says USD conversion, settlement, and bank transfers are for US-based businesses. The public settlement marketing page still says eight local currencies and does not list them. I used the newer support article for current availability and retained the conflict above.
- OpenNode's 2026 KYB page says registered businesses only, while its 2024 terms refer to businesses and individuals aged 18+. I treated the more recent onboarding page as the current rule. Whether a registered sole trader qualifies is not settled.
- The official press room and company-news archive were checked for acquisition, licence, and closure notices; their newest company item is from 2022. Current product pages and the status page remain live. No official shutdown or acquisition notice was found.
- The OpenNode Stripe page still says “Coming soon,” despite a 2022 launch announcement. Current availability is unclear. The 2020–22 official material also promoted remittance and local-currency corridors; no formal sunset date was found for that offer.
- A 2025 complaint filed in Prime Trust bankruptcy proceedings alleges OpenNode used Prime Trust for payment rails, settlement, liquidity, and custody in 2023. It is a court-filed allegation, not a finding; current bank partners are not named in OpenNode's current USD guide.
- FinCEN's official MSB search interface loaded, but the research interface did not return an OpenNode registrant result. OpenNode's own registered-MSB statement is recorded separately and is not treated as independent confirmation or as proof of state licensing.
- The bank-transfer guide labels the $500 scheduled minimum as a “scheduled bitcoin transfer” inside its USD bank transfer section. The amount is recorded as the section's USD bank-transfer minimum; the wording looks like a page typo.
