# dLocal Go

- **Official URL:** https://dlocalgo.com/
- **apps.md status:** active
- **Product scope:** Self-serve payment collection for growing businesses. Covers payment links and buttons, recurring subscriptions, hosted checkout, integrations, payment API and split payments. The merchant remains the seller. dLocal enterprise is a separate negotiated product with broader coverage and its own onboarding.
- **Current status and company news:** Active. The current dLocal Go marketing site, setup articles and status page are live. I checked the dLocal Go blog and dLocal newsroom; the Go blog's newest visible articles are from June 2023, while the parent newsroom has 2026 releases. I found no dLocal Go shutdown or sunset notice. dLocal's newsroom reports an intention to acquire AZA Finance in 2025; I found no notice on the checked pages that the transaction changes dLocal Go ownership or availability.

## Cost examples

The September 2026 schedule says processing fees exclude local taxes and that local tax applies only to the fee. These examples are our calculations from the published schedule, not quotes. They assume the percentage applies to a R$100 transaction and omit local tax because the current tax table could not be reliably mapped:

- **Brazil card example:** R$100 × 2.99% = **R$2.99 processing fee before local tax**. This is our calculation; tax is additional.
- **Brazil Pix example:** R$100 × 0.99% = **R$0.99 processing fee before local tax**. This is our calculation; tax is additional.
- A refund is listed as USD 1 in the current schedule, so a USD 100 refund has a **USD 1 published refund fee** (our lookup of the stated fee, not a percentage calculation).

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Current signup coverage is unclear at the edges. The July 2026 registration article gives 17 seller countries, while the October 2026 country article describes additional international-only seller bases. Both are official dLocal Go pages. I list the complete claims in F03 and do not treat buyer coverage as seller access.
- dLocal Go's terms link to a current transactional-rate PDF valid as of September 2026. Its extracted tables lose column alignment for many country-method and tax-rate rows, so I recorded only rates whose market/method mapping is readable and marked the rest check_failed. The older April 2023 pricing PDF remains on the site but is legacy, not the current rate source.
- The Go terms page says it was updated January 7, 2026, but an internal passage says February 2023 and its body repeatedly refers to “Dlocal Direct.” It conflicts with current product help on T+3/T+7 versus 14-day availability. I preserve both and give the more recent product timing in F33.
- The current September 2026 schedule states USD 1 per refund. Older help pages differ on whether a USD 10 threshold applies; the newer schedule is used for the current public fee.
- The terms list a 5% deposit that may increase to 10%, discretionary holds and a six-month rolling reserve after closure. The current public help pages do not explain these reserve clauses, and the terms' inconsistent dating limits confidence that they apply unchanged to every Go account.
- dLocal's public company releases announce an intention to acquire AZA Finance in 2025. The newsroom and Go blog checked did not state that this transaction changes Go ownership, coverage or operation.
- The live country and method selector is in the authenticated dashboard. I did not log in; the public payment-method PDF predates current country additions and omits Panama and the Dominican Republic.

## Field problems

- Seller signup and buyer collection are easy to confuse: dLocal Go publishes separate seller-registration, international-only seller and buyer-collection country lists, and the seller pages do not fully reconcile.
- Pricing is split between the current marketing/FAQ pages and a September 2026 rate PDF. Rates are country-and-method specific, with local tax on the fee; do not compare the listed base percentage as an all-in rate.
- The dLocal Go terms page is internally inconsistent and includes “Dlocal Direct” legacy wording. Enterprise dLocal products, payment methods and country reach were kept out of Go facts.
- Current marketing says 250+ methods, while public schedule extraction leaves some country/method columns unclear. Exact account-level method availability requires the public country/method selector or seller dashboard.
