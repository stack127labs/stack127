Scope: PAYCOMET's Spanish online gateway, hosted checkout, payment links and available local payment methods. This is a gateway/acquiring offer, not a merchant-of-record service. Some onboarding routes use PAYCOMET acquiring and others connect to a bank's TPV; settlement and pricing can therefore vary by route. Card-present terminal prices are excluded.

Current owner and status: PAYCOMET S.L.U. is part of Banco Sabadell Group. Banco Sabadell's 2025 consolidated accounts report 100% ownership at 31 December 2025. The service grew out of PayTPV and adopted the PAYCOMET name in 2019. PAYCOMET's current product pages and 2026 news archive show ongoing operation; I found no official shutdown notice. The Marketplace page currently says its SEPA mode is temporarily unavailable; Split is available by request and project review. No private people's names are included.
