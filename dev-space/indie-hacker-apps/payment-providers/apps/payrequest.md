## Status and product scope

PayRequest is the Dutch payment-links and billing platform listed at `payrequest.io`. It does not process fiat payments itself: it connects a seller's own accounts with Stripe, Mollie, PayPal and selected other services. Those providers charge their own processing fees. PayRequest also offers non-custodial USDC payment links, where funds move on-chain to the seller's connected wallet.

The live pricing page lists Free at €0/month with a 2% fee on successful standard payments, Premium at €5/month, and Business at €20/month. Premium and Business show a 0% PayRequest fee on standard payments while active; a separate 2% fee, capped at €25 per payment, remains for commission payments on every plan. The page shows monthly pricing and no annual-price switch. It does not say whether plan prices include VAT.

The site, pricing page, and registration route are live. The registration page is reachable, but its interactive form was not submitted. PayRequest's blog and Company News filter show 2026 posts. I found no official acquisition, shutdown, or product-sunset notice in the company pages, terms, blog, or Company News listings. The terms identify the company only as PayRequest in the Netherlands; the privacy policy calls it a Dutch business registered with the Chamber of Commerce. To avoid naming a private operator, this file describes it as a small company in the Netherlands.

## Cost examples

**Our calculation — Netherlands seller, €100 successful standard EEA card payment through Stripe on Free:**

- PayRequest: €100 × 2% = €2.00. The €25 cap does not apply at this amount.
- Stripe: €100 × 1.5% + €0.25 = €1.75.
- Total listed platform and provider fees: €2.00 + €1.75 = **€3.75**, before any applicable taxes, refunds, or currency conversion.

The same payment on active Premium would have €0 standard PayRequest transaction fee plus €1.75 Stripe fee, and the separate €5 monthly Premium subscription. This is a worked example, not a quote; provider eligibility and prices can change.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Current pricing lists Free (€0/month), Premium (€5/month), and Business (€20/month), while the June 2026 terms still say Business is quote-based and Free includes every feature. The terms say current pricing is on the website; this file records the current public pricing page and flags the stale terms text.
- The current pricing page says Premium and Business have 0% standard PayRequest fees; the USDC feature page still says USDC payments cost 2% capped at €25. Whether a paid subscription waives that crypto fee is not stated.
- PayRequest's provider-comparison page is marked updated April 2026 and shows different headline prices for PayPal and Mollie than their current dedicated price pages. I used the provider's own current country-specific fee pages for those examples.
- Plan prices are in EUR/month, but the pricing page does not say whether VAT is included. The monthly cost calculator says taxes are excluded from its estimate; it does not settle VAT treatment on the plan subscription.
- Seller eligibility, payment methods, payout currencies, settlement timing, and provider reserves depend on the connected processor and seller country. PayRequest's “200+ countries” marketing is not a complete seller-country list.
- The Company News page and blog are active in 2026; no official acquisition, shutdown, or sunset notice was found in the pages searched.

- PayRequest's legal terms and its current pricing page disagree on the Business plan and Free-plan feature scope. The dedicated pricing page is used as the public current price list; the conflict is recorded under F19.
