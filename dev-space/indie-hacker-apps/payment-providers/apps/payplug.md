## Cost examples

Our calculations, using the current France Starter tariff. Inputs: €100 card payment; eurozone consumer card; online processing; no refund; transaction price is stated excluding tax.

- Processing fee: €100 × 1.5% + €0.25 = **€1.75 HT**.
- Mainland-France VAT example from Payplug’s published 20% fee-VAT rule: €1.75 × 20% = €0.35 tax; total provider charge = **€2.10 TTC**. This uses the published 2023 VAT help rate and is not a tax calculation for other seller locations.
- In-store equivalent: €100 × 1.5% + €0.10 = **€1.60 HT**; adding the same 20% fee VAT gives **€1.92 TTC**.
- Italy Starter eurozone consumer online card has the same published rate: €100 × 1.5% + €0.25 = **€1.75 excluding taxes**. Italy’s indicative plan turnover band is different.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Seller-bank eligibility:** the 2026-11-04 signup page lists Switzerland and Monaco and says both company and bank must be in a listed country. Current CGV say payout bank must be in the EEA. These rules conflict for Switzerland and Monaco; no resolution found.
- **Payplug/Dalenys combination date:** Payplug’s history says the brands united in 2022; BPCE’s key-figures page dates their merger under the Payplug brand to 2023. BPCE’s 2026 news separately says Dalenys was integrated in 2023. This may reflect announcement versus completion, but the pages do not say so directly.
- **Licence date:** current CGV state authorization in 2011; an older VAT help article says Payplug became an EU payment institution in 2016. Current CGV used for the legal fact; discrepancy retained.
- **Legacy pricing:** the older 2023 offers help article lists Starter 1.2% + €0.25 and Pro 0.8% + €0.15. The current tariff pages give different current transaction rates and take precedence for this record. The outdated rates were not carried into current pricing rows.
- **Card category rule:** current tariff labels eurozone consumer, Business and outside eurozone but does not define its card-classifier; older help does not settle the current rule.
- **PayPal:** checkout artwork contains a PayPal mark, but the current methods catalogue and tariff table do not establish availability or a fee. Marked unclear.
- **Hardware:** the public price and in-store pages plus current TPE sale/rental CGV annexes were checked; no current public numeric Payplug terminal purchase/rental amount found. Terminal costs therefore remain not published and are not mixed into card transaction rates.
- **Other missing public terms:** minimum payout, universal reserve amount/duration, exact refund of original processing fee, customer-token portability and a universal response-time promise were not found. For each, the facts rows list the pricing/docs/terms/help pages searched.
- **VAT:** all tariff amounts are explicitly HT/net of tax. The 2023 help page gives fee VAT examples by French territory and EU VAT-number case; it is not a comprehensive current VAT matrix for every seller country.

## Field problems

- F03 and F05 differ in scope: the current help page treats the named country as both company and bank location, while current CGV restrict the payout bank to the EEA. Switzerland and Monaco cannot be reconciled from public sources.
- F08’s broad goods/services contract language does not provide a clear approval list for SaaS, courses, memberships or downloads. These products are not assumed approved.
- F12/F23 method markets are the rate page’s listed markets, not a complete buyer-country authorization map. Wero is being rolled out by bank and date.
- F20/F21 pricing category names are published, but card issuer/currency rules are not. Do not infer which individual card belongs to a class.
- F29/F30 distinguish merchant tax liability from VAT on Payplug’s own processing charges. They should not be collapsed into a claim that Payplug handles seller taxes.
- F41’s 2011 vs 2016 wording may refer to different legal milestones, but the source pages do not define the difference.
- TPE pricing is unavailable in public text even though the CGV contain separate sale and rental annexes; exact account quotes may differ.
