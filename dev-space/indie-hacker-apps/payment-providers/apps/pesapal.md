- An official Payments Page brochure crawled as published about 3.3 years ago states free signup, 3.5% per transaction and next-business-day settlement. Current country pages describe per-transaction pricing but publish no numerical rates. The older brochure does not establish a current rate, market, card class or effective period. Source: [older Pesapal Payments Page brochure](https://www.pesapal.com/media/117979/pesapal-payments-page-one-pager.pdf).
- Current country FAQs for six markets say 72 working hours, up to three working days. The generic withdrawal guide says 48 business hours and the statement guide says T+48 hours. These may describe similar timelines using different units, but the exact start, cutoff and meaning are not resolved.

Several /rw online, invoice, FAQ and terms routes currently render Tanzania titles or content. Rwanda-specific rate, charge currency, method list and settlement timing could not be established from those mislocalized pages.

Pages searched include the country buyer terms, country business FAQs, account support index, refund docs, current product pages and API docs. The required merchant agreement is not publicly linked.

- The FAQ calls a link a “pricing page,” but it resolves back to the FAQ and does not show a numerical schedule. Current prices cannot be compared or used in cost examples.
