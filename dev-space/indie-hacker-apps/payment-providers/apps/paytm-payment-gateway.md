# Paytm Payment Gateway

Shutdown, sunset and acquisition check: The gateway remains publicly offered. One97's 24 April 2026 disclosure says the RBI cancelled the banking licence of the separate Paytm Payments Bank Limited (PPBL) from close of business that day, and explicitly says Paytm Payment Gateway continues uninterrupted. This is not a gateway shutdown. PPSL received final online payment-aggregator authorisation on 26 November 2025 and additional physical/cross-border authorisation on 17 December 2025. No whole-gateway shutdown or acquisition notice was found in the official product, corporate and policy sources reviewed.
