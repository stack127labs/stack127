## Cost example

Social Shop Classic; our calculation for a successful 10,000 FCFA transaction, assuming the merchant remains in the published Classic monthly-volume band and the transaction fee is calculated on the sale amount:

- Inputs: 10,000 FCFA sale; 3%; 100 FCFA fixed fee.
- Formula: 10,000 × 0.03 + 100 = 400 FCFA.
- Result: estimated provider fee 400 FCFA; estimated merchant proceeds 9,600 FCFA before any Senegal financial-transactions tax or other applicable charge.
