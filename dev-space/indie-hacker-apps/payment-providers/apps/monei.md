Scope: MONEI's online payment platform, including MONEI One, checkout, payment links, subscriptions and local methods. Spain and Andorra have different contracting/acquiring routes. Prices below are not transferable between them. The pricing page also has an Online/In person switch; in-person card pricing is kept separate from online fees.

Current owner and status: The operating company is MONEI DIGITAL PAYMENTS, S.L., a Spanish payment institution registered with Banco de España under licence 6911. The official legal, news and press pages reviewed do not identify an ultimate parent or announce an acquisition. The official MONEI News page includes 2026 updates and the current account/pricing documentation is active; no shutdown notice was found. MONEI is currently open to businesses registered in Spain and Andorra. Portugal, France, Italy, Mexico, Brazil and Chile are described as planned expansion markets, not currently available. Andorra accounts use MONEI's technology with Creand as the payments provider. No private people's names are included.

## Facts

States apply to each row's stated value. For Spain, MONEI's current public plan is MONEI One. Card prices have two components: MONEI's fee and Interchange++ (network charges plus 0.10% acquiring fee when MONEI acquires). ## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Pricing controls checked:** the current pricing page has an Online/In person switch, a sale-amount input, selectors for payment location and monthly card sales, and an “adjust assumptions” control for card type. I recorded the published tier table rather than its sample estimate, which depends on those settings.
- **Spain and Andorra use different contracts.** Spain's public MONEI One rates include IVA treatment. Andorra pricing is set by Creand; MONEI lists its credit-card fee without IVA treatment, while Creand gives a separate Bizum fee of 0.50% (minimum €0.15) plus IGI from 2027-01-01. An older Creand blog says the Bizum fee starts in 2026; the newer Creand e-commerce page gives the 2027 effective date, which I used.
- **Country and method scope:** onboarding is open only to Spain and Andorra today, while other countries are planned. The method overview names fewer methods than the marketing page's “30+” claim. Creand lists Amazon Pay for its MONEI e-commerce route, but the MONEI Andorra method list omits it; I left this method conflict visible.
- **Ownership:** MONEI's legal notice identifies the operator. Its reviewed News and press pages contain current activity but do not identify an ultimate parent or shareholder owner; no acquisition or shutdown announcement appeared there.
- **Contract terms:** the public `/es/page/terms-of-service/` page contains English service-contract text even though the URL is Spanish. Spanish legal notice explains that Andorra merchants use a technology-platform relationship with Creand and are not contracting MONEI payment services. I used country-specific Spanish docs for onboarding, prices and settlement.
- **Pricing source details:** card Interchange++ varies with network/issuer/card assumptions; PayPal's own negotiated rate is paid directly to PayPal. High-volume Bizum has a volume discount by quote. These are not replaced with guessed fixed rates. Creand's Andorra TPV Virtual advertises no setup or maintenance charge, but its page does not spell out whether that exact statement applies to every MONEI e-commerce onboarding.
- **Unknowns:** full seller country/legal-form exceptions outside the published Spain/Andorra scope, all 30+ method prices, a published cross-border/FX rate, a minimum payout, account-wide processing limits, data/token migration, PayPal's separately negotiated fee, and Andorra card IVA details were not published in the checked official country, pricing, product, support and terms pages.
