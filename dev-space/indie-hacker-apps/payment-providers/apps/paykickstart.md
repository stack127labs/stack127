## Cost examples

These are our calculations, using the published Connect gateway schedule. They exclude refunds, taxes, gateway FX, and any platform subscription/revenue fee.

- **$100 card payment, Connect at up to $5,000 monthly Connect volume:** formula: `$100 × 2.80% + $0.25` = **$3.05 Connect fee**; seller receives **$96.95 before other adjustments**. The rate is based on the previous month’s successful Connect volume, not this one transaction.
- **$100 card payment, Connect at $500,001–$1,000,000 monthly volume:** formula: `$100 × 2.30% + $0.15` = **$2.45 Connect fee**; seller receives **$97.55 before other adjustments**.
- The **6% Launch Plan** is not included in these examples because current terms do not define its fee base and the public launch offer’s dates are past.

- **Current software pricing:** `paykickstart.com/pricing/` returned access denied. The 2025 changelog lists $79/month, while newer 2.0 pages describe one-to-three-year launch packages, annual processing caps, overages, and up to 25% annual savings. Its January 2026 launch schedule is past, the package cards are images with no accessible text, and the live price/period switch could not be inspected. The official JV page gives an aggregate $1,000–$35,000 range, not each plan price or cap. Current evergreen prices and exact cap/overage amounts remain not published in accessible pages.
- **Launch Plan:** current signup language confirms it is offered at 6% of revenue with no subscription. The detailed calculation base and any exceptions are not published.
- **Connect availability:** the current Connect support guide says Connect is now global. An older country/currency article says Connect supports USD/CAD; an older Connect article says US/Canada only. I used the current guide for current seller scope and kept the USD/CAD base-currency restriction.
- **Connect rate scope:** the fee schedule says Connect rates depend on the vendor plan and company country, but only one US/Canada table is linked. The page does not define international-card, FX, or refund processing charges.
- **Affiliate payouts:** legacy help lists PayPal, Payoneer, wire, and e-check, with instant/automatic payments only for eligible PayPal sales. The new Wallet launch page markets bank wire, PayPal, Venmo, and bank card withdrawals. A legacy commission-payout guide limits automation to Growth/Scale plans, while the 2.0 page says all features are included. Country coverage and current availability are not fully documented.
- **Tax:** internal tax tools calculate checkout tax; the terms leave tax compliance and remittance with the vendor. PayKickstart is not a merchant of record under the standard terms.
- **Company ownership:** the official page says a sale occurred in 2023 and former leadership returned in 2025, but the buyer/current beneficial owner is not named. Press, About, and current product updates were checked; no shutdown notice was found.
