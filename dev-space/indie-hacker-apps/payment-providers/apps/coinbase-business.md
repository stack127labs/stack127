**Operating and regulatory status:** Coinbase Business is active for eligible legal entities in the United States and Singapore. Coinbase announced on October 16, 2025 that it would fold Coinbase Commerce into Coinbase Business, then launched Business in Singapore on November 12, 2025. Coinbase’s transition notice gave Commerce merchants until March 31, 2026. That date has passed. The notice says the Commerce portal and withdrawal tool become inaccessible, so former merchants should treat exports, API migration, and wallet withdrawal as overdue; Coinbase does not say in the notice that it has extended the deadline. Existing Coinbase Exchange-settled merchants were to transition automatically. Current payment help lists USDC and USDT, so its newer help page supersedes the transition notice’s stale FAQ that still says USDC only and USDT “coming soon.” The Singapore entity holds a Major Payment Institution licence. The SEC’s 2023 civil case against Coinbase was dismissed with prejudice on February 27, 2025; the SEC said the dismissal was not a decision on the merits. The Coinbase blog and newsroom search did not surface an acquisition or a separate Coinbase Business rebrand. The Commerce change is a product consolidation.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The transition page’s FAQ says USDC only and USDT coming in March 2026. The current payment-link help page, checked the same day, lists USDC and USDT. The current help page is used for present checkout assets.
- The transition page says Coinbase Business charges migrating Commerce merchants 1%; the October and November 2025 product posts also stated 1% at launch. Current help and API docs direct merchants to view their actual fee inside the account, so I have not treated 1% as the current rate for all new accounts.
- General product pages say the current seller markets are the US and Singapore. US account terms describe US-formed businesses; Singapore terms require a Singapore-formed legal entity and local office. The US help page explicitly lists sole proprietorships, while the US agreement names C corporations and multi-member LLCs or other entities Coinbase elects to accept. Treat US sole-proprietor approval as subject to Coinbase’s eligibility review.
- The Singapore launch post says transfers through a local banking relationship are real-time. Current general Singapore cash-out help lists FAST for SGD and SWIFT for USD but does not promise an exact Business account bank-arrival time.
