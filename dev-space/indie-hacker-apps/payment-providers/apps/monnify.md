## Shutdown, sunset, acquisition, or rebrand check

Monnify appears active: its current pricing and product pages are live, its developer blog published a post on 2026-10-08, and the Monnify blog lists posts through 2026-07-16. I checked the official Monnify blog, Monnify developer blog, Moniepoint company news/blog, the company About page, and official searches for acquisition, shutdown, sunset, and rebrand notices. I found no Monnify shutdown, sunset, acquisition, or product-renaming notice. Monnify is a TeamApt Ltd product. TeamApt Inc. rebranded as Moniepoint Inc. in 2023; Moniepoint says it is the parent of TeamApt Limited. Moniepoint's 2026 acquisition of Orda Africa concerns food-service software, not Monnify. These are public-page checks, not proof that no private corporate change exists.
