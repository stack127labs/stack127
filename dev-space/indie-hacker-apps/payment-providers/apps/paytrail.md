**Current status and ownership:** Paytrail is active. Paytrail Oyj is a Finnish payment institution and part of Nexi/Nets Group. Nexi’s 2025 consolidated annual report lists Paytrail Oyj as a 100%-owned subsidiary of Nets Denmark A/S. Nets acquired Paytrail in 2014. Paytrail acquired Checkout Finland from OP in 2021; the Checkout service and merchants moved under the Paytrail brand. Paytrail announced that the legacy Checkout API and Checkout.fi domain closed on 30 June 2026. Paytrail’s current news and status pages were checked; the service status page showed all systems operational on the research date. No Paytrail shutdown notice was found.

**Scope:** This file covers Paytrail’s online payment service and related online checkout/payment-link tools. It is a payment service provider/gateway, not the seller or merchant of record. The merchant sells to the buyer and is responsible for the product, delivery, returns, customer complaints, sales taxes, and store terms. Paytrail’s separate Omnichannel offer includes card terminals and is not included in the online plan figures below.

**Pricing switches checked:** The Finnish pricing page has S, M, Shop-in-Shop, and Enterprise plan cards and separate fee columns for S and M. There is no currency selector or annual/monthly period switch on that page. It states that shown prices apply to companies registered in Finland; foreign-company prices may differ. The English pricing page is a separate language page rather than a switch. Monthly plan fees are listed in EUR; no currency conversion was applied. Plan changes are allowed once in each 12-month period.

## Cost example

**Our calculation, Finland, one successful €100 Visa/Mastercard payment in a month, before any refund or optional surcharge:**

- S variable charge: €0.50 + (3.25% × €100) = €3.75. S monthly fee including 25.5% VAT is €14.90 × 1.255 = €18.70. One-payment total including that month’s plan fee: €22.45.
- M variable charge: €0.50 + (2% × €100) = €2.50. M monthly fee including 25.5% VAT is €59 × 1.255 = €74.05. One-payment total including that month’s plan fee: €76.55.
- The plan’s first month without a monthly fee is excluded from the example. Card fee is VAT 0%; no non-EU/business-card uplift, recurring surcharge, individual settlement, or refund is included.

This is our calculation from the published Finland price list, checked 2026-11-07. Monthly VAT totals are rounded to cents.

## Field problems

- The public general terms permit registered EEA sole traders, but Shop-in-Shop separately excludes foreign sole traders. These are different contracting flows; the country/form rows state the scope.
- Paytrail displays its Finnish seller prices in EUR and explicitly warns that foreign-company prices may differ. No conversions or foreign quote assumptions were made.
- The bank-payment fee on M has a condition: all method categories must remain enabled; disabling any category changes the bank percentage. Some shop platforms may add 0.25%.
- Transaction fees are shown as VAT 0%, while monthly plan fees add Finnish VAT at 25.5%. PayPal/direct-provider fees add the Paytrail fee and the provider’s own fee, with 25.5% VAT on Paytrail’s direct-provider transaction charge.
- The current support article describes payout frequency choices and a usual daily-settlement delay, but the merchant’s exact active schedule and delay are visible only in its account.
- The legacy Checkout API/service sunset must not be confused with Paytrail’s current payment service, which remains active.
