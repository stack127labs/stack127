**Regulatory and operating status:** Cryptomus's merchant gateway and documentation were live on 2026-11-07. On 2025-10-16, Canada's FINTRAC confirmed an administrative monetary penalty of **CAD 176,960,190** against Xeltox Enterprises Ltd., operating as Cryptomus and formerly Certa Payments Ltd. FINTRAC found 2,593 contraventions across six violation types after considering the company's submissions. The company appealed to the Federal Court; FINTRAC's public record does not show an appeal outcome. On 2025-05-20, the British Columbia Securities Commission (BCSC) issued a temporary order against Xeltox concerning securities trading and promotional activity. The order expired on 2025-06-04; BCSC says the extension request was withdrawn on 2025-05-30. Its hearing page still labels the proceeding pending. The order was not a final finding about merchant gateway processing. Cryptomus's blog was checked for a company response or shutdown notice; no detailed response to FINTRAC was found there. FINTRAC's notice of the company's appeal is the published response evidence located.
