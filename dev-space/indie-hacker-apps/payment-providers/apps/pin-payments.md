- Active Pin Payments merchant offer; no whole-service shutdown notice found. Current site and status page checked 2026-11-05: https://pinpayments.com/; https://pin.statuspage.io/.
- Fat Zebra acquired Pin Payments on 11 December 2024. The earlier Checkout.com acquisition was announced on 26 May 2020. Current ownership should not be copied from older partner articles. Official notices checked 2026-11-05: https://pinpayments.com/blog/fat-zebra; https://www.fatzebra.com/blog/fat-zebra-acquires-pin-payments; https://www.checkout.com/ja-jp/newsroom/checkoutcom-acquires-australian-startup-pin-payments.

Replace legacy API/Hosted Fields references with `pinpayments.com`. Notice dated 3 October 2024; checked 2026-11-05: https://pinpayments.com/blog/sunsetting-the-pin-net-au-domain-on-the-30th-of-November-2024.

## Cost examples: our calculations

Inputs and source rates checked 2026-11-05. Assume an approved account, successful captured sale, no refund/dispute/reserve unless shown, and the stated card origin and charge/settlement currency. The percentage applies to the stated total charge. Australian transaction fees include GST; New Zealand listed fees exclude GST. These are processing components, not a full business bill. External software, bank charges and any applicable additional tax remain separate.

| Scenario and inputs | Formula | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AU seller, Australian-bank card, AUD 10 charge; 1.6% + AUD 0.30 | AUD 10 × 0.016 + AUD 0.30 | Fee AUD 0.46; charge less fee AUD 9.54; effective fee 4.6%. |
| AU seller, same tariff, AUD 100 charge | AUD 100 × 0.016 + AUD 0.30 | Fee AUD 1.90; charge less fee AUD 98.10; effective fee 1.9%. |
| AU seller, foreign-bank card charged in AUD 100; 3.4% + AUD 0.30 | AUD 100 × 0.034 + AUD 0.30 | Fee AUD 3.70; charge less fee AUD 96.30. No currency conversion in this scenario. |
| NZ seller, domestic card, NZD 100 charge; 1.9% + NZD 0.30 | NZD 100 × 0.019 + NZD 0.30 | Fee NZD 2.20 before applicable GST; NZD 97.80 before that fee tax. This is not the final payout. |
| AU foreign settlement enabled; USD 100 charged/settled in USD; 3.9% + USD 0.30 | USD 100 × 0.039 + USD 0.30 | Fee USD 4.20; charge less fee USD 95.80; no Pin conversion component. |
| Refund the original AU domestic AUD 100 sale | Original fee AUD 100 × 0.016 + AUD 0.30 retained; additional refund fee AUD 0 | Processing cost retained AUD 1.90 after returning AUD 100 to buyer. |

AU rate/definition source: https://pinpayments.com/support/faqs/your-account/pricing. NZ tariff and fee-GST treatment: https://pinpayments.com/pricing. Foreign-settlement USD example: https://pinpayments.com/support/faqs/your-account/pricing. Refund cost: https://pinpayments.com/support/faqs/charges-and-refunds/refund-cost. All checked 2026-11-05.

**FX example, our calculation:** assume USD 100 charged with conversion into AUD and a purely illustrative interbank rate of AUD 2 per USD. This is an input assumption, not a current market quote. Adjusted rate = 2 × (1 − 0.017) = 1.966. Converted amount = USD 100 × 1.966 = AUD 196.60. Processing = AUD 196.60 × 0.034 + AUD 0.30 = AUD 6.9844. Amount after this processing component = AUD 189.6156. For illustration only, rounding the last two results to cents gives AUD 6.98 and AUD 189.62; Pin's exact general rounding rule was not established. The 1.7% exchange adjustment occurs before processing, rather than being added as a second processing percentage. Source checked 2026-11-05: https://pinpayments.com/support/faqs/your-account/pricing.

## Field problems

- F03–F06 need country domicile and beneficial-owner checks separately. Sole-trader eligibility does not remove ABN/NZBN, establishment or bank verification requirements.
- F11/F32 need a country column: broad charge-currency support and Australian foreign settlement do not establish NZ multi-currency settlement.
- F14/F15 need schedule-specific retry limits. Billing intervals shorter than seven days lose the retry cycle documented for longer plans.
- F19–F28 need the percentage base, replacement versus additional fees and tax scope. An FX adjustment to an exchange rate is not the same calculation as adding processing percentages.
- F26/F36 must distinguish retained processing, temporary FX buffer, refund/dispute offsets and an account reserve.
- F35 uses minor units, including IDR sen. The maximum-charge article uses unqualified dollar symbols and does not define country/currency equivalence.
- F41 cannot convert a provider security statement, ABN or parent/acquirer relationship into an independently verified financial licence.
- F43 “split payments” means distributing proceeds to sellers; it is different from splitting a buyer's sale across cards or using the account for unauthorized third parties.
- C01–C05 do not apply. Do not confuse this Pin Payments with Pay It Now/PIN Network.
- apps.md: no definite row error found. Gateway is a reasonable short type, with processing/acquiring bundled into the account. The row's AU/NZ seller region is confirmed; the acquisition and legacy-domain sunset are added above.
