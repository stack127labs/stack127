**Regulatory and operating status:** CryptAPI's product site, API documentation, blog and changelog were live on 2026-11-07. Its current terms identify CryptAPI Inc. in Panama. The terms prohibit activity involving Iran, Syria, North Korea, Cuba, Belarus, Russia, Crimea/Sevastopol, and the Donetsk and Luhansk regions, and allow transaction reviews or delays. I searched the Panama SMV's crypto notices and investor-alert pages and the SBP site for the company name; I found no CryptAPI-specific notice. The UAF site could not be searched through the research browser because access was blocked. CryptAPI does not publish a licence number or claim a financial licence. Panama's SMV was still discussing the scope of digital-asset regulation in October 2026, so this file does not infer whether CryptAPI needs a licence. I found no acquisition or shutdown notice in CryptAPI's product pages, blog, press page or changelog. Its October 20, 2026 blog post changed volume tiers effective November 1, 2026. The service remains marketed as a no-signup API.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **KYC and account requirements:** The public API FAQs say no account is needed, while the privacy policy says KYC/KYB is mandatory at account creation and the terms permit identity checks. The docs do not explain how those clauses apply to the account-free route. See F06; pages checked: API registration FAQ, privacy policy and terms.
- **Minimum fee wording:** The homepage, plugin and fee FAQ refer to a minimum fee for some coins; the service information API says there is currently no minimum service fee. The coin matrix separately publishes minimum incoming amounts and daily network-cost estimates. See F28; pages checked: homepage, WooCommerce plugin, fee FAQ, service information API and cryptocurrency matrix.
- **Recurring payments:** The WooCommerce changelog lists Subscriptions support, but the plugin, custom-flow and webhook docs do not say whether renewals are collected automatically or initiated by the buyer (F14).
- **Seller countries:** The terms publish restricted places, but no complete allowed-country list; the UAF company-name search and live status metrics could not be checked (F03, F39, F41).
- **Timing and fee estimates:** Forwarding waits for the configured blockchain confirmations. Network fees in C01 are daily estimates for one output and can change before a transaction is forwarded.

## Field problems

- The standard product is account-free, so account-creation and account approval fields do not map cleanly. The privacy policy still describes account KYC/KYB; recorded as conflicting rather than assuming it applies to ordinary API users.
- The coin matrix is dynamic and has 106 assets. To keep the record readable, assets are grouped by network, while each ticker retains its own published minimum and network estimate in C01.
- The service-fee minimum wording is inconsistent with the current service-info API. It is recorded once as conflicting; transaction minimums and chain fees are separate.
