## Shutdown, sunset, merger and name notices

- **Active provider; historical merger and rebrand:** on 24 December 2018, 智通數位科技股份有限公司 merged into surviving 藍新科技股份有限公司. 智付通 / Spgateway became NewebPay; the surviving company assumed the obligations. The old Spgateway app was retired in favor of the replacement merchant app. This was not a shutdown of the continuing payment platform. [official merger source](https://www.newebpay.com/info/news/news_detail?post_data=f14f48ff4ed0b4e886b0bd45f6d1fa2f69871133b1dabab4a3145fcebe0c99a9) Checked 2026-11-05.
- **Fula retired:** new activations stopped 12 August 2024; transactions stopped 23 September 2024; the partner's service ended 1 October 2024. It is not a current BNPL option. [official fula source](https://www.newebpay.com/info/news/news_detail?post_data=2834d39ce7b347ad799e38118d52449590282cf574e7f504875642791eea38ce) Checked 2026-11-05.
- **Other material changes:** current 2026 documentation uses BitoPro instead of BitoPay. OK/來來 shipping references conflict: the 29 May 2026 term revision removes it from pickup logistics, but some current page/API text still names it. No precise universal cutoff was established. [official terms source](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/new_service_policy); [official rules source](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/product_use_rule); [official mpg source](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/download_file?name=%E7%B7%9A%E4%B8%8A%E4%BA%A4%E6%98%93%E2%94%80%E5%B9%95%E5%89%8D%E6%94%AF%E4%BB%98%E6%8A%80%E8%A1%93%E4%B8%B2%E6%8E%A5%E6%89%8B%E5%86%8A_NDNF-1.2.6.pdf). Checked 2026-11-05.
- No provider-wide current closure or additional acquisition notice was found in the official product, news, terms and developer pages reviewed. This is a bounded research finding, not a promise about future availability.

## Cost examples — our calculations

Checked 2026-11-05. These use standard Taiwan seller pricing, successful payments, no negotiated discount, no disputes, no optional invoice service and no currency conversion. Rates on the transaction tariff include tax. Card and ATM examples follow the published rounding/minimum rules; no extra 5% tax is added. Sources: [fare](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/service_fare), [rules](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/product_use_rule), [bitoman](https://www.newebpay.com/dw_files/BitoManual.pdf?20261015).

| Scenario and inputs | Formula | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One TWD 1,000 domestic single card payment | Fee = max(TWD 1, round(1,000 × 2.8%)); balance proceeds = 1,000 − 28 | TWD 28 fee; TWD 972 before bank withdrawal. |
| 100 domestic card payments of TWD 1,000; one withdrawal among the first five that month | Gross = 100 × 1,000; fees = 100 × round(1,000 × 2.8%); withdrawal = 0; net = 100,000 − 2,800 | TWD 97,200 net; effective provider cost = 2,800 ÷ 100,000 × 100 = 2.8%. |
| Same batch, but withdrawal is the sixth of the month | Net = 100,000 − 2,800 − 10; effective cost = (2,800 + 10) ÷ 100,000 × 100 | TWD 97,190 net; effective cost 2.81%. No fees for the earlier withdrawals are assigned to this batch. |
| One TWD 1,000 foreign-issued card payment, charged in TWD | Fee = round(1,000 × 3.5%); net = 1,000 − 35 | TWD 35 fee; TWD 965 before withdrawal. The 3.5% replaces 2.8%; buyer issuer charges are outside this calculation. |
| One TWD 5,000 traditional ATM payment | Fee = min(20, max(1, round(5,000 × 1%))); net = 5,000 − 20 | TWD 20 fee; TWD 4,980 before withdrawal. |
| One TWD 5,000 Smart ATM 2.0 payment | Fee = max(10, round(5,000 × 1%)); net = 5,000 − 50 | TWD 50 fee; TWD 4,950 before withdrawal. The older ATM cap is not applied. |
| One TWD 100 Smart ATM 2.0 payment | Fee = max(10, round(100 × 1%)); net = 100 − 10 | TWD 10 fee; TWD 90 before withdrawal; effective payment fee 10%. |
| One TWD 1,000 BitoPro order | Fee = round(1,000 × 2%); net = 1,000 − 20 | TWD 20 fee; TWD 980 credited on the published claim-date +10-day schedule, before withdrawal. Buyer USDT/USDC quantity depends on the live quote and is not invented. |
| New optional ezPay Basic annual invoice subscription | Setup 3,000 + annual Basic 6,000 | TWD 9,000 including tax in the first year, for up to 500 invoices each month; payment processing is additional. |

These are provider-cost examples, not estimates of seller profit or tax due. Unsupported or negotiated prices are not replaced with zero.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Personal collection cap:** [signup](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/register) says TWD 100,000/month starting capacity; [fare](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/service_fare) says TWD 200,000 personal/TWD 600,000 business without a period. [terms](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/new_service_policy) allows adjustable limits. No public explanation reconciles the tiers; ask for the actual approved limit.
- **Refund deadline:** [rules](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/product_use_rule) says ordinary cards have 89 calendar days after claim and UnionPay 29 days after payment. FAQ 74 on [faq](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/faq), updated 2 September 2026, says cards/UnionPay 89 days after transaction creation. Both are retained. DCC's separate 59-day rule is not silently applied to ordinary cards.
- **Shipping:** the 29 May 2026 comparison on [terms](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/new_service_policy) removes OK/來來 from pickup logistics. [fare](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/service_fare) lists three C2C chains, while [rules](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/product_use_rule) still names 來來 in COD text and [mpg](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/download_file?name=%E7%B7%9A%E4%B8%8A%E4%BA%A4%E6%98%93%E2%94%80%E5%B9%95%E5%89%8D%E6%94%AF%E4%BB%98%E6%8A%80%E8%A1%93%E4%B8%B2%E6%8E%A5%E6%89%8B%E5%86%8A_NDNF-1.2.6.pdf) still has an OK shipping option. [signup](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/register) also says four-chain logistics. This does not establish a current OK route or an exact universal shutdown date.
- **Old BitoPro promotion:** [bito](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/bito_pro) still advertises zero fees until 7/31 (Wednesday), without a year. [fare](https://www.newebpay.com/website/Page/content/service_fare) and the September 2026 [bitoman](https://www.newebpay.com/dw_files/BitoManual.pdf?20261015) publish 2%.
