## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Egypt onboarding:** Tap's help page says it accepts no new merchants from Egypt, Jordan, or Lebanon. Tap later announced an Egyptian payment-facilitator/service-provider license, and its current news materials mention regulatory approvals in Egypt. The license does not settle seller onboarding, so the no-new-merchants statement is retained as the operative onboarding restriction.
- **Supported currencies:** Tap's currency help says 10 currencies but names nine. A webhook guide includes EGP and JOD as amount-format examples, not a definitive enabled-currency list. I did not guess the missing currency or claim all examples are chargeable.
- **Apple Pay in Oman:** A support help article lists Oman among six supported countries; the newer Apple Pay developer article lists UAE, KSA, Kuwait, Qatar, and Bahrain, plus USD. This leaves Oman unclear. The merchant needs Tap to confirm availability for its account.
- **Google Pay coverage:** The support payment-method matrix lists UAE and Kuwait; the developer integration page lists UAE, Oman, Kuwait, Qatar, and Bahrain, plus USD. The developer page is more specific and appears newer, but account enablement remains required.
- **UAE payout frequency:** The localized merchant agreement says AED wallets settle T+5 daily and USD wallets T+5 weekly on Tuesday. The dedicated UAE payout help page says transactions settle in 5 business days and bank payouts run weekly Tuesday, with Friday availability for corporate bank transactions. These statements may describe different wallet/account routes; both are preserved rather than collapsed.
- **Refund timing:** The general refund page says Tabby refunds take 14–21 business days; Tabby's own FAQ says 14–21 working days. Exact unit is inconsistent.
- **Price gaps:** Public schedules exist for Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, and UAE. Bahrain, Oman, and Qatar terms were checked individually; they do not expose a fixed online fee schedule. Tap directs sellers to customer experience for price plans.
- **Sources checked for unpublished prices and limits:** Each relevant market's terms agreement, Tap's supported-countries and price-plan help, charge API reference, and relevant payout or method-specific help/docs. Exact URLs are attached to each unknown row.
- **Shutdown/acquisition check:** Reviewed the Tap blog homepage, official announcements archive, Tap About/team and product pages. No shutdown, sunset, acquisition, or rebrand notice was found. The company does not expose a separate newsroom link from its current home page.

- The supported-country help page embeds its country list as an image. I relied on the explicit no-new-merchants notice, current six-GCC payout page, current homepage, and regulatory announcement rather than treating that image as readable evidence.
- Currency help gives a count that does not match the named list; other developer examples are not a currency allowlist.
- Public fixed and processing fees are not equally available across countries. Bahrain, Oman, and Qatar have agreement pages but no public online fee schedule in the reviewed page text.
- Terms pages in Arabic say Arabic controls if Arabic and English versions conflict; the fact table uses the English agreement text.
- The generic refund help uses “business days” for most methods but a separate Tabby FAQ uses “working days.”
