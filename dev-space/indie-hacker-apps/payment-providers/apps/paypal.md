All factual source checks below were made on 2026-11-04. Page-update dates and promotion dates are recorded separately where available. Public legal and help pages were read without signing in. Unknown means the stated pages did not establish an answer; it does not mean the feature is absent.

## Field problems

- F03/F05/F10/F11/F32: seller registration, owner residency, bank location, buyer access, charge currency and balance/payout currency are different. A worldwide marketing total is not a seller-admission list. Main markets are researched here; other countries are not exhaustively classified.
- F19 to F28: wallet checkout, guest-card agreements, advanced cards, IC++, Payment Links, payment requests, invoice payments and legacy Pro/recurring products have different fees. Their percentages/fixed components cannot be merged into a single PayPal rate. International wallet classification follows account residence rather than automatically following the card issuer.
- F20/F21: Germany/France are independent country records, not a euro-area template. Australia also has a governing financial disclosure with separate wallet/direct-card rates. Fixed fees follow received currency, not merely seller nationality.
- F22: an exchange-rate spread is not the same as a separate percentage debited from gross sales. Live FX rates were not requested, currencies were not converted, and converted final payouts are not guessed.
- F24: recurring invoices/reminders, actual automatic Subscriptions and optional legacy recurring tools are different. A fee called Invoice Subscription Service is not automatically the Subscriptions product's billing surcharge.
- F25/F27: fraud tools and paid Chargeback Protection have enrollment, exclusions and loss-cap conditions. Paid US protection excludes recurring agreements and cannot run alongside the named fraud tools. Standard Seller Protection is also conditional; a hosted checkout does not guarantee coverage.
- F26/F29/F30: retaining receiving fees after refund, provider-fee tax, buyer sales tax, merchant VAT information and PayPal reporting/withholding are distinct obligations. Manual tax lines are not merchant-of-record tax handling.
- F28/F33/F34/F35: available balance, holds, daily initiation, bank arrival, minimum withdrawal, fee-free threshold and minimum fee need separate values. A US sending limit is not a verified merchant receiving cap.
- F37: CSV activity reports, direct-card migration and PayPal wallet mandates have different portability. The Payflow-oriented vault migration help is not an unrestricted promise for every product.
- F41: country entities and their stated regulatory/security scope are recorded from official documents. No broad trust score or independent current certificate/register status is inferred.
- C01 to C05 apply because Pay with Crypto is available within eligible US merchant accounts. Consumer crypto trading, optional PYUSD balances and Enterprise payouts are not treated as identical to crypto checkout acceptance.
