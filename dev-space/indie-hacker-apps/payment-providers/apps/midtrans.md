## Scope and evidence

This record covers Midtrans payment acceptance, its connected subscriptions/invoicing, ordinary merchant withdrawals and the payout/partner distinctions needed to compare costs. It does not turn the wider GoTo group into one payment offer. Midtrans processes charges in IDR and publishes a foreign-business onboarding checklist, but not a complete approved foreign seller-country list. There is no separate country tariff inferred from that checklist. [Currency rules](https://docs.midtrans.com/docs/can-i-receive-payments-using-other-currency-than-idr) · [Seller documents](https://docs.midtrans.com/docs/what-are-the-legal-documents-required-for-midtrans-account-registration).

The public activation guide says agreed merchant terms are delivered by email and downloadable after 24 hours. A complete public merchant agreement was not recovered: the attempted terms URL failed, and public registration returned no readable text. No signup, login, form submission or private account access was used. Contract-specific reserve, exit and financial-release terms remain unknown. [Activation and terms delivery](https://midtrans.com/passport) · [Attempted terms address](https://midtrans.com/terms-and-conditions) · <https://dashboard.midtrans.com/register>.

Marketing pricing, method help, API references, refund/withdrawal guides, current notice pages and the privacy notice were read. Public HTML docs worked even when direct HTTP/Markdown requests returned 403; those readable facts are not marked failed merely because one access route failed.
