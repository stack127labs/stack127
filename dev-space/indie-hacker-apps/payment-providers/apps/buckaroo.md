## Cost examples

All examples use Buckaroo's published Dutch online tariff checked 2026-11-06. Buckaroo says percentages apply to gross volume and prices exclude BTW. These are **our calculations**, before BTW on Buckaroo's fee and excluding any buyer tax, method-owner surcharge, refund, or custom contract cost.

- Netherlands iDEAL by Wero, one EUR 20.00 payment: EUR 0.30 fixed fee = **EUR 0.30**.
- EU-issued consumer credit card, one EUR 20.00 payment at the published starting rate: EUR 0.25 + (EUR 20.00 × 1.80%) = **from EUR 0.61**.
- EU-issued consumer debit card, one EUR 20.00 payment at the published starting rate: EUR 0.25 + (EUR 20.00 × 1.20%) = **from EUR 0.49**.
- Netherlands iDEAL by Wero bundle with 20 monthly transactions: EUR 4.99 monthly bundle ÷ 20 = **EUR 0.2495 effective bundle cost per included transaction** (before BTW), if all 20 are used; overage is EUR 0.30 per transaction.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The current tariff page says online prices are per transaction, percentage fees use gross volume, and tariffs exclude BTW. It also warns that PayPal, in3 and gift cards can have additional method-owner commissions. Those additions are not quantified publicly.
- Card rates are labeled “from” and the card documentation describes Interchange++ pricing. The starting rates do not settle every card's exact cost. No universal cross-border surcharge was found.
- The Buckaroo tariff page gives method-specific instant-refund amounts while its instant-refunds product page uses a different presentation of standard/variable and premium charges. I did not combine them into a universal refund rate; use the contracted method tariff for an actual quote.
- The September 2026 company profile names Keensight Capital as owner. The July 2026 leadership release describes management and Keensight ownership more broadly; the public sources do not give a current ownership percentage split. This file records Keensight as the named owner without claiming a precise stake.
- Country-method coverage describes where buyers can pay. Buckaroo's full seller-country whitelist, owner residency rules, accepted legal forms, and a country-by-country bank matrix were not found.
- Checked for shutdown/acquisition/rebrand signals in Buckaroo's official [press releases](https://www.buckaroo.eu/press-releases), [blog](https://www.buckaroo.eu/blog), [Payconiq partnership announcement](https://www.buckaroo.eu/press-releases/strategic-partnership-between-buckaroo-payconiq-international-and-epi-officially-in-effect), and [Wero migration notice](https://www.buckaroo.eu/en-lu/blog/buckaroo-brings-wero-to-luxembourg-merchants). No Buckaroo shutdown notice found. Payconiq's Luxembourg app sunset is a product migration to Wero, not a Buckaroo shutdown.

## Field problems

- F03/F04: Buckaroo publicly describes an EU bank-account condition and collects legal-form details, but its application guide does not publish a complete seller-country or legal-form eligibility table. These are recorded separately from buyer markets.
- F20/F21: “From” card pricing and Interchange++ mean card-origin and scheme detail can change the final price. The public headline table is not a merchant quote.
- F23: Some methods have separate method-owner commissions, and SEPA Direct Debit is quote-based. Bundling methods into a single “local payments” rate would hide real differences.
- F26: Refund rates vary by card, Apple Pay and Trustly, and two official pages display instant-refund pricing differently. The general refund fee is therefore not treated as settled.
- F38: Customer-service channels exist, but support hours and guaranteed response times are not published for every merchant tier.
- C01–C05: Crypto fields are conditional and not applicable because Buckaroo's public catalogue lists no crypto payment offer.
