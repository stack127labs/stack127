- **Product researched:** PayTabs online payment gateway. Paymes Super App, orchestration, POS, issuing and acquiring-switch products are kept distinct.
- **Current operator/owner:** PayTabs Group operates the PayTabs brand. Official company material says the group is backed by private Saudi investment but does not disclose the current ultimate equity holders. PayTabs' own release calls this “full ownership” in its headline and one paragraph, but “majority stake” elsewhere; a later company recap says the acquired stake was 51%, so the exact resulting percentage is unresolved by the reviewed text.
- **Shutdown, sunset, rebrand or acquisition notices:** No official shutdown or sunset notice found. The status page said all listed services were online when checked. PayTabs Group announced it acquired 100% of UAE contactless platform TAPn’GO on April 19, 2026. On October 27, 2026 it announced an MoU to acquire 100% of SeaPay; this is a proposed deal, not a confirmed completed acquisition. The PayTabs Egypt ownership wording is noted above.
