**Regulatory and operator notice (checked 2026-11-07):** Heleket’s current terms do not name a contracting legal entity. The UK Sanctions List names Xeltox Enterprises Ltd. as a designated entity and lists “Cryptomus” and “Heleket” as name variations, with `heleket.com` as a website. Its statement of reasons says the designation concerns Xeltox’s ownership of Cryptomus and activities linked to and continued via Heleket. The list applies asset-freeze, trust-service, director-disqualification, internet-service, correspondent-banking and payment-processing restrictions. The UK record gives a British Columbia registration number and a Vancouver address. FINTRAC separately says Xeltox was incorporated in British Columbia, imposed a CAD 176,960,190 penalty on 2025-10-16, and records that Xeltox appealed. A B.C. Securities Commission temporary order against Xeltox doing business as Cryptomus expired on 2025-06-04; the extension application was withdrawn on 2025-05-30. The current product site, docs and blog load. The official blog and product materials disclose no acquisition or shutdown notice; the official UK regulator record establishes the Cryptomus/Heleket link described above. Heleket’s public terms do not state a financial licence.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Heleket advertises payment processing “from 0.4%,” while its comparison article says the exact rate is set per project. Its authenticated payment and payout service-list endpoints return account-specific fees and limits, so the public pages do not give a full current schedule.
- Heleket’s comparison article says 17 cryptocurrencies across 8 networks; the developer reference lists 19 asset symbols across 13 network codes. The pages do not explain the difference.
- Recurring billing is described in Heleket marketing, and a static-wallet API page references recurrence payment IDs. Public API navigation and guides do not show how to create or manage recurring charges, renewals, or retries.
- The terms do not name the contracting company. The official UK Sanctions List names Cryptomus and Heleket as name variations of Xeltox Enterprises Ltd.; FINTRAC and BCSC records concern Xeltox/Cryptomus. The public records do not establish more about the internal company structure.
- Heleket’s documented settlement is crypto. A payout amount can be expressed in fiat for conversion, but the payout is sent to a crypto address; no bank payout route was found.

## Field problems

- F03 publishes a non-serviced-jurisdiction list but no complete positive list of eligible seller countries. “Not listed” does not establish acceptance.
- F14 is difficult to resolve because marketing and a static-wallet API reference suggest recurrence support, while public developer instructions do not describe the feature.
- F19/F28 exact payment, conversion and withdrawal fees vary by asset/network or account. The live API service lists require merchant credentials, so no login was attempted. The public docs do specify a one-time 2 TRX static-wallet creation fee.
- The sitemap URL could not be verified in the web fetch; newsroom and changelog checks rely on the visible site/docs navigation and published blog instead.
