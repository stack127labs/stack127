## Service notices checked first

All notice and status sources below were checked on **2026-11-05**.

- **Acquisition:** Razorpay announced a majority-stake acquisition of Curlec on **8 February 2022**. Curlec now identifies itself as Razorpay Curlec, part of the Razorpay group; its company name is Razorpay Curlec Sdn. Bhd., formerly Curlec Sdn. Bhd. The announcement does not establish a 100% purchase. <https://razorpay.com/blog/razorpay-acquires-leading-malaysian-fintech-startup-curlec/>; <https://curlec.com/about/>
- **Current offer:** the official Malaysian site, pricing, account-setup instructions and product documentation remain available. No whole-service shutdown, sunset or replacement-brand notice was found in the official company/product pages and targeted official-site notice searches. This is a public-evidence check, not a live transaction or settlement test. <https://curlec.com/>; <https://curlec.com/pricing/>; <https://curlec.com/docs/payments/set-up.md>; <https://curlec.com/about/>
- **Important policy conflict for this directory:** the General Terms' prohibited list includes intangible goods/services, with software downloads as an example, while Curlec's own Malaysian marketing promotes SaaS subscription collections. Digital-product approval is unresolved; do not silently treat every SaaS, ebook or course seller as eligible. <https://curlec.com/s/terms-of-use/>; <https://curlec.com/blog/how-recurring-payments-benefit-subscription-businesses-in-malaysia/>; <https://curlec.com/subscriptions/>
- **Security-document qualification:** the currently linked PCI DSS certificate was issued **4 November 2025** and valid through **3 November 2026**. It had expired two days before this check. A renewed certificate was not found through the current link. This does not prove actual current noncompliance. <https://curlec.com/payment-gateway/>; <https://www.ampcuscyber.com/certificate/razorpay-curlec-sdn-bhd-pci-dss-coc.pdf>
- **Product limits:** DuitNow Pay remains marked early access for selected businesses. DuitNow Pay/QR have no test mode and do not support recurring payments. These restrictions are method-specific; they do not mean the whole gateway is retired. <https://curlec.com/docs/payments/payment-methods/duitnow/pay.md>; <https://curlec.com/docs/payments/payment-methods/duitnow/qr.md>

Scope: **Malaysian Razorpay Curlec**, Basic/Premium/Enterprise gateway pricing, managed subscriptions, separately contracted bank Direct Debit/outgoing Payouts, cross-border Indian UPI and Route split payments. Razorpay India pricing, Indian seller onboarding and INR settlement examples are not imported into this record. MYR/RM means Malaysian ringgit; FPX is online banking, DuitNow Pay/QR are separate bank-payment methods, SSM is Malaysian business registration, ROS is societies registration, KYC is business/identity checking, and SST is service tax.
