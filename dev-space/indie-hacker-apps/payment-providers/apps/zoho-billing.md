Scope: Zoho Billing is cloud billing and subscription-management software, not a payment processor or merchant of record. The business using it sells to its own customer. Billing connects to separately contracted gateways such as Zoho Payments, Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, Paytm, ICICI Bank EazyPay, Verifone, GoCardless, Authorize.Net, CSG Forte, and Braintree; available gateways depend on the Zoho edition and country. Zoho says it does not add a fee for online payment collection through these integrations. The gateway’s processing, refund, dispute, conversion, and payout charges remain separate. No account was created or accessed.

The US and India pricing pages were checked as separate regional pages. Each showed Monthly and Yearly controls, with the monthly price and the lower per-month price billed annually displayed together. The US page returned USD prices; the `/in/` India page returned INR prices. Both pages say local tax is extra (US: local taxes; India: GST). The Standard and Premium plans have a 100,000-invoice annual limit plus a region-specific annual billed-amount threshold. Current US and India product pages say there is no permanent free Zoho Billing plan and offer a 14-day trial. An older Qatar help page still says the app itself has a Free plan with one organization and five customers; this is recorded as legacy/conflicting evidence, not as a current US or India limit. Zoho Billing also lets its customers make a zero-priced subscription plan for their own end users; that feature is different from a free Zoho Billing license.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Free software plan:** current US FAQ and current US/India price pages show a 14-day trial and no ongoing free Zoho Billing license. A legacy Qatar help page says Free allowed one organization and five customers; it gives no effective date. The current pages are used for current US/India pricing; the older cap is kept as conflicting historical evidence. The Billing feature called “Free Plans” means a seller can set a zero-priced plan for its own subscribers.
- **Cancellation/free edition wording:** the account-management help still says “Move to Free Edition,” while the current US FAQ says Zoho Billing has no free plan. The page appears older/region-dependent and does not settle whether a no-cost Billing license remains available in all editions.
- **Gateway fees:** Zoho Billing has no universal card or local-method rate. The Paytm schedule is shown only as one official India example from Zoho’s integration help. Other gateway fees, international-card add-ons, refund retention, dispute costs, conversions, payout timing, and payout minimums belong to the chosen gateway and bank.
- **Signup availability:** the product signup asks for the business country and API docs list regional data centers, but the pages checked do not provide a complete current seller-country allowlist or business-form matrix.
- **Feature retirement check:** the Zoho Billing blog archive and corporate press page were searched for rebrand, acquisition, closure, and retirement news. They show the 2023 Zoho Subscriptions rebrand and later Billing/Enterprise updates, but no Zoho Billing shutdown announcement. A specific legacy PayPal/Payflow integration is marked deprecated; it is not a shutdown of the Billing product.
- **Tax scope:** Billing offers tax setup, tax calculations/reports, and India GST features. Its terms and product role do not show a universal transfer of seller tax registration, filing, or remittance responsibility to Zoho.

## Field problems

- **F03–F06 and F45:** these fields are written for payment processors and merchant underwriting. Zoho Billing is subscription software, so its login/contract and each connected gateway’s eligibility review are kept separate.
- **F10–F12, F20–F28, and F31–F36:** buyer methods, processing fees, disputes, reserves, and settlement are set by the connected gateway. The Billing product does not have one global answer for those fields.
- **F19/F40:** the live regional pricing pages, an older Qatar help page, and a cancellation article use different “Free” language. The live US/India price page and the dated legacy source are reported separately; the help pages do not resolve whether legacy free-license access remains available across editions.
- **F29:** tax calculation and reporting are not the same as taking on the seller’s legal tax duties. India help also references online filing features elsewhere in Zoho Finance, so this record does not attribute a separate tax filing/remittance service to Zoho Billing.
- **F43:** Zoho Payments has an India split feature in Early Access, but this is a separate product. It is not counted as a Billing feature.
- **C01–C05:** the crypto-only fields were attempted and marked not applicable because Billing is not a crypto checkout or wallet product.
