## Field problems and index notes

- **F03–F06:** individual signup is real, but the public corporate checklist and foreign-business marketing do not answer every nationality, corporate-form or bank-country exception.
- **F14/F15/F19/F24:** automated card/DANA collection, recurring emailed invoices and the merchant's paid dashboard subscription are different things. The dashboard's one-month trial is not a free trial feature for the merchant's own subscribers.
- **F20/F28/F33:** direct acquiring, aggregator gateway settlement, SendMe transfers and Cash Out have different fees and schedules. Their tables cannot be flattened into one fee or payout time.
- **F29/F30:** E-Faktur support and invoice tax codes do not make Faspay a merchant of record or establish that it registers/files the seller's taxes.
- **F35/F36/F44:** provider-published payment limits and conditional dispute holds are not universal account limits or a universal reserve schedule.
- **F40:** the one-week termination clause concerns calculating over/underpayment. Set-off and surviving liabilities prevent treating it as an unconditional final payout deadline.
- **Language precedence:** [Business terms](https://faspay.co.id/en/term-conditions-business/) and [operating terms](https://faspay.co.id/en/ftnc-ops-business-billing/) say Indonesian prevails on interpretation. Indonesian legal/FAQ pages were also opened; unresolved public differences are retained.
- No definite `apps.md` row error found. The “gateway; Indonesia” row fits the primary offer; foreign businesses collecting from Indonesia are a conditional route, not a separate seller-market price list.
- Only companies/projects are named. Private individuals and sole traders are not identified by personal name.
