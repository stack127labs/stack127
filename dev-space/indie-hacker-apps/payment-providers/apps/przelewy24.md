**Current status:** Active. Przelewy24 is operated by PayPro S.A., which is part of Nexi Group. The latest company news and blog pages checked show current activity; I found no Przelewy24 shutdown notice. Przelewy24 completed its Dotpay service migration in October 2023, and PayPro merged eCard into itself in December 2024. Neither notice says Przelewy24 is ending. In February 2026 the company announced strategic cooperation with Polskie ePłatności (PeP); the announcement describes alignment of online and in-person payment capabilities, not a product rebrand or legal merger.

Scope: Przelewy24's online payment gateway, hosted/embedded checkout, links, and separately identified installment and deferred-payment methods. The buyer pays the merchant for the merchant's goods or services; Przelewy24 is the payment provider, not a general merchant of record. I have separated the domestic PLN rate from foreign-currency methods and payout conversion. The current Polish agreement (OWU v2.0, effective 2026-05-15) says provider fees are increased by applicable VAT. No currency conversions are made in this file. No account or form was submitted, and no private people's names are included.

## Cost examples

Our calculation, using a PLN 100.00 domestic bank-transfer, BLIK or standard domestic-card payment:

- Inputs: PLN 100.00 payment; standard fee 1.29% + PLN 0.30; VAT excluded.
- Formula: 1.29% × PLN 100.00 + PLN 0.30 = PLN 1.29 + PLN 0.30.
- Result: **PLN 1.59 provider fee before applicable VAT**, leaving PLN 98.41 before VAT on the provider fee, payout fees, refunds or any account-specific terms.

The agreement says VAT is added under applicable law. I have not assumed a VAT rate or calculated tax.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The live marketing fee table shows wallets with the same headline 1.29% + PLN 0.30 card rate. The current OWU shows a 0% + PLN 0 wallet add-on and says wallet charges use the card-processing fee plus any listed extra wallet charge. I treat the OWU row as **no extra wallet surcharge**, while the base card-processing rate still applies.
- The public marketing page advertises installment pricing “from 0% + PLN 0.30”; OWU v2.0, effective 2026-05-15, gives the actual merchant fee by installment model and option. I use the current OWU rates above and note each option rather than treat the headline as the full schedule.
- The current payment-method page says payments can be handled in more than 50 currencies, but the public fee appendix names only explicit currency-priced transfer/card rows. I recorded those rows and did not infer that every currency is available to every seller or method.
- Foreign seller countries: the seller registration page says foreign-headquartered firms need a written agreement and gives merchant-service contact, while the current public terms and pricing do not publish an accepted-country list. I recorded a manual route, not blanket worldwide eligibility.
- Ownership: official company sources identify PayPro as P24's direct operator; Nexi's 2025 reporting and P24's January/February 2026 company news support the Nexi group connection. Dotpay's brand migration and the eCard legal merger are historical changes; P24 itself remains active.
- The company security page currently claims PCI DSS, but its linked certificate is dated 2023–2024. The current ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certificate is dated 2026-01-20. I did not treat the old PCI certificate as proof of current certificate validity.

## Field problems

- Seller-country coverage is not a public whitelist. Domestic Poland signup is documented; foreign-incorporated businesses must request written terms, and their accepted countries are not listed.
- Fees are not always a single rate: current OWU splits installment rates by payment model and number of installments, foreign currencies by charge/payout route, and FX conversion by source currency and monthly/transfer amount. Account-specific commercial terms may differ from the standard table.
- The short marketing rate for installments is less complete than the current agreement. The agreement is used for detailed prices; the marketing headline is retained as context above.
- The “50+ currencies” statement has no complete public static fee matrix beside it. Explicit PLN, EUR, CZK, GBP and USD fee rows are recorded; other currency access remains unverified.
