**Current status:** Active Czech payment gateway. GoPay is now GoPay Czech, a branch of Worldline N.V. Worldline agreed to buy a majority stake in 2020, completed a 53% acquisition that year, and GoPay’s own press archive says it became 100% part of Worldline in 2022. The current legal structure took effect on 1 April 2025. I checked GoPay’s company blog and press archive; they show current gateway products and no shutdown or sunset notice. This is the Czech/Slovak gateway at gopay.com, not the unrelated Indonesian GoPay wallet.

Scope: Online payment gateway, recurring card payments, payment links, and country-specific buyer methods. The merchant remains the seller. Public sources show a new-merchant Start/Growth/Custom offer and a separate detailed fee schedule effective 1 November 2026. Those current pages disagree on several prices. I preserve both with their stated scope rather than assuming one replaces the other. The fee schedule explicitly says all listed fees are exempt from Czech VAT (DPH); the plan page does not state tax treatment for its promotional and flat fees. No account was created and no form was submitted. No private people are named.

All 45 non-crypto fields, including optional fields, and all five crypto-conditional fields were attempted. GoPay documents Bitcoin as a gateway method, so the crypto fields apply. The GoPay help pages document seller onboarding for Czech and Slovak markets and also contemplate businesses headquartered elsewhere, but do not publish a complete accepted-seller-country list.

## Cost examples

Our calculations use the current public pages and are not a seller quote.

- **New-merchant advertised flat-rate example, CZK 1,000 card sale:** inputs are CZK 1,000 and the plan’s 0.95% rate after the free period or threshold. Formula: 0.0095 × CZK 1,000 = **CZK 9.50**. The plan page does not state DPH treatment or reconcile this rate with the detailed fee schedule.
- **Standard schedule example, CZK 1,000 consumer debit Visa sale:** inputs are the schedule’s 2.15% average total rate and CZK 3 fixed amount. Formula: 0.0215 × CZK 1,000 + CZK 3 = **CZK 24.50**. The fee schedule says its fees are exempt from DPH.
- **Standard schedule bank transfer example, CZK 1,000 payment:** inputs are 2.20% and CZK 3. Formula: 0.022 × CZK 1,000 + CZK 3 = **CZK 25.00**, before any separate plan payout charge. The fee schedule says the listed fee is exempt from DPH.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The current pricing page advertises new-merchant processing at 0.95% + CZK 0 after its free periods. The detailed fee schedule, effective 1 November 2026, shows card-category rates from 2.14% to 4.68% + CZK 3 and bank/wallet/Bitcoin at 2.20% + CZK 3. Both were live on 6 November. GoPay does not say publicly which plan or existing-account cohort the new fee schedule covers. A seller’s signed registration offer may set different account-specific rates.
