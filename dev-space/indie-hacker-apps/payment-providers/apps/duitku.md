## Service notices and scope

No provider-wide shutdown, sunset or acquisition notice was found in the official company pages and change notices checked. The current gateway offer remains available. Material retirements do apply: the October 2025 change notice deprecates plugins that are no longer actively maintained, and the September 2026 API change notice removes QRIS Gudang Voucher. Sources: [official change log](https://www.duitku.com/changelog/), [current plugin guide](https://docs.duitku.com/payment-gateway/plugin/), [current payment API](https://docs.duitku.com/api/en/) and [company page](https://www.duitku.com/en/about-us/), checked 2026-11-05.

This record covers Duitku's Indonesian fiat payment gateway, operated by PT Kharisma Catur Mandala. Ordinary merchant settlement, Instant Withdraw, early-settlement products and the separate disbursement product have different rules. A Foreign Business signup route is published, but no complete foreign seller-country list was established. Sources: [operator/privacy](https://www.duitku.com/kebijakan-privasi/), [activation guide, official indexed text; direct fetch blocked](https://docs.duitku.com/en/account/), [withdrawal guide](https://docs.duitku.com/dashboard-feature/withdraw/) and [disbursement overview](https://docs.duitku.com/disbursement-feature/overview/), checked 2026-11-05.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Refund availability:** [pricing](https://www.duitku.com/en/pricing/) and [general refund FAQ](https://learning.duitku.com/migration/bagaimana-tentang-transaksi-refund) say merchant-managed/no refund service. The [card FAQ](https://www.duitku.com/en/frequently-asked-questions/) and [direct-card API](https://docs.duitku.com/direct-credit-card/en/) explicitly support card refunds/voids. Card-specific availability is recorded; a blanket “no refunds” was rejected. [Wallet guidance](https://learning.duitku.com/migration/apakah-saya-bisa-melakukan-refund-pembayaran-e-wallet) directs the merchant to manual transfer or paid disbursement.
- **Card authentication/recurring:** [FAQ](https://www.duitku.com/en/frequently-asked-questions/) says card details/CVV/OTP are needed each time; [current token API](https://docs.duitku.com/direct-credit-card/en/) describes saved tokens and recurring charges without further authentication. Acquirer approval still matters. Old subscription fields were removed in 2025 in [V2](https://docs.duitku.com/api/en/)/[card](https://docs.duitku.com/direct-credit-card/en/); no provider-hosted subscription scheduler was established.
- **Payment minimums:** [English](https://www.duitku.com/en/frequently-asked-questions/)/[Indonesian FAQ](https://www.duitku.com/frequently-asked-questions/) list method-specific small amounts but also a generic IDR 10,000 minimum. - **Payout timing:** [English pricing](https://www.duitku.com/en/pricing/) promises under 24h after request; [Indonesian pricing](https://www.duitku.com/harga/) advertises realtime/under 1h in working hours. ## Field problems and index notes

- **F03–F06:** foreign-business eligibility, owner residency, document requirements, local bank location and available methods are different questions. The indexed account table helps with eligibility, but not every foreign exception.
- **F14/F15/F39:** reusable card tokens and removed subscription fields are not a native SaaS billing engine. Method-specific token charging must not be replaced by the FAQ's broad VA-recurring answer.
- **F20/F28/F33/F34:** aggregator card balance, direct facilitator settlement, normal withdrawal, Instant Withdraw, Instant/Advance Settlement and disbursement are distinct flows.
- **F25/F29:** VAT-inclusive processor prices do not establish seller sales-tax collection/registration/filing. The dedicated early-settlement fee does not clearly state the same VAT treatment.
- **F35:** API validation ranges, default approved merchant limits and buyer-bank limits should all be kept, with their separate scope.
- **F36/F40/F44:** settlement waiting times and privacy retention are public; the full seller reserve and exit contract is not. They cannot substitute for one another.
- **F41:** provider licence statements were attributed; the general regulator page and failed certificate lookup do not constitute independent verification.
- No definite `apps.md` row error found. “Gateway; Indonesia” fits the collection market. Foreign Business registration is a conditional route to Indonesian payment methods, not an exhaustive worldwide seller-country promise.
- No private operator/sole-trader names recorded.
