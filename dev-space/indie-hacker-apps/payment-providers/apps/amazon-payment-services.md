- Current country page and one English FAQ answer list nine seller markets. Other FAQ answers omit Bahrain or list only six. The current coverage page says nine; see F03.
- Official pricing variants disagree on Qatar local-method fees and Kuwait’s KNET setup fee. The USD-denominated plan differs by USD 0.05 per transaction across the variants and lacks an extracted country heading. See F19, F20 and F23.

- Standard pricing warns international rates may differ but does not publish those rates. A full charge-currency list, conversion markup, payout fees, minimum payout and standard reserve are also not public.
- The FAQ’s broad “1–2 weeks” settlement answer sits alongside specific UAE and KSA estimates. Actual schedule is set by the seller’s acquiring-bank agreement.
