## Conflicts and unknowns

- Current Costa Rica tariffs show installment prices that differ from a Tilopay blog comparison published in 2025. I used the current tariff and kept the older rates out of current-price conclusions. The account agreement says final fees are agreed during affiliation and may vary by bank/integration.
- Current Costa Rica public pricing does not state IVA inclusion. The settlement breakdown has separate IVA fields, but it does not establish whether the displayed CR fee includes IVA. Panama explicitly says ITBMS is additional.
- Guatemala, El Salvador and other regional signup/pricing details are not a complete public matrix. The exact El Salvador, Honduras and Nicaragua start pages could not be fetched; that is recorded as `check_failed`, not as a claim that those countries are unsupported.
- Tilopay's processing footprint across 33 markets and 47 partner banks is not a seller-country whitelist. Public readable signup documents were verified only for Costa Rica, Panama and Guatemala.
- Costa Rica terms use both 3- and 5-business-day references for dispute/review evidence. The file marks that deadline `conflicting` and does not guess which applies to a specific claim.
- Pricing says initial affiliation review takes up to 48 hours; the onboarding guide says within 48 business hours. The file records this timing as `conflicting`; extra review stages remain unpublished.
- Public payout frequency is account-specific, and the exact minimum payout, transfer fee, FX markup, refund-tax treatment and foreign-card uplift are not stated in the reviewed public pages.
- The official blog/about pages were searched for acquisition, shutdown and sunset notices; none was found. No separate newsroom page was identified in the official site navigation.

## Field problems

- F03 distinguishes seller signup from buyer reach/local processing. Tilopay's “33 markets” claim does not list which sellers can contract, so it cannot fill the signup matrix.
- F20/F23 show fixed fees in USD while percentage fees apply to local-currency transactions in some markets. The current CR tariff does not state the precise fee-conversion basis or IVA inclusion. Panama adds ITBMS explicitly.
- F29 requires separating IVA/ITBMS on Tilopay's own fee from the merchant's sales-tax duties. The published contract leaves merchant tax compliance with the seller.
- F43 split payments are confirmed as an advertised feature, but no public eligibility, flow or price documentation was found; the contract requires approval for third-party-benefit activity.
- C01–C05 are conditional crypto fields. They were attempted and marked not applicable because the terms prohibit virtual currency and no crypto product is advertised.
