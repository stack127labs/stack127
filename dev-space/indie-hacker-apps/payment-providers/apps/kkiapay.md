## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Country-specific plan pricing:** the current tariff page asks the reader to select a country, then exposes repeated plan tables without the country names in the text view. Its Mobile Money percentages and payer differ by product and table block. The separate developer fee guide gives named country rates, but those rates do not map one-to-one to the tariff blocks. I recorded each named country rate from the guide and left the plan-to-country mapping unresolved rather than guessing. Pages checked: current French tariff page, developer fee guide, homepage and official company pricing update.
- **Seller eligibility versus market availability:** the FAQ explicitly names four seller-registration countries (Benin, Togo, Côte d’Ivoire, Senegal). The homepage lists six countries where the service is available (adds Niger and Burkina Faso). The activation guide shows document lists only for Benin and Côte d’Ivoire. These sources do not establish seller eligibility for Niger or Burkina Faso.
- **Account legal form:** FAQ says an account needs a registered business, but the activation guide has an individual-consultant route in Benin. I could not reconcile the wording or confirm that consultant route in other countries.
- **Payout pricing:** Terms waive fees for partner-bank (Ecobank/UBA) payouts and charge 7,000 FCFA per other-bank payout. FAQ and developer payout guide say bank payouts cost 7,000 FCFA without repeating the exception.
- **Startup offer:** startup page advertises two free subscription years, 1.3% commission and one free withdrawal per month; its comparison tariff is older than current company-announced standard prices. Exact country, account-plan, payout and application terms are not shown.
- **Legal entity:** Terms say Open SI SAS; legal notices and privacy policy say Open SI SA, using the same company registration number. The official pages do not explain the difference.
- **Privacy retention:** privacy policy says data are retained indefinitely and never deleted, while also promising rights to deletion and forgetting. No separate retention schedule was found.
- **Current operation:** KKiaPay is actively presented in its product pages, docs, company pages and blog. The status board was reporting a partial outage at the time of check. No shutdown, sunset or acquisition announcement surfaced on the official blog, official company pages or official LinkedIn page.

## Field problems

- The pricing page’s country selector/table labels are not exposed in the text extraction. The six market tariff blocks could not be safely mapped to countries, so plan-specific monthly and transaction fees remain separated from named country guide rates.
- Seller-country, legal-form and payout requirements are written for four-country Benin/UEMOA use and do not provide one complete country-by-country eligibility matrix.
- FCFA is the published currency wording. The docs do not provide an ISO currency code or a full balance/payout-currency list.
- Several broader fields (subscription billing, tax collection, portability, digital fulfillment and crypto custody) are not described as products; absence from checked pages is recorded as not published or not applicable, not as proof of a feature prohibition unless the Terms say so.
