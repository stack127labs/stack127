## Cost examples: our calculations

All examples are **our calculations**, checked 2026-11-05. They use TWD sales amounts as ECPay's fee base. The examples exclude product taxes owed by the merchant, shipping, optional invoice/marketing services, disputes/reserves and any private contract changes. Processing tax is included where stated; a reserve is not treated as a fee. No currency conversion is performed.

Card source rule: calculate each percentage fee using the tax-inclusive rate to three decimal places; add the day's closed-order percentage fees and round that subtotal to whole TWD; then add the TWD 1 tax-inclusive processing fee per order. This means one isolated transaction and 100 transactions settled together can have slightly different effective rates. Sources: <https://www.ecpay.com.tw/Business/payment_fees>; <https://support.ecpay.com.tw/4809/> (checked 2026-11-05).

| Example and inputs | Formula | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General seller; one domestic-card TWD 1,000 order, only closed card order in that daily batch | Percentage fee = 1,000 × 2.75% × 1.05 = 28.875; daily rounded fee = 29; processing = 1 | Total processing TWD 30; ECPay balance credit TWD 970; effective 3.0% before withdrawal |
| General seller; 100 domestic-card orders of TWD 1,000 in one daily closing batch; gross TWD 100,000 | round(100 × 28.875) + 100 × 1 = 2,888 + 100 | Total processing TWD 2,988; balance credit TWD 97,012; effective 2.988% |
| Same 100-order batch, one subsequent bank withdrawal | SinoPac: 97,012 − 0; interbank: 97,012 − 15 | Bank receipts TWD 97,012 or TWD 96,997; total fees TWD 2,988 or TWD 3,003 (2.988% or 3.003%) |
| Contract seller; one foreign-card TWD 1,000 order, isolated daily batch; quoted rate within published 3.5% to 3.8% Chinese range | Low: round(1,000 × 3.5% × 1.05) + 1 = 38; high: round(1,000 × 3.8% × 1.05) + 1 = 41 | Processing TWD 38 to 41; balance credit TWD 962 to 959; effective 3.8% to 4.1%, excluding contract fixed costs |

## Field problems and apps.md row review

- **F01/F02/F12/F18:** ECPay collection, bank gateway, partner wallets, ECPay Pay, ECShop, terminals and ECTicket share branding but differ in agreements, methods and settlement. A single account or group logo cannot make their fees interchangeable.

- **F14/F15/F24:** fixed scheduled cards, variable merchant-initiated bound-card charges, shopper installments and payment reminders are separate capabilities. None alone establishes metering, proration or full subscription management.

The limited 1.68% offer and the English/Chinese Zingala mismatch prevent a single unqualified headline rate.

- **F33/F34/F35/F36/F44:** payment notification, trust settlement, ECPay balance credit and bank withdrawal are separate stages. Automatic sweep amounts are not minimum payouts; withheld funds are not fees.
- **F39/F40:** a discontinued integration/shipping route or app-function pause does not mean the payment provider closed. Records with transactions may be deactivated while data remains for at least five years.
