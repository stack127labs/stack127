**Scope and current status:** Frisbii sells subscription billing software and a payment gateway/checkout layer. It is not a merchant of record: the seller remains Frisbii's business customer, and acquiring banks, PPRO, or other payment providers carry out the actual payment processing under separate contracts. Their processing fees are separate from Frisbii's software and gateway charges.

The current plans page shows EUR monthly prices and says VAT is added. It has no currency or billing-period selector; its FAQ says discounts for longer commitments are discussed with sales. The German plan page shows the same EUR prices. A separate gateway page still lists a €19/month gateway license, while the newer plans page describes standalone Pay at €0.08 per transaction and does not show that monthly license. I leave that monthly charge conflicting.

Frisbii's documented account-acquiring footprint is EU27/EEA, subject to the selected acquirer and its approval. That does not mean every payment method or acquirer is available in every country. The current fee sheet is denominated mainly in EUR, with a SEK fee for Swish; it does not show DKK subscription plan prices.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Standalone gateway monthly license:** the recurring-billing price page says Pay standalone costs €0.08 per transaction plus method fees. The separate Payment Gateway Pricing page says €19/month plus €0.08 per transaction. Both pages are official and accessible; the applicable contract price is unresolved. The transaction-based fee is corroborated by the 2026 fee PDF.
- **PSP/acquirer costs:** the fee schedule was updated 22 April 2026. It lists method/card fees but says individual acquirer contracts and processing fees govern. PPRO/direct-provider fees may vary by merchant contract. The sheet does not state VAT treatment for those method fees.
- **Seller availability:** accepted-account help says EU27/EEA acquiring coverage, but account opening, legal form and method approval still depend on a selected acquirer/provider. Broader acquirer geography is not treated as Frisbii seller availability.
- **Payout schedule:** current docs make settlement dependent on provider contract. A legacy Frisbii Transform FAQ says 2–3 days, but it is not a general current Billing/Pay promise.
- **Pages checked for unpublished answers:** current pricing and gateway pricing, terms, accepted-country article, Frisbii Billing and Pay docs, acquirer-application guidance, fee sheet, refund/chargeback help, hosted-page/API/webhook docs, status page, blog and newsroom.
- **News/rebrand check:** official blog/newsroom and company help confirm the 2021–2025 history above and the 2025 Infer acquisition. Current pages show active product/status and no shutdown or general product-sunset notice.

## Field problems

- F03 account-country scope is described through acquiring-bank availability rather than a country-by-country merchant signup matrix. Recorded as EU27/EEA with acquirer-approval condition.
- F04 sole-trader eligibility is provider-specific: PPRO expressly excludes sole traders, but this does not establish a Frisbii-wide ban for every method.
- F19 the dedicated payment-gateway page and newer recurring-billing page disagree about a €19 monthly standalone gateway license.
- F20–F23 the rate sheet does not state whether payment-method/acquirer fees include VAT or whether a negotiated PSP contract replaces every listed amount.
- F31–F36 settlement funds and reserve/limit decisions are controlled by external providers; Frisbii does not publish one universal payout profile.
- F41 Frisbii is a software/gateway layer and its supported acquiring partners are external; a Frisbii-specific payment institution licence was not established by the reviewed pages.
