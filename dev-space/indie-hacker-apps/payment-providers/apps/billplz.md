## Service notices checked first

- **Active Malaysian gateway; no whole-service shutdown, sunset, replacement-brand or acquisition notice found** in the official company, pricing and help pages or targeted official-site searches. Billplz identifies itself as part of Qapital. <https://main.billplz.com/about>; <https://main.billplz.com/>
- **Upcoming wallet deadline: 12 November 2026.** Existing GrabPay, ShopeePay, Touch 'n Go or Boost collections must switch to DuitNow QR. Unmigrated collections lose wallet acceptance until changed; other methods are unaffected. Existing-wallet migration is free and per collection. This is a channel migration, not a Billplz shutdown. <https://support.billplz.com/guide/e-wallet-migration>
- **Card migration completed: 29 August 2026.** The guide says Billplz now processes Visa/Mastercard directly. Merchants who missed enabling Billplz Card on their collections can still do so; legacy transactions retain their old agreements. <https://support.billplz.com/guide/visa-and-mastercard-migration>
- **Cash and fee caveats:** minimum payout, Friday processing and credit-funding rules differ between sources. New card/QR assessment charges also qualify the no-setup-fees claim. These are retained below. <https://main.billplz.com/terms#member-payment-methods>; <https://main.billplz.com/terms#member-billplz-fee>; <https://support.billplz.com/guide/payout-policy>; <https://support.billplz.com/guide/pricing>

Scope: Billplz online collections, Auto-Deduct, automatic Malaysian-bank payouts and Payment Order. Related Catalog features are labelled separately. Basic/Standard public rates are not Enterprise quotes. KYB means business verification; SSM is the Malaysian business registry, ROS the societies registry, FPX online bank payment, and SST tax on the provider's service.

## Cost examples — our calculations

Checked 2026-11-05. These are MYR calculations for a Malaysian merchant using published Basic/Standard prices and 8% SST on the provider fee. They show known charges, not a promise of an all-in cost: activation, optional add-ons, disputed payments, separately quoted terms and unpublished bank/payout charges are excluded unless named. No currency conversion or silent rounding is used.

Inputs: method/plan rates from <https://main.billplz.com/pricing>; fee taxation and deduction rules from <https://support.billplz.com/guide/pricing>; retained FPX fee and credit rules from <https://main.billplz.com/terms#member-billplz-fee>. Standard is an additional annual purchase.

| Basic FPX B2C, customer pays MYR20 | Fee = 1.25 × 1.08; fee/payment = 1.35 ÷ 20 × 100 | MYR1.35 known fee, 6.75% of sale. MYR20 enters automatic payout before any credit auto-reload; the MYR1.35 comes from Credit Balance. |
| Standard FPX B2C, customer pays MYR20 | Fee = 0.75 × 1.08; fee/payment = 0.81 ÷ 20 × 100 | MYR0.81 known fee, 4.05% of sale, before allocating the annual membership. |

## Field problems and directory-row review

- F20/F21/F11: “MYR/non-MYR” pricing labels can look like charge currencies. The card guide instead describes local/foreign card origin; the API explicitly charges only MYR. These scopes are kept separate.
- F28/F31/F33/F35: automatic merchant payout, outgoing Payment Order, fee Credit Balance and Payment Order Limit are different functions. The same “payout” or “limit” wording cannot be carried across them.
- F14/F15/F24: tokenized card collection does not supply a renewal scheduler. The merchant operates the schedule; published Auto-Deduct rates are complete rates, not additions to one-time processing.
- F41: the security page distinguishes the payment environment's own PCI statement from cloud-inherited ISO/SOC standards. Provider claims are recorded with their scope, not converted into an independent trust score.
- C01–C05: these conditional crypto fields do not apply to the MYR gateway. The Appendix also prohibits crypto/NFT businesses.
- No clear apps.md row error was found. Its gateway/Malaysia/active classification matches the offer; the detailed record adds current migration restrictions. Regional coverage is not a claim that businesses throughout Southeast Asia can sign up.
