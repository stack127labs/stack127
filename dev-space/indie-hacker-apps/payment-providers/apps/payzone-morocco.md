# Payzone Morocco

- Official URL: [Payzone Morocco](https://payzone.ma/)
- `apps.md` row: active; gateway; Middle East and North Africa; Morocco. The URL is the Moroccan Payzone site, not the UK or Irish businesses with similar names.
- Product researched: Payzone E-Com for online card checkout, payment by email/link, recurring card payments, and MOTO. Payzone Cash is mentioned only as a separate online-order cash-payment option; physical POS hardware is outside scope.
- Current operator: Vantage Payment Systems (VPS), a Moroccan payment institution. Payzone is its payment-services brand.
- Current owner: VPS's official 2026-10-23 update says Equity Invest now owns 100% of VPS after buying Cross Switch's stake. FIVE, an AfricInvest investment vehicle, invested in Equity Invest as part of the transaction; the source does not state FIVE's stake in Equity Invest.
- Rebrand, acquisition, and regulatory notes: VPS and Payzone received refreshed brand identities in March–May 2025; Payzone continued as VPS's service brand. VPS was approved by Bank Al-Maghrib as a payment institution on 2024-02-09. VPS reported the Cross Switch exit and Equity Invest's 100% ownership on 2026-10-23. The official company and product news pages were checked for shutdown or enforcement notices; current product, contact, documentation, and merchant-access pages remain live. No shutdown notice was found.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Fees:** The FAQ says the structure includes a transaction commission, discounts by volume, and optional premium services, then directs merchants to request an individualized quote. I found no public numeric MAD fee table, VAT label, or pricing switch. No fee was inferred as zero.
- **Payout timing:** An official back-office guide states bank credit 48 hours after sale through CMI. Current product and company materials do not restate that schedule, and the service and ownership have changed since the guide; current timing and settlement partner remain unclear.
- **Seller eligibility:** The public checklist supports Moroccan companies, auto-entrepreneurs, associations, and cooperatives. It accepts passports for non-resident foreign responsible persons in a company file, but does not say foreign-incorporated or non-resident sellers can contract.
- **Card methods:** Current merchant FAQ confirms Visa and Mastercard and says Amex needs a specific contract. A separate SaaS page also mentions PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers, but public E-Com docs do not explain how those methods are activated, so they remain unclear.
- **Merchant terms:** I checked the public website terms, FAQ, documentation page, and product pages. The accessible terms govern website use; no public merchant acquiring contract, chargeback policy, hold schedule, account-closure terms, or post-closure payout rule was found.
