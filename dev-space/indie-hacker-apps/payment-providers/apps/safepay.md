## Cost examples: our calculation

These examples use the public Pakistan Standard prices, excluding taxes, withholding, FX, refunds, disputes and unpriced bank charges. Rs denotes Pakistani rupees here; no currency conversion is performed. Foreign-card examples below are charged in PKR, so they do not assume an FX rate. Fee sources: https://safepay.pk/pricing; https://safepay.helpscoutdocs.com/article/131-safepay-fee-breakdown; tax exclusions and conversion basis: https://safepay.pk/legal/content/services-agreement (checked 2026-11-05).

| Scenario | Inputs | Formula: our calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small domestic card sale | PKR 100 successful charge; Standard 2.9% + Rs 30 | 100 × 0.029 + 30 | Rs 32.90 processing; 32.90% of gross; Rs 67.10 remains before taxes/other deductions |
| Domestic card sale/subscription renewal | PKR 1,000 successful Standard domestic card charge; no subscription surcharge | 1,000 × 0.029 + 30 | Rs 59 processing; 5.9% of gross; Rs 941 before taxes/other deductions |
| International card, PKR charge | PKR 1,000; Standard 3.2% + Rs 30 | 1,000 × (0.029 + 0.003) + 30 | Rs 62 processing; 6.2% of gross; excludes any issuer charge |
| Local wallet headline | PKR 1,000 successfully charged under published Wallets 1.5% category | 1,000 × 0.015 | Rs 15 published percentage component; no unsupported assumption about every individual wallet's price |
| Raast headline maximum | PKR 1,000 successful charge; up to 1.5% | 1,000 × 0.015 | Up to Rs 15 for the published percentage component; actual rate may be lower and other unpriced conditions remain |
| Standard monthly volume | 100 successful domestic-card charges × PKR 1,000; no setup/monthly fee | 100 × (1,000 × 0.029 + 30) | Rs 5,900 published processing charges on PKR 100,000 gross |
| Instant payout | PKR 10,000 eligible Instant Payout volume; 1.5% | 10,000 × 0.015 | Rs 150 optional payout fee, separate from prior payment processing |
| Routing with ecommerce plugin | Rs 15,000/month; 100 successful plugin charges × PKR 1,000; +0.35% plugin fee | 15,000 + (100 × 1,000 × 0.0035) | Rs 15,350 known Safepay components plus primary gateway MDR, taxes and other contract costs; no subscription entitlement |

A full refund of the PKR 1,000 domestic example returns PKR 1,000 to the buyer. Our calculation leaves Rs 59 original processing cost, plus its applicable taxes, with the merchant; this is not an additional Rs 59 refund fee. The original payment charge is counted once. Source: https://safepay.helpscoutdocs.com/article/174-what-happens-to-safepay-charges-when-i-issue-a-refund (effective 2026-08-01; checked 2026-11-05).

Final bank receipts cannot be calculated universally. The settlement-file guide describes province-dependent sales tax on fees and withholding fields, while exemptions, merchant classification and applicable rules can alter those deductions. Those tax deductions are not silently bundled into the processing percentages above. Sources: https://safepay-docs.netlify.app/setup-safepay/operations/settlement-file/; https://safepay.pk/legal/content/services-agreement (checked 2026-11-05).

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Bank-certificate freshness differs.** The document checklist updated 27 October 2026 allows certificates up to three months old; the bank guide updated 23 January 2023 says four weeks. Both were read. The more recent checklist explicitly includes unregistered proprietors and freelancers, but the earlier instruction is still publicly linked. Sources: https://safepay.helpscoutdocs.com/article/152-list-of-documents-you-need-to-provide-safepay; https://safepay.helpscoutdocs.com/article/83-what-bank-account-to-link-to-safepay (checked 2026-11-05).
- **Refund timing differs across dated sources.** The older card refund guide says dashboard refunds within 60 days. The agreement, updated 7 October 2025, says 90 calendar days in the dashboard, then written request before 120 days. It disclaims responsibility after 120 days. The fee-specific notice effective 1 August 2026 confirms original charges/taxes remain and no separate refund fee. Keep the processing window and fee rule separate. Sources: https://safepay.helpscoutdocs.com/article/141-how-to-refund-car-new; https://safepay.pk/legal/content/services-agreement; https://safepay.helpscoutdocs.com/article/174-what-happens-to-safepay-charges-when-i-issue-a-refund (checked 2026-11-05).
- **Standard bank refunds and Raastwire are separate.** Ordinary direct-bank refunds remain the merchant's responsibility unless agreed otherwise; the older guide says the dashboard bank-refund flow is accounting only. Approved Raastwire aggregators have an asynchronous refund API for specified reasons. This is not evidence that any Standard merchant may automate every bank refund. No acceptance or subscription-method matrix confirms SGD or all seven currencies on every method. Read Money, reporting, plan and contractual currency sections; settlement itself is PKR at the acquiring bank's transaction-date rate.
