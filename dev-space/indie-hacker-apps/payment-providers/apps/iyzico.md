**Current status:** iyzico remains an active Turkish payment provider under its existing brand. It was acquired by PayU in 2019 and is part of the Prosus group. iyzico completed its own acquisition of Paynet in February 2025. I checked iyzico’s official blog and press archive for acquisition, shutdown, or rebrand notices; I found no shutdown or rebrand notice. The Central Bank of Türkiye register still lists iyzi Ödeme ve Elektronik Para Hizmetleri A.Ş.

Scope: online Virtual POS, payment links, subscriptions, iyzico checkout, and marketplace split payments. Physical POS, mobile POS, and consumer wallet products are separate offers and are not included in this comparison. The virtual POS page shows %4.29 plus ₺0.25 for monthly sales over ₺20,000 and advertises next-day payout. I checked the current Virtual POS, Link, subscription, and Help Centre pages for plan, period, or currency selectors; no selector was visible. The public offer is priced in TRY. Terms can still vary by seller and contract.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Payout timing:** the Virtual POS page advertises next-day payment. The Help Centre gives a following-Wednesday schedule for companies and a first-Wednesday-after-seven-days schedule for individuals, but also describes a 3-week rolling hold. The contract says the agreed contractual term applies. I left the general schedule conflicting because the public pages do not explain when each applies.
- **Taxes on iyzico’s fees:** the corporate 4.29% rate is explicitly marked BSMV-inclusive. I found no public statement confirming whether KDV is included in processing, Link, subscription, or add-on prices. The terms permit statutory taxes but do not settle the displayed-price treatment.
- **Foreign sellers:** official pages describe Turkish onboarding and some foreign buyer payment options, but I found no seller-country list or clear statement on foreign-incorporated businesses. I checked the Virtual POS page, Help Centre, developer docs, and terms.
- **International cards and conversion:** foreign-card activation may depend on the account. The Multi Currency fee for annual use is ₺99, but the separate fee on converted payouts is not quantified.
- **No minimum payout or universal reserve percentage:** I checked the Help Centre and general terms. The public 3-week hold description is not a universal reserve amount or a promise for every account.
- **Pricing controls:** I checked Virtual POS, Link, Subscription, and Help Centre pricing for currency, plan, and billing-period selectors. No selector was visible. The Virtual POS rate is explicitly qualified for monthly sales above ₺20,000, and individual Link pricing says “starting at.”

## Field problems

- **F03/F05:** iyzico describes domestic Turkish onboarding but does not publish a foreign-seller or foreign-bank eligibility list. Buyer acceptance from abroad is a separate fact.
- **F20/F25:** BSMV inclusion is stated for the corporate rate only. KDV treatment is not stated for the displayed processing and add-on prices.
- **F33:** the marketing claim, seller-type FAQ schedules, rolling-hold explanation, and contract’s account-specific maturity do not reduce to one universal payout answer.
- **F35:** the public developer error page gives an amount threshold without a clear currency context. It is not safe to present it as a TRY-wide transaction cap.
- All other fields were applicable or conditionally attempted. The crypto fields are not applicable because iyzico does not offer a crypto checkout product in the official pages checked.
