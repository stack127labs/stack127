## Cost examples

These are **our calculations**, checked 2026-11-05. They use only the public Stax Pay membership, per-payment and ACH components. They are not full processing quotes. The $ notation is preserved; the common pricing page does not supply a separately labelled Canadian schedule. Source for all inputs below: https://staxpayments.com/pricing/.

| Scenario | Inputs and conditions | Formula | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Online sales at $100 each | 100 payments/month × $100 = $10,000/month; constant annual volume $120,000, within the $99/month entry tier; all online/keyed payments. | Published membership/transaction component = $99 + (100 × $0.15). Component percentage = $114 ÷ $10,000 × 100. | $114/month; 1.14% of sales, **before interchange, network and other applicable charges**. |
| Small online sales at $10 each | 1,000 payments/month × $10 = the same $10,000/month; constant annual volume $120,000; same published tier. | Published membership/transaction component = $99 + (1,000 × $0.15). Component percentage = $249 ÷ $10,000 × 100. | $249/month; 2.49% of sales, **before interchange, network and other applicable charges**. |
| One $100 ACH payment | ACH enabled; count only its published variable collection fee. | min($100 × 1%, $10). | $1 ACH collection component; membership/other applicable fees remain separate. |
| One $2,500 ACH payment | Same ACH fee; apply the cap explicitly. | min($2,500 × 1%, $10). | $10 ACH collection component; 0.4% of the payment before allocating membership/other costs. |

The card examples deliberately leave interchange, network assessments, applicable card-category charges, returns/disputes, optional services, hardware, funding charges and fee taxes outside the numerical component.
