## Status and product scope

MoonClerk is an active recurring payment forms service that runs on Stripe. Merchants need their own Stripe account, and Stripe handles payments, refunds, and payouts. Stripe processing fees are separate from MoonClerk's monthly software fee. The current MoonClerk pricing page uses monthly payment-volume tiers. It shows a volume slider, not an annual billing switch.

The current MoonClerk pages and terms are dated 2026. Its blog and company pages show a 2026 redesign and current product activity. I checked the company site, About page, terms, blog, and searched the official site/blog for acquisition, shutdown, and sunset notices; I found none. The About page says MoonClerk has been independent since it started in 2012. No private people's names are recorded.
