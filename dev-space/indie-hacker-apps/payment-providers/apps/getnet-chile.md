## Conflicts and unknowns

- Online numeric rates: I checked Web Checkout, payment-link details, the official tariff selector, simulator and tariff FAQ. The selector and simulator expose dynamic placeholders in the public rendering, while the FAQ says rates vary by merchant and method and references a 2022 effective schedule. Exact current domestic and international online rates remain not published in the sources I could read.
- Non-Santander payout timing: the onboarding page says two business days and the non-client FAQ says 72 hours. Those units are not equivalent, so both are retained as a conflict.
- Public agreement version: the detailed affiliation contract is dated March 2021 and the separate portal contract is older. Terms can differ in a seller's later application or addenda. The 2021 agreement's cancellation and dispute thresholds are recorded with that date caveat.
- The agreement's 0.02% dispute threshold uses both amount and operation count, but the public wording does not settle how to calculate the trigger or the tariff above it.
- An older FAQ referred to a Santander-only presale, while current onboarding provides a non-Santander route. I used the current onboarding page for bank eligibility.
- Ownership: Getnet's corporate page rounds the stake; the March 2026 interim financial report gives exact 50.01% and 49.99% figures.
- Online product pages do not publish a complete seller-country matrix, legal-form matrix, buyer-country blocklist, supported charge-currency list, product-category approvals, charge limits, or payout minimum.
- Exact post-closure payout timing, online processing-fee refund treatment, token portability and PCI certificate scope were not published in the pages checked.

## Field problems

- F20 and F21 online tariff selectors depend on dynamic values that were not exposed in the browser/text rendering; terminal rates are available but must not be substituted for online rates.
- The public contract is a March 2021 version. Its cancellation, dispute and termination terms are useful evidence but may be superseded for an individual merchant by later documents.
- F33 uses different time units for non-Santander payout: “two business days” versus “72 hours.”
- The public contract's 0.02% dispute threshold is not operationally clear, and its excess tariff is not numerically published.
- The site uses Chilean legal and tax terms. “Natural person with business activity” is not the same as unrestricted individual signup.
