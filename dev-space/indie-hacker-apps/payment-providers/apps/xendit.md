## Scope and reading notes

This covers Xendit's public payment-acceptance offer and the connected billing, withdrawal and platform features that affect a solo seller. Eight payment markets appear in the current pricing table: Indonesia, Philippines, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Singapore, Hong Kong and Mexico. Mexico has preview/pilot restrictions. A priced collection market, an incorporation country, a permitted buyer country and a payout destination are different things. [Market pricing](https://www.xendit.co/en/pricing/) · [Account activation](https://docs.xendit.co/docs/create-account.md) · [Mexico preview](https://docs.xendit.co/docs/payout-coverage-mexico.md).

The public schedule separates the payment-method/payout fee from Xendit's fixed processing fee. Both components stack. The processing fee also applies to failed attempts. Published prices generally exclude service-fee taxes; explicit exceptions and Malaysian component-specific tax treatment are retained below. Contract rates may differ. [Pricing](https://www.xendit.co/en/pricing/) · [Fee application](https://docs.xendit.co/docs/transaction-fees.md) · [Fee taxes](https://docs.xendit.co/docs/value-added-tax-vat.md).

The global agreement, service terms, all eight territory amendments and privacy pages were inspected. Local/legacy agreements are separately listed on the legal hub; this record does not claim the global agreement overrides every existing merchant's signed contract. Territory amendments inspected carry version 2026.04, modified 18 September 2026 where stated. [Global agreement](https://www.xendit.co/en/xendit-service-agreement/) · [Service terms](https://www.xendit.co/en/legal-regional-product/) · [Privacy entities](https://www.xendit.co/en/global-privacy-policy/).

## Cost examples — our calculation

Checked 2026-11-05. These are collection-fee calculations for one successful online domestic card attempt, with no FX, billing, Shopify or other optional product. Inputs come from [the standard pricing table](https://www.xendit.co/en/pricing/) and [the fee-tax documentation](https://docs.xendit.co/docs/value-added-tax-vat.md). The final column is sale value less the stated collection fees; it is **not a promise of withdrawable cash**. Monthly minimums, reserves and any withdrawal costs still matter. No currency conversion is performed.

Malaysia is shown with three decimal places because that is the exact arithmetic result. No assumption about the provider's per-component or invoice rounding is made. The Vietnam amount is within the printed VND 100,000 card ceiling; the low ceiling is not silently corrected. Charge-limit and multi-currency ambiguities remain as recorded in F35.

Two additional examples show why a headline percentage is insufficient:

- **Singapore monthly minimum — our calculation:** after the first transacting month, assume ten successful SGD 100 domestic card payments and no other qualifying fees, disputes, retries or add-ons. Collection fees = 10 × SGD 4.10 = SGD 41. The published SGD 70 minimum gives a top-up of max(70 − 41, 0) = **SGD 29**, so total monthly fees are **SGD 70**, leaving **SGD 930 before reserves and any withdrawal cost** from SGD 1,000 sales. This uses the published local minimum, not a USD conversion. [Minimum-fee schedule](https://help.xendit.co/hc/en-us/articles/59516240127129-Xendit-Pricing-Policy) · [Top-up rule and first-month exception](https://docs.xendit.co/docs/transaction-fees.md).
- **Indonesia full refund — our calculation:** refund the IDR 100,000 ordinary-card sale above, with one successful refund attempt and no other charges. Original collection fees retained = IDR 9,879. Additional processing = IDR 4,000 × 1.11 = IDR 4,440. Total fee burden = **IDR 14,319**, in addition to returning the IDR 100,000 principal. This uses the October 2026 refund policy, not the older no-additional-fee dispute guide. [Current refund rule](https://help.xendit.co/hc/en-us/articles/59516240127129-Xendit-Pricing-Policy) · [Fee VAT](https://docs.xendit.co/docs/value-added-tax-vat.md).

A converted-payment total, seller-withdrawal net total and new-version subscription total are not calculated because necessary rates or applicability remain unresolved. Future Shopify and XenPlatform fees are not added to current ordinary-card examples.
