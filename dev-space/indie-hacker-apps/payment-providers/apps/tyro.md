Material service changes and sunsets:
- Tyro's notice says Tyro-managed surcharging on eftpos, Mastercard, Visa and Amex was removed on 1 October 2026. External POS settings need separate attention. Source: https://www.tyro.com/blog/rba-surcharge-ban-confirmed-what-your-business-needs-to-know/. Checked: 2026-11-05.
- In-person Android Tap to Pay needs an MPoC-compliant route for new merchants from 26 August 2026. Existing merchants on the older embedded route can continue until 31 December 2027, subject to six-monthly SDK maintenance. This is not a Pay Online shutdown. Source: https://docs.connect.tyro.com/docs/in-person-payments/tap-to-pay/mpoc-pci-upgrade. Checked: 2026-11-05.
- Support also retains a notice about Telstra's 28 October 2024 3G shutdown affecting older Tyro terminal SIMs. That hardware notice is separate from online checkout. Source: https://www.tyro.com/support/. Checked: 2026-11-05.
