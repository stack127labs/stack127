- **Current status / owner notice:** PayTR is operating and accepting applications. The latest ownership table found is the 2023 audited report: Actera-associated Financial Technologies S.à r.l. held 60%; an individual held the remaining 40% (name omitted). PayTR news in 2025 still described the Actera partnership, but no later share table was found. No official acquisition-after-2021, shutdown, or sunset notice was found in the company newsroom or blog pages checked.
- **Price-page check:** The current Virtual POS page showed a starting rate and quote form. I saw no seller-country, plan, currency, or billing-period selector. The final price and payout period are given in the seller-specific offer and Merchant Panel.
- **Scope:** Turkish and Northern Cyprus sellers using PayTR online payment products. PayTR is the payment provider; the merchant sells to the customer and remains responsible for delivery and sales taxes.
