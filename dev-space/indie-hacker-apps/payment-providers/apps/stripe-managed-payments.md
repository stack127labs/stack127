## Field problems

- F02 needs room for a merchant-of-record/agency arrangement in which the product seller remains seller of record. A single reseller yes/no would lose this contractual distinction.

- F03/F10/F29 must keep 37 seller locations, buyer restrictions and tax-coverage countries separate. Domestic tax exceptions depend on both seller and buyer location and, in Singapore, the buyer's business status.

- F11/F12/F14/F15 need service-specific controls: standard Stripe or Link features, generic Checkout parameters and ordinary Billing examples do not automatically prove Managed Payments compatibility. The remaining uncertain features are named rather than inferred.

- F20 to F28 require seller-country examples and separate fee stages. This file records UK/Ireland standard card pricing and selected local-method rates, not every country or negotiated plan. Adaptive Pricing's buyer FX charge, seller balance conversion, non-primary settlement and payout fees must not be counted twice.

- F32/F34 can depend on a dynamic currency/bank table omitted from the public Markdown response. Standard UK/Ireland/US payout minimums are recorded as scoped examples; other-country and extra-currency thresholds are not silently generalized.

- F35 technical currency/API limits are not a guaranteed account limit or approved order maximum. F33/F44/F45 similarly need distinct balance availability, payout, reserve and verification stages.

- F37 must separate report exports from credential portability. Stripe excludes Link-saved credentials from exports; a subscription object or contractual exit right does not guarantee portable payment consent.

- No field was dropped as not worth collecting. All 40 core fields, five optional fields and five crypto-applicability fields were attempted.
