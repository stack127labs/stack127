**Current status and recent changes:** WooPayments is an active online payment gateway from Woo, built in partnership with Stripe. It only works with a WooCommerce store. The WooCommerce core plugin and WooPayments plugin are free; hosting, domain, and optional extensions are separate costs. Stripe supplies the underlying processing and creates a connected Stripe Express account during onboarding. The seller remains responsible for the goods, customer, tax obligations, refunds, and disputes.

Recent official updates checked: the plugin changelog lists version 11.2.0 (2026-10-08), including Onelink availability in Great Britain; a WooPayments security update advisory dated 2026-11-03 says to use 11.2.1 or the fixed release for the installed release line. Przelewy24 (P24) was closed to new activations on 2026-08-17, and its remaining users must meet additional company and tax-ID rules. iDEAL was relabelled iDEAL / Wero on 2026-03-31. WooCommerce announced seven new countries for in-person card-present payments on 2026-05-21; these are not new online seller countries. The fee page has country jump links and country-by-country rates; it has no subscription billing-period switch because pricing is pay as you go. (Sources: [WooPayments product page](https://woocommerce.com/payments/); [plugin changelog](https://wordpress.org/plugins/woocommerce-payments/); [local payment methods and P24 change](https://woocommerce.com/document/woopayments/payment-methods/local-payment-methods/); [iDEAL / Wero update](https://woocommerce.com/product-update/more-payment-improvements/); [in-person country update](https://woocommerce.com/posts/in-person-payments-point-of-sale-expansion/); [startup guide and security advisory](https://woocommerce.com/document/woopayments/startup-guide/).)

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Country-based card fees:** The pricing page has a list of country links and a separate current fee section for each supported market. I grouped markets only where the card rate and add-on pattern match. Local method and BNPL fees still vary within the same region.
- **International cards in Europe:** WooPayments defines “European cards” using a list of card-issuing countries. A card from outside that list triggers the international add-on for affected European account markets; the rule is not limited to EU/EEA membership.
- **Local payment method changes:** The main method list labels P24 as legacy; its dedicated policy says it cannot be newly activated after 2026-08-17 and restricts continuing accounts. The dedicated current policy controls availability.
- **Currencies shown with `$`:** Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, and the US fee sections use the dollar sign. I used the fee section's seller market together with the official currency list to name AUD, NZD, SGD, and USD; the rates are not converted.
- **Recent news:** The May 2026 new-country announcement is about card-present readers and WooCommerce POS; it does not expand online account availability. No official online fee change or online seller-country expansion was identified in the current product-update archive checked.
- **Unpublished values:** The pricing page, startup guide, country list, KYC and policy pages, payout docs, and terms were checked for a universal business-size minimum, standard approval time, standard payout fee, product-wide licence statement, and a fixed reserve percentage/duration. They do not publish one general value for those items.

## Field problems

- **F20–F23 country and payment-method fees:** WooPayments publishes a long fee table with a country selector. Card rates are grouped by identical published pattern; BNPL and several local methods have country-specific rates. Processing-tax treatment is separate from the quoted base rate.
- **F03–F05 seller eligibility:** Eligibility is by registered business country, with specific territory and local legal-form restrictions. The page does not provide one global bank-country rule independent of Stripe's local account requirements.
- **F14–F15 subscriptions:** Native WooPayments subscriptions are retired. Recurring billing depends on a separate WooCommerce Subscriptions extension; Stripe Billing has a distinct US surcharge.
- **F39 release history:** The latest public WordPress.org release entry and a later security advisory describe different version numbers; the advisory is the current security instruction, while the plugin changelog page is used for published release history.
