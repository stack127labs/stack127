**Product and change notice (checked 2026-11-07):** This file covers Amazon Pay checkout on third-party merchant sites, including the local Japanese and Indian merchant offers. Amazon Pay remains active on its product, help, developer, and merchant-registration pages. Amazon’s business blog and newsroom searches did not find a notice closing the whole merchant service in a country. The current standard merchant FAQ lists the US, UK, 15 other European countries, and Japan; India has a separate local merchant integration and onboarding documentation. A country selector on the EU fee page still shows India even though the standard FAQ’s eligibility list omits it, so treat India only under its separate India-specific product. Amazon’s old third-party Shopify integration ended on 2025-01-06; the replacement Amazon Pay with Shopify Payments is currently limited to US sellers charging USD. Japan’s Amazon Pay help says using Amazon Pay to pay national tax bills ended on 2026-01-03. Amazon’s Checkout v2 page says Checkout v1 ended on 2026-03-31, while its linked help page says v1 remains supported throughout 2026. These are product or integration changes, not evidence that Amazon Pay closed across those countries.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Checkout v1 sunset:** Amazon’s EU upgrade page says discontinuation was 2026-03-31, while its help page says v1 stays supported throughout 2026. Both were current official pages checked 2026-11-07. The legacy version’s actual service status is unresolved.
- **EU/UK cross-border fee:** the fee schedule presents two separate tables under the same Cross Border Fee heading, with different region groupings and rates. It does not explain which schedule applies to a given account. The applicable surcharge is therefore conflicting.
- **France authorization fee:** the French schedule table shows EUR 0.25 while accompanying prose/example shows EUR 0.35. Left unresolved.
- **US payout arrival:** the detailed Getting Paid page says ACH typically takes 3–5 business days after settlement, while a welcome FAQ says 1–2 business days. The detailed payout page is recorded, with the shorter claim retained as a conflict.
- **India:** the general merchant country FAQ omits India, but a country selector on the EU fee help page still lists India and Amazon publishes separate India merchant APIs and onboarding. India is recorded only as a separate local offer; its fees and complete seller-form/currency/payout rules are not published in the pages checked.
- **Market shutdowns:** Amazon Pay’s official business blog, Amazon newsroom, product FAQs, and country pages were searched. Japan national-tax payments ended on 2026-01-03; this is a narrower local use case retirement, while Japanese merchant checkout remains available. No whole-country closure announcement for the current merchant checkout was found. The confirmed changes are Shopify’s legacy integration retirement (2025-01-06) and the conflicting Checkout v1/v2 transition dates. Consumer products or Amazon Payment Services were not treated as this offer.
- **VAT/GST:** European processing rates use the transaction amount including VAT, shipping and minus discounts. The EU dispute fee is explicitly excluding VAT; general VAT treatment of all other Amazon fee lines is not fully stated. Japanese provider-fee consumption-tax treatment was not found.
- **Buyer-country list:** Amazon does not publish one buyer-country allowlist across all account regions and payment methods. Local method, issuer, sanctions, and account rules can narrow acceptance.
- **Sources checked for not-published values:** merchant FAQ, regional pricing, registration and verification guides, local India onboarding/API docs, Japan help, payout/settlement, integration docs, and terms/policies are listed on the relevant rows.
