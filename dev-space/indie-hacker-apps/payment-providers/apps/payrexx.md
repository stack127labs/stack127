Scope: Payrexx's online payment gateway and platform, with Swiss Payrexx Pay and European Payrexx Pay Plus routes separated. The company also offers in-person terminals and Tap to Pay; their hardware and in-person rates are outside the online fee rows below.

Current operator and status: PAYREXX AG (Thun, Switzerland) operates Payrexx. Payrexx Europe AG in Liechtenstein is a wholly owned subsidiary for EEA market entry and promotion. Payrexx is linked to TWINT as an accepted payment method and offers TWINT QR checkout; its official pages do not say TWINT or a Swiss bank owns Payrexx. Payrexx disclosed a CHF 2.5 million Series A investment involving INS Holding AG and an Icelandic investor consortium in December 2023, but does not publish the current shareholder percentages or control. Its current press page and active 2026 offer show ongoing operations; no acquisition or shutdown notice was found. Payrexx Pay Plus was formerly Payrexx Direct, and Payrexx Pay was formerly Payrexx Swiss Collecting.

## Facts

Every fee below is the published Payrexx amount. The current terms say fees are exclusive of applicable taxes unless explicitly stated. The merchant's signed contract can set account-specific fees. `CHF` is the Swiss pricing version; `EUR` is shown on Payrexx's European English/German pricing versions. The pricing page has 1-, 2-, and 3-year controls for Standard and Premium, a country/currency presentation (CHF on `/pricing`, EUR on `/en/pricing`), and a card-mix/sales-channel estimator. The public text does not expose all duration-dependent plan totals, so no discount is inferred.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Pricing switches checked:** `/pricing` displays CHF; the European `/en/pricing` and `/de/pricing` versions display EUR. Standard and Premium expose 1-, 2- and 3-year choices. The price-page text also exposes a card-mix/sales-channel estimator. Current Payrexx 2026 comparison/guides publish CHF/EUR 0, 19 and 49 per month, but the exact total under each term selector was not visible in readable page text; I did not infer a multi-year discount.
- **Free plan feature conflict:** the live pricing feature list includes API/plugins in the Free column, while the plan documentation says shop plugins and custom API integrations are not included in Free. Treat Free integrations as unresolved until confirmed in the merchant plan.
- **Payrexx Pay versus external PSPs:** Payrexx Pay is the Switzerland route and Pay Plus is the listed European route. Outside those seller countries, the platform can still connect to external providers, but each has its own seller eligibility, transaction fees and payout rules. The public 1.00%/0.50%/0.25% external-provider surcharge (Free/Standard/Premium) is in addition to the provider's own fee.
- **TWINT/ownership:** Payrexx publishes TWINT checkout integration and a joint QR use case. Its official imprint names Payrexx AG and its EEA subsidiary; the reviewed company press/news pages did not publish the ultimate shareholders or an acquisition by TWINT/a bank.
- **Regional fee schedule:** CHF and EUR pages have matching domestic plan rates, but card class, buyer market, method and merchant contract can change actual cost. The pricing table marks business/inter-region cards separately; exact individual account fees appear in the merchant contract/dashboard. Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes.
- **Crypto:** Payrexx publishes a Crypto checkout fee and a separate Centi method, but its public pages do not describe the asset/network matrix, wallet custody, conversion path or crypto settlement. Dashboard refunds are not supported for Crypto.
- **Other unknowns:** complete charge-currency support by method, a complete list of external-provider signup countries, universal approval time, exact reserve values per merchant, and card credential portability were not published in the checked pricing, docs, terms and help pages.
