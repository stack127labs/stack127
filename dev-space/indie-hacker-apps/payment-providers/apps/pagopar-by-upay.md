- **Pagopar and upay relationship:** the current upay Pagopar page and Pagopar.com root redirect use the upay destination. Pagopar terms and About page still name GRUPO M S.A. and describe upay as a processing network. The official sources show integration/co-branding, not an announced acquisition.

- **Monthly plans:** Pagopar terms list PYG 49,000 / 99,000 / 199,000 plans. The current upay homepage lists Base at PYG 0 and Pro at PYG 45,000/month after six free months, plus Enterprise by quote. Public pages do not say which plan applies to a Pagopar merchant account or whether fees stack.
