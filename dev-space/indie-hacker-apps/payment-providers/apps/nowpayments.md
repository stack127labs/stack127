## Status and scope

NOWPayments is an active crypto payment gateway for merchants. The current merchant terms name **FD Transfers LLC**, registered in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, as the contracting company. NOWPayments' official help says the service launched by ChangeNOW in 2019 and later became an independent payment gateway while keeping a partnership with ChangeNOW. The official blog and updates archive show ongoing product changes. I found no NOWPayments acquisition, licence-grant, regulatory-action, shutdown, or product-sunset notice in the official blog/update pages checked; I did not find a separate newsroom. The current terms are dated October 19, 2026.

The current terms exclude service to residents or citizens of the EU, UK, US, UAE, Russia, and jurisdictions where crypto services are restricted or prohibited. This is narrower and more explicit than the general help-page wording that it serves globally subject to law; the terms are used for the seller eligibility rows below. Seller eligibility is not the same as fiat on-ramp reach, which the product page describes separately.

Pricing has no visible country, plan, billing-period, or fiat-currency switch. The live pricing page shows crypto service-fee tiers and network fees. Its newer 1.5% multi-currency/fixed-rate language and the September 2026 pricing-update post are used where older help answers still show 1% for fixed-rate payments.

## Cost examples

Our calculations, before variable network fees:

- **Single-currency payment:** $100 × 1% = **$1.00 NOWPayments service fee**; about $99 remains before any applicable network fee.
- **Multi-currency or fixed-rate payment:** $100 × 1.5% = **$1.50 NOWPayments service fee**; about $98.50 remains before any applicable network fee.

The buyer generally pays its own wallet's send fee. Network fees for processing and external-wallet settlement can also reduce the merchant's received amount. NOWPayments does not publish a fixed dollar gas cost because it varies by asset, network load and transaction path.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The current pricing page and September 2026 pricing update show 1% single-currency and 1.5% multi-currency fees, including fixed-rate/autoconverted flows. Older FAQ text still says 1% for fixed-rate payments. I used the current price page and recent pricing-update post for fixed-rate costs.
- The live price page lists off-chain conversion at 1%; an FAQ answer says manual Custody conversion is 0.5%. The labels may refer to distinct operations, but the public pages do not explain the distinction. Check the displayed quote in the merchant account before using that operation.
- Current terms name FD Transfers LLC and exclude EU, UK, US, UAE and Russian residents/citizens, while general help says services are global except where prohibited by law. Seller eligibility for all other countries is not published as an allow-list.
- Fiat withdrawals are third-party provided. Current terms specify EUR and USDT TRC20 for the withdrawal route, but partner fees, accepted bank countries, payout minimums and bank-arrival timing are not given as a universal table.
- The official blog/update pages checked show ongoing product work. I found no official acquisition, licence-grant, shutdown, or merchant-service-sunset announcement. NOWPayments' FAQ says FD Transfers LLC does not require a specific licence under its incorporation law; this is the company's statement, not regulator confirmation.
- No public standard reserve percentage, seller-volume cap, individual country/sole-trader matrix, merchant cancellation timeline, or post-closure fund-release schedule was found.

## Field problems

- C01 changes frequently and contains hundreds of assets. The file records major coins and representative stablecoin/network variants, with the live supported-assets page as the complete current list.
- F03/F10 need seller eligibility separated from buyer/on-ramp availability. The terms provide an explicit restricted-user list, but not an exhaustive whitelist or buyer checkout matrix.
- Fee wording has legacy FAQ text. The current pricing page and recent update were treated as primary for standard rates; a separate off-chain conversion mismatch remains unresolved.
- Fiat settlement is supplied by partners. Partner routes can vary by merchant account, country and time; public partner-specific rate tables were not consolidated here.
