## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Product retirement:** the 2026-10-20 release note sunsets Express Checkout on 2027-01-01. It says accounts, balances and other Payphone services continue and directs merchants to Cajita. This is not a company shutdown.
- **Current versus older withdrawal timing:** the Personal help page last updated 2026-10-13 says 48 business hours. The newer Terms 2026.2, effective 2026-11-01, specify up to one business day for Payphone processing plus a usual one to three business days for bank posting, with review cases up to ten business days. The newer terms govern the record.
- **Withdrawal price is not a conflict:** the current tariff's 1% + IVA line is for optional mass “Payouts”; current terms separate it from ordinary seller Cash-Out and state ordinary withdrawal has no Payphone commission, apart from receiving-bank costs and specific channel tariffs.
- Payphone's current terms say detailed acceptable-use and chargeback policies are forthcoming. The linked policy URLs were attempted but inaccessible; specific restricted-business lists and dispute fees remain unpublished.
- No official beneficial-owner/shareholder disclosure was found. The current terms identify the group head and Ecuador legal entity only.
- The official website's About/history, product release notes, current terms and site search were checked for acquisition or company shutdown notices. No such notice surfaced. The official release-note page was usable; attempted URLs `https://payphone.app/noticias` and `https://blog.payphone.app` did not provide accessible editorial pages.
- Country onboarding outside Ecuador, international charge-currency matrix, foreign-card surcharge, exchange-rate markup, minimum payout and bulk export/payment portability remain unpublished in the official sources checked.

## Field problems

- F03/F04/F05: the public site advertises wider Latin American reach, but current terms do not give a complete seller-country, legal-form or bank-country eligibility table. This record confirms the Ecuador route and keeps other-market availability conditional.

- F14/F15: Payphone has both tokenization and a separate recurring module. The docs explicitly say tokenization itself is not recurring billing; feature and price rows keep those distinct.
- F20/F21/F25: the fee is quoted in dollars on Ecuador pages. The API confirms USD; IVA is on Payphone's fee, not the transaction. No foreign-card surcharge is published.
