## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Account caps:** the Business pricing FAQ reports USD 500/month for an individual and USD 1,000/month for a company, each with a USD 100 per-transaction limit. A newer support guide says personal accounts can process USD 3,000/month and Business accounts can exceed USD 3,000 with company documents. The terms do not reconcile these figures, so the account-tier limits remain conflicting. The merchant portal may show a seller-specific limit.
- **Declined transaction fee:** current pricing and the standalone transaction-cost help page list USD 0.35 + ITBMS. The separate cost-calculation guide contains a worked example using USD 0.30 + ITBMS. I use USD 0.35 as the current headline value and retain the older/example discrepancy here.
- **Yappy fee stacking:** the Yappy FAQ states 1% for PagueloFacil and 1% for Yappy, but does not clearly say whether the standard card processing rate also applies. No combined rate is inferred.
- **Arbitration tax:** the contract lists a USD 500 arbitration amount but does not specifically say whether that fee adds ITBMS. Its general price clause says prices are USD plus ITBMS, so the specific treatment is unclear.
- **Crypto:** Business checkout advertises crypto payments. No supported coin/network list, custody path, conversion/settlement policy, confirmation rule, crypto refund flow or crypto fee table was found. Personal crypto card assets are kept separate.
