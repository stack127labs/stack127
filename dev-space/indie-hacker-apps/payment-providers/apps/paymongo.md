## Shutdown, sunset, acquisition and access notices

All notices below were checked on **2026-11-05**.

- **New sign-ups paused:** the actual public signup page says account creation is paused while upgrades are rolled out. It asks for an email notification request or sales contact and supplies **no reopening date**. No form was submitted. The product is not a confirmed open-signup choice for a new solo seller today. <https://dashboard.paymongo.com/signup>; <https://www.paymongo.com/en>; <https://www.paymongo.com/en-ph/pricing>
- **Dated Payment Pages URL sunset:** official Storefront docs say Pages were moved into Storefront by **24 September 2026**. Existing `pages.paymongo.com` links/QR codes keep working only until **23 November 2026**, after which the old addresses stop answering. Existing sellers should use the replacement `storefront.paymongo.com` addresses/codes. Payment Pages remain free; this is not a whole-provider shutdown. <https://docs.paymongo.com/docs/payment-channels-storefront.md>
- **Method restrictions:** the **8 October 2026** direct-online-banking guide marks **BDO and Metrobank via Brankas temporarily unavailable**, despite their continued appearance in the pricing list. Current method warnings qualify the product list. <https://docs.paymongo.com/docs/payment-acceptance-direct-online-banking.md>; <https://www.paymongo.com/en-ph/pricing>
- **Service remains offered:** official product/pricing/docs and the operational-status source remain available. No whole-company acquisition, whole-service shutdown or replacement-brand notice was found in the reviewed official company, policy, product and status material and targeted notice searches. The signup pause is a real access restriction, not evidence that all existing accounts have closed. <https://www.paymongo.com/en/about>; <https://status.paymongo.com/>; <https://www.paymongo.com/en-ph/pricing>
- **Other limited/future products:** Invoices is beta early access by organization, and repeating invoices roll out separately. Multi-currency/crypto wallets and cross-border fiat/stablecoin disbursement rails are marked coming soon. Forex stablecoin-quoting docs do not prove those wallet/payout products are generally live. <https://docs.paymongo.com/docs/payment-channels-invoices.md>; <https://docs.paymongo.com/docs/payment-channels-invoices-repeating.md>; <https://docs.paymongo.com/docs/money-movement-wallets.md>; <https://docs.paymongo.com/docs/money-movement-disbursements.md>; <https://docs.paymongo.com/docs/money-movement-forex.md>

Scope: PayMongo's **Philippine merchant payment acceptance**, Standard/custom pricing, domestic and foreign-registered entity onboarding, hosted/no-code checkout, subscriptions and related settlement, wallet/transfer and platform-account functions. Optional Online Store, Protect, managed support and Forex are separated from basic processing. PHP/₱ means Philippine peso; VAT is tax on the provider fee; KYC checks identity; KYB checks the business; DTI/SEC are business registries; BIR is the Philippine tax authority; BSP is its central bank. A foreign buyer, foreign business registration, owner nationality and overseas settlement destination are different facts.

Their accordion content is loaded from the site's public JavaScript; the exact first-party asset is cited where it supplies clauses absent from initial HTML. Archived versions in that same asset were not applied as current rules. ## Cost examples — our calculations

Inputs checked **2026-11-05**: Standard Philippine pricing, PHP payment amount, merchant absorbs the processing fee, and **12% VAT applied to the published fee**. The formula is:

`processing cost including VAT = (payment × published percentage + published fixed fee) × 1.12`

We retain the actual published decimals. Displayed processing costs are **rounded to two decimal places using half-up rounding only at the final total**; the amount after processing is payment less that rounded total. Actual provider ledger rounding can differ. These are the named payment component, before optional product/billing/support charges, disputes/refunds, withholding tax and any outbound transfer. Source for every payment-rate/tax input: <https://www.paymongo.com/en-ph/pricing>; current legal tax treatment: <https://www.paymongo.com/terms>; <https://www.paymongo.com/_next/static/chunks/6355-710d6aee3ecd204d.js>

**Wallet-to-bank example — our calculation:** after the PHP1,000 local-card example, the wallet has **PHP950.00** from the payment component. If one chargeable outgoing bank transfer costs PHP10 before VAT, and the weekly free-transfer allowance has already been used, the transfer cost is PHP10 × 1.12 = **PHP11.20** and the amount left to send is **PHP938.80**. This assumes no withholding/other adjustments, sufficient transferable balance, enabled external transfers and one transfer charged at the public rate. Receiving an ordinary settlement is free under the payout guide; the subsequent bank transfer is separate. If the free weekly allowance applies, this paid-transfer example does not apply. Sources checked 2026-11-05: <https://www.paymongo.com/en-ph/pricing>; <https://docs.paymongo.com/docs/money-movement-payouts.md>; <https://docs.paymongo.com/docs/money-movement-disbursements.md>

**Withholding example — our calculation:** if the account's **separately determined qualified gross-remittance tax base** is PHP1,000, the provider's published 0.5% rule yields PHP1,000 × 0.5% = **PHP5** withholding. It is not added universally to the examples above: thresholds, sworn declarations, validated exemptions and whether the base equals the original customer charge affect the actual deduction.
