Scope: Pabbly Subscription Billing is subscription billing and checkout software. It is not a payment processor or merchant of record: the merchant sells to their own customer and connects a separate gateway. Pabbly names direct Stripe and PayPal connections, Indian gateways Razorpay, Instamojo, and Cashfree, and custom-gateway integrations; its stated gateway count differs across pages. Pabbly says it takes no per-transaction fee. The selected gateway's processing, refund, dispute, conversion, and payout terms remain separate. Pabbly sells the product on its own and also includes it in Pabbly Plus.

The dedicated pricing page has USD and INR purchase links and Monthly, 12 Month, 24 Month, and 36 Month selectors. Prices for the longer periods are shown per month; the page text does not show the full amount due at checkout. The same page's discount badges do not match all the listed prices. The current product landing page shows a different free/Standard/Unlimited plan set and a different yearly discount selector. Both page versions are preserved below because I could not reconcile them from public Pabbly pages. No account was created or accessed.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The dedicated pricing URL lists Basic/Standard/Pro/Ultimate with revenue caps and 12/24/36-month selectors. The live product landing page shows Free/Standard/Unlimited and a separate monthly price schedule. Pabbly's own pages do not explain the difference. Both were checked on 2026-11-07.
- The dedicated pricing page labels terms 30%, 40%, and 50% off, but some displayed per-month prices do not line up with those percentages when compared with the “normally” price beside them. I recorded the displayed amounts and labels without recalculating a purchase total. The checkout total and whether each longer-term amount is paid all at once were not stated in the text pages checked.
- The product's currency claims differ: the plans guide says 25+ currencies, the home page says around 55, and an older guide says one currency per account while the current page advertises multi-currency accounts.
- The official page says both 35+ and 40+ gateways. It names Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, Instamojo, and Cashfree in text; custom gateway examples include PayFast and Authorize.net/PayPro. The public supported-gateway image in the forum thread was not used as a text-readable exhaustive list.
- The product home page's broad 30-day guarantee is qualified by terms that exclude discounted and promotional purchases. The terms were checked on 2026-11-07 and contain the detailed restrictions.
- Country-level merchant eligibility, a full end-buyer country matrix, the complete fee schedule for connected gateways, and a full billing-data deletion schedule were not published in the signup, pricing, terms, API, help, or privacy pages listed above.
- Blog/news check: searched the official Pabbly blog and About page plus product page, terms, docs index, and recent help articles for acquisition, rebrand, shutdown, or sunset notices. No notice was found. No separate newsroom was linked from product navigation.

## Field problems

- F03/F04/F05/F06 are designed for payment processors. For this billing product, the relevant questions concern software account signup; they do not establish the separate gateway's seller eligibility or KYC.
- F10–F12 need country and gateway details. Pabbly delegates buyer payment availability to gateways and does not provide one complete text table of seller countries, buyer countries, methods, and currencies.
- F19 has two materially different live public price presentations. The dedicated price page is useful for USD/INR and revenue-cap comparisons, but it does not reconcile with the landing page's current Free/Standard/Unlimited schedule.
- F20–F28 and F31–F36 mostly describe processing and settlement. They are not applicable to Pabbly's software layer; the underlying gateway determines those facts.
- F42–F43 are optional marketplace and product-delivery fields; the public docs do not establish a native digital fulfillment or split-settlement feature.
- C01–C05 are not applicable because Pabbly is not a crypto offer and its terms prohibit virtual currency/crypto products.
