**Current status:** Active Czech payment gateway operated by Comgate a.s. Its 2024 annual report identifies CGX a.s. as its 100% shareholder. “Comgate” is the brand; the legal company changed from ComGate Payments, a.s. to Comgate a.s. on 18 January 2024. I checked the company blog and press/news page for acquisition or shutdown notices. They show current services and no notice that the gateway is closing or being acquired. This is the Czech gateway listed in apps.md, not another similarly named service.
Scope: Online gateway, hosted and embedded checkout, payment links and recurring card payments. In-person terminal hardware and its fees are excluded. Public pricing has different Czech and Slovak Start, Easy and Profi schedules, plus a custom offer. The current terms say provider fees do not include VAT (DPH). For some methods, the provider lists its own fee but not the external lender’s conditions; those limits are shown separately.

## Cost examples
Our calculations use the published Czech gateway prices. They exclude VAT (DPH), because the contract says fees do not include VAT. The charge amount is assumed to be CZK 1,000, with no foreign-currency surcharge, refund or monthly fee.
- **Easy, EU consumer card during the introductory period:** 0.6% × CZK 1,000 + CZK 0 = **CZK 6.00**, plus VAT if due. The lower rate ends after three months or CZK 1,000,000 in payments, whichever comes first.
- **Easy, same card at the ordinary rate:** 1% × CZK 1,000 + CZK 0 = **CZK 10.00**, plus VAT if due.
- **Profi, EU consumer card at the ordinary rate:** 0.67% × CZK 1,000 + CZK 1 = **CZK 7.70**, plus VAT if due. During the introductory period the fee is 0.47% × CZK 1,000 + CZK 1 = **CZK 5.70**.
## Conflicts and unknowns
- The help article about payment methods lists PayPal, but the current Czech and Slovak 2026 gateway tariffs do not list a PayPal fee. The enabled-method list is merchant-specific, so I could not confirm that PayPal is available for new gateway accounts.
- Public seller eligibility is explicit for Czechia and Slovakia. Other seller countries are assessed individually; Comgate does not publish a complete list of eligible foreign sellers.
- Country-specific buyer card limits live in the merchant portal, not in a complete public table. Non-EEA card acceptance needs approval.
- Comgate’s public gateway fees exclude VAT. The file does not add a VAT rate to estimates because the actual tax treatment can depend on the merchant’s invoice and circumstances.
- Custom plan rates are quoted individually. BNPL and instalment providers set some buyer eligibility and maximums outside Comgate.
- The general help article’s PayPal mention may reflect a broader or older method description. I retained it as unclear rather than treating an absent line in the current tariff as proof that the method is unavailable.
## Field problems
- F08: Comgate describes general goods/services but does not classify SaaS, online courses or software subscriptions individually. Its restricted-business policy does name hosting, online/data services and some memberships, so approval must be checked for those models.
- F12: the general methods help page and current plan schedules do not give the same confidence about PayPal availability; merchant-specific enabled methods are not public.
- F10/F03: seller-country acceptance and buyer card-issuer-country acceptance are different. Foreign sellers need an individual review, while buyer-card countries are configured in each merchant portal.
- Fee schedules are plan- and country-specific. The calculation examples are for Czech fees only; the Slovak schedule is recorded separately in the facts table.
