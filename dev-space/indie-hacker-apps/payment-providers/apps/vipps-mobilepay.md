**Current status, ownership, and changes:** The service is active. Vipps and MobilePay merged into one company in 2022; the cross-border merger was completed in 2023, and a shared app/platform was rolled out in Finland in January 2024 and Denmark in March 2024. The apps kept the Vipps brand in Norway and MobilePay in Denmark and Finland. On 6 October 2026, the company announced that the Danish and Finnish MobilePay apps will change their name to Vipps on 12 January 2027; the new Vipps smile icon is already being introduced. The company says existing app features will stay the same. The company is owned 72.2% by the consortium of Norwegian banks behind Vipps and 27.8% by Danske Bank.

On 26 March 2026, Vipps MobilePay announced the sale of its all-in-one Checkout solution to Swedish company Kustom. The transition of existing Checkout merchants to Kustom is scheduled to run gradually through 2026. Vipps MobilePay says its standard online payment products, payment links and recurring payments continue. Separately, the legacy MobilePay Subscriptions API and MobilePay App Payments API were closed in production and test in April 2026; use the current Recurring API/ePayment API. The developer changelog also says the developer-docs domain and Danish/Finnish payment landing-page domains are planned to change on 12 January 2027. The official status page showed the services operational when checked; it lists a brief incident on 6 November 2026 affecting webhooks and payments, resolved after four minutes.

**Scope:** This record covers direct merchant online payments through ePayment, recurring payments, and payment links for the Norwegian, Danish, and Finnish merchant markets. The payment APIs include Vipps/MobilePay wallet flows, and ePayment also supports standalone Visa and Mastercard flows. The bundled Vipps MobilePay Checkout product acquired by Kustom is treated separately. Vipps MobilePay is a payment service provider, not the merchant of record: merchants sell their own goods or services and remain responsible for fulfillment, customer terms, consumer obligations and taxes.

**Pricing pages and switches checked:** Checked current Norwegian (`/nb-NO/pricing`), Danish (`/da-DK/pricing`), and Finnish (`/fi-FI/pricing`) localized pages on 2026-11-07. They are country pages, not a country selector; the pages have no plan, currency, or monthly/annual switch. The public standard-tier prices are per transaction in NOK, DKK, or EUR. Enterprise/growth pricing may be available by quote or direct agreement. The Danish page says the integrated-payment and recurring rate will automatically rise on 2027-01-01. An older Danish product page still showed 1.49% + 1 DKK for integrated payments; the current price list is used here and that older page is noted as stale, not combined with the current rate. All prices exclude VAT unless the provider expressly states otherwise; the terms say VAT is added where the service is subject to it.

## Cost examples

Our calculation, using a 100-unit online integrated-payment transaction at the current public standard price, before any applicable VAT:

- Norway: 100 NOK × 2.99% + 1 NOK = **4.00 NOK**.
- Denmark through 2026-12-31: 100 DKK × 0.59% + 1 DKK = **1.59 DKK**. From 2027-01-01: 100 DKK × 0.89% + 1 DKK = **1.89 DKK**.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Denmark integrated/recurring pricing:** the current Danish pricing page lists 0.59% + 1 DKK until 2026-12-31, then 0.89% + 1 DKK from 2027-01-01. An older Danish product page still shows 1.49% + 1 DKK. The current pricing page was checked directly on 2026-11-07 and is used as the current rate; the older page is retained as a stale-page note.
- **Checkout ownership:** company news and the Checkout changelog say Kustom is acquiring the bundled Checkout product and existing merchants are transitioning. Standard direct payment integration, recurring payments, and payment links continue. I did not treat “Checkout” as wholly discontinued across all Vipps MobilePay services.
- **Brand transition:** MobilePay remains the Denmark/Finland app name on the research date. Vipps MobilePay announced the change to Vipps for 2027-01-12; old payment landing-page hostnames and the developer-docs domain are also scheduled to change then.
- **VAT:** all rates are displayed as excluding VAT under the merchant terms unless otherwise noted. Whether VAT is due for a particular service and merchant situation depends on applicable law; the public pages do not say that VAT is included in these rates.
- **Unpublished details:** country-specific standalone card surcharges, separate refund transaction fee, payout fee/minimum, foreign-exchange fee, universal reserve amount, full billing-suite features, and full activation times were not stated in the pages listed in each relevant table row.
