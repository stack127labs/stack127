## Cost examples

These are our calculations for a R$ 100,00 payment. They exclude tax on provider fees, account-specific pricing and other optional charges.

- **One-time credit card, standard:** R$ 100,00 × 2,99% + R$ 0,49 = **R$ 3,48 fee**; calculated remainder **R$ 96,52**.
- **One-time credit card, 3-month promo:** R$ 100,00 × 1,99% + R$ 0,49 = **R$ 2,48 fee**; calculated remainder **R$ 97,52**.
- **Six-installment card sale, standard:** R$ 100,00 × 3,49% + R$ 0,49 = **R$ 3,98 fee**; calculated remainder **R$ 96,02** before any installment-financing or anticipation terms.
- **Pix collection, standard:** **R$ 2,15 fee**; calculated remainder **R$ 97,85**.
- **Pix collection, 3-month promo:** **R$ 1,08 fee**; calculated remainder **R$ 98,92**.
- These use the public standard and promotional prices checked on 2026-11-06; promo begins when the account is created and runs for 3 months.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The current live pricing page shows Pix and boleto at R$ 2,15 standard and R$ 1,08 during the new-account promotion. An older search-indexed snippet showed lower values; I used the current live page and its current fee-help article, and did not carry the older table forward.
- Current card-band help from October 2026 omits Hipercard, although an older help article includes it. I used the newer card guide. The pricing FAQ separately says 13–21 installments are supported only on Visa and Mastercard.
- The country article lists 24 locations blocked from platform access. A foreign applicant still needs active Brazilian CPF/CNPJ, RNE/CRNM and a Brazilian phone. Asaas does not publish a complete list of all allowed foreign seller locations.
- Foreign card buyer prices, a full list of accepted foreign cardholder countries, foreign payout, buyer-side FX costs, detailed forbidden-product matrix, exact token portability, and a standard reserve schedule were not published. The page searches tried are listed in the related rows.
- The current news check found Asaas acquisitions of Mutuus and HelenaCRM in 2026; these were purchases by Asaas. No official sunset or sale of Asaas payment services was found.

## Field problems

- `apps.md` says “Latin America; Brazil.” Official materials confirm the Brazil-based account, Brazilian bank and requirements for eligible foreigners. They do not give a full affirmative list of Latin American seller domiciles; residence in one of the blocked countries prevents access.
- Some docs show generic payment methods while account activation or checkout type can limit them. I kept international cards, subscriptions, Pix and boleto split by method and approval conditions.
- The pricing page includes Asaas Tap, card withdrawals, transfers, invoices and collection reminders. I separated Tap rates from online checkout and recorded add-on and payout prices only under their own fields.
