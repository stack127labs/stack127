Scope: ChargeOver is recurring billing and invoicing software. The merchant stays the seller and must connect its own payment gateway or merchant account. ChargeOver says it is not a gateway and does not charge its own per-transaction or revenue-share fee. Gateway fees, settlement, refunds, disputes, and payout rules remain separate. Sources: https://help.chargeover.com/docs/getting-paid/; https://help.chargeover.com/; https://chargeover.com/pricing.

Absence of a notice on these pages does not prove that no private transaction occurred. Sources: https://chargeover.com/blog; https://chargeover.com/about; https://chargeover.com/terms-of-service.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Pricing: the SaaS page’s visible tier card calls 1,000+ customers “Custom,” while its FAQ lists Enterprise Growth at USD 999/month for 1,000 customers and Enterprise Plus at USD 1,999/month for 2,500. The main pricing page starts at USD 229/month and does not display the whole tier table. I kept both current official statements and did not infer which offer sales will quote.
- Billing period: the public pricing pages show monthly prices and no period switch. Generic Terms allow monthly or annual subscriptions where offered. No annual amount was published on the pricing pages checked.
- Payment gateway wording: one marketing page calls ChargeOver a gateway, but the support FAQ and setup docs say it is not one and require the merchant’s own processor. The product docs and fee explanation are used for product classification.
- Seller eligibility: ChargeOver says businesses can use the software from any country. That does not mean every connected processor accepts sellers from every country.
- Not published after checking: legal-form eligibility, ChargeOver KYC checklist, exhaustive permitted business categories, full buyer-country blocks, international/local gateway fee schedules, refunds/dispute fees, add-on prices, tax filing/remittance responsibility, precise payment amount caps, support hours/languages, and standard approval time. The relevant official Terms, pricing, processor index, currency docs, tax pages, and support/onboarding pages are linked in the corresponding rows.
- No separate ChargeOver newsroom or press page surfaced in the company-site search. I checked the official blog and About page for acquisition or shutdown notices.

## Field problems

- ChargeOver is software that orchestrates invoices and gateway requests, not a processor that owns the payment fees or settlement stages. Payout, reserve, and crypto fields therefore use not_applicable where ChargeOver has no such role; gateway-specific rules are left to that gateway.
- ChargeOver has a public all-country software-support statement but no matching universal processor eligibility matrix. The connected gateway must be checked separately.
- Pricing is relatively transparent by active customer count, but the same SaaS page uses a Custom card and fixed Enterprise Growth/Plus prices in its FAQ. Sales confirmation is needed above 1,000 customers.
