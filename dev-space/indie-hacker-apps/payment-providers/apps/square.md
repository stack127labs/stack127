- Refund window: the live US refund guide allows one year, but section 34 of the July 2026 US payment terms still says 120 days. Both retain original processing fees. ## Field problems

- F02: Square uses merchant of record in some card-security descriptions. The merchant still supplies the goods/services and owes sales taxes. Card-network responsibility is different from a reseller handling global digital taxes.
- F03 to F06: seller country, owner/representative residency, bank location and buyer reach are separate. Worldwide developer access does not expand the eight payment-processing seller countries.
- F19 to F25: US bundled plans, legacy individual subscriptions, non-US Plus/Premium packages, hosted checkout, online API, stored-card manual charges and invoice payments have different prices. Per-location software charges must be kept separate.
- F20 to F23: international pricing uses card issuance, not buyer travel location. UK/EEA higher online rates must not receive a second international surcharge. Ireland adds VAT; Australian standard-processing GST wording remains unclear.
- F14/F15/F24: recurring invoice series, item subscriptions, API subscriptions and a seller's own subscription to Square are different. API ACH limitations do not remove the documented automatic bank-on-file feature in US recurring Invoices.
- F29/F30: US tax enrollment configures calculation without registering the business. Manual VAT fields, a Japanese seller QII and tax exemption settings are not proof of automated buyer-ID validation or global tax filing.
- F33 to F36: balance availability, payout initiation, bank arrival, instant minimums and charge limits differ. Reserve reviews are separate from release dates. Fast-transfer availability can depend on bank support and seller history.
- F37/F40: exporting customers, transferring stored cards and moving subscription/access logic are separate tasks. Deactivation removes access to history and does not cancel every paid subscription automatically.
- F41: licence/security statements here are provider statements with product scope. No independent live licence registry or current audit certificate was checked.
- C01 to C05: the crypto fields apply because eligible US merchants can accept BTC through Lightning, including the documented invoice flow. Custodial BTC, USD settlement, daily card-sales conversion, buy/sell and external withdrawal have separate conditions and costs.
