## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Pricing:** Ghana card/mobile-money/payout details conflict between country pricing/general fee help and the country operating guide. Uganda domestic cards and the exact mobile-payout boundary also differ. These source pairs remain in the relevant country rows. Nigeria's broad local-fee notice and the older dedicated Account Debit tariff were both checked; the latter is not silently added to the former.
- **Limits:** the general and Rwanda-locale versions of the same help route publish different ceilings for Cameroon, Côte d’Ivoire, Kenya, Malawi, Rwanda, Senegal, Uganda and Zambia mobile-money collection, and for Ghana/Nigeria/South Africa bank collection. Ghana's operating guide adds another card ceiling. The source's $5,000 benchmark is approximate; its opening sentence calls it daily while the table says per transaction. No independent currency conversion or daily aggregate was manufactured.
- **Malformed values:** Nigeria's daily bank-transfer figure, some Rwanda-locale payout numbers, Indian-rupee currency code, and the XAF/XOF method-code mapping contain apparent errors. Kenya/South Africa/Rwanda onboarding also have inconsistent ID-expiry wording. Original descriptions are preserved or flagged; no hidden corrections were made.
- **Refunds/disputes:** Dashboard help, settlement FAQ, international-processing help and developer docs were all read. Their refund estimates and chargeback response units differ. A precise universal original-fee return rule, local dispute charge and refund of the international dispute fee on a win were not established. Actual account/dispute notices may specify the operative deadline.
- **Stablecoins:** token/network examples and fiat-wallet lists differ between API and help. The help page says automatic stablecoin settlement is not yet available, while the MSA allows a conditional USDC request. The commercial blog describes selected merchants. The stablecoin API, overview, funding/transfer guides, chain verification and merchant terms were checked before leaving these details unresolved. Exact new-seller country enablement, private-key control, chain confirmation counts and general crypto checkout-refund rules were not published in those sources.
- **Signup and countries:** country KYC guides, account-type help, payment-method coverage, regional pricing routes and public terms were checked. Public merchant prices are not proof of unrestricted signup. Generic rest-of-world onboarding does not establish an unlimited seller-country list. Send App consumer verification/transfer tiers were excluded.
- **Taxes and extra fees:** pricing links were followed into fee VAT, international APM processing, direct debit and transfer help. Fee VAT is separate from sales tax on the merchant's goods. Invoice tax fields do not establish registration/filing coverage. A universal fiat FX spread, separate billing surcharge and automatic-settlement fee distinct from outgoing Transfers were not found.
- **Moving/closing:** transaction/customer exports, tokenization, subscription management, privacy and termination clauses were checked. Report exports do not prove reusable payment-token migration. One-month contractual notice, reserve release and post-termination business-day holds are separate conditions.
- **Access notes:** some direct blog fetches returned only a page shell. Official indexed article contents were readable through web search and used for the dated company announcements. The blog and Inside Flutterwave index also returned mostly client-rendered shells, so the company-news check followed indexed official press articles and targeted official-site searches. US/UK/Egypt pricing routes returned regional landing pages rather than price schedules. These are not recorded as proof that an offer is closed or a fee is zero.

All source checks in this file were on 2026-11-05. Specific published fee-change dates are recorded in the facts. No private signup, contract negotiation or authenticated API action was performed.
