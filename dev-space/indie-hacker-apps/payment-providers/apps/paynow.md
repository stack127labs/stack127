## Cost examples

These are our calculations, checked 2026-11-04. They are illustrations, not a quote for a particular seller. Card examples use the published 2.90% + 30¢ regional rate and the calculator's USD denomination. They assume eligible methods, no affiliate split, no over-limit rate, no extra card override and no conversion. Provider-fee taxes, plan subscriptions, refunds and payouts are excluded unless a row expressly includes them.

Let B be the product price before sales tax, T the sales tax, G = B + T the customer charge, r the gateway percentage, f its fixed fee, and p the plan's platform percentage. The official calculator applies:

- Gateway fee H = G × r + f.
- Platform fee P = (G - T - H) × p, with a zero floor in the calculator.
- Transaction fees = H + P.
- Creator net before payout and plan cost = G - T - H - P.

This fee order is supported by the pricing page, transaction guide and the calculator's own public formula. It subtracts sales tax and gateway fees before applying the platform fee; the gateway percentage in the calculator applies to the tax-inclusive charge. Sources: https://paynow.gg/pricing; https://docs.paynow.gg/guides/payments-and-payouts/transactions.md; https://paynow.gg/pricing-calculator; https://paynow.gg/_next/static/chunks/app/(paynow)/(landing)/pricing-calculator/page-f471a20d073f1a69.js (checked 2026-11-04).

## Field problems

- F02 needs the actual legal role beside the marketing label. Here the contract says MoR/commercial agent and retains the creator's fulfillment contract; a simple reseller checkbox would lose that distinction.
- F03, F05, F10, F11 and F32 must separate seller location, verified owner, bank country, buyer-method market, charge currency and settlement currency. 'Global' and 'Worldwide' cannot fill all six.
- F20/F21 are hard to map to a domestic/international comparison because the public rates say 'region' without defining the card-country test. F23 is a method schedule with country flags, not a promise that every method supports subscriptions.
- F19/F35 need plan limits and their conflicting enforcement rule together. Fixed plan prices, gateway fees and the post-tax/post-gateway platform base must remain separate.
- F22/F28 need checkout FX, payout FX and the withdrawal rail separated. Exact boundaries and PayPal cap scope should be collected as published rather than repaired by assumption.
- F29/F30 require registration coverage and tax-ID workflow, not only 'MoR handles tax'. F37 must distinguish transaction CSVs, inbound catalogue migration and outgoing reusable payment credentials.
- F36/F44 overlap here: public setoff powers are known, but no standard rolling-reserve percentage or duration is given. An account's settlement countdown is not the missing reserve policy.
- C01/C04 are especially easy to overstate from API examples. An example asset or confirmation count is not an acceptance matrix or completion threshold. The crypto fields apply because crypto checkout is offered despite fiat-only payouts.
