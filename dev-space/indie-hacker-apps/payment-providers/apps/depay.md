The affiliate is not named or tied to this product. For this listing, the product-specific direct-wallet description is the operative fact.
- **Conversion risk:** Pricing markets “no conversion risk,” while the terms say real-time decentralized-exchange quotes vary and DePay is not responsible for liquidity, volatility, or rate differences. The contractual price-risk warning is recorded alongside the marketing claim.
- **Seller and buyer geography:** Pricing claims 195 countries and 160+ currencies, but the terms, pricing, and onboarding docs searched do not give a seller eligibility list, buyer-country matrix, or complete charge-currency list. DePay reserves the right to reject applicants.

FINMA’s authorised-institutions page and fintech licence list were searched for DePay AG, with no matching entry surfaced. This does not prove the company has no other registration or licence.
