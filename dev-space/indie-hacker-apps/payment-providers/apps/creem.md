## Field problems

- F03 needs named seller locations plus bank-route restrictions. The source's inconsistent count makes a single coverage number unreliable.
- F05 must distinguish verified beneficiary identity, owner residence, tax residence and bank country. Only the documented requirements can be filled.
- F08–F09 need conditional approval and established-history requirements. A digital-product label or a guide's example is not blanket category admission.
- F11 needs actual charge currencies. A free-form ISO-code example or multi-currency marketing would incorrectly imply broader support than the current EUR/USD API enum.
- F12, F14 and F16 need checkout-mode scope. A wallet working on a hosted custom domain does not prove it works inside an iframe or on renewal.
- F15 needs units and release scope. Repeating discounts are measured in months; payment retries differ from webhook retries; support-enabled auto-refill differs from a self-service portal feature.
- F19–F25 need base processing, negotiated plans and feature usage separated. A feature built into the platform can still carry a percentage or usage charge.
- F20 needs the fixed-fee currency and tax-inclusive base. The finance example uses imprecise fixed-fee typography/rounding; USD 0.40 comes from the explicit pricing schedule.
- F22 and F32 need charge, held balance, bank payout and stablecoin conversion kept separate. No USD/EUR ledger architecture or exact token conversion is inferred from withdrawal thresholds.
- F29–F30 need the buyer transaction and supplier-to-Creem self-billing treated separately. Estonian supplier VAT obligations are not erased by buyer MoR tax handling.
- F33, F36, F40 and F44 cover different waits and retention rules. A specific investigation limit is not a standard percentage reserve or guaranteed closure deadline.

- F41 is optional and was attempted, but neither a company registry number nor partner processing establishes a regulated licence or certification.
- C01–C05 apply here because stablecoin payouts exist. Most buyer blockchain-payment rules do not apply to the evidenced checkout; payout custody, exchange and recovery details still deserve explicit unknowns.
