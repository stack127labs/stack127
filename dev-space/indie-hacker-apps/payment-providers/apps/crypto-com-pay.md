**Operating and regulatory status:** Crypto.com Pay for Business is active. Its merchant help center was updated in July 2026; Crypto.com’s status page showed Checkout, Invoice, Subscriptions, Refunds, and Merchant Dashboard operational when checked. Crypto.com’s newsroom reported new Pay merchant distribution through Stripe and Yuno in January 2026 and a UAE checkout launch with Dubai Duty Free in August 2026. I found no company notice that the direct merchant service was retired, acquired, or renamed. The current name is Crypto.com Pay for Business; the row in apps.md points to its merchant signup page, not the consumer app’s Pay features. Do not read the US, Irish, or UAE regulatory registrations below as one licence covering all Crypto.com Pay services. The merchant contract is country-specific, and the direct signup page does not publish a full country-to-entity matrix.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Direct merchant price:** the current merchant API documentation says there is no transaction fee and a payout fee applies. The merchant agreement says account-specific fees are in the Commercial Agreement. I recorded the public processing fee as zero and the payout charge as a real but unpublished cost.
