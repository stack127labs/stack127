## Cost examples — our calculation

These examples use Bangladesh Basic's published successful-payment percentages and one-time setup amount. They are calculations of known components, not complete quotes. Sources: https://sslcommerz.com/pricing/ (checked 2026-11-05). The page does not identify VAT treatment, a fixed transaction component, fee minimums/caps, foreign-card additions, conversion spreads or payout deductions. No unknown amount is assumed to be zero.

| Example | Inputs and conditions | Formula — our calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One standard payment | BDT 1,000 successfully collected by a standard channel at 2.5%; setup excluded | 1,000 × 0.025 | BDT 25 headline processing component; BDT 975 before unpriced deductions |
| One Amex payment | BDT 1,000 successfully collected at 3.5%; setup excluded | 1,000 × 0.035 | BDT 35 headline processing component; BDT 965 before unpriced deductions |
| First period, all standard | 100 successful payments × BDT 1,000; standard rate 2.5%; new merchant pays setup once | Gross = 100 × 1,000 = 100,000; processing = 100,000 × 0.025 = 2,500; known components = 25,500 + 2,500 | BDT 28,000 known setup + processing; 28% of this period's gross. This percentage includes the one-time setup, so it is not a recurring processing rate. |
| First period, mixed cards | 80 standard and 20 Amex successful payments × BDT 1,000; setup once | (80 × 1,000 × 0.025) + (20 × 1,000 × 0.035) + 25,500 | BDT 28,200 known components: BDT 2,000 standard processing + BDT 700 Amex processing + BDT 25,500 setup |

For an international buyer or non-BDT input, a complete calculation is unavailable.
