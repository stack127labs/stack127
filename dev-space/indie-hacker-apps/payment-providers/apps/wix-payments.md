**Scope and current status:** Wix Payments is Wix's integrated payment service for sellers using a Wix site. It is active. It is not a merchant of record: the seller remains the seller and handles the customer, product, tax, refund, and dispute obligations. A published site and a Premium or Studio site plan that allows payments are required. Wix plan subscription prices are separate from transaction processing fees.

Wix lists 15 seller countries. Its current announcement dated 2025-12-16 describes Stripe-powered local methods in 11 European markets. The official developer changelog records several Payments API methods and events deprecated on 2026-09-30; this affects custom integrations, not ordinary checkout. The Giropay connection article says the method is no longer supported, although the fee article still shows its old rate. The current Wix plan page says prices and currency vary by location and annual prices are paid up front; its region-specific checkout did not render in the public page extract. Wix's November 2026 plan article gives dollar-sign examples but does not identify the ISO currency, so those are not treated as confirmed US plan prices. (Sources: [Wix Payments country and product overview](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments); [Wix Europe payments announcement](https://www.wix.com/press-room/home/post/wix-payments-grows-europe-coverage-in-collaboration-with-stripe-offering-more-local-payments-options); [Wix developer changelog](https://dev.wix.com/changelog?featureType=REST); [Giropay availability](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-giropay-as-a-payment-method-through-wix-payments); [Wix plans](https://www.wix.com/plans); [Wix plan guide](https://www.wix.com/blog/wix-premium-plans).)
