## Cost examples: our calculation

All checked 2026-11-04. These examples use the standard USD rate, not a negotiated contract. Assume no affiliate referral, abandoned-cart recovery, refund, dispute or extra tax on provider fees. They calculate proceeds before payout unless stated. Product/sales-tax amounts are stated scenario inputs, not claims about tax law in every jurisdiction.

Source-backed fee inputs: 5% + USD 0.50/order from https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing; total-order calculation base, outside-US +1.5%, subscriptions +0.5%, PayPal +1.5% and payout rates from https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees. Dates: 2026-11-04.

| Scenario | Inputs and formula | Our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US card, one-time, small sale | USD 5 total; fee = 5 × 0.05 + 0.50 | Fee USD 0.75; seller proceeds USD 4.25; fee/charge 15%. |
| US card, one-time | USD 20 total; fee = 20 × 0.05 + 0.50 | Fee USD 1.50; proceeds USD 18.50; fee/charge 7.5%. |
| Outside-US card, tax added | USD 20 product + assumed USD 4 sales tax = USD 24 charge; fee = 24 × (0.05 + 0.015) + 0.50 | Fee USD 2.06; sales tax USD 4 removed; proceeds USD 17.94. Fee/charge 8.583333…%; fee/product price 10.3%. |
| US card subscription | USD 20 total; fee = 20 × (0.05 + 0.005) + 0.50 | Fee USD 1.60; proceeds USD 18.40; fee/charge 8%. |
| Outside-US PayPal subscription | USD 20 total; fee = 20 × (0.05 + 0.015 + 0.015 + 0.005) + 0.50 | Fee USD 2.20; proceeds USD 17.80; fee/charge 11%. |

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Approval estimates conflict: https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/activate-your-store says 2 to 3 business days, while https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/guides/getting-started says 1 to 2. Re-verification is a separate stage. No launch-date guarantee follows from either estimate.
- Refund fee return is unresolved: https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/payments/refunds-chargebacks describes the payout deduction as refund minus platform fee, while https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/terms says fees are non-refundable unless otherwise expressly stated. https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/orders/refund-order did not clarify the original-fee accounting. Do not force this into a simple retained/returned boolean.
- Tax liability wording needs clarification: the sales-tax help page and buyer terms describe MoR handling; merchant terms section 7.5 also assigns sales/use-tax responsibility to Customer. Keep the operational MoR facts and legal wording separate. The full tax-registration/filing jurisdiction matrix was not found; MoR coverage does not erase supplier income-tax duties.
- Terms also disclaim certain seller risks broadly while MoR docs describe taking responsibility. The specific chargeback docs still describe seller deductions. Do not market this as no seller financial exposure.
- Payout schedule page says twice monthly and gives the 1st/15th and 14th/28th dates, but its threshold paragraph says rollover happens “each week.” Twice-monthly schedule is recorded with this inconsistency retained. Bank arrival is stated as 1 to 5 days without a business/calendar label.
- Seller list is payout-based. The complete bank-country list is recorded as ISO codes in F03; PayPal country availability is delegated by Lemon Squeezy to PayPal. A full per-country seller legal-form/owner-residency/bank-country matrix was not established. India has the explicit bank-onboarding caveat. Do not interpret “Global” in apps.md as unrestricted signup everywhere.
- Fees not established: full local-method-specific exceptions, fee-tax treatment by seller country, any inactivity/minimum charge, separate metering add-on, returned-on-win dispute fee, and specific closure fee.
