Scope: Chargebee Billing is subscription billing software. It is not a merchant of record or payment processor for this record. It connects to gateways including Stripe, Braintree, Adyen, and others; those gateways set and charge their own processing fees. Chargebee separately lists Chargebee Pay for pre-qualified Billing customers; that distinct acquiring offer is outside this Billing comparison. No account was created or accessed. Sources: https://www.chargebee.com/company/terms/ and https://www.chargebee.com/docs/payments/2.0/payment-gateways-and-configuration/getting-started.

The main pricing page now shows Flow and Enterprise, a monthly invoicing-volume slider, and two Flow fee choices. An official secondary Chargebee pricing page still shows older Starter and Performance prices and a currency selector. Both are recorded below; the older values are not treated as the current primary offer. The main page's slider was visibly set up to USD 500,000 monthly invoicing volume and shows a USD 66,000 breakeven marker; no annual/monthly switch appears there. The secondary page exposes USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, and INR buttons, but its indexed USD view was the only price value recoverable during this check. Sources: https://www.chargebee.com/pricing/ and https://www.onechargebee.com/pricing/index.html.
