## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Country coverage:** The support page lists eight seller countries. The current payment-method docs list collection options for five. Terms allow companies outside Benin to apply, subject to Compliance review, but give no country list. The Status page lists additional operators in Burkina Faso, Mali, Guinea, Côte d’Ivoire, and Senegal, several marked major outage. These pages do not settle a single current seller and method matrix.
- **Currencies:** The current funds page says only XOF transactions are supported. The current country-support page labels Guinea as GNF; an older docs-v1 page lists XOF and GNF for cards. Current GNF processing is unresolved.
- **Methods and status:** Current integration docs omit some rails shown on Status; the Status page showed several major outages without visible timestamps. I recorded the listed methods and outage labels separately.
- **Recurring billing:** Marketing and help pages use “subscription” or “recurring,” while product documentation only confirms repeatable collections/pages. Automatic renewals, stored credentials, and retries were not documented.
- **License wording and dates:** Legal Notices call EP.BN.004/2026 an aggregator accreditation but say the page was last updated in 2023; Privacy Policy calls it an Establishment of Payment licence. No official registry entry was checked in this research.
- **Legacy company name:** 2018 docs-v1 identify Nautilus Technology SARL; current legal pages identify FEDAPAY SA. The official pages checked do not explain the corporate relationship.
- **Pricing scope:** The live pricing page presents the full collection fee schedule for Benin. Rates for other countries and international-issued cards were not found. Fee amounts and payout bands were readable page text; only decorative method icons were images, so OCR was not needed. Mass-payout fee bands are published, but the fee unit (per recipient or batch) is not explicit. One band is printed with the unusual lower bound “50.001”; its punctuation is left unresolved.
- **Other values not published:** The pages checked do not state card dispute fees, standard reserve percentage/duration, tax filing or remittance responsibilities, conversion charges, payment-credential migration, or a fixed timeline for paying ordinary remaining funds after closure. Privacy retention periods are recorded in F40.
