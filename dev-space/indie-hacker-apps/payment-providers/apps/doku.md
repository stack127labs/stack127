## Acquisition, rebrand and service notices

**DOKU is now branded DOKU by Antom.** Its [official 2026 announcement](https://www.doku.com/blog/locally-trusted-globally-connected-dokus-next-step) says it became part of Antom, Ant International. It also records the earlier NSIApay name and the 2022 senangPay acquisition. A percentage ownership stake is not given. Checked 2026-11-05.

No provider-wide shutdown or sunset notice was found in the official news/docs searches. Current signup, pricing and October/November 2026 QRIS notices remain public. This is not a claim that every service has no current incident: the [status page](https://status.doku.com/) could not be read without JavaScript. Checked 2026-11-05.

**Important current fee change:** eligible new QRIS merchants approved by PTEN on/after **1 November 2026** pay IDR1,000 plus 11% PPN for transactions up to IDR100,000. Existing merchants follow their individual arrangements. The separate MDR change started 1 October 2026. [Official QRIS notice](https://docs.doku.com/changelog/october-2026/qris-mdr-and-processing-fee-updates.md), checked 2026-11-05.

Scope: DOKU gateway acceptance and its related billing/checkout features. Indonesia is the index row's main market. Malaysia is recorded separately where official DOKU documents publish different conditions. The separate senangPay-DOKU index entry overlaps this country offer; see index notes below. Domestic Payouts and sub-accounts are separate optional products.
