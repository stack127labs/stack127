## Status evidence beyond a homepage

- [Coinbase's transition notice](https://help.coinbase.com/en/transitioning-from-coinbase-commerce-to-coinbase-business) supplies the Commerce cutoff. The current Business offer was also opened.
- [Sellix's official statement](https://sellix.com/enhasa) acknowledges the seizure. It does not establish a relaunched merchant service.
- [Synergy Software's merchant support notice](https://www.synergy.com/knowledge-base-2/who-processes-the-online-orders-and-how-do-i-contact-them/) says its former providers, MyCommerce and Digital River, ceased operations. This is a former merchant's statement, not a current statement from Digital River. The old MyCommerce domain is now unrelated.
- [Lemon Squeezy's acquisition post](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/blog/stripe-acquires-lemon-squeezy) and [2026 update](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/blog/2026-update) show Stripe ownership and ongoing communication. Its current product site still offers signup. Keep Lemon Squeezy in the main list; acquisition alone does not mean shutdown.
- [Payoneer Checkout](https://www.payoneer.com/checkout/) contains conflicting-looking entry terms on the same page. This needs a fresh product-specific check before research assumes a $20,000 minimum applies to every offer.
- [Maxio pricing](https://www.maxio.com/pricing) supplies its public entry cost. A high cost is a fit question, not proof that a solo founder is barred.

No allegation from a community post was used as shutdown, fraud, payout, licensing, or product-eligibility proof.
