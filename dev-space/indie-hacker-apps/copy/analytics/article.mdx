Each tool is priced for one site and one user at 10,000, 100,000, or 1 million pageviews a month. A tool billed another way, such as by tracked revenue, shows "not applicable" instead of a number.

"Cookieless" means the tool says it sets no cookies by default. It doesn't cover every kind of visitor tracking, so read the note next to it.

Unknown means we couldn't find the answer on the vendor's pages. It never means no.
