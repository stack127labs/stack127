Each service is priced for 1,000, 10,000, 50,000, or 100,000 users who log in during the month. Libraries are free code you add to your app. Self-hosted servers are free software, and you pay for the server they run on.

Business login (SSO) and organization limits are priced on their own. The SSO line on each card shows that price, or says it isn't published.
