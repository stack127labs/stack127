Every host is priced for the same three workloads:

- **Static site:** 1 million requests and 100 GB of data out a month.
- **Small app API:** 1 million requests and 100 GB out a month.
- **App + Postgres:** the small app API plus a Postgres database with 1 GiB of memory and 10 GB of storage.

A server panel runs on your own server, so you pay for that server on top of any panel fee.

The free label says whether you get a free plan, a trial, credits, or no free start.
