# Email and newsletters: app discovery

Checked on 2026-11-04. This is a discovery list, not finished app research or a ranking. Each official URL below was opened. A working product page, documentation, download, or current offer supports inclusion. No account was created and no email was sent. A live website does not prove uptime, delivery quality, or that a new account will be approved.

Types: **Newsletter** publishes to an audience; **Marketing** sends campaigns and sequences; **Delivery** sends email through an API (a connection from your code) or SMTP (a standard way to pass email to a sending server); **Lifecycle** sends based on customer actions; **Notification layer** manages messages through another sending service. One primary type per row keeps counts clear. Secondary uses and hosting labels stay in the type column.

`active` means the official material presents an available product. `gray` means the product exists but its fit, entry cost, maintenance, or current positioning needs an owner decision. Unconfirmed sites are in excluded.md, marked `check again`, rather than counted as confirmed apps. Self-hostable means you run the software; it does not mean open source. An open-source claim below still needs its exact license checked in the next phase.

Region describes an obvious market or language focus on the opened site, not a verified company address or data location. `Not established` is deliberate. Discovery codes link to the source register in notes.md. `Seed` means supplied in the brief. `Sweep` means a researcher-supplied candidate checked directly on its official site; it does not claim an independent recommendation.
