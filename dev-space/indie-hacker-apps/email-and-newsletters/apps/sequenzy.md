## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Free sending and payment card:** Pricing and About say the free plan starts without a card; the Terms require a valid payment method before any sending and manual review before full sending. Public pages do not clarify whether a free account may send before adding a card.
- **Daily sending limit:** Pricing says there is no daily email limit. The current billing docs give a hard 300,000 accepted emails per rolling 24 hours, per company workspace. Use the explicit docs cap for capacity planning, but preserve the conflict.
- **Sender-health thresholds:** Terms say sending may be paused above 5% bounce or 0.1% complaints. The detailed sender-health docs instead use volume-aware counts/rates on all-time totals. The pages do not fully reconcile the legal and operational rule sets.
- **Overage billing:** Docs say usage keeps accumulating beyond the monthly tier, but pricing, billing docs, and Terms do not publish an overage price, automatic upgrade rule, spend cap, or credit expiry.
- **Data residency:** The DPA names subprocessors and says specific hosting regions can be provided on request. No fixed customer-selectable data region or residency commitment is published.
- **Customer account security:** Security pages document internal MFA and OAuth sign-in, but do not establish a customer-facing second-factor option or API key scope controls.
- **Newsroom and status:** Direct requests to `/newsroom` and `status.sequenzy.com` did not load. The official blog and current product pages show activity; a public status history could not be verified.
- **Newsletter policy pages searched:** Terms, pricing, campaign docs, subscriber docs, and double-opt-in docs. The Terms are the definitive published consent and content rules; no separate newsletter-specific policy was found.
- **No independent delivery evidence:** Sequenzy's own dashboard, customer stories, and deliverability claims are vendor statements. No reproducible independent inbox test was located in the official materials checked.
