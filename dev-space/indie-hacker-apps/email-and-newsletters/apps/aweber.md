**Paid-newsletter example — our calculation, incomplete all-in total:** US creator; 1,000 total subscribed contacts, including 100 paying readers at USD 10/month; 100 successful US domestic-card payments, no conversion, refunds or disputes. Send four free issues to all 1,000 plus four premium issues to the 100 tagged paying readers: (1,000 × 4) + (100 × 4) = 4,400 recipient emails. One premium saved segment is enough for this example. Both 1,000-contact Lite and Plus fit; recurring billing does not by itself require Plus.

- Gross monthly revenue = 100 × USD 10 = USD 1,000.
- Lite: USD 25 subscription + (1% × USD 1,000 = USD 10 AWeber fee) + [100 × (2.9% × USD 10 + USD 0.30) = USD 59 Stripe processing] = **USD 94 in the listed components**. Gross minus these components = USD 906.
- Plus: USD 45 subscription + (0.60% × USD 1,000 = USD 6 AWeber fee) + USD 59 processor fees = **USD 110 in the listed components**. Gross minus these components = USD 890.
