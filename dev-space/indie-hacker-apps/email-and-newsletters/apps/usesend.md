## Cost examples

Prices are shown with the `$` symbol used by the vendors; neither pricing page displays an ISO currency code. useSend’s calculator labels its estimate before taxes. Formulas and outputs below are **our calculations**.

### Hosted Cloud

- **Newsletter baseline:** one publication, one user, one verified sender domain, no attachments, four campaigns per month, and one email per subscribed contact per campaign. Monthly total is `max($10 minimum usage, marketing emails × $0.001)`. At 1,000 subscribers, 4,000 sends cost $4 in usage, so the minimum bill is $10. At 5,000, 10,000, and 50,000 subscribers the send totals are 20,000, 40,000, and 200,000; estimated bills are $20, $40, and $200.
- **App-email baseline:** one user, one recipient per message, one sender domain, shared sending, no attachments, volume spread across the month. Monthly total is `max($10 minimum usage, transactional emails × $0.0004)`, except the Free plan covers 3,000/month and 100/day. The 3,000 case is $0 only when spread at no more than 100/day; 10,000, 50,000, and 100,000 sends cost $10, $20, and $40 before tax.

### Self-hosted open-source edition

- **Send-volume-only scenario:** one recipient per email, AWS US East (N. Virginia), `us-east-1`, SES Essentials plan, 0–10 million outbound emails/month; AWS shows `$` without an ISO code. Formula: `email recipients × $0.16 / 1,000`. For F09 this gives 4,000 = $0.64; 20,000 = $3.20; 40,000 = $6.40; 200,000 = $32. For F10 it gives 3,000 = $0.48; 10,000 = $1.60; 50,000 = $8; 100,000 = $16. This new-account/account-region Essentials assumption follows the AWS plan effective July 21, 2026; eligible accounts can switch to à-la-carte pricing.
- These are AWS SES send-volume charges only, **not a full self-hosted total**. AWS separately lists outbound mail data at `$0.12/GB`; total cost also depends on message size, host, PostgreSQL/Redis, SNS usage, domain/DNS, storage, region, and tax. useSend publishes no fixed hosting baseline, so a complete total cannot be calculated from the available facts.
- A new SES account starts in the sandbox per region: 200 messages per rolling 24 hours, 1 per second, and verified recipients only. A production-access request is needed for ordinary recipient sending. These AWS constraints do not apply to hosted Cloud customers as their own account limits; hosted Cloud has its separately published useSend caps.
