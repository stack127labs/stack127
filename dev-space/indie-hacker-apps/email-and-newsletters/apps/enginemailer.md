## Cost examples: our calculation

All figures below are USD. The [current pricing page](https://www.enginemailer.com/pricing) publishes the selector's monthly fee and per-email overage in both option attributes and structured offers. These agreed when checked on 2026-11-05. This avoids using a search snippet or assuming every tier has the entry tier's overage rate.

Formula for a paid tier: monthly email fee = base subscription + max(0, recipient emails − included quota) × that tier's overage rate. [Terms](https://www.enginemailer.com/legal/termsofservice), checked 2026-11-05 and updated 2026-08-28, permit overage and say unused allowance expires each cycle.

Enginemailer does not promise to select the cheapest option automatically; the customer must choose their tier.
