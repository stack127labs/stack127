## Cost examples: our calculation

These are calculated pre-tax software-and-sending subtotals, not checkout quotes or delivery guarantees. No promotional coupon, free credit, SMS, paid-reader revenue or optional add-on is included.

Inputs: one publication/business, one user and an existing verified domain. Use the standalone Standard plan at USD 19/month for up to 10,000 subscribed contacts, or Unlimited at USD 79/month. Current card metadata identifies USD and supplies monthly and prepaid totals: https://www.pabbly.com/email-marketing/ (checked 2026-11-05; update not stated). Plan/cap guide: https://docs.pabbly.com/pabbly/doc/pabbly-email-marketing-unlimited-susbscribers-and-email-sending-supsbfqg4j (checked and updated 2026-11-05). The 50,000-contact example needs approval above the initial 25,000; terms cap reviewed increases at 100,000 per Unlimited plan: https://www.pabbly.com/terms-conditions/ (checked 2026-11-05; updated 2026-10-22).

Named external sender: Amazon SES, us-east-1, shared IP, production access approved and quota sufficient for the workload. Explicitly choose **à la carte** at USD 0.10 per 1,000 outbound emails, with no minimum fee. New accounts may default to Essentials, which has a different tariff. No attachments, paid validation, dedicated IP, Global Endpoints, Mail Manager, archiving or deliverability add-on. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/ses/pricing/ (checked 2026-11-05; no single page update date stated). Quotas: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/manage-sending-quotas.html (checked 2026-11-05; update not stated). Domain registration, a mailbox if wanted, tax and implementation work are outside these subtotals.

Formula: `monthly subtotal = standalone monthly plan + (recipient emails / 1,000 × USD 0.10)`.
