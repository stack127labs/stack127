## Cost examples: our calculations

Checked 2026-11-04. Newsletter baseline: one publication, one user, one audience, unique subscribed contacts, and four emails to each contact per month. There are no duplicate audience memberships, unsubscribed/non-subscribed billable records, SMS, paid-reader revenue, ads, optional IPs or other add-ons in this example. Peak stored billable count stays at the stated baseline throughout the month.

Essentials supplies the needed newsletter job. Its send allowance is 10 × the selected contact limit. Sources: https://mailchimp.com/help/about-mailchimp-pricing-plans/, https://mailchimp.com/en/pricing/marketing/compare-plans/. The current calculator did not expose three selected-band fees. The USD 385 tier at 50,000 contacts is explicitly published in the comparison table.

| Subscribed contacts | Our send calculation | Essentials monthly allowance | Our monthly cost calculation, before tax |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 | 1,000 × 10 = 10,000 | Unknown selected-band base + USD 0 baseline overage; total unresolved |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | 5,000 × 10 = 50,000 | Unknown selected-band base + USD 0 baseline overage; total unresolved |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | 10,000 × 10 = 100,000 | Unknown selected-band base + USD 0 baseline overage; total unresolved |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 4 = 200,000 | 50,000 × 10 = 500,000 | USD 385 base + USD 0 baseline overage = USD 385/month |

No full annual upfront amount was established. Multiplying a monthly price by 12 would be a projection at monthly rates, not an annual-plan quote. Current introductory offers are excluded. Counts of unsubscribed and non-subscribed records can raise the real bill even though those records cannot receive an ordinary marketing campaign. Contact/send overage blocks depend on the tier and billing term; source https://mailchimp.com/help/about-additional-charges/.

For lifecycle marketing at the same audience/send pairs, select a plan with the needed flow steps and branches, then price that contact tier. Standard is required for the documented advanced branching features. Those larger Standard contact prices were not recovered; do not reuse the USD 20 entry base for a 50,000-contact journey. No independent per-workflow-run price was established.

App-email baseline: one user, one verified/authenticated sender domain, shared Transactional sending IP, one recipient per message, no attachments, zero marketing workflow runs, and no marketing audience above the Standard plan's included 500 contacts. Recipient data remains in the application, as documented at https://mailchimp.com/developer/transactional/docs/fundamentals. If the creator also stores more marketing contacts, the marketing base must be repriced.

Our formula is `total/month = USD 20 Standard base + ceil(outbound emails / 25,000) × USD 20 per block`.

| App emails/month | Our block calculation | Purchased quota | Our total/month before tax | Uniform schedule: planning daily maximum over 30 days |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3,000 | ceil(3,000 / 25,000) = 1 | 25,000 | 20 + 1 × 20 = USD 40 | 100 |
| 10,000 | ceil(10,000 / 25,000) = 1 | 25,000 | 20 + 1 × 20 = USD 40 | 334 |
| 50,000 | ceil(50,000 / 25,000) = 2 | 50,000 | 20 + 2 × 20 = USD 60 | 1,667 |
| 100,000 | ceil(100,000 / 25,000) = 4 | 100,000 | 20 + 4 × 20 = USD 100 | 3,334 |

Sources: https://mailchimp.com/pricing/transactional-email/ for blocks and the required plan; https://mailchimp.com/en/pricing/marketing/compare-plans/ for the ordinary Standard base. Daily planning values are rounded upward from volume ÷ 30 and are **our assumptions/calculations**, not vendor daily quotas. Spread sends through each day; no per-second burst is promised. Reputation-based hourly quotas and a 48-hour backlog limit still apply (https://mailchimp.com/developer/transactional/docs/reputation-rejections). Unused block credits expire at renewal; they are not a balance to carry into later months. The monthly marketing allowance is not used to pay for these separately purchased Transactional blocks.

A dedicated Transactional IP would add USD 29.95/month if selected and approved; our optional example totals are the above base-plus-block amounts + USD 29.95. Domain registration/mailbox, integrating application hosting, tax, SMS, premium integration fees and paid support arrangements remain outside these provider-only totals. Source: https://mailchimp.com/developer/transactional/docs/authentication-delivery.

Paid-newsletter needs are an external-integration scenario. Our formula is `Mailchimp contact-band cost + paid-newsletter integration fee + reader-payment processor fees + applicable FX/tax/payout charges`. Mailchimp lists Campaignzee/Stripe and MoonClerk integrations (https://mailchimp.com/integrations/campaignzee/, https://mailchimp.com/integrations/moonclerk/), but these pages do not establish an exact worldwide total. The same marketing plan can send the resulting paid-reader segment; this is not proof that the checkout/membership service costs nothing.

## Field problems

- F06/F09 compare unique subscribed readers, while Mailchimp bills a larger set of contact statuses and duplicates across audiences. Baselines need one audience and no extra billable records; peak billing must remain visible.

- F13/F14/F16 need to keep external reader billing and recurring-payment mandates separate from Mailchimp contact exports.

- F19 needs the editor edition as well as the paid plan. Block-level HTML editing does not settle whole-template import support.
- F29/F30 distinguish disabling reports, abuse-mitigation redirects, bot filters, message body storage, logs, statistics, archives and backups. No single tracking-off or retention number covers all of them.
