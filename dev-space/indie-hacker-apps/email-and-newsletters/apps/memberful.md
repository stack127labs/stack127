## Cost examples — our calculation

### Free-reader newsletter baseline on a live account

Inputs: one publication, one user, one native hosted newsletter; unique subscribed members; four free issues a month on separate days; zero paid-reader revenue, sales, ads or external email-service use. An eligible connected Stripe account and active Standard software subscription are required to email posts. This is a production workload, not free test mode.

Formula: recipient emails = contacts × 4. Known monthly Memberful cost = USD 49 + (4.9% × USD 0) = **USD 49**. Native newsletter sending has no separate published charge.

| Unique contacts | Issues/month | Recipient emails/month | Known Memberful subtotal/month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 4 | 4,000 | USD 49 |
| 5,000 | 4 | 20,000 | USD 49 |
| 10,000 | 4 | 40,000 | USD 49 |
| 50,000 | 4 | 200,000 | USD 49 |

Our calculations from [pricing](https://memberful.com/pricing), [Memberful’s own USD price statement](https://memberful.com/memberful-vs-substack) and [native newsletter inclusion](https://memberful.com/newsletters), checked 2026-11-04; source update dates not stated.

The docs’ [ten-email daily limit](https://memberful.com/docs/overview/setup-guides/member-newsletter) appears in the post-publishing workflow. Four issues on separate days fit that cadence, but the documentation does not define recipient-per-second throughput or a numeric monthly recipient quota. No capacity or inbox-placement test was run. The unlimited-member price statement is not an email delivery guarantee. Exact all-in cost is not established: software-invoice tax, optional domain/mailbox costs and any outside integrations are excluded. Free test mode cannot email or schedule posts; free reader registration does not waive live software fees.

No annual software offer or upfront annual total was established. Reader annual memberships are priced by the creator and are not an annual Memberful software discount.

### Paid-newsletter example

Inputs: one Standard live account; US business using USD; 100 paid readers charged USD 10 each in a month by domestic online card; every payment succeeds; no sales tax, discounts, refunds, currency conversion, instant payouts or external provider. Four newsletter issues are included.

- Gross monthly revenue = 100 × USD 10 = **USD 1,000**.
- Software subscription = **USD 49**.
- Memberful transaction fees = 4.9% × USD 1,000 = **USD 49**.
- Stripe card processing = 100 × [(2.9% × USD 10) + USD 0.30] = **USD 59**.
- Known fee subtotal = USD 49 + USD 49 + USD 59 = **USD 157**.
- Gross less these known fees = USD 1,000 − USD 157 = **USD 843**.

Our calculation, not an exact all-in take-home guarantee. Sources: [Memberful pricing](https://memberful.com/pricing), [its payment-processing explanation](https://memberful.com/docs/overview/getting-started/payment-processing) and [Stripe US pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing), checked 2026-11-04; update dates not stated. Applicable taxes, account-specific processing terms, payout/bank extras and any other applicable billing services can change the result.

Stripe separately publishes 0.7% of Billing volume. Public Memberful material does not settle that charge for every configuration: the payment guide says Memberful manages subscriptions, the migration guide removes old Stripe Subscription objects, and comparison copy says Memberful uses Stripe Subscriptions. Therefore the example does not silently assert either universal Billing exemption or universal applicability. **If** 0.7% applies to all USD 1,000, our additional calculation is USD 7, producing USD 164 in known fees and USD 836 left before other costs. This is a conditional calculation, not an extra quoted Memberful charge. Sources and scope are recorded in F14/F16.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **No unresolved direct price conflict found.** Current Standard pricing, live billing and official comparisons agree on USD 49/month plus 4.9%. Enterprise is custom priced. No annual author-plan price, guaranteed author-refund window or author-invoice tax treatment was established after checking pricing, live billing, terms and closure help.
- **Stripe ownership does not settle recurring-payment portability.** The [ownership article](https://memberful.com/blog/member-data-ownership) says the connected Stripe account and payment relationship belong to the creator (published 2026-08-03). The [incoming migration guide](https://memberful.com/docs/overview/import-members/using-stripe-subscriptions) describes deleting old Stripe Subscription objects after migration, while the [beehiiv comparison](https://memberful.com/memberful-vs-beehiiv) says both platforms use Stripe Subscriptions. The latter two have no captured update date; all checked 2026-11-04. These could refer to different stages or implementations, so the record marks the representation unclear rather than asserting a proven contradiction. A portable payment method is not a transferred renewal schedule.
- **Move before closing.** [Stripe account switching](https://memberful.com/docs/account-management/account-settings/switch-your-stripe-account) requires migration and support resync before renewals work. [Data Protection](https://memberful.com/data-protection) says account closure deletes Memberful data and discusses deleting data from connected services, with external-service limitations. [Closure help](https://memberful.com/docs/account-management/account-settings/close-your-account) instructs export first. Checked 2026-11-04; update dates not stated. No universal automatic outgoing subscription-transfer procedure was established.
- **Ten email sends versus recipients.** Both the [post guide](https://memberful.com/docs/newsletters/hosted-newsletter/create-a-post) and [newsletter setup guide](https://memberful.com/docs/overview/setup-guides/member-newsletter) state ten emails/day, in the context of publishing issues to member audiences. Checked 2026-11-04; update dates not stated. Neither defines a per-recipient rate, reset timezone, burst guarantee or monthly recipient maximum. Do not convert this to ten delivered recipient messages or unlimited delivery throughput. Higher daily use requires support discussion.
- **Free mode is a test environment.** [Test-mode setup](https://memberful.com/docs/account-management/modes/manage-your-account-in-test-mode) and [post publishing](https://memberful.com/docs/newsletters/hosted-newsletter/create-a-post) establish the live-Stripe requirement and deletion of test members/orders. Checked 2026-11-04; update dates not stated. Adding a billing method enables limited transactional tests, not production newsletter sending.
- **Two sender paths must stay separate.** [White-label SMTP](https://memberful.com/docs/configuration/emails/white-label-transactional-emails) documents transactional messages and moves delivery logs to the provider. It does not explicitly say that native newsletter posts can use a custom From domain, custom SMTP or a branded tracking domain. Searched newsletter/editor, branding/domain, SMTP, security and email-template docs; those newsletter controls and native SPF/DKIM/DMARC setup remain unknown.
- **Exports have useful scope, with gaps.** [Export help](https://memberful.com/docs/metrics-and-analytics/compare-and-export/export-data) includes Subscribed to Posts, fields and subscription state, and warns that one person can have several subscription rows. Searched member management, post/API docs, terms and closure help too. Full historical consent, media-complete content export and transferable bounce/complaint suppression history were not established. Checked 2026-11-04; update date not stated.
- **Native safeguards not fully established.** Searched security, staff, profile, API and changelog pages for two-factor/MFA, passkeys and key permissions. Staff email verification, restricted dashboard access and SSO are documented, but no native MFA configuration or complete SSO plan/provider eligibility was established. General blog advice about other products and AWS facility access controls were not used as account-MFA proof.
- **Privacy scope is broader than the US production-hosting statement.** [Data Protection](https://memberful.com/data-protection) identifies US application/database hosting. The [DPA](https://memberful.com/data-processing-addendum) allows international processing and says the complete Annex I subprocessor list is available on request. Checked 2026-11-04; protection update not stated, DPA latest listed change 2024-03-15. Exact email-routing/log/backup regions, residency choice and every processor’s data-erasure schedule remain unknown. No request was submitted.
- **Deletion has a legal qualification.** Public protection/terms pages describe 30-day log/backup destruction, while the DPA allows legally required retention and isolated backups. Retain the contractual qualification rather than promise a universal deadline covering every external processor and legal obligation.
- **No general sending API or external benchmark established.** GraphQL, membership webhooks and SMTP configuration are integration interfaces. Searched those docs for arbitrary email sending, rate limits, delivery callbacks, message idempotency and inbound routing. No general relay workload quote or reproducible independent inbox test was established.
- **Fetch limits did not block the core evidence.** Guessed /privacy/ failed, but the linked /privacy-policy page worked. Several advertised Markdown documentation URLs were rejected by the web tool; their HTML equivalents were readable. A direct read-only local fetch failed DNS and was not used for facts. GitHub readme/raw fetches failed, but the official plugin PHP header exposed license/version/requirements. None of these recovered failures is counted as an unresolved field failure.
