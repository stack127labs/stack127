## Conflicts and unknowns

- **100,000 API price:** The current Email API pricing page exposes the starting 50,000 tier and the slider range, but not the selected 100,000 amount in its text view. Official Elastic Email comparisons say $29/month at 100,000, including the July 2026 SMTP2GO comparison and the May 2026 Resend comparison. Because those price snapshots are older than the current check, the exact current figure remains unclear.
- **Sender domain vs single sender email:** The current Getting Started guide says an account requires at least one verified domain. A separate sender-email guide says an owned address can be verified when full domain verification is not possible. The pages do not settle whether the address-only route meets the current account-level requirement.
- **Complaint threshold:** The Delivery Status help page links a Complaints Threshold Guide at `https://help.elasticemail.com/reports/what-is-the-complaints-threshold`. Opening the linked page and its direct URL returned a cache-miss fetch error. Terms and Acceptable Use describe suspension for complaints but no percentage threshold. This field is `check_failed`, not a claim that no threshold exists.
- **Not published in checked sources:** Account-country eligibility; complete language list; API billing rule for multi-recipient messages; current tax policy; exact paid-plan email size and messages-per-second maximum; duplicate-send/idempotency guarantee; named subprocessor schedule; exact Stripe processing rates and payout-country list; full content and consent-event export; recurring paid-subscriber migration; automation-run quota; referral/ad network; ultimate parent and headquarters; and independent inbox-placement results.
- **Shutdown/acquisition/news check:** Checked About, the company blog, the blog's Updates category, current Terms, and official-site searches for shutdown, sunset, acquisition, and rename notices. No such notice was found. The blog listing shows current 2026 posts, and current pricing/help pages remain live.
