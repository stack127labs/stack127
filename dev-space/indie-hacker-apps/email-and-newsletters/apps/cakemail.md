## Cost examples — our calculation

All examples use ordinary monthly USD rates before tax and optional services. They are our calculations, not vendor quotes for an individual customer. Sources were checked on 2026-11-05. No introductory discount, currency conversion or annual discount is applied.

### Newsletter baseline

One publication, one list, one user and unique subscribed contacts. Each subscriber receives four campaigns per month. No SMS, ad spend, paid-reader income or extra coaching service is included. The [official Growth rate table](https://www.cakemail.com/email-marketing/pricing) supplies both contact and monthly send allowances. [List counting](https://docs.cakemail.com/en/docs/audience/managing-contact-lists) confirms that duplicate addresses on separate lists count again.

Formula: recipient emails = unique subscribers × 4; monthly base = the Growth price whose contact and send caps both cover that workload.
