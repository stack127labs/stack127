## Cost examples — our calculation

### What the free plugin includes

Lite's software licence costs **USD 0**, with no plugin-imposed subscriber/email allowance. Delivery is a separate cost and host/provider limits still apply. It can use an existing WordPress mail transport or an additional compatible mailer plugin. The separately offered Icegram Mailer has **200 free emails/month**, usable with either Lite or paid Express; it cannot cover any of the four newsletter baseline volumes.

Sources: https://www.icegram.com/express/pricing/, https://plugins.svn.wordpress.org/email-subscribers/trunk/readme.txt, https://www.icegram.com/docs/express/configure-email-sending/ and https://www.icegram.com/docs/mailer/faq/, checked 2026-11-05. Do not report “free unlimited newsletter sending” as zero total cost. No extra paid Express licence is needed merely to retain a large audience in Lite; advanced features/native provider connectors have separate plan gates.

### Named paid setup used for the calculations

This is **one documented setup, not a cheapest-provider ranking or a measured server-capacity claim**:

- **Express Max:** USD 99 paid annually, covering up to three live sites; this model uses one site and one user. Our monthly accounting equivalent is `99 ÷ 12 = USD 8.25`. There is no ordinary USD 8.25 monthly plugin checkout offer. Source: https://www.icegram.com/express/pricing/, checked 2026-11-05.
- **Hosting:** one Amazon Lightsail Linux/Unix WordPress blueprint with public IPv4, **1 GB memory, 2 vCPUs, 40 GB SSD and 2 TB transfer**, **USD 7/month**, assuming us-east-1. This is a named cost illustration, not a vendor-published minimum or proof it can process 50,000 contacts on a busy store. Sources: https://aws.amazon.com/lightsail/pricing/ and https://docs.aws.amazon.com/lightsail/latest/userguide/amazon-lightsail-launch-and-configure-wordpress.html, checked 2026-11-05.
- **External sender:** Amazon SES **Essentials**, one region, shared sending IP, no dedicated IP or additional paid sending features. Its 0–10M band is **USD 0.16 per 1,000 recipient emails**, with no fixed Essentials account/region fee. Native SES integration requires Max. Sources: https://aws.amazon.com/ses/pricing/, https://www.icegram.com/docs/express/configure-amazon-ses-send-emails/ and https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/dedicated-ip.html, checked 2026-11-05.
- **Inputs:** one verified sender domain, permissioned recipients, one recipient per message, no attachments, no SMS/ads/paid-reader income, no introductory/free AWS credits. Other WordPress transactional messages, retries and tests are additional sending volume.
- **Variable costs:** AWS lists additional outgoing-mail-data charges and examples at USD 0.12/GB; network overages, domain/mailbox, backup storage, tax and operator work may also add cost. Exact billed mail-data volume and these extras are not specified by the newsletter count. The figures below are known subscription/hosting/outbound subtotals, not exact all-in invoices. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/ses/pricing/ and https://aws.amazon.com/lightsail/pricing/, checked 2026-11-05.

AWS now defaults eligible new accounts/unused account-region combinations to Essentials from July 21, 2026. Its separate à-la-carte outbound rate is lower, but that is a different selected billing option. These calculations deliberately use the current named **Essentials USD 0.16/1,000** rate rather than silently assuming the historical/à-la-carte USD 0.10 rate. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/ses/pricing/, checked 2026-11-05.

Known monthly-equivalent formula — **our calculation**:

`USD 99/year ÷ 12 + USD 7/month + (recipient emails/month ÷ 1,000 × USD 0.16)`

Full-cost formula — **our calculation**:

`known subtotal + billed outgoing-mail-data charge + network/storage/domain/mailbox/tax/operations extras`

The licence remains an annual cash payment. Hosting and SES are separately billed. An existing adequate WordPress site may have a different incremental hosting cost; do not add USD 7 automatically to every existing installation.
