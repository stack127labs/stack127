Illustrative paid-reader fee calculation, **not an exact take-home quote**:

- Inputs: 100 readers paying $10 monthly; one monthly transaction each; the vendor's displayed Stripe example of 2.9% + $0.30; Lite's $59 monthly entry price at a 1,000-contact audience. Sources: [subscription feature page](https://www.beehiiv.com/features/paid-subscriptions) and [current price guide](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/email-marketing-pricing), both checked 2026-11-04.

- beehiiv reader-subscription platform cut: $1,000 × 0% = $0.
- Known subtotal under those assumptions: $1,000 − $59 − $0 − $59 base = **$882/month before other costs**.
