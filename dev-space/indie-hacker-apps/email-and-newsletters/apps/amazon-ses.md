## Status, ownership, and product changes

Amazon SES is an active, hosted email delivery service. AWS still publishes current SES prices, docs, release notes, and support terms. I found no SES shutdown, sunset, or acquisition notice in the AWS Messaging Blog, AWS What's New, AWS service terms, or central services-in-sunset list checked. Amazon Pinpoint is a separate AWS service and reached its published end-of-support date on 2026-10-30. Its end of support is not an SES shutdown. Pinpoint used SES functionality for some email sending, and AWS published migration guidance before the deadline.

SES supports both transactional and marketing email through its API or SMTP relay. It is infrastructure, not a full newsletter editor. It has one account contact list and topic-level subscription management, but users supply their own campaign composer, scheduling and application logic. Amazon SES Mail Manager is a separately metered inbound processing and routing feature; its price is not included in the outbound examples below.

AWS introduced Essentials, Pro, and Enterprise pricing plans on 2026-07-21. New or inactive account-and-region combinations default to Essentials. The current USD SES price page does not assign its prices to a named AWS Region. The fixed Pro and Enterprise plan fees are explicitly per account per region. AWS also publishes an AWS European Sovereign Cloud SES pricing page in euros. That page has the former à-la-carte-only wording and still deducts 3,000 free messages in an example, while the newer central pricing announcement says the SES-specific free tier ended for new customers. The two sources do not explain whether the exception applies to the European Sovereign Cloud. I keep that price and free-tier question unresolved below.

## Cost examples

These are our calculations for SES outbound only, before tax, excluding other AWS products, dedicated IPs, deliverability add-ons, receiving, and support. Both public rate tables are email-delivery rates, not the cost of a hosted newsletter editor.

AWS's current USD pricing example uses a 32KB message and charges outgoing message data at USD $0.12 per GB. To make the message-size assumption visible, calculations below use 0.000032 GB per email. Essentials is USD $0.16 per 1,000 emails for the first 10 million emails/month. These use the displayed rate table, not an AWS calculator quote for a named commercial Region.

Formula for each monthly total: (recipient emails × $0.16 ÷ 1,000) + (recipient emails × 0.000032 GB × $0.12 per GB). One recipient per email. Campaign scenarios assume one publication and four sends to every unique contact monthly. Sending scenarios assume volume is spread across the month; production access is needed to send to unverified recipients.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Regional pricing:** The central USD SES page gives plan rates without a named AWS Region; Pro and Enterprise fixed fees are explicitly per account per Region. AWS Price List documentation describes regional price lists, but the public SES page did not identify a named-region USD amount. Do not describe USD examples as a US East or EU commercial-region quote.
- **European Sovereign Cloud price and free tier:** The European page lists euro à-la-carte pricing, says there are no subscriptions, and subtracts 3,000 messages in an example. The newer central SES plan announcement says plans are available in all SES Regions except UAE and Bahrain and says the SES-specific free tier ended for new customers. AWS did not explain the European page's difference. Its page also does not name the SES endpoint Region; the portal is for the European Sovereign Cloud. The free allowance for new European Sovereign Cloud SES accounts remains unresolved.
- **Free AWS credits:** Up to USD $200 in AWS Free Tier credit is account-level and eligibility-based. It is not included as a guaranteed SES allowance in the cost examples.
- **Approval:** AWS states an initial response within 96 hours for production-access reviews, but approval can take longer if AWS requests information. No universal production quota is published; it varies by account use case and Region.
- **Data location:** AWS documents regional resources and contractual DPA safeguards, but the pages checked do not give a complete per-message map of abuse scanning, short-term processing, and backups.

## Field problems

- The current USD SES pricing page does not state the AWS Region tied to its per-email rates. Its fixed plan fees are per account per Region. Cost calculations therefore retain the missing regional label rather than guessing one.
- The AWS European Sovereign Cloud SES price page retains a legacy free-message calculation and à-la-carte-only description that conflict with the current central AWS pricing announcement. Its displayed euro rate is not used to overwrite central USD prices.
