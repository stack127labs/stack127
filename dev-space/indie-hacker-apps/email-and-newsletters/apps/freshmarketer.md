## Cost examples — our calculation

These are our calculations for a new, verified USD Enterprise account. They use ordinary month-to-month service, one user, one newsletter where applicable, an already owned verified sending domain and no dedicated IP, attachments, SMS, WhatsApp, AI add-ons or paid-reader revenue. Taxes are excluded. The public price selector gives USD 18/month; USD 15 is the annual monthly equivalent, so the annual base is 12 × USD 15 = USD 180.

### Four newsletters per month

Inputs: N unique subscribed contacts, four recipient emails per contact per month. No extra opt-in, welcome or journey mail is included.

- Recipient emails = 4 × N.
- Included marketing contacts = 500.
- Extra contact blocks B = ceiling(max(0, N − 500) / 5,000).
- Modeled contact capacity = 500 + 5,000 × B.
- Starting-rate arithmetic = USD 18 + B × USD 100 per month.

| 1,000 | 4,000 | 1 | 5,500 | USD 18 + 1 × USD 100 = USD 118 | Unresolved |

These amounts illustrate the advertised starting contact-block rate; they are not validated quotes or claimed price floors. “Starting at” does not establish a flat rate at every larger tier. Confirmation mail consumes both contact and send allowances, so onboarding a new list changes the workload.
