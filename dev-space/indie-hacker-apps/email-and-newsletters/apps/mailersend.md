## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Annual pricing:** current pricing display and help pages disagree on yearly prices. The visible “Save 10%” label also does not match the current displayed annual amounts, which calculate to 20% below monthly. Monthly prices were used in examples.
- **Overage:** Hobby is consistently $1.50 per 1,000. Starter/Professional are published as starting at $1.30, while the current pricing card at 50,000 displays $0.90. The public pages do not explain which volume or configuration accounts for the difference.
- **Dedicated IP:** current pricing page restricts access to Enterprise accounts above 100,000 sends/week. Help lists Professional or Enterprise and $50/IP/month. Availability needs a current account-level check.
- **Professional limits:** pricing page says unlimited domains/templates subject to limits; help comparison gives 1,000 domains and 5,000 templates. The usage wording is not reconciled.
- **Webhook retries:** Help says 3 attempts at 10 and 100 seconds; API reference describes retries with exponential backoff for about 3 days. The docs do not clearly explain version or event conditions.
- **Unpublished or unclear items:** exact customer-country eligibility beyond sanctioned locations; dashboard/support language list; exhaustive billable event rules; a complete one-click account export; duplicate-send protection; separate named transaction/marketing streams; custom send region; backup deletion schedule; and numeric account reputation thresholds.
- For unclear or unpublished values, I checked relevant pricing, help, API docs, Terms, Anti-Spam Policy, Privacy Policy, or DPA as named in each row. A missing public statement is not treated as “no.”
