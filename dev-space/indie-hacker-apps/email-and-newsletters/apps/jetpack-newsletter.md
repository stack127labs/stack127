These examples use one publication and one user, before tax, with no advertising or paid-reader revenue in the baseline. Four emails to every unique subscribed contact produce the volumes below. They assume readers joined directly rather than importing a list. Jetpack's unlimited claim remains subject to fair usage, with no numeric burst guarantee established. | 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 | 40,000 | 0 incremental | Not comparable: hosting and other setup costs required |

For an existing imported list at every baseline size, Free's 100-import allowance is insufficient. Current Pro/Complete allow unlimited imports, with review above 10,000 in the import guide. The country/currency offers are kept separate. Sources checked 2026-11-04: https://jetpack.com/pricing/ and https://jetpack.com/social/.

Paid-reader illustration, also our calculation: 100 readers paying US$10 monthly = US$1,000 gross, with 100 successful US domestic-card transactions at Stripe's standard 2.9% + US$0.30. Stripe component = 1,000 × 0.029 + 100 × 0.30 = US$59. ## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Plan generations:** current pricing gives Free/Pro/Complete and explicitly establishes Pro's unlimited imports and 2% fee. The fee and import articles retain Growth/Security/legacy names. The Security page explains that existing Security/Growth customers keep their plans, while new bundle purchases use Pro/Complete. This resolves the names for current core Newsletter entitlements; older fees are recorded only for the named old editions. WordAds' current Pro eligibility is still unclear. Pages checked: https://jetpack.com/pricing/, https://jetpack.com/security/, https://jetpack.com/support/newsletter/import-subscribers/, https://jetpack.com/support/jetpack-earn-transaction-fees/ and https://jetpack.com/support/ads/.
- **10,000 is a review boundary:** the import article's “up to 10,000” wording is qualified by its statement that paid imports are unlimited and larger lists are reviewed. Free's 100-import limit is cumulative; directly acquired subscribers follow a different rule. This is not treated as conflicting evidence for a hard paid subscriber cap.
- **Monthly prices:** pricing, Security and Social pages were opened, including linked support material. Displayed monthly equivalents require annual or two-year payment. No checkout, currency selector requiring interaction, login or purchase was used. Ordinary monthly purchase prices and a globally cheapest import plan remain unknown. Regional EUR and R$ offers are not a contradiction or currency conversion.
- **Audience portability:** export, import, migration, paid-newsletter and Payments Block docs establish CSV email export and some migration paths. Complete consent/unsubscribe export and outbound recurring-payment continuity are not established. Do not promise that removing a subscriber also cancels a Stripe contract, or that a CSV transfers payment authority. Pages checked: https://jetpack.com/support/newsletter/export-subscribers/, https://jetpack.com/support/newsletter/import-subscribers/, https://jetpack.com/support/subscription-migration-tool/, https://jetpack.com/support/newsletter/paid-newsletters/ and https://jetpack.com/support/jetpack-blocks/payments-block/.
- **Sender and templates:** checked Newsletter overview, customisation, Subscribe block and delivery troubleshooting. Own sending/tracking-domain configuration, arbitrary email HTML templates and personalisation variables were not established. Email style/branding customisation is explicitly unavailable in the customisation guide. Provider From address, sender display name and website domain are recorded separately. ## Field problems

- F03/F12/F33/F34 span two operating models: the plugin and website are installed by the owner, while cloud newsletter sending remains hosted. Source licensing must not imply that the sending stack can be independently self-hosted.
- F08/F09 need ordinary monthly prices, but public extraction exposed annual/two-year equivalents and regional currencies. Free direct-signup sending can be compared incrementally; an exact new-site total or cheapest imported-list monthly total cannot.
- F14 combines subscription revenue, processor terms and bundle purchases. US Stripe fees are only an illustration, and no unsupported currency conversion or Stripe Billing assumption is added.
- F15/F16 must separate content ownership, email CSV portability, audience-record completeness and recurring payment authority. A single export checkbox would conceal unresolved conditions.
- F20/F29 need to distinguish website analytics segmentation from recipient send targeting. Subscriber profile data and website pageview caps do not by themselves establish email audience segments or send limits.
- F23/F27 describe a publisher rather than a message API: post recovery limits are useful, but application-email cost scenarios and API delivery promises are not comparable here.
- F37 remains an optional unknown. No independent inbox-placement study with a reproducible method was established; a plugin install count cannot replace it.
