## Cost examples — our calculation

### Free newsletter baseline

Inputs: one publication, one user, unique subscribed contacts, four free issues per month, no paid-reader revenue, no one-time purchases and no ads. Organic members or approved email-only imports are assumed eligible for the post audience. We use USD for the zero-charge illustration; no currency conversion is performed.

Formula: recipient emails = contacts × 4. Platform fee = 10% × paid revenue = 10% × USD 0 = USD 0. Additional email charge = USD 0 under the official newsletter offer.

| Contacts | Issues/month | Recipient emails/month | Platform and email fees/month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 4 | 4,000 | USD 0 |
| 5,000 | 4 | 20,000 | USD 0 |
| 10,000 | 4 | 40,000 | USD 0 |
| 50,000 | 4 | 200,000 | USD 0 |

These are our calculations from the [newsletter offer](https://www.patreon.com/product/newsletter) and [pricing](https://www.patreon.com/pricing), checked 2026-11-04, source update dates not stated. They are published fee results, not a tested throughput guarantee. [Imports require review](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/42838275491341-Importing-your-email-list-to-Patreon-Beta) (checked 2026-11-04; updated 2026-01-23). No numeric daily/monthly technical send allowance was established. Member preferences, consent and post access can reduce actual sends. For this workload, use free posts, not locked-post preview emails with different delivery rules. Optional domain registration is excluded.

### Paid-newsletter example

Inputs: a new standard-plan creator based in the US; USD payout currency; 100 monthly paid readers each charged USD 10 by web credit card; every charge succeeds; no sales tax, fee tax, currency conversion or refunds; one USD ACH payout. The newsletter has four issues that month. Email volume adds no separate fee.

- Gross monthly reader revenue = 100 × USD 10 = **USD 1,000**.
- Platform fee = 10% × USD 1,000 = **USD 100**.
- Processing = 100 × [(2.9% × USD 10) + USD 0.30] = 100 × USD 0.59 = **USD 59**.
- One ACH payout fee = **USD 0.25**.
- Known fees = USD 100 + USD 59 + USD 0.25 = **USD 159.25**.
- Creator receives = USD 1,000 − USD 159.25 = **USD 840.75** under these assumptions.

Our calculation, not a quoted subscription plan or a promise of take-home income. Rates and ACH fee: [standard-plan guide](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/36426991446797-A-standard-platform-fee-for-new-creators-effective-after-August-4-2025), checked 2026-11-04, updated 2026-11-02. No iOS App Store fee applies to this web-card example. Creator taxes, bank extras, disputes, refunds, failed payments or another payout route can change the result. Annual reader billing changes payment timing and the number of fixed processing charges; it is not an annual discount on Patreon’s creator platform fee. No annual software price is invented.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Export is not unrestricted portability.** The [newsletter product page](https://www.patreon.com/product/newsletter) says the mailing list belongs to the creator and emails can be exported. The [Creator Privacy Promise](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026912432-Data-Processing-Agreement-Creator-Privacy-Promise) treats Patreon as controller, restricts use of Member Data to membership services, and requires destruction when the creator leaves. Its [appendix](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/46148437741069-Appendix-to-creator-Privacy-Promise) confirms the controller/processor roles. All checked 2026-11-04; product update date not stated, agreement/appendix updated 2026-05-26 with agreement dated 2026-05-27. This needs a clear official explanation before promising unrestricted off-platform list use. Separate prior consent collected outside Patreon was not assessed.
- **Imported readers versus members.** The [2026-01-23 beta guide](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/42838275491341-Importing-your-email-list-to-Patreon-Beta) says readers without Patreon accounts stay outside active-member reports and Relationship manager. The [2026-07-06 newsletter guide](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/40292528073101-Post-and-share-your-newsletter-on-Patreon) broadly calls imports free members. The [2026-06-02 preference guide](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-gb/articles/45880305367565-Email-import-preferences) describes account-holder regional defaults. Checked 2026-11-04. Account status appears relevant, but these pages do not completely explain every import/reporting case or guarantee export coverage for email-only readers.
- **Fees vary by plan, transaction and payout route.** The [fee overview](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/11111747095181-Creator-fees-overview) explicitly applies standard-plan processing rates to all payment amounts. The [standard-plan guide](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/36426991446797-A-standard-platform-fee-for-new-creators-effective-after-August-4-2025) still labels processing tables above USD 3/local thresholds. Checked 2026-11-04; updated 2026-09-11 and 2026-11-02 respectively. No opposite standard-plan small-payment rate was established; the USD 10 example avoids that ambiguity. Legacy processing rates must not be applied to a new creator.
- **One-time-sale legacy dates differ.** The [fee FAQ](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/22581195376909-Creator-fees-FAQ) uses 2025-05-26 while the [overview](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/11111747095181-Creator-fees-overview) uses 2025-05-22 for older one-time-sale terms. Checked 2026-11-04; updated 2026-07-19 and 2026-09-11. No precise legacy one-time-sale tariff is assigned here. This discrepancy does not change the stated free newsletter or new-creator monthly-membership calculation.
- **A changing billing model is not a changed platform plan.** The [current billing guide](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002355991-How-membership-billing-works) and [annual guide](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041721372-Annual-memberships-creator-overview) describe the passed 2026-11-01 migration deadline. Checked 2026-11-04; updated 2026-11-02 and 2026-11-03. Older localized pages still describe charge-upfront/per-creation options; current US guides govern this record. Actual completion on any individual account was not tested.
- **Privacy access failed.** The current [Privacy Center policy](https://privacy.patreon.com/policies) returned only a JavaScript-required notice. Checked 2026-11-04; update date unreadable. The official help article labelled “Former Patreon Privacy Policy” and a dated 2020 policy URL were found, but neither was used to assert current residency, subprocessors or retention. Terms, Creator Privacy Promise, its appendix and deletion help were read; storage and mail-routing region choice remain unverified.
- **Sending infrastructure remains unknown.** Searched pricing, newsletter/editor instructions, domain setup, delivery troubleshooting and API documentation for SPF/DKIM/DMARC, custom From/tracking domains, suppression/bounce logs, IP choices and technical sending limits. No usable creator setup or numeric operational guarantees were established. Website DNS and API request counts are not substitutes.
- **Export and migration detail remains incomplete.** Searched member CSV help, Relationship manager, beta imports, post/API resources and terms for complete content downloads, consent/unsubscribe export, email-only-reader export and payment-token migration. Search results from ordinary creator-hosted Patreon pages were excluded as vendor proof. No recurring payment continuity off-platform was established.

Checked 2026-11-04; absolute update dates not captured. No current incident status, observed uptime or contractual uptime percentage is assigned.

## Field problems

- F06 should distinguish free members, paid members and email-only imported readers. These are audience/reporting units, not billable contact tiers.
- F08/F09 fit a revenue-share platform poorly if interpreted as fixed software subscriptions. USD 0 is valid only for the no-revenue baseline; paid revenue introduces processing, payout and possible tax costs.
- F10 is not comparable for Patreon’s native membership lifecycle messages. Their audience, trigger and run count cannot be replaced by an arbitrary outbound-email count.
- F15 “ownership” needs separate answers for export access, content copyright, member privacy choices and continued legal use after leaving.
- F18/F24 must separate a website custom domain from an authenticated sending domain. The former does not prove the latter.
- F23/F27 API request limits and membership webhooks describe integration traffic, not email transport or delivery callbacks.
- F28’s “DPA” checkbox could mislead: this agreement makes the creator the processor of Patreon Member Data. The appendix is not Patreon's own hosting/subprocessor inventory.
- F30 cannot infer log, backup or statutory retention from the account-deletion help’s 30-day process.
- F37 remains optional. No independent test was performed, and the official-source rule does not create independent delivery evidence.
