## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Business vs Pro label:** The current pricing cards and billing docs say Business for the $0.005 tier. A lower comparison block on the same pricing page retains “Pro” text. The calculations use the current Business name and billing rule; the conflicting page text should be cleaned up before publication. Sources checked 2026-11-06: https://www.courier.com/pricing; https://www.courier.com/docs/workspaces/billing.
- **EU region availability and setup:** The product docs describe an EU region in Ireland, available to paid plans, but the Security docs say it requires arrangement and the DPA updated September 12, 2026 says Courier data-center infrastructure is in the United States. The product docs do not settle how the formal DPA statement applies to EU-configured workspaces. Confirm the residency claim and activation path with Courier before relying on it. Sources checked 2026-11-06: https://www.courier.com/docs/workspaces/datacenters; https://www.courier.com/docs/workspaces/security; https://www.courier.com/data-processing-addendum.
- **Newsletter policy:** Terms ban spam and unauthorized or unsolicited advertising. Courier's marketing guide describes opt-in, unsubscribe, and preference controls. The policy does not amount to a ban on all marketing newsletters; the connected provider's policy also applies. Sources checked 2026-11-06: https://www.courier.com/terms-of-service; https://www.courier.com/guides/transactional-vs-marketing-notifications; https://www.courier.com/docs/guides/build-a-preference-center.
- **Complete recipient export:** Profile retrieval, list/audience APIs, and deletion endpoints exist. The public docs checked did not describe a single full-account export of recipients, preferences, content, and message history. Sources checked 2026-11-06: https://www.courier.com/docs/workspaces/compliance; https://www.courier.com/docs/recipients/overview; https://www.courier.com/docs/monitor/logs.
- **Email total cost:** Courier's per-send price excludes the downstream email provider. Provider fees, send caps, suppression, IP choices, and inbox placement depend on the connected provider and its account. Sources checked 2026-11-06: https://www.courier.com/docs/workspaces/billing; https://www.courier.com/docs/guides/send-your-first-email; https://www.courier.com/docs/integrations/overview.
- **Newsroom:** Official blog and changelog were checked for acquisition/shutdown news. The direct `/newsroom` URL did not load through the research browser; this does not prove Courier lacks a newsroom. Sources checked 2026-11-06: https://www.courier.com/blog; https://www.courier.com/changelog; https://www.courier.com/newsroom.

## Field problems

- F04 cannot give a complete country or interface-language list from the pages checked. The Terms only state U.S. sanctions restrictions.
- F09 and F10 price Courier's orchestration usage, not a complete delivered-email bill. The provider sends separately and owns its delivery limits and sender reputation.
