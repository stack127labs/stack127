## Status, ownership, and product changes

Mailgun is an active hosted email sending service owned by Sinch. Sinch completed its acquisition of Pathwire, the former owner of Mailgun and Mailjet, in 2021. Mailgun remains a standalone brand. No Mailgun shutdown notice was found in the Mailgun blog, Sinch newsroom, or current product pages checked.

Mailgun Send is the API/SMTP sending service. It can send marketing messages to lists, but Mailgun says it has no campaign “send button”; users send through its API or SMTP. It does not have a separate self-service marketing campaign plan with a visual campaign composer. Mailgun Optimize is a separate deliverability and testing product. Mailjet is a separate Sinch email marketing product with its own campaign editor and plans; its prices are not included in Mailgun costs below.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Mailgun's current pricing FAQ says it has no campaign send button; the mailing-list and batch-send docs do support sending marketing messages through the API/SMTP. This means Mailgun can deliver newsletter-style messages but does not offer a self-service campaign composer. Mailjet is the separate Sinch marketing application.
- Public Send pricing lists Basic, Foundation, and Scale overage rates as “from” amounts, and exact high-volume selector output is not exposed in the static page. I used exact published base plans where the included quota matches and marked the 200,000-send scenario as an estimate.
- The marketing-style cost examples use the same Mailgun Send product and message quotas; Mailgun does not publish a contact-count fee for those lists. The absence of a contact price is not treated as a guaranteed unlimited-storage commitment.
- Country eligibility, full product/support language coverage, all message-count edge cases, and a general self-service SLA were not established from the pages searched.
- The Free-plan sending cap also differs between current pricing (100 emails/day) and the domain-verification guide (verified domains are not subject to a 300 emails/day limit). The pages do not reconcile whether these are different account limits.
- Pricing's 1-day Free log retention conflicts with general event documentation stating at least 2 days for Free and 30 days for paid. Kept both claims and marked the exact event-log period conflicting.
- The current Free pricing card gives a 100/day limit, while the domain verification guide says verified domains are not subject to 300/day. These may refer to different account/product limits, but the docs do not reconcile them; I left the discrepancy conflicting.
- Current public materials checked do not show a Mailgun Send shutdown or sunset notice. The Sinch ownership and Mailgun brand continuation are documented above.
- No correction to the Mailgun row in apps.md was needed. The row's active status, official URL, delivery category, and Sinch connection remain accurate.

- F04: Terms, pricing, help, and public product pages do not list a complete set of countries where a customer may open/pay for an account. Website translations do not prove control-panel or support-language availability.
