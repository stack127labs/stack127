## Cost examples — our calculation

These are **our calculations**, not checkout quotes, measured capacity or guaranteed inbox delivery. Inputs: one publication, one user, one verified sender domain, one recipient per email, no attachments, no other sends, no SMS/ads/paid-reader revenue and no introductory discounts/free allowances. Currencies remain separate; no EUR/USD conversion is performed.
