# Resend

## Cost examples

All amounts use the `$` symbol as Resend publishes it, per month, before unlisted tax. These are **our calculations**, not quotes. Marketing and transactional plans are priced separately; add both prices if a project needs both products. Baselines assume one account/user, one verified sender domain, a shared IP, one recipient per transactional message, and volume spread across a 30-day month. Marketing calculations assume exactly the subscribed contacts shown and no extra unsubscribed contacts, which also count toward quota.

### Marketing newsletter workload

Formula: unique subscribed contacts × 4 campaigns = monthly broadcast recipient deliveries. Resend's bill is based on contacts; its Marketing plan does not cap broadcast email volume.

| Contacts | Our calculation: deliveries/month | Marketing plan | Our calculated monthly plan price |
| ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 | Free | $0 |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | Pro | $40 |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | Pro | $80 |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 4 = 200,000 | Pro | $250 |

### Transactional workload

Formula: monthly outbound messages × 1 recipient/message = monthly recipient sends. Inbound email also uses transactional quota. Each row assumes the applicable monthly plan allowance and no overage. Free is usable for 3,000/month only when each UTC day stays at or below 100 sends.

| Outbound emails/month | Our calculation: recipient sends | Transactional plan | Our calculated monthly plan price |
| ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| 3,000 | 3,000 × 1 = 3,000 | Free, at most 100/day | $0 |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 1 = 10,000 | Pro, includes 50,000 | $20 |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 1 = 50,000 | Pro, includes 50,000 | $20 |
| 100,000 | 100,000 × 1 = 100,000 | Pro per price schedule or Scale per visible plan card | **Conflicting: $35 or $90** |

At 100,000 monthly messages, the official sources do not support one exact total: the pricing schedule and Resend's migration comparison list Pro at $35/month, while the visible pricing card lists Scale at $90/month for 100,000. Ask Resend to confirm which current option is purchasable before comparing this workload. A separate Marketing subscription is not included in transactional examples.
