## Cost examples: our calculations

Checked 2026-11-04. These are workload calculations and qualified public price references, not account-specific quotes. Keep the published $ symbol; the current calculator has a region selector, and a complete country/currency tariff was not recovered. No currency conversion or assumed tax rate is used.

### Four newsletters per subscriber each month

Inputs: one publication, one user, 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 unique active subscribed contacts, four campaigns on separate days, existing own verified private sending subdomain, no attachments. Sending is included; no external sender account is added.

Formula: recipient emails = contacts × 4. For Pro with a verified private domain, included monthly sends = selected contact limit × 10. The workload uses 4 ÷ 10 = 40% of that send allowance at a matching contact tier. This is allowance arithmetic, not a measured throughput or inbox-placement result.

### Annual entry illustration

For the publicly displayed 500-contact entry only, **our calculation**:

- Monthly route for 12 months: $12 × 12 = $144.
- Annual route: $10 monthly equivalent × 12 = $120 paid upfront.
- Arithmetic saving: $144 − $120 = $24; $24 ÷ $144 = 1/6, or 16⅔%.

Help instead says 15% annually. Applying that statement produces $12 × 12 × 0.85 = $122.40. Because that differs from $120, no blanket 15%, 20% or inferred discount is applied to the four larger audiences.
