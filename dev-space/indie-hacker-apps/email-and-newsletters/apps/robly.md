## Cost examples: our calculation

Newsletter inputs: one publication, one user, an existing sender domain and 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 unique subscribed contacts. Send four campaigns to each contact every month: 4,000 / 20,000 / 40,000 / 200,000 recipient emails. No SMS, paid-reader revenue, extra design service or promotional discount. Delivery is included; there is no separate external sender bill in this hosted-marketing scenario.

The current pricing FAQ says unlimited email sending applies up to and including 50,000-contact plans. Above 50,000, the allowance becomes ten times the plan's contact count. No undocumented daily/per-second throughput guarantee is assumed. Source: https://www.robly.com/pricing (checked 2026-11-05; update not stated).

The smaller tiers are visible pricing cards. Larger tiers are in the pricing page's loaded calculator script. Its monthly array is `l`, annual monthly-equivalent array is `u`, and contact thresholds are `d`. For the needed larger tiers, `d[0] = 10,000` and `d[2] = 50,000`; monthly values are `92` and `270`, annual equivalents `79` and `229`. The billing-cycle handler selects the proper array. Source: https://img.robly.com/assets/marketing_v2-4eec31fc7248193843825378b9ad0dd8af109b79826abecb1d09b3e8df6eeaa9.js (checked 2026-11-05; source update not stated), loaded by https://www.robly.com/pricing. No account endpoint or checkout was called to obtain these prices.

Formula: `recipient emails = unique subscribed contacts × 4`. For these four ordinary marketing workloads, `known monthly subtotal = applicable monthly subscriber tier + $0 separately billed delivery`.
