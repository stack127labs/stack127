## Cost examples — our calculation

These are transparent component calculations, not an AcelleMail managed-service quote. An exact all-in total is **not comparable** without domain, backup, administration, tax, notification usage and any optional AI/verification/plugin costs. The licence includes six months of updates/support; later update access is shown separately.

Inputs, all in USD before tax:

- Direct Regular licence: **80 once**, one production installation, one user, own business email. No monthly licence bill, per-contact fee or per-message software fee. The model is free-to-read; permission to sell access to the marketing application would require Extended.
- DigitalOcean **Basic Regular**, **24/month**, 2 vCPU, 4 GiB RAM, 80 GiB SSD and 4,000 GiB transfer. Web application, database and queue are on this named illustrative server. The vendor recommends a similar small-volume shape, but no capacity benchmark was run. Host billing currency is confirmed by DigitalOcean's invoice documentation.
- Amazon SES **Essentials**, one account/region, one verified sending domain, shared sending infrastructure, one recipient/message, no attachments, 32 KB per message. AcelleMail has a native SES API connection, so the example does not require direct SMTP from the host.
- SES rate: **0.16/1,000** at these volumes. Follow AWS's 32 KB example: 0.000032 GB/message × **0.12/GB**. AWS separately offers à-la-carte pricing; its 0.10/1,000 headline is a different selection. No free credits or dedicated IP charges are included.
- Single opt-in for an already permissioned app audience in the lifecycle example. The separate built-in list welcome is disabled, so one configured welcome-flow send is counted per subscriber. Confirmation messages, unrelated system mail and extra journey steps are excluded.
- External SNS request/notification costs, domains, off-server backups, monitoring, labour, tax, currency-conversion fees, AI usage and optional paid verification are outside these components. The SES setup requires working SNS callbacks; their traffic/cost is not assumed to be zero.

For `M` monthly recipient emails:

```text
SES monthly = M × 0.16 / 1,000 + M × 0.000032 × 0.12
            = M × 0.00016384

Host + SES monthly = 24 + M × 0.00016384

First-year licence + host + SES components
  = 80 + 12 × (24 + M × 0.00016384)

Later-year host + SES components, no new licence
  = 12 × (24 + M × 0.00016384)

First-month licence + host + SES components
  = 80 + 24 + M × 0.00016384
```

Exact decimal arithmetic is retained below. Actual invoices may round to currency units. The newsletter annual figures assume the stated monthly workload for all 12 months. They do not include a promise of future releases after the included update period.
