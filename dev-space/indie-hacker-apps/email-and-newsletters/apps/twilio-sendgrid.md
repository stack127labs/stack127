## Status, ownership, and product changes

Twilio SendGrid is active. Twilio acquired SendGrid in 2019. Twilio announced that sendgrid.com content would move to twilio.com on February 26, 2026; it described this as a website and content consolidation and said the SendGrid products and pricing were continuing. The SendGrid Free Email API and Free Marketing Campaigns plans were retired beginning May 27, 2025, with a 60-day transition for existing free accounts. Current new accounts receive one 60-day trial instead. No SendGrid shutdown notice was found in the Twilio blog or newsroom pages checked. Twilio Email is a separate product and is not included in the pricing below.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The current Marketing Campaigns page has stale FAQ copy describing an ongoing 2,000-contact/6,000-email free plan and a “free SMTP plan.” Its active comparison grid and current account-upgrade guide specify a single 60-day trial capped at 100 contacts and 100 Marketing/Email API sends per day. The dated May 27, 2025 changelog says the old free products were retired after a 60-day transition. I used the current trial and retirement notice for present-day limits.
- The Marketing Campaigns page publishes starting prices ($15/month Basic, $60/month Advanced), contact/send quotas, and overage rates, but not a current exact base price for each tier in its public static page. An official invoice example shows a Basic 5K customer billed $15.65 for one month, but that example includes usage and does not disclose a universal tier base price. I did not treat old/legacy plan tables as current or invent exact totals.
- Customer country eligibility and full UI/support language coverage were not established from Twilio terms, billing, and docs checked.
- The public terms and policy pages explain processing and deletion, but do not publish one physical storage/backup location for every data type. Regional Email has specific EU requirements; email activity remains global.
- Marketing Campaigns Automation docs say Advanced is required, while the pricing comparison appears to show Automation in the Free Trial column. I left trial automation access conflicting rather than assuming the pricing grid or docs take precedence.
- No fixed SendGrid-specific service-level uptime or public inbox-placement measurement was identified.
- No correction to the Twilio SendGrid row in apps.md was needed. Its status is active and the listed official URL is current.
