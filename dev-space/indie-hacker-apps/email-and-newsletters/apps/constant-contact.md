## Cost examples: our calculations

Checked **2026-11-04**. All examples are **our calculations**, using stated assumptions and original currencies. They are not test invoices. The main comparison uses US/USD ordinary monthly Lite pricing, not the annual headline or a first-month promotion.

Inputs: one publication, one user, 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 unique active email contacts; four campaign emails per contact per month; no SMS, ads, reader revenue, attachments or paid services. Assume the account has not previously grown into a higher retained contact tier. Each example is exactly at a published tier's upper boundary.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 | 1,000 × 10 = 10,000 | USD 30 | 0 | USD 30 + 0 = **USD 30/month** |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | 5,000 × 10 = 50,000 | USD 80 | 0 | USD 80 + 0 = **USD 80/month** |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | 10,000 × 10 = 100,000 | USD 120 | 0 | USD 120 + 0 = **USD 120/month** |

Use the **upper tier boundary**, not necessarily the actual audience count. For example, 1,500 active contacts occupy the 2,500 tier and receive 25,000 Lite campaign sends. ### Campaign overage and lifecycle scope

General formula from https://knowledgebase.constantcontact.com/email-digital-marketing/articles/KnowledgeBase/46046-Constant-Contacts-email-send-allowance-and-overage-fee?lang=en_US:

`Monthly total = matching plan/contact-tier fee + max(0, counted campaign recipient sends − included monthly allowance) × USD 0.002 + applicable extras/tax.`

For an existing 1,000-contact Lite account sending **12,000 counted campaign emails**, our calculation is:

`USD 30 + (12,000 − 10,000) × USD 0.002 = USD 30 + USD 4 = USD 34/month.`

This is a marketing-campaign example. It is not a quote for 12,000 password resets or a separately metered SMTP service. Automated welcome/cart/series emails are excluded from the campaign-send allowance under the help page. Path count, template eligibility and the 50-step maximum still apply (https://knowledgebase.constantcontact.com/email-digital-marketing/tutorials/KnowledgeBase/43904-Getting-started-with-the-Automation-Path-Builder?lang=en_US). Premium is required for custom/ecommerce paths; the complete Premium quote at each workload tier was not recovered, so no precise advanced-lifecycle total is invented.

There is no published unused-send carryover or hard spending-cap promise in the inspected pricing/billing/quota pages. The separate Starter Email edition stops at its monthly cap rather than using the main plan's overage rule (https://knowledgebase.constantcontact.com/email-digital-marketing/articles/KnowledgeBase/55248-Manage-your-Starter-Email-account?lang=en_US). Its indexed UK £4 / 2,500-send offer would not cover even 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 campaign sends. Its live availability remains unclear.

### External paid-reader illustration

No native paid-newsletter checkout/paywall or complete recurring subscription-state workflow was verified. The native Stripe integration syncs payment customers to contact lists and can request marketing consent (https://knowledgebase.constantcontact.com/email-digital-marketing/tutorials/KnowledgeBase/45842-Connect-your-Constant-Contact-account-with-Stripe?lang=en_US); paying alone does not establish that consent. The following is an **illustrative custom build**, not a working setup confirmed through signup or payment testing.

Inputs: US Stripe account on standard domestic online-card pricing; 100 successful recurring payments of **USD 10** each per month; Stripe Billing pay-as-you-go; 1,000 total subscribed contacts including those 100 readers; Lite newsletter sending as above; no refunds, disputes, foreign cards, FX, tax automation, ads or SMS.

Our calculation of known components:

- Gross payments: `100 × USD 10 = USD 1,000`.
- Stripe Payments: `USD 1,000 × 2.9% + 100 × USD 0.30 = USD 59`.
- Stripe Billing, if used at the published rate: `USD 1,000 × 0.7% = USD 7`.
- Known illustrated processor and newsletter components: `USD 59 + USD 7 + USD 30 = USD 96/month`.
- Amount after those named components only: `USD 1,000 − USD 96 = USD 904`.

Sources checked anew: https://stripe.com/pricing, https://stripe.com/billing/pricing, https://www.constantcontact.com/features/integrations?cc=customer_CasaQ. **USD 904 is not an exact take-home amount.** Paid-access website/software, integration work, entitlement synchronization, country-specific fees, any additional platform charges and taxes remain unpriced. No universal 0% Constant Contact revenue share is assumed. If paid-reader gating needs Premium/custom paths, replace the Lite component with its matching quote.

Event ticket fees (https://knowledgebase.constantcontact.com/email-digital-marketing/articles/KnowledgeBase/48284-Payment-methods-for-an-event?lang=en_US) must not be applied to recurring readers. That page describes processor fees plus a separate Constant Contact event platform fee, with an “8/1” change date whose year is not explicit. It is evidence for Events, not native newsletter monetization.
