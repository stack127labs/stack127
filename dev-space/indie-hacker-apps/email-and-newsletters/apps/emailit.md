## Cost examples: our calculations

All examples use one recipient per contact, four campaigns or sends per month where applicable, a 30-day month, before VAT, and no AppSumo or partner bonus. Emailit’s included monthly credits reset; purchased credits do not expire. Purchases are whole-dollar amounts from $20 to $500, so the first payment can exceed the usage value shown below.

### Newsletter campaigns

Campaign sends cost 2 credits per recipient. Pay as you go includes 3,000 credits monthly, but the $20 minimum purchase adds 100,000 purchased credits and raises the daily sending cap from 100 to 5,000.

| Subscribers | Formula | Monthly campaign use | Plan and credit cost | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 x 4 x 2 credits | 4,000 recipient emails; 8,000 credits | Pay as you go: 5,000 paid credits at $1 usage value; first top-up $20 | The 100,000-credit top-up lasts about 20 months at this use if no other paid-credit use. Our calculation. |
| 5,000 | 5,000 x 4 x 2 credits | 20,000 recipient emails; 40,000 credits | Pay as you go: 37,000 paid credits at $7.40 usage value; first top-up $20 | Purchased credits carry forward; this monthly use is below the 10,000 subscriber-per-audience cap. Our calculation. |
| 10,000 | 10,000 x 4 x 2 credits | 40,000 recipient emails; 80,000 credits | Pay as you go: 77,000 paid credits at $15.40 usage value; first top-up $20 | Exactly at the Pay as you go audience cap, assuming no extra unsubscribed subscriber records. Our calculation. |
| 50,000 | 50,000 x 4 x 2 credits | 200,000 recipient emails; 400,000 credits | Pro: $25 monthly or $20/month billed yearly, plus 300,000 credits at $30; total $55 monthly or $50 annual-equivalent | Pro supports up to 50,000 subscribers per audience. At this workload, plan limits start at 5,000 emails/day; request or build sending history for a higher cap. Our calculation. |

The credit-value math uses $2 per 10,000 purchased credits on Pay as you go and $1 per 10,000 extra credits on Pro. The usage value is not always the amount charged that month because top-ups are at least $20 and unused purchased credits remain available.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **AUP wording:** The AUP says all recipients must have opted in and prohibits bulk unsolicited email without prior permission, but then has a paragraph beginning “If sending to unsolicited emails.” Production-access docs expressly ban cold email and purchased, rented, scraped, or shared lists. The safe reading is permission-based sending only. Sources: https://emailit.com/acceptable-use-policy/ and https://emailit.com/docs/workspaces/production-access/.
- **Old free-tier copy:** A prior indexed version of the pricing page said Emailit had retired its free tier. The current live page and the October 11 billing changelog show 3,000 free credits/month on Pay as you go. The current plan is used. Sources: https://emailit.com/pricing/ and https://emailit.com/docs/changelog/billing/.
- **Price-page update:** An older indexed version showed a dedicated IP at $300/year. The live pricing page currently shows $30/month per IP and recommends use from about 1,000 sends/day. The current live page is used. Source: https://emailit.com/pricing/.
- **Segments:** Plan comparison pages and docs list segment capacities, but the current docs say segments are in alpha and open only to Emailit staff. The feature is treated as unavailable to ordinary accounts. Sources: https://emailit.com/pricing/, https://emailit.com/docs/segments/, and https://emailit.com/docs/changelog/2026-10-18-segments/.
- **Company entity:** Current Terms call FunFirst s.r.o. the provider and describe FunFirst, Inc. as its US daughter company. Privacy, security, and pricing pages also identify FunFirst, Inc. as operator or copyright holder. Their exact contracting roles are not consistent across pages. Sources: https://emailit.com/terms-of-service/, https://emailit.com/privacy-policy/, https://emailit.com/docs/security/, and https://emailit.com/pricing/.
- **Status page:** Emailit links a public status page, but it could not be read during this check. Current incidents and uptime history are unknown. Sources: https://status.emailit.com/ and https://emailit.com/docs/security/.
- **Audience-count assumptions:** The audience limit includes unsubscribed subscriber memberships. A single contact can belong to multiple audiences. The cost scenarios assume only the stated subscriber count in one audience.
- **Sending-limit assumptions:** Monthly volume does not guarantee a burst allowance. New accounts must first receive production approval; Pay as you go starts at 100/day, and the first credit purchase raises it to 5,000/day. Pro/Business limits grow with sending health.
