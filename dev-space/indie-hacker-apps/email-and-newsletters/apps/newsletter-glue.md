## Cost examples: our calculations

Sources checked 2026-11-05. Ordinary month-to-month prices, before applicable tax and with no nonprofit discount, are the main comparison. Annual prices are separate commitments.

Price evidence: https://newsletterglue.com/pricing/ contains monthly/yearly controls and explicit per-tier values. Its inline script maps Connect to **US$149/month** and **US$99/month billed annually**. The public product page (https://newsletterglue.com/downloads/newsletter-glue/) supplies full USD prices and price IDs; matched to those controls, Connect is **US$1,188/year**, not US$99 paid monthly. Native entry is **US$29/month** or **US$288/year**, displayed as US$24/month annually. No checkout button was used to establish these figures.

The generic “2 months free yearly” banner is not used as an arithmetic rule. Published plan amounts differ from simply multiplying monthly prices by ten; the actual full annual product amounts are used below.
