## Cost examples — our calculation

These examples are our calculations, not a vendor's bundled hosting/delivery quote.

### Manual newsletters

Inputs: one WordPress site, one operator, one publication, one verified sending domain, shared SES sending infrastructure, no attachments, four campaigns per contact per month. Choose free Newsletter core plus its free SMTP connector, Amazon SES Essentials and a DigitalOcean Basic Regular 2 GiB / 1 vCPU / 50 GiB SSD server at its USD 12 monthly cap. Assume the site is already configured, runs for a full month, and can reach SES SMTP over TLS.

Sources checked 2026-11-05: [free features and licence scope](https://www.thenewsletterplugin.com/premium), [free SMTP connector](https://www.thenewsletterplugin.com/documentation/addons/delivery-addons/), [connector setup](https://www.thenewsletterplugin.com/documentation/addons/delivery-addons/smtp-extension/), [SES SMTP requirements](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/send-email-smtp.html), [SES rates and 32 KB worked example](https://aws.amazon.com/ses/pricing/), [host configuration/price](https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing/droplets).

Let N = subscribed contacts and M = 4 × N recipient emails. Assume each complete message is 32 KB, using AWS's published example convention of 0.000032 GB/message.

- Software: USD 0.
- SES sending: M × USD 0.16 / 1,000.
- SES outgoing data: M × 0.000032 GB × USD 0.12/GB.
- Monthly modeled subtotal B = USD 12 + sending + outgoing data.
- First-year Professional variant = USD 140 + 12 × B. The USD 140 is an upfront ordinary licence purchase with one year of support/updates, not a monthly contract. It covers up to three sites, although this example uses one.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 4,000 | USD 0.64000 | USD 0.01536 | USD 12.65536 | USD 291.86432 |
| 5,000 | 20,000 | USD 3.20000 | USD 0.07680 | USD 15.27680 | USD 323.32160 |

Five decimal places preserve the formula results; an actual provider invoice can round differently. Professional's current USD 99 promotion is excluded. Its USD 140 ordinary price is published in the pricing page's inline `full_USD` value; EUR has a separate matching 140 schedule. No currency conversion is used.

These are **infrastructure subtotals, not exact all-in costs or capacity promises**. Excluded: tax, domain registration, backups, operational work, mailbox costs, any additional AWS services and host/transfer overages. The plugin licence's exact tax presentation remains unclear. No introductory AWS credits are applied. DigitalOcean plan specifications do not prove that a particular WordPress installation will process 200,000 monthly messages.

Free SMTP does not provide the paid SES add-on's automatic feedback synchronization. The free baseline requires the operator to handle complaints/bounces and update suppression appropriately. The Professional variant can instead use the native SES integration and its SNS feedback handling. See [subscriber suppression](https://www.thenewsletterplugin.com/documentation/subscribers-and-management/subscribers/) and [SES integration](https://www.thenewsletterplugin.com/documentation/addons/delivery-addons/amazon-ses-extension/), checked 2026-11-05.

### Paid-reader example

Use the 1,000-contact newsletter scenario, including 100 paying members charged USD 10/month by an external membership system. Select Newsletter Professional and optional PMPro Standard support/add-ons at USD 499/year; self-hosted PMPro core is also available for USD 0. The paid integration maps membership levels to newsletter lists. It does not collect or pay out the reader's money itself.

Known modeled first-year components = USD 140 + USD 499 + 12 × USD 12.65536 = **USD 790.86432**. Gross reader billings = 100 × USD 10 × 12 = **USD 12,000/year**. Gross minus those known components = USD 11,209.13568, **before gateway/application fees, refunds, chargebacks, tax, backups, domain and labor**. Net income cannot be exact because those components and reader/merchant locations are unspecified. Do not interpret the lack of a published Newsletter revenue fee as proof of zero fees across this stack.

Sources checked 2026-11-05: [membership tier gate](https://www.thenewsletterplugin.com/premium), [PMPro level/list sync](https://www.thenewsletterplugin.com/documentation/addons/integrations/pmpro-extension/), [PMPro core and Standard prices](https://www.paidmembershipspro.com/pricing/), plus the sender/host sources above. This is a USD illustration, not a universal processor or payout-country assumption.

### Approval and burst conditions

All examples require [SES production access](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/request-production-access.html) and appropriate [regional sending quotas](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/quotas.html), checked 2026-11-05. Sandbox permits only verified destinations, 200 recipients/24 hours and 1 recipient/second; it is not sufficient for these campaign workloads.

Spread monthly use through the month, but verify the actual campaign-day peak. A 50,000-contact issue is still 50,000 recipient emails when it runs. The plugin's [hourly/per-second settings](https://www.thenewsletterplugin.com/documentation/installation/newsletter-configuration/) and WordPress scheduling can spread that work and delay delivery. Production SES daily/rate limits are account-specific, not a universal allowance.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Refund scope:** [pricing](https://www.thenewsletterplugin.com/premium) and [support](https://www.thenewsletterplugin.com/support) offer a broad 30-day message; [terms Art.11–12](https://www.thenewsletterplugin.com/legal/terms) restrict withdrawal/guarantee rights by consumer versus professional purchaser and exclude Professional Setup. Keep the difference visible before promising refunds to a business.
- **Scale:** [homepage](https://www.thenewsletterplugin.com/) says 400k+ active installations, but the [WordPress API](https://api.wordpress.org/plugins/info/1.2/?action=plugin_information&request[slug]=newsletter) returned `active_installs: 200000` on 2026-11-05. Dates/definitions may differ; no reconciliation was found. Neither figure is a delivery-quality measure.
- **Price rendering:** the text-only pricing view shows "-" placeholders. The public HTML's inline `files` array and [theme script](https://www.thenewsletterplugin.com/wp-content/themes/tnp/js/tnp.js?ver=1) supply USD/EUR prices and distinguish current from full prices.
