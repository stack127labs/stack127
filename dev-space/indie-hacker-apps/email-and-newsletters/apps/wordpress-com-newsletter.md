## Cost examples: our calculations

Baseline inputs: one site/publication, one user, unique confirmed email subscribers, four new public newsletter posts per month, no reader revenue, ads, SMS, extra plugins or attachments. Use the included WordPress.com site address and native sending. Readers must choose instant notifications to receive all four separate issues; daily/weekly preferences can change actual deliveries.

Formula: `recipient emails = subscribed contacts × 4`. Free native sending is unlimited, so `native sending/site software = USD 0/month`. Existing-list import is a separate scenario because Free allows only 100 cumulative imports. The lowest listed paid plan is Personal, USD 9/month with ordinary monthly billing. Paid plans remove the import cap; review still applies above 10,000 imported subscribers.

| Confirmed contacts | Recipient emails | Directly acquired audience: Free | Imported existing audience: Personal monthly | Import condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 | USD 0/month | USD 9/month | Paid plan needed for import over 100 |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | USD 0/month | USD 9/month | Same import condition |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | USD 0/month | USD 9/month | Review threshold is greater than 10,000 |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 4 = 200,000 | USD 0/month | USD 9/month | Manual import review; timing unknown |

These are **our calculations**, not verified checkout invoices or delivery-speed promises. No separate sender/host bill is added. Tax, domain registration/renewal and optional services remain outside the baseline. Sources checked 2026-11-04: https://wordpress.com/newsletter/, https://wordpress.com/support/import-subscribers-to-a-newsletter/, https://wordpress.com/pricing/.

Annual alternatives, separate from the monthly baseline: Personal `USD 4 × 12 = USD 48 upfront`; Premium `8 × 12 = USD 96`; Business `25 × 12 = USD 300`; Commerce `45 × 12 = USD 540`. These are our calculations from current annual-equivalent prices, not claims of monthly installment billing. A custom domain’s first year is included on eligible annual plans; later registration costs depend on the domain. Billing country and then-current renewal pricing can change the final charge.

Paid newsletters can start on Free; there is no mandatory higher tier solely to activate native paid tiers. Each website plan instead changes the platform percentage. For a concrete **US illustration**, assume 100 readers each successfully pay USD 10 once in a month, all domestic online cards, no FX/refunds/disputes/taxes:

- Gross revenue: `100 × USD 10 = USD 1,000`.
- Base Stripe processing component: `0.029 × 1,000 + 0.30 × 100 = USD 59`.
- Free known components: `USD 0 site + 0.10 × 1,000 platform + USD 59 processing = USD 159`; remainder before other applicable charges: `1,000 − 159 = USD 841`.
- Premium monthly known components: `USD 18 site + 0.04 × 1,000 + USD 59 = USD 117`; remainder before other applicable charges: `USD 883`.

These are **our partial calculations**, not exact net payouts. Stripe separately lists Billing at 0.7% where applicable, but applicability to the connected WordPress account was not established. Account contract, tax, international-card, FX or other fees can change totals. Sources checked anew: https://wordpress.com/support/wordpress-editor/blocks/payments/, https://stripe.com/pricing/, https://wordpress.com/pricing/ (2026-11-04).

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Import capacity versus audience capacity:** unlimited direct signups/sends coexist with Free’s 100 cumulative imported-contact cap. The 50,000 existing-reader scenario needs a paid plan and manual review. No universal approval or review-completion time was found in pricing, newsletter setup, import help or terms. Source: https://wordpress.com/support/import-subscribers-to-a-newsletter/.
- **Paid migration:** https://wordpress.com/support/import-from-substack/import-subscribers/ specifies connecting the same Stripe account and mapping subscriptions. https://wordpress.com/creators/ says paid readers re-subscribe through a new Stripe connection. General CSV help at https://wordpress.com/support/subscribers/ imports only email addresses and asks readers to purchase anew. Keep dedicated Substack mapping separate from ordinary CSV; exact automatic charge continuity and outbound payment migration need confirmation.
- **Automatic-send switch:** https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter-settings/ describes the global default toggle without the Business/Commerce restriction. https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/disable-newsletter-emails/ restricts its global-switch section to those plans. Per-post sending controls are documented clearly; do not silently settle the global plan gate.
- **Domains and authentication:** https://wordpress.com/support/email-and-smtp-plugins/ explicitly fixes native Newsletter’s From address and says SMTP plugins do not change it. https://wordpress.com/support/domains/set-up-email-authentication-for-your-domain/ documents own-domain website/plugin mail. Its wpcloud records are not proof of a customer-authenticated native newsletter domain. Custom tracking-domain support, full native email-brand removal and exact native SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment details remain unestablished after these guides, settings and email appearance docs.
- **Targeting, workflows and technical limits:** categories, paid/free access, admin filters, scheduling, welcome messages, REST and OAuth docs were read. General contact tags/properties, behavioral journey branching/exit rules, direct delivery callbacks, idempotency/retries, raw message-log retention and exact daily/per-second/byte/attachment ceilings were not established. Product FAQ recommends plugins for standalone campaigns/drip sequences. Third-party plugin listings hosted on WordPress.com are not proof of native capabilities.
- **Exports:** subscriber CSV has an explicit current schema, including email status and categories when enabled. No complete historic unsubscribe/complaint ledger, consent text/IP history or arbitrary field/tag export was established in subscriber, export, import, GDPR and privacy sources. Content XML contains media references, not a complete site backup. Relevant URLs: https://wordpress.com/support/subscribers/, https://wordpress.com/support/export/, https://wordpress.com/support/your-site-and-the-gdpr/.
- **Payments:** WordPress publishes its own supported creator-country list. It includes supported charge currencies such as INR without listing India as a supported creator country. The linked processor page https://stripe.com/en-sg/global does not expand the integration’s list. Signup eligibility, creator payment access and delivery destinations are different facts. US processing is an example; separate Billing/FX/tax and contractual rates remain account-dependent.
- **Privacy and retention:** https://wordpress.com/support/choose-your-sites-primary-data-center/ covers primary website hosting and cross-region replication, not every newsletter/contact/log location. https://automattic.com/privacy/ permits international processing and retention for necessary/legal purposes. https://wordpress.com/support/data-processing-agreements/ supplies an account-based request route; no request was made. Current newsletter-specific storage/routing, complete subprocessor regions and backup purge timing were not established. A guessed https://automattic.com/subprocessors/ route was inaccessible; it is not cited as a valid registry.
- **Tracking:** newsletter stats, settings, subscriber preferences, https://wordpress.com/support/tracking-opt-outs/ and privacy were inspected. Account-usage analytics controls do not establish a publisher-level email-pixel/link-tracking switch. Complete bot/Apple privacy filtering was not documented in these sources.
- **Status and guarantees:** https://automatticstatus.com/ returned no readable content in two attempts. Official staff reference: https://wordpress.com/forums/topic/sla-uptime/. That older answer is not a current newsletter SLA. No current numerical newsletter uptime/response or delivery-time contract was established from terms, pricing and support. Scale claims are vendor-reported, with no independent inbox-placement test linked.

All URLs in this section were checked on 2026-11-04.
