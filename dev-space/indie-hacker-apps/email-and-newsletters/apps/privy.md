# Privy

Status, shutdown and acquisition check: Privy is active at the ecommerce product's official URL from `apps.md`. No shutdown or product-sunset announcement was found in its official blog, Press & Media page or official-domain news searches. The company says it was divested from Attentive in 2023. It later acquired Emotive (July 8, 2025) and Sendlane (February 2, 2026). Sources: https://www.privy.com/press, https://www.privy.com/blog, https://www.privy.com/blog/emotive-joins-privy and https://www.privy.com/blog/privy-acquires-sendlane (checked 2026-11-05; acquisition posts dated 2025-07-08 and 2026-02-02). These are acquisitions by Privy, not evidence that Attentive still owns it.

Current legal owner/operator: **Privy Operations, LLC**. The April 3, 2026 terms name that company and say Privy exclusively owns the service and documentation. The May 19, 2026 privacy policy names the same operator. A current ultimate parent or complete post-divestment equity structure was not established from the official material inspected. Sources: https://www.privy.com/terms-of-service and https://www.privy.com/privacy (checked 2026-11-05).

**A Shopify store is not required.** Official docs cover BigCommerce, Wix, WooCommerce and custom ecommerce integrations. Features differ by platform. Onsite forms/tracking require site integration; a custom sending domain requires DNS access and a website at the root of an owned domain. Sources: https://help.privy.com/docs/learn/integrations/custom-integration, https://help.privy.com/docs/learn/integrations/woocommerce, https://help.privy.com/docs/learn/integrations/bigcommerce, https://help.privy.com/docs/learn/integrations/wix and https://help.privy.com/docs/learn/deliverability/use-a-custom-sending-domain (checked 2026-11-05). ## Cost examples — our calculation

All amounts here are **USD**, ordinary monthly subscription subtotals, without an introductory promotion. Inputs: one business/publication, one user, an existing website/store and domain, no attachments, no paid SMS purchase, and only permissioned mailable contacts. The included 250 SMS credits are unused. Email delivery and Displays are included in the Email + SMS plan; no separate external sender subscription is part of this documented setup. Billing is based on the cycle's highest mailable-contact count, not just the count on its last day.

Sources for every price/formula below: current pricing https://www.privy.com/pricing (pricing record updated 2026-10-06), current billing/counting guide https://help.privy.com/docs/learn/account-settings-and-billing/account-settings/2025-plans-and-billing (updated 2026-08-18), both checked 2026-11-05. The current billing guide applies to accounts activated on/after July 8, 2025.

Each accepted distinct event is intended to cause one Flow entry and one single-recipient email. Keep one verified sender domain, one user and no SMS or attachments.
