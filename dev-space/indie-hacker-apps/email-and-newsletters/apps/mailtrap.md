## Conflicts and unknowns

- **API/SMTP free quota:** The live pricing page checked 2026-11-06 gives 4,000 monthly sent/received messages. The sending-limits help article still says 3,500 even though it displays a recent update date. I use the current pricing page's 4,000 value; the older help figure remains recorded above.
- **Sandbox free quota:** The live pricing page checked today gives 50 monthly tests; the Sandbox troubleshooting page says 500. I use 50 from current pricing.
- **100K API/SMTP option:** The live pricing page checked today displays Business 100K at $85/month. An official article published about two months earlier lists a Basic 100K option at $30/month. I use the current live card's $85 for the 100K scenario and record the older Basic amount as an unresolved alternative. Do not treat the tiers as identical: Business includes a dedicated IP and more features.
- **Marketing 50K pricing:** The live English pricing page checked today exposes a 50K contact selector but its static text renders only the default 25K Pro tier. The official Spanish pricing page shows the 50K Pro tier at $109/month; an official pricing article lists its 250K monthly email quota. Both sources are linked in the cost example.
- **Annual Marketing Pro arithmetic:** The current page shows $47/month equivalent and $566 billed annually, a $2 arithmetic gap over 12 months. The file records both exact displayed values and does not derive the upfront price from the monthly equivalent.
- **Data location:** The GDPR help page says US hosting on AWS us-east-1 and Google US and says EU storage was planned for 2026. The newer subprocessor list (effective 2026-10-06) still lists US locations. I could not confirm an EU-only storage option or whether that plan launched.
- **Newsroom:** https://mailtrap.io/newsroom/ failed to open. I checked the active official blog, About page, and changelog for shutdown, sunset, or acquisition notices; none appeared in those sources. This is not proof that none exists.
- **Other unknowns:** No complete customer-country matrix, exact app/support language list, contact deduplication rule across lists, public Marketing overage rate/cap, Sandbox overage behavior, full-list ownership language, paid-reader billing, cross-product IP isolation, or contract-level SLA terms were established.
- **Independent placement testing:** EmailToolTester's 2026 newsletter deliverability test was reviewed but does not list Mailtrap. No qualifying Mailtrap-specific study with an inspectable inbox-placement method was found.

## Field problems

- **F04:** Legal country restrictions are clear, but the pages do not publish a complete supported-customer-country matrix or product/support language list.
- **F06:** Pricing defines contacts as addresses in the list, but the reviewed pages do not say how duplicate addresses across lists are counted.
- **F07/F10:** Current pages conflict with older quota or price detail. The newest live pricing display is used where it states the limit; dated official articles are used for tiers hidden by the selector, with conflicts disclosed.
- **F11:** Marketing FAQ mentions overage without an exact current rate or cap; API/SMTP rules must not be applied to Marketing or Sandbox.
- **F13/F14/F16:** Mailtrap documents email campaigns, not reader payments. These payment/migration fields are not applicable to the documented product.
- **F27:** API request rate, email send rate, SMTP connection limits, and campaign throttling are separate limits and should not be collapsed into one throughput figure.
- **F28:** Processor-country disclosures do not prove exact storage regions; the EU storage plan has no clear launch status in the pages checked.
- **F33/F34:** The hosted product has no user-operated install; the conditional self-host fields apply to Mailtrap Local only.
