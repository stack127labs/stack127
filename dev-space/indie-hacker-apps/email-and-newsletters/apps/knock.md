## Cost examples

All examples are **our calculations** of Knock's platform component. They assume one user, one verified sender setup, one email per recipient per campaign, no other channels, no guide recipients or agent-credit overages, before tax, and evenly spread volume where a daily average is shown. The price page displays `$` without an ISO currency code. External sender fees are extra and depend on the provider; therefore these are not all-in email-service totals.

| Scenario | Inputs and formula | Knock platform result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Newsletter, 1,000 readers × 4 campaigns | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 messages; within Free's 10,000 included | $0/month |
| Newsletter, 5,000 readers × 4 campaigns | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 messages; Starter required above Free's 10,000, within 50,000 included | $250/month |
| Newsletter, 10,000 readers × 4 campaigns | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 messages; within Starter's 50,000 included | $250/month |
| Newsletter, 50,000 readers × 4 campaigns | 50,000 × 4 = 200,000 messages; $250 + (200,000 − 50,000) × $0.005 | $1,000/month on Starter; Enterprise may quote differently |
| App email, 3,000 messages | 3,000 successful messages; 3,000 ÷ 30 = 100/day average; within Free | $0/month |
| App email, 10,000 messages | 10,000 successful messages; 10,000 ÷ 30 = 333.33/day average; at Free allowance | $0/month |
| App email, 50,000 messages | 50,000 successful messages; 50,000 ÷ 30 = 1,666.67/day average; within Starter allowance | $250/month |
| App email, 100,000 messages | 100,000 successful messages; 100,000 ÷ 30 = 3,333.33/day average; $250 + (100,000 − 50,000) × $0.005 | $500/month on Starter; Enterprise may quote differently |

Daily averages are rounded to two decimals and are not burst-rate guarantees. If a workflow sends both email and another channel to each user, Knock counts each channel message separately.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The terms, privacy policy, and DPA name **Knock Labs, Inc.**; the subprocessor page says **Knock, Inc.** The sources conflict on the entity label. The U.S. country is consistent.
- Prices show `$` without a currency code. The monthly plan table does not state tax treatment or paid-plan minimum commitment. Check the checkout/contract before treating the platform component as a final invoice total.
- Production email is sent by the connected provider. The complete email bill, domain authentication records, provider quotas, IP arrangement, and provider-country routing depend on that provider.
- Knock does not publish a full eligible-country or interface-language list on the pages checked.
- Signup forms, double opt-in, a complete account export, paid-reader migration, referral/sponsorship features, and native reader payments were not established. Pages checked for newsletter functions: broadcasts, audiences, preferences, commercial unsubscribe, pricing, and terms.
- Account deletion documentation explains a 24-hour deletion window; routine message/log retention is 30 days for Free/Starter and 90 days for Enterprise, with API logs at 30 days. These are separate policies.
- The AUP allows newsletter and marketing email only when each recipient gave prior express consent for that type of communication. Purchased/rented/scraped lists without specific consent are prohibited.
- No shutdown or acquisition notice appeared in the official pages reviewed. The active blog and changelog support current operation. The standalone newsroom and status-page fetches failed in the research browser; those failures are recorded as access failures, not product-status evidence.
- No independent inbox-placement result was established. Knock's reported scale and customer case study are vendor claims, not delivery tests.

## Field problems

- F06: Knock meters successful user-channel messages, not contacts, so the newsletter scenarios use unique subscribers only as an input to calculate recipient-message volume. A duplicate-email rule was not published.
- F09 and F10: Knock's price is only the orchestration component. Provider pricing is separate, and no provider was selected, so an all-in total is not comparable.
- F24–F27: sender authentication, delivery suppression, sending IP, and provider quotas differ by integration. Knock's own API request limits must not be read as email throughput.
- F28/F39: subprocessor location is listed by country, while exact data region is not stated; headquarters is not a substitute. The Knock Labs, Inc./Knock, Inc. entity-label conflict needs legal clarification.
- F32/F40: public newsroom and status pages could not be retrieved by the research browser. The company blog/changelog remained accessible and recent.
- F33–F34: source license and own-server work do not apply to the hosted Knock service; SDK repositories are not the service edition.
