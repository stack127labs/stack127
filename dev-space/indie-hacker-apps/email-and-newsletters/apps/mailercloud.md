## Cost examples: our calculation

Prices below are USD before VAT/GST or other applicable tax, with ordinary monthly billing and no coupon. The [terms](https://www.mailercloud.com/terms-and-conditions/) expressly exclude tax. The [pricing guide](https://help.mailercloud.com/en/articles/61-what-are-the-plans-and-pricing-at-mailercloud) lists 20% VAT for UK clients and 18% GST for Indian clients; actual applicability depends on the bill. No currency conversion is made.

The current [Marketing pricing page](https://www.mailercloud.com/pricing/) and [Email API pricing page](https://www.mailercloud.com/pricing/api/) embed public tier data used by their calculators. These were read without an account or form submission. Relevant USD tier inputs were recovered directly from that page data, rather than guessing an inaccessible slider position. All checked 2026-11-05.

### Newsletter workload

Inputs: Marketing Platform; one publication; one user; unique subscribed contacts; four campaigns to each contact per month; existing authenticated domain/mailbox; shared IP. No paid-reader revenue, advertising, API subscription, dedicated IP, verification service or Inbox Tracker.

Formula: recipient emails/month = unique subscribed contacts × 4. Premium cost = the contact-tier subscription; paid marketing sends have no per-email fee within fair use.

| Contacts | Emails/month, our calculation | Plan | Monthly subscription, our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 | Marketing Free | $0 |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | Premium 5K | $30 |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | Premium 10K | $50 |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 4 = 200,000 | Premium 50K | $230 |

The Free example assumes the four 1,000-recipient issues go on separate days: each fits the public pricing data's 1,000/day cap, and the 4,000 monthly total fits 12,000. This does not establish an unlimited burst rate. Approval/authentication and anti-spam rules still apply.

The embedded contact list explicitly maps 1K → $10, 5K → $30, 10K → $50 and 50K → $230 for Premium. The same page's static comparison column pairs “$10” with “5,000 contacts”; that static pairing does not match the calculator's per-contact list. We used the explicit tier list, supported at 10K and 50K by the page's side-by-side comparison prices. Recheck the selected tier before publication or checkout.

Annual comparison, kept separately: the marketing page advertises 30% off, and the embedded USD annual amounts agree for these tiers.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Free remains $0; Premium's 1K annual amount is provided to show the paid entry, not to add an unnecessary subscription to the Free scenario. Native paid-reader checkout was not established, so no paid-newsletter revenue variant is invented.

Inputs: Email API product only; one verified domain; one user; default shared IP; no attachments; one recipient per message. No Marketing Platform/contact-tier purchase and no native/external workflow runs. Spread sends across the month and stay below the selected tier's published daily maximum. No instantaneous burst is assumed. The plan's full monthly allowance is bought even at a low actual volume.

### What is outside the total

- Subscriptions include sending infrastructure; no customer Azure/SES/other external sender bill is required in these scenarios.
- Customer-supplied domain and any mailbox purchase/renewal are excluded. The model assumes these already exist.
- Optional API add-ons are separate: dedicated IP $50/IP/month on Pro/Platform; Inbox Tracker $120/month on Pro/Platform; Email Verification $110/month listed across API plans. The verifier quantity and any negotiated service charges are not modeled.
- Dedicated-IP guide also publishes $480/year/IP, a separate 20% annual IP discount. Do not apply the email subscription's 30% to this add-on.
- Taxes depend on billing location. Optional external integration/AI-tool subscriptions are additional if chosen.
- Actual invoices outside the stated model cannot be exact here: extra verification usage/services, special agreements, out-of-quota API charging and disputed Basic expanded entitlements need confirmation.
