## Cost examples: our calculations

All sources checked **2026-11-04**. Figures below are **our calculations**, not tested invoices. Main examples use ordinary USD month-to-month rates, before tax, with no WELCOME, Black Friday, annual or two-year discount. USD/EUR choices are documented at https://www.sender.net/help/account-settings-and-billing/how-sender-pricing-works/; the vendor's price methodology at https://www.sender.net/blog/email-list-management-software/ explicitly identifies USD.

### Four newsletter campaigns per month

Inputs: one publication, one user, one verified sending domain, unique active subscribed contacts, no attachments, shared IP. Four campaign emails per contact monthly. Exclude SMS, ads, reader revenue, optional services, external domain/site costs and tax. Assume no other audiences or sends consume the account's counters.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 | Free Forever | 15,000 | USD 0 + USD 0 = **USD 0/month** |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | Standard 5,000 | 5,000 × 12 = 60,000 | USD 39 + USD 0 = **USD 39/month** |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | Standard 10,000 | 10,000 × 12 = 120,000 | USD 69 + USD 0 = **USD 69/month** |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 4 = 200,000 | Standard 50,000 | 50,000 × 12 = 600,000 | USD 259 + USD 0 = **USD 259/month** |

Free limits: https://www.sender.net/pricing/free-plan/. Published monthly Standard table: https://www.sender.net/getresponse-alternative/. The USD 39/5K amount also appears on https://www.sender.net/email-marketing-cost-calculator/ when opened and in https://www.sender.net/blog/email-list-management-software/, updated 2026-10-19, which says prices checked October 2026. Older indexed calculator content said USD 33/5K, but the opened current page shows USD 39. The current table is used; the index/live difference is recorded.

Paid quota formula: https://www.sender.net/help/account-settings-and-billing/how-sender-pricing-works/. Each scenario fits its active-contact, unique-emailed and total-send allowances. No overage charge is added because the workload is within those allowances. The plan selector itself was not changed or used to obtain a checkout quote. Recheck the selected live tier before publication.

The USD 0 example retains Sender branding. If a 1,000-contact newsletter needs branding removal, published monthly references disagree: https://www.sender.net/getresponse-alternative/ lists USD 9/1K, while https://www.sender.net/blog/best-newsletter-platforms/ lists USD 15/1K; the main pricing page now shows the USD 9 entry at 500 contacts. **An exact unbranded 1K upgrade total is unresolved.** The free newsletter example does not depend on that conflict.

Counter formula: current active contacts and distinct addresses emailed in the billing month must both fit the tier (https://www.sender.net/help/account-settings-and-billing/active-vs-unique-subscribers/). Removing an emailed address does not release the monthly unique allowance. Thus adding a new replacement audience can block sending even when the current visible list is below the limit.
