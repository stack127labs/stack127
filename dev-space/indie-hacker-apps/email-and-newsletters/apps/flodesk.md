## Cost examples

All examples below are **our calculations**, before tax, for ordinary month-to-month new-member plans. One brand, one user and an existing sender domain/mailbox are assumed. No referral discount, annual discount, paid design service, dedicated IP or external integration subscription is included.

The fetched [pricing page](https://flodesk.com/pricing) exposes an ordered list of subscriber capacities and matching monthly/annual price pairs. We matched each pair to its capacity. The FAQ confirms the order at 1,000 subscribers: Lite 25/19, Pro 28/25, Everything 54/49, all USD. The initial zero placeholder is not treated as a free paid plan. Pricing checked 2026-11-04; source update date not stated. [Subscriber counting and upgrades](https://help.flodesk.com/en/articles/8923841), checked 2026-11-04, edited 2026-07-09.

### Paid-reader proceeds

Illustrative inputs: US seller eligible for Checkout, Stripe Standard, 1,000 total active readers including 100 paying USD 5/month, 100 successful domestic-card charges, no refunds/disputes/FX and Everything monthly USD 54.

Our calculation:

- Gross: `100 × USD 5 = USD 500/month`.
- Conditional US standard card processing: `100 × ((USD 5 × 2.9%) + USD 0.30) = USD 44.50`.
- Subtotal after those card fees and Flodesk software: `USD 500 − USD 44.50 − USD 54 = USD 401.50/month`.
- If the connected account also incurs published Stripe Billing pay-as-you-go pricing: `USD 500 × 0.7% = USD 3.50`; conditional further subtotal `USD 401.50 − USD 3.50 = USD 398.00`.

**Exact take-home amount is not established.** Flodesk's account-specific Standard processing terms, any Billing charge, Stripe Tax/other fees and seller taxes must be verified. The 2.9% + USD 0.30 rate is a declared US domestic-card reference, not a global rate. The legacy Express 3% + 0.30 table is not added on top of Standard fees.
