## Cost examples — our calculation

These are **our calculations**, not bundled quotes from Mailster. There is no ordinary monthly software subscription in the visible current plan cards. Use the ordinary annual Starter amount, not the first-year Black Friday discount.

### Newsletter baseline

Inputs: one WordPress site/domain, one operator/publication, four campaigns per unique subscribed contact per month; one verified sender domain, one recipient per message, shared SES sending infrastructure and no attachments. Select:

- Mailster Starter: USD 79 paid for one year, tax excluded; 3 active automations and 10 prechecks/month.
- Mailster SES extension: free connector; AWS service charged separately.
- DigitalOcean Basic Regular: 2 GiB RAM, 1 vCPU, 50 GiB SSD, 2,000 GiB transfer; USD 12/month cap for the full-month server.
- SES Essentials: USD 0.16 per 1,000 recipient emails below 10M/month. Assume complete messages are 32 KB, using AWS's example convention of 0.000032 GB per message, with USD 0.12/GB outgoing data.

All inputs checked 2026-11-05: [Mailster plans](https://mailster.co/pricing/), [SES extension and setup](https://kb.mailster.co/send-your-newsletters-via-amazons-ses/), [AWS SES rates/worked example](https://aws.amazon.com/ses/pricing/), [DigitalOcean server specifications/prices](https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing/droplets).

Let N = contacts and M = 4 × N monthly recipient emails.

- Send charge = M × USD 0.16 / 1,000.
- Data charge = M × 0.000032 GB × USD 0.12/GB.
- Monthly host + sender subtotal B = USD 12 + send charge + data charge.
- First-year known component subtotal Y = USD 79 + 12 × B.

### Limits and missing cost components

These are known-component infrastructure examples, **not exact all-in bills or capacity guarantees**. Excluded: taxes, domain, backups, operational work, optional templates, customization, server scaling/transfer overages and any additional AWS services such as SNS feedback traffic. Sender/host accounts and billing are separate. No new-customer credits are assumed. No observed newsletter workload benchmark proves this server will process a particular large list.

SES requires [production approval](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/request-production-access.html) and suitable [daily/per-second regional quotas](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/quotas.html), checked 2026-11-05.
