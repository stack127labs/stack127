The pricing help article clarifies that “unlimited” refers to the number of Broadcasts, Flows, Email objects, Segments, and team members; actual sending still has a separate quota and overage price.
- The pricing page displays a default 2,000-subscriber price. I checked the official pricing page and the linked pricing-help article, but the text view did not expose selectable rates for 5,000, 10,000, or 50,000 subscribers. Exact totals for those scenarios are left unavailable.
- The published overage is `$100 per 100,000 emails`; pricing and billing pages do not state whether partial units are prorated or rounded up. The 50,000 and 100,000 app-email totals therefore show both a linear estimate and the full-block interpretation.
- The direct official newsroom URL could not be retrieved. The blog and official Product Updates changelog were checked; no shutdown, acquisition, or sunset notice was found there.
- API docs confirm the request endpoint and 202 success response, but no published rate limit, idempotency guarantee, delivery callback, or retry guarantee was found. The transactional-email overview, technical overview, reference, developer documentation index, and help center were checked.
- The privacy page gives general cross-border processing language; the compliance portal identifies AWS Ireland for application data. The precise routing region for outbound mail and the retention period for campaign content were not stated.
