## Cost examples: our calculation

All sources checked 2026-11-05. Use one store/publication, one user and the stated unique subscribed contacts. Assume all readers remain billable active contacts, the list size stays constant, the existing store/domain are already paid, and there is no SMS, back-in-stock traffic, automation, resend, add-on or other email use in the newsletter baseline. These are software components before any applicable tax or external-store charges.

### Newsletter baseline

Inputs: 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 active readers; four emails each per month. Formula: sends = readers × 4. Free does not fit any scenario because its contact limit is 200.

Published monthly formulas: **Essential = USD 10 + USD 0.0125 × billable contacts**; **Pro = USD 49 + USD 0.01 × billable contacts**. Our annual formula at a constant starting audience is **monthly amount × 12 × 0.70**, with a full-year commitment. Monthly send capacities are contacts × 12 or × 24 respectively.

| Readers | Campaign workload | Selected plan and monthly formula | Monthly software result | Included monthly capacity | Annual upfront formula/result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 | Essential: 10 + 0.0125 × 1,000 | USD 22.50 | 1,000 × 12 = 12,000 sends | 22.50 × 12 × 0.70 = USD 189.00 |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | Essential: 10 + 0.0125 × 5,000 | USD 72.50 | 5,000 × 12 = 60,000 sends | 72.50 × 12 × 0.70 = USD 609.00 |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | Essential: 10 + 0.0125 × 10,000 | USD 135.00 | 10,000 × 12 = 120,000 sends | 135.00 × 12 × 0.70 = USD 1,134.00 |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 4 = 200,000 | Pro: 49 + 0.01 × 50,000 | USD 549.00 **conditional formula result** | 50,000 × 24 = 1,200,000 sends by published rule | 549.00 × 12 × 0.70 = USD 4,611.60 **conditional** |

These are **our calculations**, not quotes copied from a checkout. Sources: [plan rates and capacities](https://docs.smartrmail.com/en/articles/8226194-what-are-the-differences-between-the-essentials-and-the-pro-plan), [USD pricing basis](https://docs.smartrmail.com/en/articles/591259-how-is-pricing-calculated), [current pricing and public calculator](https://www.smartrmail.com/pricing) and [annual terms](https://docs.smartrmail.com/en/articles/8710632-how-does-the-smartrmail-annual-plan-work), checked 2026-11-05.

The public calculator's script/configuration confirms the base-plus-contact formula and 0.70 annual multiplier. Its slider ranges only from 200 to 15,000. The current FAQ also calls the plans tiered without supplying a 50,000-contact table. Therefore the **50,000-reader exact offered price remains unverified**; USD 549.00 and USD 4,611.60 are conditional results if the published Pro rate applies unchanged. The same formulas are directly within the calculator's range at the smaller three audiences. All rows still depend on the unresolved active-contact definition. No hidden zero-price placeholders are treated as real paid prices.

For comparison, applying Essential's rate at 50,000 gives USD 10 + 0.0125 × 50,000 = USD 635.00/month, so Pro's formula is lower. This arithmetic does not resolve the high-volume offer.

If Pro-only testing, resend or browse features are needed at the smaller audiences, our calculation gives USD 49 + 0.01 × readers: **USD 59.00 / 99.00 / 149.00 per month** at 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 respectively. Those features are outside the plain four-campaign baseline. A native paid-reader billing variant cannot be priced because it was not established.

Annual growth is extra, not silently included: if an Essential account prepays at 5,000 and the billable count later reaches 5,100, our calculation for those extra contacts is **100 × USD 0.0125 = USD 1.25** for that month, on top of prepaid access. The vendor's actual billing/rounding and active-status determination still apply. Source: [annual growth rules](https://docs.smartrmail.com/en/articles/8710632-how-does-the-smartrmail-annual-plan-work), checked 2026-11-05.

The SavedBy partnership is excluded from ordinary pricing. It requires package-protection participation, a qualifying Shopify store, $500K+ annual revenue and an eligible country. It is not a general free plan for a new solo newsletter.
