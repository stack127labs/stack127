## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Prepaid packages:** The newest located international pricing page, updated 2026-02-12 in the Indonesian locale, lists prepaid resource packages in USD and says they last six months. The Japanese international pricing page, updated 2024-01-02, says SES stopped supporting package purchases and uses postpaid billing; the English international pricing page updated 2024-11-20 lists only pay-as-you-go and the free gift quota. The newer Indonesian page is the most current official rate source, but it does not establish that packages are available to every account country or service region. Treat package availability as conflicting until Tencent confirms the account market. Sources: https://indonesia.tencentcloud.com/ind/document/product/1084/39335 ; https://indonesia.tencentcloud.com/jp/document/product/1084/39335 ; https://intl.cloud.tencent.com/document/product/1084/39335 .
- **Pricing geography:** Pay-as-you-go and dedicated-IP prices are stated in USD without a named SES service region. The API has multiple regional endpoints, but endpoints do not prove regional rate differences or data residency. No currency conversion was made.
- **Newsletter links:** The template FAQ says emails must not include URLs or redirection links, while the sending guide says Tencent can automatically insert a system unsubscribe URL, and the current product updates recommend `Unsubscribe` for marketing email. Read this as a restriction on user-authored URLs with a separate built-in unsubscribe mechanism; confirm with support if a newsletter needs other links. - **Personal-account domain limits:** Specs say an account can have up to 10 sending domains. The 2025-12 product updates say newly verified personal accounts can no longer delete a domain after creation and are capped at 10 per main account; organization-verified accounts are suggested for more flexible management. ## Field problems

- **F04 country coverage:** Tencent separates customer account eligibility, contracting entity, service region, and recipient delivery coverage. Its “200+ countries/regions” statement is a delivery claim, not an account-eligibility list.
- **F08–F12 cost comparisons:** Pricing is low per email but region is omitted; package availability conflicts across international localizations. The cost scenarios therefore use the clearly stated USD pay-as-you-go rate and exclude package discounts.
- **F17 URL policy:** The old template FAQ bans URLs, but the service injects its own unsubscribe link. A creator should confirm policy treatment for ordinary newsletter links before sending.
- **F22/F27 region limits:** SES request-region documentation lists many endpoints, while individual APIs can support fewer regions. API rate and region support are action-specific; do not treat the broad endpoint list as universal for every API.
- **F28 retention/privacy:** The general Tencent Cloud DPSA governs processing and deletion, but no SES-specific breakdown by message, recipient, callback, and backup data type was located.
- **F40 evidence count:** “not published” records list the official pages checked; a missing statement was not treated as proof of a negative.
