## Cost examples

The four F09 newsletter examples and four F10 app-email examples are **our calculations**, not vendor quotes. They use the Mailazy pricing page's illustrative provider input of $0.10 per 1,000 emails (the page says Amazon SES lists about that amount). The pricing page does not state a currency code or provider region, so the dollar symbol and example SMTP amounts are kept as shown.

The examples assume one Mailazy product, one user, no tax, no extra fees, and a sender whose current price matches that example. For F09, each unique subscribed contact receives four campaigns. For F10, one recipient per message is assumed and volume is spread over 30 days. Exact total cost cannot be established without the chosen SMTP sender, its region, current rates, and contact count. Mailazy's own per-month plan price is shown separately from the illustrative SMTP charge.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Product count and quota scope:** the current billing guide says a plan covers one product. The developer plan-limits page says all products in an organization share limits, while the current site also says an organization can hold multiple products. I treated this as unresolved and used one product in the cost examples.
- **Old plan documentation:** current pricing and the directly opened current billing guide agree on Free, Pro, and Business. Search-indexed extracts from the plan-limits and billing pages still show the former Starter and Scale plans, with old prices and quotas. The current pricing page and current direct billing guide are newer and were used for current plan amounts; the stale extracts are not used as current prices.
- **Monthly reset date:** current billing guide says the paid-plan renewal date and Free signup date; the older plan-limits extract says the first of each month UTC. The billing guide is newer, but the technical guide has not been reconciled.
- **Duplicate addresses:** the contacts guide describes case-insensitive lowercased email uniqueness, while the API guide says API-created mixed-case addresses may duplicate imported addresses. Billing treatment for that edge case is not settled.
- **AI charges:** pricing includes plan-limited AI credits; the setup guide says AI drafting uses a separately paid OpenRouter account/key. Current inclusion versus external-key requirements needs clarification.
- **Bounce enforcement:** the 2020 anti-spam policy promises automatic pause thresholds; the current unsubscribe guide says Mailazy has no connection to sender bounce reports and bounced suppressions are manual.
- **Press/newsroom freshness:** the press room lists May 2026 as its newest press release, while the blog carries a newer November 2026 product launch and references an external release. No closure or acquisition notice was found in either source.
- The direct https://mailazy.com/newsroom/ path failed to fetch; the official https://mailazy.com/press/ room was used as the newsroom source.
- **Legal and privacy:** Terms were updated 2026-10-03, but the Privacy and Anti-Spam policies show 2020-01-12 and the Information Security Policy shows 2022-12-01. The public DPA link did not load, and the default data region is not named.
- **Signup:** pricing offers a Free plan and Start free CTA, but the portal returned no readable public page. I did not create an account, so the final registration step and any manual approval remain unchecked.
- **Owner:** official press materials give the public company name and San Francisco headquarters, but the legal operating entity and parent are not identified.
