## Cost examples: our calculations

The main comparison asks for ordinary month-to-month billing. **No month-to-month price was found.** The following annual alternatives retain their commitment; they are our calculations from published “from” rates, not checkout quotes or a new monthly offer. EUR amounts exclude VAT/statutory charges. No promotions, SMS/RCS credits, optional attachment/restore/domain/consulting services or outside app/website costs are included.

Sources checked 2026-11-05: English https://mailup.com/pricing/email/ ; Italian https://mailup.it/prezzo/email/ ; VAT/payment and order conditions https://mailup.com/app/uploads/V04_20250715_MailUp_terms_conditions_ENG.pdf (effective 2025-07-15). No absolute pricing-page update date stated.

### Newsletter baseline

Inputs: one publication, one included user, one list, one authenticated sender domain, four campaigns/month to 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 unique subscribed contacts. Starter's unlimited contact/send model fits the quantity, at its starting purchased speed of 2,000 emails/hour, provided the completion window is acceptable. Assume no other competing traffic, no quiet hours or pauses, and no faster-speed purchase. Suppressed recipients still must not be mailed.

Formulas:
- Monthly recipient emails = subscriber count × 4.
- Annual entry-price alternative = EUR 48/month equivalent × 12 = **from EUR 576/year**.
- Nominal campaign queue-service hours = recipients ÷ 2,000.
- Four-campaign queue-service hours = campaign hours × 4.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 | 1,000 ÷ 2,000 = 0.5 hour; × 4 = 2 hours | Month-to-month unknown; annual Starter alternative from EUR 576/year |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | 5,000 ÷ 2,000 = 2.5 hours; × 4 = 10 hours | Month-to-month unknown; annual Starter alternative from EUR 576/year |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | 10,000 ÷ 2,000 = 5 hours; × 4 = 20 hours | Month-to-month unknown; annual Starter alternative from EUR 576/year |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 4 = 200,000 | 50,000 ÷ 2,000 = 25 hours; × 4 = 100 hours | Month-to-month unknown; annual Starter alternative from EUR 576/year |

These times are our simple purchased-speed calculations, not promised inbox-delivery deadlines. Errors, queue competition, maintenance and operational settings can delay sends. A 50,000-contact campaign cannot be modelled as a one-hour broadcast at the base speed. Faster-speed amounts require a quote.

Alternative annual edition calculations: Plus from EUR 113 × 12 = **EUR 1,356/year**; Premium from EUR 310 × 12 = **EUR 3,720/year**. These upgrade features/speed, not contact capacity. Exact invoice timing and the final configuration follow the order.
