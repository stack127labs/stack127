## Cost examples — our calculations

All workloads assume one publication, one user, no SMS/WhatsApp, no paid-reader revenue, no attachments and shared sending IPs. Domain registration/mailbox costs and applicable tax are extra. The zero-cost result is the platform subscription, not a complete business operating cost. Original currency selectors/dollar notation are retained; no exchange-rate conversion was made.

### Four newsletters per reader each month

Formula: recipient emails/month = unique subscribed contacts × 4.

| Contacts | Formula | Recipient emails/month | Plan with capacity | Ordinary monthly platform cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 | 4,000 | Forever Free | $0 |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 | 20,000 | Standard | Not exact: selected paid price unavailable |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 | 40,000 | Standard | Not exact: selected paid price unavailable |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 4 | 200,000 | Standard | Not exact: selected paid price unavailable |

Free fits because 1,000 ≤ 2,000 contacts and 4,000 ≤ 6,000 monthly emails. The pricing FAQ's three-campaign wording is an arithmetic illustration for the ongoing plan; its explicit three-campaign limit belongs to the 14-day trial. Standard's contact ceiling is 100,000 and its email volume is unlimited, subject to policy/throttling. These facts were checked on 2026-11-04 and pricing was rechecked on 2026-11-05: https://www.zoho.com/campaigns/pricing.html and https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/campaigns-3-0/user-guide/settings/general/articles/v3-subscriptions.

For paid contact count N, monthly platform total = current Standard monthly price for N contacts + any selected extras + applicable tax. The missing first term prevents an exact numeric result. This is a fetch/calculator limitation, not evidence that these tiers are quote-only. Annual commitment advertises 25% saving, but selected annual price and full upfront payment were not verified.

The official comparison article, updated June 11, 2026, quotes $4/month at 500 contacts, $30/month at about 5,000 and approximately $54/month at 10,000. Its scenarios, feature claims and billing mapping do not reliably identify the current checkout selection. These are contextual figures, not our current baseline quotes: https://www.zoho.com/campaigns/marketingmatchbox/best-smb-email-marketing-software.html (checked 2026-11-04).

Campaigns PAYG is a separate alternative: required email credits for this workload = 4 × N, so 4,000 / 20,000 / 40,000 / 200,000 credits respectively. One credit is one recipient email, and unused credits do not expire. Actual spend = sum of prices of purchased packs sufficient to cover the credits; pack denominations/prices were not verified, so these are credit requirements, not exact cash totals. Credits cannot be combined with a concurrent Campaigns monthly plan: https://www.zoho.com/campaigns/pay-as-you-go-plan.html (checked 2026-11-04).

No exact paid-newsletter revenue scenario is given. Native reader checkout/paywall/recurring entitlements were not established, and the review FAQ disallows direct payment links in campaigns. Adding an assumed processor fee would invent a supported workflow: https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/campaigns-3-0/faqs/campaign-review/articles/v3-campaign-review-faq (checked 2026-11-04).
