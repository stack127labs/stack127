## Cost examples — our calculation

These are our calculations from public rates, not checkout quotes. They use ordinary month-to-month plans with no introductory or annual discount. BigMailer fees retain the published `$`; AWS fees use `US$`. BigMailer's currency code was not verified, so a BigMailer dollar fee and an AWS US-dollar fee are shown as components rather than silently added into an exact total.

Inputs: one brand/publication, one user, unique stored contacts equal to the stated audience, one verified sender domain, shared IP, no attachments, no validation purchase, no paid recommendation purchases and no optional white label. Contact and email counts are different units.

For a Business plan, define:

- `C` = stored contacts, `E` = recipient emails/month.
- `B = max(4, ceil(C / 5,000))` contact blocks. The minimum is four blocks, or capacity for 20,000 contacts.
- Selected contact capacity `K = 5,000 × B`.
- Lite platform fee `L = $7.50 × B`; Pro platform fee `P = $15 × B`.
- Included paid sending `Q = 10 × K`.
- BigMailer overage `O = max(0, E − Q) / 1,000 × $0.75`. All examples below have `O = $0`; they do not test fractional overage billing.
- SES à la carte base sending `A = E / 1,000 × US$0.10`. This requires selecting that AWS option; it is not assumed to be every new account's default.
- Pro result `P + O`. Lite components `L + O`, plus `A` and any separately applicable AWS charges.
