## Cost examples — our calculation

These are **our calculations**, using published ordinary prices before tax. All sources were checked on 2026-11-05. One account/publication, one user and one existing verified sender domain are assumed. No introductory discount, charity discount, SMS, ads, optional AI work or custom services are included. Monthly billing has a **minimum three-month commitment**; annual prices are separate.

### Newsletter baseline

Formula: unique active subscribed contacts × four campaigns/month = recipient emails. Select the next sufficient contact band on Profi, whose marketing sending is unlimited. The 1,000-contact workload uses the 2,000 band because there is no 1,000 band in the public calculator.

Sources: [English USD pricing](https://ecomail.app/pricing/), [Czech pricing](https://ecomail.cz/cenik/), the [public rate table](https://ecomail.app/_nuxt/BnCZFwEg2.js), the [calculator display logic](https://ecomail.app/_nuxt/CIsFUQAt2.js), and [billing help](https://support.ecomail.cz/cs/articles/2636778-predplaceny-tarif-nebo-kreditovy-balicek).

Annual English Profi base fees from the same table are USD 350 / 600 / 900 for the first three workloads, paid yearly. They are not ordinary monthly subscriptions. At the 500-contact entry band the actual annual fee is USD 200, while the displayed monthly equivalent is rounded to USD 16.67; multiplying the rounded display by 12 would wrongly produce USD 200.04.

CZK and USD are separate published schedules, not currency conversions. The Czech page quotes prices before 21% VAT; customer-specific tax treatment and any external domain/mailbox costs remain outside these base totals.

There is no priced native paid-reader newsletter variant because paywall/recurring reader billing was not established.

### App-email baseline

[Transactional help](https://support.ecomail.cz/cs/articles/6197248-transakcni-e-maily) permits informational app email on a credit programme or paid Marketer+/CDP, through a verified transactional domain. It says monthly transactional sends should not exceed ten times the contact database size. Recipients themselves do not have to be in that marketing database.

The examples below therefore specify **existing active, consented marketing contacts** as a separate input. They are not a suggestion to import app users without consent, and not a claim that every app has such a database. They conservatively follow the ten-times guidance using the actual stated count rather than unused capacity in a purchased band.

Other inputs: one user, one verified transactional domain, ordinary shared service, no attachments, one recipient per message, **zero marketing campaign sends and zero workflow runs**. Spread sends across 30 days; the last column gives our workload ceiling, not a vendor daily quota. API request rate is separately 1,000/minute per key.

One sent email consumes one credit. These are prepaid inventories, not monthly plans. For example, our calculation for 100,000 messages bought through the 200,000-credit pack is **CZK 12,000 cash paid**, 100,000 credits consumed and 100,000 remaining. Allocating half the pack price to that month's consumption gives **CZK 6,000**, not a CZK 6,000 invoice for buying that pack.

Do not turn leftover credits into permanent cash savings: current terms and help disagree on expiry and on how unused value survives a switch to subscription. The credit programme's permitted quota for a zero-contact account was not independently resolved. The main app-email examples use subscription accounts with explicit contact counts, so their totals do not depend on these credit uncertainties.

- **Status and company change:** Official home/pricing, current terms, company/about material, corporate notice and live status were checked for acquisition, shutdown and sunset notices. - **Regional product/pricing scope:** The English site publishes USD Profi/Marketer+; Czech pricing additionally publishes CDP, credits, EMA and local currency schedules. The calculator contains data for other regions and hidden placeholder keys. We read its display logic rather than treating every number as an offered plan. 50,000+ is quote-only; English USD CDP zeroes are not a free plan.
- **Free and timed trial:** Czech paid-programme and signup help establish both 200 stored contacts and 200 sends/month. This resolves the terse price matrix's contact-only wording. English cards also say “Start 14-day free trial”, but signup/help/terms do not establish a separate larger trial quota or feature period. - **Credit expiry:** English/Czech terms effective 2026-06-16 limit email credits to 24 months; Czech terms limit EMA credits to 12 months. September 2026 help and Czech pricing FAQ say email credits never expire. Both were read directly. Do not publish unlimited credit validity as settled.
- **Unused credit value:** Current terms offset the paid value of unused email credits against the new tariff invoice. Help says to use remaining credits before switching or they lapse. No support request was sent to reconcile this. Credit balances are not silently valued as a guaranteed future discount.
- **Annual discount explanations:** Current Czech entry annual prices/percentage savings do not all equal the help article's generic two-month saving. Exact rate-table annual fees are used; discounts are not generalized across bands or converted from rounded monthly displays.
- **Transactional quota/model:** Current transaction guide, API help, actual send schema and pricing gates were read. Recipients may be outside the database, but the guide also says volume should stay within 10× database size. No explicit unlimited or zero-database credit/transaction quota was found. The calculated scenarios state actual active contacts and zero workflows to avoid hiding the dependency.
- **Countries, SMTP and SDKs:** Account/eligibility, both pricing pages, current terms, API introduction, transactional and integration guides were checked. No exhaustive country list, public external-app SMTP hostname/port or maintained first-party language-SDK inventory was established. - **Advanced operational limits:** API and transactional schemas, size/quota docs, webhook events and domain help supply request rate, 4 MB message size, 2 MB attachment size and callback retries. Send-request idempotency, exact provider redelivery timetable, numeric separate daily cap, max batch count, domain ceiling, inbound reply parsing and a guaranteed limit-increase process remain unestablished. - **List boundaries:** The list-management, contact-type and unsubscribe guides were read. Same-list imports preserve exclusions and duplicate protection; other-list import can create an active subscription after an opt-out elsewhere. Unique account billing and list-specific permission are different rules. No global cross-list suppression guarantee is claimed.
- **Branding and other extras:** Domain, HTTPS, editor, pricing, user and legal sources were checked. The unsubscribe-form system attribution is explicitly immutable. Exact ordinary campaign-logo removal rules, a hosted custom-domain publication website, dedicated-IP pricing and an exhaustive seat-fee schedule were not established. - **Data handling:** The public registry specifies Frankfurt primary infrastructure and potential US data; current DPA includes named processors/security annexes. English/Czech account privacy policies have different effective dates. Those controller policies, recipient-processing agreement and infrastructure statement have separate scopes; they do not establish selectable all-data EU-only routing, complete backup geography or an independent certification.
- **Inactivity conflict:** Signup and downgrade help say deletion after one year without login. Both current legal languages say termination/deletion after two years. Read all four sources; the shorter help period is not silently replaced by the longer legal one. Published Data Act migration and DPA export windows concern different exit procedures and are recorded separately.
- **Reporting/reliability:** Detailed measurement help says accepted mail can still be in spam or quarantine, and pixel/implicit opens have limits. No reproducible independent inbox study or automatic bot-filtering methodology was established. Public status was operational at check time; historical uptime is not a contractual SLA or universal inbox-placement percentage. Terms give weekday support hours and expressly no support guarantees.

## Field problems

- **F01/F39:** A partial asset demerger is not a shutdown or a complete change of the marketing product's operator. The dated notice and current legal identity must be preserved together.
- **F06/F09/F10:** Contact count, included marketing sending, transactional guidance and API requests are different units. App-email comparisons need actual active-contact and workflow assumptions. A purchased contact ceiling alone is not a populated database.
- **F07/F08/F11:** Ongoing Free, a promoted trial, monthly billing with a three-month minimum, annual payment and prepaid credits need separate records. Both credit expiry and switching-value rules conflict.
- **F08/F09:** Regional price schedules and hidden calculator states require care. A 50k placeholder in a shared data object is not a usable published rate.
- **F15/F17/F28/F30:** Account-wide billing deduplication does not mean global opt-out. Exportable consent state, full evidence of lawful permission, vendor asset licensing and backup deletion are separate questions.
- **F23/F27/F32:** Callback retry rules, send-request idempotency, service uptime and inbox placement must not be combined into one reliability claim.
