## Cost examples: our calculations

Inputs: one publication, one user, 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 unique active subscribed contacts; four full-list issues/month. No SMS, ads, paid-reader revenue, attachments or separate host/sender purchase. Contacts are assumed to be the only billable subscribers. Unknown billing treatment of inactive records is not silently resolved.

Formula: recipient emails = contacts × 4. Monthly software cost = published monthly subscriber band. Message allowance = band maximum × 31.
