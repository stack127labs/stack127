## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Transactional price changed:** the 2024 announcement describes $1 per 2,000 transactional messages and monthly free quota wording. Current pricing and Billing docs say paid transactional sends are unlimited and free sends are capped at 4,000 over a rolling 30 days. The current pages are newer and govern the cost examples. Sources: https://loops.so/changelog/transactional-email-is-now-free ; https://loops.so/pricing ; https://loops.so/docs/account/billing (checked 2026-11-06).
- **Status discrepancy:** the live status page showed services operational, while the official status API snapshot dated 2026-11-02 still returned major/partial outage. The history page had no Nov 5 incident in the checked view. This is a stale-source conflict, not evidence that services were down on the check date. Sources: https://status.loops.so/ ; https://status.loops.so/api/v2/status.json ; https://status.loops.so/history (checked 2026-11-06).
- **Blog/newsroom check:** https://loops.so/blog returned 404; no readable newsroom or press page was found at https://loops.so/newsroom or https://loops.so/press. The official Engineering page and Changelog were checked instead for product/company updates and closure/acquisition notices. No such notice appeared there.
- **Signup and language limits:** Terms, pricing, Privacy, and docs were searched for eligible signup countries, recipient restrictions, and dashboard/support language lists. No complete list was found. Terms do list restricted territories; this does not establish all countries where service is available.
- **Approval:** Pricing, getting-started docs, and Terms do not publish a universal approval process or time. The website describes team review and Privacy permits manual review of flagged activity, but these do not settle whether every new sender is reviewed.
- **Transactional-only paid cost:** transactionals are free up to the shared free cap and unlimited on paid plans. Public pricing does not say which paid contact tier applies if an account has no subscribed contacts; $49 is the lowest displayed paid-plan tier, not an exact zero-contact quote.
- **Export scope:** the official CSV guide covers contact records and properties. It does not state whether campaign content, workflow configuration, event history, consent proof, and suppression records can all be downloaded as one package. Privacy and DPA deletion/return terms were also checked.
- **Domains and tracking:** sending-domain docs require customer DNS authentication. The pages checked do not settle a separate branded click-tracking domain, global open/click opt-out, or automated-open filtering; these remain unclear rather than assumed absent.
- **Security portal:** compliance docs link to a SOC 2 trust portal, but the portal exposed no readable report in this check. No certification was inferred from the link.
- **Performance evidence:** status is a point-in-time service indicator, not an inbox placement study. No independent reproducible test was found in the official material.
- **Legacy pricing and documentation:** the 2024 transactional pricing announcement is retained as history. Current pricing and the 2026 Billing documentation settle the live cap and plan inclusion. The old app.loops.so contact-properties page is official but may be less current than docs.loops.so.
