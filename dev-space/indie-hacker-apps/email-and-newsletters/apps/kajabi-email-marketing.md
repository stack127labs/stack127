One Newsletter product fits Basic's five-product capacity, so no separate paid-newsletter software tier was identified for a small list. This is separate from the baseline marketing-broadcast cost: Newsletter subscription and Marketing subscription are different permissions. Sources: https://help.kajabi.com/en/articles/17174574-newsletters-overview ; https://www.kajabi.com/pricing . Checked 2026-11-05.

Inputs: a US merchant on monthly Basic with no more than 2,500 total stored contacts; 100 paid readers each charged USD 10 once that month by a domestic card through Kajabi Payments; all 100 are recurring transactions; no currency conversion, refunds, disputes, optional tax tool, invoicing or instant payout.

- Gross revenue: 100 × USD 10 = USD 1,000.
- Processing: USD 1,000 × (2.9% card fee + 0.7% recurring fee) + 100 × USD 0.30 = USD 66.
- After these processing fees: USD 1,000 − USD 66 = USD 934.
- After the monthly Basic subscription: USD 934 − USD 179 = **USD 755**, before taxes and other excluded costs.

The 0.7% recurring surcharge applies to the first transaction too. These are US Kajabi Payments assumptions, not worldwide Stripe/PayPal rates. Sources: https://www.kajabi.com/pricing ; https://help.kajabi.com/en/articles/17174624-kajabi-payments-fees-united-states . Checked 2026-11-05.
