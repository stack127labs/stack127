Sources: https://www.cleverreach.com/de-de/agb/ ; https://www.cleverreach.com/de-de/preise-und-tarife/ (checked 2026-11-05). The annual one-month notice is consistent.
- **Basic A/B testing:** detailed-price help labels Basic with A/B, but both the explicit tariff matrix and current pricing reserve it for Pro/Enterprise. Use the matrix for budgeting and keep the contradiction visible. Sources: https://support.cleverreach.com/hc/de/articles/115001183694-CleverReach-Preise-im-Detail ; https://support.cleverreach.com/hc/de/articles/202372631-CleverReach-Tarife-%C3%9Cbersicht ; https://www.cleverreach.com/de-de/preise-und-tarife/ (checked 2026-11-05).
- **Hard-bounce rule:** terms describe three consecutive hard failures before blocklisting; operational help says immediate suppression for at least a year. Sources: https://www.cleverreach.com/de-de/agb/ ; https://support.cleverreach.com/hc/de/articles/202372821-Was-ist-ein-Bounce-und-wie-geht-CleverReach-damit-um (checked 2026-11-05). No numeric safe-bounce or complaint threshold is inferred.
- **Activity retention boundary:** the official table gives 50,001–250,000 recipients four months, and “from 250,000” three months. Exactly 250,000 overlaps. Reports have a separate one-year availability period and backups a separate thirty-day period. Sources: https://support.cleverreach.com/hc/de/articles/202372941-FAQ-Wie-lange-speichert-CleverReach-die-Aktivit%C3%A4ten ; https://support.cleverreach.com/hc/de/articles/360007172553-Wie-lange-werden-meine-Reports-gespeichert ; https://support.cleverreach.com/hc/de/articles/202373071-Wiederherstellung-von-Empf%C3%A4ngerdaten-mittels-Backup (checked 2026-11-05).

Cross-list duplication is explicit; a full inactive/bounced/deleted-status and billing-snapshot matrix was not. Sources: https://support.cleverreach.com/hc/de/articles/29025537195794-FAQ-Werden-E-Mail-Adressen-doppelt-gez%C3%A4hlt-wenn-sie-in-mehreren-Empf%C3%A4ngerlisten-vorkommen ; https://support.cleverreach.com/hc/de/articles/202372631-CleverReach-Tarife-%C3%9Cbersicht ; https://support.cleverreach.com/hc/de/articles/202372921-Manuelle-Abmeldung-und-Deaktivierung-von-Empf%C3%A4ngern (checked 2026-11-05).

Minimum blocks, overage, expiry and demo limits are public; exact block prices, paid burst guarantee, delivery-callback contract, replay/idempotency and message-log retention are not established. Neither unlimited marketing sends nor EUR 0.00095 overage makes the missing SMTP tariff known.
- **Paid readers:** inspected pricing, forms, automations, terms and integrations; searched German paid-newsletter terms. DigiMember’s contact-transfer page still directs users to the retired SOAP API. Native checkout, ongoing entitlement revocation, paid-reader fees and recurring-payment migration remain unknown. This is a stale integration instruction, not an apps.md row error.

Exact per-data-type backup/log regions and a verified complete current subprocessor schedule were not established.
