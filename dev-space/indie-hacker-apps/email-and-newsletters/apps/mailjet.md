## Cost examples: our calculations

Prices are USD, before VAT/tax, month-to-month, with no introductory discount. The live price update dated September 9, 2026 is used. Mailjet publishes one email-volume plan ladder covering marketing campaign sends and API/SMTP sends. The workload tables stay separate so the same subscription is not counted twice.

### Newsletter campaigns

Assumptions: one publication, one user, unique active subscribed contacts, four full-list campaigns/month, no SMS, paid-reader revenue, custom enterprise services, dedicated-IP request or domain/mailbox purchase. Total send formula is subscribers × 4. A campaign is sent to the full audience as one campaign unless otherwise noted.

| Subscribers | Formula and monthly recipient emails | Selected plan and calculation | Monthly cost | Conditions |
| ---: | --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 | Starter 8k monthly plan | $9.00 USD | Free is $0 but the editor caps each campaign at 200 recipients; five batches per issue would be needed, subject to the 200/day limit. Starter is the lowest plan for a full-list send without that restriction. |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | Essential 15k: $19 + (5 × $2.58 per additional 1k) | $31.90 USD | Essential has unlimited contacts; overage is billed at renewal. Essential 50k costs $40, so 15k plus overage is lower for this volume. |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | Essential 50k volume tier | $40.00 USD | 50,000 send allowance; no daily limit on this paid plan. |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 4 = 200,000 | Premium 100k: $112 + (100 × $1.39 per additional 1k) | $251.00 USD | Premium 250k is $265, so 100k plus published overage is lower. A dedicated IP is not requested; shared delivery is the baseline. |

### App-generated email via API/SMTP

Assumptions: one verified sender domain, one user, one recipient per message, shared IP, no attachments, no extra integrations, and sends spread evenly across a nominal 30-day month. Average daily volume equals monthly messages ÷ 30. This is an average comparison, not a burst guarantee.

| Monthly outbound messages | Formula / average daily volume | Selected plan and calculation | Monthly cost | Limit note |
| ---: | --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 3,000 | 3,000 ÷ 30 = 100/day | Free, within 6,000/month and 200/day | $0 USD | Free sending allowance includes API and SMTP on pricing page. |
| 10,000 | 10,000 ÷ 30 ≈ 333.34/day | Starter 8k: $9 + (2 × $2.58 per extra 1k) | $14.16 USD | Paid API key default daily cap is 5× plan volume; custom per-key limit can be set. |
| 50,000 | 50,000 ÷ 30 ≈ 1,666.67/day | Essential 50k | $40.00 USD | Volume allowance is exact; default paid API key limit is 5× monthly tier. |
| 100,000 | 100,000 ÷ 30 ≈ 3,333.34/day | Premium 100k | $112.00 USD | Same monthly credit schedule; Premium 100k can request a dedicated IP, but shared IP is assumed here. |

All formulas and totals above are **our calculations**, not Mailjet's quoted scenario prices. Overage formulas multiply the volume beyond plan quota by the current USD rate per 1,000. Tax, wire/SEPA fees (if any), a dedicated IP charge, domains and other optional purchases are excluded. Annual plan discounts are not mixed into monthly examples.

- F04 bundles interface language and account/payment eligibility. Language support is published, but it is not a country eligibility list.

- F13–F16 concern paid reader subscriptions and payment portability. Mailjet is documented as a sender/campaign platform, not a paid-reader billing platform; payment fees and migration are therefore not modeled.
- F27 has numeric limits at plan level and for message batches, but no published numeric API calls-per-second cap or exact temporary hourly cap for a new account.

- F32 SLA URL is an official legal page but is dated and branded for Mailgun; the current Terms make the Service Order controlling. Its applicability to each Mailjet subscription should be rechecked before publishing it as a universal guarantee.
- F33–F34 are not applicable because Mailjet is hosted software, not a self-host package.
- F37 calls for independent evidence, while this research uses official pages for vendor facts. No independent inbox-placement test is claimed.
