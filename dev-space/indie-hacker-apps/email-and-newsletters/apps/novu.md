- Status notice: Novu is active. Its official blog and changelog both show product updates dated November 2, 2026, and its status page reported all services online at 15:02 UTC on November 6, 2026. - Scope: Novu's Notify notification workflows and email channel. Novu Cloud and the free-to-Novu Community self-hosted edition are separate below. Novu Connect's agent conversations are a separate product area. Novu is not a hosted newsletter sender: a connected email provider sends each email and bills separately.

The calculations use Novu's published starting prices and exclude the separate email provider, domain, hosting for self-hosting, tax, and any quote-specific Pro base price.
