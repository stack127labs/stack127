- Current operator: ECOMZ Holding Limited, registered in Cyprus. UniOne also lists UNITHING CORP. as its US licensee. No acquisition or shutdown notice appeared in the official blog and company pages checked. I found no separate newsroom page.
- Country and sanctions note: UniOne's terms require compliance with US and applicable export and sanctions rules. Its API reference also says sending from `example.ru` is prohibited. That is a sender-domain restriction, not proof that all Russian customers are barred. A separate bug-bounty policy excludes rewards to some sanctioned locations, naming Russia, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Sudan, and Syria; that is about bounty rewards, not customer access.
- Marketing email policy: permission-based marketing is allowed, but cold outreach and purchased or rented lists are prohibited. UniOne requires double opt-in, a working unsubscribe, sender contact details, and proof of consent on request. It expects customers to store and manage subscriber lists in their own systems.
- Research scope: official public pages, docs, pricing, terms, policies, blog, and company pages only. I did not create an account, submit forms, or send messages.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Contact storage and export:** the Privacy Policy says customers' contact lists are held on UniOne's servers and can be exported; the email automation FAQ says UniOne does not provide list upload or management and that recipient data comes from the customer's system. The homepage also says it does not store personal or sensitive data. These official pages do not align. I recorded the mismatch instead of assuming full list portability.
- **Data retention:** the Terms say email records may be deleted after 35 days; the current pricing page lists up to 45 days of logs and 100 days of analytics; the Privacy Policy says up to 185 days for sending logs and 400 days for selected copies or anonymized statistics. The API reference itself describes event retention as both up to 45 and up to 32 days depending on tariff. The applicable period by data type and plan needs confirmation.
- **Soft-bounce retries:** the current API reference v1.92 says 48 hours; the Email Statuses guide says 24 hours. I use the current versioned API reference and preserve the conflict because the guide has no update date.
- **Analytics retention:** the plan page says 100 days of analytics; the Performance Report page says the report covers 185 days. They may refer to different datasets, but the pages do not explain the scope.
- **Marketing rules:** newsletters are allowed only for recipients who opted in. The EAUP requires consent evidence and unsubscribe facilities; the automation FAQ specifically requires double opt-in. The Terms say transactional messages cannot be bulk or promotional, so marketing messages must be handled as consented marketing, not disguised as transactional notices.
- **Sanctions and country restrictions:** the service EAUP incorporates US and relevant export/sanctions rules but does not list customer countries. The API reference blocks `example.ru` as a sender domain. The named-country list in the bug-bounty policy applies only to bounty rewards, not service access.
- **Plan term:** pricing shows monthly prices; the Terms have both 30-day renewal language and a separate 90-day termination clause for recurring Orders. Applicability of the longer notice clause to self-service tiers is not clarified.
- **Status:** no official status page was found on the product site, docs, pricing, or footer. Current incidents and measured uptime history are unknown.
- **Price freshness:** the public price table says it was last updated 2026-11-04. Extra-email fees are listed as “up to” $0.75 per 1,000, but the price page does not provide the exact rate at every tier.
- **Support language and country eligibility:** the pages searched do not give a complete list of eligible customer countries or the languages of the control panel and support. Unsubscribe languages are published separately.

## Field problems

- F04 combines customer eligibility, email delivery destinations, and interface languages. UniOne publishes domain-level limits and legal sanctions obligations, but not a complete signup-country list.
- F06/F09 measure emails sent, while the baseline mentions subscribers. UniOne bills for sends and expects the list itself to live in another system.

- F23 combines delivery status events with API retries and webhook retries. Those are different processes and have separate limits.
- F28/F30 privacy and storage claims differ between the homepage, pricing page, Terms, Privacy Policy, and API documentation.
