- `apps.md` row status: `active` (row checked 2026-11-04). The status and Delivery type fit the current product. The “European cloud offer” market label describes Scaleway broadly; TEM itself is available only in Paris (`fr-par`). No other row correction proposed.

- Current product note: Scaleway Transactional Email (TEM) is a live, hosted transactional email service. Scaleway’s newsroom reports its July 2026 acquisition of Qarnot, a high-performance computing company; that is not a TEM acquisition or rename. I found no TEM shutdown or sunset notice in the official blog, newsroom, or product updates checked.
- Region and currency note: TEM can send only from the `fr-par` (Paris, France) region. Its public TEM prices are in EUR, before tax. I found no regional price schedule; the service region and displayed price currency are distinct facts.
- Newsletter policy: TEM permits transactional, event-triggered messages only. Marketing, promotional, advertising, and unsolicited bulk messages are prohibited. Do not use it to send newsletters, even to an opted-in list.
- Country and sanctions note: TEM data processing is in France, but this does not establish which countries may open customer accounts or receive mail. The TEM-specific terms require compliance with applicable law and allow suspension for misuse; a complete account-eligibility or recipient-country list was not found.
- Research scope: public official product, pricing, documentation, terms, anti-spam policy, privacy pages, blog, newsroom, and status page. No account, login, form, or sending test was used.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Free allowance scope:** the current TEM pricing table calls the 300-email Essential free tier “per organization”; TEM plan documentation says plans are project-scoped. The pages do not explain whether one organization's allowance is shared across projects.
- **Partial overage unit:** TEM prices overage per 1,000 emails but does not say how it invoices a partial 1,000-email unit. Cost estimates use a proportional rate and label it as our calculation.
- **Quota and paid allowance:** the technical guide lists a default 10,000 monthly project quota, while current TEM pricing lists 300 Essential emails included and 100,000 Scale emails included. These are different concepts (technical account limit versus billed allowance), but the pages do not show how the technical quota is applied after switching plans or across projects. Support can raise it.
- **Soft-bounce retry:** the Concepts guide says up to 72 hours; the newer October 2026 privacy policy says content is deleted after 36 hours of unsuccessful soft-bounce attempts. An older changelog also says code 452 mailbox-full errors are no longer retried. I preserve the mismatch and use the newer privacy policy for data-retention wording.
- **Delivery-status wording:** the webhook guide says `email_delivered` means the destination server accepted a message and also claims that the event confirms inbox arrival; its schema defines `email_sent_at` as receipt by the destination server. It does not distinguish inbox placement from spam-folder placement, so I record the official wording as conflicting rather than treating it as an independent inbox-placement measurement.
- **MX setup:** current TEM-specific conditions and the Quickstart require SPF, DKIM, MX, and DMARC, while the API documentation says MX is optional but recommended. Confirm with Scaleway before treating MX as a hard prerequisite.
- **Webhook limit and separate cost:** the current TEM product and pricing pages say Essential has one webhook per domain and Scale has unlimited webhooks. The webhook how-to still describes one per domain and refers to beta quotas. Webhooks also use separately billed Topics and Events volume, not included in the TEM price examples.
- **Country eligibility:** the docs identify Paris as the only service region and the terms require lawful use, but none of the pages checked publish a complete list of customer countries or recipient destinations.
- **Account approval:** higher technical quotas need case-by-case support approval after identity and payment validation; no review timeframe is given.
- **Analytics:** TEM offers send activity and reputation data, but it does not track opens, clicks, or conversions. Cockpit's free/premium history windows are Cockpit terms, not TEM email retention.
- **Company notice:** Scaleway's acquisition of Qarnot is documented in the official newsroom, but it concerns HPC and does not change TEM's identity or operator.

## Field problems

- F04 combines service region, customer eligibility, recipient destination, and language. TEM publishes one data/sending region but no complete account or destination-country list.
- F06/F07 distinguish per-recipient billing, an organization-level free allowance, project-level plans, and technical monthly quotas; the docs do not fully reconcile the scopes.
- F09 is not applicable because the TEM policy expressly prohibits newsletters and promotional email.
- F10's billed email is each recipient, not each API request. The pricing page does not explain rounding for partial 1,000-email units.
- F17's unsubscribe and preference-center fields target newsletter tools. TEM prohibits marketing messages and expects the sending application to handle its own transaction workflow.
- F23 combines email retries with webhook delivery retries. TEM publishes message retry descriptions but no exact webhook retry schedule.
- F24's MX requirement differs between the specific conditions / quickstart and the API reference.
- F28/F30 need separate values for message body, metadata, webhook payload, and backups; the current policy provides those categories and says logs remain while the domain is active.

- F33/F34 do not apply to the managed service; the Terraform tutorial deploys customer infrastructure that calls TEM, not a self-hosted TEM server.
