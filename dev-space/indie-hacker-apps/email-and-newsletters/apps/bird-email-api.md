## Cost examples: our calculations

These estimates use the current USD Startup rate of $0.40 per 1,000 recipient emails. Bird charges each recipient send from wallet balance. The $15 monthly Startup deposit is not an included send allowance: it becomes shared Bird balance, and unused funds carry forward. Calculations exclude tax and any other Bird products. They assume one verified sender domain, shared IP, one user, one recipient per send, a 30-day month, and evenly spread volume. Startup allows 30 individual Send API requests/minute; use batch sends or broadcasts for larger campaigns and follow their separate limits.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Price table conflict resolved by the current source:** an older search-indexed extract for `https://bird.com/products/email/pricing` showed fixed monthly send allowances and overage prices. Direct opening that route on 2026-11-06 redirected to `https://bird.com/pricing/email`. The canonical page and current Billing plans docs say paid Email plans are funding plans: $15/$250/$1,000 monthly deposits, usage billed per 1,000 emails, no paid monthly send cap, and unspent balance carries forward. I used the directly opened current page and docs, not the older indexed extract. Current prices can still change and should be rechecked before publishing.
- **Retention conflict remains unresolved:** the Privacy Statement and DPA say email content is kept for up to 72 hours; current Email API docs and FAQ say optional sent-message content can be inspected for up to 30 days. I could not find a statement explaining whether the 72-hour legal limit excludes optional API content storage. Keep both facts visible until Bird clarifies their scope.
- **Wallet expiry is unclear:** Email plan pages say unused balances carry forward at renewal, while General Terms say prepaid balances expire after one year unless the Site or Order Form specifies otherwise. The live Email page does not say whether its rollover overrides that one-year term.
- **Older contracting entity names:** the Feb. 2024 General Terms PDF names MessageBird B.V. and MessageBird USA Inc. The current Terms page, last updated 2024-11-21, names Bird B.V. and Bird.com Inc. I used the newer live Terms page.
- Bird newsroom opened but the web extract contained no readable press items. I checked its index, the blog index, the SparkPost acquisition/rebrand announcements, current product/pricing/docs and the current changelog. No shutdown or sunset notice was found in those checked sources; this is limited to those pages.
- Full customer account-country eligibility and complete support/product language coverage were not published in the current Terms, pricing and supported-country pages checked. Bird says customer support should confirm destination restrictions, which can change.
- Automation docs describe Early access and the flow-builder page publishes invocation pricing, but I could not establish the relevant eligibility and billing rate for every Bird Email API account.
- Contact API and audience export are documented, and the broadcast reports have CSV export limits. The checked pages do not define one export that includes every contact, consent event, suppression and message body together.

## Field problems

- `F09` and `F10`: Bird's paid plan amount is a wallet deposit, not a send bundle or ordinary subscription fee. The examples therefore show usage and the $15 cash deposit separately, including the calculated amount above the deposit. The maximum lifetime of unused prepaid balance is unclear between the Email pricing page and General Terms.
- `F21`: Email API sends can be triggered by the developer's app, while Bird's native workflow builder is a separate Early access feature with separate invocation charges. Do not merge those into one automation price.
- `F28` and `F30`: region residency, subprocessors and retention periods describe different data flows. In particular, sent-content retention has an unresolved 72-hour versus 30-day difference.
- `F33` and `F34`: Bird also sells separately licensed PowerMTA for customer infrastructure. It is not the hosted Bird Email API listed in `apps.md`, so its license and setup are not scored here.
