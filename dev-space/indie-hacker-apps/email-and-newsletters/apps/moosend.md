## Cost examples: our calculations

All sources checked on **2026-11-04**. These are **our calculations**, not tested invoices. Sources print a dollar sign; the inspected extracts did not explicitly establish the billing currency code. Amounts below retain **$** without conversion or silently assigning an ISO currency.

### Newsletter: four campaigns each month

Inputs: one publication, one user, shared sending, one verified sender domain, no attachments, 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 unique active subscribed contacts. Send four emails to each contact monthly. Exclude ads, SMS, taxes, external site/domain purchases and paid-reader revenue.

Price references: https://moosend.com/blog/cheap-email-marketing/ publishes $16 / $88 / $315 for 1,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 contacts; https://moosend.com/moosend-vs-mailerlite/ publishes $48 for 3,001 to 5,000 contacts and agrees on the other bands. The current selector at https://moosend.com/pricing/ and its https://moosend.com/pricing/?no_head=1 variant did not expose selected amounts. **References are published first-party prices; the current selected monthly totals remain unverified.**

| Active contacts | Our send calculation | Pro reference fee | Marketing send allowance | Our conditional software/sending subtotal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 | $16/month | Unlimited within active-contact tier | $16 + $0 send overage = **$16/month if reference price still applies** |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | $48/month | Unlimited within active-contact tier | $48 + $0 send overage = **$48/month if reference price still applies** |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | $88/month | Unlimited within active-contact tier | $88 + $0 send overage = **$88/month if reference price still applies** |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 4 = 200,000 | $315/month | Unlimited within active-contact tier | $315 + $0 send overage = **$315/month if reference price still applies** |

Formula: `monthly subtotal = selected Pro fee for active contacts + applicable extra charges`. Subscription coverage follows https://docs.moosend.com/user-guide/articles/KnowledgeBase/54463-Subscription-plans-and-credits?lang=en_US, updated 2025-10-21. This does not promise a particular message burst or transactional entitlement. The paid-contact total must count all billable unique addresses across the account, including other lists.

Billing is not a count of every stored record. Archive excludes a contact from billing (https://docs.moosend.com/user-guide/articles/KnowledgeBase/54358-archiving-or-deleting-recipients?lang=en_US, 2025-09-11). Strong double-opt-in excludes unverified subscribers (https://docs.moosend.com/user-guide/articles/KnowledgeBase/54426-make-your-email-list-gdpr-compliant?lang=en_US, 2025-09-11). A simple unsubscribe, archive, suppression and deletion have different consequences; do not delete and replace lists to bypass tier billing.

### Credits: a separate payment model

The page displays a **350,000-credit pack for $350**, plus larger packs (https://moosend.com/pricing/). It is not established as the smallest available purchase. https://docs.moosend.com/user-guide/articles/KnowledgeBase/54463-Subscription-plans-and-credits?lang=en_US defines one credit per recipient email. Purchased credits normally do not expire; the terms reserve exceptions.

If that advertised pack is bought for newsletter campaigns, our calculation is:

| Newsletter workload | Credits used in one month | Credits left from 350,000 | Cash paid for the pack |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4,000 recipient emails | 4,000 | 350,000 − 4,000 = 346,000 | $350 once |
| 20,000 recipient emails | 20,000 | 350,000 − 20,000 = 330,000 | $350 once |
| 40,000 recipient emails | 40,000 | 350,000 − 40,000 = 310,000 | $350 once |
| 200,000 recipient emails | 200,000 | 350,000 − 200,000 = 150,000 | $350 once |

`$350 ÷ 350,000 = $0.001 per purchased credit` is **our arithmetic**, not a standalone monthly bill or confirmed transactional tariff. The purchased pack can cover multiple months until depleted. A new smaller purchase, taxes, optional add-ons and account access conditions may change the practical choice.

If a subscription and credits coexist, being over the subscriber limit can consume credits for the **entire** campaign. The guide's example is 5K subscription / 10K recipients: **10,000 credits consumed**, not 5,000. This is not an automatic $5 incremental overage. An account without sufficient credits may need an upgrade; continued sending is not guaranteed.

## Conflicts and unknowns

All sources below checked **2026-11-04**.

- **Current price selection:** https://moosend.com/pricing/ and https://moosend.com/pricing/?no_head=1 were opened. They show the plan model, discounts and credit packs but hide selected Pro amounts in extraction. https://moosend.com/blog/cheap-email-marketing/, https://moosend.com/moosend-vs-mailerlite/ and https://moosend.com/download.php?file=2026%2F01%2FMoosend-vs-Omnisend.pdf provide the price references used above. Their publication is clear; applicability to today's checkout is not. Exact annual rounding and ISO billing currency remain unknown. No third-party price is used.
- **Trial data:** pricing promises account/data preservation and offers credits after trial; terms §5 (https://moosend.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Moosend-Terms-and-Conditions-June-2025-1-.pdf, June 2025) warns of permanent loss without purchasing the same-service subscription. This is a real official-source conflict. Do not assume a credit purchase satisfies the contract's subscription condition.
- **Renewal notice:** pricing's cancel-anytime language and https://docs.moosend.com/user-guide/articles/KnowledgeBase/54428-manage-your-subscription-plans-and-credits?lang=en_US (2025-12-15) describe an in-account process/term-end cancellation. Terms §17(b) defaults to 60 days' written notice unless the purchased arrangement differs. Actual account/order conditions must resolve it. No refund of unused term is promised.
- **Unsubscribe versus suppression:** https://docs.moosend.com/user-guide/articles/KnowledgeBase/54389-customize-the-unsubscribe-settings-in-moosend?lang=en_US says direct manual/API single-add can re-add a previously unsubscribed address; https://docs.moosend.com/user-guide/articles/KnowledgeBase/54422-import-recipients?lang=en_US (2026-09-17) says bulk import removes unsubscribed/duplicate members.
