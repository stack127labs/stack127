## Cost examples — our calculation

All examples are before tax and exclude optional SMS, ads, campaign-management services, paid-reader revenue and dedicated-IP upgrades. The website shows dollar-sign monthly prices. Do not silently treat a 30-day marketplace subscription as a calendar-month direct contract. Newsletter inputs: one publication, one user, managed delivery, an existing store/data source, unique subscribed contacts only, four full-list campaigns each month, and no other billable contacts or email traffic.
