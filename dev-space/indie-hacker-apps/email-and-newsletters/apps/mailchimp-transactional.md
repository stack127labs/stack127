## Cost examples: our calculations

Prices are USD/month before tax. Baseline: one verified sender domain, one user, shared IP, one recipient per message, no attachments, messages spread across a nominal 30-day month. The block prices below cover Transactional email only. Full paid access also requires a Mailchimp Standard or Premium **monthly** Marketing plan. The current lowest published Standard starting price is $20/month for up to 500 Marketing contacts; actual price rises with contact tier. No campaign sends are needed for Transactional use, but the plan fee remains.

Formula: `blocks = ceiling(monthly transactional emails ÷ 25,000)`. For these examples, all volumes fit the first tier, so `Transactional add-on cost = blocks × $20`. `Combined minimum = add-on cost + $20 Standard base plan`. Average daily volume is monthly emails ÷ 30 and is not a burst quota.

| Monthly app emails | Average per day | Block calculation | Transactional add-on only | Minimum combined monthly cost | Conditions |
| ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 3,000 | 100 | ceiling(3,000 ÷ 25,000) = 1 block; 1 × $20 | $20 | $40 | Full paid access requires a Standard/Premium monthly plan. The separate one-time demo may cover up to 500 total messages, not a recurring 3,000/month workload. |
| 10,000 | 334 | ceiling(10,000 ÷ 25,000) = 1 block; 1 × $20 | $20 | $40 | One recurring block; unused capacity expires at cycle end. |
| 50,000 | 1,667 | ceiling(50,000 ÷ 25,000) = 2 blocks; 2 × $20 | $40 | $60 | Two blocks; assumes the published first-tier rate. |
| 100,000 | 3,334 | ceiling(100,000 ÷ 25,000) = 4 blocks; 4 × $20 | $80 | $100 | Four blocks; assumes the published first-tier rate. |

These are **our calculations**, not vendor quotes. They assume a full billing month and no promotional discount, tax, optional dedicated IP, SMS credits, or overage. Average/day figures do not establish a reliable hourly burst allowance: paid hourly quota varies by account reputation and sending history, and excess mail can queue for up to 48 hours. First-month amounts may be prorated for an existing Mailchimp account. The 50% offer applies only to qualifying 1M+ monthly send purchases and is not used here.
