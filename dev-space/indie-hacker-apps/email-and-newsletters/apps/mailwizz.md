## Cost examples — our calculation

These are stated component calculations, not MailWizz prices for managed delivery. An exact all-in operating cost is **not comparable** without the chosen domain, backup, administration, tax and any optional support/extension charges. No universal server capacity or labour estimate is assumed.

Inputs, all before tax:

- One Regular licence for the operator's own newsletter/app: USD 59 paid annually, or USD 79 paid once for the product's lifetime. Both include one month Premium Support. No ordinary month-to-month software plan is listed. The annual fee is not presented as a monthly subscription.
- DigitalOcean Basic Regular host: USD 12/month, 2 GiB RAM, 1 vCPU, 50 GiB SSD, 2,000 GiB transfer. Run the application and database together on this illustrative server; this is not a measured recommendation for 50,000 contacts.
- Amazon SES **Essentials**, one region/account, shared sending infrastructure, one verified sender domain, one recipient/message, no attachments, 32 KB per message. MailWizz supports the native SES web-API delivery route; the example does not rely on direct SMTP connectivity from the host.
- Essentials at these volumes: USD 0.16 per 1,000 recipient emails. Use AWS's own 32 KB pricing-example conversion, 0.000032 GB per email and USD 0.12/GB. The page also lists an alternative à-la-carte offer; it is a different selection, not silently mixed into this scenario.
- No promotional coupon, AWS free credits, inbound email, dedicated IP, optional validation, third-party builder subscription or extra support pack. Transactional workload needs no marketing journey runs or stored marketing audience for this calculation.

Let `M` be monthly recipient emails:

```text
SES monthly charge = M × USD 0.16 / 1,000
                   + M × 0.000032 GB × USD 0.12/GB
                   = M × USD 0.00016384

Host + SES monthly operating components = USD 12 + M × USD 0.00016384

Regular annual-licence yearly components
  = USD 59 + 12 × (USD 12 + M × USD 0.00016384)

Regular lifetime-licence first-year components
  = USD 79 + 12 × (USD 12 + M × USD 0.00016384)

Regular lifetime later-year components
  = 12 × (USD 12 + M × USD 0.00016384)
```

Amounts below retain the calculation's exact decimals; actual provider invoices may apply currency rounding. Annual totals assume the same workload every month for 12 months. Annual subscription renewal remains USD 59/year at the checked price; lifetime has no repeat software payment, but hosting/sending continue.
