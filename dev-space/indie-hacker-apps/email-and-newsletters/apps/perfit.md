# Perfit / Marketing Nube

**Rename and sunset notices:** Perfit is now presented as **Marketing Nube (ex Perfit)** by Tiendanube. The marketing product remains active; the Perfit-branded site is still live. Official listing: https://www.tiendanube.com/tienda-aplicaciones-nube/perfit (checked 2026-11-05). No full marketing-product shutdown or exact acquisition closing date was established from the inspected official material.

Preserve that restriction when considering the service for a newsletter; do not infer eligibility from a supported currency. ## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Overages:** https://docs.myperfit.com/es/articles/838-tipos-de-planes explicitly distinguishes extras at or below 5% from larger extras; https://docs.myperfit.com/es/articles/5455356-como-funcionan-los-envios-excedentes simply says extras are charged next cycle. Both were checked 2026-11-05. Confirm the small-overage exception before applying one exact formula.
- **Sender domain:** the current Tiendanube listing requires an own-domain mailbox, while https://docs.myperfit.com/es/articles/5423682-remitentes-nueva-modalidad (updated 2024-01-22) describes fallback to an authenticated Perfit domain. The linked https://docs.myperfit.com/es/articles/3275033-como-conseguir-un-correo-con-dominio-propio explains external domain/mailbox acquisition. All checked 2026-11-05. Do not promise fallback availability to a new Marketing Nube customer.
- **Annual price and tax:** read pricing, its public pricing script, plan types, payment instructions and terms. Exact annual upfront payment and a jurisdiction-specific before-tax breakdown were not established. The public catalogue returned current positive monthly tiers; initial HTML placeholders were not used as live quotes. Zero-priced catalogue entries omitted by the page were not treated as extra free plans.
- **Trial and regional payment scope:** the official Tiendanube listing advertises seven days without its detailed trial limits. Pricing FAQ restricts Argentine transfer eligibility to a 5,000-contact plan, whereas the payment-method guide lists transfer more generally. Account-specific eligibility remains unresolved. - **Retention:** terms say three months of campaign activity, while pricing says 90 days. These were kept in their original units; no exact equivalence was assumed. Brazilian inactivity-notification wording differs from Spanish terms and is recorded separately.
- **Unpublished capabilities:** paid-reader billing/migration and publication/consent-archive completeness were checked in pricing, integrations, forms, export/list actions, Contacts API and terms. - **Privacy and controls:** exact data regions, DPA and named subprocessor register were sought in privacy, both country terms, the legal collection and developer material.
