## Cost examples

All amounts below are our calculation for SMTP2GO email sending. The examples count one outbound message per recipient and use monthly billing. They exclude tax, optional add-ons, the customer's application and domain, and any mailbox needed for replies. The customer-owned domain is required for a verified sender; SMTP2GO does not charge a separate provider fee for outbound SMTP/API.

- **3,000 sends/month — our calculation:** 3,000 ÷ 10,000 = 0.3 of the minimum paid plan allowance. The 10K Starter plan is needed because free stops at 1,000/month. Total: US$15 for one month, or US$100 for an annual subscription. The pricing page advertises two months free, but that promotion does not match the displayed annual arithmetic.
- **10,000 sends/month — our calculation:** 10,000 fits within the 10K Starter allowance. Total: US$15 for one month or US$100 for an annual subscription; no prorating is applied.
- **50,000 sends/month — our calculation:** 50,000 ÷ 50,000 = one allowance. The July 2026 company comparison lists US$30/month for 50K. The current plan selector instead shows 40K, so the current availability of that exact allowance should be rechecked before publication.
- **100,000 sends/month — our calculation:** 100,000 ÷ 100,000 = one Professional allowance. Current pricing lists US$75/month or US$750/year; one dedicated IP is included. No overage is needed.

Daily peak assumption for each volume is the monthly send count divided evenly across 30 days: 3,000/30 = 100/day; 10,000/30 = 333⅓/day; 50,000/30 = 1,666⅔/day; 100,000/30 = 3,333⅓/day. The free plan's 1,000-email/month limit does not cover the 3,000-send case. A newly created account also starts at 1,000/day until its first review is complete. A 100K account can send above its allowance up to 300K after activation; current Professional overage is US$0.85 per extra 1,000 emails. An overage scenario is not substituted for the 200K volume example because the required scenario volume is 100K.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **10K Starter annual discount:** The latest monthly price is US$15 and the annual selector shows US$100/year. The page advertises two months free, but those amounts do not match a two-month discount. I preserve the stated prices and do not infer how the promotion is calculated.
- **50K plan:** A company article and the plan differences help list a 50K Starter plan, while the current pricing volume selector lists 40K. The July 2026 company article lists US$30/month for 50K. I keep this current official article's price and flag the plan-capacity mismatch.
- **Bounce thresholds:** The detailed bounce-rating help page uses 5% and 10% bands; the migration FAQ uses 8% and 12%. The detailed rating page is used for current thresholds, but the older FAQ conflict remains.
- **Independent placement claim:** SMTP2GO's November 3, 2026 company blog gives the newest figure: 96% and first place; its July 1 article says 95.5% and second-highest. The latest official page is used. This remains a vendor-reported account of an external study, not an independently verified result.
- **Webhook retry schedule:** The current developer guide says up to 35 attempts in 48 hours, but the detailed retry intervals extend beyond 48 hours and appear to exceed 35 attempts. The page does not resolve its own mismatch.
- **Email body retention:** The June 2026 company security blog says body content is not stored; the older Privacy policy says one email per 1,000 is retained for 35 days. I use the newer account as the current claim but preserve the unresolved mismatch.
- **Unknown pages searched:** Country eligibility and interface languages: pricing, signup instructions in Quick Start, About, Terms, and help home; duplicate-recipient billing: Terms, pricing, email API, billing FAQ; API request ceiling: rate-limit, API getting-started/send docs, pricing, and Terms; complete account-data export and deletion: data export, cancellation, privacy, Terms, template docs; open/bot filtering: open-tracking, charts, and FAQ. Those pages did not state a complete answer.
- **Other limits:** SMTP2GO publishes a public status page and a contractual downtime credit but no independently measured uptime history. Email bodies are not normally stored, but the privacy page describes one-in-1,000 compliance retention for 35 days; optional archiving changes that retention.

F09 and F13–F16 are therefore not applicable as native newsletter-product functions; outbound volume costs are handled in F10.
