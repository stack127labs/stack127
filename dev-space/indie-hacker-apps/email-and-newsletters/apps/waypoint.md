## Conflicts and unknowns

- The Terms, last updated 2023-04-19, prohibit advertising or promotional sends. The batch-send docs describe weekly reports, product updates and recommendations. The docs do not define whether those must be strictly non-promotional. I recorded this as a conflict; ask Waypoint before using it for any newsletter.
- Pricing and billing call the allowance “emails” but do not say if multiple recipients in one message count as one email or several. Calculations assume one recipient per message.
- The attachment docs give 5 MB standard attachment guidance and also give 2 MB Free/Starter and 5 MB Growth/Pro total-message limits, while pricing/changelog advertise 5/10/20 MB attachment sizes. The total composed-message cap is not reconciled.
- Current pricing says free access needs no card and lasts until live sending; older Terms have generic trial language about possible billing details and automatic charging. Current page describes test-only use but gives no fixed trial length.
- Annual cycle is permitted in Terms, but current displayed plan prices contain no annual amount. The rollover FAQ heading has no answer in accessible page text.
- Billing currency code, tax treatment, customer eligibility by country, supported interface languages, daily/API rate limits, precise data-center regions, DPA/subprocessor list, contact-list export, two-factor login and IP warm-up requirements were not published in the official pages searched.
- No official shutdown/acquisition notice appeared. The About page says launched 2023; the blog's newest visible post is May 20, 2025, while the product changelog has entries through June 2026.
