## Cost examples

The newsletter and application-email totals in F09/F10 are **our calculations** from the published international pay-as-you-go rate of USD 0.29 per 1,000 recipient messages. The formula is monthly recipient messages ÷ 1,000 × USD 0.29. They are ongoing totals after the account's one-time 2,000-email free allowance has been exhausted. The 1,000-subscriber newsletter case sends 4,000 messages/month; 5,000 sends 20,000; 10,000 sends 40,000; 50,000 sends 200,000. The app-email cases use 3,000, 10,000, 50,000, and 100,000 messages/month respectively. Assume one recipient per message, no attachments, one verified sender domain, shared IP, and no tax. USD values are the international-site list amounts; Alibaba may settle in eligible local currencies and apply country taxes. Sending volumes may exceed the initial 2,000/day quota, so these costs do not guarantee that the account can send the assumed daily pattern.

Resource plans are a separate prepaid option with six-month expiry. Published package prices are USD 13.05/50,000, USD 121.80/500,000, USD 230.55/1,000,000, and USD 1,123.75/5,000,000. Do not combine packages or treat their unused credits as rollover. The 10,000-message package has conflicting promo wording; see below. Dedicated IP is separate at USD 128 per IP/month in each of Hangzhou, Singapore, Germany (Frankfurt), or USA (Virginia). Email verification is a separate one-year prepaid add-on.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **10,000-message price:** The current billing guide, updated 2026-08-31, lists USD 5.22, but that row's savings column says “$0 for new customers (Save $5.22).” The current international pricing page lists 50,000 messages as its smallest package and omits the 10,000 tier. The help page points to the logged-in purchase page for current promotions. No login was attempted, so the new-customer amount remains conflicting.
- **Regional rates and settlement:** International pricing and billing pages publish standard message/package rates in USD without assigning them to one sending region. Dedicated IP is explicitly listed at USD 128/month separately for Hangzhou, Singapore, Frankfurt, and Virginia. The registration country sets account billing currency and tax; eligible accounts may settle in other currencies. No country-by-country DirectMail price table was published in the checked pages.
- **Country delivery limits:** Four active regions are documented and Sydney is marked decommissioned. The international account registration page excludes mainland China account registration, while Hangzhou service use requires real-name registration. I checked the region endpoint list, account registration guide, DirectMail FAQ, product rules, and international Product Terms; none published a full recipient-country block/allow list. Sanctions and local email laws still apply.
- **Blog/newsroom:** Checked Alibaba Cloud blog search and press-room search for DirectMail shutdown, retirement, and acquisition notices. The blog surfaced older setup/deliverability content; no closure or acquisition announcement was found. Current product and docs pages were also checked.
- **DKIM setup:** A June 3, 2026 domain FAQ says request the DKIM record by ticket (1–3 business days); the dedicated June 2, 2026 DKIM guide says copy the record from the console. The dedicated setup guide is the more specific current procedure, but the official docs are inconsistent.
- **Consent and export:** Checked product rules, template FAQ, recipient list creation, invalid-address FAQ, and product terms. Newsletter opt-in is mandatory and invalid addresses can be exported, but the docs do not establish a native signup form, a hosted preference center, or a complete export of all recipient data/consent/history.
- **Privacy and retention:** The region selected determines stated data-storage region and data statistics are retained 30 days. The DirectMail-specific location and deletion schedule for message bodies, recipient records, and backups was not published in the pages checked. Product Terms permit email scanning/storage for abuse prevention without a fixed duration in that section.
- **Support and status:** Alibaba Cloud publishes ticket support and a service status page. DirectMail's international SLA page is dated 2023-01-13 and advertises 99.5% monthly uptime for paid use, subject to exclusions and credit claim terms; no later DirectMail-specific SLA revision was found.
