# Brevo

## Cost examples: our calculation

All examples use ordinary month-to-month software prices before tax. No promotion, nonprofit discount, annual commitment, SMS, ads or paid-reader revenue is included. One user and an existing authenticated sender domain are assumed. Domain registration and location-dependent tax are outside these totals. Dollar amounts retain the source's `$` notation; no currency conversion is performed.

Sources checked 2026-11-04: [current plan tiers](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208589409-About-Brevo-s-pricing-plans), [Free limits](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208580669-FAQs-What-are-the-limits-of-the-Free-plan), [shared marketing/transactional credits](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/8292912279954-Add-or-remove-emails-from-your-plan), [prepaid credits and extras](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409354969746-Customize-your-plan-with-add-ons), and [public calculator attempted](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/). The calculator returned only a tracking frame. An authenticated account was not used to retrieve prices.
