## Cost examples: our calculation

Baseline: one publication, one user, one list of unique subscribed contacts and an existing verified sender domain. Four issues per contact each month; no ads, SMS, paid-reader revenue or tax. Main-service sending is included. Connect software and AWS are separate bills. Annual examples are separate from ordinary monthly prices, with no silent currency conversion.

Price evidence checked 2026-11-04: [main calculator](https://emailoctopus.com/pricing), [Connect calculator](https://emailoctopus.com/pricing-connect), [current factual price summary](https://emailoctopus.com/the-facts), [billing rules](https://help.emailoctopus.com/article/9-how-does-emailoctopus-pricing-work) and [subscriber counting](https://help.emailoctopus.com/article/43-how-the-number-of-subscribers-is-counted). Contact/send slider values and the month-to-month selection could not be reliably read. The public summary labels the quoted Pro figures as yearly billing; older monthly figures are not substituted.

### Connect: software plus named external sender

Named setup: hosted Connect, one AWS SES sending region, an existing verified domain, shared IP, no optional SES add-ons or attachments, and no free-tier/promotional credits. The comparison uses **SES à-la-carte** mode explicitly. It is not the default Essentials offer for every new account.

Our formula: **Connect subscription + (recipient emails ÷ 1,000 × USD 0.10) + AWS data/other applicable charges + tax**.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Billing period:** current [pricing](https://emailoctopus.com/pricing), [Connect pricing](https://emailoctopus.com/pricing-connect) and [November 2026 summary](https://emailoctopus.com/the-facts) label entry figures as billed yearly. A [September 2025 official blog](https://emailoctopus.com/blog/kit-formerly-convertkit-price-hike) also labels USD 12/28/40 as monthly. These older descriptions are not proof of current ordinary monthly quotes. The current summary supplies some annual contact-band amounts, but not every selected sending allowance.
- **Downgrade wording:** [trial help](https://help.emailoctopus.com/article/155-do-you-offer-a-trial) says all plans are rolling monthly, whereas current billing/terms explicitly offer yearly subscriptions and renewal-only annual downgrades. The current term-specific rules are used; cancel-anytime wording does not imply an annual refund.
- **Inactive Free accounts:** [cancellation help](https://help.emailoctopus.com/article/367-how-to-cancel-my-pro-plan) says deletion after one year; [current terms](https://emailoctopus.com/legal/terms) and [privacy](https://emailoctopus.com/legal/privacy) say 24 months of inactivity, with data deletion within the following month. This is retained as a conflict rather than silently combining the two.
- **Storage scope:** [older storage help](https://help.emailoctopus.com/article/8-data-storage-and-gdpr-compliance) says data is stored in Ireland. [Current subprocessors](https://emailoctopus.com/legal/subprocessors) describe primary Irish storage plus US/other-region delivery, AI screening and support/logging access. These are different processing scopes; the older short statement is not an EU-only processing guarantee.
- **External AWS price/data scope:** [SES pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/ses/pricing/) distinguishes Essentials and à la carte, and its attachment-only data label is broader in worked outgoing-data examples. Named price mode and unpriced data charges remain visible in the calculations; no universal free SES allowance is assumed.
- **SES access timing:** vendor help describes a usual 48-hour response, while [AWS production-access documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/request-production-access.html) states an initial response within 96 hours and notes approval can take longer. These describe different expectations; no fixed approval promise is assigned.
- **API access limitation:** [v2 reference](https://emailoctopus.com/api-documentation/v2) extracted zero lines; its trailing-slash retry returned 503. [Legacy v1](https://emailoctopus.com/api-documentation) is readable but explicitly no longer maintained. Main API availability/scope is independently documented, so reference extraction failure is not treated as a product outage. Current rate ceilings, payload/batch limits, official SDK support and relay-style delivery guarantees remain unverified.
- **Rate-limited consent article:** direct [double opt-in help](https://help.emailoctopus.com/article/36-how-to-edit-double-opt-in) repeatedly returned HTTP 429. The official search index supplied its complete functional explanation; billing/glossary help corroborates pending status. The note records that distinction rather than pretending all direct opens succeeded.

Rollover, prepaid per-email overage and exact Free overflow were sought in pricing/billing/terms. Pro workflow/run ceilings were sought in free/advanced automation/pricing. Help's response-time statement and marketing's average chat speed are not a contractual numerical uptime or support SLA. No independent inbox-placement study was established from official material.
