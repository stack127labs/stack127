### Newsletter and lifecycle baseline

Inputs: one WordPress site/publication, one user, one verified sender domain, unique subscribed contacts, four recipient emails per contact per month, no attachments, paid-reader revenue, SMS, advertising or extra site. Assume no additional billable WordPress users, unconfirmed/inactive contacts or distinct transactional recipients beyond the selected tier. This matters because the billing total can exceed the newsletter list.

`Monthly recipient emails = unique subscribed contacts × 4.`

Business includes the sending service and Premium. Creator includes Premium but needs another sender. Neither supplies the WordPress host. Current pricing has a 1,500 tier rather than a new 1,000 tier.

### Named host and external-sender variant

Our illustrative host is a DigitalOcean Basic regular Droplet: **US$12/month**, 2 GiB RAM, 1 vCPU, 50 GiB SSD and 2,000 GiB transfer, running WordPress/PHP/database together. This is a price input, not a tested capacity recommendation. Source: https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing/droplets.

For Creator, choose documented **Amazon SES HTTP API**, one production-approved region, ordinary shared sending, no free credits, no dedicated IP/add-ons, and **32 KB/message = 0.000032 decimal GB/message** using AWS's decimal-unit pricing example. SES Essentials input is **US$0.16/1,000 recipient emails + US$0.12/GB outgoing data**. Sources: https://kb.mailpoet.com/article/135-the-send-with-tab, https://aws.amazon.com/ses/pricing/. This API choice does not require DigitalOcean's blocked SMTP ports; source: https://docs.digitalocean.com/support/why-is-smtp-blocked/.
