- **Payment cadence:** https://buttondown.com/features/paid-subscriptions describes recurring pay-what-you-want; https://docs.buttondown.com/paid-subscriptions describes one-time. July 2026 entries at https://buttondown.com/changelog discuss monthly/yearly fixes. Fixed recurring subscriptions are clear; this optional model needs a current configuration check.
- **Dedicated IP and attachments:** https://docs.buttondown.com/dedicated-ip labels Professional at the top but requires enterprise in the body. https://docs.buttondown.com/fancy-mode says both 1 MiB and 2 MB per attachment. Neither contradiction is silently resolved into a single promise.
- **Billing, eligibility and transport gaps:** pricing, terms, account review, subscriber API/create, sending/domain and automation/timing/filter docs were checked. A complete billable-status matrix, cross-newsletter billing deduplication, cap-crossing action, all-country signup eligibility, run/concurrency caps, complete exit rules, SMTP submission route and transport byte/burst ceilings were not established. - **Authentication and unsubscribe scope:** custom-domain, infrastructure, preflight, forms/Portal and templating docs were checked. - **Portability and privacy gaps:** subscriber/content/event exports, deletion/account docs, privacy and DPA were read. Exports include status, dates, IP and metadata when present; a complete historical consent ledger and media-complete site backup were not established. Support/status/terms publish useful routes and availability, but no contractual uptime/reply-time promise or independent delivery benchmark was established. No support contact or test send was made.
