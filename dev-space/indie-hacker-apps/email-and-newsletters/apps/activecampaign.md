## Cost examples: our calculations

All calculations below use the approved fields.md scenarios and sources checked **2026-11-04**. They are **our calculations**, not vendor quotes. Keep account contact limits, monthly recipient sends, workflow runs and API requests separate. Prices are before tax; no currency conversion is performed.

### Ordinary newsletter and marketing journeys

Inputs: one publication, one user, unique subscribed contacts, four campaigns per contact per month, no inactive stored contacts, no attachments, SMS, ads, transactional mail or paid-reader revenue. Hosted marketing sending is included.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 | Starter, new-customer account | 1,000 × 10 = 10,000 | Unknown; live price not recovered |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | Starter, new-customer account | 5,000 × 10 = 50,000 | Unknown; live price not recovered |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | Starter, new-customer account | 10,000 × 10 = 100,000 | Unknown; live price not recovered |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 4 = 200,000 | Plus or higher; Starter maximum 25,000 | 50,000 × 10 = 500,000 on Plus | Unknown; live price not recovered |

Allowance source: https://help.activecampaign.com/hc/en-us/articles/10918125380764-About-email-send-limits-and-overage-fees (updated 2026-10-17). Starter eligibility/ceiling: https://help.activecampaign.com/hc/en-us/articles/14251311251356-Overview-of-ActiveCampaign-plans (opened version updated 2026-11-04). Price-control source: https://www.activecampaign.com/pricing.

The official comparison https://www.activecampaign.com/compare/hubspot advertises $15/$49/$79 per month at 1,000 contacts for Starter/Plus/Pro. It does not settle ordinary monthly versus annual billing. Those amounts are retained as leads, not inserted into comparable monthly totals. The pricing page rendered numerical price dashes, and https://www.activecampaign.com/pricing?billing=monthly&contacts=5000&currency=USD could not be fetched. No annual upfront amount is established, and no annual total is inferred from multiplying an ambiguous monthly display by twelve.

All four workloads fit their published monthly marketing-send allowances. Our calculated send-overage component is therefore **$0 if the matching contact tier and documented overage regime apply**. This is not a full plan price. For an otherwise identical account with exactly 2,000 additional marketing sends above its included quota, the help-page formula is `2,000 × $0.005 = $10` extra. The terms' stop-at-limit language conflicts with that help page, so the $10 example is conditional, not permission to rely on successful over-limit sending. Unused monthly sends are not treated as a transferable credit bank.

A lifecycle journey using the same contacts and four marketing emails per contact has the same send-count arithmetic, but feature gates can require Plus or Pro. A single Starter workflow is limited to five actions. A four-email sequence plus intervening waits can exceed that limit. Branching and custom app/billing events must be checked against the selected plan; an entry newsletter price is not automatically the price of the requested journey.

New accounts bill all stored statuses (https://help.activecampaign.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000337324-Do-unsubscribed-unconfirmed-or-bounced-contacts-count-against-my-contact-limit). If the audience has 1,000 subscribed contacts plus 500 unsubscribed records, the input is **1,500 stored billable contacts**, not 1,000. We have not recovered the available contact-band price for that case. Archiving can remove records from the billing count but prevents messaging and introduces restoration/retention conditions (https://help.activecampaign.com/hc/en-us/articles/24319534242076-Archive-contacts-in-ActiveCampaign).

### External paid-reader example

A possible paid-newsletter build uses external Stripe subscriptions and Pro/Enterprise recurring-payment triggers, with creator-owned signup/access rules and a list/segment for permitted readers. It is not a verified native paywall. Pro is selected here because both the current pricing and trigger docs support its recurring-payment use. Sources: https://help.activecampaign.com/hc/en-us/articles/13026562133276-Recurring-Payments-overview, https://help.activecampaign.com/hc/en-us/articles/218788707-Automation-triggers-explained, https://help.activecampaign.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016037040-Integrate-your-Stripe-account-into-ActiveCampaign.

Illustrative inputs: a US Stripe standard account; 100 successful domestic online-card recurring payments of **USD 10** each in one month; no refunds, disputes, international cards, currency conversion, tax service or other billing platform.

Our calculation:

- Gross receipts: `100 × USD 10 = USD 1,000`.
- Stripe Payments: `USD 1,000 × 2.9% + 100 × USD 0.30 = USD 59`.
- Stripe Billing pay-as-you-go, if used: `USD 1,000 × 0.7% = USD 7`.
- Known illustrated processor components: `USD 59 + USD 7 = USD 66`.
- Remainder before marketing software, any platform-specific charges and other costs: `USD 1,000 − USD 66 = USD 934`.

US card fee source: https://stripe.com/pricing; recurring fee source: https://stripe.com/billing/pricing. These are checked processor assumptions, not universal creator-country prices. An exact take-home figure is **not established** because the ordinary monthly Pro quote, any paid access/website software and any AC revenue-share arrangement were not verified. Do not silently replace the ambiguous advertised $79 with a confirmed monthly bill or assume a 0% platform share.
