## Cost examples

All amounts below are **our calculations** using current published rates. Bento displays `$` but does not say USD or AUD in the reviewed price/legal pages. Prices are before tax. The marketing examples are paid month-to-month Marketing Platform, one publication, one user, no Chat add-on, and four campaigns to each subscribed contact. The trial's manual approval and account-specific daily cap are not included in the monthly price calculation.

### Newsletter workload

- **1,000 subscribers:** 4,000 recipient emails/month. Formula: base `$29` covers up to 5,000 active users. **Result: `$29/month` displayed price.**
- **5,000 subscribers:** 20,000 recipient emails/month. Formula: base `$29`. **Result: `$29/month` displayed price.**
- **10,000 subscribers:** 40,000 recipient emails/month. Formula: `$29 + (10,000 - 5,000) × $0.01`. **Result: `$79/month` displayed price.**
- **50,000 subscribers:** 200,000 recipient emails/month. Formula: `$29 + (50,000 - 5,000) × $0.01`. **Result: `$479/month` displayed price.**

Paid marketing sends are advertised as unlimited after trial and warmup, subject to fair use and anti-abuse review. The price formula uses the first per-user tier after 5,000; scenarios stop at 50,000, before the lower next-tier rate applies.

### App-generated transactional email workload

These scenarios use Transactional Email by itself, one recipient per message, volume spread across the month, no attachments, no Marketing Platform charge, and no tax. The published price is 100 free emails/month, then a `$5` minimum covering up to 12,500 emails, then `$0.09` for each additional 1,000.

- **3,000 emails/month:** Formula: `$5` minimum within the 12,500-email tier. **Result: `$5/month` displayed price.**
- **10,000 emails/month:** Formula: `$5` minimum within the 12,500-email tier. **Result: `$5/month` displayed price.**
- **50,000 emails/month:** Formula: `$5 + ((50,000 - 12,500) ÷ 1,000 × $0.09)`. **Result: `$8.375/month` calculated.**
- **100,000 emails/month:** Formula: `$5 + ((100,000 - 12,500) ÷ 1,000 × $0.09)`. **Result: `$12.875/month` calculated.**

The service advertises up to 50,000 API emails/hour/site, so monthly totals do not describe burst capacity. Emails accepted into the queue are not a delivery receipt. Separate F27 rows record batch, hourly, review, daily-cap, and SMTP limits.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Currency:** Pricing shows `$` without a currency code. The pricing page, Subscription Agreement, and Contact page were checked; none identifies the billing currency. Cost examples keep the symbol as displayed and do not convert it.
- **Active-user billing scope:** Current pricing defines active users as subscribed users or people with an event in the last 30 days. Older Help/FAQ text instead describes unique contacts and excludes duplicates/unsubscribed records; the old billing article counts contacts across Sites. Current pricing does not clarify those site/duplicate edge cases. Ask Bento before modelling multi-Site accounts.
- **Dedicated IP eligibility:** Current pool guidance says above 500,000 emails/day may be suitable; FAQ says 100,000+ emails/month. The public docs do not reconcile these thresholds or publish a price, so this row is `conflicting`.
- **Refund wording:** The Docs FAQ says refunds are reviewed case-by-case, while the later Subscription Agreement says subscription, setup, and usage fees are generally non-refundable except where law requires. The Agreement says it controls commercial terms, so the paid-plan summary follows it.
- **Unavailable newsroom:** `https://bentonow.com/newsroom` did not load in the browser. The current `/posts` blog, product/pricing pages, contact page, and legal pages were checked for activity and acquisition/shutdown news.
- **Status widget:** The official status page loaded, but uptime widgets did not return the figures. The page is recorded as available; no uptime percentage is inferred.
- **Unpublished items:** Country eligibility, interface language options, annual price, branding removal terms, specific log retention, customer MFA, paid-newsletter payouts, and independent inbox-placement results were not found on the cited official pages.
