## Cost examples: our calculations

These are fixed-plan examples using the explicitly documented Basic 10,000 plan at $15/month and $1.80 per additional 1,000 emails. They are **not verified lowest current prices** at larger volumes. The pricing selector's higher-volume offers could not be checked. Do not use these examples as Postmark's optimized price curve or rank them against another vendor's cheapest tier.

Evidence for every formula and price below: https://postmarkapp.com/support/article/1107-how-does-monthly-pricing-work (checked 2026-11-04; updated 2025-10-29). The default Basic 10k price and rate agree with https://postmarkapp.com/pricing (checked 2026-11-04).

- F23 combines three different retry systems: sending email to recipient servers, outbound event callbacks, and inbound email JSON callbacks. Keep separate schedules and failure conditions.
- F28 region fields need more than a corporate address or an SMTP routing region. Exact log/backup geography remains uncertain.
- F37 asks for independent evidence while this task permits official sources only as proof. No external deliverability score used; accepting a third-party experiment needs a separate evidence rule.
