# CampaignLark

Lifecycle and relationship to Maileroo (checked 2026-11-09): CampaignLark is Maileroo Group Pty Ltd's current email-marketing product. The company spun the earlier Maileroo marketing tools into it; Maileroo itself was not renamed and remains focused on SMTP and Email API. CampaignLark's own About page says its infrastructure was isolated. Whether it shares any Maileroo delivery vendor, IP pool, or SMTP backend is not published.

Newsletter policy (checked 2026-11-09): permission-based newsletters and marketing are allowed when recipients gave verifiable consent. Cold outreach and bought, rented, scraped, or other third-party lists are prohibited. A working unsubscribe is required.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Sending relationship:** The Jan 2026 launch post and About page say CampaignLark is a separate, isolated marketing product under the same operator. The legal DPA describes the operator's overall email API, SMTP relay, validation, marketing, and analytics services together. None of these pages says whether CampaignLark shares any Maileroo IP pool or delivery backend. Exact technical sharing remains unclear.
- **Data location:** Terms say EU processing and storage; Privacy Policy identifies Germany and also allows service providers in other countries. The DPA lists sub-processors in several countries and provides transfer clauses. The sources do not give a single complete location map for each data type.
- **Retention:** Terms allow email logs and analytics up to 90 days after cancellation. The DPA says email content and logs up to 14 days, analytics up to 2 years or as configured, and backups up to 30 days. The scopes are not reconciled in the public text.
- **Volume pricing:** Terms establish USD and tax exclusion, and the static pricing view exposes $15/month at 5,000 emails. Higher-volume selector prices did not appear in the accessible official page text. No amount was estimated.
