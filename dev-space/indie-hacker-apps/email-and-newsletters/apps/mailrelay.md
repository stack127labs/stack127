## Cost examples — our calculation

These are euro prices before VAT. No exchange-rate conversion, paid-reader income, SMS, WhatsApp, dedicated IP or expert services are included. One user/publication, one verified domain and shared sending IPs are assumed. Ordinary business/list content must be eligible for shared IPs under the anti-spam policy.

An email consumes one credit only if the full encoded message, including headers and attachments, is at most 100 kB. The formula is:

```text
credits per recipient = ceiling(encoded message size in kB / 100)
monthly credits = recipient messages × credits per recipient

newsletter recipient messages = subscribed contacts × 4

no-commitment subscription price
  = fixed-term monthly tier price × 1.15
```

For example, 250 kB × one recipient = 3 credits. A 50,000-contact newsletter sent four times is 200,000 credits only under the <=100 kB assumption. Do not model it as 200,000 credits if every message consumes two or three.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Cancellation:** pricing FAQ says one month before fixed-term non-renewal, while both language versions of the contract require three months. The contract says Spanish prevails on interpretation. No-commitment plans have a separate ten-calendar-day rule. Sources: https://mailrelay.com/en/pricing/, https://mailrelay.com/en/terms-of-use/, https://mailrelay.com/es/condiciones-de-uso/.
- **Cap versus overage:** pricing, billing and contract clause 2.5 describe stopping sends at the monthly credit/recipient ceiling, but clause 16.6 prices additional sends at €0.01 and mentions blocking on request. Read the Spanish wording too; no definitive default charge-versus-block reconciliation was found. Sources: https://mailrelay.com/en/pricing/, https://help.mailrelay.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000160773-billing, https://mailrelay.com/en/terms-of-use/, https://mailrelay.com/es/condiciones-de-uso/.
- **Prepaid deadlines:** exhaustion without renewal and expiration of a not-yet-fully-used pack are described with different ten-day/30-day provisions. Validity is twelve months, but the practical extension/cancellation window needs confirmation for the actual pack.
