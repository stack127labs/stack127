## Cost examples — our calculation

These are **our calculations**, not tax-inclusive checkout quotes or measured server benchmarks. No introductory discounts, AWS free allowances, paid-reader revenue, SMS, ads or optional paid support are included. Currencies are kept separately; no EUR/USD conversion or combined currency total is made.

Inputs: one publication, one user, unlimited stored contacts under the published plans, one verified sender domain, **Send with Keila** managed delivery, four campaigns per month and no other sends. Formula: `E = unique subscribed contacts × 4`; choose the cheapest listed monthly plan with `included emails >= E`. No extra-credit price is published, so this model selects a sufficient tier rather than inventing an overage rate.
