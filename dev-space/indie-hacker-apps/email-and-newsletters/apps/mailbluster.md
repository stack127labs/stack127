## Cost examples — our calculation

All figures below are **our calculation**, using ordinary current USD rates, one brand/publication, one user and existing authenticated domain. No coupon, affiliate reward, charity discount, free AWS credit or promotional sending allowance is used. All sources were checked on 2026-11-05. Prices are before any applicable tax; the conflicting tax statements are recorded separately.

### Newsletter baseline: ordinary monthly Managed plan

Workload = 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 unique subscribed contacts × four campaigns each month. That gives 4,000 / 20,000 / 40,000 / 200,000 recipient emails. No SMS, ads, paid-reader income or attachments. Lead storage is not priced by contact count.

Sources: [current pricing](https://mailbluster.com/pricing), its [public calculator rate data](https://mailbluster.com/wp-content/themes/mailbluster/dist/scripts/pricing_50e7eb38.js), [Managed plan help](https://mailbluster.com/help/managed-smtp), [Managed launch FAQ](https://mailbluster.com/blog/mailbluster-smtp) and [daily capacity help](https://mailbluster.com/helps/mailbluster-daily-sending-capacity).

Formula: monthly fee = selected pack base + max(0, monthly recipient emails − included pack emails) ÷ 1,000 × that pack's overage rate. These workloads use whole thousands, so no unknown fractional-thousand rounding affects the result.

| Subscribers | Recipient-email calculation | Monthly pack | Included emails | Overage calculation | Our monthly base result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 | Managed 10,000 | 10,000 | 0 | USD 17 |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | Managed 20,000 | 20,000 | 0 | USD 29 |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | Managed 40,000 | 40,000 | 0 | USD 49 |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 4 = 200,000 | Managed 150,000 | 150,000 | (200,000 − 150,000) ÷ 1,000 × USD 1.27 = USD 63.50 | USD 165 + USD 63.50 = **USD 228.50** |

The 200,000-send calculation intentionally uses normal overage. Pricing/launch documentation says the campaign continues above quota. We compared all 14 packs in the current public calculator: the 150,000 pack plus overage has the lowest base total at that workload. A 250,000 pack with no overage costs USD 272/month instead. This is our choice under the published rules, not a vendor promise that its own calculator will recommend the cheapest overage combination.

Managed includes the delivery infrastructure. No BYO annual subscription or additional SES invoice is added. Paid Managed has no separate daily cap according to help, so a 50,000-recipient campaign is not disqualified solely by a daily volume cap. Actual sending speed, reputation pauses, approval and content limits still matter.

For annual Managed entry, the 10,000 pack is USD 15.30/month equivalent, with a full annual base of 12 × USD 15.30 = **USD 183.60**. The 10% yearly discount applies to the base pack, not overage. This longer commitment is kept separate from monthly comparisons.

### Annual-commitment BYO alternative with Amazon SES

This is a second cost model, not an ordinary monthly Pro subscription. [Pro](https://mailbluster.com/pricing) requires **USD 60 per year per brand upfront**, plus USD 0.60 per 1,000 emails. The named external sender is **Amazon SES Essentials**, one region, approved production access, shared IP, no attachments, no optional validation/dedicated IP/global endpoint or other add-ons. [AWS's own rate](https://aws.amazon.com/ses/pricing/) is USD 0.16/1,000 for the first 10 million emails under Essentials, with no fixed monthly Essentials fee.

Our monthly equivalent for the known subscription/sending components:
USD 60 ÷ 12 + recipient emails ÷ 1,000 × (USD 0.60 MailBluster + USD 0.16 SES).
