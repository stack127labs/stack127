## Cost examples: our calculations

Checked on 2026-11-05. Ordinary EUR monthly prices before VAT, no annual or introductory discount. ### Newsletters

Inputs: one publication, one user, one list of unique subscribed contacts, four campaigns/month, no SMS, advertising or paid-reader revenue. Each recipient email consumes one credit. Email-volume plans permit unlimited imported subscribers, so subscriber-priced plans are optional for this workload. No workflow-run charge or additional sender subscription is assumed.

| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | 20,000 emails included | EUR 31 + EUR 0 overage | EUR 31/month |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | 40,000 emails included | EUR 58 + EUR 0 overage | EUR 58/month |

These are selected plans that cover the entire workload, not claims that every possible overage combination or promotion has been optimized. They allow unlimited sends but do not enable SMTP. One contact appearing in several lists can count several times on that model.

A paid-newsletter variant cannot be priced as an exact native bundle: a recurring reader checkout, paywall, processor fees and payment migration were not established. Any external membership/payment layer would add its own costs and integration work. No zero-fee payout assumption is made.

### App-generated email

Inputs: one verified sender domain, one user, one recipient per message, shared sending infrastructure, no attachments, no concurrent marketing campaigns, no audience automation flows. SMTP does not require importing subscribers; contact-profile and workflow-run counts are therefore not pricing inputs for this direct-send example. Support must enable the eligible paid account. Monthly volume is spread over a 30-day comparison month; an average is not a daily/burst quota.

### What is included, excluded and unresolved

Our selected recurring total is `Basic email-volume subscription + documented extra recipient-email charges`. Hosted sending is included. There is no separately priced customer server, WordPress license or SES account. VAT, domain registration/mailbox, optional SMS, paid address verification, custom design work and higher support/IP tiers are excluded. Their absence from the baseline does not mean they are universally free. No exact tax-inclusive total can be given without billing location.

Prepaid is a different purchase model. Public packages are EUR 60/5,000, EUR 104/10,000, EUR 288/50,000 and EUR 407/100,000 sends, each paid once, expiring after two years. These are not monthly subscription prices or recurring allowances.

Annual full upfront cost remains unresolved. Pricing says one annual invoice with 20% savings, but the 20,000-email Basic display shows EUR 24/month equivalent while EUR 31 × 80% = EUR 24.80. Multiplying these alternatives by 12 would yield EUR 288 or EUR 297.60 respectively; **these are our diagnostic calculations, neither is a verified annual charge**. USD annual data also contains EUR symbols. Keep monthly EUR examples as the comparable baseline and confirm a written annual/settlement quote before publishing an annual total.
