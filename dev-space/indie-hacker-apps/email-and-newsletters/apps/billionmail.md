## Cost examples: our calculation

The software is free under its published AGPLv3 licence. There is no monthly/annual paid software tier in the inspected self-hosted offer. This does not buy infrastructure or an external sending allowance. ### Named setup and assumptions

Our illustrative outbound setup uses a **DigitalOcean Basic Regular Droplet, 2 GiB RAM, one vCPU, 50 GiB SSD and 2,000 GiB transfer, USD 12/month**, running Linux and BillionMail's Docker stack. The published install minimum is one core, 1 GB RAM and 20 GB disk. This server is our cost assumption, not a tested capacity recommendation for 50,000 contacts.

Hosting/source requirements: https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing/droplets ; https://www.billionmail.com/start/preparation.html ; https://github.com/Billionmail/BillionMail/blob/dev/docker-compose.yml . Checked 2026-11-05; Compose modified 2026-04-20, other source update dates not captured.

**This host must not be modelled as a working direct-delivery server.** DigitalOcean documents blocking SMTP 25, 465 and 587. Our outbound-only example instead assumes configuring the supported external SES SMTP relay on **STARTTLS port 2587**, which AWS documents. Port 2587 is not one of the ports in DigitalOcean's published block list, but that is not proof of a successful connection. Actual egress, relay-port/TLS configuration and provider permission must be verified before deployment. No server/account was created, connected or tested. Inbound Internet mail hosting is outside this example.

Sources: https://docs.digitalocean.com/support/why-is-smtp-blocked/ (last verified 2026-07-13); https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/smtp-connect.html ; https://www.billionmail.com/start/smtp.html . External sender: **Amazon SES Essentials**, shared sending infrastructure, one recipient/message, no attachments, 32 KB/message interpreted as 0.000032 GB, no promotional credits and no dedicated-IP/VDM extras. Choose its published **USD 0.16/1,000 outbound recipients + USD 0.12/GB**. AWS has other purchasing modes; this explicitly selected plan is not claimed to be the lowest possible SES tariff.

Source: https://aws.amazon.com/ses/pricing/ . Checked 2026-11-05; source update date not stated.

- Sender USD/month = (M ÷ 1,000 × 0.16) + (M × 0.000032 × 0.12).
- Known infrastructure USD/month = 12 + sender; software licence component = 0.
- Annual known infrastructure = 12 × monthly result, assuming the same workload each month. This is not annual prepayment or an annual discount.
- Total all-in cost remains unknown: domain, independent backups, extra storage/transfer/services, tax, operational messages, AI providers and labour are excluded.
- Optional professional deployment is also excluded from both baselines.
