# Shopify Messaging

Official URL: https://www.shopify.com/email-marketing
Current app listing: https://apps.shopify.com/shopify-messaging
Status in `apps.md`: `active`, site confirmed 2026-11-04. Row type: Marketing; commerce add-on. Market note: Not established. The store-ecosystem requirement is correct. Its current channel description is incomplete: official docs/app listing now also include WhatsApp alongside email and SMS.

Shutdown, sunset and acquisition check: **Shopify Email was renamed Shopify Messaging.** A December 10, 2025 changelog announcement describes the former name and introduction of SMS. Current docs and the app listing also include WhatsApp. No email-product shutdown, sunset or separate acquisition notice was found in the inspected initial notice searches, product, changelog, setup, app and terms pages. This is a continuing product with expanded channels. Sources: https://changelog.shopify.com/posts/shopify-messaging-now-supports-sms-marketing ; https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/shopify-messaging ; https://apps.shopify.com/shopify-messaging ; https://www.shopify.com/email-marketing . Checked 2026-11-05.

Store requirement: **A Shopify store is required; this is not standalone email service.** Production sending needs an active paid store, the Online Store channel and a payment provider. Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus are explicitly supported. Free trial and Pause and Build can draft/test but cannot send to subscribers. Shopify Network Intelligence must also remain enabled. Sources: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/shopify-messaging/requirements ; https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/shopify-messaging ; https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/privacy-and-security/privacy/customer-privacy-settings/privacy-settings . Checked 2026-11-05.

Cost distinction: Messaging itself is free to install and has no fixed monthly email subscription. Its email allowance is 10,000 free sends per calendar month; additional email starts at USD 1 per 1,000. **The Shopify store subscription is still payable separately.** Store-plan fees, email usage, safe-send capacity and SMS/WhatsApp charges are recorded separately below. Sources: https://apps.shopify.com/shopify-messaging ; https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/shopify-messaging/email/pricing ; https://www.shopify.com//pricing ; https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/shopify-messaging/email/deliverability . Inputs: one Shopify store/publication, one owner, unique opted-in contacts, four marketing broadcasts per subscriber per calendar month, no other chargeable marketing automations and no SMS/WhatsApp. No paid-reader revenue is included.
