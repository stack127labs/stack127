## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Backup deletion conflict:** the Terms, last updated Oct 11, 2026, say terminated account data is permanently deleted with no backups retained. The Privacy policy and DPA, both last updated Feb 18, 2026, say deleted data can remain in backups for up to 30 days. The DPA says it takes precedence over the Terms for data-processing conflicts, but the public wording still differs. I kept the row as conflicting.
- **Hosted versus self-hosted scope:** Plunk pricing and billing rules apply to hosted use. Self-hosters pay their own providers. The Terms' anti-spam rules apply to both, while the Privacy policy and DPA cover hosted processing only.
- **Price currency:** Plunk and AWS show $ without an ISO currency code on the checked price pages. No currency conversion was made.
- **Newsletter scenarios:** hosted estimates use the paid usage plan after upgrade, with no free-credit offset. Self-host scenarios use new-account AWS SES Essentials in us-east-1; they are not a full infrastructure bill.
- **Entry approval:** public pricing advertises self-service start, but whether hosted accounts undergo a manual sender review is not settled by public pricing/terms. I did not access signup.
- **Data exit:** Terms and Privacy describe access/JSON portability, while the DPA says Plunk does not provide a termination export and customers should retrieve data through API before deletion.
