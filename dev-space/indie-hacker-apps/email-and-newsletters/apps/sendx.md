## Cost examples — our calculation

Dollar signs below follow SendX's display; no currency conversion was made. These are ordinary pre-tax prices, not introductory discounts. A currency ISO code was not established in the retrieved price text. The current Core table, rather than the older one-plan help table, supplies the marketing prices. Sources: [current pricing](https://www.sendx.io/pricing), [older billing guide](https://support.sendx.io/en/articles/13559152-know-your-subscription-plan-and-billing). Checked 2026-11-05.

### Newsletter baseline

Inputs: one publication, one user, an existing authenticated sending domain, a fixed list of unique subscribed contacts and four campaigns to every contact each month. No SMS, paid-reader revenue, ads, dedicated IP, extra specialist services or separately metered hygiene/testing usage. Sending and hosting are included. A domain or mailbox bought separately and location-dependent tax remain outside these totals.

Formula: recipient emails/month = subscribed contacts × 4. Monthly cost = current Core contact-tier fee because marketing sends are unlimited.

| Subscribed contacts | Formula for recipient emails/month | Core monthly fee | Total/month, our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 | $10 | $10 |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | $40 | $40 |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | $60 | $60 |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 4 = 200,000 | $230 | $230 |

Annual comparison, our calculation: monthly list price × 0.75 × 12 paid upfront. The stated 25% reduction gives:

| Contacts | Annual formula | Annual upfront amount | Exact monthly equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | $10 × 0.75 × 12 | $90 | $7.50 |

| 10,000 | $60 × 0.75 × 12 | $540 | $45 |

The all-subscribed baseline avoids the contact-status dispute; it does not settle bills for a database containing unsubscribes or bounces. A native paid-reader checkout/access system was not established, so a paid-newsletter variant cannot be priced responsibly.

These gaps remain unknown, not negative feature claims.

Terms body says July 31, 2026 while metadata says December 8, 2025; privacy body says January 1, 2025 while metadata says July 1, 2025. Feature FAQs say June 2026 despite later page metadata.
