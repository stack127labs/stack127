### Annual-price uncertainty

At 3,000 contacts, the selector displays Basic USD 17/month and Unlimited USD 35/month under annual billing. Its public implementation calculates `round(monthly price × 0.9)`.

Our calculation from those rounded displays:

- Basic: USD 17 × 12 = USD 204.
- Unlimited: USD 35 × 12 = USD 420.

Our separate calculation from the FAQ's stated exact 10% annual saving:

- Basic: USD 19 × 12 × 0.90 = USD 205.20.
- Unlimited: USD 39 × 12 × 0.90 = USD 421.20.

The two methods differ by USD 1.20 per year for each plan. No actual annual invoice total is published in the inspected price/help/terms pages. Neither result is presented as a verified charge, and the selector's rounding is disclosed rather than silently applied. The ordinary monthly comparisons above avoid this ambiguity.
