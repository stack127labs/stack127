## Cost examples: our calculation

Checked 2026-11-05. All examples use ordinary published pricing, with no promotion, SMS, ads, extra traffic or paid-reader revenue in the basic workload. Dollar signs stay as published; the subscription's ISO currency code and applicable taxes were not established. These are software-price comparisons before any tax, domain purchase or optional external service, rather than exact final invoices.
