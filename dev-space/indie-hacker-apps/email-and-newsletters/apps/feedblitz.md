## Cost examples: our calculations

Newsletter inputs: one publication, one user, a stable average of 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 unique active subscribers, four full-list campaigns/month, no attachments, ads, SMS, extra users or paid-reader revenue. FeedBlitz supplies hosting and sending. Before applicable tax; domain registration/mailbox, external checkout and optional service contracts excluded. Checked 2026-11-04.

Formula: `monthly cost = published Core subscriber-band fee + $0 marketing-send overage`. The zero volume surcharge follows the unlimited-send offer, subject to permission and list-quality enforcement. Band source: https://support.feedblitz.com/support/solutions/articles/43000682112-feedblitz-pricing-tiers; allowance source: https://www.feedblitz.com/pricing/. The complete band table was modified **2023-01-27**. Its entry monthly values agree with the current pricing page; larger subscriber inputs and annual checkout totals were not independently exercised.
