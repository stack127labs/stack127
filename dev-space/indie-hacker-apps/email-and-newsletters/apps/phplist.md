## Cost examples — our calculation

All inputs were checked on 2026-11-05. One publication, one user and one existing sending domain; no SMS, paid-reader revenue, ads, attachments or dedicated IP. Hosted prices are **VAT-inclusive**, as published. A common pre-tax figure cannot be derived without a country/tax assumption; none is invented.

### Hosted weekly newsletter — our calculation

Inputs: 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 unique subscribed profiles. Four campaigns to everyone each month.

Formula: recipient sends = subscribers × 4. For the ordinary tariff, choose the cheaper sufficient subscriber/message tier with **USD**, **No discount** and **Plus off**. The subscriber plan shows 1,000,000 messages/month. Public calculator data and page controls were read directly; no form, account or checkout was submitted.

| Subscribers | Recipient sends/month | Ordinary Standard, monthly | Ordinary Plus, monthly | Standard annual equivalent and full prepayment, our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 4 = 4,000 | US$10 | US$12.50 | US$9/month × 12 = US$108 |
| 5,000 | 5,000 × 4 = 20,000 | US$25 | US$31.25 | US$22.50/month × 12 = US$270 |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 4 = 40,000 | US$45 | US$56.25 | US$40.50/month × 12 = US$486 |
| 50,000 | 50,000 × 4 = 200,000 | US$175 | US$218.75 | US$157.50/month × 12 = US$1,890 |

Source: [calculator's published price arrays](https://www.phplist.com/js/pricingjs.php), [pricing and plan caps](https://www.phplist.com/pricing) and [payment/allowance rules](https://www.phplist.com/knowledgebase/credits-limits-and-discounts/). Plus is the variant for custom public/tracking domain and branding removal, not native paid-reader checkout. Annual totals above multiply the displayed annual monthly equivalents; they are our calculations, not inspected invoices. Longer prepayment does not pool monthly quotas.

The current **US$1/month** offer fits 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 sends, with Plus included. The advertised cap is 3,000 subscribers/9,000 messages, so it cannot serve all four scenarios. No expiry appears on current pricing, but a [2018 announcement](https://www.phplist.com/blog/segmentation-release/) says all-feature access is time-limited. It is shown separately from the ordinary tariff because its continuing price/feature term is not established. The Free for Open Source program could cover all four volumes for an approved qualifying project; it is not a general free-price baseline.

The four campaigns are sent on separate days. Hosted review, queue batches and domain throttles can delay completion; fitting a monthly allowance does not guarantee a campaign finishes inside a chosen window. See [Send Speed](https://www.phplist.com/knowledgebase/send-speed/). No overage, top-up or loyalty discount is assumed.

### Self-hosted newsletter — our calculation

Named setup: free phpList community software, Apache/PHP and MySQL or MariaDB together on a DigitalOcean Basic regular 2 GiB/1 vCPU Droplet. Its monthly server price is **US$12**, including 50 GiB SSD and 2,000 GiB transfer. Use Amazon SES **Essentials** in one region, a shared sending IP, production-approved sending identity/quotas and authenticated SMTP with STARTTLS on port **2587**.

Sources: [phpList requirements](https://resources.phplist.com/system/start), [sending configuration](https://resources.phplist.com/system/sending), [TLS configuration option](https://resources.phplist.com/system/config/constants), [Droplet tariff](https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing/droplets), [DigitalOcean SMTP block](https://docs.digitalocean.com/support/why-is-smtp-blocked/) and [SES permitted TLS ports](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/smtp-connect.html). DigitalOcean blocks 25, 465 and 587; selecting one of those would make this example unsuitable. Port 2587 is documented by SES, but no live networking or delivery test was performed.

Sender inputs: **US$0.16 per 1,000 emails** plus **US$0.12 per GB** outgoing mail data. Assume each complete outgoing message is **32 KB = 0.000032 GB**, using the decimal-GB convention in AWS's own example. No AWS free credits, validation, dedicated IP or optional services are applied. The page also offers à-la-carte pricing; this example deliberately uses Essentials. [SES pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/ses/pricing/).

Formula: known monthly subtotal = US$0 software + US$12 server + (N ÷ 1,000 × US$0.16) + (N × 0.000032 GB × US$0.12/GB).

| 1,000 | 4,000 | US$0.64 | US$0.01536 | **US$12.65536/month** |

These retain the calculated precision; an actual invoice may round differently. **They are component subtotals, not exact all-in totals.** Extra costs are unknown for domain purchase/renewal, a bounce mailbox/complaint integration, backups, monitoring, support, tax and operator work.
