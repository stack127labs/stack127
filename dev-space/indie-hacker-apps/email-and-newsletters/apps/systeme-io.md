## Field problems

- F06/F09: Unique subscribed contacts are a scenario assumption. Billing treatment of other stored statuses remains unknown.
- F07/F08: “Free forever” is qualified by inactivity, contact/feature limits and lifetime sales. Annual totals are derived from two months free, not an inspected checkout.
- F10/F21/F22: Rule count, configured workflow count, executions and API requests must remain separate. A campaign sender cannot be priced as a generic relay solely from unlimited email advertising.
- F11: Paid excess can trigger an automatic price increase. Free restriction can stop sales, newsletters and course access; recovery does not replay every missed schedule.
- F13–F16: Checkout and paid tags are useful building blocks, but payment and access migration are separate from list migration. Processor fees cannot be treated as zero.
- F18/F24: Website domains, authenticated sender domains, tracking links and email footer branding are different controls.
- F19/F27: Newsletter attachments are linked files. This does not establish MIME attachments or message-size limits.
- F23: Business-event webhooks are not a complete email-delivery event feed. Retry durability does not guarantee exactly-once processing.
- F28/F30: Privacy policy as controller is not a complete subscriber-data DPA. Each deletion/retention period needs its own data-type scope.
