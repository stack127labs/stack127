## Cost examples — our calculation

Checked 2026-11-05. Inputs: [public pricing](https://betterlytics.io/pricing), [billing/event definitions](https://betterlytics.io/docs/pricing), [public volume/currency table](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/betterlytics/betterlytics/main/dashboard/src/lib/billing/plans.ts) and the [pricing component importing that table](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/betterlytics/betterlytics/main/dashboard/src/components/pricing/PricingComponent.tsx). The initial live page shows free Growth and USD 7/month Professional at 10,000 events, matching the source. Higher bands and EUR amounts come from the named public source values, not a submitted calculator or checkout. Verify the payable quote before purchase.

Formula:

`billable events = pageviews + custom events + enabled outbound-click events + enabled Web Vitals measurements + enabled client-error events`

`monthly subscription = price of chosen feature tier at the smallest published event band covering that workload`

`currency amount = published currency-specific cents ÷ 100`

USD and EUR are distinct tariffs. No exchange rate is applied. Billing is monthly only; tax, if applicable, is not determined here. No payment-processing fee for Betterlytics' own customer billing is added because no separate customer surcharge was established.

**Web traffic:** one site, one user, pageviews only. Optional outbound, Web Vitals and error collection disabled; no custom events. Replay is not included in these examples because its full billing/quota treatment is not established.

| Monthly pageviews = billable events under these assumptions | Growth | Professional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10,000 | USD 0 / EUR 0 per month | USD 7 / EUR 6 per month |
| 100,000 | USD 14 / EUR 12 per month | USD 21 / EUR 18 per month |
| 1,000,000 | USD 59 / EUR 51 per month | USD 89 / EUR 77 per month |

Example: 100,000 pageviews plus 100,000 custom events gives `100,000 + 100,000 = 200,000 events/month`. The published source tariff gives Growth USD 25/EUR 22 or Professional USD 38/EUR 33 per month. This is a separate workload, not a claim every pageview creates one custom event.

**Product-style total event workloads:** active-user count is unspecified and is not inferred from events.

| Total billable events/month | Growth | Professional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100,000 | USD 14 / EUR 12 | USD 21 / EUR 18 |
| 1,000,000 | USD 59 / EUR 51 | USD 89 / EUR 77 |
| 10,000,000 | USD 169 / EUR 147 | USD 254 / EUR 221 |
| More than 10,000,000 | Contact sales | Contact sales |

These totals must already include all applicable billable event categories. They do not establish logged-in identity or retention-cohort support.

**Replay workloads:** 1,000, 10,000 and 100,000 recorded sessions/month cannot be priced exactly from the public event tariff. The [replay guide](https://betterlytics.io/docs/integration/session-replay) gives 5% default eligible-session capture, 15s minimum, 600s inactivity cutoff, 1,200s maximum and 60-day hosted retention. It does not establish complete recorded-session/storage allowances or a recording-to-billable-event formula. Recorded-session count is an independent input; no session/pageview ratio is assumed. Raising capture to 100% does not prove entitlement to unlimited storage.

**Native MAU and MRR:** no native SDK/MAU tariff or native subscription-revenue meter is established. A web purchase event with a price property is not an MRR accounting system.

**Self-hosting:** `software license price (0) + server/disks + backup storage + optional object storage/mail/services + operator upkeep`. The [setup guide](https://betterlytics.io/docs/installation/self-hosting) supplies 2GB RAM/few-GB-disk starting requirements but no provider bill at these workloads. An exact all-in cost cannot be calculated without sizing, event/replay volume and selected providers. It is not listed as free managed hosting.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Cancellation is materially inconsistent:** [terms](https://betterlytics.io/terms), [privacy](https://betterlytics.io/privacy) and [DPA](https://betterlytics.io/dpa) say subscription data is retained one month, inaccessible after cancellation takes effect, then deleted. The [cancellation](https://betterlytics.io/docs/pricing/cancellation) and [subscription-management](https://betterlytics.io/docs/pricing/managing-subscription) guides instead say a downgrade to free Growth retains accessible history under 1+ year limits. The guide also says one remaining dashboard, while current [pricing/source capabilities](https://betterlytics.io/pricing) / [capabilities](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/betterlytics/betterlytics/main/dashboard/src/lib/billing/capabilities.ts) say Growth allows two. Do not assume the more generous outcome.
- **Immediate deletion has a backup exception:** the [privacy policy](https://betterlytics.io/privacy) says all personal and analytics data is deleted immediately with “No exceptions.” [Terms section 5.3.2](https://betterlytics.io/terms) explicitly permits encrypted backup copies for up to 30 days. The file preserves both rather than promising every copy disappears at account deletion.
- **Query data needs a narrower claim:** [privacy](https://betterlytics.io/privacy) describes paths without query parameters. The [dynamic URL guide](https://betterlytics.io/docs/integration/dynamic-urls) says query parameters, including UTM, are preserved during normalization. These may refer to different processing stages, but the checked docs do not establish complete query-string redaction throughout the pipeline. Sensitive data can also enter custom properties and error messages; the terms acknowledge that risk.
- **Open-source scope:** [home](https://betterlytics.io/) says every line is open source under AGPL-3.0. The [repository](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/betterlytics/betterlytics/main/README.md) and [license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/betterlytics/betterlytics/main/LICENSE) establish public application licensing, while [terms section 10.5](https://betterlytics.io/terms) exclude hosted/infrastructure/service components outside those repositories. Do not publish a blanket promise about every deployed hosted component.
- **Core privacy versus replay:** no-banner/no-browser-storage language on [privacy/about](https://betterlytics.io/privacy) / [about](https://betterlytics.io/about) concerns default anonymous analytics. [Replay integration](https://betterlytics.io/docs/integration/session-replay) explicitly requires enabled capture plus consent and sampling, and says sampling decisions are temporarily cached client-side. Its exact cache mechanism/expiry was not established. Optional features and accidental PII are covered separately in legal pages; a blanket no-consent claim is inappropriate.
- **Residency scope:** hosted analytics/replay providers are described as EU-based, but the [processor list](https://betterlytics.io/subprocessors) explicitly puts Stripe subscription billing in the USA under SCCs. The broad all-processing-EU wording should not become a promise about every account/payment operation. A complete backup-region map and cloud region selector were not found.
- **Current source pricing:** exact higher-volume USD/EUR rates are public in [plans.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/betterlytics/betterlytics/main/dashboard/src/lib/billing/plans.ts), imported by the [pricing UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/betterlytics/betterlytics/main/dashboard/src/components/pricing/PricingComponent.tsx). Initial-page values match the 10K band. No purchase or live calculator interaction was used to validate all other bands. Negative source sentinel values at 10M+ mean contact sales, not a negative or zero price.
- **Retention scope:** marketed 1+/3+/5+ years are separate from source ceilings of 365/1095/1825 days and the dashboard's default 365-day retention setting. Hosted replay is 60 days. For self-hosted recordings, [setup docs](https://betterlytics.io/docs/installation/self-hosting) distinguish ClickHouse cleanup from S3 bucket lifecycle. Do not carry the cloud or ClickHouse expiry rule into every bucket.
- **Self-host parity and external dependencies:** the current [installation overview](https://betterlytics.io/docs/installation) says Cloud features arrive first and images update periodically; current setup includes replay/uptime/status pages. Older indexed `dev.betterlytics.io` pages still describe earlier limits and were not used as the current product record. Self-host setup can involve MaxMind, mail providers, DuckDuckGo favicon requests and opt-in telemetry. The [privacy policy](https://betterlytics.io/privacy) separately governs enabled instance telemetry.
- **Overage actions:** [pricing](https://betterlytics.io/pricing) warns excess data can be deleted after more than 30 days over quota; [terms](https://betterlytics.io/terms) describe contact after two consecutive over-limit months and no extra charge for occasional spikes. These are different actions, not a guaranteed zero-loss grace period or a metered overage tariff.
- **Unknowns searched:** current pricing and all billing guides, FAQ, dashboard/metric/MCP docs, integration/replay/WordPress/self-host docs, legal pages and targeted official searches were checked for country eligibility, legal entity registration, event pooling, raw exports/API limits, historical migration, native SDKs, identity merge, user-retention cohorts, replay quotas and erasure-by-ID. Missing mentions remain unknown.
- **Lifecycle:** shutdown/sunset/acquisition searches led to current official product, changelog and repository pages; no applicable notice was found. This is a source-search result, not a promise of indefinite operation.
- **Method limits:** no account, form, checkout, SDK, deployment, browser tracker, customer-site inspection, crawl, API query or env-file read was performed. One malformed orchestration call was corrected before it ran. All source values are dated and no runtime performance/privacy result is claimed.
