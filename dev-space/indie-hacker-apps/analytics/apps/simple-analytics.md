## Cost examples: our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-04: [public traffic/seat prices and plan data](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/pricing), [pricing selector and annual formula](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/_nuxt/C9q3g3HM.js), [pageview/event data-point definition](https://docs.simpleanalytics.com/api/export-data-points), [VAT and contract conditions](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/general-terms-and-conditions).

The text fetch showed EUR; a direct read of the same public page supplied USD currency state and plan data. These examples use that **USD** response, without converting currencies. The page's referenced calculator exposes 100k, 250k, 500k, 1m and 2.5m volumes and multiplies monthly prices by 10 for annual charges. It also contains unused lower/upper bands. The 20k/USD 15 band is not a current selector option and is not presented as a purchasable entry price.

Assumptions: one site, one included user, stable monthly usage, no extra seats, no optional pings or other add-ons, no discount, before VAT. This avoids averaging a sudden spike over a different prior workload. Exact totals involving operations with unpublished billing treatment cannot be promised.
