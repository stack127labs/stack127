## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs: one eligible new Pro account, one app, one dashboard user, core analytics only, no paid add-ons or negotiated discount. Amounts are USD. Assume the stated MTR belongs to the applicable measured rolling/billing window, with no refunds, adjustments or customer purchase-tax ambiguity. Any tax on Adapty's service fee and separate store/payment-processing costs are excluded.

The current published meter includes tracked subscriptions, renewals and one-time purchases **before the platform cut**. Before paid activation, Adapty checks a rolling 30-day window; crossing $5K begins paid monthly billing. The published paid formula applies 1% to the tracked amount, rather than only the part above a deductible allowance. Sources checked 2026-11-05: https://adapty.io/pricing/ ; https://adapty.io/blog/adapty-new-pricing-2026/ ; https://adapty.io/terms/.

| Inputs | Formula | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $1,000 MTR; account has never crossed the activation threshold | Below the free-offer threshold | Our calculation: $0 core fee |
| $10,000 MTR in the charged period | $10,000 × 0.01 | Our calculation: $100 core fee |
| $50,000 MTR in the charged period | $50,000 × 0.01 | Our calculation: $500 core fee |

These examples avoid exactly $5,000 because the public wording does not settle that boundary. The $0 example also avoids a previously activated paid account, whose automatic return-to-free rule was not established. They are core fee calculations, not verified checkout invoices.

**Required MRR workloads:** exact fees at $1,000, $10,000 and $50,000 monthly recurring revenue cannot be determined from MRR alone. Annual prepayments, renewal timing, one-time purchases and the billing window change tracked revenue. If all subscriptions bill monthly inside the measured window, no other transactions occur, and tracked revenue equals those MRR amounts, the conditional core results are $0/$100/$500; the $0 result still assumes paid billing has never been activated.

For example, 100 active $120/year subscriptions normalize to $1,000 MRR: 100 × ($120 ÷ 12). If all 100 payments are tracked inside a chargeable period, that period has $12,000 tracked revenue and a calculated core fee of $12,000 × 1% = **$120**. This is our illustrative calculation, not a claim that every $1,000-MRR app pays $120. MRR definition source: https://adapty.io/docs/analytics-charts; fee sources: https://adapty.io/pricing/ and https://adapty.io/terms/, all checked 2026-11-05.

**Add-ons remain separate:** pricing advertises Refund Saver at 0.2% of monthly revenue, Ads Manager at 3.5% of monthly ad spend, and Attribution by custom price after its free allowance. These are different meters/scopes. No add-on is assumed enabled, and the all-in total cannot be exact without selections, usage, contract and tax inputs. Source checked 2026-11-05: https://adapty.io/pricing/.

No exact annual total or discount is claimed. Contract terms normally bill monthly unless an order form says otherwise. Enterprise and legacy pricing require their own agreement. No currency conversion was performed.
