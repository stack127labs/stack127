## Cost examples — our calculation

Amounts are **USD**, excluding applicable taxes. Checked 2026-11-05. Inputs: [current pricing/catalog](https://amplitude.com/pricing), [Plus offer](https://amplitude.com/plus), [Plus Addendum](https://amplitude.com/plus-plan-terms) and the [official calculator rendering code](https://amplitude.com/_next/static/immutable/chunks/3j5ih_9ds-qw9.js).

The pricing page embeds `plus_v4` records with `quotaType: event-volume`. The renderer explicitly filters to those records, computes monthly price as `monthlyAmount / 100`, and annual monthly equivalent as `yearlyAmount / 100 / 12`. It does **not** present these as incremental event-rate bands. The same embedded data also includes MTU records and add-on amounts; those are not treated as current visible self-serve choices without an applicable offer/entitlement.

All scenarios use one organization/project, one website/app and one dashboard user, with no paid add-ons unless stated. They are capacity/price examples, not checkout or invoice tests.

### Website traffic

Assume only the stated pageviews are counted as analytics events; zero extra sessions, forms, downloads, clicks, replay markers, backend events or other activity. Normal browser defaults capture additional event types, so this is an explicit mathematical workload rather than a promise about a default site.

| Monthly pageviews | Counted events | Our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10,000 | 10,000 | 10,000 ≤ 2,000,000 Free allowance → **$0/month** |
| 100,000 | 100,000 | 100,000 ≤ 2,000,000 → **$0/month** |
| 1,000,000 | 1,000,000 | 1,000,000 ≤ 2,000,000 → **$0/month** |

These use the **current event-based Free offer**. The billing FAQ's 10,000-MTU description is separately conflicting and is not silently imposed on this scenario. Free feature gates and one-year data access still apply.

### Product analytics events

Inputs are **total monthly events**, including automatic/custom events in the workload. User counts are independent and not inferred.

| Events/month | Selected capacity and formula — our calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100,000 | Within current 2m Free allowance | **$0/month** |
| 1,000,000 | Within current 2m Free allowance | **$0/month** |
| 10,000,000 | Plus 10m event tier: `72000 / 100` monthly | **$720/month** |
| 10,000,000, annual term | Same tier: `691200 / 100` for year; `$6,912 / 12` monthly equivalent | **$6,912/year**, equivalent to **$576/month** with a 12-month subscription |

The 10m tier is a sufficient published base plan, not proof of the cheapest possible overage/contract strategy. No group-analysis package, longer replay retention, extra replay volume or negotiated discount is included.

Cross-check at the first paid published volume: 3m events uses `9400 / 100 = $94/month`, or `90000 / 100 = $900/year`, equivalent to $75/month. The free 2m tier uses $0. Do not assume a constant annual discount across all tiers or subtract the free 2m again from a fixed tier price.

Plus overage contracts use 1.0× the purchased plan's per-unit rate and allow suspension above 200% of purchased capacity. The public sources do not supply a universal marginal rate after the **$0** starting tier. We do not infer that overspending at this tier is free, nor price a 10m workload by intentionally exceeding the 2m starting capacity.

### Recorded sessions

Assume the stated number of **eligible captured sessions**, each 10 minutes, with a 100% sampling rate for the chosen eligible traffic. Ten minutes is our scenario, not a vendor default or average. Web/native replay support exists, but the public table does not provide separate native per-recording rates.

| Recorded sessions/month | Capacity conclusion — our calculation | Exact total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 ≤ current Free/Plus 10,000 replay allowance | **$0/month on Free**, only if analytics events and all other use also stay within Free limits |
| 10,000 | Exactly current Free/Plus 10,000 replay allowance | **$0/month on Free**, with the same other-usage assumption |
| 100,000 | Exceeds Growth 20k and Enterprise 50k included sessions; additional capacity needed | **Quote required** for base plan and replay add-on |

No fake recorded-session-to-event/user ratio is used. Default replay `sampleRate` is zero until configured; sample code setting it to one is an explicit capture choice. Once quota is reached, replay capture stops until reset, so a quota cannot be ignored when assuming 100% capture.

Default raw replay retention is 30 days from ingestion. Additional-volume/retention packages differ by browser/RN versus native/Flutter path. Bookmarks and comments can extend actual replay retention. Heatmaps require a paid plan/entitlement and replay-derived coverage; they are not included in the Free scenario merely because the app captures recordings.

### Native mobile usage — explicit event scenario

The current visible pricing meter is events, not MAU. Assume **20 total analytics events/user/month**, including any automatically captured lifecycle/session events counted in this scenario. No replay, extra package or legacy MTU contract is assumed.

| MAU | Formula — our calculation | Current event-based price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × 20 = 20,000 events | Within 2m Free → **$0/month** |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × 20 = 200,000 events | Within 2m Free → **$0/month** |
| 100,000 | 100,000 × 20 = 2,000,000 events | Exactly 2m Free → **$0/month** |

Twenty events is an explicit assumption, not a conversion ratio supplied by Amplitude. Real capture can exceed it. Existing MTU-based agreements have their own purchased user volume and average 1,000-events-per-MTU guardrail, so these event-offer examples must not be applied to those contracts.

### Revenue analytics

The **$1,000, $10,000 and $50,000 MRR** scenarios cannot yield an exact bill. Current prices meter events (or some existing contracts MTUs), not MRR or a percentage of tracked revenue. Neither MRR nor gross sales determines how many events your site/app captures.

Known structure: required base analytics tier + any Accounts/replay/other add-ons + contractual overages + tax. Event-based purchase capture can fit in a free allowance, but **Revenue LTV specifically requires Growth/Enterprise**, which is quote-priced. We cannot price that gated chart at $0 merely because a small number of purchases is captured. General revenue events, charts and custom recurring-revenue formulas are also not automatically a reconciled subscription-finance ledger.
