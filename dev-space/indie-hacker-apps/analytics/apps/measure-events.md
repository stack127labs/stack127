## Cost examples — our calculation

Checked 2026-11-05. Inputs: one website/site record, one account owner, monthly billing and no additional sites. The first three workloads are pageviews only; the last three are independent path-based server-event workloads. Active users, events and pageviews are not converted into each other.

Published inputs: $29/month covers 40 sites; each additional site costs $0.50/month. Changes are prorated. Current tariff sources: https://measure.events/pricing; https://measure.events/terms. The vendor explicitly says there are no event tiers and traffic spikes do not change the fee: https://measure.events/blog/measure-vs-posthog-2026. Server paths and bulk capture are documented at https://measure.events/blog/analytics-for-remix-apps-2026; https://measure.events/blog/how-to-monitor-mcp-server-traffic.

Formula for full monthly periods: subscription(S) = $29 + $0.50 × max(0, S − 40), where S is the number of billable sites. This is our calculation from the site tariff, not a traffic-to-sites ratio. Proration for partial periods is not calculated because the exact billing dates and day-count rule are not supplied.

For S = 1: $29 + $0.50 × max(0, 1 − 40) = $29/month.
