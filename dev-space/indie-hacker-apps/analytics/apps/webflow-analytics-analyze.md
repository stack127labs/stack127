## Cost examples — our calculation

Checked 2026-11-05. Inputs: [current public pricing](https://webflow.com/pricing), including the actual Analyze session-option HTML attributes, and [add-on billing rules](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/33961239129619-Manage-add-ons). Prices below are USD before tax. One site, one owner using the included full seat, annual billing, no extra seats, CMS, localization, Optimize, domain registration or third-party consent-tool costs. Basic is used for a simple site that does not need CMS. A paid Workspace upgrade is not assumed.

The annual-equivalent formula is:

`monthly equivalent = USD 15 Basic Site plan + Analyze annual-price monthly equivalent for the chosen session tier`

`annual commitment = monthly equivalent × 12`

The add-on follows the Site plan's billing cycle. These are annual commitments expressed per month, not monthly payments.

An existing customer who already pays for a suitable Site plan would treat the Analyze amount alone as incremental cost. That does not make hosting free for a new customer. The Enterprise pricing card's “1 million+ page views/year” is a separate published contract description, not a per-month session conversion.

Product-event, recorded-session, native-MAU and MRR workloads are not priced here: the documented core meter is website sessions. A custom conversion event, purchase value or AI prompt quota does not establish a tariff for those other workloads.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Ecommerce eligibility:** [Manage add-ons](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/33961239129619-Manage-add-ons) explicitly excludes Ecommerce and Starter Site plans. The [pricing page](https://webflow.com/pricing) puts “Available with a paid Site plan” in its Ecommerce Analyze comparison. No public checkout was opened to resolve this. A Basic/Premium site is the documented ordinary route; do not promise Analyze on Ecommerce from the comparison alone.
- **AEO gate:** the [product FAQ](https://webflow.com/feature/analyze) uses both Team/Enterprise wording and an Enterprise-only answer. The [dedicated AEO overview](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/51704299506195-AEO-analytics-overview) explicitly says Team or Enterprise Platform, with Analyze and Workspace AI enabled. Keep the conflict visible rather than promise a specific Team entitlement. That overview also says prompt limits vary by contract; it is not an unlimited self-service feature.
- **Earlier 5,000-session offer:** the [June 25, 2025 update](https://webflow.com/updates/analyze-optimize-new-pricing-tiers-and-usage-updates) announced a 5,000-session tier at USD 19/month yearly or USD 25/month monthly. The current [pricing selector](https://webflow.com/pricing) instead lists 2,000, 10,000, 25,000, 50,000, 100,000, 250,000 and 500,000. The historical announcement is retained as context, not sold as a current option; continued legacy-account eligibility was not established.
- **Pageviews and time windows:** [traffic-source help](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/48824608583187-About-Analyze-traffic-sources-sessions-and-page-views) describes the UI as session/visitor reporting. The [current API guide](https://developers.webflow.com/data/docs/working-with-analyze) supports pageview metrics. The UI's 90-day selection limit, API's 100-day request limit and audience behavior's 180-day retention describe different things. None establishes a universal deletion deadline.
- **Anonymous defaults versus optional identification:** the [intro FAQ](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/34200153798163-Intro-to-Webflow-Analyze) describes anonymous, non-PII visitor reporting. [Off-site goals](https://developers.webflow.com/browser/custom-goals/off-site-conversions) and [setUserId()](https://developers.webflow.com/browser/custom-goals/off-site-conversions/setUserId) explicitly link known IDs, while [Enterprise audiences](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/47281920598035-Analyze-audiences) document reverse-IP firmographics. Those opt-in/edition-specific workflows prevent extending the default anonymity claim to every configuration.
- **Unknown commercial and technical details:** searched product/pricing FAQs, purchase and cancellation help, usage policy, report API, global policy, DPA and privacy FAQs for trial terms, hard spend caps, a full residency/backup map, full retention/erasure schedules, raw-IP and query/form treatment, storage-key lifetimes, historical imports and export fees. The corresponding rows state exactly what remains unestablished. No missing mention is treated as a negative capability.
- **Access failures:** direct HTTP reads of two Help Center articles returned 403. Their official pages were subsequently readable through web retrieval. These recovered reads are not counted as failed evidence. Some combined tool outputs were too long; the relevant sources were then retrieved in smaller groups. No account-only documentation or form submission was used.
- **Lifecycle:** searches for Analyze shutdown, sunset, discontinued and acquisition notices led to current official sales/help pages and product updates. No applicable retirement or product-transfer notice was found. Hosted customer pages and uploaded third-party documents on Webflow's CDN were not treated as Webflow corporate notices.
