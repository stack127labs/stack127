## Field problems

- F08/F12/F13/F14: simultaneous users, pageviews, website count and seats are different units. A monthly pageview scenario cannot determine a Pro plan, and multi-site concurrency pooling is unexplained.
- F07/F09: general Content licensing and permitted website widget use have different wording. Do not reduce this to a simple open-source or unrestricted-commercial-use badge.
- F17: Heatspy click/scroll maps are not session replay. Geography maps are also different from on-page interaction maps.
- F21/F22/F23/F24: vendor NPII terminology, ad-free status and opt-out do not settle legal consent, partner processing, storage location or erasure.
- F25/F26: raw data before aggregation, visible history windows and exported history are different scopes. The API's availability does not establish complete migration portability or a fixed all-in API price.
- F32: current default snippets use waust.at, while older documentation uses whos.amung.us image widgets. Keep current and legacy rules separate. Browser-only detection can miss API-based use, and an exposed widget does not identify Pro or Heatspy activation.
