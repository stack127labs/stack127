## Cost examples

**Our calculation.** Checked 2026-11-04. Inputs: the [Standard no-charge clause](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/terms/us/) and [Firebase Analytics price](https://firebase.google.com/pricing). Assume one Standard property, one site or app, one account user, no Analytics 360, no BigQuery export, and no separately charged Firebase or Cloud services. Zero is the Analytics service component, not an estimate of the builder's other costs. No currency conversion is involved.

| Job | Monthly input | Formula | Result: Analytics service component |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Website traffic | 10,000 pageviews | Standard no-charge fee | 0 |
| Website traffic | 100,000 pageviews | Standard no-charge fee | 0 |
| Website traffic | 1,000,000 pageviews | Standard no-charge fee | 0 |
| Product events | 100,000 event occurrences | Standard no-charge fee | 0 |
| Product events | 1,000,000 event occurrences | Standard no-charge fee | 0 |
| Product events | 10,000,000 event occurrences | Standard no-charge fee | 0 |

| Native app | 10,000 monthly active users | No-charge Analytics component; no per-user tariff | 0 |
| Native app | 100,000 monthly active users | No-charge Analytics component; no per-user tariff | 0 |
| Separate mixed-event scenario | 100,000 pageviews plus 20,000 custom events | 100,000 + 20,000 = 120,000 specified occurrences; Standard fee | 0 |

The mixed-event count excludes any additional automatically collected events. No event-to-user conversion was assumed. A zero fee does not remove collection, reporting or history limits. Monthly totals do not establish whether a daily export limit is met.

**Our calculation, Analytics 360:** `quote_required`. Formula = contractual base + applicable billable-event bands above the included volume. The structure is documented, but neither the base nor rates can be priced exactly from the public [360 billing guide](https://support.google.com/marketingplatform/answer/9013858?hl=en), checked 2026-11-04. Even a workload below the included event volume has an unknown contractual base charge.

**Our calculation, separate streaming component:** assume exactly 1 GB of exported streaming data. Formula = 1 GB × $0.05/GB = **$0.05**, in the source's dollar notation. The [export guide](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9358801?hl=en), checked 2026-11-04, also requires applicable storage and processing charges. This is not an exact total Cloud bill; storage duration, query bytes, location, free allowances and contractual/tax conditions have not been specified. Do not convert pageviews or events to GB using an approximate ratio.

Replay workloads of 1,000, 10,000 or 100,000 recorded sessions are `not_comparable`: a native GA4 replay product and recording tariff were not established in the [feature page](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/features/) or [Explore guide](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7579450?hl=en), checked 2026-11-04. Revenue workloads of $1,000, $10,000 or $50,000 MRR are also `not_comparable`: the [monetization definition](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/13409465?hl=en), checked 2026-11-04, does not establish a subscription-ledger tariff. These are not zero-cost estimates for products Google has not documented here.

## Conflicts, qualifications and unknowns

No unresolved pair of contradictory sources was found. These differences need their scope preserved:

- **“500 events” is not 500 occurrences.** The [Firebase overview](https://firebase.google.com/docs/analytics) uses a broad event-count description; the [collection limits](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9267744?hl=en) define distinct event names per app user and distinguish web streams. Checked 2026-11-04.
- **Free Standard, contracted 360 and Cloud export have separate charges.** The [US agreement](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/terms/us/) contains a legacy UA allowance beside the GA4 no-charge clause. The [360 billing guide](https://support.google.com/marketingplatform/answer/9013858?hl=en) supplies its own meter. Checked 2026-11-04. No legacy monthly hit tariff was transferred to GA4.
- **The 360 sample default and maximum differ.** The [storage/display guide](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/13888627?hl=en) describes default behavior; the [edition comparison](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11202874?hl=en) gives the larger Explore ceiling. Checked 2026-11-04.
- **Identity association has a time boundary.** The [User-ID guide](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9213390?hl=en) illustrates same-session pre-login association, while earlier pre-implementation data is not reprocessed. Checked 2026-11-04. Neither unlimited historical stitching nor “no anonymous association” is supported.
- **Event import and metadata import differ.** [Event CSV import](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10325025) ingests recent events; [custom metadata import](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/15086891) enriches queries. [UA history migration](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/14196937?hl=en) is explicitly unavailable. Checked 2026-11-04.
- **Late-arrival clocks differ.** [Native processing](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11198161?hl=en), [Measurement Protocol timestamps](https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/protocol/ga4/sending-events) and [warehouse late updates](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7029846?hl=en) describe different stages. Checked 2026-11-04. None establishes indefinite offline acceptance.
- **Store purchases need platform-specific setup.** [Android](https://firebase.google.com/docs/analytics/android/measure-in-app-purchases) and [Apple](https://firebase.google.com/docs/analytics/ios/measure-in-app-purchases) guides distinguish automatic and manual collection. Checked 2026-11-04. Automatic collection does not deduplicate every manually logged purchase.
- **Old Firebase terms are archived.** The [archive notice](https://firebase.google.com/terms/analytics) and [Firebase privacy explanation](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), checked 2026-11-04, prevent treating the old GA4F contract as the current universal agreement.

## Field problems

- **F08–F13:** distinguish a free service fee from linked warehouse costs. Event names, event occurrences, billable events, bytes and API tokens need separate units. Monthly usage cannot prove daily quota compliance.
- **F14:** Analytics accounts, properties and streams differ from Firebase projects and Cloud billing accounts. Access roles do not establish an unlimited seat allowance.
- **F17:** the schema should distinguish website heatmaps from table-cell heat coloring. Event paths and scroll events do not establish replay or its masking defaults.
- **F18/F21/F22:** one family record needs separate platform/version/consent entries. Device reset, denied storage, cookieless signals and server-side erasure are different operations.
- **F19:** subscription purchase revenue is not enough to classify a product as an MRR ledger. A price based on MRR cannot be derived from these event metrics.
- **F23/F25:** collection, processing, backups and warehouse copies need separate location/history records. Aggregate reporting and event-level retention must stay separate.
- **F27/F28:** recent raw-event import, query-time enrichment and historical migration differ. Sampling, privacy thresholds, cardinality aggregation, bot exclusion and session approximation need separate rows.
