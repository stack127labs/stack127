- New-customer status: the homepage advertises Basic signup and a 30-day Pro trial with no card, but the linked /signup route failed to fetch. The marketing offer is current; the working registration flow was not confirmed.

- Billing period: homepage rates are monthly and the FAQ says users can cancel any time; Terms also refer to annual subscriptions and yearly upgrades. No current annual rate is published.
- Data sources: Stripe and Paddle are direct integrations. Other processor names are examples for manual/API data, not documented native connectors.
- Freshness: an MRR blog article says the dashboard can be checked in real time; API docs give a more specific metric recalculation cadence of every few hours, with manual refresh available.
- Privacy: terms say data is not encrypted at rest. They specify immediate account closure/removal at cancellation and full deletion from backups within 180 days. Public pages do not say where servers are located or how long active account data is retained.
- API: public docs show JSON CRUD endpoints and pagination up to 100 records, but do not publish an API rate limit or a downloadable CSV/export option.
- Acquisition/shutdown: official blog/product/legal checks found no notice. The Privacy Policy’s hypothetical acquisition/business-closure clause is boilerplate rather than an announcement.
