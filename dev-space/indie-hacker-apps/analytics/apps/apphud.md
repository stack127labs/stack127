## Cost examples: our calculation

Inputs: current live pricing https://apphud.com/pricing; invoice currency/organization scope https://docs.apphud.com/docs/plan-and-billing; MRR definition https://docs.apphud.com/docs/mrr. Checked 2026-11-06. Amounts are USD, one organization, no add-ons or credits. Let T be actual monthly tracked gross revenue under the vendor's meter.

- Pro: if T < $1,000, $0; otherwise max($49, T × 0.0099) monthly.
- Expert: if T < $1,000, $0; otherwise max($59, T × 0.0119) monthly.
- Free: core features and basic reports up to $10,000 MTR. It does not include Pro cohorts or Expert export/API features.
- Enterprise: quote required.

| Explicit MTR input | Free | Pro: our calculation | Expert: our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $500/month | $0 within allowance | $0, below-$1K waiver | $0, below-$1K waiver |
| $1,000/month | $0 within allowance | max($49, $9.90) = $49/month | max($59, $11.90) = $59/month |
| $5,000/month | $0 within allowance | max($49, $49.50) = $49.50/month | max($59, $59.50) = $59.50/month |
| $10,000/month | $0 at stated up-to limit | max($49, $99) = $99/month | max($59, $119) = $119/month |
| $50,000/month | Above allowance; upgrade needed after grace | max($49, $495) = $495/month | max($59, $595) = $595/month |

The percentage applies to tracked revenue with a minimum, not an assumed subtraction of the Free allowance. At exactly $1,000 the below-$1K waiver no longer applies. Current live cards are the basis; older indexed fixed-plus-included-MTR examples are not used. Source checked 2026-11-06: https://apphud.com/pricing.

The field list asks for $1,000/$10,000/$50,000 **MRR**. Those amounts alone cannot produce exact invoices: Apphud MRR is proceeds after commission/VAT and normalizes subscription periods, while MTR is gross tracked purchases and includes one-time purchases. Sources checked 2026-11-06: https://apphud.com/pricing ; https://docs.apphud.com/docs/mrr.

| Required input | Exact cost from MRR alone | Explicit conditional example: our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $1,000 MRR | Not comparable without gross MTR | If actual MTR is separately known to be $1,000, Pro $49 / Expert $59 monthly |
| $10,000 MRR | Not comparable without gross MTR | If actual MTR is separately known to be $10,000, Pro $99 / Expert $119 monthly |
| $50,000 MRR | Not comparable without gross MTR | If actual MTR is separately known to be $50,000, Pro $495 / Expert $595 monthly |

These are conditional MTR inputs, not a conversion from net MRR. No event/active-user-to-revenue ratio is assumed. Annual prepayments, taxes, commission, refunds, source mix and transaction timing can alter the relationship.

Optional refund recovery adds 7% of Won Back Value. Example, **our calculation**: $50,000 MTR on Pro plus explicitly $1,000 of declined refund requests classified as won back gives $495 + ($1,000 × 0.07) = $565 before any additional taxes/fees. The $1,000 saved value is a separate input, not total app revenue. Sources checked 2026-11-06: https://apphud.com/pricing ; https://docs.apphud.com/docs/win-back-refunds-add-on.

A full combined total is not exact without invoice tax treatment, contractual billing adjustments, Flows charges and external store/payment-provider costs. Billing docs describe per-organization periods; do not assume the calendar-month period of another vendor. Sources checked 2026-11-06: https://docs.apphud.com/docs/plan-and-billing ; https://apphud.com/legal/terms ; https://apphud.com/pricing.
