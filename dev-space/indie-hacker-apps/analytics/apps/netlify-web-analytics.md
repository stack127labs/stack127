- **Current base prices:** The public [pricing page](https://www.netlify.com/pricing/) contains Personal $9/$12/$15 and Pro $20/$25 monthly variants. Its embedded price-selection logic uses named variants and cached account/team assignment, with a dashboard fallback for unresolved signed-in pricing. No experiment override, account lookup or login was used. Current live billing docs defer monetary tier prices to that page; older search-index extracts still show fixed prices and were not substituted for the live page.
- **Age:** The [subscription agreement](https://www.netlify.com/pdf/self-serve-subscription-agreement.pdf) permits access from 13 subject to local law. The [privacy statement](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) says Netlify does not knowingly provide the service under 16 and closes such accounts. [Website terms](https://www.netlify.com/legal/terms-of-use/) use 13 but explicitly distinguish the website from the service agreement. A clear service-age reconciliation was not found.
- **Deploy scope:** The [RUM comparison](https://docs.netlify.com/manage/monitoring/real-user-monitoring/) labels Web Analytics production-only, while the [production checklist](https://docs.netlify.com/resources/checklists/production-checklist/) asks customers to distinguish deployed branches to monitor production only. Netlify's [privacy statement](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) describes wider account/website data and cookies; those must not be assigned wholesale to visitor Analytics capture.
