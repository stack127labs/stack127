## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Lifecycle scope:** Searches for shutdown, sunset, acquisition, acquired, discontinued and closing down led to current official pages and the plan-change announcement. The free tier's retirement and old-script migration were checked directly. Neither establishes that the hosted product is closing.
- **Grandfathered prices:** The [February announcement](https://tinylytics.app/changelog/unlimited-hits-forever-on-every-paid-plan) promises existing prices locked forever, and [billing](https://tinylytics.app/docs/account/billing.md) says classic arrangements stand. [General terms](https://tinylytics.app/docs/terms.md) also permit price changes with at least 30 days' email notice. No explicit grandfathered-contract exception resolving these statements was found. Current plans are priced separately; older account prices are not guessed.
- **Infrastructure IP logs:** [Technical privacy docs](https://tinylytics.app/docs/trust/privacy.md) distinguish seven-day application logs from size-rotated proxy access logs that contain IPs. The [formal privacy policy](https://tinylytics.app/docs/privacy.md) says server logs contain no client IPs except circumvention of Cloudflare. These may refer to different layers, but exact proxy logging scope and expiry remain unclear. “No IP stored on a hit” does not mean an IP never reaches infrastructure.
- **Approximate identity:** [Unique-hit docs](https://tinylytics.app/docs/analytics/unique-hits.md) describe daily estimates and a salt changing twice daily. The hash is not a stable person ID; the same visitor may split across browsers/rotations and shared network/browser combinations may merge people. [API](https://tinylytics.app/docs/api.md) optionally accepts caller-supplied visitor_id. Durable identity merging, retention cohorts and cross-device/account reports were not established.
- **European hosting has exceptions:** [Hosting docs](https://tinylytics.app/docs/trust/hosted-in-europe.md) place the app and individual hits in Germany, while the [privacy policy](https://tinylytics.app/docs/privacy.md) describes AI-derived counts/text leaving Europe. Ultra's automatic dashboard summary has no switch. Insights' setup box is ticked by default; Ask starts on but waits for a question. This is not evidence that all data stays in the EU or every AI feature is opt-in.
- **DPA and backups:** Read [formal privacy](https://tinylytics.app/docs/privacy.md), [terms](https://tinylytics.app/docs/terms.md), [technical visitor handling](https://tinylytics.app/docs/trust/privacy.md), [European hosting](https://tinylytics.app/docs/trust/hosted-in-europe.md) and the [Trust/docs index](https://tinylytics.app/docs); also searched the official site for DPA/data processing agreement. No public DPA, formal complete processor registry, selectable region or backup location/purge schedule was established. The named processors above remain useful partial evidence.
- **Trial end versus deletion:** [Account setup](https://tinylytics.app/docs/getting-started/account.md) and [billing](https://tinylytics.app/docs/account/billing.md) say choose a plan after 14 days. [Terms](https://tinylytics.app/docs/terms.md) discuss inactive-account notifications, seven-day removal for unconfirmed accounts and legacy free locks. Those are different cases; none supplies a universal unpurchased-trial collection/deletion deadline. Explicit account deletion has its own 14-day restoration period.
- **Payment details:** Checked homepage pricing, billing, formal terms and payment-processing policy for ISO currency code, tax treatment, recurring refunds and prorating. These were not established. New payments use Stripe; older processors do not prove the current merchant's tax rules.
- **Sampling, weight and source license:** Reviewed metrics/quality/API docs for sampling, embed/custom-client docs for a current numeric script payload, and official docs/terms for a whole-product repository/license. No explicit sampling guarantee, comparable size number or server-product license was established. No script measurement or installation was performed.
