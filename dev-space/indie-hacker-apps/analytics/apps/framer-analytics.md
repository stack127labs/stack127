## Cost examples — our calculation

### Core website traffic

Inputs: One Basic site, one owner, pageviews only, no Convert, paid collaborators or other extras. Use the displayed **$10/month yearly-billing rate**, with a 50 GB monthly hosting allowance. Annual subscriptions are paid upfront. Original dollar symbols are retained: the readable quote did not establish a currency code or localized checkout country. Sources: https://www.framer.com/pricing; https://www.framer.com/help/articles/subscriptions-and-charges/; https://www.framer.com/analytics (checked 2026-11-05).

| Monthly pageviews | Inputs and formula — our calculation | Known base subtotal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10,000 | 1 Basic site × $10/month × 12 months; core Analytics included | $120/year before applicable tax and extras |
| 100,000 | 1 Basic site × $10/month × 12 months; core Analytics included | $120/year before applicable tax and extras |
| 1,000,000 | 1 Basic site × $10/month × 12 months; core Analytics included | $120/year before applicable tax and extras |

These are conditional subscription calculations. Each assumes hosting usage remains within 50 GB/month. Pageviews alone do not establish bandwidth, so the exact total cannot be guaranteed for any workload without page-transfer/hosting usage. No bytes-per-page assumption or currency conversion was made.

The Free site has a $0 site-plan price and 1 GB bandwidth. It is not priced here as a guaranteed million-pageview hosted solution. Core Analytics being included does not remove hosting constraints or paid-history differences.

### Convert event workloads

Inputs: One Pro site, one owner, no extra editors/locales/hosting/AI purchases. Displayed yearly Pro base: $30/month equivalent. Convert: $50 per 500,000 monthly events, publicly expandable to 1.5 million events on Pro. Pageviews, tracked link clicks and tracked form submissions share the documented meter. Sources: https://www.framer.com/pricing; https://www.framer.com/help/articles/how-to-run-an-a-b-test-on-your-framer-site/; https://www.framer.com/help/articles/subscriptions-and-charges/ (checked 2026-11-05).

**Our scenario assumes purchasing whole 500,000-event blocks**, with no fractional-block proration:

`monthly equivalent = $30 Pro + $50 × ceiling(total monthly billed events / 500,000)`

| Monthly event workload | Formula — our calculation | Scenario result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100,000 total billed events | $30 + $50 × 1 block | $80/month equivalent, before tax/extras |
| 1,000,000 total billed events | $30 + $50 × 2 blocks | $130/month equivalent, before tax/extras |
| 10,000,000 total billed events | Exceeds published Pro maximum of 1,500,000 | Enterprise/custom quote required; total cannot be calculated |
| 1,000,000 pageviews + 100,000 tracked link clicks + 20,000 tracked form submissions | 1,120,000 events; $30 + $50 × 3 blocks | $180/month equivalent, before tax/extras |

The final row is an explicitly chosen workload, not an assumed conversion rate. No active-user-to-event ratio was used. The public pages do not separately establish exact block rounding/proration, every custom/trigger event's billing treatment, overflow collection behavior or a hard spend cap. These scenario amounts are not asserted as exact checkout invoices.

Core pageview-only reporting does not require Convert. With Convert enabled, pageviews count toward its event meter; do not price only the conversion clicks. Site-plan commitment and add-on/usage billing can have different invoice timing.

Session replay, arbitrary native-MAU and SaaS-MRR benchmarks are not comparable: no corresponding native tariff was established. Website conversion goals are not recurring revenue, and no event/user/order/revenue conversions were invented.
