## Cost examples

Our calculation, checked 2026-11-06. This estimates Polar's transaction fees, not a separate charge for analytics. The pricing page does not bill by MRR. To make the comparison workload calculable, assume the monthly Polar order total equals the stated MRR, every successful order is USD $100, and the merchant is a new Starter organization. Ignore tax, international-card fees, payouts, disputes and refunds.

Formula: Starter transaction fee = 5% × successful Polar order total + USD $0.50 × successful order count.

| Revenue analytics workload | Assumed monthly orders | Formula | Our calculated Polar fee |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| USD $1,000 MRR equivalent | 10 orders × $100 | 5% × $1,000 + $0.50 × 10 | USD $55/month |
| USD $10,000 MRR equivalent | 100 orders × $100 | 5% × $10,000 + $0.50 × 100 | USD $550/month |
| USD $50,000 MRR equivalent | 500 orders × $100 | 5% × $50,000 + $0.50 × 500 | USD $2,750/month |

The dashboard has no separately listed license fee. These examples are not a quote and do not imply that MRR equals monthly orders. Actual fees depend on the plan, transaction amount and count, card origin, payouts, currency conversion, disputes and refunds.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Analytics price vs. merchant fees:** Analytics is described as built in, and the current price table does not list an analytics add-on. Polar still charges its plan and per-transaction fees. Cost Insights is labeled beta and has no separate price, but the pages checked do not say whether beta access is enabled on every plan.
- **Only Polar-recorded billing data:** Analytics is based on Polar orders and subscriptions. Stripe customers and subscriptions can be migrated, but old Stripe payments remain in Stripe and do not become Polar orders. No external processor connector for the native analytics dashboard was found.
- **Refund totals:** Refund controls and order refund statuses are documented, but the analytics pages do not say how revenue totals are recalculated after a partial or full refund.
- **Currency aggregation:** Polar documents the currency charged on each order and USD settlement conversion. The Metrics API says money values are returned in cents but does not state the currency used for aggregate reports.
- **Retention:** Analytics retention is not specified. The Terms' 30-day return/destroy clause and the DPA's term-plus-three-years retention language cover different data categories and do not resolve every analytics record's retention.
- **Cost Insights availability:** It is labeled beta. The pricing page identifies selected preview features as paid-plan-only but does not list Cost Insights, so its plan availability is unclear.
- **Public research limits:** I reviewed the official analytics, customers, orders, refunds, pricing, merchant fees, supported countries, API, migration, company, blog, terms, privacy, DPA, sub-processor and status pages. I found no separate newsroom page, and no signup, login, form submission, API request or crawl was performed.
- **Pages checked for missing details:** Current Pricing and Fees for plan gates, trial, card requirement, quotas and analytics price; Analytics and Metrics API for refund handling, currency, history, export, sampling and API limits; DPA, Sub-processors and Master Services Terms for data location and retention; Customer Management for redaction and identity; Migration for non-Stripe imports; company Blog and Company pages for lifecycle notices; repository and older Getting Started docs for self-hosting.

## Field problems

- F19 is a group that mixes revenue accounting, subscription health and customer unit economics. These are separate measures and need their own definitions.
- F22 consent fields are designed mainly for visitor trackers. Polar is a billing-data product, so merchant privacy obligations and customer-data processing are the useful answers; visitor-cookie controls are not applicable.
- F23 locations are listed by sub-processor and purpose. Polar does not state one universal storage country or a user-selectable region.
- F25 has an unresolved contract-scope difference between the Terms and DPA. Neither page gives a single analytics-history retention period.
- F32 detection class N reflects that dashboard use is not visible on a merchant's public site. A checkout snippet is only evidence of Polar checkout integration, not proof that the merchant uses the analytics dashboard.
