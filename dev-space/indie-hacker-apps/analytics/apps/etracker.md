## Cost examples — our calculation

All checked 2026-11-05. Inputs: [German pricing and overflow tariff](https://www.etracker.com/preise/), [billable-hit definition](https://help.etracker.com/article/hits/) and [contract payment/renewal terms](https://www.etracker.com/agb/). All figures are **EUR excluding VAT**, in the original currency. Monthly license prices are reference rates for a minimum 12-month commitment, prepaid for the service period; these are not monthly cancellation offers.

Comparison workload: one account/site, one main user, pageviews only, zero other **billable** events/redirects/ad views/consent-dialog impressions; no B2B/raw-export/consulting/collective-license add-ons. Automatic scroll measurement is expressly excluded from hit billing. With these stated inputs, one accepted pageview is one hit. The vendor's rough 1.15 x pageviews or 4.5 x sessions heuristic is not applied. Default auto-events can increase actual usage.

These choose the published quota meeting the specified volume. They are license components within quota, not a promise of the lowest bill among every possible smaller-quota/overflow combination. VAT-inclusive invoice totals cannot be exact without the buyer's tax/billing treatment.

Additional scenarios, also **our calculation**:

- **Custom events share the hit budget:** 100,000 pageviews + 100,000 billable custom interactions = 200,000 hits/month. Select the 250,000-hit tier: Basic €34.80 x 12 = **€417.60/year**; Pro €82.80 x 12 = **€993.60/year**; Enterprise €118.80 x 12 = **€1,425.60/year**, all excluding VAT and add-ons. Reference monthly license €22.80 + €5.75 = **€28.55** excluding VAT; the annual base license remains €273.60 prepaid and overflow is invoiced separately monthly. This uses whole 1,000-hit blocks and the published tariff; actual booked overflow terms control.

Whether included or additional is unresolved, so the complete export-inclusive total is **not exact**. Do not invent an activation fee or assume the advertised line settles every delivery charge.

Native app access is Enterprise, but SDK emissions/offline behavior and a fixed event-per-user ratio are not established. No conversion from monthly active users, recorded sessions or revenue is guessed.
