## Cost examples — our calculation

These are capacity calculations, not vendor quotes or checkout totals. Prices retain the vendor's displayed **$**; the subscription currency code and applicable tax were not established. No exchange-rate conversion is made. Inputs were checked 2026-11-05: [current free/Agents offer](https://humblytics.com/), [team pricing](https://humblytics.com/pricing), [monthly prices and Scale 1-million add-on example](https://humblytics.com/platform), and [detailed billing/add-on FAQ](https://docs.humblytics.com/frequently-asked-questions).

Assumptions: one website, one owner; pageview examples count only pageviews, with **zero** billable clicks, forms, custom events or purchase events. This is a mathematical workload, not a claim that a normal auto-tracked site has no extra interactions. Product examples are already-stated total billable events; active-user counts are not inferred. Monthly team costs and prepaid credits are different products.

### Revenue workloads and other jobs

The requested **$1,000, $10,000 and $50,000 MRR** examples are **not directly comparable**. MRR means normalized recurring revenue; Humblytics publishes monthly **tracked revenue** caps and payment attribution, without a matching MRR formula.
