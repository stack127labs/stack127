## Cost examples — our calculation

Checked **2026-11-05**. Inputs: [current price table](https://chartmogul.com/pricing/) and [its public ARR-slider logic](https://chartmogul.com/pricing/index.js). The slider code was read, not executed. We matched its seven ARR bands to the prices embedded in the official page.

Assumptions: one business/workspace, one billing-system connection, one operator, core subscription analytics, no partner custom-sync logic, professional services or Startup Program discount. Revenue is entirely recurring and expressed in the same dollar unit as the website's tariff. No currency conversion, historical-cash-to-MRR conversion or customer-count assumption.

**Formula:** ARR = monthly recurring revenue × 12. Price is the plan's ARR-band fee. It is not a percentage deducted from each payment.

| Monthly recurring revenue | Our ARR calculation | Eligible simple setup | Base monthly subscription | Annual option: our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $1,000 MRR | $1,000 × 12 = $12,000 ARR | Free | $0 | $0 |
| $10,000 MRR | $10,000 × 12 = $120,000 ARR | Free at the current pricing page's inclusive boundary | $0 | $0 |
| $50,000 MRR | $50,000 × 12 = $600,000 ARR | Starter; above $500,000 through $1,000,000 band | $119/month | $99/month displayed equivalent × 12 = $1,188/year |

For the $600,000 ARR workload, **Pro is $209/month**, or **$174 × 12 = $2,088/year** at the displayed annual rate. It may be needed for multiple direct billing systems, more than three members, warehouse destinations or advanced CSV exports. These are feature/source constraints, even when the revenue itself fits Starter.

At the lowest paid ARR band, Starter is $69/month or $57 × 12 = $684/year; Pro is $119/month or $99 × 12 = $1,188/year. At above $7M through $10M ARR, Starter reaches $849/month and Pro $1,439/month. Enterprise starts at $19,900/year for larger/complex setups, but that starting amount is not an exact quote for an arbitrary $10M+ business.

These are **our base-fee calculations**, using the annual per-month equivalents shown by the vendor, not checkout-tested invoices. Preserve the original `$` symbol; a subscription currency code was not explicit in the pages checked. Tax depends on the customer and order. Tier audits, proration, contractual commitments and additional services can alter the invoice. The advertised 17% saving is rounded wording; we use the displayed annual rates rather than silently applying an exact 17% reduction.

Standard listed SaaSync integrations add **$0 connector fee** according to the [official partner page](https://chartmogul.com/integrations/saasync/), checked 2026-11-05. If custom sync logic is required, that page quotes $100/month paid directly to SaaSync. Example: the $600,000 ARR Starter base plus that optional logic is **$119 + $100 = $219/month**, before tax, assuming all source limits still fit. This is our conditional calculation, not the default cost of using Paddle.

A single billing source can mean one Stripe account: the [Stripe setup guide](https://help.chartmogul.com/article/247-configuring-stripe-as-a-source), checked 2026-11-05, says each Stripe account must be added separately. Running several small products through several connected billing accounts can therefore change the needed plan. Custom/API import paths have separate source limits.

Pageview, general product-event, mobile-MAU and replay workloads cannot price the core revenue service without an actual recurring-revenue input. No event-to-revenue ratio was invented.
