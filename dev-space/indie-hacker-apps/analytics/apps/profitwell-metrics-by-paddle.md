# ProfitWell Metrics by Paddle

Lifecycle and ownership check: **Paddle announced its ProfitWell acquisition on 25 May 2022. ProfitWell Metrics is still offered under that name**, also shortened to Metrics in Paddle navigation. Current product pages advertise free standalone signup and access inside Paddle Billing. **Paddle Billing is not required**: the current site explicitly supports businesses using other billing systems, including Stripe. Sources checked 2026-11-05: https://www.paddle.com/blog/paddle-acquires-profitwell ; https://www.paddle.com/profitwell-metrics ; https://developer.paddle.com/concepts/retain/metrics/ ; https://www.paddle.com/integrations/profitwell-metrics-stripe.

Account deletion notice: Paddle is contacting some **free standalone Metrics accounts unused for over 12 months**. Those notified can prevent deletion by logging in within 30 days of the email. Deletion is permanent; the notice explicitly excludes Paddle Billing, Paddle Classic and standalone Retain accounts. This is an inactive-account cleanup, not a product-wide shutdown.
