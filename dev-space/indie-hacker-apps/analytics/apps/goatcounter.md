## Cost examples — our calculation

These are conditional calculations, not published volume tiers. Assumptions: one public website, one user, pageviews only, no voluntary donation and no extra custom events. The inputs are the [current pricing section](https://www.goatcounter.com/) and [commercial-use terms](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/terms), checked 2026-11-04. Both say hosted use is free when reasonable, allow personal sites and small-to-medium businesses, and exclude millions of pageviews per day. They publish no exact monthly threshold or purchasable overage rate.

| Monthly pageviews | Inputs and formula — our calculation | Result and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10,000 | Accepted reasonable public use × no service fee; donation assumed 0 | Hosted service fee 0 if accepted. No currency applies to a zero-fee offer. An exact capacity guarantee is not published. |
| 100,000 | Same stated condition and no service fee; donation assumed 0 | Hosted service fee 0 if accepted. No paid quota or fixed monthly eligibility threshold establishes unconditional acceptance. |
| 1,000,000 | Same stated condition and no service fee; donation assumed 0 | Hosted service fee 0 if accepted. Do not equate a monthly workload with the terms' daily exclusion or infer a guaranteed allowance. |

For a guaranteed-capacity price comparison these workloads are **not_comparable**: the service has a qualitative fair-use boundary rather than a public numeric tariff. There is no exact paid upgrade total to calculate. Custom events also consume collection resources, but no shared pageview/event quota or event-based tariff is published; no conversion between those counts is assumed.
