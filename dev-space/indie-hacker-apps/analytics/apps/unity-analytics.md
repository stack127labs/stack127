## Cost examples: our calculation

Checked 2026-11-05. Currency is US dollars. Use one organization, one project, one environment and stable player IDs. These are Analytics-service calculations before taxes, other Unity products, any applicable Editor license, customer Snowflake costs or negotiated fair-use charges.

Let M be billed Analytics MAU. Reading the published “after” thresholds as marginal bands, our calculation for M up to 5,000,000 is:

`0.00360 × min(max(M − 50,000, 0), 100,000) + 0.00315 × min(max(M − 150,000, 0), 350,000) + 0.00293 × min(max(M − 500,000, 0), 500,000) + 0.00225 × min(max(M − 1,000,000, 0), 4,000,000)`

The official pricing-change explanation also says the bill charges at each tier exceeded. Above 5,000,000 MAU requires a quote. Sources: https://docs.unity.com/en-us/analytics/pricing-and-billing/mau-based-pricing ; https://unity.com/products/gaming-services/pricing ; https://discussions.unity.com/t/faq-analytics-mau-pricing-model-update/919301 ; https://unity.com/legal/terms-of-service

## Conflicts, resolved differences and unknowns

- **Current versus legacy:** current Analytics uses `com.unity.services.analytics` or Collect REST. Legacy event forwarding ended January 31, 2024; old consumption-model prices are excluded. The staff pricing-change notice also revised the initially announced MAU rates. Current docs and the live pricing table supply the recorded rates, not early forum replies or attached historical screenshots. https://discussions.unity.com/t/unity-analytics-faqs/859414 ; https://discussions.unity.com/t/faq-analytics-mau-pricing-model-update/919301 ; https://docs.unity.com/en-us/analytics/get-started/get-started ; https://docs.unity.com/en-us/analytics/pricing-and-billing/mau-based-pricing
- **Applicable legal pages:** the Analytics overview still links an old Operate terms URL, which now opens Advertising Additional Terms. That page is not a reason to apply advertising pricing or controller assumptions to standalone Analytics. Current Unity Cloud Additional Terms expressly cover Analytics/UGS, alongside the general terms and applicable DPA. https://docs.unity.com/en-us/analytics ; https://unity.com/legal/cloud-terms-of-service ; https://unity.com/legal/terms-of-service ; https://unity.com/legal/unity-data-processing-addendum-dpa
- **Consent versions:** Unity 6.2+/SDK 6.1+ uses Developer Data consent state. The earlier path starts dormant on SDK 5+ and requires session-level activation. These are documented version differences, not contradictory defaults. Unity Ads choices remain separate. https://docs.unity.com/en-us/analytics/get-started/get-started ; https://docs.unity.com/en-us/analytics/privacy-and-consent/manage-data-privacy
- **Retention unresolved:** raw-event retention is consistently 13 months. The FAQ separately says metric/profile data remains until deletion, while the privacy overview says personal data defaults to 13 months. The checked pages do not explain exactly which user-profile information is exempt or aggregated. One conflicting row preserves this narrower issue; it does not mark the entire raw-data policy unknown. General DPA/policy purpose-based retention language does not settle this product-specific overlap. https://docs.unity.com/en-us/analytics/faq ; https://docs.unity.com/en-us/analytics/privacy-and-consent/privacy-overview ; https://unity.com/legal/unity-data-processing-addendum-dpa ; https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
- **Deletion bounds:** REST instructions say player deletion within seven days; FAQ says within 30 days. A seven-day completion is consistent with a broader 30-day upper bound. SDK integration, account cancellation and environment deletion have their own procedures and timelines. No single deletion number is applied to all copies or actions. https://docs.unity.com/en-us/analytics/rest-api/request-data-deletion-rest-api ; https://docs.unity.com/en-us/analytics/faq ; https://docs.unity.com/en-us/analytics/privacy-and-consent/manage-data-privacy ; https://docs.unity.com/en-us/analytics/game-data-deletion ; https://docs.unity.com/en-us/analytics/get-started/analytics-access
- **Roles and platforms:** old staff FAQ examples name an older Editor minimum and event-size limit. Current setup/current FAQ supply 2021.3+ and the SDK 4MB limit.
