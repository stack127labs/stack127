## Cost examples — our calculation

Checked 2026-11-05. Inputs: [Ghost pricing](https://ghost.org/pricing/), its [public price configuration](https://ghost.org/js/pricing.min.js), [billing rules](https://ghost.org/help/manage-your-subscription/) and [fair use policy](https://ghost.org/fair-use/). The pricing JavaScript was read as public source text; it was not executed. No signup, quote request or calculator interaction was performed.

Assumptions: one Ghost publication, one owner/staff user, up to 1,000 registered members in total, native web analytics enabled, ordinary publication use, no optional services, and no payment processing charges included. The registered-member count is an independent input. It is not inferred from visitors, pageviews, active users or revenue.

Publisher is the entry managed plan with advanced native web analytics. Starter's member/email reports do not make Starter the entry price for the full web suite.

`annual plan = USD 348/year`

`monthly equivalent of annual plan = USD 348 ÷ 12 = USD 29/month`

`monthly-billed plan = USD 35/month; twelve payments = USD 420`

Tax, payment processing, custom-domain renewals and optional third-party services are excluded from these calculations. Public pages did not establish a universal tax amount or inclusive/exclusive pricing rule.

| Monthly pageviews | Registered members fixed separately | Our calculated annual-plan baseline | Monthly-billed alternative |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10,000 | Up to 1,000 | USD 29/month equivalent; USD 348/year | USD 35/month |
| 100,000 | Up to 1,000 | USD 29/month equivalent; USD 348/year | USD 35/month |
| 1,000,000 | Up to 1,000 | USD 29/month equivalent; USD 348/year | USD 35/month |

Formula in each row: choose Publisher by registered-member band, then use its published subscription price. The tariff publishes no per-pageview multiplier for these rows. This does not guarantee a server performance level or exempt a publication from fair use. Do not use pageviews as the member count, or assume that one million views always means one thousand members.

For a different independently supplied audience:

| --- | --- | --- |
| 10,000 | USD 1,056/year ÷ 12 | USD 88/month equivalent; monthly-billed alternative USD 105 |

| More than 100,000 | Public selector directs to the team | Quote required |

**Revenue workloads:** USD 1,000, USD 10,000 and USD 50,000 MRR do not determine a hosting price without total registered members and required features. Ghost charges no percentage platform transaction fee on the paid plans shown, but Stripe processing charges still apply. The USD 29/month annual equivalent is conditional on the independently specified 1,000-member band, not a price guaranteed at every MRR amount.

**Self-hosting:** the formula is `Ghost/TrafficAnalytics software license cost (0) + server + mail delivery + Tinybird compute/storage/transfer + other chosen services`. An exact total cannot be calculated from pageviews alone. [Docker guidance](https://docs.ghost.org/install/docker) supplies the infrastructure/dependency route, while [Tinybird pricing](https://www.tinybird.co/pricing) includes a resource-limited free plan and usage-based paid resources. Its paid starting-price text is inconsistent ($49 versus $25), so neither is used as an exact universal backend bill. A free backend allowance is not proof all three workloads fit free resources.

Generic app-event, native-MAU and recorded-session scenarios are not priced as Ghost-native equivalents. The documented offer is publication/member analytics rather than a per-event product analytics or replay service.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Starter annual price:** the initial [pricing card](https://ghost.org/pricing/) displays USD 18/month billed yearly. The page's [active price script](https://ghost.org/js/pricing.min.js) instead pairs Starter with USD 180/year and USD 18/month. USD 180 ÷ 12 is USD 15. This discrepancy is preserved; neither value is used to price advanced native web analytics, which Starter excludes.
- **Publisher staff:** the [feature comparison](https://ghost.org/pricing/) says three staff users, while the [price configuration](https://ghost.org/js/pricing.min.js) has a Publisher staff limit of two. No billing/account inspection was performed to establish the actual enforced entitlement. One owner fits either value in our examples.
- **Business member label:** the initial card repeats a 1,000-member label, but the [comparison and public renderer](https://ghost.org/pricing/) / [configuration](https://ghost.org/js/pricing.min.js) enforce a 10,000-member Business display minimum at the same base price. The row uses that minimum, with the initial-card caveat. Higher active member bands are recorded as published.
- **Tinybird starting price:** its own [pricing page](https://www.tinybird.co/pricing) says $49/month on the Developer card and “Starting at $25/month” in the comparison. Compute, storage and transfer are additional workload considerations. This does not change Ghost(Pro)'s published hosting price.
- **Hosted plan scope:** [Tinybird integration help](https://ghost.org/integrations/tinybird/) broadly says Ghost(Pro) analytics is automatic, but the [current comparison](https://ghost.org/pricing/) and [6.0 launch](https://ghost.org/changelog/6/) put advanced native web analytics on Publisher and higher. Starter still has member/email metrics. The broad setup wording is not treated as a free advanced-analytics entitlement.
- **Privacy scope:** [native web help](https://ghost.org/help/native-analytics/) says no persistent browser identification and daily unique counting; it also explicitly records logged-in member IDs. [Source code](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TryGhost/TrafficAnalytics/main/src/services/user-signature/UserSignatureService.ts) uses IP and user agent to form a daily signature, and [raw processing code](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TryGhost/TrafficAnalytics/main/src/transformations/page-hit-transformations.ts) carries those values before enrichment. The public evidence does not justify “no IP is processed,” “all activity is anonymous,” or “all raw IP is immediately deleted.”
- **EU storage versus all processing:** [native help](https://ghost.org/help/native-analytics/) says managed analytics storage is in EU regions. [Privacy](https://ghost.org/privacy/) describes Amsterdam servers and third-party transfers; the [DPA](https://ghost.org/dpa/) permits safeguarded processing elsewhere. A first-party collection URL does not remove Tinybird or other backend processing. A full current subprocessor inventory is offered only on request in the DPA; none was requested.
- **Export scope and older instructions:** [current export help](https://ghost.org/help/exports/) describes the all-data ZIP, including media on Ghost(Pro), while [billing/cancellation help](https://ghost.org/help/manage-your-subscription/) still refers to older Labs exports and requesting complete files from support. Use the specific current export guide for the workflow; neither establishes that the ZIP contains all raw web analytics. [Post analytics](https://ghost.org/help/post-analytics/) documents a separate last-1,000-post CSV.
- **Trial unknowns:** checked pricing, hosting billing help, terms and official trial searches. The 14-day duration and no automatic charge at expiry are explicit; exact trial card/analytics quota terms remain unestablished. The separate [reader-trial guide](https://ghost.org/help/free-trials/) concerns a publisher's subscribers and cannot answer the publisher-hosting trial.
- **Other unknowns searched:** native/post analytics, member management, cookie/disable controls, legal policies, Docker setup, official traffic-service source and export help were checked for active retention by data type, analytics erasure by visitor, full raw-event exports/API limits, query/form redaction, DNT/GPC and headless parity. Unknowns are kept in their rows; absence of a mention is not proof of absence.
- **Retrieval limits:** an obsolete `ghost.org/docs/install/docker/` route and attempted Admin API routes returned redirect/fetch errors; the current Docker documentation was read successfully. Two candidate source paths returned 404; the official TrafficAnalytics README and named processing files supplied usable source evidence. No fact depends on those failed paths. No env file, account or runtime tracker was read or executed.
- **Lifecycle search:** official shutdown/sunset/acquisition searches led to current Ghost 6.0 analytics, plan and improvement pages. No applicable retirement or transfer notice was found. Forum posts by other hosting providers were not used as proof of Ghost's own support.

## Field problems

- F02/F15/F19: this product combines web traffic, email engagement and subscription reporting. Those surfaces do not share one identity model or one definition of “paid member.”
- F08/F12: registered members, visitors, pageviews and MRR are independent inputs. A hosting price at a fixed member count is not a per-pageview tariff or capacity test.
- F06/F07/F30: open-source Ghost and its MIT traffic service do not imply the documented production backend is automatically installed, free, or entirely local. The Docker guide remains labelled Preview.
- F21/F22/F24: cookie-free daily anonymous counting must be separated from account cookies, member-linked browsing, email engagement and backend IP processing.
- F25/F26: “all time” reporting and “all data” export are not promises of indefinite raw-event retention or complete traffic-history portability.
- F13: the fair use page uses older plan names for headless asset bandwidth. Those legacy quotas were not silently mapped to Starter/Publisher/Business.
- F32: the default source signal is useful, but filename/build/custom-frontend differences remain unvalidated. Ghost platform detection alone cannot identify native analytics activation.
- No clear `apps.md` row error found. The active status, publication/subscription scope, hosting/email overlap and conditional C class fit the evidence. The row's request to verify hosted/self-hosted differences is addressed here.
