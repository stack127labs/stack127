## Cost examples: our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: current Pro pricing https://qonversion.io/pricing; chart MRR definition https://documentation.qonversion.io/docs/revenue. Amounts below preserve the pricing page's `$` symbol. An explicit service-invoice ISO currency and tax clause were not established. Analytics' default USD display is a separate fact.

Let `T` be the actual calendar month's billable MTR under the vendor's definition. Standard Pro software fee:

- If `T <= $7,000`, fee = `$0`.
- If `T > $7,000`, fee = `T × 0.008`, applied to the full amount.

| Explicit input | Formula: our calculation | Standard Pro result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $1,000 MTR/calendar month | Threshold not exceeded | $0/month |
| $7,000 MTR/calendar month | At the free boundary | $0/month |
| $8,000 MTR/calendar month | $8,000 × 0.008 | $64/month |
| $10,000 MTR/calendar month | $10,000 × 0.008 | $80/month |
| $50,000 MTR/calendar month | $50,000 × 0.008 | $400/month |

The $8,000 and $50,000 results also match the vendor's examples. There is no $7,000 deduction once the threshold is crossed. Each month is recalculated: a later month at or below $7,000 is free again. Source checked 2026-11-05: https://qonversion.io/pricing.

The field list asks for $1,000, $10,000 and $50,000 **MRR** scenarios. MRR alone does not give payment amounts processed during a calendar month. Annual prepayments, one-time purchases and purchase/renewal timing can change MTR. Qonversion also normalizes subscription prices using its documented duration factors and limits MRR to auto-renew-enabled subscriptions. Sources checked 2026-11-05: https://qonversion.io/pricing ; https://documentation.qonversion.io/docs/revenue.

| Required workload | Exact price from MRR alone | Conditional result: our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |

These conditional figures are workload assumptions, not a conversion rule from MRR. No event-to-revenue or monthly-active-user-to-revenue conversion is assumed.
