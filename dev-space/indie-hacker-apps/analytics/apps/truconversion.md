## Cost examples — our calculations

Checked 2026-11-05. Tariff inputs: https://www.truconversion.com/pricing/. Counting rules: https://help.truconversion.com/knowledgebase/what-are-the-pageviews/. Extra usage: https://help.truconversion.com/knowledgebase/how-to-buy-more-pageviews/. Prices retain the published `$` symbol; ISO currency and tax treatment were not established. Figures below are advertised subscription amounts, not guaranteed tax-inclusive checkout totals.

### Website pageviews

Assumptions: one site, one dashboard user, monthly billing, active campaigns covering the stated monthly pageviews. The same page’s overlapping tools do not multiply the billable pageview count. Formula: choose the smallest published **base plan** with `included monthly pageviews >= required monthly tracked pageviews`.

| Required tracked pageviews/month | Calculation | Published base-plan result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10,000 | 10,000 <= 100,000 Basic allowance; 1 × $49/month | $49/month, Business Basic |
| 100,000 | 100,000 <= 100,000 Basic allowance; 1 × $49/month | $49/month, Business Basic |
| 1,000,000 | 1,000,000 > 500,000 Business Pro; 1,000,000 <= 1,000,000 Agency Basic; 1 × $490/month | $490/month, Agency Basic |

All three include the product’s core heatmap/recording/form/funnel/survey tool families within the pageview budget. Advanced functions differ by plan. Business Basic excludes recording triggers/sharing, heatmap downloads and many filters. Agency Basic includes recording sharing.

These are calculable base-plan choices, not guaranteed cheapest complete quotes. Extra pageviews are purchasable, but their public unit price and validity period were not established. A lower base plan plus an add-on might cost differently. Pages without active campaigns are not automatically deducted under the documented counting rule; therefore total site traffic and charged campaign traffic can differ.

### Session replay

Requested workloads: **1,000; 10,000; 100,000 recordings/month**. Each is **not comparable to an exact subscription total without a separate tracked-pageview workload**. No ratio between sessions and pageviews is assumed.

Known inputs: Business Basic is $49/month with 100,000 tracked pageviews; the plan guide calls recording count unlimited subject to pageview usage. Formula would be `plan price covering the actual tracked pageviews + any purchased pageviews`. The recording count alone cannot supply that input. Even 100,000 requested sessions must not be priced by pretending each has only one pageview.

Scope: web replay, with heatmaps in the base tool family and no separate mandatory heatmap add-on shown. Desired capture is 100% of the requested eligible sessions, but browser exclusions, campaign recording-count settings, event targeting and pageview limits can prevent that. Maximum recording length was not established. Terms use a one-hour recording-session inactivity definition; this is not a maximum duration. The tariff displays six months generic data storage, without a distinct replay-specific promise. Inputs: https://www.truconversion.com/pricing/ ; https://help.truconversion.com/knowledgebase/pricing-structure/ ; https://help.truconversion.com/knowledgebase/create-your-first-visitors-recording/ ; https://www.truconversion.com/term-of-use/ ; https://help.truconversion.com/knowledgebase/does-truconversion-track-all-visitors-2/.
