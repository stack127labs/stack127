## Conflicts and unknowns

- Pricing: the public tariff is a monthly-revenue table from $20/month up to $2,250/month through $5M/month, followed by custom pricing. The terms also allow annual plans, but no current annual tariff is shown. Exact revenue basis, currency code, taxes and included source counts remain unclear. The displayed bands overlap at their boundaries; the page's examples put $10K at $20 and $50K at $100.
- Plan/history labels: current pricing uses revenue bands, while a history page says Starter (2 years) and Growth (5 years), and another import FAQ still lists Scale (7 years). Do not map the old plan names to current revenue bands without confirmation.
- Subscription platforms: the current Subscriptions dashboard lists Stripe, WooCommerce, EDD, Braintree, Authorize.Net and Inbound. An older official subscription-metrics page also lists PayPal and Gumroad. Confirm those two before relying on their subscription reports.
- Web Analytics: its feature page and setup guide advertise a first-party snippet; a recent blog says its audience reporting draws on Google Analytics and Search Console rather than native traffic collection. Both remain in the facts table.
- Data removal: the Privacy Policy says source-side deletion is immediate, while the Security page says server cleanup is periodic and immediate deletion requires email. No exact purge or backup schedule is public.
- Ownership details: Terms identify Leap Ventures, Inc. as the legal entity; the contact page also names LeapVen Solutions Private Limited. The public pages do not fully explain the relationship. No acquisition notice was found.
