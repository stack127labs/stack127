## Cost examples — our calculation

Checked 2026-11-05. Inputs: one website, one owner/member, no additional sites/seats, basic analytics with Free's 60-day live history. Pageview workloads and event workloads are separate; no pageview/event/user conversion ratio is assumed. Original currency is EUR.

Published plan inputs: Free €0 with 3 sites/3 members/60 days; Pro €15/month or €150/year with 10/5/365; Business €39/month or €390/year with 50/20/1,095. Core reports, API and exports are included on Free. Source: https://hitkeep.com/pricing/. Terms impose no fixed published hit/event quota but reserve fair-use measures or a more appropriate plan for disproportionate resource use: https://hitkeep.com/legal/terms-of-service/. Ingest separately defaults to 20 requests/second/IP and burst 40: https://hitkeep.com/guides/tracking/server-side-tracking/; https://hitkeep.com/guides/tracking/custom-events/.

Formula: advertised monthly fee = selected team's plan price, provided its sites, members, history and feature gates fit and the workload is accepted under fair use. There is no published per-pageview or per-event overage term to add. An unconditional high-volume service total is `not_comparable` when fair-use acceptance or a requested replacement plan cannot be determined.
