## Cost examples

These are **our calculations**, checked 2026-11-05. Inputs: one website, one user, pageviews only, no custom events, current **Lite** discounted price. All three cases fit Lite's published site/seat allowances. Values come from [pricing calculator source](https://publytics.net/pricing), whose displayed price uses DISCOUNTED_* arrays while higher list prices sit in line-through elements. The [overage guide](https://publytics.net/documentation/overage) independently uses €55/€70 for the 1M/2M Lite bands.

| Monthly pageviews | Monthly calculation | Annual calculation under steady monthly traffic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10,000 | 1 Lite 10k/month plan × €5 = **€5/month** | 10,000 × 12 = 120,000 annual hits; 1 annual plan × €50 = **€50/year**. |
| 100,000 | 1 Lite 100k/month plan × €15 = **€15/month** | 100,000 × 12 = 1,200,000 annual hits; 1 annual plan × €150 = **€150/year**. |
| 1,000,000 | 1 Lite 1M/month plan × €55 = **€55/month** | 1,000,000 × 12 = 12,000,000 annual hits; 1 annual plan × €550 = **€550/year**. |

Annual traffic is a total over the yearly billing cycle, supported by the calculator's annual volume labels and [overage docs](https://publytics.net/documentation/overage). It is not silently treated as a separate hard monthly cap. Annual amounts are the full yearly subscription components, not monthly payments. No automatic conversion, exchange rate or rounded equivalent was used.

**Threshold caveat:** These examples sit at the advertised band ceilings. [Tier guide](https://publytics.net/documentation/how-to-choose-the-right-subscripion-tier) warns that reaching the cap can stop dashboard access, while overage docs describe exceeding it. Exact boundary behavior is unresolved. For operational headroom, the user may need a larger band; no additional traffic amount was invented or priced into the base scenarios.

**Custom-event scenario, our calculation:** 100,000 pageviews + 100,000 custom-event occurrences = **200,000 billable hits/month**. That exceeds Lite's 100k band and fits its 250k band: **1 × €25 = €25/month**. If that workload repeats for twelve months: 200,000 × 12 = **2,400,000 hits/year**, within the 3M annual band at **€250/year**. Assume one named custom event within Lite's feature allowance; no event-to-person conversion is made.

If Business features are required, the same pageview-only examples cost **our calculation: €10/month or €100/year at 10k monthly traffic; €30/month or €300/year at 100k; €110/month or €1,100/year at 1M**, using corresponding annual volumes. Enterprise and over 10M monthly/120M annual hits need a quote; no linear extrapolation.

**Upgrade example, our calculation:** Lite 1M €55 to Lite 2M €70 yields **€70 − €55 = €15** extra for the upgrade, as the vendor's own guide describes; billing date stays unchanged. This is a subscription upgrade, not a published per-hit overage charge.

An exact VAT-inclusive checkout total cannot be calculated: [billing details](https://publytics.net/documentation/billing-details) discusses company tax treatment, but the checked public tariff/terms do not give enough conditions for a buyer-specific total. Prices remain in euros. [Terms](https://publytics.net/terms-of-service) set one-month/one-year minimums, renewal and non-refundable fees. Advertised discounted rates have no published end date established here. Website hosting and third-party CMS/integration costs are outside these analytics subscriptions.

- **Calculator prices:** Plain HTML/text extraction leaves live numbers blank because Alpine bindings populate them. - **Overage behavior:** FAQ says data keeps collecting and history survives. Terms 2.2 further inhibit configured extra events; dashboard docs block affected reports, and [API/MCP docs](https://publytics.net/documentation/api) reject overage data access. The statement “collection continues” must therefore remain qualified by pageview versus custom-event behavior.
- **Privacy/custom identifiers:** [DPA](https://publytics.net/dpa) prohibits reidentifying visitor IDs, describes daily anonymous hashing and says raw IP/UA never stored. [Terms](https://publytics.net/terms-of-service) prohibit sensitive-data misuse. - **Scope of privacy policy:** Default visitor analytics, subscriber login/security data and advertising-related landing pages have different treatment. - **Imports:** [FAQ](https://publytics.net/faq) names GA4/Plausible/Fathom and asks customer to follow an after-signup Import Data guide then contact provider to finalize. All paid cards include history import. A guessed public /documentation/import-data URL returned 404. - **Tax/restrictions:** Checked pricing, full terms, billing docs and account signup for tax/country/age/commitment rules.
