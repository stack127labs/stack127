## Conflicts and unknowns

- **“Real-time” marketing vs reporting freshness:** The product page uses “real-time revenue reporting.” The report docs say CSV data may be delayed up to 24 hours and Explore refreshes on a 24-hour cycle. The product page does not define “real-time” for each report, so the current docs’ specific freshness limits are recorded.
- **Revenue measures are not net payout:** Explore Revenue excludes taxes, Paddle fees, refunds and chargebacks. The help article’s Billing Revenue chart also excludes tax and Paddle fees and does not subtract refunds or chargebacks. Payout reconciliation is the separate report for balance movements and payout fees.
- **Billing Reports vs ProfitWell Metrics:** Billing reports export Paddle account entities and payout records. Explore offers Paddle-account timeseries and breakdowns. ProfitWell Metrics is a distinct free subscription analytics module with MRR, churn, LTV, ARPU, cohorts and benchmarks; its docs list connections to other billing platforms. Billing Reports and Explore are not multi-provider analytics connectors.
- **Ownership:** Paddle’s official terms identify operating/contracting entities, and its funding announcement names investors. The pages checked do not state the current ultimate owner or ownership percentages.
- **Historical reporting:** Paddle Classic reporting history stays in Classic after migration. Paddle Billing can import subscriptions, but the migration docs do not say that historic Classic reports are copied into Billing.
- **Data location:** Paddle’s privacy notice describes international processing and transfers; its DPA references a third-party data center without naming a region and is dated February 2023. A specific current storage region was not published in those pages.
- **Pages checked for unlisted details:** Current pricing and terms for plan fees, report pricing, and custom pricing; onboarding and supported-country pages for access restrictions; reporting docs, report-specific docs, Explore and API references for report types, filters, definitions, freshness, retention and limits; team permissions for seats; the DPA, privacy and GDPR pages for data location and consent; migration docs for imported data; Paddle’s announcements, developer changelog and status page for acquisition, closure and current-operation news. No separate newsroom was identified; Paddle’s official announcement blog and developer changelog served as the news sources.

## Field problems

- **F09–F12:** The analytics features are embedded in a transaction platform. There is no separate report plan or usage price to compare; seller transaction fees still apply. The cost table therefore calculates only the incremental report price.
- **F15–F18 and F21:** These fields assume visitor or app-usage analytics. Paddle Billing provides checkout-session conversion and billing records, not general traffic, behavioral replay or native app-use tracking.
- **F19:** “Revenue” can mean gross completed transaction value, recurring subscription metrics or reconciled payout cash. Paddle exposes all three in separate reports/views; they should not be merged into one net revenue number.
- **F23–F25:** Seller billing data, buyer personal data, exported CSV availability and historical snapshots have different terms and lifetimes. The current public pages do not provide a single storage location or one retention period for all reporting data.
- **apps.md row note:** The status and detection class look right. Its note says “payment providers overlap”; that is true for the separate ProfitWell Metrics product, not the built-in Paddle Billing Reports/Explore modules. Merchant eligibility has now been checked against Paddle’s supported-country help page.
