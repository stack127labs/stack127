## Cost examples — our calculations

Checked 2026-11-05. Inputs are the [current pricing tariff](https://www.statsig.com/pricing), its metering FAQ, and the [replay limits](https://docs.statsig.com/session-replay/configure). These examples use the displayed $ currency and monthly billing, one project and one user/seat. Taxes are excluded. Enterprise orders explicitly use USD in the [Enterprise terms](https://www.statsig.com/enterprise-terms); a private Enterprise quote is not estimated here.

Let **E** be the final monthly **Metered Event** total after billable exposures, additional dimensions, custom metrics and imports have been counted. It is not simply the number of SDK calls or people.

- Developer core analytics: **$0 when E ≤ 2,000,000**, subject to its feature/retention limits.
- Pro: **$150 + max(E − 5,000,000, 0) / 1,000 × $0.05**.
- A Pro feature requirement can make the price $150 even when the same volume fits Developer. Session Analytics/User Profiles have the unresolved entitlement issue noted above.
