Terms define MAU precisely but do not tie it to price. The public record does not establish whether price scales by MAU, revenue, apps, seats, features or a different meter.

- **Current product activity:** the official status page listed core systems operational; changelog lists SDK 6.2.0 and the public site invites sales meetings. These show current service and customer-acquisition activity, but do not verify a self-serve account creation or a completed new sale.
