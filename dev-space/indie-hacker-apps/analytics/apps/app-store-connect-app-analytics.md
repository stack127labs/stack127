# App Store Connect App Analytics

- Official URL: https://developer.apple.com/app-store-connect/analytics/
- Required account: Apple Developer account and App Store Connect access through an eligible Apple Developer Program membership or an authorized team role. Standard program membership costs USD 99/year; Analytics is included at no additional charge. An ordinary free developer registration alone is not this entitlement.
- Platform restriction: Apple-platform apps only. Dashboard usage covers iOS/iPadOS, macOS, tvOS and visionOS and excludes watchOS; the Subscription Event API report has an explicit watchOS exception. No Android or arbitrary-website analytics collector is established.
- Lifecycle check: no product-wide shutdown/sunset/acquisition notice found. Apple's March 25, 2026 update expands current reporting. The current report guide says new subscription reports replace legacy Sales and Trends subscription reports; this does not retire the entire Sales and Trends or financial reporting service.
- Owner/operator: Apple Inc.; Apple privacy-controller affiliates can differ by region.

## Cost examples — our calculations

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: [Apple's included-analytics statement](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/get-started/), [standard enrollment price](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/), [membership fee/tax terms](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/), and [fee-waiver rules](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/fee-waivers).

Assume one standard Apple Developer Program membership, one Apple-platform app, one authorized account holder, and no approved fee waiver. Apple publishes USD 99 per membership year as its reference price; local currency/prices vary by region. Applicable taxes remain separate. The annual membership is a prerequisite for the publishing/account capability, not an Analytics subscription.

| Cost component | Inputs/formula | Our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Additional App Analytics tool fee | Included with eligible Developer Program membership | USD 0 additional analytics charge |
| Standard Developer Program membership | One membership year × USD 99/year | USD 99/year, plus applicable taxes |
| First-year reference total for a new standard member | USD 99 membership + USD 0 additional Analytics | USD 99/year, plus applicable taxes |
| Existing eligible member enabling Analytics | Membership already held; no separate Analytics implementation or tier purchase | USD 0 incremental Analytics charge; existing annual membership still costs money |
| Monthly accounting allocation only | USD 99 ÷ 12 | USD 8.25/month equivalent; billed yearly, not an offered monthly plan |
| Approved eligible fee-waived organization | Membership fee waived + included Analytics | USD 0 membership fee and USD 0 extra Analytics; approval/continued eligibility required |

The waiver is not generally available to solo proprietors or commercial app businesses. Its rules exclude individual/single-person businesses, the Paid Applications Agreement and otherwise selling digital goods/services through apps. It must not be used to price an ordinary paid app or subscription business at zero.

| Requested workload | Our calculation/result | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 / 10,000 / 100,000 monthly active users | USD 0 extra Analytics; USD 99/year standard membership separately | No user-priced tier. Apple's usage metric counts opted-in active devices, not necessarily distinct people; visibility thresholds still apply |
| USD 1,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 MRR from supported Apple subscriptions | USD 0 extra Analytics; USD 99/year standard membership separately | MRR does not meter the Analytics fee. Store commerce fees, commissions, taxes and refunds are separate business costs, not calculated here |
| 100,000 / 1,000,000 / 10,000,000 arbitrary custom product events/month | Not comparable | No general custom-event ingestion/analytics tariff established |
| 10,000 / 100,000 / 1,000,000 website pageviews/month | Not applicable to general website analytics | Store product-page impressions/referrers are a different workload |
| 1,000 / 10,000 / 100,000 recorded replay sessions/month | Not comparable | Session counts are supported; a visual recorder/replay tariff is not established |

No event-to-user, device-to-person or MRR-to-purchases conversion is assumed. No Apple transaction commission is guessed: final app-commerce cost depends on applicable agreements, programs, products, region and transactions. The calculations price only the included Analytics capability and its separately stated membership prerequisite. API throughput limits can restrict downloads but are not a published paid-overage tier.

## Resolved differences and remaining unknowns

- **Current product and lifecycle:** checked the current product/help/enrollment/legal pages and official shutdown/sunset searches. The [March 25, 2026 announcement](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=hh6v4b55) and current guide describe expanded subscriptions, cohorts, revenue and reporting. They are used instead of outdated assumptions that App Analytics only measures basic downloads. No shutdown or acquisition notice was established. Product labels vary between App Analytics, Analytics in App Store Connect and the App Store Connect Analytics help collection; no separate pricing products are inferred.
- **Account versus fee:** [free developer registration](https://developer.apple.com/support/compare-memberships/) provides development resources, while program membership provides App Store Connect access. [Analytics is included](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/get-started/) with that program. An invited team member can receive access without buying their own duplicate membership. The standard USD 99/year, regional prices, identity/account rules and approved waivers are recorded separately. This is not a standalone no-account Free analytics plan.
- **Apple platforms only, with a report-specific exception:** [dashboard help](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/overview/analytics-dashboard) covers iOS/iPadOS/macOS/tvOS/visionOS and explicitly excludes watchOS. The [App Sessions API report](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/analytics-reports/app-sessions.json) also excludes watchOS, while the [Subscription Event API report](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/analytics-reports/subscription-event.json) explicitly includes it. This is a dashboard/report/platform scope difference. No Android or arbitrary website collector is established.
- **Different reporting tools:** [App Analytics, Sales and Trends, and Payments and Financial Reports](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/measure-app-performance/overview-of-reporting-tools/) have different jobs. Analytics estimates sales/proceeds, subscription performance and usage; final payments use settled transactions and the Apple fiscal calendar. The current API guide says new subscription reports replace legacy Sales and Trends subscription reports, not the entire Sales and Trends or financial reporting service. No app-store commission was treated as an Analytics usage fee.
- **Conversion and retention definitions:** use [current metric definitions](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/reference/metrics-definitions) for conversion: total downloads and pre-orders divided by unique device impressions, without counting a fulfilled pre-order twice. The [specific retention guide](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/engagement/app-retention) excludes installations that have never opened and explains that late first opens can increase the historical denominator. This avoids using an abbreviated marketing explanation as a different formula.
- **Privacy populations:** opt-in usage metrics, store discovery, Apple Account-based payer metrics and transaction data are different populations. [Report privacy](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/analytics-reports/privacy.json) adds thresholds/noise where specified. A blank row can reflect privacy suppression or delayed data rather than zero activity. [Peer benchmarks](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/benchmarks/peer-group-benchmarks) are privacy-preserving distributions, not named-competitor estimates or developer permission to build a cross-developer data repository.
- **Report timing:** the [overview](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/overview/analytics-reports-api) gives a generic two-day completeness description, but current report-specific pages say App Sessions completes within five days and Subscription Event within three. The specific report windows are recorded for those reports rather than one universal two-day promise. Weekly/monthly and corrected instances have separate behavior; use the [completeness guide](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/analytics-reports/data-completeness-corrections.json) when updating stored exports.
- **Export history versus file retention:** [generated API report instances](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/analytics-reports.json) are available for 35 days. A monthly one-time snapshot can retrieve available historical data. App Sessions starts January 1, 2024; Subscription Event starts January 1, 2025. The [app-transfer rule](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/transfer-an-app/overview-of-app-transfer/) gives the recipient available Analytics since April 1, 2015 or launch. That is not a claim every newer API report has data since 2015 or that all dashboard records are retained indefinitely.
- **Membership exit:** [renewal docs](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/renewal/) explain distribution loss and retention of certain App Store Connect features after expiry, but do not establish a precise Analytics-only access/history window. The [agreement](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/) provides fee, termination and service limits. No claim of continued Analytics access forever or immediate universal data deletion is made. Unspecified backup purge and post-expiry report access remain not published.
- **Data location and agreements:** reviewed [general privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/), [App Store privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/), [Developer privacy](https://developer.apple.com/support/privacy/), the public agreements index and the program agreement. Apple states general US storage/worldwide processing and regional Apple controllers/SCCs. No dedicated App Analytics data-center/backup map, customer-chosen EU-only region, named subprocessor inventory or standard developer-as-controller processor DPA was established. Legal/controller location is not app-data residency.
- **Data removal:** general privacy rights/contact channels are published; a developer-controlled endpoint for deleting one person's contribution from aggregate Analytics reports was not established after reviewing privacy/API/help/terms. Long-term Apple purchase-record retention is separately disclosed and must not be substituted for the lifetime of developer downloads or app usage reports.
- **Other bounded unknowns:** current dashboard, role, account, report/API and metric guides were checked for full UI language lists, organization app/seat limits, arbitrary custom events, user identity merges, named-account reports, replay, internal-device/bot exclusion, historical imports and export-size/manual-chart format guarantees. No missing statement was turned into a zero, unlimited allowance or unsupported capability.

## Field problems

- **F04/F08–F12:** an Apple Developer account, eligible program membership and the Analytics capability are related but not the same price component. Show the mandatory USD 99/year reference membership fee separately from USD 0 additional Analytics. Fee waivers and invited-user access need their own conditions.
