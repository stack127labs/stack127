The vendor says services were restored, its core/customer-data systems were not compromised, and the Mobile SDK was unaffected. It cannot see all activity within exposed visitor browsers. These are vendor findings, not our forensic verification. Source: https://www.appsflyer.com/march-2026-faq/ (checked 2026-11-05).

## Cost examples — our calculation

These are illustrative **mobile billable-conversion workloads**, not event or MAU estimates. Public standard Growth rate: USD $0.07 per conversion after Welcome. Currency is explicit on the official Spanish pricing page; the English page publishes the same numerical rate. The one-time Welcome allowance is 12,000 conversions within the first 12 months. Sources: https://www.appsflyer.com/pricing/ ; https://www.appsflyer.com/es/pricing/ (checked 2026-11-05).

Inputs: one account, standard advertised Growth rate, no premium add-ons, no negotiated package, before tax. For the Welcome example only, assume all 12,000 conversions remain at the beginning of the illustrated month and the account is still within its first year.

| Measured billable conversions in illustrated month | After Welcome: our calculation | If full Welcome balance remains: our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1,000 × USD $0.07 = **USD $70** | max(0, 1,000 − 12,000) × USD $0.07 = **USD $0** |
| 10,000 | 10,000 × USD $0.07 = **USD $700** | max(0, 10,000 − 12,000) × USD $0.07 = **USD $0** |
| 100,000 | 100,000 × USD $0.07 = **USD $7,000** | (100,000 − 12,000) × USD $0.07 = **USD $6,160** |

Welcome does not reset monthly. Continued paid measurement after its limit requires the appropriate paid setup; without it the account becomes Zero. The table does not establish automatic paid collection after an un-upgraded free account expires.
