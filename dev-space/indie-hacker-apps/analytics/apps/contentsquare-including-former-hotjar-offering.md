# Contentsquare, including former Hotjar offering

**Lifecycle and new sales:** Contentsquare acquired Hotjar in 2021. [New Hotjar accounts can no longer be created](https://contentsquare.com/hotjar/); new customers sign up for Contentsquare Free or purchase current Contentsquare plans. Existing Hotjar customers can [opt out before their notified migration deadline](https://support.contentsquare.com/hc/en-us/articles/46690997637137-Moving-from-Hotjar-to-Contentsquare-FAQs) and continue their Hotjar experience/subscription. Licensed Ask, Observe and Engage services still have a [current Hotjar service schedule](https://contentsquare.com/legal/service-schedule-hotjar/). No current standalone new-customer Hotjar/Engage tariff was established.

**Migration deadline conflict:** The [migration help page](https://support.contentsquare.com/hc/en-us/articles/46690997637137-Moving-from-Hotjar-to-Contentsquare-FAQs) promises **12 months** of read-only Hotjar access after moving, while the [July 2026 legal schedule, §2.4.3](https://contentsquare.com/legal/service-schedule-hotjar/) says **90 days**, then deletion. Verify the account notice/contract before relying on either period. Historical Observe analytics does not automatically transfer; surveys and feedback do.

**Plan boundary:** All new-sale prices below are Contentsquare prices checked today. Legacy Hotjar plan names and migration discounts are explicitly scoped. Experience Analytics, Voice of Customer, Product Analytics and the newer Experience Platform packages are kept distinct.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Hotjar remains a legacy account/service name, not a separate public new signup.** The [current landing page](https://contentsquare.com/hotjar/) explicitly closes new Hotjar accounts and directs new users to Contentsquare. Existing customers can [opt out of their notified move](https://support.contentsquare.com/hc/en-us/articles/46690997637137-Moving-from-Hotjar-to-Contentsquare-FAQs) and continue their Hotjar subscription. The [Hotjar service schedule](https://contentsquare.com/legal/service-schedule-hotjar/) still governs licensed Ask/Observe/Engage services and recognizes old plan names. No current public standalone Engage tariff was established from its redirected pages and current schedule.
- **Migration access deadline conflicts:** [help](https://support.contentsquare.com/hc/en-us/articles/46690997637137-Moving-from-Hotjar-to-Contentsquare-FAQs) promises 12 months read-only; [July 2026 legal schedule §2.4.3](https://contentsquare.com/legal/service-schedule-hotjar/) says 90 days, followed by deletion. Neither should silently replace the other. Check the actual account notice and governing contract before relying on time to export. The phased FAQ names first contacts from 26 August but gives an individual start/deadline to each account; it is not one universal shutdown date.
- **Current and old plans differ.** [Pricing](https://contentsquare.com/pricing/) separates Experience Analytics, Voice of Customer and Product Analytics, and now introduces Experience Platform Pro/Enterprise above a comparison explicitly scoped to Experience Analytics. No old Hotjar numeric price was reused. The detailed table's Pro API/add-on/history entitlements are not presumed to describe the newer Experience Platform contract.
- **Growth cancellation/refunds conflict:** [marketing](https://contentsquare.com/pricing/) says cancel any time and offers a 30-day money-back guarantee; [Free/Growth schedule](https://contentsquare.com/legal/service-schedule-free-growth/) requires cancellation at least 5 days before monthly renewal or 30 days before annual renewal and describes committed fees as nonrefundable. Record the purchase terms and confirm the specific guarantee; do not give an unconditional refund promise.
- **Reset dates conflict:** [session limits](https://support.contentsquare.com/hc/en-us/articles/49355673591057-Sessions-replays-and-plan-limits) repeatedly use calendar-month resets but distinguish annual subscription-date refreshes; the [billing guide](https://support.contentsquare.com/hc/en-us/articles/37271803160849-Contentsquare-Free-and-Growth-plans) speaks of the billing cycle. This is unresolved for a universal annual-account reset date. Quota exhaustion stops collection rather than ensuring a full month of data.
- **Sampling is conditional, not a universal 100% badge.** Ordinary [analytics and replay allocations](https://support.contentsquare.com/hc/en-us/articles/49355673591057-Sessions-replays-and-plan-limits) differ. The [migration sampling FAQ](https://support.contentsquare.com/hc/en-us/articles/52397284524433-Understand-sampling-and-pricing-when-moving-from-Hotjar-to-Contentsquare) describes bespoke analytics sampling and a one-year loyalty discount, despite broader migration language about 100% capture. Individual notices supply the applicable rate and price; sampling need not end when the discount does.
- **Migration is not historical Observe import.** [Migration guidance](https://support.contentsquare.com/hc/en-us/articles/46690997637137-Moving-from-Hotjar-to-Contentsquare-FAQs) keeps historical heatmaps/recordings/funnels/dashboards in Hotjar, while surveys/feedback transfer. It also says suppression carries over, but IP exclusions, integrations and cookie-manager configuration need attention. Historical exports have the [legacy limitations](https://help.hotjar.com/hc/en-us/articles/36820033667729-How-to-Share-and-Export-Data) recorded above.
- **Annual/volume price precision is bounded.** The [page](https://contentsquare.com/pricing/) publishes entry prices, a volume selector, yearly equivalents and discounts; it does not establish every selected high-volume numeric tariff in the default response inspected. Exact annual entry billing is unresolved because the displayed equivalent and percentage yield different arithmetic. No inferred price or currency conversion fills that gap.
- **Privacy defaults do not sanitize every data channel.** [Web masking](https://support.contentsquare.com/hc/en-us/articles/50072500900881-How-to-set-up-and-manage-Data-masking) defaults to Full; [detailed redaction docs](https://docs.contentsquare.com/en/web/personal-data-handling/) identify custom/identity/property channels that are sent as supplied.
