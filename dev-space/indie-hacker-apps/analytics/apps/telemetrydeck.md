## Cost examples — our calculations

Checked 2026-11-05. Current public self-service prices: https://api.telemetrydeckapi.com/api/v3/stripe/prices/list, used by https://dashboard.telemetrydeck.com/pricing-calculator and https://dashboard.telemetrydeck.com/plans. Pricing-change eligibility: https://telemetrydeck.com/blog/pricing-update-2026/. Public price records state EUR, recurring period, included event volume and tax-exclusive amounts. Amounts below normalize EUR cents as `unitAmount / 100`; no currency conversion.

Assumptions: one app/site, one dashboard user, new account after July 1, 2026, monthly billing, no other organizational app traffic and the stated total production-event workload. All automatically emitted SDK events and webhook events must fit that total. Taxes depend on billing details and are additional. These are base subscription estimates, not guaranteed future bills if auto-upgrades apply.

### Product events

Formula: choose the smallest published tier covering the monthly total. Free new accounts allow 50,000 events; paid bands begin at 750,000.

| Monthly event input | Formula | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100,000 | 100,000 > 50,000 free; 100,000 <= 750,000 Indie; 1 × €9 | €9/month |
| 1,000,000 | 1,000,000 > 750,000; <= 1,500,000; 1 × €27 | €27/month |
| 10,000,000 | 10,000,000 <= 10,000,000; 1 × €180 | €180/month |

Annual alternatives for the same monthly event tiers: €90/year, €270/year and €1,800/year respectively, from exact annual tariff records. Monthly event allowances do not become annual pooled event allowances.

Existing free accounts created before July 1, 2026 keep 100,000/month, so the first workload could remain €0 for that specific grandfathered cohort. Current paid customers may retain old rates until plan change or automatic upgrade. No obsolete tariff is applied to new customers.

### Replay, MRR and annual-discount limits

- **1,000 / 10,000 / 100,000 replay sessions/month:** not comparable; no replay offering established. Recording length, replay capture rate, replay-specific retention and heatmap add-on price are not applicable to the documented event product. See https://telemetrydeck.com/platforms/, https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/basics/ and https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/web-setup/.
- **$1,000 / $10,000 / $50,000 MRR:** not a price meter. The event volume from a RevenueCat integration is needed; MRR alone cannot determine it. See https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/integrations/revenuecat/ and https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/articles/preset-purchases/. Dollar inputs are not converted into EUR.
- **Annual-discount arithmetic:** the deployed pricing copy advertises 20%, but Indie is €9/month or €90/year. Our calculation: twelve monthly payments = `12 × €9 = €108`; saving = `€108 − €90 = €18`; percentage = `€18 / €108 = 1/6 = 16.67%`, rounded to two decimals. All seven returned paid tiers have annual price = ten monthly payments. We use the actual annual tariff rather than manufacture a 20% price.

## Conflicts and unknowns

1. **Lifecycle:** no platform shutdown/acquisition established in official-domain searches and current home/legal/pricing sources. https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/articles/preset-purchases/ does explicitly sunset the direct-SDK Purchase Tab for new apps; existing enabled apps can retain it. RevenueCat remains documented. https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/articles/grand-rename/ describes an SDK/repository/event-schema rename, not a separate product.
2. **Pricing provenance:** /pricing/ returned 404 and public dashboard pages have JavaScript-only HTML. Their published bundle https://dashboard.telemetrydeck.com/assets/main-C-sFMd8W.js identifies the unauthenticated price-list request https://api.telemetrydeckapi.com/api/v3/stripe/prices/list. That exact public pricing endpoint was read without credentials; no customer-data/API query was made. Free entitlements come from the public calculator’s embedded free-plan object, not an invented €0 API tariff.
3. **Annual discount:** https://dashboard.telemetrydeck.com/assets/main-C-sFMd8W.js says 20% savings; https://api.telemetrydeckapi.com/api/v3/stripe/prices/list yields 16⅔% against twelve monthly payments. See the explicitly labelled arithmetic above. Exact invoice tax/rounding and non-self-service prices were not verified.
4. **Quota enforcement:** https://telemetrydeck.com/blog/pricing-update-2026/ describes namespace ingestion stopping and up to two days of retroactive recovery, while paid auto-upgrade text says collection continues with a two-month threshold. Current public free FAQ copy says ingestion pauses/no surprise charge; another pricing string says queries may be restricted. The precise free grace/restriction thresholds and paid auto-upgrade suppression/financial caps were not established.
5. **Free cadence:** the free product description says once a day; FAQ/explanation strings say every few hours, and another status string describes hourly-to-daily round-robin processing. These are not an exact fresh-data SLA. Paid products explicitly advertise realtime; long/complex queries can still have their own refresh behavior.
6. **Hosting versions:** https://telemetrydeck.com/blog/europe-based-app-analytics-service/, published July 7, 2026, describes Hetzner core hosting since April and chooses Helsinki, while retaining Azure static hosting and some customer-specific AWS components. https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/privacy-faq/ still lists Amsterdam/Frankfurt/Falkenstein/Nuremberg, and https://telemetrydeck.com/use-case/architecture-security/ says Nuremberg/transition pending. No blanket “everything in Germany” statement is adopted. https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/articles/hosting-solutions/ gives an EU cloud model but not exact backups/region selection.
7. **Privacy scope:** the controlling German https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/ concerns vendor website/account/contact processing and explicitly covers website IP logs/cookies/providers. Product analytics https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/privacy-faq/ says no retained analytics IP or personally identifiable data. These are different scopes; they must not be flattened into either “no data anywhere” or “SDK sends website account data.”
8. **Automatic collection and timestamps:** https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/privacy-faq/ says collection is not automatic and timestamps are rounded to the hour. https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/swift-setup/, https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/android-setup/ and https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/web-setup/ explicitly emit automatic events. https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/ingest/v3/ and https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/ingest/default-parameters/ describe v3 receivedAt retained as supplied, including a precise timestamp. Therefore neither generic manual-only collection nor universal hour rounding is treated as settled.
9. **SDK/version gaps:** Android setup https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/android-setup/ documents 6.0.1/API 21; current https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TelemetryDeck/KotlinSDK/main/README.md documents 7.3.1/API 23 with toolchain changes. These are version-specific, not one compatibility promise. JavaScript’s default queue is volatile; Kotlin has persistent defaults with an explicit in-memory-only mode. Exact Swift retry/expiry and all-framework parity were not established.
10. **Anonymization and contract:** https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/articles/anonymization-how-it-works/ documents optional stable/cross-device matching with shared custom salt/identifier. https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/ingest/v3/ and https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/swift-setup/ say arbitrary parameters are stored as supplied/not automatically hashed. Customer must prevent personal identifiers in those fields. https://telemetrydeck.com/dpa/ is a public privacy framework that explicitly denies an Article 28 processor relationship for anonymous service data; it is not represented as a standard processor DPA. It says German prevails, while the displayed English is a convenience translation. No signed agreement or TOMs requested.
11. **Retention:** current paid price products promise 24 months and embedded Free object 3 months of history. https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/privacy-faq/ separately has no cold-storage deletion schedule and merely expects 7–10 years without a guarantee. It says analytics/organization metadata remain after account closure.
