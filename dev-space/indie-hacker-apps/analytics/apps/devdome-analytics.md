## Cost examples: our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: https://devdome.com/pricing/; https://devdome.com/terms-of-service/. One site, one ordinary account user, human pageviews only. All amounts are USD. Applicable VAT/sales tax is extra and depends on checkout location. Existing website/WordPress hosting is outside these hosted analytics subscription prices. The WordPress integration has no separate published license charge.

| Monthly workload | Published inputs and selected tier | Our monthly-billing calculation | Our annual-billing calculation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10,000 human pageviews | Free: 50,000/month, unlimited websites, 90-day history | 1 account × $0 = $0/month | 12 × $0 = $0; permanent Free access, not an annual paid commitment |
| 100,000 human pageviews | Pro: 500,000/month, $49/month, 13-month history | 1 × $49 = $49/month; 12 × $49 = $588 for twelve monthly payments | $49 × 12 × (1 − 0.20) = $470.40/year before tax |
| 1,000,000 human pageviews | Business: 2,000,000/month, $99/month, two-year history | 1 × $99 = $99/month; 12 × $99 = $1,188 for twelve monthly payments | $99 × 12 × (1 − 0.20) = $950.40/year before tax |

The annual figures are **our calculations from the published 20% discount**, not amounts obtained by exercising checkout. Agency's corresponding calculation is $199 × 12 × 0.80 = $1,910.40/year before tax. No rounded monthly-equivalent display is substituted for an annual total.

Bot traffic does not consume the human-pageview allowance. A workload expressed as total human-plus-bot traffic cannot be priced without the split; no split was invented. Overage grace is not treated as a lasting substitute for the right tier. One-site examples avoid the unresolved precise multi-site pooling formula. Additional events/clicks have no complete published ingestion-quota matrix here; no unlimited-ingestion promise is made.

A paid-feature free trial is not established, and the 14-day first-payment refund is not free trial time. Upgrade/downgrade execution is subject to the conflicting billing pages below. Exact tax-inclusive totals cannot be calculated without checkout information.

Recorded sessions, native active users and MRR are not converted to this pageview meter. The checked sources do not establish comparable replay, native mobile or subscription-finance tariffs. No independent self-hosted backend price is given; a GPL WordPress connector is not a fully operated local analytics platform.

## Conflicts and unknowns

1. **Upgrade timing:** the live pricing FAQ says purchasing the higher tier stacks already-paid time and the new plan begins when the previous period ends. Terms section 16 and refund-policy section 3 say upgrades apply immediately with proration. Both are retained; neither is selected as authoritative. Sources: https://devdome.com/pricing/; https://devdome.com/terms-of-service/; https://devdome.com/refund-policy/.
2. **Downgrade data:** refund-policy section 5 first says data above the new allowance is kept for 30 days, then promises complete restoration if upgraded within 60 days and possible pruning only after 60 days. Pricing says nothing is deleted after cancellation; privacy/DPA still impose plan retention and daily pruning. These pages do not reconcile the grace window and ordinary history limits. Sources: https://devdome.com/refund-policy/; https://devdome.com/pricing/; https://devdome.com/privacy-policy/; https://devdome.com/dpa/.
3. **Smaller-plan workflow:** timing is period-end in the pages checked, but pricing describes cancelling and then buying the smaller plan while refund policy says pick a downgrade in Billing. This is recorded as an unclear workflow rather than guessing which interface a customer will see. Sources: https://devdome.com/pricing/; https://devdome.com/refund-policy/.
4. **Daily ID versus lasting network hash:** daily visitor identity is site-specific and resets at midnight, but the privacy policy and DPA separately disclose a stable, shared-key network hash held for the site's retention period and used in own-account journey views. A simplified 'no lasting identifiers' description would omit that second value. No ability to identify a real person from the hash was tested. Sources: https://devdome.com/privacy-policy/; https://devdome.com/dpa/; https://devdome.com/trust/.
5. **New versus upgraded sites:** fresh WordPress installs are cookieless and exclude administrators/editors by default; existing settings can retain returning-visitor cookies. The manual tracker also does not inherit the connector's DNT-on setting automatically. Both modes are documented and source-scoped, rather than treated as conflicting defaults. Sources: https://devdome.com/wp-plugins/analytics/; https://plugins.svn.wordpress.org/devdome-analytics/trunk/devdome-analytics.php; https://plugins.svn.wordpress.org/devdome-analytics/trunk/assets/track.js.
6. **First-party delivery:** local copies and relay paths change public visibility and transport, while events still reach DevDome's hosted stores. A source comment from an old processdome/productdome snippet is not a verified former brand or a current customer installation rule. Sources: https://devdome.com/wp-plugins/analytics/; https://plugins.svn.wordpress.org/devdome-analytics/trunk/includes/class-devdome-tracker.php; https://plugins.svn.wordpress.org/devdome-analytics/trunk/assets/track.js.
7. **Demo and status:** the demo is seeded traffic, and the status page is a one-place instantaneous probe with no history or uptime calculation. Neither is independent service-quality or adoption evidence. Sources: https://devdome.com/demo/; https://devdome.com/status/.
