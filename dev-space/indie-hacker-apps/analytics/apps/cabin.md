News sweep 2026-11-09: Cabin says it became independently owned and operated after its former studio closed in January 2025. In July 2026 it replaced unlimited free pageviews with a 10,000-pageview monthly Free cap; existing legacy Pro is separate.

Cabin is a hosted web analytics service with Free, Plus and Scale plans. Its public browser tracker is MIT licensed; that does not establish an open-source or self-hostable analytics backend. Operator identity is recorded only as the company named in its policies. No private person's name or address is recorded.

Current pricing includes conditional migration notices for legacy Pro and early complimentary accounts. These public notices are not treated as information about any account belonging to this session. Plan lookback, continued recording, deletion and recovery are kept separate because their public wording differs.

No signup, login, form submission, purchase, installation, tracker execution, database access, account-data API query, MCP connection, website crawl or script-size measurement was performed. Cabin's own documented carbon-page analysis is a product feature; this research did not trigger it.
