## Cost examples — our calculations

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: current license prices at https://www.uxwizz.com/pricing ; standard optional renewal prices at https://license-api.uxwizz.com/support/prices ; capacity guidance at https://docs.uxwizz.com/installation/requirements/server-specs-cpu-ram.md ; recording and retention behavior at https://docs.uxwizz.com/api/session-recording.md and https://docs.uxwizz.com/installation/optimization-tips/auto-delete-old-data-cron-jobs.md.

These are **software-license calculations**, not fully operated hosting prices. Assume one analytics server, one dashboard end-user, websites the license permits, a new Solo perpetual license, and Ask AI disabled. All amounts are USD; new-license pricing says applicable VAT/sales tax is included. Infrastructure, backups, administration and any later external AI usage remain separate.

There is no extra per-event/pageview license charge in these examples. Active-user counts are not inferred from event counts; neither count changes the published Solo price. These calculations do not assert that the same hardware serves all six workloads.

Let `H` be the chosen monthly infrastructure/backup cost and `O` annual operations cost. **Our first-year total formula:** `$299 + 12 × H + O`. No dollar value is invented for H or O. If a single permitted server is insufficient, a higher server-count license or custom arrangement changes the software assumption.

**Our second-year examples:** with no update/support renewal, incremental software renewal payment = `$0`, while the perpetual installed version continues. With the standard 12-month Solo extension, incremental renewal payment = `1 × $49.99 = $49.99`; cumulative two-year software spend = `$299 + $49.99 = $348.99`. Infrastructure and operations continue in both cases. This is not a $0 hosted analytics plan. Renewal checkout tax/currency and any license-specific custom price may differ from the standard published list.
