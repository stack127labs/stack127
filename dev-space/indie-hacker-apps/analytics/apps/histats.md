## Cost examples: our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: the hosted offer is described as 100% free, with a stated capacity of up to 10 million hits/day, and permits up to 300 sites/account. Source: https://www.histats.com/?act=2.

Assumptions: one website, one account user, pageviews only, no additional events or duplicate tags. For capacity comparison only, assume each pageview produces one hit. Histats does not fully define its hit meter or the daily allowance's site/account scope. These examples are conditional on being within the published free offer; they do not turn an unknown meter into a confirmed one. There is no currency conversion or invented per-hit tariff.

Formula: eligible free account service fee + published paid usage charge = zero service fee + no published paid charge = zero Histats service fee within the offer. This is our calculation, not a vendor quote. Monthly pageviews are not converted into an average day to hide possible peaks.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10,000 pageviews | 10,000 hits/day if all occur in one day | Below 10 million hits/day | Zero; vendor calls the service free, with no currency tariff |
| 100,000 pageviews | 100,000 hits/day if all occur in one day | Below 10 million hits/day | Zero; same free-service assumptions |
| 1,000,000 pageviews | 1,000,000 hits/day if all occur in one day | Below 10 million hits/day | Zero; same free-service assumptions |

These totals cover the Histats service only. Website hosting, implementation time and any other analytics products are outside them.
