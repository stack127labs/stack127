## Cost examples: our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-04: one self-hosted WordPress site connected to Jetpack Stats, one dashboard user, pageviews only. Basic reports use the current [plugin Free plan](https://jetpack.com/support/jetpack-stats/free-or-paid/), which allows any site type and traffic level with full history. Advanced reports require [Paid Stats](https://jetpack.com/stats/). Website hosting, domain and site maintenance are separate costs, not included below.

The directly read localized offer displayed **₹489.95/month equivalent, ₹5,879.40 billed yearly**, with a first-year-only discount, for up to 10,000 views/month. The web fetch displayed a separate **R$37.95/month equivalent, R$455.40 billed yearly** offer. These are original vendor currencies, not conversions. [Terms](https://wordpress.com/tos/) say browsing prices are estimates based on location, final prices depend on billing country and taxes can be additional. No checkout was opened.

Alternative 10K display, **our calculation**: 1 annual entry plan × R$455.40 = **R$455.40 introductory year**, before applicable tax; R$37.95 × 12 = R$455.40. This is a separate observed localized offer, not a universal selectable price or a converted equivalent of the INR estimate.

A rounded `$8.33/month billed annually` FAQ reference does not establish an exact annual bill or its currency code. We do not silently round that into a $100 annual price. Numeric full-price renewal and higher-tier/additional-million rates remain unpublished in the public pages checked.

Quota inputs and limits from [tier billing](https://jetpack.com/support/jetpack-stats/free-or-paid/paid-stats-tier-billing/):

- Standard bands: 10K, 100K, 250K, 500K and 1M views/month; above 1M adds a charge per extra million, amount not public.
- Two highest-traffic days are excluded from monthly quota calculation. The examples choose enough quota to cover the stated total without relying on that forgiveness. Concentrated spikes can change the required tier; no daily traffic distribution is assumed.
- Bundle capacity can add to a standalone allowance. For example 100K Complete + 250K standalone = 350K views/month, **our capacity calculation**, matching the vendor example. This does not give a bundle price.
- Three consecutive over-limit months can lead to limited report access without an upgrade. Full collection continues; this is not a documented automatic per-view charge.
- Exact operating total remains **not exact** because hosting, tax, future renewal and unavailable paid-band prices are not known.

No event/session/user conversion is used. No replay/native-mobile/MRR price scenario is prepared: those jobs are not established as this product's core metered offering. WordAds or WooCommerce revenue views do not supply a general subscription-accounting price model.
