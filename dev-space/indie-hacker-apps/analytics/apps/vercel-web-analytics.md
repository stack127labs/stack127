## Cost examples: our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05:

- One Vercel project, one deploying dashboard user, one monthly billing period.
- [Event tariff](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/limits-and-pricing.md): USD $0.03 per 1,000 collected events on Pro; pageviews and custom events share the meter.
- [Pro platform](https://vercel.com/docs/plans/pro-plan.md): USD $20/month includes the first deploying seat and $20/month shared usage credit. It is not an event-count allowance.
- [Web Analytics Plus](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/limits-and-pricing.md): optional USD $10/month/team fixed add-on; same event rate, 24-month reports, eight properties and UTM reporting.
- [Tax/pricing policy](https://vercel.com/pricing): USD before applicable VAT/GST/other taxes.
- No Drains, additional team seats, marketplace integrations or other add-ons in these base scenarios.

Let `E` be the total monthly pageviews plus custom events. Let `U` be other usage billed against the shared Pro credit.

`Analytics gross usage A = (E / 1,000) × $0.03`

`Pro platform + metered usage = $20 + max(0, A + U − $20)`

`Pro with Web Analytics Plus = $20 + $10 + max(0, A + U − $20)`

These are **our calculations** from the published tariff and shared credit. The examples below set `U = $0` to show the credit effect clearly. That is an explicit scenario, not a promise that hosting a real site consumes no other billable resources. Fixed seats/add-ons are separate from metered budget usage; see [Spend Management](https://vercel.com/docs/spend-management.md).

### Website traffic scenarios: our calculation

Pageviews only, zero custom events:

| Monthly pageviews | Gross Analytics usage formula | Gross Analytics usage | Pro scenario, U = $0 | Pro + Plus scenario, U = $0 |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10,000 | 10,000 / 1,000 × $0.03 | USD $0.30/month | $20 + max(0, $0.30 − $20) = **USD $20/month** | $30 + max(0, $0.30 − $20) = **USD $30/month** |
| 100,000 | 100,000 / 1,000 × $0.03 | USD $3.00/month | $20 + max(0, $3 − $20) = **USD $20/month** | $30 + max(0, $3 − $20) = **USD $30/month** |
| 1,000,000 | 1,000,000 / 1,000 × $0.03 | USD $30.00/month | $20 + max(0, $30 − $20) = **USD $30/month** | $30 + max(0, $30 − $20) = **USD $40/month** |

If other resources already consume the whole $20 credit, Analytics adds its gross $0.30/$3/$30 metered cost instead. The real combined invoice also includes those other resources, so it cannot be calculated exactly here.

[Hobby](https://vercel.com/docs/plans/hobby.md) costs USD $0 and includes 50,000 Analytics events/month, but is only for personal/non-commercial projects. A 10,000-pageview, zero-custom-event scenario fits that Analytics allowance. A 100,000- or 1,000,000-pageview month is **not fully covered by the free allowance**. Grace-period collection must not be sold as a guaranteed full-month free service; pause/reset wording is conflicting.

### Custom-event scenarios: our calculation

Pro/Enterprise support custom events; Hobby does not. These scenarios contain only the stated custom events and **zero pageviews**. They do not assume any active-user count or events per visitor.

| Monthly custom events | Gross usage formula and result | Pro scenario, U = $0 | Pro + Plus scenario, U = $0 |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 100,000 | 100,000 / 1,000 × $0.03 = USD $3/month | USD $20/month | USD $30/month |
| 1,000,000 | 1,000,000 / 1,000 × $0.03 = USD $30/month | USD $30/month | USD $40/month |
| 10,000,000 | 10,000,000 / 1,000 × $0.03 = USD $300/month | USD $300/month | USD $310/month |

A mixed workload of 100,000 pageviews + 100,000 custom events = 200,000 total events. **Our calculation:** 200,000 / 1,000 × $0.03 = USD $6 gross usage/month; with unused shared credit and one deploying seat, the Pro scenario is USD $20/month or USD $30 with Plus.

Pro's two-property limit versus Plus/Enterprise eight-property limit can determine which edition fits these events. No event-to-user, pageview-to-session or revenue conversion is used.

### Export and incomplete totals

[Drains](https://vercel.com/docs/drains.md) add USD $0.50 × billed GB, where billed bytes are uncompressed serialized JSON records. **Our formula**, not a priced event-volume estimate: `Drain fee = G × $0.50`, where `G` is unknown billed gigabytes. Destination storage/ingestion can add costs. Without record sizes and configured sampling, there is no exact event-to-GB total.

Enterprise is quote-priced. Real full bills remain **not exact** without other metered hosting usage, tax, extra seats, chosen add-ons and any export-destination costs. The numeric scenario totals above are conditional on their stated inputs.
