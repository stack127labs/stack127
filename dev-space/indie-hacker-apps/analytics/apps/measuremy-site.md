## Cost examples: our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: https://measuremy.site/pricing; https://measuremy.site/terms. One site, one user, pageviews only, no badge assumed. Prices are retained with the published `$` symbol: the checked pages do not identify an ISO currency code or clearly state whether tax is included. These are therefore exact displayed-plan arithmetic, not confirmed USD or tax-inclusive checkout totals.

| Monthly workload | Published inputs | Our monthly-billing calculation | Our annual-billing calculation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10,000 pageviews | Starter: 100,000 pageviews/month, three sites, $9/month or $90/year | 1 subscription × $9 = $9/month; 12 × $9 = $108 for twelve monthly payments | 1 subscription × $90 = $90/year, paid yearly |
| 100,000 pageviews | Starter: 100,000 pageviews/month, three sites, $9/month or $90/year | 1 × $9 = $9/month; 12 × $9 = $108 for twelve monthly payments | 1 × $90 = $90/year, paid yearly |
| 1,000,000 pageviews | Scale: 2,000,000 pageviews/month, 25 sites, $79/month or $790/year | 1 × $79 = $79/month; 12 × $79 = $948 for twelve monthly payments | 1 × $790 = $790/year, paid yearly |

Every hosted feature is included. The one-site workload avoids unresolved multi-site pooling. Custom events do not count toward the pageview allowance, so no event-to-pageview ratio is needed. This does not establish unlimited ingestion or the absence of separate rate/abuse limits.

The badge changes capacity, not the published subscription price. Our calculation is base allowance × 1.5: Starter 150,000, Growth 750,000 and Scale 3,000,000 pageviews/month while the badge qualifies. Growth with the badge still cannot cover one million pageviews, so Scale remains the applicable published tier for that example. The badge can also add 30 trial days once; our calculation is 30 base days + 30 additional days = 60 conditional trial days. Source: https://measuremy.site/pricing.

For the self-hosted edition at each of 10,000, 100,000 and 1,000,000 monthly pageviews, the known software-license charge is zero. Our cost formula is **software charge 0 + Cloudflare compute/database/storage and any other operating costs**. An exact total cannot be calculated from pageviews alone without infrastructure usage, custom-event load, query frequency, retention and account allowances. The vendor's claim that small sites often fit a free Cloudflare allowance is not a checked infrastructure quote. Sources: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/developeron29/measuremysite-mcp/main/README.md; https://raw.githubusercontent.com/developeron29/measuremysite-mcp/main/LICENSE; https://raw.githubusercontent.com/developeron29/measuremysite-mcp/main/wrangler.jsonc. No currency conversion, hosting estimate or operator labor estimate was invented.

Event totals, recorded sessions, native active users and MRR are not substituted for the published pageview meter. This product does not establish native mobile/replay/revenue-accounting workloads that could be priced comparably here.

## Conflicts and unknowns

No unresolved direct contradiction was established. The main distinctions are conditional or edition-specific:

- **Names:** the old agentlytics-mcp name applies to the source project and retained API/worker names. It is not a separate directory app. Sources: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/developeron29/measuremysite-mcp/main/README.md; https://raw.githubusercontent.com/developeron29/measuremysite-mcp/main/package.json.
- **Consent wording:** privacy/home state that this tracker needs no banner; the cookieless guide and GDPR article explain that location, legal basis and other site behavior still matter. These qualifications are preserved instead of creating a universal legal-compliance badge. Sources: https://measuremy.site/privacy; https://measuremy.site/guides/cookieless-analytics; https://measuremy.site/blog/gdpr-and-website-analytics-what-small-sites-should-know.
- **Daily identifiers:** hosted privacy describes a changing daily secret/salt. The source collector derives a daily salt from a configured root secret and UTC day. These describe different editions and do not establish that the hosted code uses the exact public implementation. Sources: https://measuremy.site/privacy; https://raw.githubusercontent.com/developeron29/measuremysite-mcp/main/src/collect.ts.
- **Referrers and URLs:** hosted policy allows a sanitized linking-page address. The public source collector stores only the referrer hostname, and its browser tracker sends a full page URL before the collector stores the path/selected UTM tags. Stored minimization is not the same as never transmitting the original URL. Sources: https://measuremy.site/privacy; https://raw.githubusercontent.com/developeron29/measuremysite-mcp/main/src/tracker.ts; https://raw.githubusercontent.com/developeron29/measuremysite-mcp/main/src/collect.ts.
- **Retention:** immediate database deletion and up-to-30-day backups are explicitly different stages. Raw records, hourly aggregates and unused unpaid accounts also have different rules. No single history number is applied to all of them. Source: https://measuremy.site/privacy.
- **Permissions:** normal keys named read keys can create campaigns/funnels/annotations and change public sharing through MCP. The separately published demo key is read-only. No normal key is described as universally read-only. Source: https://measuremy.site/docs/mcp.
- **Traffic quality:** known bot filtering and a public demo's likely-bot warning can coexist. No zero-bot guarantee or measured completeness score is inferred. Sources: https://measuremy.site/guides/cookieless-analytics; https://raw.githubusercontent.com/developeron29/measuremysite-mcp/main/src/collect.ts; https://measuremy.site/share/measuremy.site.
