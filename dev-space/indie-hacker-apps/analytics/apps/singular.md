## Cost examples: our calculation

These are **conversion-rate components, not guaranteed total invoices**. The published Growth rate is $0.05 per conversion. Standard terms say USD unless a Service Entitlement specifies another currency. Inputs: conversions that are billable under the agreed counting rule, the public rate, no remaining free balance applied, before tax, optional products or contract minimums. Formula: **billable conversions × USD $0.05**. Sources: https://www.singular.net/pricing/ ; https://www.singular.net/terms/ ; https://support.singular.net/hc/en-us/articles/20605832449307-Singular-s-FREE-Tier-FAQ (checked 2026-11-05).

| Explicit workload during an illustrative month | Formula: our calculation | Known component | Exact total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 billable conversions | 1,000 × USD $0.05 | USD $50 | Not established without order/minimum/add-on terms |
| 10,000 billable conversions | 10,000 × USD $0.05 | USD $500 | Not established without order/minimum/add-on terms |
| 100,000 billable conversions | 100,000 × USD $0.05 | USD $5,000 | Not established without order/minimum/add-on terms |

A new Free/Starter account receives **15,000 starting attributed conversions**, not a documented 15,000 monthly reset. Within that remaining allowance, the advertised conversion charge is USD $0: for example, 1,000 eligible conversions × USD $0 = USD $0, provided the account still has at least 1,000 free conversions remaining. That does not include Enterprise features. On reaching the cap, dashboard access is blocked until purchase, while collection continues. Do not assume that unused Starter credits carry into a new paid contract or that paid access starts automatically. Source: https://support.singular.net/hc/en-us/articles/20605832449307-Singular-s-FREE-Tier-FAQ ; https://www.singular.net/pricing/ (checked 2026-11-05).

The directory’s **1,000/10,000/100,000 MAU**, **100,000/1,000,000/10,000,000 events**, and **10,000/100,000/1,000,000 pageviews** are `not_comparable` from this tariff: none specifies attributed conversions. Likewise, **US$1,000/US$10,000/US$50,000 MRR** does not specify ad spend or attributed conversions. Ad-spend contracts, Enterprise web measurement, ETL and Private Cloud need commercial terms. Replay-session workloads cannot be priced because that product was not established. No conversions-per-user assumption or currency conversion was made. Sources: https://www.singular.net/pricing/ ; https://support.singular.net/hc/en-us/articles/20605832449307-Singular-s-FREE-Tier-FAQ ; https://support.singular.net/hc/en-us/articles/360041876791-Web-Attribution-FAQ ; https://support.singular.net/hc/en-us/articles/360037917571-Singular-ETL-FAQ-and-Troubleshooting (checked 2026-11-05).
