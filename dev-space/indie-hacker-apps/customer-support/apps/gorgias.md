## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked **2026-11-04**: [current pricing](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing), [ticket definition and overages](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/how-youre-billed-for-using-gorgias-199385), [human chat availability](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/configure-chat-for-your-gorgias-helpdesk-81789), [MSA/tax terms](https://www.gorgias.com/legal/terms-of-service). Amounts are **USD**, before applicable taxes, without currency conversion.

The cheapest ongoing published fee is **Starter USD 10/account/month**, covering **50 replied Helpdesk tickets and three users**. Email and human website chat are supported. No ongoing free offer was established. AI Agent and optional self-service automations are off for this base comparison.

Task-matched inputs: one product; one support address for email or one widget for website chat; 100 distinct customer conversations that each receive a human reply during the month; one or three working people including owner. Treat those as 100 billable tickets only under these conditions: no later inactivity splits, no extra replied threads and no optional automation events. A generated support address is sufficient; exact Starter own-domain/OAuth setup availability is not assumed.

| Task / people | Cheapest ongoing suitable plan | Monthly cash calculation for 100 tickets | Annual upfront purchase | Annual alternative |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Email, one person | Starter | USD 10 + (100 − 50) × USD 0.40 = **USD 30/month** | No annual Starter plan published | Basic **USD 600 upfront/year**, equivalent USD 50/month |
| Website chat, one person | Starter | USD 10 + 50 × USD 0.40 = **USD 30/month** | No annual Starter plan published | Basic **USD 600 upfront/year**, equivalent USD 50/month |
| Email, three people | Starter | USD 10 + 50 × USD 0.40 = **USD 30/month** | No annual Starter plan published | Basic **USD 600 upfront/year**, equivalent USD 50/month |
| Website chat, three people | Starter | USD 10 + 50 × USD 0.40 = **USD 30/month** | No annual Starter plan published | Basic **USD 600 upfront/year**, equivalent USD 50/month |

Basic has **300 tickets/month and 500 included seats** in the current pricing comparison, so its annual alternative is **USD 50 × 12 = USD 600**, with no staff multiplier for one or three users. At this 100-ticket workload, Starter with overages costs less. Twelve unchanged Starter monthly bills would sum to **USD 30 × 12 = USD 360**; that is a year of monthly payments, **not an annual upfront price**. [Pricing](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing), [billable events](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/how-youre-billed-for-using-gorgias-199385).

Store subscriptions, external email-provider fees, taxes, Voice/SMS, WhatsApp business charges, professional services and other apps are outside these totals. Human chat can be installed on an ordinary website; AI Agent's Shopify requirement does not apply to that base chat scenario. Pricing's “included” AI interactions belong to paid AI-on packages, not the AI-off Helpdesk bill. [Chat setup](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/configure-chat-for-your-gorgias-helpdesk-81789), [pricing](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing).

### AI examples — our conditional calculation

Use the **published monthly AI-on Starter preset**, not an assumed free allowance: USD 10 Helpdesk + USD 30 AI package, with 50 Helpdesk tickets and 30 automated interactions. Extra Starter tickets cost USD 0.40 each; extra AI interactions cost USD 1.50. Assume every example interaction is a full **AI Agent resolution with no human within 72 hours**, so each also uses one Helpdesk ticket. One or three people cost the same. Assume an existing eligible Shopify store; its subscription is not included. [Preset and excess prices](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing), [current billing rules](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/how-youre-billed-for-using-gorgias-199385).

Formula: **Helpdesk base + AI package + max(0, AI-resolved tickets − included Helpdesk tickets) × ticket excess rate + max(0, AI resolutions − included AI interactions) × AI excess rate**.

| Conditional monthly scenario | Inputs and formula — our calculation | Gorgias subtotal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter preset, 100 AI-resolved tickets | USD 10 + USD 30 + (100 − 50) × USD 0.40 + (100 − 30) × USD 1.50 | **USD 165** |
| Starter preset, 500 AI-resolved tickets | USD 10 + USD 30 + (500 − 50) × USD 0.40 + (500 − 30) × USD 1.50 | **USD 925** |
| Basic preset alternative, 500 AI-resolved tickets | USD 60 + USD 30 + (500 − 300) × USD 0.40 + (500 − 30) × USD 1.50 | **USD 875** |

These are exact arithmetic for the named **presets and assumptions**, not a promise that the presets are the cheapest 100/500-interaction packages. Billing settings allow selecting different usage allowances; a tailored package may cost less. They expand the base workload rather than squeezing 500 resolutions into 100 conversations. Non-AI self-service can have different ticket-fee treatment. A final negotiated bill, other AI quantities, outside Shopify fees and tax remain outside these examples.
