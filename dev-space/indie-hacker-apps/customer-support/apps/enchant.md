## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: [current pricing](https://www.enchant.com/pricing), [USD base price](https://www.enchant.com/contact-center-software), [terms/tax basis](https://www.enchant.com/terms), [AI billing definitions](https://help.enchant.com/article/217/enchant-ai-pricing), [AI Agent beta cap](https://help.enchant.com/article/230/ai-agent).

Scenario: one product, one support email address, 100 customer conversations/month, one or three working users including owner. AI is off in the base example. No assumed conversion between conversations, customers helped, messages and AI resolutions. Taxes, phone/SMS/WhatsApp usage, outside subscriptions and optional branding/security add-ons are excluded.

| Task | One working agent | Three working agents | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cheapest ongoing supported task | 1 Free account × USD 0 = USD 0 | Free does not fit 3 users; 3 × USD 20 = USD 60/month | Free supports Messenger/chat/KB within 2-user and 3-Messenger/KB limits; numerical history/volume caps not established |
| Paid email task | 1 × USD 20 = USD 20/month | 3 × USD 20 = USD 60/month | Native email channel; forwarding and valid sending configuration required; 100 conversations is our scenario, not a published allowance |
| Paid website-chat task | 1 × USD 20 = USD 20/month | 3 × USD 20 = USD 60/month | Native Messenger included; Free is also an ongoing solo option above |
| Twelve-month budget, monthly payments | 12 × USD 20 = USD 240 | 12 × USD 60 = USD 720 | Not an annual-payment plan or price lock; future rate/user changes excluded |

**AI examples:** suppose the average number of customers helped in the preceding three calendar months is exactly 100 or exactly 500. Both are within the documented first 0–500 tier. The AI line is `1 tier × $30 = $30/month`. The known base-plus-AI expression is `USD 20 + $30/month` for one working user or `USD 60 + $30/month` for three. If all add-on dollars use the base tariff's USD, those totals are **USD 50/month** and **USD 90/month**, before tax. The add-on's own lines do not restate the currency code, so those combined USD totals retain that explicit condition. The first account months' averaging rule and exact expanded feature quotas remain unpublished.

These are customer-volume tier examples, not buying 100/500 AI chats. Standard counts customers whether or not AI helps them. Agent beta permits up to 100 chats/month at no additional Agent charge for eligible AI Standard customers. There is no published route/priced formula to 500 autonomous Agent chats under this beta. Starter's unknown numerical limits prevent a guaranteed free 100-unit example.

Optional displayed charges: Advanced Security `$5 × working users/month`; Remove Branding `$50/month`. For a paid one-user account, the known branding expression is `USD 20 + $50/month`; for three users, `USD 60 + $50/month`. Neither is needed for the base support task. No annual contract is published. External channel charges depend on provider/country/use and cannot be made exact from the chosen scenario.
