## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked November 5, 2026: [current prices, seats, profiles, and VAT](https://crisp.chat/en/pricing/), [Free setup](https://help.crisp.chat/en/article/getting-started-with-the-free-plan-1rjhsh5/), [Mini mailbox/chat scope](https://help.crisp.chat/en/article/getting-started-with-the-mini-plan-1g3m98/), [annual prepayment](https://help.crisp.chat/en/article/how-to-switch-to-a-yearly-subscription-billing-hbwqet/), and [Hugo credit billing](https://help.crisp.chat/en/article/how-does-hugo-ai-pricing-and-billing-works-h78hkv/).

Base comparison: one product/workspace, one support address where email is required, 100 customer conversations/month, owner included in one/three working operators, AI off. Assume a new/small contact database within the selected profile allowance. A conversation is not a new profile; Free's 100 profiles include stored customers, not a guaranteed fresh allocation per month. Prices exclude VAT and existing mailbox/domain costs. Optional phone/SMS/WhatsApp providers and integrations are off.
