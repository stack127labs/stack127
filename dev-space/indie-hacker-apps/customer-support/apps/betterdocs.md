[Vendor refund policy](https://wpdeveloper.com/refund-policy/) requires an unresolved unusable bug and excludes change of mind or mistakes. The [Shopify page](https://betterdocs.co/shopify/) separately advertises a seven-day money-back promise. These are edition-specific offer statements, not a single universal trial guarantee.
- **Shopify AI entitlement conflict:** [App Store Free](https://apps.shopify.com/betterdocs-knowledgebase) includes AI Autowrite; the [vendor Shopify page](https://betterdocs.co/shopify/) places Magic AI Autowrite under Pro. [Setup guide](https://betterdocs.co/docs/magic-ai-autowrite-feature/) requires the merchant’s OpenAI key but does not settle the plan difference. Its [configuration guide](https://betterdocs.co/docs/how-to-configure-betterdocs-ai-chatbot/) additionally documents Gemini and auto-sync. WordPress writing, Pro search, the paid chatbot and Shopify authoring remain separate capabilities.
- **Pricing/currency/renewal:** direct live [pricing](https://betterdocs.co/pricing/) was used over older indexed discounts. Checked [terms](https://wpdeveloper.com/terms-and-conditions/), [cancellation](https://wpdeveloper.com/docs/cancel-my-subscription/), [expiry](https://wpdeveloper.com/docs/plugin-work-not-renew-license/) and the public [checkout shell](https://store.wpdeveloper.com/checkout/).
