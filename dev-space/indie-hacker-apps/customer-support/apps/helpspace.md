## Cost examples — our calculation

All inputs checked 2026-11-05: [USD pricing and limits](https://helpspace.com/pricing), [EUR pricing](https://helpspace.com/de/preise), [official monthly/annual pricing component](https://framerusercontent.com/sites/2HKwDB0A8wwm5b9voXZiHB/FfWdlbR0HsZV084Vw0GuYVSy3r5nt8wS7GNtlhZN0N4.DJGFoE-i.mjs), [subscription cadence](https://supportcenter.helpspace.com/en/article/39/create-a-subscription), [terms/currency/taxes](https://legal.helpspace.com/en/terms-conditions), [email channel](https://supportcenter.helpspace.com/en/article/29/manage-channels).

Scenario: one product, one support email address, 100 customer conversations/month, one or three working agents including the owner. AI off. No permanent free offer is established. Cheapest ongoing and paid task-matched email both use Starter; its two-user minimum exceeds the one-person workload. Starter includes two email channels, one Docs site, two boards, unlimited widgets/articles/customers/quick replies, and team ticket work. No numeric ticket/storage cap or per-conversation tariff was established; 100 conversations is our scenario, not a vendor-guaranteed allowance.

**AI examples:** native AI requires Business, minimum five paid users even for one or three working agents. Known base formula is `max(working users, 5) × USD 45 = USD 225/month` or `5 × USD 40 × 12 = USD 2,400/year`, before tax. Both workload sizes have this same minimum base. No separate native-AI line item is published, but no quota/unit supports a guaranteed price for 100 or 500 drafts/messages/resolutions. The honest formula is `required Business base + any applicable usage charges`; do not equate feature inclusion with unlimited use. The same published euro minimum computes to EUR 225/month or EUR 2,400/year, without conversion.

**Branding:** Business is the first plan whose comparison marks No Powered By attribution available. Its minimum is the Business amount above; the exact removal scope across Docs, widget and outgoing email remains unclear. This is not a promised complete white-label price for every customer-facing surface.

**Separate integrations:** automatic article translation needs Team/Business plus a DeepL API key. branchly chatbot uses a separate branchly account and Docs-only integration token. Neither outside-service cost is silently included in native HelpSpace AI examples.
