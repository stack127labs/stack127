## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked on 2026-11-05: https://fin.ai/pricing ; https://fin.ai/help/en/articles/13975800-fin-pricing-outcomes ; https://fin.ai/help/en/articles/13975799-faqs . These examples use the published US$49/month / 50-included external-helpdesk offer, not an assumed US$49.50 commitment or an Intercom seat bundle.

### Base and required helpdesk

- One working person: subscribed Fin component US$49/month, plus H1, the price of the selected one-person human helpdesk/channel setup. With Fin paused/AI off, no new outcomes are assumed, but the subscribed minimum remains a commitment. Exact H1 and complete all-in total are unknown.
- Three working people: Fin component remains US$49/month, plus H3 for three working humans in their helpdesk. There are no additional Fin teammate-seat fees. Owner is included in three; H3 is not presumed to be 3 × H1.
- Twelve unchanged minimum monthly payments: 12 × US$49 = US$588 Fin-only. This is our annualized cash example for twelve monthly subscriptions, not a published annual-payment plan/discount. A separate annual Fin offer/contract invoice was not established.
- Website AI: generic Fin Messenger can work without an Intercom human inbox, using URL/JavaScript/API handoff or no-human informational mode. The named US$49 offer is Fin for existing helpdesks; no separate generic/API contract price is assumed if Sales requires different terms. A do-nothing informational flow cannot meet the human-email-support task.
- Fin + Intercom: fresh pricing advertises US$19/Full seat/month for a new-customer offer plus outcome usage. Its precise eligible cadence/offer and the Fin allowance/minimum for that bundle are not assumed from standalone Fin. No old Intercom-file price or entitlement was reused; obtain the applicable order for an exact one/three-seat combined bill.

### Support outcome arithmetic and vendor error

Formula from the published plan inputs: **Fin monthly component = US$49 + max(0, support outcomes − 50) × US$0.99.** One and three Fin users have the same Fin component. H1/H3, channel/provider bills, tax and optional add-ons remain additional.
