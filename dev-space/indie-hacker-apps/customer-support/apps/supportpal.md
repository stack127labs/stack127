## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: [current core/add-on prices](https://www.supportpal.com/product/pricing), [licence scope](https://www.supportpal.com/company/eula), [free Chat add-on](https://marketplace.supportpal.com/channels/chat), [server needs](https://docs.supportpal.com/current/System+Requirements), and [realtime configuration](https://docs.supportpal.com/current/Broadcasting+Configuration). Prices are USD except the separately noted dollar-only Twilio listing. All approximate monthly equivalents below are explicitly rounded to two decimal places; invoices use published exact cash prices.

| Scenario | Inputs and formula | Monthly licence cash | Annual licence cash | Annual licence ÷ 12 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cheapest ongoing solo email support | 1 core installation, 1 included brand, 1 operator | USD 29.95 | USD 299.95 upfront | 299.95 ÷ 12 ≈ USD 25.00/month |
| Three working operators, email | Same installation/brand; unlimited operators | USD 29.95 | USD 299.95 upfront | ≈ USD 25.00/month |
| Solo email plus website messaging | Core + free Chat channel; 1 widget, AI off | 29.95 + 0 = USD 29.95 | 299.95 + 0 = USD 299.95 upfront | ≈ USD 25.00/month |
| Three operators, email plus website messaging | Same core/brand/widget; AI off | USD 29.95 | USD 299.95 upfront | ≈ USD 25.00/month |
| Optional attribution-removal version of any above | Core + removal add-on | 29.95 + 4.95 = USD 34.90 | 299.95 + 49.95 = USD 349.90 upfront | 349.90 ÷ 12 ≈ USD 29.16/month |
| Optional second brand | Core + 1 additional brand | 29.95 + 9.95 = USD 39.90 | 299.95 + 99.95 = USD 399.90 upfront | 399.90 ÷ 12 ≈ USD 33.33/month |

One product, one support address and 100 customer conversations/month are assumed. There is no licence ticket/operator counter to convert into a conversation overage. The owner counts among the operators, and all three can work normally; no restricted viewer substitution. The core fee applies once, not once per agent. Chat is persistent website messaging with real-time interaction; it retains asynchronous return-later behavior rather than guaranteeing that a human is online.

**These are software-licence totals, not full running bills.** A complete total is the licence plus actual server/database/storage, backup, email, realtime transport, maintenance and tax costs. Server needs depend on workload; no hosting provider, configuration quote or labour rate was supplied. Default polling avoids a mandatory external realtime subscription, but consumes server/bandwidth resources; optional Pusher-compatible WebSockets can have separate infrastructure/service costs. One normal internal/external IP configuration is assumed. A multi-IP option is priced, but its per-purchase IP quantity is not explicit enough to invent a clustered deployment total.

There is no current perpetual purchase option for a new buyer. A historical owned licence and its support renewal should not be treated as a cheaper available one-time purchase. A 30-day evaluation is not an ongoing free plan and is not licensed for production.

For AI, the [Assistant plugin](https://marketplace.supportpal.com/plugins/ai-assistant) adds **USD 0 in SupportPal plugin licence charges**. For 100 or 500 generated replies, the total remains **base licence + selected model/embedding usage + vector storage/compute**, with unknown amounts. The [setup guide](https://docs.supportpal.com/current/AI+Assistant+Plugin) allows separate LLM and embedding providers, local Ollama, and local/cloud vector storage. It publishes no fixed tokens per reply, required model mix or common response allowance. A local model can avoid a hosted model-provider usage bill but does not remove compute, storage, power or administration. No exact 100/500-answer total is manufactured.

Twilio messaging/voice/WhatsApp would add the [marketplace's displayed $5/month or $50/year channel subscription](https://marketplace.supportpal.com/channels/twilio), plus provider number/usage costs. The listing does not independently name its currency code; it is not included in USD email/web-chat calculations. Remote help at USD 75/30 minutes is optional, not a guessed setup requirement. No currency conversion was performed.
