## Cost examples: our calculation

Inputs were checked on 2026-11-04: [Omni pricing](https://hiverhq.com/pricing/hiver-omni), [Gmail pricing](https://hiverhq.com/pricing/hiver-in-gmail), [Omni purchase guide](https://help.hiverhq.com/admin-setup/manage-subscription?product=hiver-omni), [Gmail purchase guide](https://help.hiverhq.com/admin-setup/purchase-your-hiver-subscription) and [terms](https://hiverhq.com/terms). Terms specify USD and exclude tax, processing and conversion charges. No conversion is performed.

### Cheapest ongoing route

A current zero-cost working route cannot be confirmed: [a live official comparison](https://hiverhq.com/blog/front-pricing) advertises Free, while both current edition pricing pages show paid plans and suspension after seven trial days. No eligibility/cohort explanation was found. Do not advertise a confirmed cheapest ongoing USD 0 plan.

The lowest current paid headline is Growth, with at least two seats on current pricing. That makes the public starting subscription USD 70 monthly or USD 600 for twelve months at the annual rate. A single working founder pays for spare capacity. The Gmail purchasing guide instead starts at five seats; that conflict is material and not silently discarded.

### Paid email and human website chat

Use Hiver Omni Growth for one product, one support email address and 100 monthly conversations, AI off. For the chat example use one website widget with the same team, AI off. Omni includes email and live chat on Growth. No separate mandatory human-chat add-on is published. The table gives the known subscription calculation; a complete invoice still depends on the unresolved seat rules, taxes/fees and any unpublished usage conditions.

| Actual working people, including owner | Seats under current pricing schedule | Monthly-payment formula and result | Annual formula and twelve-month payment | Annual amount / 12 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 | 2 × USD 35 = USD 70/month | 2 × USD 25 × 12 = USD 600/year | USD 50/month equivalent |
| 3 | 5 | 5 × USD 35 = USD 175/month | 5 × USD 25 × 12 = USD 1,500/year | USD 125/month equivalent |

The same known Omni subscription base applies to the stated email and human-chat tasks; this is not a separate charge per 100 conversations. Annual billing is a paid twelve-month purchase, not twelve monthly payments at the equivalent rate. Email hosting/domain fees remain separate. Growth includes two shared inboxes, so one email inbox and one chat inbox fit that published count without assuming unlimited channels or sites.

**Seat uncertainty:** pricing says two, then five, then increments of five. [Gmail purchasing](https://help.hiverhq.com/admin-setup/purchase-your-hiver-subscription) says five, ten, fifteen. Terms 8.3 generally says to pay the whole purchased tier and move up when its maximum is exceeded, but its examples say seven users pay at the five-seat tier and eight users can pay the five-seat tier. These examples contradict a straightforward upper-limit interpretation. The table uses current pricing's explicit schedule; it is not a verified invoice or an inference that another tier boundary is certainly applied in checkout.

For a Gmail-edition purchase following its five-seat guide, **our alternative calculation** is USD 175/month monthly, or USD 1,500/year at the annual rate, equivalent to USD 125/month, for either one or three working people. Its current pricing page's two-seat minimum would lower the one-person example to the two-seat amounts above.
