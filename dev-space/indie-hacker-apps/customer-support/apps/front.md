## Cost examples: our calculation

Inputs checked on 2026-11-04: [current seat rates](https://front.com/pricing), [USD currency and one-seat minimum](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2178), [Resolve without AI](https://help.front.com/en/articles/4836160) and [tax/advance-payment terms](https://front.com/legal/saas-services-agreement). Monthly values were read from the live pricing page's embedded `monthlyPrice` values: Starter 35, Professional 85; Enterprise says annual only.

The cheapest published ongoing working-agent offer is Starter. There is no established permanent free agent plan. Each row uses 100 conversations/month and AI off; it is a separate scenario, not a combined Starter email-and-chat package.

| Task | Full working agents | Monthly-payment calculation | Annual payment calculation | Annual payment divided by 12 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One support email address | 1 | 1 × USD 35 = USD 35/month | 1 × USD 25 × 12 = USD 300/year | USD 25/month equivalent |
| One support email address | 3 | 3 × USD 35 = USD 105/month | 3 × USD 25 × 12 = USD 900/year | USD 75/month equivalent |
| One human Resolve chat widget | 1 | 1 × USD 35 = USD 35/month | 1 × USD 25 × 12 = USD 300/year | USD 25/month equivalent |
| One human Resolve chat widget | 3 | 3 × USD 35 = USD 105/month | 3 × USD 25 × 12 = USD 900/year | USD 75/month equivalent |

For email and Resolve together, Professional is the lowest applicable plan: one agent is 1 × USD 85 = USD 85/month, or 1 × USD 65 × 12 = USD 780/year; three agents are 3 × USD 85 = USD 255/month, or 3 × USD 65 × 12 = USD 2,340/year. Annual equivalents are USD 65 and USD 195/month. These are additional comparison scenarios, not extra subscriptions to add to the Starter table.

Annual fees are ordinarily paid in advance for the term, subject to an Order Form or other agreed terms. Rates exclude taxes. External email/telephony accounts, WhatsApp traffic, implementation and optional AI are not included. The mandatory onboarding threshold exceeds these examples. Guest accounts cannot replace replying agents.

No ordinary per-conversation charge was located for these non-AI scenarios. However, the [Usage Policy](https://help.front.com/en/articles/3506304) states optimal processing of 300 messages/seat/day and possible throttling beyond it. A conversation may contain many messages. Without message count and daily distribution, the seat total is exact but an unconditional all-in operating cost is not.

Optional AI example: [Autopilot](https://help.front.com/en/articles/4890624) is billed monthly by achieved outcome. The following uses qualifying resolutions after the AI trial, no volume agreement, no Copilot and no other billable conversations. These are separate AI-volume scenarios; the 500-resolution case replaces the base table's 100-conversation volume. A conversation is billed once at the highest outcome reached; triage/handoff are not stacked on top of its resolution charge.

| Qualifying monthly resolutions | Extra AI calculation | One annual Starter seat equivalent + AI | Three annual Starter seats equivalent + AI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 100 | 100 × USD 0.89 = USD 89 | USD 25 + 89 = USD 114/month equivalent | USD 75 + 89 = USD 164/month equivalent |
| 500 | 500 × USD 0.89 = USD 445 | USD 25 + 445 = USD 470/month equivalent | USD 75 + 445 = USD 520/month equivalent |

On monthly-paid Starter, replace the USD 25/75 base with USD 35/105: totals become USD 124/194 for 100 resolutions and USD 480/550 for 500. Our calculation keeps the annual subscription payment and later monthly AI invoice separate. Resolution qualifications include the channel-specific 24/72-hour human-follow-up window; 100 incoming conversations are not automatically 100 charged resolutions. An external agent's own model and hosting bill cannot be calculated from these native Autopilot prices.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Starter Slack:** the [Slack guide](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2053) says all plans and the [pricing matrix](https://front.com/pricing) permits single-type Slack, but the FAQ on that pricing page limits Starter choices to email, Resolve or SMS. The cost examples avoid this disputed channel.
- **Copilot access and daily translation:** [Copilot documentation](https://help.front.com/en/articles/4848832) sells the USD 20 add-on on Starter/Professional, while the [Usage Policy](https://help.front.com/en/articles/3506304) says no access for purchases after April 28, 2026. The same policy says 1,500 daily Translate requests, against pricing's 200. Neither discrepancy is silently resolved using an older legacy quota.
- **Reporting history and API upgrades:** [pricing](https://front.com/pricing) gives Starter six months of analytics; the [Usage Policy](https://help.front.com/en/articles/3506304) says up to twelve. The [API guide](https://dev.frontapp.com/docs/rate-limiting) and pricing agree on baseline plan request rates but disagree about whether Starter can purchase an increase.
- **Resolve notification replies:** the [current Resolve guide](https://help.front.com/en/articles/4836160) contradicts itself about whether direct email replies enter the ticket. It also explicitly supports a default Front sender, so human website chat need not silently acquire a second email channel for this cost comparison. Web-based follow-up remains documented.
- **Old versus new chat:** [legacy Front Chat](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2062) has different settings and language coverage. Its loader remains a detection candidate, but new accounts should use [Resolve](https://help.front.com/en/articles/4836160). The new SDK supports only one instance per page; an account-wide domain limit was not found in its [reference](https://dev.frontapp.com/docs/autopilot-resolve-sdk-reference), setup guide or pricing.
- **Leaving and deletion:** read the [SaaS agreement](https://front.com/legal/saas-services-agreement), [DPA](https://front.com/legal/data-processing-addendum), [privacy notice](https://front.com/legal/privacy-notice), [account deletion policy](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2133) and [message deletion policies](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2083). The thirty-day paid-account export window is established; exact completion of account deletion and backup erasure are not. The privacy notice's controller retention language is not a customer-message purge schedule.
- **Other unknowns:** checked [pricing](https://front.com/pricing), [app store](https://front.com/integrations), [integration guides](https://help.front.com/en/categories/331), [channel guides](https://help.front.com/en/categories/232896), [preferences](https://help.front.com/en/categories/209), [contact page](https://help.front.com/en/contact-us), [Help Center customization](https://help.front.com/en/articles/251136) and [Resolve setup](https://help.front.com/en/articles/4836160), plus targeted official-site searches. Direct Discord/WooCommerce support, general email-branding removal, complete agent-interface languages, customer-surface WCAG/VPAT audit, support staffing hours, universal external-agent fees and a complete AI-feedback export remain unestablished. No account, contact form or access request was used to fill those gaps.

## Field problems

- F04/F11 need current seat minimums. Older team-oriented pricing assumptions would incorrectly exclude a one-person buyer.
- F09/F12/F13 need both payment cadences and the selected channel type. Starter's price is not an omnichannel email-and-chat price. Taxes, external accounts and message-volume conditions remain separate.
- F14/F15 need outcome definitions. The USD 0.05 entry rate is triage, not an autonomous-resolution price; different outcomes do not stack on one conversation. Native and external AI costs are not interchangeable.
- F17/F19/F32 require the chat generation. Front Chat is legacy; its removal settings, languages and installation code cannot automatically describe Resolve.
- F20/F21 count different resources: channels, workspaces, knowledge bases, articles and domains. Translations are not additional articles. A public unlisted category is not an authenticated private site.
- F23 distinguishes published Front content updates from manually refreshed external sources, and selecting knowledge from approving every generated reply.
- F28 requires multiple retrieval routes. Shared-inbox CSV/EML excludes private content and contacts; article exports and file downloads are separate. Reports and CSAT files are not a whole-account backup.
- F30/F31 must keep Front's service improvement separate from provider foundation-model training, and separate deletion start, export access, backup purge and source-provider copies. Analytics history is not message retention.
- D01 to D04 remain candidate detection rules. Add H to the inventory proposal and keep current Resolve and legacy FrontChat signatures separate. No crawl or saved-homepage scan was run.
