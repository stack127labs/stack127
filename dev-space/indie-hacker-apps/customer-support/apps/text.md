## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs: [Text pricing](https://www.text.com/pricing/) shows USD 25/seat/month on monthly Essential billing and USD 19/seat/month equivalent billed yearly. [Terms](https://www.text.com/legal/terms-of-use/) state payment in U.S. Dollars, recurring subscription, and tax treatment. The [LiveChat product page](https://www.livechat.com/solutions/small-business/) explicitly labels Text's displayed prices USD. These are base plan costs, not a promise of the final checkout total.

| --- | --- | --- |
| One working support agent, monthly | Essential: 1 seat × USD 25/month. | **USD 25/month**, before tax. |
| One working support agent, annual billing | Essential: 1 seat × USD 19/month equivalent × 12. | **USD 228/year paid annually**, equivalent to USD 19/month, before tax. |
| Three working support agents, monthly | Essential: 3 seats × USD 25/month. | **USD 75/month**, before tax. Owner is counted as one seat. |

| 100 AI resolutions in a monthly cycle | One Essential seat USD 25 + 2 packages × 50 resolutions × USD 49.50/package. | **USD 124/month** for capacity up to 110 resolutions in that cycle. This assumes manually selected monthly recurring packages; it is not a bill for exactly 100 consumed resolutions. |
| 500 AI resolutions in a monthly cycle | One Essential seat USD 25 + 10 packages × USD 49.50/package. | **USD 520/month** for capacity up to 510 resolutions in that cycle. Fixed 50-resolution package steps leave capacity above the example volume. |

Taxes are not included. Twilio, Meta/WhatsApp, external integration providers, optional API calls, and negotiated Enterprise services are outside the totals. Unused resolutions do not carry over. This conflict is unresolved, as noted under F16.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **AI add-on behavior:** The [Terms of Use](https://www.text.com/legal/terms-of-use/), updated Nov 9, says an automatic refill is added when AI usage exceeds the plan. The dedicated [AI Agent resolutions guide](https://www.text.com/help/ai-agent-resolutions/), dated Nov 6, says packages must be added manually and no unselected packages are added. The [pricing FAQ](https://www.text.com/pricing/) and a FAQ block in [Subscription](https://www.text.com/help/subscription/) also describe automatic refills. The Terms page is newest by update date; the dedicated help page is more specific. These current official statements conflict, so confirm billing behavior in the subscription UI or with Text.
- **Plan prices:** The [pricing comparison](https://www.text.com/pricing/) publishes fixed Essential and Growth prices, but its FAQ says there is no fixed one-size-fits-all price and directs buyers to request a matched quote. The public table values are recorded here; no checkout was opened.
- **Enterprise AI quota:** Current pricing table says custom; its FAQ says “2,000+” monthly. The sales offer is needed to know the contracted limit.
- **Multilingual plan gate:** Pricing repeats multilingual support in two places with conflicting tier availability. Widget interface language and AI response language are also different features.
- **Currency and country:** Text's solution page labels displayed prices USD, and terms say fees are paid in U.S. Dollars. No separate country-specific currency schedule was found in the checked pricing/terms pages.
- **Help center:** Pricing calls out knowledge base management, while the help guide describes internal AI/Copilot knowledge sources. Product, pricing, and help pages checked do not establish a separate public customer article portal with custom domain and article limits.
- **Data location and AI training:** The current privacy policy, DPA, transfer page, terms, and AI setup pages were checked. They describe international processing and AI features but do not provide a complete customer-content hosting region, built-in model name/selection, or a clear training-use commitment for customer conversations.
- **Migration:** LiveChat has an explicit migration offer. HelpDesk docs preserve the previous workflow, and ChatBot docs preserve its Visual Builder for existing users; no universal retirement date or equivalent guaranteed data-migration inventory was found for those old workflows.
- **Acquisition/lifecycle review:** Text blog, product updates/changelog, Press/newsroom, investor news, and status page/history were checked, alongside a recent web search combining Text with acquired/acquisition, shutdown, and rebrand. No sale or shutdown notice for Text itself was found. The company's 2023 corporate rename and 2026 launch of the Text suite are recorded as separate history.
- **Detection:** The account-generated widget snippet is not shown in public install docs. Legacy platform docs expose shared LiveChat tracking assets, so the proposed rule is a candidate and cannot cleanly distinguish Text from sibling products without a validated discriminator.
