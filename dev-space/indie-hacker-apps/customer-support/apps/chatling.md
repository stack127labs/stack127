## Cost examples

All figures below are our calculations from the official pricing page. The currency code is not stated there; prices remain in the displayed `$` notation. Taxes are extra. No model-provider invoice or external messaging fee is added.

- **One person, 100 website chats/month, AI off:** Free includes one seat and unlimited chats, so the published price floor is `$0/month`. The exact human-first, AI-disabled configuration is not explained in the docs, so this is not a verified setup quote.
- **Three people, 100 website chats/month, AI off:** Starter has two seats. Add one seat at `$10/month`: `$25 + $10 = $35/month`. The exact human-first, AI-disabled configuration is not explained. Annual Starter is `$250/year`; annual add-on billing is not published.
- **100 AI replies on GPT-6 Luna:** `100 replies × 1 credit = 100 credits`; Free includes 200, so subscription cost is `$0/month` if there is no other AI usage.
- **500 AI replies on GPT-6 Luna:** `500 × 1 = 500 credits`; Starter includes 1,750 for `$25/month`, leaving 1,250.
- **100 AI replies on GPT-6 Astra:** `100 × 25 = 2,500 credits`. Starter is short by 750; one 1,000-credit add-on costs `$10/month`, so `$25 + $10 = $35/month`, leaving 250 credits.
- **Email support, 100 conversations/month:** Not applicable as a Chatling email-helpdesk scenario. The sources checked document outbound email actions and email replies to offline web chats, but not an inbound shared email inbox.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Pricing and AI-credit documentation disagree on rates for Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-5.6 Terra, GPT-5.6 Luna, and GPT 5.4. Examples use the rate shown on the current pricing page; the conflict remains unresolved.
- The pricing page shows `$` but not an ISO currency. Envision Labs' Canadian address does not establish that the price is CAD.
- Free plan is clearly listed. Some official use-case pages also say a free trial exists, but the trial duration, card requirement, and end state are not published. A 7-day refund promise is not a trial.
- Terms require account users to be at least 13; the Privacy Policy says 18 or a parent/guardian's consent.
- There is no published channel-specific fee schedule for messaging-provider charges. No native incoming email, SMS, or phone support channel was found in the pages checked.
- Exact plan gates for some comparison-table features, availability of add-ons on Free, annual add-on billing, an export format, attachment export, site count, total knowledge-source count, auto-recharge, purchased-credit expiry, and cancellation deletion timing are not published in the sources cited above.
- The Terms' no-model-training statement names OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. The current provider list also names other AI providers; coverage of all providers and Chatling's own internal research use are not stated as a universal no-training promise.
- No accessibility statement or agent-interface language list was found in the official sources checked.
