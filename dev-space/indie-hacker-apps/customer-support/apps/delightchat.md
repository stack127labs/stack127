## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-04: [primary pricing](https://www.delightchat.io/pricing/), [alternative public grid](https://www.delightchat.io/help-center/welcome-popup-faqs), [terms](https://www.delightchat.io/legal/terms-of-service), [email setup](https://www.delightchat.io/help-center/how-to-integrate-email-with-delightchat) and [chat setup](https://www.delightchat.io/help-center/how-to-setup-livechat-on-delightchat). Terms establish USD. Totals exclude applicable taxes. These are advertised-offer calculations, not verified checkout invoices.

Cheapest ongoing offer: Startup is the lowest published usable paid bundle for these tasks. A usable ongoing free offer cannot be established, even though docs refer to a Free account state. Primary monthly price is USD 29; the conflicting help grid says USD 39. Both include five agents, so the conflict changes the price but not the one-versus-three comparison.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One person, email | One working owner; one support address; 100 distinct human-replied tickets/cycle; one Startup bundle; AI off. USD 29 × 1 bundle + USD 0 ticket overage. | USD 29/month at canonical pricing; alternative live grid USD 39/month. | Exact annual invoice/cash schedule unknown. Conditional 30% discount calculation: 29 × 12 × 0.70 = USD 243.60/year; /12 = USD 20.30/month equivalent. Requires sales activation and applicable offer. |
| Three people, email | Three working members including owner fit five included members; otherwise same inputs. USD 29 × 1 bundle, not ×3. | USD 29/month total; alternative grid USD 39/month total. | Same conditional USD 243.60/year and USD 20.30/month equivalent; actual annual quote/payment schedule unknown. |
| One person, website chat | One Shopify store, one live-chat channel, 100 distinct human-replied tickets/cycle; one bundle. USD 29 + USD 0 overage. | USD 29/month; alternative grid USD 39/month. | Same conditional annual arithmetic; not proof the annual invoice is charged upfront. |
| Three people, website chat | Three working members, one chat channel, 100 tickets; one bundle. USD 29 + USD 0 overage. | USD 29/month total; alternative grid USD 39/month total. | Same conditional annual arithmetic and uncertainty. |
| Email and chat together | One email channel plus one store-chat channel fits the primary three-channel bundle; 100 human-replied tickets total across both; one or three people. | USD 29/month total under primary offer; alternative grid USD 39/month total. | Same conditional annual arithmetic; no extra channel bundle assumed. |

The source counts tickets, not a universal definition of conversation. We explicitly assume 100 distinct tickets replied to by a human in one 30-day cycle. Reopening those tickets in a later cycle can consume that later cycle's quota. Multiple channel tickets for one customer can also break the assumed one-to-one mapping. Shopify subscription/domain/mailbox costs are prerequisites outside this support comparison; no hosting or email-delivery total is invented.

Annual arithmetic is shown because the public page advertises 30% off, but the page requires sales activation and does not publish a complete payment schedule. USD 243.60 is therefore a conditional annual subscription amount, not a confirmed upfront charge. Applying the same 30% to the alternative USD 39 grid would be another unsupported combination, so no alternate annual total is claimed.

AI helper examples use **tokens**, not messages or resolutions. Assuming a fresh monthly allowance and no other helper use: max(100 − 10,000, 0) = 0 and max(500 − 10,000, 0) = 0 paid tokens, so additional helper usage is USD 0. For whole advertised paid units: (110,000 − 10,000) / 100,000 × USD 1 = USD 1; (510,000 − 10,000) / 100,000 × USD 1 = USD 5. Add the primary USD 29 base to obtain USD 30 or USD 34 in a month before tax. These use the displayed undated discount and do not resolve the alternative AI grid. Autonomous Agent costs for 100 or 500 answers/resolutions cannot be calculated exactly from the published material; no answer-to-token conversion or definitive Agent excess rate was found.
