## Cost examples: our calculations

Inputs checked **2026-11-05**: [current pricing](https://www.smartsupp.com/pricing/), [plan limits and EUR monthly/annual prices](https://help.smartsupp.com/en/articles/12647877-which-package-should-i-choose), [working-seat rules](https://help.smartsupp.com/en/articles/12648781), [conversation limits](https://help.smartsupp.com/en/articles/16641029), [email setup](https://help.smartsupp.com/en/articles/12647589-email-integration), [Mira purchase/units](https://help.smartsupp.com/en/articles/12647226-mira-ai-shopping-assistant-faq), and [tax/payment terms](https://help.smartsupp.com/en/articles/12647835-terms-of-service).

These use the **original EUR offer**, with one full working owner or three full working people including the owner. AI is off in base examples. One product, one website, one support address and 100 human conversations/month are assumed. No extra site, operator, automation host, discount or paid outside integration is included.

| Scenario | Cheapest ongoing usable offer | Task-matched paid offer | Monthly payment: our calculation | Annual payment: our calculation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One person, low-volume website chat | Free: one operator, 25 conversations/month, 14-day history; EUR 0 | Solo for the 100-conversation task | 1 Solo package × EUR 20 = **EUR 20/month** | 1 × EUR 14 × 12 = **EUR 168/year upfront**; EUR 14/month equivalent |
| One person, email, 100 conversations/month | Solo; Free has no connected email mailbox | Solo; one of its three included addresses | 1 × EUR 20 = **EUR 20/month** | 1 × EUR 14 × 12 = **EUR 168/year upfront** |
| Three people, website chat, 100 conversations/month | Plus; three included working seats | Plus | 1 Plus package × EUR 30 = **EUR 30/month** | 1 × EUR 21 × 12 = **EUR 252/year upfront**; EUR 21/month equivalent |
| Three people, email, 100 conversations/month | Plus; three included working seats | Plus; one of its ten included addresses | 1 × EUR 30 = **EUR 30/month** | 1 × EUR 21 × 12 = **EUR 252/year upfront** |

The same Solo/Plus package can contain both email and website chat, but the table evaluates the two tasks separately. Free fails the 100-conversation website task and the connected-email task. Prices are package prices: three working people do **not** cost three times Plus. A blocked, non-chatting owner can avoid a seat, but that does not meet our working-owner scenario. Microsoft 365 shared mailboxes need a different supported mailbox arrangement and potentially an external licence; email hosting is not included in the Smartsupp price.

EUR annual Solo/Plus totals above are **our calculation** from published annual monthly equivalents. Their public annual saving figures reconcile: Solo EUR 20 × 12 − EUR 72 = EUR 168; Plus EUR 30 × 12 − EUR 108 = EUR 252. No checkout invoice was obtained. USD annual figures and Expert EUR annual figures do not reconcile consistently with advertised savings, so no exact invoice total is asserted for them. Taxes, currency/payment adjustments and third-party costs remain additional or unknown.

Mira examples buy **100 or 500 AI conversations of monthly allowance**, not 100/500 messages, contacts or guaranteed resolutions. One visitor session can contain many replies, and returning later can use another session. The published price unit is 100 conversations: monthly EUR 20 per block, or EUR 16/month per block with annual billing. Quotas reset each calendar month. The 500 example uses five blocks; a top-up mid-term may instead be prorated as shown in checkout.
