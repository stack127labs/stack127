# Freshchat

Lifecycle and product relationship: **Freshchat is still offered on its own.** Its current page has a Freshchat trial route and its own Free/Growth/Pro/Enterprise prices. Freshworks also promotes **Freshdesk Omni** as the upgrade for advanced ticketing and knowledge bases. The standalone upgrade FAQ says a new Omni account and manual setup are needed; separate phased migrations cover eligible older Freshdesk/Omni accounts with Freshchat usage. No official standalone Freshchat sales cutoff or service shutdown notice was found in the sources checked. Sources: https://www.freshworks.com/live-chat-software/ ; https://www.freshworks.com/live-chat-software/pricing/ ; https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000011818-how-to-upgrade-to-the-new-freshdesk-or-freshdesk-omni ; https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000012005-upgrade-instructions (checked 2026-11-05).

Related sunset: pricing still mentions Google Business Messages, although Google closed that legacy chat service on July 31, 2024. This is a channel-source conflict, not a Freshchat shutdown. Sources: https://www.freshworks.com/live-chat-software/pricing/ ; https://support.google.com/business/answer/14919056?hl=en (checked 2026-11-05).

## Cost examples — our calculations

Inputs: [Freshchat current pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/live-chat-software/pricing/) and [email setup](https://crmsupport.freshworks.com/support/solutions/articles/50000009057-add-support-emails-in-freshchat), checked 2026-11-05. Monthly and annual values come from explicit USD/INR fields in public page data, not an assumed percentage discount. Before buyer-specific taxes.

Assume one product/site, one support email address and 100 human customer conversations/month, with either one working owner or that owner plus two working helpers. AI off. Email and website chat both qualify as documented channels on Free/Growth; the same account can serve both, so their seat fees should not be added twice. There is no proved human conversation/message cap or plan-specific history period; the examples show known licence fees, not guaranteed unlimited volume/history. Existing external mailbox/platform subscriptions are not priced here.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cheapest ongoing / email and separately web chat / USD or INR | USD 0 or INR 0 | USD 0 or INR 0 | One Free account × zero subscription charge; 10 included agents. Widget branding stays. No ongoing native AI or paid social channels included. |
| Growth / email and separately web chat / USD monthly payment | USD 23/month | USD 69/month | USD 23 × 1 or 3 full agents; no AI/Copilot/campaign add-on. |
| Growth / USD annual payment | USD 228/year, equivalent USD 19/month | USD 684/year, equivalent USD 57/month | USD 19 × 1 × 12 or USD 19 × 3 × 12. Annual payment, not a monthly invoice of USD 19. |
| Growth / INR monthly payment | INR 1,799/month | INR 5,397/month | INR 1,799 × 1 or 3; independently published INR values. |
| Growth / INR annual payment | INR 17,988/year, equivalent INR 1,499/month | INR 53,964/year, equivalent INR 4,497/month | INR 1,499 × 1 × 12 or INR 1,499 × 3 × 12. |

No larger seat minimum or standard setup fee was found in pricing/terms. Those examples assume purchase at the advertised one-agent unit and exclude any negotiated order-form terms. Growth is the lowest paid task match and first documented widget-branding-removal tier; Free remains a cheaper option for its documented scope.

AI examples are **after the one-time 500-session allowance**, using native Freddy **chat** AI sessions. Assume auto-recharge is off and no preview/test sessions are added to the quoted workload. Do not apply Freshdesk email AI's different time window or assume a session is a resolved issue. A preview can consume a session too. Rate: USD 49 per pack of 100, from [pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/live-chat-software/pricing/); renewal, expiry and recharge from [Studio billing FAQ](https://crmsupport.freshworks.com/support/solutions/articles/50000011711-introduction-to-ai-agent-studio), checked 2026-11-05.

Formula for monthly payment: `USD 23 × full human seats + USD 49 × ceil(required AI sessions / 100)`.

| AI chat volume / USD | One human, monthly base | Three humans, monthly base | One human, annual-base equivalent | Three humans, annual-base equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 100 sessions/cycle | 23 + 49 = USD 72/month | 23 × 3 + 49 = USD 118/month | 19 + 49 = USD 68/month equivalent | 19 × 3 + 49 = USD 106/month equivalent |
| 500 sessions/cycle | 23 + 49 × 5 = USD 268/month | 23 × 3 + 49 × 5 = USD 314/month | 19 + 49 × 5 = USD 264/month equivalent | 19 × 3 + 49 × 5 = USD 302/month equivalent |

For steady 100 or 500 sessions **every month over an annual billing cycle**, the annual base plus corresponding 1,200/6,000 annual AI sessions is our calculation: one human `USD 228 + USD 49 × 12 = USD 816/year`, or `USD 228 + USD 49 × 60 = USD 3,168/year`; three humans `USD 684 + USD 588 = USD 1,272/year`, or `USD 684 + USD 2,940 = USD 3,624/year`. These are usage-assumption totals, not a published bundle. Pack purchasing/invoice timing follows the account’s payment cycle, packs expire at its end, and recurring quantities must be explicitly reduced. The one-time allowance could lower an initial bill; it is excluded from ongoing comparison. Online auto-recharge may buy extra packs below 50 remaining sessions if enabled.

Copilot is optional and excluded. A single selected Pro/Enterprise human would add USD 35/month on monthly billing or USD 29 × 12 = USD 348/year on annual billing, beyond that plan's base fee. Native Freddy session pricing does not specify a model-token conversion or require a separate model API-key bill in the checked offer. Optional external apps/telephony, messaging and translation services need their own checks.
