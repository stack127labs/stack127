## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-04: [current HelpDesk.com pricing](https://www.helpdesk.com/pricing/), [official current/previous workflow distinction](https://www.helpdesk.com/help/set-up-custom-domain-and-inbox/), [standalone pricing documentation](https://www.helpdesk.com/help/pricing-and-plans-in-helpdesk/), [service terms](https://www.helpdesk.com/legal/terms-of-use/) and [AI resolution/refill rules](https://www.text.com/help/ai-agent-resolutions-and-automatic-refill/). Terms establish USD, excluded applicable taxes and recurring billing. Current annual pricing charges twelve months upfront.

Cheapest ongoing advertised new-customer offer is **Essential**, not a permanent free plan. The website recommends Text App for new setups; the following current-offer amounts must not be presented as a newly purchasable standalone Team licence. We assume exactly one or three purchased seats; an explicit larger minimum was not located. Unlimited tickets means 100 cases do not themselves add a per-ticket charge, but history, storage, AI and automation allowances still apply.

| Base task, AI off | Inputs and formula | Monthly cash | Annual upfront cash and monthly equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One working owner, one email, 100 conversations/tickets | Essential; 1 × USD 25; one forwarding address included. | USD 25/month. | 1 × USD 19 × 12 = USD 228/year; /12 = USD 19/month equivalent. |
| Three working people including owner, one email, 100 total conversations/tickets | Essential; 3 × USD 25; not three forwarding addresses. | USD 75/month total. | 3 × USD 19 × 12 = USD 684/year; /12 = USD 57/month equivalent. |
| One person, one website, 100 human chat conversations | Current Essential includes website chat; AI Agent offline; 1 × USD 25. | USD 25/month. | USD 228/year upfront; USD 19/month equivalent. |
| Three working people, one website, 100 total human chats | Same current offer; 3 × USD 25. | USD 75/month total. | USD 684/year upfront; USD 57/month equivalent. |

Email and website chat can share the same current subscription; they are not two separately purchased seat bundles. The example assumes no private API client or additional billable automation beyond included limits, an existing mailbox/domain if used, no Twilio/WhatsApp sending and no paid marketplace app. Current AI can be set offline; included AI allowance does not force its use. Taxes and any buyer-specific external subscriptions are excluded.

For existing **previous HelpDesk Team** users, the documented annual rate yields 1 × USD 29 × 12 = **USD 348/year**, equivalent USD 29/month, or 3 × USD 29 × 12 = **USD 1,044/year**, equivalent USD 87/month. Exact monthly-payment rates were not established. Previous human chat uses a separate LiveChat integration, so an exact standalone-plus-chat subscription total is not calculated. No new-customer availability or automatic migration saving is assumed for those older rates.

AI examples use **total billable resolutions within a monthly-payment cycle**, not messages or all incoming tickets. Assume no existing extra resolution packages, only the included allowance, and no other usage. Additional packages are purchased in complete blocks of fifty.

These examples show why the cheapest base plan need not be cheapest with AI. At one seat Growth costs less for both sample AI volumes; at three seats Essential costs less for these two samples. This compares the sample amounts only, without assigning value to different features. We do not multiply ten or 200 organization resolutions by three seats. Per-user reply suggestion/text-edit quotas are a different meter.

At 100 Essential resolutions, ninety excess units need two packs, so USD 99 is the added pack purchase rather than USD 89.10. At 500 Essential resolutions, ten packs cover 500 additional units and leave ten unused units after the included ten are used. Unused resolution capacity does not roll into the next cycle.
