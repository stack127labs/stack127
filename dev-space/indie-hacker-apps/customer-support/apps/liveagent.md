## Cost examples — our calculation

Annual amounts are paid upfront, not monthly instalments. The annual equivalent is shown separately. No currency conversion or silent rounding.

One or three working people includes the owner. There is no published ongoing free offer. Starter supports both tasks and is the cheapest ongoing and task-matched paid choice.
