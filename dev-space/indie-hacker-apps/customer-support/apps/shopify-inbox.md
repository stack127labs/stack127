# Shopify Inbox

Lifecycle: **Shopify Inbox is active and requires a Shopify store.** Its storefront chat and optional Inbox agent remain available. **Shop app merchant chat ended on February 19, 2025**, including access to old Shop-origin conversations; online-store conversations remained available. Source: https://changelog.shopify.com/posts/sunsetting-shop-inbox-on-shop-app (checked 2026-11-05).

Related transition: the old **Shopify Knowledge Base app is no longer installable and is scheduled for deprecation on January 15, 2027**. Its replacement is under Sales channels > Agentic > Knowledge base. This is a knowledge-source transition, not an Inbox shutdown. Sources: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/knowledge-base/managing-faqs ; https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/knowledge-base (checked 2026-11-05). No Inbox-wide sunset or acquisition notice found in checked official sources. The separate archived shop-chat-agent example is not production Inbox.

## Cost examples — our calculations

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: [Inbox free listing](https://apps.shopify.com/inbox), [eligible host plans and agent requirements](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/inbox/assigning-your-ai-staff-member), [US/USD pricing](https://www.shopify.com/pricing), [Basic staff restriction](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/intro-to-shopify/pricing-plans/plans-features/basic-shopify-plan), [Grow allowances](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/intro-to-shopify/pricing-plans/plans-features/grow-plan), [owner exemption](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/users/users-plan-requirements), [annual payment timing](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/intro-to-shopify/pricing-plans/free-trial), and [tax terms](https://www.shopify.com/legal/terms).

No purchase volume or sales revenue supplied, so transaction/payment fees cannot be calculated. Prices below exclude applicable taxes, optional apps/domain/theme and external mail service.
