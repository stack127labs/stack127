## Cost examples: our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: current prices and quota-tier configuration at https://www.tidio.com/pricing/ ; explicit USD and monthly Starter amount at https://www.tidio.com/live-chat/ ; Free capabilities at https://help.tidio.com/hc/en-us/articles/5423925248412-The-free-version-and-downgrading-your-account ; vendor-authored annual amounts at https://apps.shopify.com/tidio-chat ; automatic upgrades at https://help.tidio.com/hc/en-us/articles/13590446473372-Automatic-upgrades ; taxes/refunds at https://www.tidio.com/terms/ . No payment checkout was accessed.

Cheapest ongoing use is Free for either one or three working agents, including the owner, while staying within **50 human conversations per project/month**. For the task-matched example, everyone works on one product/project with one support address and **100 customer conversations handled by humans each month**, AI off. Ten included seats cover either headcount. Website chat is a separate matched scenario with the same base price, not an additional 100 conversations on top of email. No claim that one quota covers 100 emails plus another 100 chats.

Direct-web checkout tax, final invoice rounding, and custom negotiated conditions were not verified. Renewal is recurring monthly/annually as selected; cancelling a paid plan retains paid access until the current period ends, then Free. AI examples are after the lifetime free allowance, with **auto-upgrades disabled**, fixed standard monthly tiers, no Premium resolution contract, and no separate model-provider invoice. These are 100/500 counted AI threads, not guaranteed successful answers, unique visitors, or human conversations. They can be additional AI-only threads alongside the 100 human-handled monthly base workload; a handoff with a human reply can consume both meters. No assumed handoff/resolution rate is inserted. If configured quotas change, automatic upgrades remain enabled, more than 100 human threads need replies, or paid channels/Actions dependencies are added, these totals no longer describe the bill.
