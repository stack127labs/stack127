# Heroic KB

**AI sunset notice:** Heroic KB is still sold, but its **built-in AI Help Assistant is being replaced by HelpJet**. The official [4.7.0/4.8.0 changelog](https://herothemes.com/changelogs/hkb/) says new activation is blocked, legacy training is retired, conversation analytics is frozen with CSV export, and the built-in assistant and AI Article Writer will be removed in the next release. Existing trained answers continue only until removal. No calendar removal date was found. This is a component sunset, not closure of Heroic KB.

The current KB sales page calls HelpJet-powered AI included with Pro, while HelpJet publishes separate hosted plans. No definite included HelpJet allowance for KB Pro buyers was found. Do not promise unlimited or bundled paid AI usage. No whole-product shutdown or acquisition notice found in the official sources checked.

## Field problems

- F01/F38 must distinguish the continuing plugin from a retiring AI component. Marking the whole app closed would be wrong; listing old AI setup as current would also be wrong.
- F05/F14 need product boundaries: self-hosted GPL-v3 KB, separately licensed sample content, associated themes and inboxes, and hosted HelpJet are not one licence/allowance. The phrase “included with Pro” lacks the hosted entitlement detail needed for a firm AI bill.
- F12/F13 assume working inbox agents. Here the priced people are documentation editors. The record therefore gives a clear supported KB cost and marks the native mailbox/human-chat task not applicable.
