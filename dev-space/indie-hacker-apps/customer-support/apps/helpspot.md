## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Pricing display arithmetic:** Annual totals in calculator and structured USD offers agree. For 5 agents, USD 1,299 ÷ 60 = USD 21.65, which agrees. Use exact annual total and label our derived equivalent; do not recover an annual bill by multiplying the rounded label.
- **Current/legacy subscription rights:** Licence distinguishes owned pre-January 4, 2016 licences from current subscriptions and discontinued Free License. General month-to-month licence wording is not a published current monthly-price offer. Current public price schedule is annual. Read licence/pricing/account-management guidance; no order/checkout requested to fill tax, renewal/cancellation or failed-payment details.
- **AI providers:** Operational data-transfer page updated October 29, 2026 describes OpenAI and explicitly no longer Groq. Current AI page also names Anthropic.
