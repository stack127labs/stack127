# Zammad

Lifecycle check: Zammad remains active, with hosted and free self-hosted editions. A security incident on September 21, 2026 involved an attacker using two Zammad vulnerabilities to breach DIVD’s self-hosted ticket system; DIVD reports a partial leak of its CSIRT ticket-system data. This is not evidence of a breach of Zammad’s hosted service or other customers. DIVD’s separate “Zammad data” category remained under investigation in its October 9 update. **Zammad 7.2.2**, released **October 8, 2026**, is a security update affecting DEB/RPM package installations; the notice says hosted SaaS instances are unaffected. The old dedicated Slack integration was deprecated for removal in Zammad 7; current docs provide Slack notification webhooks instead. This integration change is not a product shutdown.

## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: [current plans](https://zammad.com/en/pricing), [plan comparison](https://zammad.com/en/pricing/table), [one-to-five-agent buying scenario](https://zammad.com/en/pricing/find-your-plan), [AI subscription rules](https://admin-docs.zammad.org/en/latest/system/subscription.html), [AI request definition](https://support.zammad.com/help/en-us/33-billing-and-contract/807-pricing-modell-of-zammad-ai-hosted-by-zammad-gmbh), and [self-hosting requirements](https://docs.zammad.org/en/latest/prerequisites/hardware.html).

Cheapest ongoing software offer is the free self-hosted edition. It requires infrastructure and ongoing operation. For a managed hosted comparison, use one product, one support email address, 100 customer conversations/month, AI off and one or three working people including owner. A working founder is not an admin-only free account. All paid amounts below are EUR before VAT.

| Scenario | Inputs and formula | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solo hosted email, monthly | 1 Starter v2 agent × 9 | **EUR 9/month** |
| Solo hosted email, annually | 1 × 7 × 12 | **EUR 84/year upfront**, EUR 7/month equivalent |
| Three hosted email workers, monthly | 3 × 9 | **EUR 27/month** |
| Three hosted email workers, annually | 3 × 7 × 12 | **EUR 252/year upfront**, EUR 21/month equivalent |
| Solo hosted website chat, monthly | 1 Professional v2 agent × 18 | **EUR 18/month** |
| Solo hosted website chat, annually | 1 × 16 × 12 | **EUR 192/year upfront**, EUR 16/month equivalent |
| Three hosted chat workers, monthly | 3 × 18 | **EUR 54/month** |
| Three hosted chat workers, annually | 3 × 16 × 12 | **EUR 576/year upfront**, EUR 48/month equivalent |
| One/three self-hosted email or chat workers | 0 core licence + actual infrastructure/mail/domain/backup/maintenance | **0 licence fee; exact operating total unknown** |

Email and chat are alternate task-matched views, not two bills added together. Hosted quotas include normal ticket handling, but storage/attachment limits remain. Do not use pricing's general “Save 10%” slogan to recalculate explicit plan amounts: the displayed monthly/annual tariffs determine these totals. Optional self-hosted support adds EUR 2,999/5,999/9,999 per year for Business/Enterprise/Corporation, separately from infrastructure. No mandatory support purchase, server quote or labor estimate is invented.

AI examples use **AI calls**, not resolved conversations. The pricing page gives EUR 0.03/call; the billing guide qualifies this as approximate and quota-dependent. Therefore the following are illustrative calculations, not guaranteed pack invoices. Use Professional v2 as lowest hosted AI-eligible base.

| Scenario | Formula at headline rate | Result and conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh solo account, 100 or 500 AI calls | EUR 18 monthly base + 0 recharge while initial 500 calls cover usage | **EUR 18/month base**, if no other AI activity consumes that initial balance |
| Solo, 100 calls after initial allowance | 100 × 0.03 ≈ 3 usage value; cash purchase minimum 400 × 0.03 ≈ 12; base 18 + 12 | **About EUR 30 cash in purchase month**; 300 unused calls remain, expiry not established |
| Solo, 500 purchased calls | 18 + (500 × 0.03) | **About EUR 33 in purchase month** |
| Three agents, 500 purchased calls shared by instance | (3 × 18) + (500 × 0.03) | **About EUR 69 in purchase month** |

Annual Professional bases are EUR 192 or EUR 576, with separately purchased AI quotas; no annual AI discount inferred. Automated jobs, repeated summaries and reindexing can consume calls. A ticket may need multiple interactions, so 100 calls do not guarantee 100 resolved conversations. External/local models have their own usage/compute costs; no exact all-in self-hosted AI total is calculated.
