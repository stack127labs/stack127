## Cost examples: our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-04: [pricing](https://www.dragapp.com/pricing/), [AI tariff](https://www.dragapp.com/ai-credits/), [live chat/setup FAQ](https://www.dragapp.com/live-chat/) and [payment terms](https://www.dragapp.com/terms/). Pricing's structured offers identify USD; the same page republishes the AI action prices. No currency conversion is used. Buyer-specific taxes and external mailbox/domain/automation/model-client costs are outside these known subscription calculations.

### Cheapest ongoing paid route and email task

There is no ongoing free offer. Starter is both the cheapest published paid route and the task-matched email route: one product, one support email address, 100 customer conversations/month, AI off. No extra seats or AI purchase are required by the public schedule.

| Working people, including owner | Monthly-payment formula | Monthly payment | Annual-upfront formula | Annual payment | Annual amount / 12 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 × USD 16 | USD 16/month | 1 × USD 12 × 12 | USD 144/year | USD 12/month equivalent |
| 3 | 3 × USD 16 | USD 48/month | 3 × USD 12 × 12 | USD 432/year | USD 36/month equivalent |

The service is paid per seat, not per one of these 100 email conversations. This is not a claim of infinite sending/storage; Google and any unpublished service limits remain separate. Terms say yearly payment is in advance. An annual monthly equivalent is not an option to pay that annual rate one month at a time.

### Website human chat

Use one configured website/widget and the same Starter team, with Varley off. The [chat FAQ](https://www.dragapp.com/live-chat/) expressly includes human live chat on every paid plan with no per-chat fee. Known subscription calculations are therefore the same USD 16/48 monthly or USD 144/432 annually for one/three people.

**Availability condition:** current [pricing](https://www.dragapp.com/pricing/) marks Live chat Early Access. The setup page publishes installation steps and a loader, but an exact approval/rollout timetable was not established. The costs apply when the account can enable the feature; they are not proof that every new account can activate it immediately. Pro is required for its business-hours/away controls and vendor-mark removal, which the base human-chat task does not require.

### 100 and 500 autonomous Agent resolutions

Use Pro because autonomous Agents are not included in Plus or Starter. Count only conversations satisfying Drag's [published definition](https://www.dragapp.com/ai-credits/): Agent finishes with the customer's question answered and no human participation. Human handoffs are not counted as billable resolutions. Other separately billed AI actions, drafts, Copilot questions, translation and Help Center/chat answers are off in this scenario; the full monthly allowance is available. This is a separate volume example, not a conversion from the human-chat comparison.
