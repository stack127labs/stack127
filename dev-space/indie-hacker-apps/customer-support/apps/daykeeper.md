## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: [current pricing](https://www.mydaykeeper.com/pricing), [terms/taxes](https://www.mydaykeeper.com/terms), [current access scope](https://www.mydaykeeper.com/llms.txt), [agent onboarding](https://www.mydaykeeper.com/ai-onboarding).

Scenario: one workspace/product, one support email address, 100 new customer support threads/month, one or three working agents including owner, AI off. One thread counts once in the month it begins. Replies, retries, private notes, status checks and switching human/software handler do not create extra units. No conversion to another vendor's messages/resolutions.

| Task | One working agent | Three working agents | Included scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cheapest ongoing web/API support | 1 Free workspace × USD 0 = USD 0 | Same: USD 0 | 100 conversations/month, 1 inbox, web messenger/API, 30-day history; no verified own-domain support inbox |
| Task-matched paid support email | 1 Pro workspace × USD 19.99 = USD 19.99/month | Same: USD 19.99/month | 2,000 conversations, 5 inboxes, web + verified email, 1-year history; AI off |
| Paid website-chat option | USD 19.99/month Pro | USD 19.99/month Pro | Free is also eligible at 100 new threads; Pro adds headroom/history |
| Twelve monthly Pro payments | 12 × USD 19.99 = USD 239.88 | USD 239.88 | Budget only; no annual prepaid rate or price lock published |
| Scale, if that volume/history is needed | 1 × USD 99.99 = USD 99.99/month | USD 99.99/month | 10,000 conversations, 25 inboxes, 2-year history; not needed for the 100-thread baseline |

No per-seat multiplication. Tax, external email/domain service, external AI/coding-agent subscriptions and custom backend implementation are outside the platform bill. No mandatory paid setup fee found. A 10-slot startup promotion advertises Pro free for 12 months then USD 19.99/month; do not make this limited, unverified-availability deal the universal recurring email price.

**AI-volume examples:** if the work consists of 100 new support threads/month, Free's platform fee is USD 0 for web/API support. At 500 new threads, Pro is USD 19.99/month. With support email, Pro is required at both volumes. One or three working people have the same workspace bill, and no separate per-resolution surcharge is advertised. These are **support-thread platform calculations**, not a claim that one AI answer/resolution equals one thread or that every external model call is free. Exact total is `workspace fee + any external agent/model/tool charges + approved overage`; token/unit conversion and exhaustive external billing scope are not published.

**Optional overage:** Pro USD 12/1,000 extra conversations; Scale USD 8/1,000. It starts off, requires verified human-owner approval and a hard monthly budget, and cannot be approved by the agent itself. Partial-block treatment is not published; no silently prorated/rounded fractional total calculated. At the limit, accepted messages do not disappear and history stays readable within its plan's history entitlement.
