### AI examples — our calculation

Sources, checked 2026-11-04: [Growth price and seats](https://respond.io/pricing), [monthly price](https://respond.io/blog/respondio-pricing), [credit consumption and rounding/cap rules](https://respond.io/help/organization-settings/ai-credits) and [monthly usage invoicing](https://respond.io/help/organization-settings/billing-usage).

Select entry **Growth**, because Starter cannot publish autonomous AI Agents. Growth has ten included users, 1,000 MACs and 10,000 included AI credits/month. One or three working people have the same base. Assume no other AI consumption, no extra MACs and no paid external channel traffic.

| Actual AI unit count | Formula for added usage cost | One/three users, monthly-payment total | Annual-base monthly equivalent plus AI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 100 credits used within allowance | max(0, 100 − 10,000) = 0 excess; USD 0 | USD 199 + 0 = **USD 199** | USD 159 + 0 = **USD 159/month equivalent** |
| 500 credits used within allowance | max(0, 500 − 10,000) = 0 excess; USD 0 | USD 199 + 0 = **USD 199** | USD 159 + 0 = **USD 159/month equivalent** |
| Allowance exhausted, then 100 extra credits | ceil(100 ÷ 1,000) × USD 15 = **USD 15** | USD 199 + 15 = **USD 214** | USD 159 + 15 = **USD 174/month equivalent** |
| Allowance exhausted, then 500 extra credits | ceil(500 ÷ 1,000) × USD 15 = **USD 15** | USD 199 + 15 = **USD 214** | USD 159 + 15 = **USD 174/month equivalent** |

An annual Growth subscriber pays **USD 1,908 upfront**, then any actual monthly overage invoices. Included credits reset monthly and do not roll over. On-demand credits cannot be disabled on current paid plans. Growth's 10,000 included plus at most 10,000 extra credits gives our calculation `ceil(10,000 / 1,000) × USD 15 = USD 150` maximum AI-credit overage for that allowance, before tax. AI pauses at 20,000 total credits; that cap does not cap MAC/provider charges.

For response context only, the vendor gives **typical** AI Agent consumption of 2–5 credits/response. Our illustrative ranges are `100 × 2–5 = 200–500 credits` and `500 × 2–5 = 1,000–2,500 credits`. These are not guaranteed tariffs, resolutions or conversation conversions. Real replies, calls, tests, summaries and sentiment share the credit pool, so an exact bill from 100 or 500 customer conversations is not established.
