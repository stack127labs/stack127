## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: [annual USD seat prices and gates](https://www.teamwork.com/desk/pricing/), [agent and part-time definitions](https://support.teamwork.com/desk/billing/agents-and-part-time-agents), [payment cadence and additional sites](https://support.teamwork.com/desk/billing/subscribing-to-teamwork-desk), and [website live-chat setup](https://support.teamwork.com/desk/advanced/creating-an-embeddable-contact-form). Exact current monthly seat prices and a minimum paid seat count remain unresolved after the pricing/billing/terms checks. The following are **conditional published seat subtotals**, not verified checkout totals.

| Scenario | Inputs and formula | Annual seat cash payment | Annual seat cost ÷ 12 | Monthly-payment cash |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cheapest published paid tier; one unrestricted agent; email | Starter; 1 × USD 9.50 × 12 | USD 114 upfront | USD 9.50/month equivalent | Exact monthly rate not established |
| Cheapest tier; three unrestricted agents; email | Starter; 3 × USD 9.50 × 12 | USD 342 upfront | USD 28.50/month equivalent | Exact monthly rate not established |
| One unrestricted agent; email plus website live chat | Pro; 1 × USD 17.00 × 12 | USD 204 upfront | USD 17.00/month equivalent | Exact monthly rate not established |
| Three unrestricted agents; email plus website live chat | Pro; 3 × USD 17.00 × 12 | USD 612 upfront | USD 51.00/month equivalent | Exact monthly rate not established |

Each scenario assumes the chosen seat count can be purchased, the owner is included, and one inbox/site/widget is enough. There is no ongoing free Desk plan in current pricing. Unlimited ticket replies by a paid full-time agent cover the stated workload without a published ticket-meter charge, subject to fair usage. No equivalence between conversations, tickets, analyses, drafts, or resolutions is assumed. A ticket can contain several replies; a chat can later become a ticket.

A limited three-person setup could use one paid agent plus two free part-time agents. The two free agents each get 10 unique tickets per 30-day period; the paid agent would handle the rest of the 100-conversation workload. Conditional annual seat subtotal would remain USD 114 on Starter or USD 204 on Pro. This is useful for occasional helpers, but it is not three unrestricted support seats. Whether chat interactions use precisely the same part-time ticket counter was not established, so this is not a verified three-person live-chat staffing allowance.

AI is off in the base examples. For 100 generated drafts and no more than 1,000 analyzed tickets, the [advertised Dotty offer](https://www.teamwork.com/desk/pricing/) adds $69/month to the whole account, not to each seat. If the add-on follows the page's USD context at the advertised monthly cadence, the one-agent Starter equivalent is USD 9.50 + USD 69 = **USD 78.50/month equivalent**. The base seat payment remains USD 114 upfront; this does not establish a single monthly combined invoice, an exact annual Dotty price, tax, or the first checkout payment. At three Starter seats the same conditional arithmetic is USD 28.50 + USD 69 = **USD 97.50/month equivalent**.

For 500 drafts, the included 100 leave **500 − 100 = 400 additional drafts**. The [purchase guide](https://support.teamwork.com/desk/teamworkai/purchase-dotty-ai-teammate-in-teamwork-desk) advertises extra suggested replies for $9/month but does not identify the quantity bought. The extra cost and full total therefore cannot be exact. Five hundred ticket analyses alone fit within the 1,000-analysis allowance, but analyses do not guarantee drafts or resolved outcomes.

No currency conversion was performed. Taxes, external email/domain/website service, optional Teamwork.com project plans, extra Help Docs sites, automation services, and any excessive-use charges are outside these subtotals. Additional Help Docs sites are displayed as $20/month in the billing guide; that guide does not independently spell out its currency code.
