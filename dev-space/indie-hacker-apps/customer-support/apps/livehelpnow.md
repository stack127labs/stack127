## Cost examples: our calculations

Inputs checked **2026-11-05**: [current pricing](https://livehelpnow.net/pricing), [detailed official schedule](https://help.livehelpnow.net/1/kb/article/4916/livehelpnow-pricing-schedule), [agent licences](https://help.livehelpnow.net/1/kb/article/61280/agents-explained), [vendor per-agent description](https://livehelpnow.net/blog/how-add-ons-inflate-help-desk-pricing-by-30-to-60/), [unlimited human-volume FAQ](https://help.livehelpnow.net/1/kb/article/1582/is-there-a-limit-to-the-number-of-chats-i-can-have-each-month) and [email workflow](https://help.livehelpnow.net/1/kb/article/2982/emailticket-management-system-description).

**Exact totals cannot be certified.** New pricing says **from $39/$59/$79 per month** without printing an included seat count or per-agent label next to those figures. Agent documentation requires licences, and the vendor describes its model as per-agent. The illustrations below therefore **assume the new headline applies per licensed working agent**, no larger minimum, one site and one support address, 100 monthly human conversations, and AI off. Each working person includes the owner. Visitor tracking is assumed disabled or within any licensed allowance. USD is confirmed for these base/AI prices. Unknown tax, quote conditions and external channel bills remain outside the numbers.
