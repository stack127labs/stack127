## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05 UTC: [USD pricing and annual structured offers](https://docsbot.ai/pricing), [team roles](https://docsbot.ai/documentation/doc/how-to-share-your-ai-chatbot-with-other-users), [recurring add-ons](https://docsbot.ai/documentation/doc/add-ons-faq), [AI Credit definitions](https://docsbot.ai/documentation/doc/ai-credits-faq), [Help Scout assistance](https://docsbot.ai/documentation/doc/help-scout-integration), and [terms](https://docsbot.ai/legal/terms-of-service).

One person means the owner; three means owner plus two Editors. The table separates bot administrators from human support agents. Free permits personal use, one user, 50 pages, and 100 monthly credits, with the lifespan conflict above. No ongoing free three-user team is published. Standard's five-user bundle is the cheapest published route to three editing users because extra-user add-ons are limited to Business/Enterprise.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cheapest personal bot, one operator | Free, within 50 pages/100 credits and disputed lifespan | USD 0 | USD 0; no guarantee of permanent hosting from conflicting sources |
| Paid website AI bot, one operator | One Personal team subscription | USD 49/month | USD 492/year; 492 / 12 = USD 41/month equivalent |
| Paid website AI bot, three editing operators | One Standard team; three of five users | USD 149/month | USD 1,488/year; 1,488 / 12 = USD 124/month equivalent |

| Matched AI-off email, three working people, one address, 100 conversations | Same product boundary | No DocsBot-only matched total | Bot-management users do not replace human inbox agents |
| Matched AI-off website chat, one or three working people, 100 conversations | No native human-agent chat workflow established | No DocsBot-only matched total | Requires external human chat/help-desk arrangement |
| AI-assisted support email, one or three humans | DocsBot Standard + external Help Scout mailbox/agent plan | USD 149 + unknown external monthly cost + applicable tax | USD 1,488 + unknown external annual cost + applicable tax |

The supported AI bot prices are subscription subtotals. They are not an AI-off comparison disguised by leaving the AI allowance unused. For 100 customer conversations, total AI Credits depend on turns, model, searches, actions, context and other work. There is no exact public conversion from 100 conversations to consumed credits.

For the requested small AI examples, use **100 and 500 actually consumed AI Credits**, not messages or resolutions. With no other usage:

- Personal: `49 + 0 add-on = USD 49/month` for either 100 or 500 credits, within 5,000; annual `USD 492`, equivalent `492 / 12 = USD 41/month`.
- Standard, three editing users: `149 + 0 = USD 149/month` for either amount, within 15,000; annual `USD 1,488`, equivalent `1,488 / 12 = USD 124/month`.
- Free can accommodate 100 actual credits, within its personal-use scope and disputed lifespan. It does not provide a 500-credit allowance or three editing users.

A model-sensitive capacity example is useful but is not an exact conversation bill. Assume ordinary chat requests charged solely at the published model multiplier, no actions/search/extra context work, and no BYOK. Let `N` be requests and `w` the multiplier. Required credits `Q = N × w`. On Personal, additional monthly blocks `B = max(0, ceil((Q − 5,000) / 5,000))`; monthly subscription subtotal `49 + 49 × B`.

With BYOK, normal-answer DocsBot credits generally become 1x but an additional provider bill remains unknown; it is not free premium-model usage.

Do not add an invented USD-per-bot branding surcharge. Self-hosted Enterprise, phone routing, provider bills, external help-desk seats, and tax have no exact all-in total in this record.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Free lifetime:** [pricing](https://docsbot.ai/pricing) says no 30-day expiry, but [terms](https://docsbot.ai/legal/terms-of-service) allow deletion of free bot data 30 days after creation. Both claims are retained; no promised permanent free commercial service.
- **Image plan names:** pricing puts HTML/Markdown image training on Standard; [image guide](https://docsbot.ai/documentation/doc/enable-learn-from-public-images-docsbot) and [parsing guide](https://docsbot.ai/documentation/doc/advanced-document-parsing) say Pro+, a name absent from current pricing. Older [quality advice](https://docsbot.ai/documentation/doc/improving-response-quality) broadly says images/non-text cannot be processed; the specific current parsing/image docs describe the supported routes.
