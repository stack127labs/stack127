## Cost examples — our calculation

Prices below use published USD values without converting other offers. Taxes excluded.

Cheapest ongoing use for one or three working people is **Free at USD 0**, for email/portal/form support within **50 tickets/month account-wide**. Three people must include the working owner. Free does not fit 100 separate tickets; adding seats does not multiply the free ticket quota. Free accounts also need new-ticket activity to avoid the three-month deletion rule.

No larger minimum seat purchase or numeric paid ticket cap was established. The published price is per agent, and no per-ticket charge is displayed; that does not justify inventing an unlimited paid-ticket promise. A conversation can include multiple messages, and one customer can open several tickets, so the comparison explicitly chooses 100 distinct tickets rather than asserting a vendor conversion.

Own-domain email assumes an existing mailbox/forwarding/domain setup. No extra Microsoft 365 fee is added to email-only use: portal email/password authentication is documented. ## Conflicts and unknowns

- **AI Teams launch:** [AI marketing page](https://www.desk365.io/ai-agent/) still says Teams is coming soon. [Current Teams AI setup](https://help.desk365.io/en/articles/microsoft-teams-ai-agent-setup-guide/) and the [October release](https://www.desk365.io/blog/desk365-product-updates-october-2026/) say it is launched. Use current dated evidence for availability and preserve the stale claim.
- **API capacity wording:** [API guide](https://help.desk365.io/en/articles/desk365-api/) heading says 10,000 tickets per call; body specifies 30/50/100 per page and 10,000 per hour. Pricing's rate-row heading says requests/minute while Standard explicitly reads 100/hr; the API guide confirms 100/hour. Units were kept as published rather than silently normalized.
- **Language counts:** Pricing/FAQ say 30 generally; current language docs distinguish 20 agent-interface and 34 portal/bot options. These are surface-specific claims. Multiple simultaneous language choices require Plus/Premium; translating interface strings does not translate article bodies or custom notification content automatically.
- **AI credit expiry:** Setup/billing guides shorten this to no expiration while subscribed. AI terms explicitly require an eligible Plus/Premium subscription and forfeit unused paid credits when eligibility ends. This is a legal qualification, not a promise of portable or refundable credits. Models, weights and exact zero-balance handling remain unestablished after reading setup, billing and AI terms.
