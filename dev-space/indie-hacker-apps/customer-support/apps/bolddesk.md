## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: [human-only published rates](https://www.bolddesk.com/pricing/help-desk), [current subscription guide](https://support.bolddesk.com/kb/article/21603), [AI pricing and packs](https://www.bolddesk.com/pricing), and [contract/fair use](https://www.bolddesk.com/legal/terms-of-use). Owner counts among the agents. One product, one email inbox, one optional website chat widget, 100 customer conversations/month, AI off.

| Scenario | Inputs and formula | Monthly cash | Annual cash | Annual cash ÷ 12 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cheapest ongoing published per-seat offer; 1 working agent, email | Human-only Enterprise; 1 × rate | USD 25 | USD 240 upfront | USD 20/month equivalent |
| Same solo plan, email plus website chat | Same seat; native chat included | USD 25 | USD 240 upfront | USD 20/month equivalent |
| Three working agents, email | Human-only Enterprise; 3 × rate | 3 × 25 = USD 75 | 3 × 240 = USD 720 upfront | USD 60/month equivalent |
| Same three-agent plan, email plus website chat | Same 3 seats; chat included | USD 75 | USD 720 upfront | USD 60/month equivalent |

These are **conditional seat subtotals**, not verified order totals. Human-only Enterprise is currently sales-assisted. The live pricing and subscription guide do not clearly publish its minimum seat quantity. An indexed older search extract said five agents, but the current live article no longer includes that sentence; the comparison calculator starts at five only for comparing teams. Neither justifies presenting five as the current contractual minimum or one as a confirmed purchasable minimum. If sales requires more seats than the scenario, the bill will rise accordingly. The formula remains purchased seats × USD 25/month or × USD 240/year.

There is no permanent general free plan. An approved startup with ARR below USD 1 million, funding below USD 10 million, and no current/prior paid BoldDesk subscription can obtain the [current 12-month offer](https://www.bolddesk.com/help-desk-software-for-startups). One and three working agents both fit its stated five-seat limit, so their first-year licence subtotal is **USD 0**, conditional on approval and grant terms. This is not an ongoing price or a promise of a second free year. Branding-removal restrictions apply. Do not promise the 10 agents/one-time credits described by legacy offer docs to a new applicant.

The unlimited-human AI plans are **not used for the AI-off comparison**. The current contract requires active AI operation after onboarding and restricts primarily human-only use. In particular, rolling 90-day use after the first 60 days can fail fair use if AI handles less than 50% of created tickets or consumes less than 40% of included credits while humans handle tickets. Scale AI's USD 99 annual monthly equivalent is therefore not a valid cheaper human-only substitute for a five-seat or other human-only quote.

For an AI example using honest units, **100 consumed credits** or **500 consumed credits** fit Scale AI's 500 monthly allocation. Additional credit-pack purchase is **USD 0** in either example. Base payment is **USD 116 for a monthly subscription**, or **USD 1,188 upfront for a year**, equivalent to **USD 99/month**. Headcount 1 versus 3 does not change that AI-plan price. Low AI usage with humans also working can still fail fair-use conditions; the arithmetic is not a guarantee of suitability for an indefinitely repeated low-volume workload.

For context only, current pricing estimates simple generations at 1–2 credits. Our illustrative ranges are **100 × 1–2 = 100–200 credits**, and **500 × 1–2 = 500–1,000 credits**. With a fresh 500-credit allowance and no other AI usage, the latter extremes require either no top-up or **one USD 40 pack of 1,000 credits**. The pack can leave credits for a later month. This is not a guaranteed 500-response bill: other docs give different approximate complexity bands, follow-ups/greetings/Assist work consume credits, and successful resolution is not the meter. No exact annual pack count is inferred from one month's usage.

Included credits reset monthly, including annual subscriptions. Purchased credits carry forward during the subscription, but unused balances generally forfeit at termination. Optional recharge can create further purchases if enabled. External email/site/domain, WhatsApp/SMS/voice carriers, third-party service licences, tax, and custom contract costs are outside the subtotals. Unlimited tickets/articles do not mean unlimited attachment storage. No currency conversion or silent rounding was performed.
