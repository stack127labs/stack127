## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05 UTC: [pricing](https://www.chatnode.ai/pricing), [credit/reset FAQ](https://www.chatnode.ai/faq-chatnode), [team roles](https://www.chatnode.ai/docs/user-guides/users/teams), [human mode](https://www.chatnode.ai/docs/user-guides/users/live-chat), [SDK controls](https://www.chatnode.ai/docs/developer-guides/javascript-sdk/helper-methods), and [current terms](https://www.chatnode.ai/terms-conditions). Prices keep the published $ symbol; ISO currency is unestablished. Fees exclude tax. Annual equivalents are rounded to two decimals, explicitly approximately; cash totals are unrounded published amounts.

One working person means owner/admin. Three means one admin plus two Members, not three additional people. Current main pricing has one Lite member, three Standard, five Scale. Native human live chat starts on Standard, so that is the public subscription floor for either one or three people serving website visitors. Exact global human-first routing was not found; use the plan floor conditionally, rather than presenting a verified AI-off deployment.

A conversation can contain many AI responses, and model credit weighting changes usage. Free 35 credits does not guarantee 100 conversations. Human-mode support is documented, but a global AI-disable/human-first default was not. No per-human-message credit tariff was published in the checked pages. Base examples exclude optional branding/domain/agent/credit add-ons, tax and external systems; no complete zero-AI-usage workflow was tested.

AI comparisons use **100 or 500 actually consumed credits**, with all other usage zero:

- One-person Lite: 100 and 500 are below 1,250; `35 + 0 extra = $35/month`, or `$350/year`, equivalent `350 / 12 ≈ $29.17/month`.
