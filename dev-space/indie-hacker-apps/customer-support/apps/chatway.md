One product/site, one support email address, 100 human conversations/month, one or three working people including owner, AI off. No reset, optional integration, added workflow or discount assumed. Business includes two people total, **including the owner**. Three workers therefore require one extra seat. Monthly and annual are alternative payment cadences. The extra-seat annual amount is unresolved. These are our calculations of conflicting source interpretations, not two confirmed offers. Confirm the invoice before treating either annual total as exact.

The website and email cases are evaluated separately, though the same package includes both. Free fails the 100-conversation workload and integrated-email requirement. The paid email path creates an @chatwaymail.com address; forwarding an existing support mailbox may require an outside email subscription. Taxes/payment adjustments, Meta charges and external model/runner hosting are additional or unknown. The source does not establish an English-offer currency code, so no USD totals are asserted.

AI examples use **billable resolved conversations as advertised**, not received chats or messages. Our calculation: 100 × $0.50 = **$50**; 500 × $0.50 = **$250**. Adding a monthly Plus base of $199 gives **$249** or **$449** before extras. These are conditional partial totals: Plus is selected to avoid assuming a free reset for a larger workload, and its 1,000 monthly conversation allowance is assumed sufficient. The setup guide instead describes a credit per handled conversation, and native-AI use of the base allowance is not fully defined. Credit purchase minimums/expiry and the true handled-to-resolved conversion remain unknown. No exact wallet purchase or guaranteed all-in AI bill is claimed; no trial credit deduction is assumed.

For external AI, no common $0.50 native-AI charge is imposed by this calculation. The provider's model/token bill, workflow host and plan conversation allowance are separate inputs, none inferred from free MCP/API availability.
