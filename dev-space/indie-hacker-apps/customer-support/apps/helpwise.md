## Cost examples: our calculation

### Cheapest ongoing and paid email task

No current ongoing free plan is published. Standard is the cheapest published paid plan for the stated email task. The owner is included in the working-person count. Its three shared inboxes cover one support address; AI is off. The public tariff does not price by conversation, but it also does not establish an unlimited message allowance.
