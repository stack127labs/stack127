## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: [core and unlimited agents](https://github.com/freescout-helpdesk/freescout), [installation/email needs](https://github.com/freescout-helpdesk/freescout/wiki/Installation-Guide), [module billing](https://freescout.net/modules-faq/), [Chat](https://freescout.net/module/chat/), [End-User Portal](https://freescout.net/module/end-user-portal/) and [AI Integration](https://freescout.net/module/ai-integration/).

Cheapest ongoing email option is self-hosted core. For one product/address and 100 conversations per month, one or three working people can use it without a FreeScout seat/conversation charge. There is no vendor cloud plan being priced here.

Let **H** be actual hosting, mail delivery, domain, backup/storage and maintenance costs for the chosen deployment. Let **C**, **P**, **S** and **W** be verified current one-instance purchase prices for Chat, End-User Portal, Saved Replies and Workflows, respectively, in the actual displayed checkout currency. These amounts remain unknown; they are not placeholder price estimates.

| Scenario | Formula | Result and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solo email, AI off | 0 core licence + H | Software licence 0; exact monthly/annual operating total unknown. |
| Three-person email, AI off | 0 core licence + H | Same software licence 0; no multiplier for three working agents. Infrastructure requirements may differ. |
| Solo or three-person website chat | 0 core + C once + H | One chat-module purchase per instance, not three seat purchases. Exact total unknown. |
| Chat with offline contact-form fallback | C once + P once + H | Two modules required for this fallback. Operating hours alone do not supply the form. |
| Paid email workflow example | S once + W once + H | Adds saved replies and rules; optional, not required for the base email task. No invented bundled discount. |
| Agent AI, 100 or 500 drafts/summaries | 0 AI module + H + actual provider/inference costs | No exact total: provider/model, input/output sizes and local compute unspecified. No draft-to-resolution conversion. |

The public HTML includes `data-price` numbers such as 13.00 for Chat, 12.99 for End-User Portal, 4.99 for Saved Replies, and 14.99 for Workflows. **These are observations of embedded attributes, not verified sale amounts.** The [site's price-replacement JavaScript](https://freescout.net/wp-content/cache/minify/4d239.js) replaces them using localized FastSpring pricing. Neither those figures' current applicability nor final currency/tax was established. They were not used to produce a false exact total. No checkout or purchase was submitted.

One-time module purchases are separate from recurring hosting and external usage. Do not report a lifetime purchase divided by twelve as FreeScout's monthly subscription. Core licence fee is zero in both monthly and annual views; the rest needs a deployment budget. No hosting quote or labor hours were invented.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Prices/currency/tax:** read catalogue, individual module pages, FAQ, terms and public price-loading JavaScript. Numeric HTML attributes differ from the localized-display mechanism, so current checkout amounts remain unclear. We did not submit checkout or query private pricing endpoints. Exact chat/paid-email totals cannot be guaranteed from those attributes.
- **Operator:** the web reader returned a Hong Kong company attribution in the terms, but direct reads of both hostname variants omitted that sentence. It is preserved under F03 as unresolved, without assuming an owner change or acquisition date.
- **Module rights:** [FAQ](https://freescout.net/modules-faq/) explains AGPL rights and says the one-instance request may be disregarded through modification, yet separately says resale is not allowed. Do not turn the request into an unqualified proprietary single-install restriction or silently resolve the redistribution wording.
- **Refunds:** catalogue/FAQ summarize purchases as non-refundable; the [specific refund policy](https://freescout.net/legal/refund-policy/) permits duplicate-purchase refunds. The specific exception is recorded, not a broad money-back promise.
- **Exports:** [CSV module](https://freescout.net/module/export-conversations/) omits thread text. [API reference](https://api-docs.freescout.net/) documents bodies, note types and attachment links; extraction is implementation work, not a tested complete archive. [Backup](https://github.com/freescout-helpdesk/freescout/wiki/Backup) preserves native application/database state, not an interchangeable export for another product. Article-specific portable export was not found in KB/export/API documentation.
- **Channels:** checked Chat, Facebook, WhatsApp, SMS Tickets, Slack, module catalogue and repository. No official Instagram/Discord channel or native customer mobile SDK verified. Phone tickets do not prove a live phone service. WhatsApp requires external providers and has one-number/customer-initiation limits; Facebook business verification may prevent immediate solo onboarding.
- **AI:** checked module guide and official AI repository. No autonomous customer bot, knowledge-source sync, fixed token allowance, token-to-draft conversion, model tariff, training-use promise or exact language list established. Local provider support does not mean every configuration keeps data local.
- **Retention/privacy/accessibility:** checked installation/FAQ/backup, GDPR module, website privacy, module docs and README. No fixed ticket-history expiry, backup-purge promise, or dated WCAG/VPAT audit found. Website purchase data and self-hosted customer data have different operators and controls.
- **Support:** FAQ/contact/terms specify limited bug fixing, not personal assistance. No response SLA or staffed hours found. Buying paid modules does not purchase managed operations.
- **Lifecycle:** official site/repository/releases were checked first. No official sunset, shutdown or acquisition notice found. Current release and module documentation support active status. Third-party hosting recommendations were not treated as separately sold official editions.
- The terms' old `/privacy/` reference and an initially guessed privacy-policy URL failed; the footer's exact `/legal/privacy/` page succeeded. An initially guessed `/module/live-chat/` failed; official catalogue links `/module/chat/`, which succeeded. These recovered reads are not failed fields.

## Field problems

- F09–F13 must distinguish free core licence, one-time module purchases, and recurring infrastructure. Public localized price loading prevents reliable reuse of static purchase attributes.
- F05 needs source licence rights and activation conventions separately. The paid-module source is AGPL, and the FAQ itself qualifies the single-instance convention.
- F17 must distinguish phone records, Slack event notifications, native chat and customer messaging; they are not equivalent channels.
- F19 must keep free outgoing-email attribution removal separate from paid widget white-labeling and optional interface customization.
- F23/F25/F36 autonomous-agent questions do not apply to the published copilot role. Unknown context limits or provider policy should remain unknown instead of adding unsupported bot features.
- F28 must record that CSV excludes message text. Native database backups and API methods do not prove a complete portable export.
- F30/F31 hosted-region/cancellation-grace fields fit poorly for a self-operated application. Region and backups follow the buyer's infrastructure, while website purchases have a separate privacy policy.
- F35 applies only to an actual customer feedback product; internal Kanban boards do not meet that definition. D01 should allow optional W alongside S/N; no detection rule was tested.
