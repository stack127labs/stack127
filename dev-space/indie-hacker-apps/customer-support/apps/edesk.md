### Autonomous AI — our calculation

Inputs: [outcome rate](https://www.edesk.com/pricing/), [USD/EUR/GBP rate definitions in the public calculator code](https://www.edesk.com/_astro/calculator.astro_astro_type_script_index_0_lang._XjCVkCr.js), and [billable outcome rules, packs and month-end billing](https://support.edesk.com/ai-automation). All checked 2026-11-04.

The actual unit is a **billable automated outcome**. Action outcomes count immediately; reply outcomes require the documented 72-hour checks. Chatbot outcomes have their own 24-hour checks and cannot include human transfer/follow-up. Each ticket is charged once. These examples assume no pack or free promotional allowance and enough qualifying traffic to create the stated number of outcomes.

- 100 billable outcomes × USD 0.99 = **USD 99** usage.
- 500 billable outcomes × USD 0.99 = **USD 495** usage.

| Working users | Monthly base + 100 outcomes | Monthly base + 500 outcomes | Annual-base monthly equivalent + 100 outcomes | Annual-base monthly equivalent + 500 outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | USD 55 + 99 = **USD 154** | USD 55 + 495 = **USD 550** | USD 45 + 99 = **USD 144/month equivalent** | USD 45 + 495 = **USD 540/month equivalent** |
| 3 | USD 165 + 99 = **USD 264** | USD 165 + 495 = **USD 660** | USD 135 + 99 = **USD 234/month equivalent** | USD 135 + 495 = **USD 630/month equivalent** |

For annual subscribers, the actual cash pattern is USD 540 or USD 1,620 base upfront, then actual AI usage billed monthly. The annual-equivalent columns do not mean an annual subscription can be paid monthly. The 500-outcome example is a separate volume example; it does not claim 100 customer conversations can produce 500 billed outcomes. A universal conversation-to-outcome conversion is not published.

AI Assist is optional and excluded. If enabled at published monthly USD rates, it adds our calculation `users × USD 39`: USD 39 for one user or USD 117 for three. On annual pricing it adds `users × USD 30 × 12`: USD 360 or USD 1,080 upfront for a year, equivalent USD 30 or USD 90/month. This helper subscription does not pay for autonomous outcomes.
