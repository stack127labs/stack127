## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: [pricing](https://www.useresponse.com/pricing), its [public calculator script](https://www.useresponse.com/assets/js/landing.js?version=1.0.5), [email setup](https://help.useresponse.com/knowledge-base/article/mailboxes-notifications-tickets-replies), [chat setup](https://help.useresponse.com/knowledge-base/article/setup-live-chat-support), and [AI prices](https://help.useresponse.com/knowledge-base/article/ai-features-overview-and-plans).

Cheapest ongoing offer found is the paid cloud suite. The homepage's USD 0 is a trial, not an ongoing free support plan. The comparison uses one product, one email address and 100 customer conversations per month, one or three working agents including the owner, and no AI. Requests/messages are not billed individually at ordinary usage, so no conversation-to-ticket conversion is needed for this example. Abnormal-activity thresholds remain unspecified.

| One person, email, annual payment | 2 × USD 59 × 10 charged months | **USD 1,180/year upfront** | USD 1,180/12 ≈ USD 98.33/month, rounded to two decimals; no monthly payment at that equivalent rate. |
| Three people, email, monthly payment | max(3, 2) × USD 59/month | **USD 177/month** | Owner plus two colleagues; all are working paid agents. |
