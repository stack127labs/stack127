## Cost examples

Sources: [Helpdesk pricing](https://frappe.io/helpdesk/pricing), [Cloud Site prices/resources](https://frappe.io/cloud/sites), [billing cycle](https://docs.frappe.io/cloud/billing/billing-cycle), [email account setup](https://docs.frappe.io/helpdesk/email-communication) and [tax terms](https://docs.frappe.io/legal/terms/frappe-cloud-terms).

Scenario: one product, one support email address, 100 customer conversations/month, and one or three working agents including the owner. AI off. Neither ticket count nor conversation count determines CPU/storage use exactly. Entry hosting is suitable only if actual usage fits the plan. No provider bill, tax, domain, implementation or labour total is invented.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- **Server headline price:** [Helpdesk pricing](https://frappe.io/helpdesk/pricing) lists USD 20/INR 1,800 and USD 60/INR 5,400 server starting offers. [Cloud pricing](https://frappe.io/cloud/pricing) starts servers at USD 40/INR 3,600. The consistent USD 5 site offer is used for small-team costs; the file does not resolve a server quote by guessing.
- **Storage suspension:** the [site FAQ](https://docs.frappe.io/cloud/faq/site) says seven consecutive over-limit days. The [notice effective 16 June 2025](https://docs.frappe.io/cloud/notice) says fourteen days with daily warnings. Both were read; no exact grace period is guaranteed here.
- **Backups:** the [privacy policy](https://docs.frappe.io/legal/policies/privacy-policy) says backups can remain up to six months, whereas [site backups](https://docs.frappe.io/cloud/sites/backups) list ten yearly restore points as part of rotation. The backup page itself describes both daily automated backups and a six-hour default later on. Entry AWS pricing marks no offsite backup; Hetzner entry pricing marks offsite backups, while the generic backup guide says USD 25+. Do not promise one frequency/retention/entitlement for every provider. Check the chosen site plan before relying on it.
- **Support scope:** [Helpdesk pricing](https://frappe.io/helpdesk/pricing) mentions guidance/feature help under product warranty; [current service terms](https://docs.frappe.io/legal/terms/frappe-cloud-terms) exclude functional how-to, configuration, implementation and training services. The [SLA](https://docs.frappe.io/legal/policies/service-level-agreement) limits standard warranty targets to eligible plans, generally USD 50 and above. Do not apply them to a free install or USD 5 site.
- **Billing precision:** the [current billing-cycle page](https://docs.frappe.io/cloud/billing/billing-cycle) uses calendar-day accrual and shows some rounded samples. The [older billing FAQ](https://docs.frappe.io/cloud/faq/billing) uses a 30-day example. Costs here use full-month headline prices. Twelve-month figures are our spend calculation, not an advertised annual discount. The internal pricing-data name USD 37.5 is not used to override a displayed USD 38 monthly price.
- **Trial card and resource promises:** checked Helpdesk pricing, Cloud pricing, installation/signup explanation, billing FAQ and terms.
