Elite's exact purchase link does display three agents and USD 2,159.64/year, making it a concrete quantity example. This is not a claim that Elite is always the cheapest way to employ three people or enable chat.

For the cheapest solo website-chat route, [ProProfs Chat pricing](https://www.proprofschat.com/pricing/) advertises a free single operator, while Desk Free costs zero. The checked sources do not clearly establish the free connected configuration's 100-conversation capacity and integration gate, so a guaranteed USD 0 combined task total would be premature. A final combined cheapest bill remains unresolved. Elite and Suite above are explicit broader offers; no connected-product fee is silently omitted.

Free's 100-ticket boundary needs care. An email conversation with replies, a WhatsApp message updating an existing ticket, and a new ticket are not interchangeable counts. No complete quota-counting definition was found. AI is off in all base scenarios. Owner is included in the stated headcount; Watcher cannot reply and is not used as a working seat.

The base seat fee is known; incremental AI cost is not. Elite's AI-first ticket allowance is not a price per model call, draft, or resolved outcome.

All amounts retain their published currency and cadence. No conversion or silent rounding was performed. Tax, ordinary external email/website/domain hosting, carrier/Meta/Twilio charges, optional connected subscriptions, and unusual processing/late-payment charges are outside these licence subtotals. A yearly price divided by 12 is not a monthly payment option.
