## Cost examples — our calculation

All inputs checked 2026-11-05 UTC: [pricing, including annual cash totals](https://sitegpt.ai/pricing), [monthly pricing](https://sitegpt.ai/pricing?interval=month), [owner/member counting](https://sitegpt.ai/docs/guides/account/team-members.md), [human-first setup](https://sitegpt.ai/docs/guides/human-support/set-up-human-support.md), [channel modes](https://sitegpt.ai/docs/integrations/overview.md), [email add-on](https://sitegpt.ai/docs/integrations/email.md), [message-count rules](https://sitegpt.ai/docs/reference/plans-and-limits.md), and [model weighting](https://sitegpt.ai/docs/guides/answers/choose-a-model.md).

The owner is one working person. Three people means that owner and two working Agents. Select human-first mode to keep AI off in the base scenarios. Email drafts awaiting approval still consume AI quota, so drafting is not the AI-off scenario. No ongoing free plan exists. There is no separate published fee per human-handled conversation; the monthly message metric is not a 100-conversation allowance. Exactly how many quota-counted messages the 100 conversations create depends on their actual contents, and the sources do not establish every manually written message's billing treatment. These are exact subscription subtotals, not guaranteed all-in totals at an invented conversation-to-message conversion.
