## Cost examples: our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-04: [current USD prices, included resources and annual totals](https://supportbee.com/pricing), [advance payment/cancellation terms](https://supportbee.com/terms), [billing changes](https://supportbee.com/docs/account-settings/administering-billing-and-plan-options), [email setup](https://supportbee.com/docs/email-setup/connecting-your-email-inbox-with-supportbee) and [portal plan gate](https://supportbee.com/docs/customer-portal-software/ticket-tracking-with-customer-portal-software). All results here are our arithmetic, not vendor quotations or verified checkout invoices.

### Cheapest ongoing offer and paid email task

No current ongoing free tier is published. Startup is the cheapest public suitable plan for email. Assume the buyer can purchase exactly one or three full working seats: no higher minimum was found in pricing, signup, terms or billing docs, but a checkout minimum was not independently verified. Do not silently replace working agents with comment-only collaborators.

| Working people | Monthly cash | Annual cash paid upfront | Annual monthly equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One, including the owner | `1 × USD 20 = USD 20/month` | `1 × USD 17 × 12 = USD 204/year` | `USD 204 ÷ 12 = USD 17/month` |
| Three, including the owner | `3 × USD 20 = USD 60/month` | `3 × USD 17 × 12 = USD 612/year` | `USD 612 ÷ 12 = USD 51/month` |

Scenario: one product, one support email address and 100 customer conversations each month; AI off. Both plans explicitly include unlimited email inboxes/tickets. Conversations and tickets need not be identical, but their mapping does not create a stated ticket-volume charge in this offer. Mailbox-provider sending limits and per-reply attachment limits still apply. The source does not publish a tax treatment, mandatory-fee schedule or total account-storage limit; an all-in invoice cannot be guaranteed from the public tariff alone.

### If the customer portal is required

Enterprise is the first current plan that explicitly includes customer portal access and customer-group visibility. This is an optional task extension, not a requirement for ordinary email replies.

| Working people | Monthly cash | Annual cash paid upfront | Annual monthly equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One | `1 × USD 25 = USD 25/month` | `1 × USD 21 × 12 = USD 252/year` | `USD 252 ÷ 12 = USD 21/month` |
| Three | `3 × USD 25 = USD 75/month` | `3 × USD 21 × 12 = USD 756/year` | `USD 756 ÷ 12 = USD 63/month` |

Same seat-count assumption and unknown taxes/external mailbox costs apply. Portal customers use emailed private links rather than working agent seats. The public docs do not specify a separate customer-seat charge; this is not a claim that every future capacity/custom requirement is free.

### Discount wording and changes

The pricing banner says Save 20%. Using its actual amounts, Startup annual savings are `(USD 240 − USD 204) ÷ USD 240 = 15%`. Enterprise savings are `(USD 300 − USD 252) ÷ USD 300 = 16%`. Calculations use the stated annual cash totals, not a twenty-percent reduction that would contradict them.

Payment is monthly or annually in advance. The billing guide describes prorated credits/differences when changing frequency or plan. The terms say cancellation carries no penalty but unused subscription time is not refunded; the billing guide directs cancellation requests to support. Those are different situations, not a promise of a cash refund for an annual cancellation.

### Website human chat and AI

Native website human-chat comparison: **not applicable**, for both one and three people. [SupportBee explicitly describes itself as not a live-chat tool](https://supportbee.com/blog/best-live-chat-software). Hive search and a contact form provide self-service/asynchronous ticket creation. A buyer choosing another live-chat product would need its separate tariff, integration route and usage inputs; no guessed combined total is supplied.

AI100 and AI500 examples: **not calculable from a published native unit**. Current public pricing/docs do not identify a chargeable draft, credit, message, token or autonomous resolution. The vendor's generic AI article and coding-agent integration instructions do not supply such a unit. No zero-cost native AI allowance, external-model bill or 100-conversation-to-100-resolution conversion is invented.
