The AI examples assume the advertised allowance is unused for the selected billing period, and that the base add-on is subject to the subscription payment fee. They count AI messages, not support resolutions or customer conversations. Growth is the stated minimum AI-eligible tier; automatic human handoff additionally needs assignment entitlements/configuration. The price conflict means these are conditional arithmetic, not guaranteed totals. Shopify/voice AI and external services are not included.

For WhatsApp support, 100 customer conversations does not fix the delivery bill. The honest formula is:

`subscription + 2% subscription convenience fee + country/category billable messages × their rates + applicable credit-load convenience fees + optional services + taxes`.

The plan page lists 1,000 free service messages before the service rate applies, but the linked older guide describes monthly service conversations and completely free non-template replies.
