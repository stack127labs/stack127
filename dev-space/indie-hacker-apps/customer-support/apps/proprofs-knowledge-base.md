## Field problems

- F09/F10 need separate audience models. External authors and Internal viewers are different units; the 50-user minimum makes the private headline misleading for a solo buyer unless the minimum total is shown. A 300-page plan does not mean 300 pages per purchased author unless explicitly stated.
- F07/F20/F21 expose inconsistent generic and tier-specific copy. Record the concrete tier and the contradictory wording; do not silently choose unlimited or assume every Free feature has the same eligibility as Elite.
- F14/F15 can price included writing/search using ProProfs’ explicit own-product claims. They cannot price a promised future bot, equate searches with resolutions or turn an uncapped usage claim into unlimited AI storage or rate guarantees.
- F19 must separate logo/design changes, removal of attribution and the custom-domain package. F28 must distinguish a PDF, page-detail export, ZIP backup and API HTML; no one of those establishes a complete migration archive.
- F30/F31 need public/private and free/trial/paid scope. Content reuse/training rights and public retention survive closure in some cases; a generic privacy promise or 30-day cancellation note is not sufficient on its own.
- F33 provides a group WCAG 2.1 AA review commitment, not a product-specific certificate. D02 still needs an exact readable widget signature; a vendor help hostname and a generic ProProfs loader must not be treated as equivalent evidence.
