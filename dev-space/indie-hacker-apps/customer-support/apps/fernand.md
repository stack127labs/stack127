## Cost examples — our calculation

Inputs checked 2026-11-05: [current pricing](https://getfernand.com/pricing/), [active/passive user rules](https://help.getfernand.com/getting-started/common-questions), [terms/currency/taxes](https://getfernand.com/terms/), [widget/email setup](https://help.getfernand.com/getting-started/setting-up-a-communication-channel).

Scenario: one product, one support address, 100 customer conversations/month, one or three working agents including owner; AI off. Both native email and website chat exist on Free and paid. Free's 100 **sent messages**, shared by the workspace, cannot be equated to 100 **customer conversations**. Multiple replies, follow-ups or other counted sends can make that scenario exceed Free. Exact automated/AI/failed-send counting and reset boundaries were not established.

No ISO code is silently assigned to `$`. Terms describe EUR billing for many EU entities and U.S. dollars, Swiss francs or local currency elsewhere, while the public tariff gives `$29`. No exact euro/franc/local tariff or annual amount was found. Taxes are extra. Domain/mailbox, external billing/issue tools and custom action backends are separate if needed. No mandatory setup fee was published; managed helpdesk migration is listed on paid.

**AI examples:** current pricing includes AI replies/chat agent without a per-resolution meter. For a paid account, the published base expression for either 100 or 500 resolutions is still `1 responder × $29 = $29/month`, or `3 responders × $29 = $87/month`; **no additional per-resolution amount is advertised**. This is not a guaranteed throughput, no-fair-use promise or conversion from a resolution to one message. Paid training is listed as 50 MB, Free 10 MB; the older AI guide still states 10 MB total. No separate native model-provider bill is required by the offer. A guaranteed Free 100/500-AI-answer total cannot be calculated without knowing how bot replies count toward the 100-sent-message cap.

**Brand removal:** outgoing Sent via Fernand email line is removed on paid. Known email-removal base is $29/month for one responder or $87/month for three. Pricing also says No branding generally, but precise Free widget/KB attribution and per-surface controls remain unclear.
