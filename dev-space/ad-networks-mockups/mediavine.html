How do you join Mediavine?

You apply with your site and give Mediavine read access to your Google Analytics. They check your traffic and content against the rules here. If you're approved, you add their script and their ads.txt lines, and they set up and run the ads for you.

What if your site is too small for Mediavine?

Mediavine runs a second program, Journey, for smaller sites. It has a lower traffic rule and the same company behind it. Journey's page lists its rules and the sites that use it.

What does the pay day mean?

Mediavine pays each month's earnings a set number of days after the month ends. Earnings from January arrive a couple of months later, not at the start of February. Plan for that gap if you switch from a network that pays faster.

Can you run other ads with Mediavine?

Check the exclusive rule under Rules. A network that wants to be your only display ad partner manages every display ad slot on your site. Affiliate links and sponsored posts are usually separate, but confirm with the network before you sign.

How can you check if a site uses Mediavine?

Look for Mediavine in the site's ads.txt file, or paste the domain into our ad network checker.
