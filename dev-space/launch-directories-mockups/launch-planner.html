How to use the launch planner

  1. Pick what you're launching. A SaaS, an AI tool, a dev tool, or something else.
  2. Set a budget and a goal. Free only is fine. Pick backlinks or users.
  3. Choose how many weeks you have. Slow places start in week 1.
  4. Tick each place as you submit. Your ticks stay in this browser.

How we build your plan

Four simple rules, applied to every place in the directory.

Fits what you make

Only places that list your kind of product.

Fits your budget

Free first. A paid place joins only if its price is stated and fits.

Matches your goal

Backlinks puts dofollow first. Users puts bigger audiences first.

Slow queues go first

A known wait and a working submit page move a place up.

Questions

Why does Uneed go in week 1?

Its free queue takes 30 days to 5 months. Submitting early gives the listing the best chance of going live near your launch.

What does "Not stated" mean?

The place doesn't publish a review time, so we don't guess one. Check back on it a week after you submit.

Do I have to pay for anything?

No. With "Free only", every place in your plan has a free way in. Some sell an upgrade that skips the queue; that's the top of the cost range.

Where are my ticks saved?

In this browser only. Nothing is sent to us. Use Export CSV to keep a copy.
