        Questions

        How many places should I submit to?

        Start with 10 to 15 that fit your product. A few good fits beat 100 random listings.

        Are paid launches worth it?

        Usually only to skip a long free queue. Check the free wait first, it's on every card.

        Will a dofollow link help my rankings?

        A little, sometimes. It helps most when the listing page itself gets indexed, which we show on each place's page.

        What's DR?

        Ahrefs' Domain Rating, a 0 to 100 score of a site's link strength. Treat it as a rough guide, not a promise.

        Do you get paid by these sites?

        No. Nobody pays to be listed or ranked here. We list our own product's launches openly.

        Something's wrong. How do I fix it?

        Email us with the page and what changed. We recheck and update, usually within a week.
