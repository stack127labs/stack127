## How to read this batch

Each candidate will receive one disposition: **include** when all card facts and a live published example can be verified; **exclude** when the current official site or its purpose rules it out; **hold** when one or more useful facts, especially the actual listing link type, cannot be verified. For link type, inspect a real listing's website anchor, record its `href` and `rel` tokens, and follow redirects. A sample describes only that sample. No `dofollow` claim is made merely because a page lacks `nofollow` unless its actual link markup was read.

Category 5 has 38 entries, including Futurepedia. Category 6 has 20 entries, including LibHunt. After skipping those two existing records, this batch covers the remaining 56 candidates.
