# Launch directories research batch 04

Checked: 2026-09-29
Scope: all 14 candidates in Category 8, “Design, no-code, productivity, and creator niches,” from [the candidate inventory](plan.md). No app, database, or publishing changes were made.

**Current decisions:** The first-pass dispositions below are superseded by [research follow-up batch 04](research_followup_batch_04.md). Use that follow-up for the six includes, six holds, and two exclusions.

## Result

No candidate is ready for a public card yet. Twelve active or possibly relevant sites lack at least one required submission or link fact; one site has been repurposed and is excluded; one domain could not be reached. Keep the held sites out of the public catalogue until the missing facts can be verified. In particular, do not label an outbound link dofollow just because the official page links to the product.

The pages below were checked through the official site and search-indexed official pages. The web reader showed outbound destinations but did not expose the sampled anchors' HTML `rel` attributes. I did not infer `follow`, `nofollow`, `sponsored`, or `ugc` from the fact that an outbound link exists. Where the official site did not publish a link policy, the link type is recorded as unverified and the candidate is held.

### Land-book — Hold

- **Official site and scope:** [Landbook](https://land-book.com/) is a curated website-design inspiration gallery. It includes software and SaaS landing pages among broader design examples.
- **Submission route:** [Website submission guidelines](https://land-book.com/submission-guidelines) say anyone may submit a website. The submitter should mark authorship if they created the site; other people's sites can also be submitted for inspiration.
- **Cost and billing:** Website submissions have no fee, per the official guidelines. This is separate from template submissions, which can involve payment after approval.
- **Review timing:** The official guideline says review takes from a few days to up to one month, depending on the queue. If the submitter hears nothing within a month, it means the site was not featured.
- **Eligibility:** Any website may be submitted, but acceptance is curated. Review criteria include design trends, aesthetics, usability, accessibility, and content.
- **Published sample:** [Aloud — Say it. Ship it.](https://land-book.com/websites/100472-aloud-say-it-ship-it-feedback-your-coding-agent-can-run) is a current software/landing-page example visible on the official gallery homepage.
- **Product link type:** The official guideline says nothing about the product link's `rel` type. An external HTML audit reported its own sample findings, but that is not an official policy and was not independently rechecked for this batch. The current sample's `rel` value is therefore unverified here.
- **Why held:** The submission facts are largely clear, but the required link type is not. Also explain it as a design showcase if later included; it is not a launch ranking or product announcement board.

### Lapa.ninja — Hold

- **Official site and scope:** [Lapa Ninja](https://www.lapa.ninja/) is a curated landing-page design gallery with software and startup examples.

- **Cost and billing:** The same policy says featuring is free and the site does not accept payment to include a site in the gallery. It separately labels sponsored placements as ads.

- **Eligibility:** Submitter must own the site or have permission to share it. The gallery selects sites for landing-page design inspiration; submission does not guarantee a feature.
- **Published sample:** [Play website design](https://www.lapa.ninja/post/createwithplay/) is a public product-site entry with a “Visit Website” link.
- **Product link type:** The official policy says each entry links back to the original site, but it does not state `follow`, `nofollow`, `sponsored`, or `ugc`. The sample HTML `rel` value was not available from the page reader.
- **Why held:** The link relation and review timing are not established. This is a design gallery, so the card should describe editorial design exposure rather than imply a product launch placement.

### SaaS Landing Page — Hold

- **Submission route:** The official site links to an [advertising page](https://slpads.framer.website/). I did not find a standard maker-submission form or ordinary listing instructions on the gallery itself.
- **Cost and billing:** The official sponsorship page lists `$500/month` for ongoing sponsorship, `$550` for a one-time feature, and `$500` for one blog article. It does not publish further billing/renewal terms on the accessible page. These are sponsorship/ad prices, not a standard listing fee.
- **Review timing:** Sponsorship begins by contacting the site; no review or go-live timing is published.
- **Eligibility:** The sponsor page describes an audience of designers and founders but does not publish acceptance rules for products.
- **Published sample:** [Slite's gallery page](https://saaslandingpage.com/slite/) is marked “Sponsored” and contains a “Visit Website” link to `slite.com`.

- **Why held:** There is a clear paid exposure offer but not a complete submission flow, timing, eligibility, or link-relation fact set. Revisit only if a sponsored placement qualifies under the directory's scope.
