### FutureTools

- Website: [FutureTools](https://futuretools.io/)
- Submit here: [Submit a Tool](https://futuretools.io/submit-a-tool)
- Cost: Free to submit. Featured listings are priced by quote.
- Review time: A person reviews submissions, but there is no fixed ETA or approval promise. If a tool is not listed after one week, the submitter can resubmit it.
- Eligibility: Public AI software tools. Waitlist-only products, custom GPTs, tool aggregators, blogs, and direct links to Discord, Telegram, or WhatsApp bots are not accepted. The team also checks product and site quality.
- Link type: Follow in the Ankon AI sample. The listing anchor uses href https://futuretools.io/go/ankon-ai-kos341 and rel="noopener noreferrer". It redirected to https://ankonai.com/?ref=futuretools.io and returned 200. This describes the sample, not every listing.
- Sample: [Ankon AI](https://futuretools.io/tools/ankon-ai-kos341)
- Sources: [submission form](https://futuretools.io/submit-a-tool), [FAQ and review rules](https://futuretools.io/faq), [featured listing terms](https://futuretools.io/advertise), [sample](https://futuretools.io/tools/ankon-ai-kos341).

## Exclude

### Console.dev

[Console.dev](https://console.dev/) currently describes itself as a free weekly developer-tools newsletter. Its [selection criteria](https://console.dev/selection-criteria) invite editorial pitches and select a few tools for each issue; it does not provide a searchable product directory or persistent maker listing. The current newsletter links to Rune and Fallow with tracking hrefs and no rel attribute. Those are newsletter links, not directory profiles.

Decision: Exclude from the launch-directory page. It may fit a separate list of developer newsletters and editorial pitches.

## Check method

On 2026-09-29, I checked the official routes and policy pages in the current web reader, then used headless Chrome for public listing pages where raw HTML was reachable. For each accessible sample, I recorded the visible maker href and rel value. I followed redirects for FutureTools, selfh.st, RapidAPI, and Insidr AI. Browser 403 responses, timeouts, SSL errors, and missing submission terms are recorded as limits; they are not treated as proof that a site is closed.
