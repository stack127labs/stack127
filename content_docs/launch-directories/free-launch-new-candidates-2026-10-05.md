# Free launch and listing candidates missing from the inventory

Checked on 2026-10-05. Discovery leads came from broad web searches and the [Nick Launches 2026 roundup](https://nicklaunches.com/blog/where-to-launch-your-startup-2026/); I treated that roundup as a lead list, not evidence. Every free claim below comes from the candidate's own pricing, FAQ, or submission page.

I compared candidate domains and names against [the launch-directory plan](plan.md), its linked research reports, [launch-updates.json](launch-updates.json), and the full [final draft migration](../../supabase/migrations/20261001055949_final_launch_directory_drafts.sql), including hidden and rejected entries. ### How I would prioritize them

1. **LaunchFree and Ignlab Launch:** clearest free terms, no signup, and no reciprocal link condition.
2. **IndieNeed, Favors.dev, and BetterLaunch:** useful current boards; accept staff review, account creation, or a scheduled date as the cost of the free tier.
3. **Aura++, ScrollLaunch, LaunchIt, LaunchIgniter, LetsLaunch, KittyLaunch:** viable if the product fits the board and the maker accepts the queue, badge, or link trade. The badge-backed link terms should be treated as ongoing obligations.
4. **SaaSGrow:** add only as a narrower SaaS directory; it is related to LaunchIgniter, not an independent launch audience.

## Free path is confirmed, but the gate or queue needs care

These are included above because a first-party page confirms $0. The account state is explicit for Favors.dev, BetterLaunch, ScrollLaunch, LetsLaunch, and SaaSGrow; it is visible but not fully established for Aura++ and LaunchIt; IndieNeed and KittyLaunch's static submit pages do not expose their form state; LaunchIgniter's submit route did not expose its form. I did not create accounts or test submission. LaunchIt's current estimate is roughly two months; Firsto, assessed below, reports roughly 180 days and is not in the recommended table.
