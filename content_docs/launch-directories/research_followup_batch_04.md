Pricing correction: a later same-day check of the official [sponsorship page](https://slpads.framer.website/) replaced the SaaS Landing Page amounts below. The earlier $299/month and $350 offer is stale.

## Include in the development catalogue

### NoCodeFinder

- **Official site:** [NoCodeFinder](https://www.nocodefinder.com/) is a directory of no-code software tools.
- **Submit here:** [Submit a No-Code Tool](https://www.nocodefinder.com/submit-no-code-tool). The page's “Email Us” action opens `mailto:jg@nocodefinder.com?subject=NCF%20Request`; it does not contain a product form.
- **Cost:** The submit page publishes no listing price or fee terms. Do not describe it as free.
- **Review time:** No public review turnaround is given.
- **Eligibility:** The page invites no-code tool submissions but gives no more detailed acceptance rules.
- **Example and link:** [Webflow profile](https://www.nocodefinder.com/app/tools/webflow). Its product website anchor points to `https://webflow.com/` and has `rel="nofollow"`.
- **Card wording:** No-code tools; price and review timing are not published; no-code fit is the only public eligibility guidance; nofollow in the Webflow sample.

### NoCodeList

- **Official site:** [NoCodeList](https://nocodelist.co/) is a catalog of no-code software and related resources.
- **Submit here:** [Submit](https://nocodelist.co/submit). The page accepts software, resources, agencies, or product stacks and requires the submitter to sign in. Its email signup is visible on the page.
- **Cost:** The official page does not disclose the ordinary listing fee. Its separate [sponsorship page](https://nocodelist.co/sponsor) lists a homepage ad for `$150/week` and a Friends/Affiliates slot for `$300/year`; these are advertising prices, not listing fees.
- **Review time:** No review or publication deadline is given.
- **Eligibility:** Software, resources, agencies, or product stacks; an account is required. The site publishes no more detailed acceptance rules.
- **Example and link:** [Tango profile](https://nocodelist.co/software/tango). The rendered page has no maker-site anchor; the only external anchor found was the site credit to QREW Tech. Treat this as an observation about this sample, which has sparse product details.
- **Card wording:** A no-code directory with account-required submissions; ordinary listing fee and review time are not published; no maker website link found on the Tango sample.

### Productivity Directory

- **Official site:** [Productivity Directory](https://productivity.directory/) lists productivity apps and software.
- **Submit here:** [Submit Yours](https://productivity.directory/s/submit). Visitors are sent to sign-in. The official sign-in page says accounts are free and signed-in users can submit and manage their own listings.
- **Cost:** Free account; the listing fee is not published.
- **Review time:** No public review or publication timing is given.
- **Eligibility:** A signed-in user can submit and manage a listing. Detailed product acceptance rules are not published on the public route.
- **Example and link:** [Todoist profile](https://productivity.directory/todoist). The Website anchor points to `https://get.todoist.io/xy63ttsdn6ve` and has no `rel` attribute or qualifier.
- **Card wording:** Productivity software; free account required; listing price and timing are not published; follow link in the Todoist sample. The sample uses a tracked redirect URL.

### Land-book

- **Official site:** [Land-book](https://land-book.com/) is a curated website-design gallery, including product and SaaS sites.
- **Submit here:** [Website submission guidelines](https://land-book.com/submission-guidelines).
- **Cost:** The official guidelines say website submissions are free. This does not apply to separately sold templates.
- **Review time:** A few days to one month, depending on the queue; no reply within a month means the site was not featured.
- **Eligibility:** Any website may be submitted, but the gallery selects entries for design, usability, accessibility, and content. Submitters must identify their authorship; other people's sites can be sent for inspiration.
- **Example and link:** [Aloud listing](https://land-book.com/websites/100472-aloud-say-it-ship-it-feedback-your-coding-agent-can-run). The Visit anchor points to `https://www.aloud.sh/?ref=land-book.com` and has `rel="noopener"` only.
- **Card wording:** A curated design showcase rather than a launch ranking; free website submissions; a few days to one month; follow link in the Aloud sample.

### One Page Love

- **Official site:** [One Page Love](https://onepagelove.com/) is a gallery of one-page sites and templates.
- **Submit here:** [Submit your One Pager](https://onepagelove.com/submit). The live form accepts a site URL and a live demo URL.
- **Cost:** The submission page does not state a listing fee. Do not call it free.
- **Review time:** Submissions are reviewed daily; there is no decision deadline. The site emails the submitter if the page is a good fit.
- **Eligibility:** The site must be a one-page website. Main navigation must not link to separate pages; the form gives accepted and rejected examples.
- **Example and link:** [Flowjam listing](https://onepagelove.com/flowjam-2). The Visit Website anchor points to `https://flowjam.com/?ref=onepagelove` and has `rel="noopener"` only.
- **Card wording:** One-page sites only; price is not shown on the submission form; daily review with no promised decision date; follow link in the Flowjam sample.

### SaaS Landing Page

- **Official site:** [SaaS Landing Page](https://saaslandingpage.com/) is a gallery of SaaS landing pages. Its current offer is paid sponsorship, not a standard free product-submission form.
- **Submit here:** [Sponsor SaaS Landing Page](https://slpads.framer.website/), linked from the gallery's Advertise action; its Get in Touch link opens the sponsor contact route.
- **Cost:** Sponsorship is `$500/month` or `$550` one time; a one-article blog feature is `$500`. These are advertising packages, not ordinary listing fees.
- **Review time:** No listing review timeline is published; the advertiser is directed to contact the publisher to arrange a campaign.
- **Eligibility:** SaaS products seeking a paid home-feed placement for an audience of designers, developers, and founders. The public sponsor page does not give detailed acceptance criteria.
- **Example and link:** [Slite page](https://saaslandingpage.com/slite/) is marked Sponsored. Its Visit Website anchor points to `https://slite.com/` and has `rel="noopener nofollow"`.
- **Card wording:** Paid promotion; `$500/month` or `$550` once; one blog feature is `$500`; no published review schedule; nofollow in the Slite sponsored sample.

### Lapa Ninja

- **Official site:** [Lapa Ninja](https://www.lapa.ninja/) is a curated gallery of landing-page designs.
- **Submit here:** [Submission policy](https://www.lapa.ninja/policy/). It says a site owner or authorized person may email `hi@lapa.ninja` with a site for consideration.
- **Cost:** Editorial featuring is free.
- **Review time:** No decision deadline is published.
- **Eligibility:** Site owners or authorized submitters; the publisher chooses sites for the gallery.
- **Example and link:** [Play listing](https://www.lapa.ninja/post/createwithplay/). A direct raw HTML check on 2026-09-29 found the Visit Website anchor at `https://www.createwithplay.com/?ref=lapaninja` with `rel="nofollow noopener"`.
- **Card wording:** Free editorial submission; no review deadline; site owners or authorized submitters; nofollow in the Play sample.

## Hold from the public catalogue

### NoCode.Tech

The current [tool catalog](https://www.nocode.tech/tools) is live and the [Adalo sample](https://www.nocode.tech/tools/adalo) links to `https://www.adalo.com/?via=nile` with `rel="noreferrer noopener"` only. The [contact page](https://www.nocode.tech/contact) is a general inquiry form and does not invite or explain product submissions. No public listing price, review time, or acceptance rules were found. **Hold because a product-submission route is not verified.**

### Makerlist

The [homepage](https://makerlist.io/) says “Login to submit,” and [the submit route](https://makerlist.io/submit) redirects an unsigned visitor to Google sign-in. The current [Terms](https://makerlist.io/terms) and public pages give no listing price, review timing, or acceptance rules. A live raw HTML check of the [listai.tools sample](https://makerlist.io/tool/53108-listai-tools) found the Visit Website anchors have no `rel` qualifier, so that sample is follow. Its “free submission / paid tiers” text is the listed tool's own description, not verified Makerlist terms. **Hold until the listing terms can be established.**

### ToolsForCreators

The exact inventoried [toolsforcreators.com](https://toolsforcreators.com/) domain timed out in the current browser check. The separate [toolsforcreators.org](https://toolsforcreators.org/) catalog is not treated as an alias: no official source ties the domains together and no maker-submission route for the `.com` candidate was found. **Hold; do not substitute the `.org` site.**

### MarketingTools.com

The exact [marketingtools.com](https://marketingtools.com/) domain timed out in the current browser check, and no current first-party listing or submission page could be confirmed. Similar names are not treated as aliases. **Hold; the outage does not prove that the domain is retired.**

### SiteInspire

The live [gallery](https://www.siteinspire.com/) is a curated design showcase. Its old [submit path](https://www.siteinspire.com/submit) returns 404, while the footer provides a general contact email without saying it accepts site submissions. A live raw HTML check of [The Future In Black sample](https://www.siteinspire.com/website/13635-the-future-in-black) found a Visit anchor with `rel="noreferrer"` only, so that sample link is follow. **Hold because a public submission route is not confirmed.**

## Exclude from the current roster

- **Remote Tools:** The current [official site](https://www.remote.tools/) is a social-platform how-to blog; the former [post-product route](https://www.remote.tools/post-product) redirects to the homepage. It no longer matches the product-submission catalog.
- **Startuplandingpages.com:** The exact inventoried [plural domain](https://startuplandingpages.com/) currently returns Framer's “Site Not Found” page. The separate singular [startuplandingpage.com](https://startuplandingpage.com/) is not treated as the same service because no official alias relationship or maker-submit route was found.

## Coverage

All 14 Category 8 leads in the inventory are accounted for. Seven have complete cards; five remain held for the specific missing facts above; two are excluded. The Lapa Ninja sample was upgraded after a direct raw HTML check confirmed its nofollow relation. The exclusions reflect current scope or domain state, not an assumption based on an old third-party directory list.
