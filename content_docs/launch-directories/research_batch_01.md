# Launch directories research batch 01

Checked: 2026-09-29
Scope: categories 1 and 2 from the candidate inventory. Product Hunt, Uneed, Fazier, and TinyLaunch are the four existing sample records and were skipped as requested.

## Research method and link evidence

The assigned set has 42 candidates after those four existing records are skipped. Every assigned candidate below has an include, hold, or exclude decision. An included record has a current official home and submission route, a cost and billing description, a timing description, eligibility, a real public example, and a useful link-type value.

The current browser session was unavailable, so this batch did not inspect raw HTML link attributes on the public examples. Where an included card says `dofollow` or `nofollow`, it is a policy statement from the site's own published page. ComingUp's third-party link audit is listed as an estimate but the site is held for other missing terms. No link is called dofollow just because its HTML was not inspected. Sites without a reliable policy or independent observation are held.

The card-ready section gives a direct public example for every included site. For holds and exclusions, an official homepage or board link is not treated as proof of a sample product detail page; when no direct sample could be established, that gap is part of the decision. Inaccessible, parked, duplicate, agency, or non-directory sites have no eligible product listing to sample.
