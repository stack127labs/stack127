# Brief: Domain Registrant Lookup

## Current supporting-content direction

Approved September 6, 2026. Explain published identity versus unknown fields, declared redaction, appropriate contact paths, privacy versus proxy services, and historical-source differences. Avoid blanket legal or identity claims.

The working tool stays first. Follow `stack127_docs/writing-style.md` for writing and visual choices. Store live copy only in the development tool-page row through the reviewed content command. Publication remains a separate operation.

## Who is here

A reader wants to know what is publicly available about a domain's registrant and how to make legitimate contact when identity is withheld.

## The job

They leave knowing what was published, exactly what the registrar redacted, which contact route exists, and which questions public RDAP cannot answer.

## Show vs say

Tables separate published entities from RFC 9537 redaction declarations and translate each redaction method. A small source relationship shows registry versus registrar data. Sentences explain privacy policy, proxy services, legitimate request paths, and coverage.
