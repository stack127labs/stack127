# kdp-tools

- Reader: Independent authors preparing a Kindle or print book.
- Task: Pick the right KDP check before uploading or pricing a book.
- Explanation and visual: Explain which checks use local files, which are estimates, and when KDP remains the final source. Use the dedicated open book marker drawing on the catalog page and homepage card.
- Source of public copy: `stack127_catalog_pages` for the page, `stack127_tool_pages` for cards, and `stack127_pages` for publication and SEO.
