# Brief: Domain Expiry Checker

## Who is here

A domain owner or prospective buyer needs the registry expiry date and wants to understand what happens after it.

## The job

They leave with time remaining or elapsed, the current lifecycle state, and an honest understanding that expiry is not a predicted availability date.

## Show vs say

A timeline shows registration, today, expiry, grace, redemption, pending delete, and release. Tables carry registry and registrar dates plus status evidence. Sentences explain variable grace behavior, auto-renew uncertainty, ccTLD differences, and drop-catching limits.
