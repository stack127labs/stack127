# Brief: Domain Registration Lookup

## Current supporting-content direction

Approved September 6, 2026. Start with the public registration question, explain accepted names, then use a field table and source/date interpretation. Follow with redaction, focused next checks, and remote data handling. Keep registration history and missing evidence distinct from current published facts.

The working tool stays first. Follow `stack127_docs/writing-style.md` for writing and visual choices. Store live copy only in the development tool-page row through the reviewed content command. Publication remains a separate operation.

## Who is here

A reader needs the complete current registration record for one domain and wants to know which facts came from the registry or registrar.

## The job

They leave with dates, registrar, statuses, nameservers, DNSSEC delegation, contacts, redaction, source identity, and raw responses without conflating the two data providers.

## Show vs say

The composed answer leads. Tables carry repeated dates, statuses, nameservers, and contact fields; a small registry-to-registrar figure explains ownership. Sentences interpret conflicting dates, redaction, coverage, source freshness, and lookup limits.
