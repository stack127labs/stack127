# Brief: TLD Directory

## Who is here

A domain researcher or operator needs a filterable source-backed view of the root zone and version127's RDAP coverage.

## The job

They leave knowing who operates a TLD, its type, RDAP availability, DNSSEC state, IDN form, source, and check date.

## Show vs say

The searchable table is the page. Repeated TLD facts stay in columns and exact counts remain data-driven. Sentences explain TLD types, RDAP coverage, TLD-level DNSSEC, refresh cadence, and why prices, restrictions, and rankings are excluded.
