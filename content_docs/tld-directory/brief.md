# Brief: TLD Directory

## Who is here

A domain researcher or operator needs a filterable source-backed view of the root zone and stack127's RDAP coverage.

## The job

They leave knowing who operates a TLD, its type, RDAP availability, DNSSEC state, IDN form, source, and check date.

## Show vs say

The searchable table is the page. Repeated TLD facts stay in columns and exact counts remain data-driven. Sentences explain TLD types, RDAP coverage, TLD-level DNSSEC, refresh cadence, and why prices, restrictions, and rankings are excluded.

## Search words

Checked 2026-10-19. See "Find the words first" in `stack127_docs/writing-style.md`.

- Main phrase: list of all TLDs, domain extensions list.
- Words top titles add: a count, all, every, full list.
- SEO title: TLD list: 1,400+ domain extensions and who runs each one. The count is rounded down; update it when the records pass the next round number.
