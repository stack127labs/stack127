# Brief: Registrar Lookup

## Who is here

A domain owner or abuse reporter needs to identify the registrar, find the right contact, or understand an unfamiliar reseller relationship.

## The job

They leave with the registrar identity, IANA ID, abuse route, relevant dates, and a practical next step for access, transfer, or reporting.

## Show vs say

The registrar answer and contacts lead. A compact registry-to-registrar-to-reseller figure explains the roles; structured rows carry identifiers and source dates. Sentences cover reseller ambiguity, abuse scope, access recovery limits, and transfer history uncertainty.
