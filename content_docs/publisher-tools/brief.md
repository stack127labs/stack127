# Publisher tools

Route: `/publisher-tools`. Family: `catalog`. Development only until release authorization.

## Reader and task

Solo bloggers and content-site owners deciding which ad networks to investigate. They need to check published joining rules, see which network another site uses, and compare the networks themselves.

## Page plan

- Use the existing catalog route, shared shell, cards, and left catalog sidebar.
- Intro: start with the reader's decision and the two available checks.
- Article: a short question-to-tool table, then why a good fit is an estimate and not an approval.
- Link the ad networks directory as the next comparison step. Do not promise earnings estimates, acceptance odds, or traffic estimates.
- No quantitative chart: there are no catalog-level measurements to show.

## Initial membership

1. `ad-network-requirements-checker`, primary catalog.
2. `ad-network-checker`, move its primary catalog from Domain tools to Publisher tools. Keep any useful existing secondary memberships only after reviewing them in development.

## Publication fields

- H1: `Free publisher tools for choosing an ad network`
- SEO title: `Free publisher tools: check ad networks and requirements`
- SEO description: `Find which ad networks to apply to, best first, and check which networks other sites use. Compare joining rules, sources, and real sites in your niche.`
- Card name: `Publisher tools`
- Card description: `Check ad network requirements and see which networks other sites use.`
- Icon: use a registered publishing-related icon.
- Art brief: a small browser page beside two different gates, one with a measuring mark and one showing a tag. One simple drawing that represents choosing where a content site fits. Integration owns final marker art and registered key.
