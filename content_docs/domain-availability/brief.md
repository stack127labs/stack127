## Who is here

Someone wants to know whether a domain currently exists in its registry without using a registrar sales search.

## The job

They leave with one of three honest outcomes: registered, not registered, or cannot tell, plus the limits around reserved, premium, cached, and recently released names.

## Show vs say

The registry-backed answer and supporting registration facts come first. A small state comparison distinguishes the three outcomes. Sentences carry purchasing caveats, freshness, privacy, and why DNS is not an availability test.
