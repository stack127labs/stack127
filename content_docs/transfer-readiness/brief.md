# Brief: Domain Transfer Readiness

## Who is here

A domain owner is preparing a registrar transfer and needs to separate public blockers from account details the site cannot see.

## The job

They leave with a conclusion-first list of locks, policy timing, expiry risk, DNSSEC and nameserver concerns, plus the items they must check in their registrar account.

## Show vs say

An ordered findings list carries blocker, risk, okay, and unknown states with evidence. A short transfer sequence shows the operational steps. Sentences explain policy versioning, ccTLD differences, invisible account state, and what can break after transfer.
