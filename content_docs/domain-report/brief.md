# Brief: Domain Registration Report

## Who is here

A domain operator wants one snapshot that connects public registration facts with live DNS behavior and highlights disagreements between those systems.

## The job

They leave with a natural summary, independently reported checks, cross-source findings, and direct paths into the focused tool for each issue.

## Show vs say

Findings group by attention, decision, and context, each with its source evidence. A registry-parent-child figure shows where nameserver and DNSSEC facts can disagree. Sentences carry the summary, scope, failed-check disclosure, and snapshot limitations; there is no score.
