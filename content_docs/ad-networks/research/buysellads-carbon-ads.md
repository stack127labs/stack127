### Key numbers

- **Withdrawal balance:** $1 — Method minimums: $20 PayPal, $50 check, $500 wire; fees may apply. Source: [https://www.carbonads.net/faq](https://www.carbonads.net/faq).
- **Revenue timing:** Posted within 30 days; withdrawable within 60 days — Open Collective quarterly by request. Source: [https://www.carbonads.net/faq](https://www.carbonads.net/faq).

### Joining and payment

- **Application:** Invite-only; review usually 5–7 business days — Reviews traffic, relevance, maintenance and capacity. Source: [https://www.carbonads.net/faq](https://www.carbonads.net/faq).
- **Audience:** English-speaking developer/design audience; US, Canada, Europe strongest — Other areas depend on demand. Source: [https://www.carbonads.net/faq](https://www.carbonads.net/faq).
- **Exclusivity:** Carbon must be the only ads on the site; no backfill — Replacement, not add-on. Source: [https://www.carbonads.net/faq](https://www.carbonads.net/faq).
- **Traffic:** Monthly pageviews reviewed; no fixed minimum. Source: [https://www.carbonads.net/faq](https://www.carbonads.net/faq).
- Revenue share: Not public.
- Payment terms: Earnings posted monthly within 30 days; withdrawal within 60 days.
- Payment threshold: $1 before method minimums.
- Payment methods: PayPal, Check, Wire transfer, Open Collective, quarterly by request.
