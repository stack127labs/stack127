# SHE Media

Checked 2026-10-23.

## Official facts

SHE Media's current FAQ says its standard approval bar is **50,000 monthly pageviews**. It may consider a smaller site case by case when content is interesting, publishing is frequent and consistent, and growth potential is strong. The 50K figure is therefore the normal approval requirement with discretionary lower review, not merely a typical site size. Other current criteria are at least 30% US traffic, domain age of at least 90 days, original high-quality and brand-safe content, a good standing with Google ad products, and human-led content. It offers managed display, outstream video, native formats, sponsored campaigns, and creator support. The FAQ says 3–4 weeks for a response, not a final approval guarantee. Its current application asks for average monthly pageviews in Google Analytics, current ad networks, and publishing platform; it says Wix-built sites are ineligible and requests GA authorization to verify pageviews. Current terms say Net 35. Existing member guide (updated March 2023) says 75% share and $20 minimum; newer international guidance describes additional payout routes, so keep those older figures dated.

## Dated publisher reports

- **Oct 2020 (publisher blog)**: a lifestyle-blog owner reported $124 for a month at 25.5K sessions. This is below the standard 50K pageview figure but sessions and pageviews are different measures; the report is historical and does not prove SHE accepted the site below the rule or that the month was representative. [Review](https://imperfectidealist.com/she-media-review/)
- Targeted searches found few recent independent accounts with an application decision and explicit pageviews; vendor member stories do not fill that gap.

## Editorial inference

Strong fit for US-oriented, original creator/blog content, especially sites that can support managed ad placements and value creator programs. At 25K sessions, do not recommend based on that number alone; ask for pageviews and US traffic. A smaller site may be worth an application when it clearly meets the editorial/growth exception, but there is no public lower cutoff.

## Directory-ready corrections

- State 50K monthly PV as standard approval requirement, with case-by-case exceptions below.
- Add 30% US, 90-day age, Google-standing, and human-led original content checks.
- Do not reuse the old $124 / 25.5K-session example as a traffic floor or forecast.
- Qualify share, threshold, and international methods by the dated guide/support article.

## Evidence gaps

No current exact payout share for every revenue type or recent independent sub-50K PV approval was found. The 2020 report uses sessions rather than PV.
