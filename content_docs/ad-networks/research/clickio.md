# Clickio

Checked 2026-10-16.

## Joining

- Traffic: 100,000 to 1,000,000 pageviews by region. Note: Monthly; $500 in monthly revenue may qualify below the threshold. Source: https://docs.clickio.com/books/clickio-entry-standards/page/clickio-entry-standards. Read 2026-10-16.
- Visitor location: Sanctioned countries excluded. Note: Pageview thresholds also vary by region. Source: https://docs.clickio.com/books/clickio-entry-standards/page/clickio-entry-standards. Read 2026-10-16.
- Content: Follow AdSense policies and third-party rights rules. Source: https://docs.clickio.com/books/clickio-entry-standards/page/clickio-entry-standards. Read 2026-10-16.
- Google standing: Not public. The page requires policy compliance but does not state an account standing requirement. Source checked: https://docs.clickio.com/books/clickio-entry-standards/page/clickio-entry-standards. Read 2026-10-16.
- Must connect: Publish ads.txt; provide GA4 or a six-month report if requested. Source: https://docs.clickio.com/books/clickio-entry-standards/page/clickio-entry-standards. Read 2026-10-16.
- Approval time: Not public. The reviewed official pages do not state this rule. Source checked: https://docs.clickio.com/books/clickio-entry-standards/page/clickio-entry-standards. Read 2026-10-16.
- Site history: Six months of traffic data if external verification is unavailable. Source: https://docs.clickio.com/books/clickio-entry-standards/page/clickio-entry-standards. Read 2026-10-16.
- Ad revenue: $500 a month. Note: Possible traffic alternative; ongoing minimum account earnings. Source: https://docs.clickio.com/books/clickio-entry-standards/page/clickio-entry-standards. Read 2026-10-16.

## Pay

- No standard revenue-share rate is public. Clickio bills monthly. The payment page states Net 60, payment around the 25th to 30th two months later, and method-specific thresholds: $100 equivalent for ACH/eCheck/SEPA, check, and PayPal; $200 wire; WebMoney $10; Payoneer GBP 100. Local-currency options are available for some methods. (Source: https://docs.clickio.com/books/get-started/page/payments-billing, read 2026-10-16.)

## Rules

- Clickio's entry page states no publisher exclusivity or contract notice rule. The separate Clickio Prism CDN integration has separate requirements and is not the general managed service. (Sources: https://docs.clickio.com/books/clickio-entry-standards/page/clickio-entry-standards and https://docs.clickio.com/books/clickio-prism/page/clickio-cdn-integration-for-non-wordpress-sites, read 2026-10-16.)

## Service

- Fully Managed Service includes real-time reporting, an account manager, and technical/ad-operations support. Unified Reports can combine Clickio, AdSense/Ad Manager, and Google Analytics reporting and export data. (Sources: https://docs.clickio.com/books/clickio-entry-standards/page/clickio-entry-standards and https://docs.clickio.com/books/clickio-publisher-platform/page/unified-reports, read 2026-10-16.)
