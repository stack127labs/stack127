# PubGalaxy

Checked 2026-10-22.

## Official facts

PubGalaxy is no longer a live, separately presented publisher offer. Its publisher, terms, and Learn Center URLs now redirect to Improve Digital. Azerion announced on **September 23, 2026** that it unified the publisher supply business under the Improve Digital name, replacing the formerly separate offers. The current Improve Digital page describes two routes: **Yield**, a managed header-bidding/yield service for publishers without a dedicated ad-ops team, and **SSP**, a demand platform for publishers who run their own stack. The current access form asks whether the applicant wants Yield or SSP. The former PubGalaxy payment and entry terms cannot be treated as current Improve Digital terms.

The Improve Digital page offers an access request, but several older contact URLs currently resolve to a 404. The product page itself has the current request-access form; use that only when discussing the replacement offer.

## Dated publisher reports

- Legacy PubGalaxy customer logos and old case studies were found, but no current, independently reported PubGalaxy application example with traffic and outcome. Historical seller lines are not current admission proof.
- Searches for current Improve Digital publisher entry examples did not surface independent traffic-and-approval reports. Current official examples describe product type, not eligibility numbers.

## Editorial inference

A shortlist should mark PubGalaxy as **retired/migrated** and avoid ranking it as an available application option. If the person wants a similar current service, Improve Digital may be a sales-led alternative: Yield can replace a managed stack; SSP can supplement a publisher-owned stack. Neither route has a public traffic or payment threshold verified here. This distinction matters for current-network compatibility and the recommendation should not infer eligibility from a legacy PubGalaxy relationship.

## Directory-ready corrections

- Set `applicationUrl` to null for PubGalaxy and say the standalone offer is unavailable.
- Remove prior PubGalaxy payout/traffic numbers from current facts unless clearly labeled as historical contract terms.
- Link the Improve Digital access form as a separate alternative, not as a PubGalaxy application.

## Evidence gaps

Improve Digital's public pages do not expose a numeric joining floor or standard payment terms. Its home page showed a request-access form, while the linked generic contact route returned 404 during this check.
