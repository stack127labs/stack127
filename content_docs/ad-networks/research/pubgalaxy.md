# PubGalaxy

## Status (read 2026-10-16)

- Azerion bought PubGalaxy in late 2021 (https://www.thedrum.com/news/2021/10/21/gaming-platform-azerion-acquires-pubgalaxy-and-inskin-media). On 2026-09-23 Azerion moved its publisher services under the Improve Digital name (https://exchangewire.com/blog/2026/09/23/azerion-unifies-publisher-supply-business-under-improve-digital/). pubgalaxy.com now redirects to improvedigital.com.
- PubGalaxy's sellers.json is still live (https://cdn.pubgalaxy.com/sellers.json, 7,187 rows), and sites still name `MANAGERDOMAIN=pubgalaxy.com` in ads.txt. We keep PubGalaxy as its own network. If sites start naming an Improve Digital manager line, add Improve Digital as a network then. Don't use `improvedigital.com` seller lines as manager evidence: it appears in most ads.txt files as an exchange.

## Pay

- Published terms give publishers at least 85% of net partner revenue and at least 65% of net direct revenue. Payment is within 35 days after month-end once PubGalaxy receives advertiser funds. Threshold is $50 for PayPal or $100 for wire. Methods are PayPal and wire. Currency is not stated in the cited terms. (Source: https://www.pubgalaxy.com/terms-and-conditions/, read 2026-10-16.)

## Rules

- The terms say the publisher must own or license its content and permit PubGalaxy to manage ad inventory. They allow changes needed to improve user experience and describe direct deal obligations. The Learn Center says specific terms can vary. Exclusivity and notice should be checked in the signed order. (Sources: https://www.pubgalaxy.com/terms-and-conditions/ and https://www.pubgalaxy.com/learn-center/, read 2026-10-16.)

## Service

- The publisher Learn Center describes granular earnings reporting and direct/private-marketplace deal access. Exact support response times are not public. (Source: https://www.pubgalaxy.com/learn-center/, read 2026-10-16.)
