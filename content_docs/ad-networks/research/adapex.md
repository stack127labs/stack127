## Pay

- The FAQ says publishers are paid Net 60. Share rate, minimum payment, payment methods, and payout currency are not public. (Source: https://adapex.io/faqs/, read 2026-10-16.)

## Rules

- No long-term contract is required. A longer contract may provide preferential shares or features. The public FAQ does not state exclusivity or notice length. (Source: https://adapex.io/faqs/, read 2026-10-16.)
- Publishers can block categories, advertisers, and domains and can set category controls and floors. (Source: https://adapex.io/faqs/, read 2026-10-16.)

## Service

- A publisher dashboard reports revenue and traffic near real time, including geography and device. The FAQ says publishers retain their GAM account. (Source: https://adapex.io/faqs/, read 2026-10-16.)
