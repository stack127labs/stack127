## Editorial inference

### Practical fit

Shortlist when a publisher can verify either 2m monthly pageviews or $5k monthly ad revenue and needs managed yield optimization. A solo blog below both is not a PubGuru Premium fit.

### Estimated range and confidence

- Traffic reference: **2,000,000–2,000,000 pageviews per month**; confidence: high. This is not an admission guarantee.
  - Alternative qualifying route is $5k monthly ad revenue.
  - Basis: [https://www.monetizemore.com/solutions/pubguru-premium/](https://www.monetizemore.com/solutions/pubguru-premium/).
- Revenue reference: **5,000–5,000 USD ad revenue per month**; confidence: high.
  - Premium alternative to 2m monthly PV.
  - Basis: [https://www.monetizemore.com/solutions/pubguru-premium/](https://www.monetizemore.com/solutions/pubguru-premium/).

- Geography: No public country rule found.
- Site age: No public age minimum found.
- Confidence: **high**. Current PubGuru Premium page explicitly publishes both thresholds. Case studies confirm very large publisher profile but are vendor-reported.

### Current-network compatibility

- Use of GAM or other demand partners can aid setup; not transitive proof of qualification.
- Managed header bidding/yield service can work with existing GAM/demand; confirm exclusivity and notice period in agreement.
