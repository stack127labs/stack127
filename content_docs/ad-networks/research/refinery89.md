# Refinery89

Checked 2026-10-23.

## Official facts

Refinery89's current eligibility FAQ says publishers should contact its team to discuss the site's needs; it publishes no pageview or revenue minimum. Its service FAQ says it works with publishers worldwide. The current Get Started form accepts a site or app, asks for a pageview band and country, and offers account registration; those are intake fields, not a published cutoff. No fixed approval time is published. The FAQ describes no lock-in to start, with later commercial terms set by agreement. Payment FAQs list a €250 or $250 monthly payout threshold (regional variation is possible), Tipalti methods such as transfer, eCheck, wire, and PayPal, and no standard payment timing: the publisher should confirm it with the account manager. A universal revenue share is not public.

## Dated publisher reports

- **2025 (Trustpilot)**: one reviewer said they received no response after applying; the review provided no traffic, niche, or country. In a reply dated Feb 2026, Refinery89 said site review includes traffic and content but did not give a numeric threshold. This is a single unverified complaint and reply. [Reviews](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/refinery89.com)
- Refinery89's case study describes work with a French local-news publisher, and another company-authored story discusses a Netherlands lifestyle publisher. These are vendor case studies without public traffic or application criteria, not independent proof of a minimum. [Case study](https://refinery89.com/how-a-partnership-built-on-trust-accelerates-ad-revenue-growth/)

## Editorial inference

Treat it as a sales-led managed option for publishers looking for a single tag, ad management, or app/web monetization, with worldwide availability but undisclosed volume screening. It may be worth an inquiry for a publisher whose traffic is outside the largest Tier 1 markets, but there is no public evidence that every geography is equally attractive. No numeric range is justified. Monthly ad revenue, country mix, and current stack are useful for the initial conversation, not eligibility proxies.

## Directory-ready corrections

- Replace “no specific minimum” with “no public traffic minimum; contact the team for site review.”
- State worldwide service as availability, not a preferred geography or guaranteed acceptance.
- Keep €250/$250 as a payout threshold only; do not present it as an application revenue floor.
- Keep payment timing and revenue share contract-specific.

## Evidence gaps

No numeric entry examples, standardized payment schedule, revenue share, or independent successful application report with traffic data was found.
