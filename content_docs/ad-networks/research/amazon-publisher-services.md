## Pay

- UAM collects a 10% transaction fee from SSP bid prices before the first-price auction. It consolidates revenue into one monthly payment on Net 60 terms, with a $5 threshold. The payout method and currency are not stated in the PDF. (Source: https://aps.amazon.com/aps/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/aps_uam_update_011123-FINAL.pdf, read 2026-10-16.)
