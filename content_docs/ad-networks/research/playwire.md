# Playwire

Checked 2026-10-16.

## Joining

- Traffic: 500,000 monthly pageviews or 1,000 daily active users. Note: Qualification page separately suggests applying from 100,000 monthly pageviews. Source: https://www.playwire.com/faq. Read 2026-10-16.
- Visitor location: At least 20% of traffic from the US. Note: Qualification guidance. Source: https://www.playwire.com/qualification-criteria. Read 2026-10-16.
- Content: Unique, original content. Source: https://www.playwire.com/qualification-criteria. Read 2026-10-16.
- Google standing: Not blacklisted by Google or major ad providers. Source: https://www.playwire.com/qualification-criteria. Read 2026-10-16.
- Must connect: Implement the Playwire SDK. Note: Required to access its demand. Source: https://www.playwire.com/faq. Read 2026-10-16.
- Approval time: Not public. The reviewed official pages do not state this rule. Source checked: https://www.playwire.com/faq and https://www.playwire.com/qualification-criteria. Read 2026-10-16.

## Pay

- Playwire says payments begin monthly on Net 60 terms. It describes revenue sharing but does not publish the rate, threshold, methods, or currency on its FAQ. (Source: https://www.playwire.com/faq, read 2026-10-16.)

## Rules

- Playwire says it recommends ad layouts but publishers have final say. Its FAQ says other ad network tags cannot run together with the Playwire script. The pages do not publish contract length or notice. The quality requirements include original content and privacy/security basics. (Sources: https://www.playwire.com/faq and https://www.playwire.com/qualification-criteria, read 2026-10-16.)

## Service

- Playwire provides a dashboard, campaign tracking, and dedicated publisher-success, yield, and technical teams. (Source: https://www.playwire.com/faq, read 2026-10-16.)
