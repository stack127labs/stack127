# Revcontent

Checked 2026-10-22.

## Official facts

Revcontent's current Publisher Help article explicitly says there is no minimum pageview or traffic requirement. It also says each application and each domain is reviewed by a human team case by case; approval of the publisher account does not guarantee that every site/widget will be approved. Apply through its publisher application form; approved domains receive placement choices and widget code. The product is a content-recommendation/native-ad widget and supplemental demand, not a full display-stack replacement. Publisher terms bar categories including illegal or infringing content, pornography/obscenity, illicit drugs, and unlawful gambling. Payment methods depend on location; official payment help requires a complete payee profile and tax form. Existing public agreement facts list a $250 agreement floor, with method-level floors of $100 for US ACH, $100 for international PayPal, and $1,000 for international wire; no standard share or payment interval was found.

## Dated publisher reports

- **2019 (Reddit)**: a publisher said Revcontent held a balance after identifying invalid traffic. The post gives no site niche or traffic figures, and the dispute is unverified. [Thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/bx97d2/)
- **2016 (publisher blog)**: a publisher described about 45 days using Revcontent but gave old and limited operational details. It is too dated to estimate current acceptance or product fit. [Review](https://www.techgrapple.com/revcontent-ad-network-review-publishers/)
- Recent targeted searches mainly found application questions, not dated reports with traffic and approval outcomes.

## Editorial inference

Revcontent may be a low-volume experiment when a publisher wants native recommendations, because its published rule has no numeric traffic floor. That does not mean approval is automatic: domain/content review remains discretionary. The ad format may be a poor fit when clean editorial UX or tight brand control is more important than additional recommendation inventory. Ask about placement control, revenue share, and rejection/appeal process before applying. Do not compare its lack of a traffic minimum to managed display networks' traffic thresholds.

## Directory-ready corrections

- State “no traffic minimum published; all publisher applications and domains receive case-by-case review.”
- Keep the product role as supplemental/native recommendation.
- Separate agreement and payment-method thresholds; no standard Net term or revenue share is public.

## Evidence gaps

No current independent acceptance example with site scale, geography, and decision outcome was found. The user reports above are old and lack enough context for a recommendation band.
