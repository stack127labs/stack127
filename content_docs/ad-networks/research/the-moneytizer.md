# The Moneytizer

Checked 2026-10-22.

## Official facts

The current US validation criteria page sets a specific gate: **at least 300,000 unique visitors from organic traffic in the 30 days before registration**, with the qualifying audience described as US, UK, and Canada. This is not pageviews, sessions, or all-source visitors. Applicants must own a domain (not a subdomain or subpage), have server access for ads.txt, and offer enough current content built over time; sites with only a few posts are rejected. The page excludes streaming, illegal downloads, copyright violations, sexual or violent material, dating, crypto, social networks, gambling, and social profiles. The US publisher homepage provides the account-registration entry point. The publisher guide requires website submission, quality review, ads.txt/tags, and consent management for EU traffic. Public terms give at least 70% of pre-tax advertiser billings and a €50 invoice floor; the US FAQ lists a $50 invoice threshold and payment 60 days after invoice, by bank transfer or PayPal.

## Editorial inference

This is a potential managed primary option only for sites that can actually verify the exact organic-unique visitor count and qualifying countries. The current checker inputs of pageviews, visits/sessions, and Tier 1 share do not contain that measure. Do not derive unique visitors from sessions or pageviews, and do not infer organic share from current-network status. Ask for country-level organic unique users in the prior 30 days; if unavailable, mark eligibility unknown.

## Directory-ready corrections
