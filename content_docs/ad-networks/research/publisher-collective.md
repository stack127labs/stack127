## Pay

- The partner terms set a €500 payment threshold. Payment is made after the advertiser pays, by bank transfer, in GBP, EUR, or USD. Share percentage is set in a separate representation agreement. A blog claims 20% share, Net 30, and $150 minimum, which conflicts with the partner terms; trust signed terms for enrolled publishers. (Sources: https://www.publisher-collective.com/partners-terms-conditions and https://www.publisher-collective.com/blog/google-ad-exchange, read 2026-10-16.)

## Rules

- Terms restrict discriminatory, weapon, pornographic, hateful, and indecent material. Publishers may need to update site materials within seven days, must not change ad tags, and may need to pass leads to Publisher Collective. Terms allow suspension or termination for non-response. Fixed exclusivity and contract duration are not stated in the public summary. (Source: https://www.publisher-collective.com/partners-terms-conditions, read 2026-10-16.)

## Service

- The public FAQ directs publishers to apply and sign individual terms. Dashboard, reporting, and support details are not stated. (Source: https://www.publisher-collective.com/faq, read 2026-10-16.)
