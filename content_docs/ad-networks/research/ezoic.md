## Pay

- Ezoic's Terms say publishers receive at least 82% of net ad revenue unless another agreement applies. Payment is approximately 35 days after advertiser payment, with a $20 threshold. Ezoic's payment help page instead describes a Net 30 cycle. Trust the legal Terms for the contract statement and note this timing conflict for confirmation. Methods listed by Ezoic include PayPal, Payoneer bank transfer, and check. (Sources: https://www.ezoic.com/terms and https://support.ezoic.com/kb/article/ezoic-payments?lang=en-US&lang=it%3Fid%3Dezoic-payments, read 2026-10-16.)
