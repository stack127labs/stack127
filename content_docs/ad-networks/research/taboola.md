# Taboola

Checked 2026-10-22.

## Official facts

Taboola's new-account guide, updated October 22, 2026, directs publishers to an online form and says a team member will respond within 48 hours; this is a response target, not approval. It publishes no numeric traffic or revenue minimum. Applicants must be 18+, own and operate the site, own or license the content, follow the partner policy, and respect exclusivity against other third-party recommendation services. Publisher policy bars categories such as fake news, pornography, infringement, and criminal or threatening content. The product is a native content-recommendation widget that can supplement display advertising; it is not a full display-stack replacement. The revenue share is set in the order. Existing terms describe USD payment within 45 days after month-end; the public payment floor was not found. Most publishers use Payoneer, with country-specific exceptions.

## Dated publisher reports

- **2026 vendor case study**: The Independent, a large UK news publisher with 63M global visitors, reported 14% more Taboola advertising revenue after adding an in-article recommendation solution. This is a named customer result but vendor-published, not an independent or typical outcome and not an entry example. [Case study](https://www.taboola.com/resources/case-studies/the-independent-uk)
- A 2023 academic review of journalism literature summarized older claims that Taboola partner sites were often described at 500K–1M monthly pageviews. The paper does not establish a current Taboola rule, and the current 2026 account guide states no numeric floor. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1080/21670811.2023.2180403)
- Targeted searches found no recent independent publisher acceptance report with a clear traffic number.

## Editorial inference

Best fit is an established content publisher with recommendation inventory, meaningful engaged audience, and editorial controls—not a site seeking basic display ads only. Because no current floor is published, avoid turning historical 500K–1M claims into an official minimum. It is compatible with a separate display network if the publisher respects Taboola's exclusivity specifically against competing recommendation services.

## Directory-ready corrections

- Keep “no numeric minimum stated” and note human review plus policy compliance.
- State 48 hours as first response, not application approval.
- Clarify exclusivity applies to other third-party recommendation services.
- Label 45-day payment and Payoneer by applicable terms/location; share is order-specific.

## Evidence gaps

No official traffic benchmark, payout floor, or recent independent acceptance example was found. The Independent case is a large publisher and not a typical performance benchmark.
