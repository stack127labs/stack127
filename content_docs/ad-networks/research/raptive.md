## Joining

- Traffic: At least 25,000 pageviews. Note: Monthly. Source: https://raptive.com/creators/. Read 2026-10-16.
- Visitor location: Majority from the US, Canada, UK, Australia, or New Zealand. Source: https://raptive.com/creators/. Read 2026-10-16.
- Content: 100% original content. Source: https://raptive.com/creators/. Read 2026-10-16.
- Google standing: Not public. The reviewed official pages do not state this rule. Source checked: https://raptive.com/creators/. Read 2026-10-16.

- Approval time: Not public. The reviewed official pages do not state this rule. Source checked: https://raptive.com/creators/. Read 2026-10-16.

## Pay

- Raptive states a 75% publisher share. It pays Net 45 on the 15th. The threshold is $25, or $100 for wire. The payment help page lists ACH, PayPal, and wire. (Sources: https://raptive.com/fbp/ and https://help.raptive.com/hc/en-us/articles/202052255-When-do-I-get-paid, read 2026-10-16.)

## Rules

- Public materials reviewed do not state an exclusivity rule, contract length, or notice period. Raptive supplies standard or custom layouts based on publisher size; the signed agreement should confirm placement controls. (Source: https://help.raptive.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035078152-Getting-Started-with-Raptive, read 2026-10-16.)

## Service

- Raptive provides a dashboard, support tickets, and a dedicated manager by tier. Reporting and layout customization differ by publisher tier. (Source: https://help.raptive.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035078152-Getting-Started-with-Raptive, read 2026-10-16.)
