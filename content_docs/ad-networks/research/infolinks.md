## Editorial inference

### Practical fit

Can be shortlisted as a low-bar supplemental experiment because there is no published volume floor, but not as the main network for a publisher expecting meaningful managed-display revenue.

### Estimated range and confidence

- Traffic range: not estimated; available figures are absent or not comparable enough to support a practical band.
- Revenue range: not estimated.

- Geography: No public country rule found.
- Site age: No public age minimum found.
- Confidence: **medium**. Official help covers all publisher sizes and payment choices, but public share terms are from an old agreement and independent reports lack context.

### Current-network compatibility

- No confirmed modern coexistence rule; verify the older agreement before combining with another provider.
- Treat as supplemental format. Public policy on coexistence with specific display providers was not confirmed; review current agreement.
