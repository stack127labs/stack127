## Pay

- The public agreement gives 70% share for In-Text ads, Net 45 working days after month end, and a $50 minimum. Other format rates and payment minimums vary. Methods listed include ACH, eCheck, Payoneer, PayPal, and Western Union. The displayed agreement was last updated in 2015, so a newer signed agreement should control if different. ## Rules

- The old public agreement states a 30-day trial followed by 12-month renewals and allows termination on one business day's notice. It states restrictions on adult and illegal content. Publisher exclusivity and ad placement rights should be confirmed in current terms. (Source: https://sites0001.infolinks.com/service-agreement?email=&name=&url=, read 2026-10-16.)
