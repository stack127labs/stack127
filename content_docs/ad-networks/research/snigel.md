# Snigel

Checked 2026-10-16.

## Merged into Publisher Collective

Snigel and Publisher Collective announced their merger on 11 February 2025. We list the combined company as Publisher Collective. Snigel now supplies its ad technology and legacy contracts. The live `snigelweb.com/sellers.json` lists `admin@publisher-collective.com` as its contact. Publisher-side `MANAGERDOMAIN=snigelweb.com` and ad-system evidence belong to Publisher Collective under the usual verification rules. Seller lines alone still do not prove active use. PubGalaxy remains separate.

Sources: [merger announcement](https://www.publisher-collective.com/blog/snigel-and-publisher-collective-939e2), [current company description](https://publisher-collective.com/about), and [seller file](https://snigelweb.com/sellers.json). Read 2026-10-16.

## Pay

- Snigel's publisher guide states an 80% publisher share. Its FAQ says Net 30 payment by wire in EUR or USD. A current payment threshold is not stated in the cited FAQ. (Sources: https://snigel.com/blog/quick-guide-to-header-bidding-publishers and https://snigel.com/faq, read 2026-10-16.)
