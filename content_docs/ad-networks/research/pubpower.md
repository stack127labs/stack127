# PubPower

Checked 2026-10-23.

## Official facts

PubPower sells managed monetization to smaller publishers and also describes custom-bidder infrastructure for publishers with their own stack. Its official website-requirements help page says “around 100,000 visitors/month,” alongside traffic quality and content checks. The article is marked as last updated about three years ago, so preserve it as dated official guidance, not a confirmed current hard cutoff. The page bars pop-ups/redirects and disallows or restricts unsafe, illegal, gambling, and infringing content. Onboarding instructions describe adding a site and its ads.txt/ad tag, with review timing stated as 24–48 hours in the quick start and 2–4 days in FAQ guidance. Payment help, also last updated about two years ago, lists Net 0 above $3,000 monthly revenue and otherwise Net 30; method minimums are bank $1,000, PayPal $100, Payoneer $50, and crypto-specific floors. Current indexed official package copy says Essential revenue share is tailored for smaller publishers while the Advanced custom-bidder option has no revenue share; it does not publish the Essential percentage. The PubPower marketing page could not be opened directly in this check, so confirm the current rate in the account terms.

## Dated publisher reports

- **About Jun 2024 (Reddit)**: an anime-site owner said the site was seven months old, had “around 100k traffic,” and was running both AdSense and PubPower; they said PubPower's CPM was below AdSense at that time. The metric behind “traffic” is ambiguous, and no application outcome or revenue was reported. [Post](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/1dpv6e3/)

## Editorial inference

Potential fit is a smaller-to-mid-size publisher seeking a managed ad stack, especially where current traffic is near the dated 100K guideline. Sites already using AdSense may be compatible in at least some setups, but confirm whether the selected PubPower package is managed or custom bidders. Don't mark an old help-center number as a current strict gate. Current ad revenue is useful for package economics and Net 0 eligibility, not a substitute for the traffic metric.
