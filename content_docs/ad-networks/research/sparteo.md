# Sparteo

Checked 2026-10-22.

## Official facts

Sparteo markets managed ad monetization and publisher technology, including display yield optimization, Actirise, Viously video, FastCMP, and analytics such as revenue, viewability, time, URL, and Core Web Vitals. Its public site, publisher privacy notice, contact routes, and W3Schools case study do not publish a general traffic, revenue, site-age, country, Google-standing, or payment rule. No public standard revenue share or payout schedule was found. The W3Schools case is company-authored; it establishes an education-publisher example, not a comparable entry threshold. The product is sales-led/managed, so the route is the current [Contact form](https://corporate.sparteo.com/contact), not a known self-serve approval form.

## Dated publisher reports

- **2026 (Reddit)**: a gaming publisher with 3M monthly pageviews and roughly $2K–$3K monthly revenue said it had worked for years with an ad partner selected from a list that included Playwire, Venatus, Nitro, Ascendeum, and Sparteo. The publisher did not name which partner; this is not evidence that the site used Sparteo. [Thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/adops/comments/1sjz5b7/3_million_monthly_page_views_and_23k_monthly_ad/)
- A separate Reddit post mentioning a shift to Sparteo was removed and supplied no verifiable metrics or outcome; it is not used as evidence.

## Editorial inference

Likely better suited to publishers with enough scale and ad-operations needs to justify a managed optimization relationship, especially education and content portfolios. Public evidence is too thin to set an estimated entry range or to recommend it as a typical solo-blog choice. Ask about minimum commercial opportunity, countries, current GAM/header-bidding setup, and product scope. Current network is useful for compatibility context only; membership in a peer network list is not proof of Sparteo eligibility.
