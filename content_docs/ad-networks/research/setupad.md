# Setupad

Checked 2026-10-22.

## Official facts

Separate two products. **Setupad managed monetization** is a managed wrapper/demand service; current public pages do not provide a clear, current universal admission minimum. A Setupad requirements blog has been reported as around 100,000 monthly visitors, but other public descriptions blur “visitors” and “sessions,” so do not treat that as a verified hard rule. **Setupad Self-Serve** is software for publishers with their own Google Ad Manager account and direct SSP relationships. Its current pricing page sets plan bands of 10M+ monthly visits for Free, 10–50M for Business, and 50M+ for Enterprise. Business charges €0.05 per thousand ad requests and requires €50 prepayment; Enterprise uses a custom/postpaid fee with €1,000 minimum monthly spend. All Self-Serve applicants are reviewed for quality and significant traffic. This is a platform plan requirement, not the managed service's admission cutoff.

## Dated publisher reports

- **Oct 11, 2024 (Medium, firsthand)**: an anonymous Japanese publisher said its site had about 3M pageviews/month, mainly Asia with Europe and North America, and that it used Setupad for a year before returning to AdSense. The author reported integration, performance, and CPM concerns but no reliable monthly revenue. This shows one non-Tier-1-heavy site used the service; it does not reveal a current entry rule. [Review](https://medium.com/@vibrant_mango_impala_136/setupad-review-my-1-year-with-setupad-and-why-i-ultimately-switched-back-to-google-adsense-150fd77506d5)
- **2023 (Reddit)**: a publisher with 15M monthly pageviews and 99% Tier 2 traffic asked about Setupad but did not report an outcome. [Question](https://www.reddit.com/r/programmatic/comments/15yt6vh)

## Editorial inference

A publisher with 10M+ monthly visits and its own GAM/SSP stack may qualify for the current Self-Serve plans, but should not be recommended as a typical solo-blog starter. The managed service may be accessible earlier; public evidence supports only a dated, uncertain estimate near 100K visits. Keep the two paths separate in recommendation logic. Visits are not pageviews or sessions, and the checker cannot derive one from the other.

## Directory-ready corrections

- Separate managed Setupad from Self-Serve in joining details.
- Add Self-Serve plan bands and requirements with the metric “monthly visits.”
- Label the ~100K managed figure as older and unconfirmed; state that articles conflict between visitor/session wording.
- Distinguish the €0.05 ad-request CPM from the optional 20% reseller share and contract payment terms.

## Evidence gaps

The current official Self-Serve page gives strong plan boundaries but not an independent onboarding example. No current official managed-service traffic cutoff or country preference could be reconfirmed. The single 2024 report gives no revenue data.
