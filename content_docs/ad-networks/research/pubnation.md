# PubNation

Checked 2026-10-22.

## Official facts

PubNation is no longer available as a separate publisher program. Mediavine's current Help Center says it recently completed migration of PubNation sites to Mediavine and that the PubNation offering is no longer available. The PubNation marketing site still exists, but it is not a reliable application route against the current support notice. There is no current PubNation eligibility or payment schedule to recommend.

## Dated publisher reports

- **About March 2026 (Reddit; page displayed “7 months ago” on Oct 22)**: a gaming blogger with a nearly nine-year-old site reported acceptance to PubNation at 30–40K monthly sessions, mostly from Google. They reported about $140 after a 60K-session month on AdSense, then $621 and $614 months after moving to PubNation, with self-reported RPM around $21. This is a useful historical entry example, but the current program has since migrated; sessions are not pageviews, and no country mix was given. [Post](https://www.reddit.com/r/Blogging/comments/1s0ly5b/)

## Editorial inference

Do not recommend PubNation as a current option or infer current Mediavine eligibility from this old acceptance. The report can inform historical fit for gaming sites but cannot provide a present-day traffic range. If a user selects PubNation as their current network, flag that the publisher is likely on the successor Mediavine relationship and ask which current Mediavine program it refers to.

## Directory-ready corrections

- Mark the product as discontinued/migrated, with no standalone application route.
- Preserve the historical 30–40K sessions report only as “reported historical acceptance,” never as a current cutoff.
- Do not show old PubNation payout details as current terms.

## Evidence gaps

The current Mediavine help notice does not provide a public migration-by-migration date or explain whether every historical contract term carried over. The Reddit post is anonymous and was not independently verified.
