## Pay

- For Ad Exchange, payments are Net 45. The normal minimum is $25; wire threshold is $50. Methods include PayPal, US ACH, wire, eCheck/local transfer, and US check. Payout currency is USD, with some local-currency receipt options. Sovrn Commerce is a separate affiliate product with different Net 90 terms and is not included here. (Sources: https://authcore.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KB/pages/2693627510846 and https://knowledge.sovrn.com/kb/maintaining-your-ad-exchange-account, read 2026-10-16.)
