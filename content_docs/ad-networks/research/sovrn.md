# Sovrn

Checked 2026-10-22.

## Official facts

For **Sovrn Ad Exchange**, the current official approval article says observational traffic must be **more than 100,000 visits/month**. The official sign-up guide says to create a Sovrn platform account first, then start Ad Exchange onboarding from the account; Commerce is a separate product. It requires an operational domain and ownership/ad-system proof in ads.txt; resellers/intermediaries need sellers.json. The same help material requires a currently maintained site with a recent publication (within four months), no child-directed or exclusively under-16 audience, a privacy policy, and a brand-safety policy. No country-share rule is published. “Visits” is the network's term: do not convert it into sessions, users, or pageviews. Ad Exchange is an auction/demand product that can supplement a publisher's own stack; Sovrn Commerce is a separate affiliate product and must not be merged into this requirement or payment profile. Existing public Ad Exchange payment facts list Net 45 and $25 minimum ($50 wire), with methods including PayPal, ACH, wire, eCheck/local transfer, and US check.

## Editorial inference

This is not a likely recommendation for sites below the official 100K visits/month line. Above it, it may work as supplemental programmatic demand for publishers prepared to maintain the technical ownership records and site policies. For recommendation input, ask visits separately if available; if the user only knows pageviews or sessions, mark eligibility “unknown,” not “meets.” Country share and monthly ad revenue may inform expected commercial value but are not published gates.
