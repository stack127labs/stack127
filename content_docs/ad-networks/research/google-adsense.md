## Pay

- For AdSense for Content, publishers receive 80% after the advertiser platform fee. Google Ads demand makes the effective share about 68%; shares differ for other products. The USD payment threshold is $100, with thresholds by currency. Payments are monthly, normally issued from the 21st to 26th if there is no hold. Methods vary by country and include check, EFT, SEPA, wire, and PayPal Hyperwallet where available. (Sources: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/180195?hl=en, https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1709871?hl=en, https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7164701?hl=en, and https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1714397?hl=en, read 2026-10-16.)

## Rules

- Ad placement must comply with AdSense Program Policies. Public terms do not impose publisher exclusivity. Content restrictions are in the Program Policies. (Sources: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182?hl=en and https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724?hl=en, read 2026-10-16.)

## Service

- AdSense provides account reporting and policy/help-center support. (Sources: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724?hl=en and https://support.google.com/adsense/, read 2026-10-16.)
