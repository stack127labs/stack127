# PubFuture

Checked 2026-10-23.

## Official facts

PubFuture markets a managed monetization platform with an emphasis on H5 gaming. The coordinator opened its JavaScript-rendered [homepage FAQ](https://pubfuture.com/home) on 2026-10-23. Question 5 states a minimum of **100,000 pageviews a month**, safe content without nudity, and PubFuture lines in ads.txt. Question 8 says its tags work inside Google Ad Manager and other ad servers. Question 9 states Net 30, with January earnings paid at the end of February. The earlier text-only fetch missed these collapsed answers.

The official onboarding path is the [publisher sign-up form](https://ssp.pubfuture.com/auth/signup), followed by site review. No standard share, country percentage, or age requirement was found. An older indexed excerpt listed a $50 payment minimum and several payment methods; these were not reconfirmed in the live FAQ or terms, so they are omitted from current directory facts.

## Editorial inference

Treat PubFuture as a specialist managed option for H5 games and similar interactive inventory at or above its published floor. A generic solo blog has weaker evidence of niche fit. Vendor case results do not establish expected returns. A publisher with an existing game ad stack should confirm commercial compatibility and the revenue share.
