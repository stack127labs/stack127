# Snack Media

Checked 2026-10-22.

## Official facts

Snack Media's current publisher page offers both fully managed monetization and bespoke setups where publishers keep their own wrapper and use Snack demand. Its roots and strongest public positioning are sports publishing, sports rights-holders, and sports/entertainment communities, but it presents its services as publisher-wide. The 2025 standard agreement publishes no universal traffic minimum or revenue share; the cover sheet sets the commission and initial/renewal term. The contract requires traffic-stat access and network tags live within 14 days. It excludes hateful, pornographic, illegal, and infringing material. The standard agreement appoints Snack as exclusive provider of its defined Services during the term and grants it exclusive rights for defined ad/content sales activity; publishers should confirm the practical scope in the cover sheet. Payment is due within 30 days after the end of the month when Snack has received advertiser/licensee funds in full, so payment timing depends on advertiser payment. Referral-program criteria of 1M impressions apply to referral eligibility only, not general admission.

## Dated publisher reports

- Targeted searches found no detailed independent Snack publisher account with traffic, application result, and revenue. Public case studies and sports-partner references are company-selected, not independent admission evidence.
- **2025 agreement**: this is a contractual description, not a publisher outcome; it is more useful than older website terms for current payout and exclusivity scope. [PDF](https://cdn.snack-media.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Snack-Ad-Network-Agreement-TCs.pdf)

## Editorial inference

Best fit is a sports publisher, sports community, or publisher that wants direct-sales and managed video/CTV support. The bespoke path may complement an existing wrapper; the fully managed path may replace it. Compatibility should be based on the specific service and signed scope, not the brand name alone. With no public traffic floor, use niche fit, direct-sales capacity, traffic quality, and current setup to prioritize an inquiry; do not invent a volume threshold.

## Directory-ready corrections

- Remove any implication that exclusivity applies only when the order says so; standard terms include scoped exclusivity, while cover-sheet terms still control service scope and commission.
- Keep 1M impressions explicitly limited to the referral offer.
- Explain payment is contingent on advertiser funds and the commission is set in the cover sheet.

## Evidence gaps

No standard entry traffic, location preference, payout threshold, or payment method is public. No independent report with a clear acceptance range surfaced.
