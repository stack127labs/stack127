## Pay

- The signed cover sheet sets the revenue share. Public standard terms do not publish one rate, threshold, method, or currency. Payment is due within 30 days after the month when Snack Media receives the advertiser revenue. The older publisher terms say £100 and Net 60, which conflict with the 2025 agreement and are treated as legacy. (Sources: https://cdn.snack-media.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Snack-Ad-Network-Agreement-TCs.pdf and https://www.snack-media.com/publisher-terms/, read 2026-10-16.)

## Rules

- Exclusivity applies only if agreed in the order. The contract cover sheet defines the initial term; exact length is not public. Either party can terminate with 30 days' notice. After expiry, it can renew month-to-month; older web terms are not trusted over the 2025 PDF.
