## Editorial inference

### Practical fit

Standard service is plausible for established sites around the historical 1–20m visitor sweet spot. MAP is a lower-volume route with no PV minimum, subject to review.

### Estimated range and confidence

- Traffic reference: **1,000,000–20,000,000 visitors per month**; confidence: low. This is not an admission guarantee.
  - Historical vendor interview sweet spot, not admission range.
  - Basis: [https://www.adnimation.com/adnimation-co-founder-talks-plans-for-growth-with-mergermarket/](https://www.adnimation.com/adnimation-co-founder-talks-plans-for-growth-with-mergermarket/).
- Revenue range: not estimated.

- Geography: Global; historical interview says US publishers were a majority, including foreign-language US audiences.
- Site age: No site-age minimum found.
- Confidence: **low**. Official FAQ confirms substantial traffic and MAP without PV minimum; the 1–20m band is dated company positioning only.

### Current-network compatibility

- GAM/header-bidding experience can support implementation but does not prove acceptance.
- Standard service allows direct campaigns to continue; confirm other-network exclusivity.
- MAP payment terms are separate.
