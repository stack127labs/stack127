Checked: 2026-10-22

## Product and application

- Product: Managed publisher ad network with program tiers and Propel entry program.
- Application or contact route: [https://www.monumetric.com/get-started/](https://www.monumetric.com/get-started/).

## Editorial inference

### Practical fit

Useful small-blog option when 10k PV has held for three consecutive months, target-country traffic is over half, and the site can pay the $99 Propel fee and use required ad slots.

### Estimated range and confidence

- Traffic reference: **10,000–80,000 pageviews per month**; confidence: high. This is not an admission guarantee.
  - Propel range; requires three consecutive months; higher Monumetric tiers exist.
  - Basis: [https://www.monumetric.com/monumetric-program-overview/](https://www.monumetric.com/monumetric-program-overview/).
- Revenue range: not estimated.

- Geography: At least 50% from US/UK/Australia/Canada.
- Site age: No public age rule found.
- Confidence: **high**. Current overview gives tier ranges and three-month history. A dated personal-finance blogger reported reaching 10k in month four but applied around month twelve; earnings are historical.

### Current-network compatibility

- Existing ad-provider approval is an explicit Propel condition but does not guarantee Monumetric acceptance.
- Managed ad placements; confirm current scope before combining with an existing display partner. Propel requires six slots and major-ad-provider approval.
