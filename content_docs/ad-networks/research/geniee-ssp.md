## Editorial inference

### Practical fit

Consider for established Japan/APAC media and app publishers needing SSP infrastructure. Not a strong default solo-blog recommendation without a direct sales conversation.

### Estimated range and confidence

- Traffic range: not estimated; available figures are absent or not comparable enough to support a practical band.
- Revenue range: not estimated.

- Geography: Japan-centered provider with international publisher support; no public country minimum.
- Site age: No public age minimum found.
- Confidence: **low**. Current official page and 2025 terms describe service and contract basics without a traffic floor. Search found no reliable first-person application evidence.

### Current-network compatibility

- Existing SSP/header-bidding partners may be compatible but do not prove admission.
- An SSP can sit in a larger stack; exact tags, demand setup and exclusivity are account-specific.
