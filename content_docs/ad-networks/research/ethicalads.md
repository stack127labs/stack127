## Pay

- Publishers receive 70%. Payout is monthly, with a $50 threshold and a target payment date by the 15th. Supported methods include Stripe Connect/bank transfer, PayPal, OpenCollective, and GitHub Sponsors. (Source: https://www.ethicalads.io/publisher-policy/, read 2026-10-16.)

## Rules

- The policy permits a fallback ad or sharing visitor traffic with another network. It says not to show another ad on the same page as an EthicalAds ad. Content that violates the publisher policy can lead to ad disabling. No fixed contract term or notice period is stated. (Source: https://www.ethicalads.io/publisher-policy/, read 2026-10-16.)
