## Input grammar and bounds

The adapter accepts exactly these 16 keys and no others:

`sourceData`, `sourceUnit`, `annualGrowth`, `horizonYears`, `backupModel`, `retainedPoints`, `changedPerBackup`, `headroom`, `recoveryChecksEnabled`, `backupIntervalHours`, `targetRpoHours`, `targetRtoHours`, `localRestoreMbps`, `offsiteRestoreMbps`, `transferEfficiency`, and `displayUnit`.

Text values are parsed as text, not coerced through JavaScript floating point. Decimal fields use digits with an optional decimal point and digits after it. Whitespace trimming follows the input normalization contract. Signs, grouping separators, exponent notation, hexadecimal, underscores, a bare decimal point, and a trailing decimal point are invalid. Integer fields contain decimal digits only after trimming and are parsed as safe integers; `3.0`, `1e1`, and `+3` are invalid. Decimal percentage and Mbps fields accept at most two meaningful fractional digits; extra non-zero precision is rejected rather than rounded. Redundant trailing zeroes may normalize away.

The source quantity uses the exact supported units `GB`, `TB`, `GiB`, and `TiB`. Its conversion multipliers are `1_000_000_000`, `1_000_000_000_000`, `1_073_741_824`, and `1_099_511_627_776` bytes. The converted source must be at least `1_000_000` bytes and at most `1_000_000_000_000_000` bytes. A decimal that cannot resolve to a whole byte is invalid. The other capacity values are not accepted as units or input fields.

| Key | Accepted boundary and grammar | Invalid boundary or grammar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sourceData` | Decimal source text; `0.001` with `GB` is the 1 MB minimum; `1000` with `TB` is the maximum. | Zero, below 1 MB after conversion, above 1,000 TB after conversion, a fractional byte, signs, separators, exponent notation, or unsupported units. |
| `sourceUnit` | Exact enum `GB`, `TB`, `GiB`, `TiB`. | `B`, `MB`, `GIB`, `tb`, null, number, or another string. |
| `annualGrowth` | Decimal percent from `0` through `100`, with at most two meaningful decimal places. | Negative, above `100`, non-decimal, or non-zero precision beyond two decimal places. |
| `horizonYears` | Safe decimal-free integer from `0` through `10`; `0` means current source size. | Negative, fractional, non-safe integer text, below `0`, or above `10`. |
| `backupModel` | Exact enum `full-only` or `full-plus-incremental`. | Any other string, null, number, or missing key. |
| `retainedPoints` | Safe decimal-free integer from `1` through `1000`. | `0`, negative, fractional, non-safe, or above `1000`. |
| `changedPerBackup` | Decimal percent from `0` through `100`, at most two meaningful decimal places. | Negative, above `100`, invalid decimal grammar, or excess non-zero precision. |
| `headroom` | Decimal percent from `0` through `50`, at most two meaningful decimal places. | Negative, above `50`, invalid decimal grammar, or excess non-zero precision. |
| `recoveryChecksEnabled` | Boolean `false` or `true`. | Strings such as `"false"`, numbers, null, or missing key. |
| `backupIntervalHours` | Decimal-free integer from `1` through `168`. | `0`, `169`, negative, fractional, or invalid integer grammar when checks are enabled. |
| `targetRpoHours` | Decimal-free integer from `1` through `720`. | `0`, `721`, negative, fractional, or invalid integer grammar when checks are enabled. |
| `targetRtoHours` | Decimal-free integer from `1` through `720`. | `0`, `721`, negative, fractional, or invalid integer grammar when checks are enabled. |
| `localRestoreMbps` | Decimal Mbps from `0.01` through `100000`, at most two meaningful decimal places. | Below `0.01`, above `100000`, negative, separator, exponent, or excess non-zero precision when checks are enabled. |
| `offsiteRestoreMbps` | Decimal Mbps from `0.01` through `100000`, at most two meaningful decimal places. | Same invalid classes as `localRestoreMbps`. |
| `transferEfficiency` | Decimal percent from `1` through `100`, at most two meaningful decimal places. | Below `1`, above `100`, negative, or invalid decimal grammar when checks are enabled. |
| `displayUnit` | Exact enum `TB` or `TiB`; display-only. | `GB`, `GiB`, null, number, or any other string. |

When recovery is disabled, the serialized input shape still carries valid defaults for the five recovery assumptions. They must not affect capacity and their result values/verdicts are omitted. When recovery is enabled, all recovery fields are validated together; a missing or invalid one makes the whole calculation invalid.
