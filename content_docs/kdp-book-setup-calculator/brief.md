# Brief: KDP book setup calculator

## Who is here

An author is preparing a KDP paperback or hardcover and already knows the binding, trim, and approximate page count. They need one reliable handoff for the interior page, margins, spine, and full cover file instead of stitching together several small calculators.

## The job

They leave with a copyable setup receipt in inches, millimeters, and pixels, plus a clear warning when the selected combination or effective page count falls outside the current KDP US print table. A hardcover result must read as an estimate, not a promise of upload acceptance.

## Show vs say

The workbench comes first. Use a structured result for the cover dimensions, interior page, margins, spine, eligibility, and unit conversions. Use an exact segment diagram for paperback back, spine, front, and bleed, and for the hardcover wrap and hinge estimate. Use sentences below the tool to explain page rounding, source limits, and why hardcover dimensions need confirmation. No marker art, clean chart, screenshot, or decorative illustration is needed because the useful relationships are exact.
