# Brief: IP and ASN registration lookup

## Who is here

An operator, developer, or incident responder has one public IP address or ASN from a firewall log, DNS result, or network report. They want a registered organization and a useful abuse contact, not a broad intelligence profile.

## The job

They leave with the RIR record for the allocation or ASN, the source that returned it, and a clear explanation of whether current BGP routing adds separate context.

## Show vs say

The form and result are the first visual: one input, one primary action, and a lead sentence followed by allocation facts. Use a compact key/value table for repeated registry fields and a separate section for optional current BGP context. A small relationship strip can show IANA to RIR to allocation to route, because that is a real relationship, not a numeric chart. Keep raw RDAP in a disclosure.

Use connected prose for what the registered country means, why the RIR holder may differ from the network currently announcing a route, what an abuse contact is for, and what the tool cannot identify. Do not add a map, score, reputation badge, port list, or decorative illustration.
