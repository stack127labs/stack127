# Brief: EPP Status Code Explainer

## Who is here

A domain owner has seen an EPP or RDAP status and needs to know who set it, what it prevents, and whether they can change it.

## The job

They leave with a complete server-rendered reference and, when a domain is entered, an explanation of the statuses actually applied to it.

## Show vs say

Tables group codes by reader action: normal, registrar locks, registry locks, in progress, and lifecycle periods. A small registrar-versus-registry figure explains ownership. Sentences answer transfer blocks, DNS holds, unknown codes, and lookup limitations.
