# MRR waterfall calculator

Route: `/mrr-waterfall-calculator`

## Research

### User job

A SaaS founder or operator wants to reconcile one reporting period's starting MRR to its ending MRR and see the revenue movements behind the change: new business, expansion, reactivation, contraction, and churn. The P4 [Notion brief](https://app.notion.com/p/3e37bc97c44381a19aeadc589e3053c6) says the tool should explain those five movements.

This is separate from the in-progress [MRR calculator in this branch](../mrr-calculator/tool-spec.md), which converts active plan counts, prices, and billing intervals into a current MRR run rate and explicitly leaves period movements out. The [SaaS metrics calculator](../saas-metrics-calculator/tool-spec.md) already calculates ending MRR, ARR, net new MRR, growth, and other metrics from one period's starting MRR, new, expansion, contraction, and churn inputs. It omits reactivation and serves a much wider retention and unit-economics task. The focused job here is to explain all five movements in one bridge.

### Competitor patterns

- [Finin2min's MRR bridge calculator](https://finin2min.com/mrr-arr-calculator.html) has six amount fields: opening MRR and the five movement amounts. It returns closing MRR, run-rate ARR, net new MRR, and monthly growth, with Calculate and Print / Save PDF actions. I entered 10,000 opening, 1,200 new, 500 expansion, 300 reactivation, 100 contraction, and 200 churn; it returned 11,700 closing MRR, 140,400 ARR, 1,700 net new MRR, and 17% growth. Clearing the nonzero New MRR field was accepted as zero without an error, reducing the result to 10,500 closing MRR and 5% growth. The field had `min=0` and `required=false`.
- [NexaCalc's MRR calculator](https://nexacalculator.net/business/mrr-calculator/) combines plan rows, direct-value entry, a movement bridge, and target calculations in one tool with four mode tabs. In Movement mode, the six movement amounts returned 1,250 net new MRR and 11,250 ending MRR. With the prefilled plan rows and target values still present, the same results page also showed 3,980 total MRR, -60.2% MRR growth, a target gap, and required accounts. This covers more workflows, but the several MRR bases make it harder to read the movement answer on its own.
- [ChartMogul's MRR Movements report](https://help.chartmogul.com/article/158-chart-mrr-movements) is a more complete, account-backed reporting pattern. Its report documents separate movement totals across periods, positive bands above zero for new, expansion, and reactivation, negative bands below zero for contraction and churn, an MRR/ARR view, estimated current-period bars, and click-through from a report cell to its subscription activity. I reviewed the public help article; the signed-in report itself was not available to operate.
- [FCF Startups' SaaS metrics calculator](https://fcfstartups.com/tools/saas-metrics-calculator/) uses opening MRR, new, expansion, contraction, and churn as part of a broader monthly metrics form. The existing SaaS metrics research records its ending MRR/ARR, retention, CAC, payback, and burn outputs; it has no reactivation input. These patterns show that the five-category bridge is established, while a focused manual calculator needs to keep the period and each movement easy to audit.

### Technical facts and sources

- MRR is a monthly-normalized recurring subscription run rate. Billing intervals, subscription status, discounts, refunds, and transaction-fee settings can affect the source value; one-time line items do not contribute to MRR in ChartMogul. Its current MRR report documents active and past-due subscriptions, normalization from billing interval, and one-time item exclusion. [ChartMogul's MRR report](https://help.chartmogul.com/article/154-chart-monthly-recurring-revenue-mrr) and [Stripe's MRR support article](https://support.stripe.com/questions/understanding-monthly-recurring-revenue-%28mrr%29-and-annual-recurring-revenue-%28arr%29?locale=en-GB) support this definition. **Inference:** because a source can include past-due subscriptions and Stripe says MRR is not GAAP revenue, MRR should be read as recurring value rather than a cash-received or recognized-revenue total. When ARR means annualized run rate, it is `12 × MRR`, not revenue already earned or guaranteed. [ChartMogul's ARR definition](https://chartmogul.com/saas-metrics/arr/) explains the distinction.
- A consistent movement bridge is `Net new MRR = New + Expansion + Reactivation − Contraction − Churn` and `Ending MRR = Starting MRR + Net new MRR`. Monthly growth, when shown, is `(Ending MRR − Starting MRR) ÷ Starting MRR`; it is undefined when starting MRR is zero. [ChartMogul's movement definitions](https://help.chartmogul.com/article/163-understanding-mrr-movements) describe each movement as a net change in subscription MRR and define the five categories. [Its growth formula](https://chartmogul.com/blog/saas-growth-rate/) supports the growth calculation. New business is a first paid subscription, expansion increases a customer's total MRR, contraction decreases it without losing the customer's last subscription, churn loses the last or only subscription, and reactivation is a formerly paying customer returning to a paid plan. Neutral plan changes have no MRR effect. ChartMogul lists Reactivation and Neutral separately; [Stripe's guide](https://stripe.com/resources/more/how-to-use-monthly-recurring-revenue-mrr-and-annual-recurring-revenue-arr-to-guide-growth) lists four movement types. **Inference:** source taxonomies differ, so follow the billing or analytics report's existing classification rather than assuming one universal mapping.
- Movement labels depend on the source system's event rules. ChartMogul can group related activity within a configured time window; a discount can count as contraction; cancellation timing depends on a churn-recognition setting; and its reports do not create movements for currency fluctuations. [Reclassification settings](https://help.chartmogul.com/article/117-reclassification-of-subscription-activity) and [churn recognition](https://help.chartmogul.com/article/242-churn-recognition) document these cases. Billing-system or reporting definitions can differ, so a calculator cannot infer a company's classifications from amounts alone. Use amounts for one period in one currency and the same MRR basis for every category.
- A true waterfall chart shows a running total as positive and negative category values are added and subtracted; intermediate bars float between the starting and ending values. That differs from a movement report that groups each category across multiple periods. [Microsoft's waterfall chart documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/visuals/power-bi-visualization-waterfall-charts) describes the visual behavior. ChartMogul's report guide describes its separate per-period stacked bands.

### Opportunity and blockers

The category set already appears in existing MRR calculators and subscription analytics reports, so the opportunity is not a novel formula. It is a small, clear manual bridge for someone who already has categorized MRR amounts: keep reactivation visible, show how each amount affects the running balance, and reconcile the final value without mixing in plan normalization, retention ratios, or broader company metrics. Make missing or blank movements explicit instead of silently treating them as zero, as observed in the operated Finin2min form.

A manual calculator needs no paid API, maintained dataset, account, or background job. It cannot check a billing export, confirm its MRR policy, or resolve how the source classifies borderline subscription events. No external blocker was found.

## Product

### Promise and scope

A solo SaaS founder or operator has categorized MRR movements for one reporting month and wants to reconcile the opening and closing recurring run rates. Enter starting MRR plus new, expansion, reactivation, contraction, and churned MRR. The tool returns ending MRR, the signed net change, and a running bridge that shows the balance after each movement. Reactivation remains its own category so a returning customer is not folded into new business.

Launch is a manual one-month calculation using one currency and one MRR definition across all six amounts. The intended inputs describe a completed month; the month field is a label and does not verify that data is final. Each amount is already normalized to monthly recurring subscription value; the tool does not normalize plans, inspect subscriptions, or decide which billing event belongs to which category. Follow the source report's classification and churn timing. A category value is the nonnegative magnitude of that movement: the engine adds new, expansion, and reactivation, then subtracts contraction and churn. A known zero must be entered as `0`; an unknown amount cannot be treated as zero.

This is the focused five-movement bridge. `/saas-metrics-calculator` uses four movements in a wider monthly dashboard with retention, accounts, margin, and burn; `/mrr-calculator` builds a current MRR total from plan counts, prices, and billing intervals. This page does not add those workflows, ARR, growth or retention ratios, forecasts, recognized revenue, cash receipts, benchmarks, health scores, import, account connection, saved scenarios, or export. It cannot verify that the supplied movement totals reconcile to a billing system's reported closing MRR because no independent closing value is entered.

### Technical contract

**Inputs and parsing.** Start with empty required fields for reporting month and all six amounts, and a visible USD selection. The month labels the completed calendar month; there is no timezone or date arithmetic. All six amount fields are required, including a movement that is exactly zero. Currency controls formatting only, never exchange conversion.

| Input | Accepted value |
| --- | --- |
| Reporting month | Strict `YYYY-MM`, January 2000 through December 2100 inclusive. |
| Currency | USD, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD, or AUD. All entered amounts must already use this one currency. |
| Starting MRR | Nonnegative amount from `0` through `1,000,000,000,000.00` inclusive. |
| New, expansion, reactivation, contraction, and churned MRR | Each a nonnegative amount from `0` through `1,000,000,000,000.00` inclusive. The person enters a magnitude; the engine supplies the category's sign. |

Trim outer whitespace. For money, accept only base-10 digits with an optional period and one or two fractional digits. Leading zeros are allowed. Reject blank, signs, commas, currency symbols, exponent notation, interior spaces, more than two decimals, nonfinite or non-string values, unsupported currency, and out-of-range values. Reject a malformed or out-of-range month; do not silently use the current month. Keep raw strings in the form and apply the same validation in the pure engine. Parse money into integer cents before calculating. The stated limits keep all intermediate balances and net change within JavaScript's safe-integer range.

**Calculation.** Let `S` be starting MRR and `N`, `E`, `R`, `C`, and `L` be new, expansion, reactivation, contraction, and churned MRR, all in cents. Compute the ordered balances `B0 = S`, `B1 = S + N`, `B2 = B1 + E`, `B3 = B2 + R`, `B4 = B3 - C`, and `B5 = B4 - L`. `Net new MRR = N + E + R - C - L`; `Ending MRR = B5 = S + Net new MRR`. Require `B5 >= 0`; when the supplied losses would make it negative, reject the complete submission rather than floor, clamp, or show a partial bridge. In this fixed order all additions precede reductions, so a nonnegative final value also keeps every displayed balance nonnegative. Do not impose further caps on individual movement categories: this calculator cannot verify the source system's classification or event timing.

**Output.** Show the entered month and selected currency with a lead ending MRR value, a signed net new MRR value, and the six ordered balances from starting through after churn. Represent the five signed movements explicitly. Format money with the selected currency, en-US digit grouping, and exactly two decimal places; do not abbreviate large values. Prefix positive movement and net-change values with `+`, negative ones with `-`, and show true zero without a negative sign. No intermediate rounding is needed because every accepted amount is already in cents. A zero starting amount, zero movement, zero net change, or zero ending amount is a valid value and must remain distinct from a missing input. The result is a calculated run-rate bridge, not cash collected or recognized revenue.

**Action and states.** `Calculate MRR waterfall` is the only primary action. A valid submission shows the whole bridge; there is no partial result when any of its six required amounts is missing. An empty, malformed, negative, excessive, or inconsistent value clears any previous result, preserves all entries, shows a precise field or movement-group error, and focuses the first affected field. A computed negative ending MRR belongs to the movement group and focuses churned MRR. Any edit to month, currency, or amount clears the previous result until recalculation; a currency edit never changes numeric input. An unexpected local calculation error clears the result, keeps the inputs, and leaves the primary action available to retry. The calculation is synchronous and local, so there is no loading, no-match, service, or network state. Reset restores empty month and amounts plus USD.

Keep month and financial figures in client component memory only. Do not put input values in the URL, browser storage, cookies, requests, logs, or analytics events. Existing site analytics may record a page visit without calculator values. Render entered values as text, never HTML; no user-supplied links are accepted.

## Interface

### Flow and control mode

The reader asks, "How did this month's five MRR movements take us from starting to ending MRR?" The lead answer is the calculated ending MRR for the entered month. `Calculate MRR waterfall` is the sole primary action. Control mode is `single`: every field is needed for the one complete bridge, and hiding a movement would hide an active assumption. Use the shared full-width workbench band with a measured inner width. Read in this order: reporting month and currency, starting MRR, three additions, two reductions, actions, one divider, ending MRR, signed net new MRR, then the running bridge.

### Controls and component decisions

| Area | Control and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Period and currency | Reuse `UiTextField` for required `Reporting month`, with `YYYY-MM` as an example, and `UiSelect` for the six currency choices. USD is visibly selected. A short note by the selector says it labels amounts and does not convert them. |
| Starting amount | Reuse required `UiTextField` for `Starting MRR`. Keep its raw string and use `inputMode="decimal"`; a numeric widget must not silently reshape invalid pasted money. |
| Additions | Reuse required `UiTextField` for `New MRR`, `Expansion MRR`, and `Reactivation MRR`, grouped under `Added during the month`. Keep reactivation visible between expansion and reductions. |
| Reductions | Reuse required `UiTextField` for `Contraction MRR` and `Churned MRR`, grouped under `Lost during the month`. They take nonnegative magnitudes; their labels and group context make the direction clear. |
| Actions | Reuse `UiActionRow` and `UiButton` for primary `Calculate MRR waterfall` and secondary `Reset`. Enter submits the form; Reset is a button. Keep Calculate enabled when fields are blank so submission can identify the missing values. |

Show the selected currency code once beside the MRR input group heading or in a fixed unit treatment; do not repeat a currency selector or helper under each amount. Say once beside the movement groups, `Enter 0 if a movement did not occur.` All seven text fields have persistent labels, visible required marks, programmatic required state, `autoComplete="off"`, capitalization and spellcheck off, and field-level errors. The article explains category classification and monthly normalization; the workbench needs only this short zero guidance. No mode switch, disclosure, slider, upload, export, or copy control serves the launch job.

### Result hierarchy and waterfall

Start with one neutral `UiPrimaryResult` labeled `Ending MRR`; its detail identifies the reporting month and selected currency. Immediately below, show one quiet labeled `Net new MRR` value with its sign. Do not also show ARR, growth, retention, or a second ending-MRR card. Use one region headed `How MRR changed` for the graphic and exact running figures:

1. A route-specific running-waterfall figure uses seven columns: opening and ending columns anchored at zero, with the five movements in Product order floating from each prior balance to the next. Thin connectors mark each successive balance. Positive bars rise, reduction bars descend; all columns share one zero-based monetary scale. This must encode cumulative balances, not five independent bars rising from zero or ChartMogul-style grouped movement bands. If the maximum balance is zero, render a flat zero baseline without invented bar height. Do not give a tiny nonzero movement a false minimum width or height.
2. Directly with the figure, reuse `UiDataTable` as its visible exact-value key and accessible alternative. Rows are `Starting MRR`, then each of the five movements; columns are stage, signed movement (`Not applicable` for the starting row), and balance after that stage. The final row's balance equals the lead ending MRR by necessity of auditing the bridge; do not create an additional ending row, table footer, or duplicate metric card. The table gives exact figures when small bars cannot be read and exposes the same sequence to screen readers.

The graphic is a route-specific component because `UiBarChart` compares nonnegative zero-based categories and `UiAllocationBar` shows parts of one total; neither can represent floating signed changes truthfully. Build the figure with semantic heading and caption, use existing chart data, text, border, and surface tokens, and hide the purely visual SVG from screen readers because the adjacent labeled table contains the data. The figure uses neutral anchor bars, cyan data fill for additions, and violet data fill for reductions; the `+`/`-` values and stage names carry meaning without color. If the graphic needs abbreviated scale ticks for width, the table still shows the full selected-currency amounts. Do not turn a negative net change into an error color or health judgment.

### States and recovery

- **Initial and empty:** month and six amounts are blank, USD is visible, and no numeric result, placeholder bridge, or fabricated sample is shown. Submitting blank fields is invalid; unknown is never rendered as zero.
- **Valid:** show the lead, signed net change, and all six running balances together; focus the labeled ending-MRR output and announce the result once. Explicit all-zero input yields a neutral `$0.00` ending and net change, a flat chart baseline, and zero table values. A zero starting amount with reactivation is valid; a zero ending amount is also valid.
- **Invalid:** keep every entered string, clear any prior result, show each missing or malformed amount error immediately below that field, and focus the first invalid control in visual order. Show an implied negative ending error once below `Churned MRR` and focus that field; do not display the negative figure as a result. Do not add an error result panel or duplicate a form summary. The engine also rejects unsupported currency even though the normal selector cannot produce it.
- **Edited after a result:** immediately clear the ending value, net change, chart, and table. Keep focus and the edited value, then announce `Inputs changed. Calculate again.` once. Changing currency invalidates the display but never converts an entered amount.
- **Unexpected failure and retry:** preserve input, clear every result, and show one `UiStatus` error beside the action saying the bridge could not be calculated and to try again. The same primary action retries. Calculation is synchronous and local, so no loading indicator, disabled calculating state, service retry, or partial result applies.
- **Reset and restoration:** clear month, amounts, errors, status, and result; restore visible USD, focus `Reporting month`, and announce reset. A clean `/mrr-waterfall-calculator` URL stays unchanged through hydration. Reload starts fresh; browser back/forward cache may restore form and result together, and the next edit clears the restored result. Do not serialize financial values into the URL or browser storage.

Use one polite status announcement for edits, reset, and failure; success is announced by moving focus to the result. Each field error must be associated with its input and read on focus. A zero is announced as a value, never as missing data.

Tab order follows visible controls, Calculate, Reset, then any result links. The shared select retains its keyboard and mobile semantics; the graphic has no interactive targets or custom keyboard shortcuts. Make the table's headers and row names explicit, with a caption connecting it to the waterfall. Use the shared focus ring, `--radius`, and both theme tokens. The real-page check should cover the decimal example, blank versus entered zero, negative-ending rejection, all-zero scale, a tiny movement beside a large base, maximum-length money values, focus and announcements, and desktop/mobile containment in both themes.
