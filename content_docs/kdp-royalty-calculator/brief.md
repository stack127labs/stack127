# Brief: KDP royalty calculator

## Who is here

An author is pricing a paperback, hardcover, or Kindle eBook for Amazon.com. They know the page count or file size and want to see whether a price leaves room for royalties and ads.

## The job

They leave with one selected-price royalty, the cost that produced it, the minimum viable price, and a small comparison that shows what changes at KDP's rate boundary. An optional monthly target turns the per-sale result into a unit count.

## Show vs say

The tool UI carries the work: a single scenario form, a royalty-first result, a cost receipt, a comparison table, and the ad and target math. A table compares price points because rate, cost, royalty, and margin repeat. Short sentences beside the result explain the US-only scope, the eBook 70% fallback, the source date, and limits such as taxes and price matching. No chart or marker drawing is needed.
