# Brief: Punycode and IDN Converter

## Who is here

A technical reader is checking an internationalized or suspicious domain and needs both its Unicode and ASCII forms.

## The job

They leave with a deterministic conversion, label limits, character code points and scripts, and an evidence-based mixed-script warning without a spoofing verdict.

## Show vs say

The browser-only converter and paired outputs lead. Tables carry per-label and per-character facts; a compact label breakdown shows expansion and limits. Sentences explain IDN encoding, browser display differences, mixed-script limits, Unicode versioning, and privacy.
