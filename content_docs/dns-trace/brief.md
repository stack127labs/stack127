# Brief: DNS Trace Explorer

## Who is here

A domain operator needs to see where one DNS question stops or changes direction between the root, TLD, and authoritative servers.

## The job

They leave with a genuine ordered iterative trace, the failed attempts and referrals behind it, and a focused next check when the path cannot finish.

## Show vs say

The result is one vertical path of trace steps with compact server, question, referral, glue, answer, timing, and raw-response evidence. Sentences interpret each transition and explain server-location, propagation, and DNSSEC limits.
