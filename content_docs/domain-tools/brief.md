# Brief: Domain tools

## Who is here

A domain owner, buyer, investigator, or operator wants a registration fact such as availability, age, expiry, registrar, status, or transfer readiness. They may still call the task WHOIS even though the tools use RDAP.

## The job

They should choose the focused lookup, see which registry or registrar supplied each fact, understand what is unknown or redacted, and know when the answer belongs in a DNS tool instead.

## Show vs say

The hub uses problem-to-tool cards rather than a prose tour. A compact source path shows how registry and registrar evidence differ, while the TLD directory carries the repeated coverage facts. Individual results keep fields structured, show registry and registrar evidence separately, and expose raw RDAP in a disclosure. Sentences explain policy, freshness, redaction, privacy, and scope where those caveats limit a result.
