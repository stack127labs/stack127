# Brief: Self-host apps

## Who is here

A self-hoster knows roughly what they want to run and is comparing apps against their server, storage, deployment method, source model, price, and maintenance expectations. They want open and closed-source choices in one place, with verified facts faster than another recommendation article.

## The job

They should narrow the directory, compare credible candidates, open a complete record, and reach the app's official site, source when available, container image, or deployment documentation without guessing which facts were verified.

## Show vs say

Filters and the card grid do the discovery work. Category, source model, deployment, and license use compact controls. Repeated facts such as RAM, storage, price, source model, release activity, categories, and deployment support stay structured on cards and in the details drawer. App logos prove identity, while one-sentence summaries explain purpose. The page uses a calm generated monogram only when no official image can be cached. Counts are live database facts. Longer prose is reserved for uncertainty and the limits of a field.
