# Brief: KDP page count estimator

## Who is here

A self-publishing author or formatter has a manuscript word count and a rough print design, but no final typeset PDF. They want to decide whether the current trim and typography are likely to fit before working on a cover or price.

## The job

They leave with a low to high paperback page estimate, the middle of that range as a planning anchor, and a clear breakdown of text pages, chapter starts, artwork pages, and front or back matter. They can also see which KDP gutter bands the range crosses.

## Show vs say

The tool UI is the main visual. The result leads with the page range, then uses an exact table for page additions and a compact model section for words per page and text-block dimensions. A short explanation should separate official KDP rules from stack127's typography assumptions and remind readers that the submitted PDF decides the final count. No marker art or chart is needed because the useful relationships are exact and fit a table.
