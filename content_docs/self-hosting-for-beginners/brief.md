# Brief: Self-hosting for beginners

## Who is here

A beginner wants to run useful apps at home but does not yet have a clear mental model for the machine, operating system, network, containers, storage, or backups. They need one calm path and enough detail to recover when a step goes wrong.

## The job

The reader should move from the idea of self-hosting to a working app they can reach safely, then understand the storage and backup choices that make it worth keeping.

## Show vs say

The course sidebar shows order and progress. The opening lesson uses the dotted entry header and one small self-hosting drawing; later lessons keep plain reading headers. Each lesson uses prose for judgment, instructions, and caveats; marker art for mental models; screenshots for real interfaces; tables only for repeated facts; and code blocks for commands. Lessons share the 760px reading canvas, directory gutters, heading rhythm, author treatment, and previous or next handoff. The course does not use a marketing landing page, decorative labels, or summary panels that repeat the lesson.
