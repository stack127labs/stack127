# Brief: DNS Lookup

## Who is here

A technical reader needs the current DNS records for an exact name or a PTR answer for an IP address and may need to compare recursive and authoritative sources.

## The job

They leave with readable records, aliases, owner names, TTL context, raw evidence, and enough guidance to choose the next diagnostic tool.

## Show vs say

The form and record result stay first. Tables compare record types, common troubleshooting jobs, and response meanings; one small path figure explains aliases. Sentences carry source scope, TTL interpretation, privacy, and the limits of a lookup.
