# Title

KDP payment date calculator: when will KDP pay you?

## Introduction

If you're planning around your book income, start with the month KDP reports your royalties for. A useful planning formula is: approximate payment month = reported earnings month + 2 calendar months for Standard, or + 3 for Expanded Distribution. Expect payment toward the end of that month, provided you've met any required payment threshold.

This month-based estimate follows KDP's [payment schedule](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GK2MKZUL6U3SFBPZ): about 60 days after the reported month ends, or 90 days for Expanded Distribution. The calculator applies the formula to your chosen month and shows the delivery guidance separately. Money takes additional time to reach your bank or mailing address, so use the result for planning rather than as a guaranteed arrival date.

## Usage

1. Enter your reported earnings month as `YYYY-MM`, such as `2026-01` for January 2026. Take the month from your KDP royalty report, rather than an individual customer's purchase date.
2. Choose Standard unless the royalties were reported through Expanded Distribution. A paperback can earn royalties through either channel; its format alone doesn't determine the schedule.
3. Select Estimate payment month. Read the approximate month together with the payment conditions and delivery guidance below it. If you change an input, select Estimate payment month again.

You can enter years from 2007 to 2100. Every entry uses the current schedule: future months are planning scenarios, and past months don't confirm that you were paid or that a payment is overdue.

## Supporting content

### Royalties from one month can follow two schedules

Suppose your January 2026 report includes both Standard and Expanded Distribution royalties. Estimate each part separately:

| Reported earnings month | Distribution | Approximate payment month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| January 2026 | Standard | March 2026 |
| January 2026 | Expanded Distribution | April 2026 |

For Standard, count forward from January through February to March. Expanded Distribution adds one more month. These examples assume any required threshold has been met. The difference matters when planning income from the same book across both channels; the calculator handles one distribution choice at a time.

### Check the report if a payment hasn't arrived

Start with your KDP Payment Report and find the relevant sales period. Check its payment status and recorded payment date. KDP defines the payment date as the date Amazon transferred the funds, which can be earlier than the day they appear in your bank account. The [Payment Report help](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201436840) explains the fields.

If you receive checks or wire transfers, compare your balance with the [threshold for your marketplace and payment method](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201207800). Each marketplace's royalties accumulate separately, and applicable tax withholding affects the amount counted toward its threshold. A balance below the threshold carries forward, so the estimate here may be too early. The calculator has no balance information and can't work out when that carried balance will be paid.

Direct deposit has no KDP payment threshold, although bank minimums can still apply after currency conversion or tax withholding. Once a payment has been issued, allow the delivery time shown with your result, counting from the recorded payment date. That guidance includes the longer direct deposit window for banks in India. If a bank payment is marked Paid but hasn't arrived after the expected delivery time, [KDP advises checking with your financial institution](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G4EQZQ73RYEX8RQQ).

For the amount you could earn from a sale, use the [KDP royalty calculator](/kdp-royalty-calculator).

### Result

Label: Approximate payment month

Value: {payment month and year}

Detail: Amazon usually issues payment toward the end of this month. Allow extra time for delivery.

Eligibility note: Direct deposit has no KDP payment threshold. Check and wire payments must meet the [threshold for each marketplace](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201207800). Balances below the threshold carry forward and can be paid later. This calculator can't confirm your payment eligibility or status.

Calculation basis, Standard: For {reported month and year}, the Standard estimate adds two calendar months, based on KDP's approximate 60-day schedule.

Calculation basis, Expanded Distribution: For {reported month and year}, the Expanded Distribution estimate adds three calendar months, based on KDP's approximate 90-day schedule.

Delivery heading: After Amazon issues payment

Delivery introduction: Allow the following time from the actual payment date. These are delivery estimates.

| Payment method | Typical delivery after issue |
| --- | --- |
| Direct deposit | 1 to 5 business days |
| Direct deposit to banks in India | 7 to 10 business days |
| Wire transfer | 5 to 10 days |
| Check | Up to 30 days |

Verification: Check your KDP Payment Report for an actual payment's date and status. [Understand your Payment Report](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201436840).

Source note: Based on [KDP payment timing](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GK2MKZUL6U3SFBPZ). Rules checked 23 September 2026.
