# Brief: DNSSEC Chain Checker

## Who is here

A domain or DNS operator needs a cryptographic verdict for one answer or denial proof, not a guess from a resolver flag.

## The job

They leave with a secure, insecure, bogus, or indeterminate result and can see which DS, DNSKEY, RRSIG, or denial step supports it.

## Show vs say

An ordered chain figure carries the trust path and the validator's evidence. Tables show keys, signatures, algorithms, validity windows, and raw responses. Sentences explain verdict meaning, unsupported cases, and where repair should begin.
