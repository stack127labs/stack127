# Brief: Privacy policy of version127

## Who is here

A visitor wants a practical account of what happens to their data when they browse the site, use a domain tool, follow an external link, or send an email. They need concrete service names and data flows, not generic privacy language.

## The job

They should understand what Cloudflare, the VPS, Umami, registries, registrars, and email can process, why each use exists, and which choices remain available to them.

## Show vs say

Policy details need full sentences because the limits and caveats matter. Headings separate collection, use, sharing, external links, choices, and future changes so the page can be scanned. A chart would imply precision that the policy does not have, and a table would separate caveats from the claims they qualify. Concrete data-flow wording replaces decorative diagrams or callout panels.
