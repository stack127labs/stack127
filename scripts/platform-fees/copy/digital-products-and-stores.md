## What will I actually pay on a sale?

Usually more than the big number on the card. Most sales pay the platform's cut and a card fee, and some add a fixed amount per order. The bar on a card counts only the percentage.

> [!EXAMPLE] A $20 download, US seller and buyer, paid by card
> | | Gumroad, direct sale | Lemon Squeezy, direct sale |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | Platform fee | $2.50 (10% + 50¢) | $1.50 (5% + 50¢) |
> | Card fee | $0.88 (2.9% + 30¢) | Included |
> | You keep | $16.62 | $18.50 |
>
> Both handle sales tax for you. Gumroad may add sales tax on its own fees. Lemon Squeezy adds 1.5% for international and PayPal payments.

## Should I sell on a marketplace or from my own page?

Your own page usually costs less per sale. A marketplace can bring you buyers, but some charge more for the sales they find:

- Gumroad Discover and the Lemon Squeezy marketplace take 30% of the sales they bring you.
- Payhip adds no extra fee to marketplace sales.
- Etsy charges its 6.5% fee on every sale. A sale from its Offsite Ads costs 12% to 15% more, up to $100 per order.
- On itch.io, you set the platform's share, from 0% to 100%, and it's the same wherever the sale comes from.

## Is a monthly plan cheaper than a cut of each sale?

It is once you sell enough each month. Divide the plan price by the share of each sale it saves you.

> [!FORMULA] When a plan pays for itself
> `plan price ÷ fee saved = monthly sales where the plan breaks even`

> [!EXAMPLE] Payhip
> Plus costs $29 a month and lowers the fee from 5% to 2%. It pays for itself above about $967 a month in sales ($29 ÷ 3%). Pro costs $99 and drops the fee to 0%. It beats Plus above $3,500 a month ($70 ÷ 2%).

Card fees apply on every Payhip plan, so they don't change the answer. Stan Store and Big Cartel charge a plan and take no cut, so you pay the plan plus card fees.

## Who handles sales tax and VAT?

The merchant of record does. That's the business legally selling to your buyer, so it collects and pays the tax.

| Who handles it | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| The platform, as merchant of record | Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Fourthwall |
| The platform, in some cases | Etsy collects VAT on digital items in listed countries and US sales tax where the law requires it. itch.io handles VAT in its Payouts mode. |
| You | Sellfy, Ko-fi Shop, and DPD can add tax at checkout, but you file and pay it. |

Open **Sources and dates** in the downloads section for each platform's tax rule. To compare checkout services such as Paddle, Polar, and Stripe, see [Payments](/platform-fees/payments).

## What does "Base cost" mean on merch cards?

You set the price, the service charges what the product and shipping cost, and you keep the rest. Marketplaces work differently. Redbubble takes a share of your earnings that depends on your account, and TeePublic and Displate pay a fixed amount per item.
