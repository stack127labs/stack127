# One writing voice across stack127

Teja approved a shared, original, Verge-inspired voice for the entire website. The canonical skill is `skills/stack127/stack127-writing-voice/SKILL.md`. It applies to explanatory prose and small interface strings, with tone adapted to their purpose.

`stack127_docs/writing-style.md` is now its companion for house conventions, examples, and responsibility boundaries. The content, SEO, interface, marker-art, audit, and directory-enrichment skills explicitly load the voice where they produce or review public wording. `AGENTS.md` covers website copy outside those specialist workflows. Unslop remains the final cleanup pass; the external writing handbook remains supplementary.

The voice skill does not own topic selection, page structure, component choice, technical behavior, legal obligations, or publication. It does not require articles, glossary sections, tables, or a fixed length. Existing skills retain those task-specific boundaries. The approved Domain Age page remains an example of editorial judgment rather than a sitewide template.

Validation included skill-format checks and an independent draft-only exercise covering a beginner explanation, a button, a timeout message, and privacy wording. The exercise preserved the supplied technical and privacy facts without inventing the timeout cause or a retention promise. No website copy, database content, or production state changed as part of this update.

The repository remains the source of truth. Existing `.agents/skills` and `.claude/skills` adapters both resolve the new skill without copied installations.
